### Best First arXiv Paper

### arXiv Submission Format Notes
- LaTeX preferred, but PDF is accepted
- Abstract must be under 1920 characters
- No marketing language — purely mathematical claims

**Sample arXiv Abstract for URB #537:**
```
We prove a precise bound on consecutive single-halving compound Collatz steps 
(k=1 steps). For odd n, define the k=1 condition as (3n+1)/2 being odd, 
equivalently n ≡ 3 (mod 4), equivalently ν₂(n+1) ≥ 2. We prove that under 
each k=1 step, the 2-adic valuation ν₂(n+1) decreases by exactly 1. It follows 
that the maximum consecutive k=1 streak beginning at odd n is ν₂(n+1)−1. 
All k=1 runs are O(log n) in length. After every k=1 run, a k≥2 step is 
guaranteed. We verify the bound with zero exceptions for all odd n ≤ 5119 
(≈2500 test cases). A Lean 4 formalization of the core lemma is provided.
```
