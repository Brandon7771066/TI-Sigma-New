## General CapCut Tips (All Videos)

### Export Settings
- Resolution: 1080p
- Format: MP4
- Frame rate: 30fps

### Text Overlay Style (Keep Consistent)
- Font: Bold sans-serif (Impact, Montserrat Bold, or similar)
- Color: White with black outline/shadow for readability
- Position: Lower third or center, never blocking key visuals
- Numbers (0.42, 0.85, etc.): Slightly larger, different color (teal or gold)

### Audio
- If Pictory's AI voice pacing feels slow, speed up in CapCut (1.1x-1.15x)
- Background music: Low and subtle. Pictory may add some — adjust volume to 15-20% of voice

### End Screens
- Leave 20 seconds of space at the end for YouTube end screen elements
- Add a static "Subscribe" graphic + "Next Video" placeholder
