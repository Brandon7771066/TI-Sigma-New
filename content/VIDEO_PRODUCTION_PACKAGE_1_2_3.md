---

# HOW TO IMPORT YOUR NARRATION INTO CAPCUT

1. Open CapCut → **New Project**
2. Tap **"+"** → **Audio** → **From files** → select your narrated MP3/WAV
3. Your audio now sits on the timeline. This is your master track.
4. Go to **"Stock"** → search each visual term below as the audio plays
5. Drag stock clips ABOVE your audio track on the timeline
6. Use **Auto-Captions** (bottom toolbar → "Captions") after all visuals are placed
7. Add text overlays where specified below
