## The Full CapCut Workflow (Per Video)

### Step 1 — Script to Video (~10 minutes)
1. Open CapCut → "AI Video" or "Script to Video"
2. Paste the 2-minute script from `content/PICTORY_VIDEO_SCRIPTS.md`
3. Let CapCut auto-generate: AI voice + stock footage + auto-pacing
4. Select voice: professional male or female (avoid the obviously robotic options)
5. Review the auto-generated draft — accept what's good, note what needs fixing

### Step 2 — Visual Review (~10 minutes)
- Replace any stock footage that clearly doesn't match the script topic
- CapCut's library is large — search terms: "consciousness," "brain waves," "network," "cosmos," "data," "mathematics"
- For the stock market video: search "stock chart," "trading," "market data"
- Don't overthink this — 70% visual match is fine

### Step 3 — Key Text Overlays (~10 minutes)
These are the high-ROI additions. Add bold text pop-ups at these moments:

**All videos:**
- "0.42" when manifestation threshold mentioned
- "0.85" when causation/persistence threshold mentioned
- "0.92" when radiant threshold mentioned
- "√2 and φ" when the constants are mentioned

**Video 1 (TI Sigma Intro):** "82%" accuracy, "330+ papers"
**Video 2 (TRALSE):** "4 TRUTH VALUES", "Indeterminate = 3rd", "Tralse = 4th"
**Video 3 (GILE):** G / I / L / E as individual badges for each dimension
**Video 4 (Stock Markets):** "82% ACCURACY" large at hook, "5 REGIMES"

**Text style:** Bold, white with black shadow, large enough to read at phone screen size

### Step 4 — Auto Captions
- Enable CapCut's auto-caption feature
- This dramatically increases watch time (most people watch without sound)
- Edit any mispronunciations: "GILE" (should be "GUY-EL" or "JILE"), "Tralse" (TRAL-ss), "Myrion" (MEER-ee-on)

### Step 5 — Pacing
- 1.1x speech speed overall feels natural and keeps energy up
- Slow to 0.9x on the hook (first 3 seconds) — let it breathe
- Slow to 0.9x on the closing CTA

### Step 6 — Hook (First 3 Seconds)
This is the most important edit. Add a large-text hook at the very start:

| Video | Hook Text |
|-------|-----------|
| Video 1 | "One framework connects consciousness, markets, and truth" |
| Video 2 | "Quantum physics isn't confusing. Our logic is." |
| Video 3 | "Four letters describe ALL of reality." |
| Video 4 | "82% accuracy. No insider information." |

### Step 7 — Outro
- Last 5 seconds: "Subscribe for more TI Sigma" text overlay
- Leave the final 20 seconds relatively clean for YouTube end screens (added after upload)

### Step 8 — Export
- Resolution: 1080p
- Format: MP4
- On mobile: CapCut watermark can be removed by exporting without template overlays, or upgrade to Pro ($8/month if needed)

## Sacred Laziness Levels (Still Applies)

**Level 1 — Maximum Sacred Laziness:**
CapCut auto-generate → accept defaults → export → upload. Skip all manual text overlays and pacing. ~15 minutes per video. Gets the content out.

**Level 2 — Minimal Touch:**
Auto-generate + hook text overlay + key number overlays. ~25 minutes per video. Significantly better retention.

**Level 3 — Full Polish:**
Everything above + captions edited + pacing adjusted + custom visuals. ~45 minutes per video. Best for high-priority videos.

Start with Level 1 for all 4 videos to get them live. Polish later based on what the algorithm responds to.

## CapCut-Specific Tips

**AI Voice options that work well:**
- "Jony" — calm, professional male
- "Aria" — clear professional female
- Avoid overly enthusiastic or obviously synthetic voices

**Stock footage search terms that work:**
- Neural network, brain scan, brain waves, consciousness
- Stock market, trading chart, financial data
- Mathematical equation, formula, blackboard
- Cosmos, universe, fractal, mandelbrot
- DNA, helix, molecule

**Template recommendation:**
Use the "Documentary" or "Educational" template base if CapCut asks — these have the right pacing and typography style for explanatory content.

**Shorts vs. Long-form:**
The 2-minute scripts can be exported as either 16:9 (YouTube standard) or 9:16 (Shorts). Shorts get far more initial views but attract a different audience. Consider making both versions of Video 1 to test which performs better.
