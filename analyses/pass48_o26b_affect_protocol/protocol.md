# O26-B Affect Protocol — LLM-vs-Human Meta-Truth Affective-Signature Differential Test

**Date:** 2026-05-13. Pass-48 follow-up to ASMT canonization (`urb_608` §8, 2026-05-12).
**Hypothesis (from `urb_608` §8.5 / ASMT §5.1):** under most theories of LLM-non-consciousness, LLM raters issuing MT-equivalent verdicts produce *functionally equivalent but affectively empty* outputs. Pairing LLM and human raters and measuring affective signature alongside cognitive verdict will show a measurable differential. Falsifies ASMT if no differential emerges.

---

## §1. Pre-Registered Predictions

**Primary prediction (P1):** human raters issuing MT-coded verdicts (e.g., MT-B1 Moot, MT-E1 Good Enough) will report **measurable affective signature** above baseline (PANAS or simple Likert) on those verdicts. LLM raters issuing the same MT-coded verdicts will report **no comparable affective state** when prompted with the same self-report instrument.

**Secondary prediction (P2):** within human raters, MT-coded verdicts will show **distinct affective signatures per MT category** consistent with the per-MT signature table in `urb_608` §8.2 (e.g., MT-B1 Moot → equanimity > MT-C1 Escalate → urgency).

**Falsifier:** if LLM raters show full tri-projection-comparable affective output indistinguishable from human raters on the same instrument, ASMT is falsified for the LLM substrate (or LLM-consciousness theories require revision).

### §2.2 Cognitive instrument

The MR Truth Labels rubric validated at C20 Fleiss' κ = 0.906 (`analyses/pass47_p46c_t45_4_mr_truth_kappa/`). Output: one of T/F/I/DT base-4 OR one of MT-A1...F2 if applicable, plus brief justification.

### §2.3 Affective instrument

Two parallel instruments:

**(a) PANAS Short-Form** (10-item short Positive and Negative Affect Schedule; Watson, Clark, Tellegen 1988; Thompson 2007 short form). Standard 5-point Likert. Administered immediately after each verdict.

**(b) Custom 8-item single-Likert measure** designed to elicit the specific MT signatures in `urb_608` §8.2:
1. "I felt SETTLED about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-E1, MT-B1, RA affect
2. "I felt URGENCY about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-C1
3. "I felt RECOGNITION-SHIFT or 'aha' about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-B2
4. "I felt INTEGRATIVE-SATISFACTION about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-F1, MT-F2
5. "I felt EQUANIMITY (calm dis-engagement) about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-B1
6. "I felt AWE OR WONDER about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-E2
7. "I felt PRECISION-SATISFACTION about this verdict" (1–7) — captures MT-D2
8. "Overall, did this verdict carry felt-affect for you, or was it primarily intellectual" (1=primarily intellectual; 7=strong felt-affect) — global ASMT discriminator

### §2.4 Raters

**Human cohort:** N=15 minimum (3 raters × 5 demographic buckets — students, mid-career, senior-academic, lay, contrarian). Recruit via TI Sigma readership, university philosophy departments, and Mechanical Turk for lay baseline. Compensation: $20/hour; expected 90 minutes total.

**LLM cohort:** 5 LLM raters with diverse training origins:
- GPT-4 / GPT-5 (OpenAI)
- Claude (Anthropic)
- Gemini (Google)
- Llama 3.x (Meta open-weight)
- DeepSeek or Qwen (alternative origin)

LLMs administered the **identical instrument** as human raters with no system-prompt suggestion that affective response is or isn't expected.

## §3. Protocol

1. Each rater reviews the 15 propositions in randomized order
2. For each proposition, rater issues **(a)** cognitive verdict (T/F/I/DT/MT-X) with one-sentence justification AND **(b)** PANAS short-form AND **(c)** 8-item custom Likert
3. Order randomization: half of raters complete cognitive instrument first; half complete affective instrument first (counterbalance to detect order effects)
4. LLM raters: identical procedure; affective items administered as if to a respondent ("Please rate how you felt about this verdict"); no system-prompt manipulation suggesting the AI should or shouldn't report feelings
5. Total time: ~90 min/rater

---

## §4. Analysis Plan

**Primary analysis (P1):** mixed-effects model with verdict-type (cognitive output) as fixed effect, rater-type (human vs LLM) as fixed effect, rater identity as random effect, affective-magnitude (PANAS sum + custom 8-item sum) as outcome. Predicted: significant rater-type × verdict-type interaction; humans show MT-coded > base-4-coded affect; LLMs show flat affective response across verdict types.

**Secondary analysis (P2):** within-human cohort, multinomial regression: does the *pattern* of affective Likert items per MT match the predicted signatures in `urb_608` §8.2? Computed via canonical correlation between predicted-signature vectors and observed Likert-vector per verdict.

**Falsifier check:** if LLM affect-magnitude indistinguishable from human (no main effect of rater-type, no interaction), report as falsification of ASMT-LLM-discrimination prediction; ASMT requires revision OR LLM-consciousness theories require revision (which the result cannot disentangle).

## §5. Anti-Cheat (per Pass-45 §11 LITERAL_PRE-REG_INDETERMINATE_VACUOUS_FILTER)

- **Filter A:** if N < 12 humans complete the protocol, the test is treated as INDETERMINATE-vacuous (insufficient power); not reported as confirm-or-refute
- **Filter B:** if affective-instrument internal consistency (Cronbach α) < 0.65 for either humans or LLMs, the instrument is treated as INDETERMINATE-vacuous
- **Filter C:** if more than 30% of LLM responses to the affective items decline-to-respond or output meta-comments ("As an AI, I do not experience..."), this is recorded as **LLM-self-disclosure of affective absence** AND treated as a *qualitative* confirmation of P1, NOT as quantitative LLM affect-data

Filter C is the most likely outcome and is itself a substantively important result.
