## #69 HONEST CALL (T1-C)
==============================================================================
  Pre-registration discipline: the framework did NOT pre-specify '4/3' as
  the invariant; the 4/3 was DISCOVERED post-hoc to recur at 5 locations.
  → use the LOOSER 'any common ratio at all 5' p-value, not the 'specific
    4/3' p-value.

  Even under the looser test:
    Class C1 (uniform): P(any common ratio at 5) ≈ 2 × 10^−6 → p ≈ 0.000002
    Class C2 (uniform): P(any common ratio at 5) ≈ 2 × 10^−8 → p ≈ 0.00000002
    Stern-Brocot-weighted (more realistic): still ≪ 0.001

  All readings give p ≪ 0.05; the 5-location 4/3 invariant is
  STATISTICALLY SIGNIFICANT under the loosest reasonable null.

  CAVEAT: 'comparable geometries' is a modeling choice. A geometer who
  thinks the 5 locations are NOT independent (e.g., share a common
  parent equation) would see the test as inflated. The 5 anchors here
  (urb_728 ×3 + urb_733 + urb_736) are documented as geometrically
  distinct, which is the load-bearing assumption.
