# Empirical Tests & Falsifiers for the Book's Most Impactful Claims

> **Companion to** `book/STRONGEST_CLAIMS_RANKED.md` (the significance triage) and
> `book/HEDGE_INVENTORY_V2.md` (the hedge audit).
>
> **What this is.** For each of the 11 ranked claims, this gathers (a) the **proposed
> empirical test(s)** that would move it from framework-internal/partial toward
> corpus-backed, and (b) the **open falsifiers** that could kill it. Tests already
> *executed* (as method-validation sims, never on human data) are marked
> **[SIM DONE]**; tests still requiring a real study are **[PRE-REG]**.
>
> **#69 / Constructive-Honesty standard (read this first).** Every executed item here
> is a **method-validation / power simulation with NO human data** — it shows a test
> is well-posed, adequately powered, and confound-robust. That is a **necessary, not
> sufficient** precondition: it is *not* evidence the underlying claim is true in
> humans. The "real test" for every claim is the pre-registered human/empirical study,
> named explicitly below. A falsifier being **OPEN** means it has not yet been run, so
> it has neither confirmed nor refuted the claim.

## Tier S

### Claim 1 — The case against physicalism / dissolving the hard problem (Ch. 11, 12)
**Anchor:** PDU-1 (`papers/PASS_77_B127_PHYSICAL_DEFINITIONAL_INSTABILITY_PDU_1_2026-06-23.md`).
This is a **conceptual/philosophical** claim, so its "tests" are argumentative
challenges, not experiments — but it has crisp falsifiers.

* **Proposed tests (conceptual):**
  - Issue the **definability challenge**: produce a fixed, non-circular definition of
    "physical" that is neither hostage to *current* physics (Hempel Horn-1, almost
    certainly false given the ~95% dark sector) nor an empty *future-physics*
    promissory note (Horn-2). Survival of the challenge = the claim stands.
  - **Strong vs weak emergence demonstration** (Chalmers): show whether felt
    experience is *derivable in principle* from physical parts (weak) or not (strong).
* **Falsifiers (OPEN):**
  - **PDU-1-F1** — someone supplies a fixed, non-circular, non-trivial definition of
    "physical." *(Would refute the central move.)*
  - **PDU-1-F2** — consciousness is shown to be merely *weakly* emergent.
  - **PDU-1-F3** — the "resist-new-phenomenon-then-relabel-it-physical" pattern is
    shown false, and physicalism is shown to make borne-out novel predictions.
* **#69:** PDU-1 is **dissolving, not constructive** — it lowers physicalism's
  standing; it does **not** prove TOF-1 (it is only *consistent* with it). The
  strong-emergence step is the most contested link. Keep chakras/intuition
  **illustrative only**.

### Claim 2 — UOP generalizes Friston's Free Energy Principle (Ch. 8)
**Anchor:** URB-525 (UOP), URB-620 (brain-imaging programme).
* **Proposed tests [PRE-REG]:**
  - On agents that provably minimize variational free energy, show the UOP's
    **two-axis** objective `TF = (1−TT)² + (1−G)²` reduces to the FEP in the
    value-free limit (G ≡ 1) and *adds* discriminating predictions when value varies.
  - **Interior-optimum recovery:** behaviourally recover a penalty for pushing
    coherence/value past the joint optimum (see Claim 11).
* **Falsifier (OPEN):** if the only empirical content of the UOP is the FEP's (no
  value-axis prediction ever discriminates), the "generalization" is decorative.
* **#69:** PARTIAL — the reduction is argued, not demonstrated on real agents; the
  symmetric equal-weighting of TT and G is a modelling choice.

### Claim 3 — i-Cell Theory generalizes the Markov Blanket (Ch. 10)
**Anchor:** URB-532 (i-Cell/tralsebit), URB-620 §i-Cell layer.
* **Proposed tests:**
  - **Reduction theorem (Level 4)** — formally confirm the i-Boundary *reduces to* the
    Markov Blanket at the biological scale (recovering FEP evidence as a special case).
  - **[PRE-REG]** the five brain-imaging contributions (GILE circuit map, Emerick
    Threshold, Tralse states, D2 contradiction meter, directed LCC-Virus) via SPM/DCM.
* **Falsifier (OPEN):** the i-Boundary fails to reduce to the Markov Blanket at
  Level 4, or the eight-level universality has no testable content above Level 4.
* **#69:** confirmed empirically **only at Level 4** (inherited FEP evidence); the
  tralsebit `z_B = s + ia` formalism is a **stub**, not finished.

## Tier A

### Claim 4 — Tralseness is fundamental; reality is 4-valued, not binary (Ch. 2, 7)
**Anchor:** MR Truth Labels ruling; PD-representation comparative study (B108).
* **Tests:**
  - **[SIM DONE]** The **gold-proposition labelling benchmark** (500 gold props × 3
    raters): NA-holding representations reach **~0.918–0.922** vs **~0.746** for
    NA-blind — the imaginary/indeterminate axis is the measured, decisive upgrade.
    *This is the corpus's single strongest empirical asset for the logic claim — lead
    with it.*
  - **[PRE-REG]** replicate the benchmark on an **external**, independently-labelled
    proposition set to rule out in-corpus rater bias.
* **Falsifiers (OPEN):** HMR-1-F6/F7/F8, MR-IDC-1-F-leeway; and at the metaphysical
  level TOF-1-F1/F2/F3, TRG-1-F1/F2/F3.
* **#69:** the **metaphysical** claim (TOF-1/TRG-1) is FRAMEWORK-INTERNAL; only the
  **labelling-benchmark** sub-claim has real numbers — and those numbers are on
  corpus-generated gold props (hence the external-replication test).

### Claim 5 — Falsifiability retired as a strict criterion (LDD-1) (Ch. 9)
**Anchor:** LDD-1 (`papers/PASS_77_B124_...LDD_1...md`).
* **Proposed test (conceptual):** apply the **Lakatos progressive-vs-degenerating**
  audit to the corpus's own refinements — a legitimate definitional defense needs
  {no equivocation, independent motivation, residual exposure to other tests}.
* **Falsifiers (OPEN):** LDD-1-F1/F2/F3 — e.g. a case where a redefinition with all
  three properties is still clearly illegitimate, or where strict falsifiability is
  shown to be indispensable after all.
* **#69:** this is the book's **most mainstream-defensible** claim (Lakatos,
  Duhem-Quine, bounded Popper). The genuine residual constraint is
  **measurement-contamination** (don't score a construct from the outcome it
  predicts), which the gate keeps even as it widens to confirmation+pragmatics.
  **De-hedge hard.**

### Claim 6 — Evidence vs weight vs proof (EVD-1) (Ch. 12)
**Anchor:** EVD-1 (`papers/EVIDENCE_DOCTRINE_CANONICAL_RULING_EVD_1_2026-06-22.md`).
* **Proposed test [PRE-REG]:** **inter-rater reliability** — independent coders
  classify a corpus of arguments into evidence (binary, authority-independent) vs
  weight (graded, signed) vs proof; high agreement + the weighting rule
  (reasoning-quality primary, track-record secondary) predicting downstream judgments
  would back it.
* **Falsifier (OPEN):** coders cannot reliably separate the three categories, or the
  ranks (reasoning-quality > track-record) must swap to fit cases.
* **#69:** CORPUS-BACKED as a **definitional/normative** claim — the right kind of
  thing to state confidently; the empirical reliability study is the upgrade.

### Claim 7 — GILE: a four-axis theory of value irreducible to reward (Ch. 5, 6)
**Anchor:** URB-620 §7 (anatomical-dissociation predictions).
* **Proposed test [PRE-REG]:** **fMRI anatomical dissociation** — G→dlPFC–ACC vs
  L→vmPFC–amygdala, with the four axes partially dissociable (not collapsing to a
  single valence/reward scalar).
* **Falsifier (OPEN):** the four axes load on a single reward/valence factor, or the
  predicted circuit dissociation is absent.
* **#69:** PARTIAL — the predictions are **sharp and falsifiable**, but **zero
  brain-imaging replications exist yet** and the GILE weights are
  empirically-motivated, not derived. State the structure confidently; mark the neural
  validation as the open experiment.

### Claim 8 — Intelligence/genius = the GILE tetrad, not raw g (Ch. 21)
**Anchor:** GIT-1 (B121/B122); packages `analyses/pass77_b121_...`, `..._b122_...`.
* **Tests:**
  - **[SIM DONE — but contaminated/structure-only]** the headline AUC numbers
    (GILE 0.862 vs g 0.699) are **no-data structure-sims that build in the
    correlation**; B122 showed an AUC=1.000 result was **measurement contamination**
    (scoring the predictor from the outcome), and the identical hindsight-peek
    inflates g's AUC to 0.991 too — the artifact is construct-agnostic.
  - **[PRE-REG] the real test:** a **prospective, outcome-blind** study measuring
    GILE-tetrad facets and later truth-vindication.
* **Falsifiers (OPEN):** GIT-1-F1..F5 (F2 restated as **anti-contamination**: any
  outcome-contaminated *measurement* invalidates the result; *defining/refining* GILE
  is fine if progressive, per LDD-1).
* **#69:** PARTIAL — the defensible core is the **substantive trait→truth** claim
  (same form as "g predicts income"), scored **outcome-blind**. Even perfect GILE only
  *leans* toward truth (cap ~0.85, never 1.0; RTI-1/TRG-1 ceiling).

## Tier B

### Claim 9 — The Law of Correlational Causation (LCC) (Ch. 13)
**Anchor:** URB-620 §6 (E1–E5 programme). **This is the most empirically active
claim — and the most fragile.**
* **Tests executed as method-validation sims (NO human data):**
  - **[SIM DONE] E3 — dyad hyperscanning** (B130): directed Granger carrier→host
    gamma asymmetry; the naive symmetric statistic is confoundable, the directed test
    isolates asymmetry.
  - **[SIM DONE] E5 — network propagation** (B131): the **aggregate logistic curve
    "confirms contagion" 96% of the time under a shared-environment confound with no
    transmission**; only the **time-stratified CMH** test isolates true contagion
    (power 0.995, FPR 0.05).
  - **[SIM DONE] E2 — Emerick Threshold** (B131): "beats a straight line" fires 56% on
    pure quadratic curvature; requiring the breakpoint to **beat the best smooth
    polynomial** recovers the true discontinuity (power 0.945) and **locates it at
    θ̂ = 0.4156 vs the predicted √2−1 = 0.4142**.
* **Falsifiers (OPEN):** E3-SIM-F1/F2/F3, **E5-SIM-F1/F2/F3, E2-SIM-F1/F2/F3** (see
  `papers/PASS_77_B131_...md` §5). The **F3** in each pair is the **real
  pre-registered human study** — the genuine test.
* **#69 (load-bearing):** LCC was **falsified** on a raw word-token substrate
  (URB-795; signal survived only in hidden-state activations), and an earlier
  human-session ratio was downgraded to **"overclaim" (n=2)**. The E-series sims are
  **necessary-not-sufficient** design validations; **do not state cross-person LCC as
  established.** This is a case where a caveat genuinely belongs.

### Claim 10 — Superintelligence as sanity / non-maximization (SIS-1) (Ch. 22)
**Anchor:** SIS-1 (`papers/PASS_77_B123_...SIS_1...md`).
* **Proposed test [PRE-REG]:** a **behaviour-blind** study — measure GILE-completeness
  and later observe whether higher-GILE agents show **more restraint** (do only the
  necessary), independent of capability.
* **Falsifiers (OPEN):** SIS-1-F1..F4 — e.g. higher GILE does **not** predict
  restraint behaviour-blind; or restraint reduces to inaction.
* **#69:** FRAMEWORK-INTERNAL but well-reasoned, with **honest load-bearing limits**:
  it **relocates** AI risk (orthogonality survives — danger = capability-*without*-
  GILE-completeness, the easier thing to engineer) → argues for **harder** alignment,
  not reassurance.

### Claim 11 — An interior optimum at G*≈0.93 (Ch. 6, 8)
**Anchor:** UOP expansion (B70); G* calibration (#69 axiom).
* **Proposed test [PRE-REG]:** recover a **penalty above the optimum** behaviourally
  (performance/wellbeing falls when any single axis is pushed past the joint optimum),
  and **re-calibrate the constant** across domains.
* **Falsifier (OPEN):** no interior optimum exists (monotone "more is better"), or the
  optimum's location is wildly context-dependent with no stable ~0.93.
* **#69:** FRAMEWORK-INTERNAL — present the **principle** (interior optimum)
  confidently; present the **0.93 constant** as a *calibrated* value resting on the
  "radiant naturalness" axiom, which would move the number if it proves
  context-dependent.

## How to use this file

1. When a reviewer asks "what would falsify X?", every Tier S/A/B claim now has a
   named, OPEN falsifier and a named real test.
2. The **executed sims** (Claims 4, 8, 9) are **design validations, not evidence** —
   always pair the number with its #69 caveat and the F3 "real study" pointer.
3. The cheapest credibility wins are the **conceptual/normative** claims (5, 6) and
   the **external replication** of the labelling benchmark (4); the **fMRI programme**
   (3, 7, 9) is well-specified but unrun.

*Sources verified against primary papers (URB-525, URB-532, URB-620, B108, B121/122,
B123, B124, B127, B130, B131) and the corpus falsifier ledger. Emerick Threshold =
√2 − 1 ≈ 0.4142 (not the ≈0.437 in some secondary summaries).*
