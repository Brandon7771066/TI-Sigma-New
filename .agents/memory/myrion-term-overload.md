# "Myrion" is triple-overloaded — do not conflate the senses

As of the EVD-1 Myrion refinement, the root "Myrion" names three different things:

1. **Myrion (balance target)** — the *ideal situation-specific balance between GILE Truth and Existence (HEM)*. The speaker's job is to "express Myrion." It is the UOP interior optimum (G*≈0.93, never 1.0) made personal + SUP-1 willed striving, applied to the act of speaking. Lives in EVD-1 §5 (the "speaker's deeper duty" key quote) and book ch12 / V2 ch12.
2. **Myrion Resolution (MR)** — the *labelling operation/pipeline* that reconciles a proposition's opposing values into a truth-label verdict (N/A → MI → True/False/Indeterminate). This is the long-established meaning (MR truth labels, HMR, MyrionOperators.lean).
3. **Grand Myrion** — a *speculative cosmology* (conjectured large-scale conscious structure / computational substrate). STYLE_GUIDE flags it as speculative; keep it flagged.

**Why:** a code review flagged semantic overload as the one real risk when the balance-target sense was added. Conflating (1) and (2) muddies whether "Myrion" is a process or a state; importing (3)'s speculative baggage into (1)/(2) would violate the secular discipline.

**How to apply:** when writing about the balance-target sense, add the inline disambiguation (already present in EVD-1 §5, ch12, V2). The balance-target sense is apt precisely *because* it is the live-discourse analogue of what Myrion Resolution does to a proposition — note the kinship, but keep the labels distinct.
