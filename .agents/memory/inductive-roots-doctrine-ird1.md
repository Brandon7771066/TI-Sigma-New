# IRD-1 — deductive proof is not a separate certainty-class

**The thesis "'given axioms, x is 100% correct' is a cheat; deductive proofs aren't a class apart; everything is inductive at its roots" is LARGELY ALREADY CANONICAL — credit heavily, do not resell as new.** It consolidates three standing results:
- **AFD-1** (Axiomatic Faithfulness): only the *conditional* `axioms→conclusion` is exception-free; the detached *categorical* theorem is an **MI move**.
- **TLT** (Tralse Limit Theorem, URB #485): nothing is held at `τ=1`; proofs included (Mertens, retractions, Gödel-2, CH/AC independence).
- **POD-1** (Pragmatic Oracle): proof = the **highest-warrant region of one fallible continuum**, not an infallible box above credence. ch16 "two senses of proof" (deductive-QED vs Dewey warranted-assertibility) is the same point.

**The delta that IS new:** (a) the *disposition* charge — practitioners admit axioms yet behave/teach/rhetorically posture with unconditional certainty; (b) "inductive at its roots" as a positive foundational thesis.

## The four #69 rails (do NOT swallow the raw thesis whole)
- **P1 — the conditional is NOT MI, only the detachment is.** "Given A, x follows" is genuinely exception-free (that's deductive validity). The MI move is keeping the certainty while dropping the unproven antecedent A. Never say "the conditional is MI."
- **P2 — two senses of "class."** Inference-TYPE (deductive validity vs ampliative induction) is REAL — keep it; flattening it is self-refuting (the argument is itself reasoning). Certainty-IMMUNITY class (deductive results immune to fallibility) is the illusory one. "Inductive at its roots" = the *grounding* (axiom-selection + rule-acceptance + consistency/faithfulness confidence) is abductive; the internal step stays truth-preserving. Deduction does NOT collapse into induction.
- **P3 — soften "cover-up."** If-then-ism / formalism (Russell/Hilbert) is an honest, common position; where a conditional is asserted cleanly with reservation there is no cheat. The charge lands on disposition/pedagogy/rhetoric only — NOT a conscious conspiracy. (Same shape as the imaginary-number "embarrassment": formal work fine, teaching overshoots.)
- **P4 — self-application.** It cuts at the framework's OWN posits (tetrad, UOP, tralse-ground) exactly as hard: admitted speculations, pragmatic-best, never `τ=1`. A doctrine that exempts its author is the cheat it names.

## Citation rails
