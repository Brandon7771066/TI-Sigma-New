# Pragmatic Sense-Makeability at the I/MI frontier

**Guard name is `in-1`** (lowercase), with falsifier `in-1-F-leeway`. Do NOT call it "MR-IDC-1" — that is a *different* MR principle family (MR-IDC-1 {MI}⊊{Incoherent}, MR-IDC-2). Getting this wrong is an easy drift error.

## Credit the pre-existing (EVD-1 — do NOT re-claim as new)
- **NGK = Negative Gap Knowledge** and the **graded "fading line, not a wall" I↔MI boundary** are BOTH already canonical (`TI_SIGMA_FOR_EVERYONE_V2`). So "there is no precise difference between high-Indeterminate and MI" is *already* corpus, not a new claim.
- The **`in-1` I/MI leeway demarcation** (leeway remaining → Indeterminate; leeway annihilated → MI) is canonical (B107, MR-labels refinement #17). Kin: POD-1 pragmatic register, CAD value-floor.

## The one durable distinction (the delta)
**Tolerability ≠ sensicality.** NGK makes a nonsense concept *holdable in mind* (tolerable) but NOT *sensical*. Coherentist support-premises are a *separate* scaffold that can confer coherent, usable value (sensicality). Two scaffolds, two different jobs.

## Why the "no precise difference" claim doesn't abolish the label gate
Keep three questions separate so they don't collide:
- **Degree** (how incoherent): continuous, no sharp line.
- **Label** (I vs MI): the `in-1` leeway gate is the operational cutoff — PRESERVED, not abolished.
- **Use** (can it be held with value): graded, scaffolding-dependent, ORTHOGONAL to the label — even an MI-labelled item can be NGK-tolerable and, if genuinely scaffolded, coherently valued.

## Platonism "does-and-does-not-exist" is scaffolded, not bare MI
"Exists in POTENTIAL" is a load-bearing premise that *splits the predicate* (potential subsistence vs official/instantiated existence), so the both-and is not `P∧¬P` over one predicate ⇒ it escapes the identity-contradiction kind of MI. "Official" is a stipulated standing qualifier; NGK covers the non-instantiated residue.

## Anti-cheat (the thing that keeps it non-vacuous)
Coherentist scaffolding must be **load-bearing** — a genuine predicate-split or standing-conferring premise that *changes what the concept licenses in use* — not verbal relabeling of nonsense as sense. Otherwise it erodes the MI screen and NGK-tolerability is doing all the work. Coherentism is a contested epistemology (BonJour 1985 isolation objection); use it only as a theory of *usable sense*, never of content-truth.
