# EMD-1 — physicalism is a metaphysics, not a method (Ch12 credit-problem subsection)

The defensible core (STRONG): science's achievements belong to **empiricism** (a method: conjecture-and-test, ancient, metaphysically neutral), not to **physicalism** (a metaphysics: "matter+energy is all"). Method ≠ metaphysics; "science accomplished X" is evidence for empiricism, not physicalism. Sibling of PDU-1 (the gatekeeping cases are the metaphysical-side mirror of PDU-1's resist-then-relabel). DISSOLVING only — proves no rival, does not establish TOF-1.

**Why:** reframes the Ch12 steel-man (which conceded physicalism too much credit for science's success) without overclaiming — credit goes to the method, not the metaphysics.

## #69 calibration: BOTH over-dismissal AND over-credulity are #69 failures
**Load-bearing rule:** #69 brutal-honesty is NOT "default to the debunking/skeptical position." Defaulting to skepticism and reaching for the debunking findings is ITSELF a #69 cherry-picking violation — exactly as much as credulity is. The standard is "report the actual mixed state, with the strongest evidence on BOTH sides."
**Why:** on contested CAM/heterodox topics (acupuncture, EFT, regenerative-vs-industrial agriculture) the reflexive-skeptic framing FEELS like rigor but quietly cherry-picks; the better evidence here (Vickers 2018 sham-superiority, Clond 2016, Rodale FST) does not support flat dismissal.
**How to apply:** for any contested empirical case, pull the strongest PRO and strongest CON evidence before landing; verify named citations before using them; if challenged with specific literature, re-check the evidence rather than defending the skeptical framing by reflex. Land on the genuine center (here: "contested/live," not "debunked," not "proven").

- **Non-materialist founders** Newton/Faraday/Mendel/Maxwell — genuinely non-materialist. Safe.
- **Empiricism is ancient** — Ibn al-Haytham controlled optics ~1000CE, Babylonian astronomy, Eratosthenes. Safe.
- **Agriculture (REVISED toward regenerative):** do NOT say "Green Revolution was necessary to avert famine." Rodale's 40-yr Farming Systems Trial shows well-managed regenerative/organic MATCHING conventional yields and OUT-yielding in drought while building soil ⇒ chemical-input dependency was a path taken, not the only one. Remaining honest caveat = Seufert et al. 2012 *Nature* organic ~20-25% avg yield gap (narrows under best mgmt) ⇒ "regenerative out-produces at ANY scale" not settled. Land on: high-yield-and-soil-building are not opposed; input-dependency was avoidable.
- **Contemplative traditions** — real measured effects, sizes debated, metaphysics separable. Safe.
- **TCM** — artemisinin (Tu Youyou, Nobel 2015) is the honest concrete win; most of TCM unvalidated. Report both.
- **Germ theory IS a gatekeeping case (do not exclude it):** the "germs are physical" objection is anachronistic. Tie to PDU-1: "physical" is present-bound; to 1850s gatekeepers (miasma orthodoxy, Semmelweis broken) invisible microbes were as "unphysical" as action-at-a-distance was to Newton's critics. It fits the resist-then-relabel pattern.
- **Energy medicine — the better evidence does NOT support reflexive dismissal:**
  - Acupuncture — genuinely CONTESTED, not debunked. Largest sham-controlled IPD meta-analysis ~21k patients (Vickers et al. 2018) found it statistically SUPERIOR to sham for chronic pain; NICE-2021 + Cochrane-migraine + major hospitals endorse. Caveat the OTHER way: true-vs-sham margin is SMALL (specific-vs-placebo + meridian mechanism still debated). Do NOT assert flat "sham ≈ real."
  - **Animal-mechanism studies are the strongest anti-"all-placebo" card (placebo cannot operate in animals):** Goldman et al. 2010 (*Nat Neurosci*) adenosine/A1-receptor analgesia; Torres-Rosas et al. 2014 (*Nat Med*) dopamine-vagal anti-inflammatory electroacupuncture; Liu et al. 2021 (*Nature*, Qiufu Ma) mapped vagal–adrenal neuroanatomy. Human sham meta-analyses test point-SPECIFICITY, a different question — they cannot explain placebo-free animal results. CAVEAT (keep #69-balanced): these validate that the *stimulation* engages real neuro-immune pathways, NOT the meridian/qi theory; most are ELECTROacupuncture (modern hybrid, not classical needling); mouse-mechanism ≠ human clinical effect.
  - EFT/"tapping" — substantial RCT/meta-analytic literature (Clond 2016 anxiety, PTSD reviews) reporting benefit. The acupoint-COMPONENT question is CONTESTED (dismantling studies DISAGREE — not "inert"); plus much positive work is from a small circle of affiliated researchers (fair independence caution).
  - Land on NEITHER "proven" NOR "debunked": real, mixed, live.

**How to apply:** keep it dissolving, keep every example two-sided in BOTH directions (never let "deserves testing" drift to "validated," and never let "contested" drift to "debunked"). Real verified citations OK (Vickers 2018, NICE NG193, Seufert 2012, Clond 2016, Rodale FST) — verify before naming, no fabricated specifics. Falsifiers EMD-1-F1/F2/F3 OPEN.
