# EMD-1 — physicalism is a metaphysics, not a method (Ch12 credit-problem subsection)

The defensible core (STRONG): science's achievements belong to **empiricism** (a method: conjecture-and-test, ancient, metaphysically neutral), not to **physicalism** (a metaphysics: "matter+energy is all"). Method ≠ metaphysics; "science accomplished X" is evidence for empiricism, not physicalism. Sibling of PDU-1 (the gatekeeping cases are the metaphysical-side mirror of PDU-1's resist-then-relabel). DISSOLVING only — proves no rival, does not establish TOF-1.

**Why:** reframes the Ch12 steel-man (which conceded physicalism too much credit for science's success) without overclaiming — credit goes to the method, not the metaphysics.

## #69 calibration landmines (these are the load-bearing honesty points — do NOT round off)
- **Non-materialist founders** Newton/Faraday/Mendel/Maxwell — all genuinely non-materialist. Safe.
- **Empiricism is ancient** — Ibn al-Haytham controlled optics ~1000CE, Babylonian astronomy, Eratosthenes. Safe.
- **Pre-physicalist wins must be reported TWO-SIDED, never one-sided:**
  - Roman concrete / Pantheon — solid, uncontroversial.
  - Traditional agriculture — real merits BUT balance against the Green Revolution (famine-aversion for hundreds of millions). "Permaculture simply beats modern farming" = OVERCLAIM; permaculture is also modern (1970s), not ancient.
  - Contemplative traditions (yoga/Buddhism/Hinduism) — real measured effects, but effect sizes debated and metaphysics separable from practice.
  - TCM — artemisinin (Tu Youyou, Nobel 2015) is the honest concrete win; MOST of TCM is unvalidated. Report both.
- **"Held empiricism back" gatekeeping cases:** Newtonian gravity ("occult action-at-a-distance"), quantum entanglement ("spooky"), continental drift, meteorites — all real. **GERM THEORY IS NOT A CLEAN "non-physical" EXAMPLE** (germs ARE physical; resistance was paradigm-bound) — explicitly excluded; it is a tempting-but-wrong example to reach for.
- **Energy medicine (highest-risk):** make ONLY the methodological point (judge empirically, don't dismiss-by-metaphysics). Evidence is mixed-to-weak, NOT confirmed:
  - Acupuncture — real symptom relief for some pain/nausea BUT **sham ≈ real** in good trials ⇒ meridian mechanism unsupported.
  - EFT/"tapping" — acupoint component looks **inert** in dismantling studies (works via bundled exposure/breathing).
  - "being seriously confirmed" is an OVERSTATEMENT to watch for; the honest line is "deserves fair testing, verdict partial/mechanism-poor."

**How to apply:** if extending this critique, keep it dissolving, keep every traditional/energy example two-sided, and never let "deserves testing" drift into "validated." Falsifiers EMD-1-F1/F2/F3 OPEN. Anchor: `papers/PASS_77_B128_EMPIRICISM_METAPHYSICS_DEMARCATION_EMD_1_2026-06-23.md`.
