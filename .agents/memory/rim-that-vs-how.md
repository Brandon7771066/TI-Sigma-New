# RIM — Related-Instated Mechanism

**Rule.** RIM = an abstract effect instantiated **by relation alone**, mechanism-agnostic. It is the framework's local name for **multiple realizability (Putnam/Fodor) + functionalism** — so the only defensible novelty is the *name* + the **THAT-vs-HOW-applied-to-dismissals** demarcation, NOT a new discovery. Candidate, not ratified; does NOT change the canonical count (80).

**Why:** After two empirical LCC negatives (B164 ds007471, B165 Depresjon) and B167's device-independence negative, the honest move is not "the brain is a quantum computer" (asserted nowhere) but "dismissing one HOW ≠ explaining away the THAT." RIM carries that weight without overclaiming.

**How to apply:**
- A dismissal (Tegmark 2000 decoherence; B167 DI Bell bounds — locality/decoherence/monogamy) refutes **one HOW**. It leaves the **THAT** (quantum-*formal* structure in cognition) standing. Say exactly that.
- Load-bearing evidence for the THAT = **quantum cognition** (substrate-agnostic by its own authors): Wang et al. 2014 PNAS (question-order, a-priori prediction), Pothos & Busemeyer 2013 BBS, Busemeyer & Bruza 2012. These practitioners do NOT claim a quantum brain — that IS the RIM point.
- Orch-OR recent support (Khan/Wiest 2024 eNeuro epothilone-delays-anesthesia; Babcock 2024 tryptophan superradiance) = **contested / LIVE, NOT vindication**. Weight nonzero per EVD-1, flagged contested.
- Classical realization of quantum-*like* contextuality (contextuality-without-nonlocality, Nat Comms 2016) ⇒ the HOW is genuinely open; it REMOVES decoherence as a defeater, does NOT support an exotic claim.
- **Anti-cheat (RIM-F2):** RIM lowers a CONCEPTUAL bar only. It lowers NO empirical bar. Never use RIM to admit a phenomenon as real without independent data. The LCC is still empirically NEGATIVE in biology; RIM does not rescue it.

**Illustration (canonical, user's):** a strictly-binary transistor array *actively runs* many-valued/tralse logic — instantiated by the relations among parts, absent in any transistor in isolation. "Components are only binary" never settles what the organized whole is doing.

**Quantum test provenance (durable caveat):** any CHSH/entanglement "demo" in this corpus so far ran on the **qiskit Aer SIMULATOR, not IBM hardware** — the IBMQ account returns "No matching instances" on every channel (persistent block). Never cite these as hardware results; the demo shows the quantum-formal coupling is *instantiable* (THAT), nothing about brains. √2−1 / cos²(π/8) constants = flagged resonances, never derivations.
