## Real citations (verified characterizations)
- **Jain & Mills 2010**, "Biofield therapies: helpful or full of hype? A best evidence synthesis," *Int J Behav Med* 17(1):1–16. Lead author **Shamini Jain** later founded the **Consciousness and Healing Initiative (CHI)**. Verdict: *strong* evidence for pain-intensity reduction, *moderate* for anxiety (hospitalized) + cancer-related fatigue, *equivocal* elsewhere.
- **Astin, Harkness & Ernst 2000**, "The efficacy of 'distant healing'…," *Ann Intern Med* 132(11):903–910. Majority of trials positive, evidence not decisive (Ernst later more skeptical).
- **So, Jiang & Qin 2008**, "Touch therapies for pain relief in adults," Cochrane. Small, low-certainty benefit.
- **Bengston & Krinsley 2000**, "The effect of the 'laying on of hands' on transplanted breast cancer in mice," *J Sci Exploration* 14(3). Full remission of a uniformly-fatal transplanted mammary cancer; treated 44/48=91.7%, on-site ctrl 33/41=80.5%, off-site ctrl 0/8=0%; "resonant bonding" split. ## Honesty rails (#69 both ways — the whole point)
**Why:** energy healing is the case most often either trumpeted or waved away; EMD-1/PDU-1 canon = "contested/live," NOT debunked, NOT proven.
- **CREDIT (undercredit banned):** Bengston's animal design is a REAL virtue — mice have no expectations ⇒ removes the placebo/expectancy confound that sinks human trials. Biofield syntheses DO show a consistent signal on self-reported pain/anxiety.
- **DISCOUNT (overclaim banned):** biofield work = **systematic reviews / best-evidence syntheses, NOT high-powered meta-analyses** (correct the word "meta-analysis" in-text); trials small/poorly-blinded/low-quality; publication bias + expectancy live; **CHI lead author is a proponent** (independence caution, same as EFT); no rigorous support for **objective** human disease outcomes. Bengston = **fringe venue (JSE), not independently replicated by mainstream oncology** (replications trace to him + associates); the **"resonant bonding" anomaly is equally an ordinary contamination/confound**; extraordinary no-mechanism claim RAISES the bar ⇒ verdict **provisional/open, Indeterminate-leaning**, neither vindicated nor debunked.
