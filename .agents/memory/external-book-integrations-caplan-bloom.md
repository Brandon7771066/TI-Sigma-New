Two popular-but-contested books are now woven into the corpus book. Both are easy to overclaim — record the exact defensible line.

## Caplan, *The Case Against Education* (2018) — Epistemology / Authority Axis
- **Framework hook:** a credential ≈ a pure *other-authority* grant ⇒ Caplan is empirical teeth for the Authority Axis claim that granted authority ⊥ actual knowledge. (Also JBK-1 justified-belief / EVD-1 authority-independence.)
- **HAZARD — the signaling share is HIS estimate (~80%), NOT a measured constant.** Human-capital economists put it much lower; real replicated skill gains exist (literacy/numeracy; compulsory-schooling natural experiments). Never write "school teaches nothing" — Caplan himself exempts literacy, numeracy, vocational training.
- **Defensible line:** *a substantial, routinely-ignored fraction of the credential premium is signal ⇒ a credential is weak evidence of knowledge.*
- **Scope:** a claim about **private economic return** only — silent on civic/cultural/intrinsic goods.
- Clean supporting cite: sheepskin effect = Hungerford & Solon 1987 (predates Caplan).

## Bloom, *Against Empathy: The Case for Rational Compassion* (2016) — Ethics
- **HAZARD — target is EMOTIONAL empathy used as a *decision procedure*, NOT compassion/caring.** Bloom KEEPS rational compassion. Never cite as "feelings don't matter."
- **Contested:** de Waal / Decety defend empathy kept in its lane; present as strong-but-debated, not settled. The sharp reading is Bloom's own: against empathy *as a moral guide*.
- Empathy's defects (real, cited): spotlight / identifiable-victim effect; Slovic *psychic numbing* (concern drops as N rises); parochial; exploitable.
- **TI Sigma synthesis (the user's framing):** reason aggregates (Bloom + utilitarian wide count); **affect still matters via "consciousness as a razor"** (ch13 — felt experience is the *ground*/precondition for anything mattering, even though it's a poor *aggregator*); **valence** = key feature of **embodied GILE**, central because affective neuroscience reads it as a **common currency** (Cabanac 1971/1992; Levy & Glimcher 2012, neural common currency of value in OFC). But the good stays **four-dimensional GILE**, NOT a hedonic scalar — "maximize happiness/minimize suffering" is overly simplistic; SIS-1 + UOP interior optimum block the "maximize valence" runaway.
- **HAZARD — "common currency" is a functional research lens, not a proven single scalar in the head.** Lean on it as a lens; the framework explicitly rejects collapsing ethics to one scalar (that's why GILE has four axes).
