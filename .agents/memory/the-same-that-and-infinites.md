# B179 — "THE SAME THAT", as-a-whole verdict, and infinites

No new principle; count stays **80**. Formal MI def `τ(P)∧¬τ(P)` and pipeline `N/A → MI → T/F/I` unchanged. These refine the *object-language reading* and *generation mechanism*, not the labels.

## 1. "THE SAME THAT" — object-specificity (NEW)
The **THAT** in the tralse/N/A/MI definitions is ONE referent, always **object-specific** (this claim/number/situation, no other):
- tralse = "the THAT which represents a deficit of reality"
- N/A = "the THAT which is tralse **and may (overall) not be** tralse"
- MI = "the THAT which **is and is not** tralse (overall)"

**Why it matters:** object-specificity IS what *generates* the MI (and certain-N/A) **identity conflict** — two verdicts colliding on one pinned referent. If "that" is allowed to **drift** to a *different* object between the two halves, there is NO conflict (just a change of subject). Use this to tell a real self-collision from a subject-swap.

## 2. "as-a-whole" not-tralse verdict — two tralsities, differing THATs (ruled earlier; APPLIED in book at B179)
When 2+ indeterminate statements are taken **together** and self-defeat (excessive nonsensical incoherence), the "**not tralse**" verdict attaches to the **statement AS A WHOLE**, never to either half. Always: **two genuine tralsities first**, then the whole earns "not tralse." Tell-tale: the **THAT is NOT the same** across the two self-referring propositions. This is *how* an MI verdict is generated (why MI says "…overall").

## 3. Infinites — REVISION with caveat (NEW)
The blunt "absolute infinites do not exist" (URB #433 §A) is now too strong given Platonism-as-tralse (B159 / TSG-1 / EPE-1):
- The **potential** of any infinite is **truly real** (notions it entails exist in potential; Linnebo mathematical potentialism = real cite/support, not a derivation).
- Only **actualized** aspects **exist** (existence-weight ≈ 0 until instantiated).
- **Actual/completed** infinites remain **MI** (the THAT that is-and-is-not tralse overall) — NOT a data-gap N/A.
- Keeps URB #433 §B (potential infinity retained) and §C (the *future* = the one honestly-argued actual infinity, lived forward never completed).
- Slogan: "**the potential of an infinite is real; its completion is MI.**"
