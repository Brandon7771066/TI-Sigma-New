# The "physical is undefined" critique (candidate PDU-1)

The corpus argues physicalism is an *unstable* thesis: it can't fix the meaning of
"physical," defends it by expansion, and substitutes "epiphenomenon" for an
explanation of consciousness/abstracta. Lives in `book/ch12_against_physicalism.md`
(subsection "A deeper problem: 'physical' has no fixed meaning") + V2 mirror; anchor
paper `papers/PASS_77_B127_PHYSICAL_DEFINITIONAL_INSTABILITY_PDU_1_*.md`.

**Real outside backing — use these, do NOT fabricate page cites:**
- **Hempel's Dilemma** (Carl Hempel; Crane & Mellor 1990; SEP *Physicalism*) is the
  legitimate, named home of the "define-by-current-physics → false / define-by-future-
  physics → empty" argument. This is the single most important citation; it makes the
  argument respectable rather than framework-only.
- **Weak vs strong emergence** + the **hard problem** (David Chalmers) back the
  "consciousness can't be built from matter" step.
- **Epiphenomenalism** + the **problem of mental causation** are the real names for the
  "consciousness as causally-idle by-product" move PDU-1 attacks.

**#69 guardrails (load-bearing):**
- The chakras/intuition examples are ILLUSTRATIVE of the *definitional* point only —
  never assert they are real; their status is exactly what's disputed.
- The "consciousness cannot strongly emerge" step is the MOST CONTESTED link — flag as
  a live philosophical position, not a settled fact.
- PDU-1 is DISSOLVING, not constructive: it lowers physicalism's standing; it does NOT
  prove TOF-1 (TI Sigma's positive monism) — that is argued separately, only *consistent*.
- The relabel-asymmetry cuts both ways; that double-edge IS the point (= Hempel Horn 2 /
  unfalsifiable-by-redefinition), so present it as content-draining, not as a cheap gotcha.

**Why:** without these guards the critique reads as a fallacious appeal to future
discovery or as smuggling in unproven spiritual claims; with them it is a clean,
literature-backed dissolution of physicalism's default status.
