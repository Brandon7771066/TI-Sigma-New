2. **Assess Intuition (i):**

   - "Aha moments" present?

3. **Assess Love (l):**
   - Genuine care for others?
   - Sacrifice without expectation?
   - Emotional resonance?

4. **Assess Environment (e):**
   - Couples with environmental rhythms?
   - Aware of ecological systems?
   - Coherent relationship with nature?

5. **Calculate:**
   ```
   I_true = 0.25(g + i + l + e)
   ```

6. **Interpret:**
   - < 20%: Not intelligent
