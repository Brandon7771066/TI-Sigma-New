## 2. SO(10) and Its 16-Spinor

SO(10) is the rotation group on 10-dimensional Euclidean space. Its lowest-dimensional **spinor representation** is **16-complex-dimensional** = 32-real-dimensional. This 16-spinor is special for GUT phenomenology because it accommodates **all 15 Standard Model fermions of one generation plus a right-handed neutrino** in a single irreducible representation:

| 16-spinor slot | SM fermion content (one generation) |
|---|---|
| 1  | ν_R (right-handed neutrino, predicted) |
| 1  | e_L (left-handed electron) |
| 2  | ν_L , e_L doublet (lepton doublet) |
| 3  | u_L , d_L color triplet |
| 3  | u_R color triplet (right-handed) |
| 3  | d_R color triplet (right-handed) |
| 3  | u_L , d_L color triplet (other chirality) |

(Standard SO(10) decomposition; counts to 16.)

The 16-spinor is **complex** and decomposes under SU(5) as **16 = 10 ⊕ 5̄ ⊕ 1**, the famous SU(5) generation structure plus a singlet (the right-handed neutrino).
