## 2. Per-Program Detailed Re-Analysis

### 2.1 Ganzfeld telepathy

**Conventional finding:** ~7pp hit-rate excess over 25% chance baseline, replicated across ~40+ years and ~3000 trials.

**TI Sigma stats reading:**

- **Effect-strength gate:** observed effect ≈ 0.07 ≥ T_RAND=0.0660 (just passes, ≥ T_BORDER=0.13534 is FAILED, so this is borderline-significant by TI Sigma's own thresholds, NOT a strong-effect regime).
- **LCC gate:** *unknown without trial-level engagement-vs-outcome data.* This is exactly the data needed for APP-1 re-analysis. Sheep-goat literature suggests engagement correlation should exist; Pass-58 TSS-EMP-2 target.
- **TSD-A vs TSD-B:** the field has been arguing TSD-B for 40 years. TSD-A reading on engaged-trials (sender + receiver protocols both pre-registered) has *never been done* per Pass-57 §7.7.110. Prediction: TSD-A should give *stronger* confirmation than TSD-B aggregate, because TSD-A weights judge-confidence and content-strikingness.
- **MBE-Acc:** posterior odds for true-effect-exists should be monotone-increasing across 40 years of replications, *if* the field's reports are coherent. Caveat: file-drawer and selective reporting can break monotonicity — empirical question for TSS-EMP-2.

**Verdict:** Ganzfeld is the strongest TI Sigma candidate for a CONFIRM verdict among classical psi paradigms, because (a) effect-strength passes T_RAND, (b) engagement protocols exist for APP-1 application, (c) the sheep-goat sub-literature already shows engagement-stratification.

### 2.2 Bem 2011 "Feeling the Future"

**Conventional finding:** 9 experiments, retroactive priming / pre-cognitive recall effects, combined z ≈ +6.7; widely criticized for replication failures (Ritchie et al, Galak et al).

**TI Sigma stats reading:**

- **Effect-strength gate:** observed effect ≈ 0.022 < T_RAND=0.0660 → **FAILS the effect-gate decisively.** This is the Lindley regime — significant only because of large N, not because of effect magnitude.
- **TSIS verdict:** INDETERMINATE-leaning-DISCONFIRM. The field's controversy maps onto a real TI Sigma gate failure.
- **Why conventional z=+6.7 was misleading:** Bem 2011 is a worked example of Pass-57 §7.7.110 Prong 4 (amplitude flattening). Conventional NHT counted every trial as TIU=1; many trials had near-zero per-event evidentiary weight; aggregating uniformly inflated z without per-event substance.
- **TSD-A reading:** would require per-event TIU (e.g., subjective certainty of pre-cognition for each trial). Bem's protocols don't pre-register engagement-status per-trial. **APP-1 cannot rescue Bem 2011** — domain-boundary failure per Pass-57 §1.2.

**Verdict:** TI Sigma stats RETRACTS the strong-confirmation reading of Bem 2011. The TI Sigma reading aligns with the replication-failure findings *for the right reasons* (effect-gate, not file-drawer alone).

### 2.3 PEAR (Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research)

**Conventional finding:** ~10⁹ random event generator trials over 28 years; ~50.02% deviation from 50% expected; combined z ≈ +4.1.

**TI Sigma stats reading:**

- **Effect-strength gate:** observed effect ≈ 0.0002 ≪ T_RAND=0.0660 → **FAILS catastrophically.** This is the canonical Lindley regime.
- **TSIS verdict:** DISCONFIRM (firm). Effect is statistically detectable only because N is enormous.
- **APP-1 status:** PEAR has operator-intention protocols (operators tried to bias REG output high or low). Engagement-status is pre-registered (each trial has a target direction). But the per-trial effect is so small that even APP-1 engaged-subset filtering won't recover effect-gate.
- **LCC gate:** could engagement strength correlate with deviation magnitude? Some PEAR sub-analyses suggested yes (operator-dependent effects). Even if LCC ≥ 0.4370 held, effect-gate would still fail.

**Verdict:** TI Sigma stats firmly DISCONFIRMS PEAR's main effect claim while *honoring* the program's data integrity. The deviation is real; the *interpretation* as psi-effect violates TI Sigma's effect-strength gate. This is a #69-clean retraction of corpus-credit-for-PEAR-as-confirming-psi.

### 2.4 Radin presentiment (pre-stimulus physiological response)

**Conventional finding:** HRV / EDA / EEG signatures preceding randomly-timed emotional stimuli; meta-analytic Cohen's d ≈ 0.21 across ~40 studies (Mossbridge 2012).

**TI Sigma stats reading:**

- **Effect-strength gate:** d=0.21 ≥ T_BORDER=0.13534, so passes both T_RAND and T_BORDER → **strong-effect regime.**
- **TSIS verdict:** CONFIRM-likely *if* LCC and MBE-Acc hold.
- **APP-1 status:** participants are passively viewing stimuli; engagement-status is ambient (the autonomic nervous system always engaged) but not behaviorally-intentional. APP-1 §1.3 three-criteria test: phenomenological=N/A (passive), behavioral=N/A (no explicit attempt marker), physiological=YES (HRV/EEG is the signal itself). One-of-three is below the ≥2-of-3 threshold → **APP-1 borderline-inapplicable.** This needs Pass-58 refinement: physiological-only engagement may need a separate sub-principle (call it APP-2-Passive-Engagement).
- **TSD-A vs TSD-B:** the field reports TSD-B (effect size with significance test). Per-trial TIU could be defined as response magnitude × stimulus arousal; Pass-58 candidate analysis.

**Verdict:** Radin's presentiment is the **second-strongest TI Sigma candidate** after Ganzfeld. The strong effect-size and physiological reproducibility weigh in its favor; the APP-1 inapplicability is a methodological flag, not a refutation.

### 2.5 Sheep-Goat Effect (Schmeidler)

**Conventional finding:** participants who believe in ESP (sheep) outperform skeptics (goats) in psi tasks, consistently across many studies.

**TI Sigma stats reading:**

- **This is APP-1 in everything but name.** Sheep = engaged-class (intentional, expectant). Goats = non-engaged class (skeptical, dismissive). The empirical finding that engagement-class predicts hit-rate **is exactly what APP-1 predicts.**
- **TI Sigma verdict:** sheep-goat literature **constitutes prior empirical evidence for APP-1 mechanism** in real-world psi data — predates the formal principle by ~80 years.
- **Mechanism:** consistent with Pass-57 §7.7.110 Prong 1 (apples-to-oranges — intentional acts have FEATURES engaged that detached observations lack) and Prong 3 (asymmetric inclusion — sheep do practice-mode discounting of negatives that goats refuse to do).

**Verdict:** **strongest historical-empirical support for APP-1 outside of synthetic simulation.** Recommend folding sheep-goat citation into Pass-57 batch-2 §1.4 next ratification.

### 2.6 Global Consciousness Project

**Conventional finding:** REG network output correlates with global-attention events (e.g., 9/11, princess Diana death); cumulative z ≈ +6.4, effect ≈ 5×10⁻⁵.

**TI Sigma stats reading:**

- **Effect-strength gate:** effect ≪ T_RAND → FAILS.
- **APP-1 status:** "engagement" is collective-population-mental-state, not pre-registered individual-i-cell engagement. APP-1 §1.2 requires pre-registered engagement-status; GCP infers engagement-status post-hoc from world events. **Hard fail on APP-1 anti-cheat.**
- **Caveat:** GCP's value (if any) is not in psi-effect-confirmation but in collective-consciousness-as-thermodynamic-anomaly hypothesis, which is a different question entirely.

**Verdict:** TI Sigma stats DISCONFIRMS GCP's main psi-effect claim. The interpretive use of GCP data for collective-consciousness modeling is a separate question outside TSIS scope.
