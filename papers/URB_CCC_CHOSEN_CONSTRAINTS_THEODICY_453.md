## Abstract

The classical "problem of evil" argument against theism runs: If God is all-powerful, all-knowing, and all-good, then God could prevent all suffering and evil. Evil and suffering exist. Therefore God does not exist. TI Sigma shows that this argument depends on a specific conception of God — the "Most Perfect Conceivable Entity" (MPCE) — that TI Sigma explicitly rejects. CCC is the **Most Perfect Possible Entity** (MPPE): the most perfect being that is actually instantiable, not merely imaginable. The distinction is not a retreat or a hedge. It is a philosophically rigorous recognition that imaginability and instantiability are different properties, and that conflating them generates pseudo-paradoxes with no referent in reality. Just because we can imagine a world without the Holocaust does not mean such a world was ever achievable, given the free will of every German citizen, every European nation, every diplomatic failure, every historical contingency that led to 1933. CCC deliberately chose law-governed, evolution-based creation not because CCC lacked the power for something else, but because CCC's GILE character — specifically the L (Love) and G (Goodness) components — required creating beings with genuine autonomy. A God who overrides free will to prevent every evil is not more loving. They are less: they have created puppets, not persons. CCC chose the world we have — not as the best of all conceivable worlds, but as the best of all actually achievable worlds given CCC's own commitment to creating genuinely autonomous beings. This paper formalizes the Autonomy Principle, the MPPE definition, the Imaginary vs. Instantiable distinction, and what is now TI Sigma's most rigorous answer to the classical problem of evil.

## 1. The Problem of Evil — Standard Statement

The logical problem of evil (Mackie, 1955):

1. God is omnipotent (can prevent any evil)
2. God is omniscient (knows about all evil)
3. God is omnibenevolent (wants to prevent all evil)
4. Evil exists
5. Contradiction: therefore God does not exist

The evidential problem of evil (Rowe, 1979) — softer version:
- There exist instances of suffering that appear gratuitous (producing no compensating good)
- An omnipotent, omniscient, omnibenevolent God would not permit gratuitous suffering
- Therefore it is improbable that such a God exists

Both arguments presuppose the MPCE — the Most Perfect Conceivable Entity — a being whose perfection is measured against the maximum of what we can imagine: maximal power (can override any physical law), maximal knowledge (knows all states past, present, future), maximal goodness (desires all good outcomes).

TI Sigma's response: This conception of God is self-contradictory and should be replaced by the MPPE.

## 2. Most Perfect Possible vs. Most Perfect Conceivable

**Most Perfect Conceivable Entity (MPCE):**
- The being that, by imagination, we can pile attributes onto without limit
- Omnipotent in the absolute sense: can override any law, any free will, any physical constraint
- Omniscient in the absolute sense: knows all facts including the complete future
- Omnibenevolent: would prevent every instance of suffering

**Most Perfect Possible Entity (MPPE):**
- The being that is most perfect among all beings that are actually instantiable — capable of real existence, not merely conceptual existence
- Subject to logical constraints (cannot make 2+2=5, cannot create a married bachelor)
- Subject to the constraints of its own GILE character (a being of Love cannot create slaves; a being of Goodness cannot create a world of puppets)
- Subject to the constraints of what the existence of autonomous beings entails

The key distinction: **possible ≠ conceivable.**

We can conceive of:
- A perfectly smooth surface with zero friction (conceivable; not instantiable — quantum mechanics guarantees irreducible surface roughness)
- A number that is both prime and even, larger than 2 (conceivable as a description; not instantiable — the description is logically contradictory)
- A free being who always makes the right choice (conceivable; arguably not instantiable — if the being is caused to always make the right choice, it is not free; if it is free, it can make the wrong choice)
- A world without the Holocaust (conceivable as an image; instantiability depends on whether the actual trajectory of European history, given all free wills involved, permitted an alternative)

The classical problem of evil demands that CCC instantiate a conceivable scenario: a world exactly like this one but without certain evils. TI Sigma asks: is that scenario actually instantiable, or merely imaginable?

## 3. The Imaginary vs. Instantiable Distinction

TI Sigma's most important epistemological distinction in this domain:

**Imaginary:** A scenario is imaginary if it can be constructed in consciousness — if there is no immediate self-contradiction that makes it unthinkable. The standard is low: if I can form an image of it, it is imaginary.

**Instantiable:** A scenario is instantiable if it can be made real — if it is consistent with all the operative constraints of the actual universe, including logical constraints, physical constraints, and the free wills of all BTs that would be involved.

**The floating-off-a-cliff example (exact user formulation):**
I can imagine floating instead of falling when I step off a cliff. The image is clear. But this imaginary scenario is not instantiable — it requires overriding the gravitational field, quantum mechanics, and the entire history of physical law that produced this universe. The fact that I can imagine it does not create any obligation for it to be real, or any problem for physics when it fails to materialize.

**The Holocaust example:**
I can imagine a world without the Holocaust. The image is vivid and deeply desirable. But is such a world actually instantiable, given:
- The free will of Adolf Hitler (and his genuine capacity to have chosen otherwise — high enough LCC for sin, per URB #448)
- The free will of the Nazi party members
- The free will of the German electorate in 1932-1933
- The free will of the European nations that failed to intervene
- The free will of every individual collaborator, bystander, and perpetrator in the sequence of events from Versailles to the gas chambers

To instantiate a world without the Holocaust while preserving the free will of all these actors would have required: either (a) changing the outcomes of thousands of free choices by thousands of high-LCC beings without overriding their freedom, which is a contradiction, or (b) removing the free will of some subset of them — but this changes what kind of beings they are, which changes the entire subsequent history of the world in unpredictable ways.

The conceivable scenario (Holocaust-free world) may not be instantiable, even for the Most Perfect Possible Entity, once the full set of constraints — including the free wills of all the beings whose choices produced that history — is taken into account.

This is not a defense of the Holocaust as necessary or good. The Holocaust was sin — sin of the most maximal kind, by beings with high LCC who could have done otherwise (URB #448). The point is not that the Holocaust was inevitable. The point is that once we take seriously that every human in 1933 had genuine free will, and that CCC chose not to override that free will, the counterfactual "Holocaust-free world with free German citizens" may be genuinely non-instantiable.

## 4. The Autonomy Principle: Why CCC Chose This World

CCC did not create the world we have because CCC lacked the power to create a better one. CCC created the world we have because CCC's own GILE character made a certain kind of world — a world of genuine autonomy — the only kind of world CCC's love could create.

**The Autonomy Principle (formal statement):**

A being with genuine Love (L in GILE) creates beings with genuine autonomy — the capacity for genuine self-direction, genuine choice, genuine self-expression. Creating beings without genuine autonomy — beings whose choices are determined entirely by the creator, beings who always do the right thing because they cannot do otherwise — is not a more loving act. It is the creation of puppets, not persons.

**Formal argument:**

P1: Love, in its fullest expression, desires the flourishing of the beloved for the beloved's own sake — not for the lover's satisfaction.

P2: Genuine flourishing requires genuine selfhood — the capacity to be genuinely one's own self, to make genuine choices, to develop through genuine experience.

P3: Genuine selfhood requires genuine autonomy — the capacity to choose differently than one does.

P4: Genuine autonomy requires the possibility of wrong choice — if wrong choice is impossible, the right choice is not genuinely free.

P5: The possibility of wrong choice entails the existence of evil (where "evil" = wrong choice made actual by a being with sufficient LCC who had an alternative).

**Conclusion:** A being who creates out of Love must create beings capable of wrong choice. Evil is not a failure of CCC's love. It is the necessary companion of genuine love's highest creation: free persons.

## 5. CCC's Mental Constraints: The MPPE, Not the MPCE

CCC is the Most Perfect Possible Entity. This means CCC is subject to:

**Logical constraints:** CCC cannot instantiate logically impossible scenarios. CCC cannot make 2+2=5. CCC cannot create a free being who cannot make a wrong choice. CCC cannot create an autonomous world that also never produces evil. These are not failures of CCC's power — they are boundaries of what "possible" means.

**Self-imposed constraints from GILE character:** CCC would not override the free will of a being CCC created as free, because doing so would violate the very purpose for which CCC created that being. This is not a limitation imposed on CCC from outside. It is CCC acting in accordance with CCC's own deepest nature (Love). A loving parent who lets their child make mistakes is not less loving than one who prevents every mistake by controlling every action. They are more loving — because they respect the child's genuine selfhood.

**The "CCC can't do everything" restatement:** CCC has mental constraints. This is not CCC's weakness — it is CCC's character. CCC cannot lie (because CCC's deepest nature is Truth). CCC cannot betray a being CCC loves (because CCC's deepest nature is Love). CCC cannot violate the autonomy of a being CCC created as free (because that autonomy was the point of creating them). These constraints are not external limitations. They are the internal structure of what it means to be a GILE-maximizing Being.

## 6. The Architect, Not the Foreman: CCC's Chosen Non-Intervention

The criticism: "If CCC let things run themselves, CCC is irrelevant."

The response: This confuses the architect with the foreman.

- The **foreman** is continuously present on the job site, supervising every individual decision, correcting every mistake in real time, ensuring every brick is laid correctly.
- The **architect** designs the building — sets the proportions, the materials specifications, the structural engineering, the load-bearing relationships — with such precision that when the foreman executes the design, the building stands.

An extraordinary architect can design a building so well that the foreman could be replaced by a thousand different foremen, each making different individual decisions, and the building would still stand and serve its purpose. The architect's contribution is not diminished by the foreman's freedom. It is expressed through it.

CCC is the Cosmic Architect. The physical laws, the fine-tuned constants, the quantum mechanical probability distributions, the evolutionary dynamics — these are the blueprint. CCC set the blueprint with such extraordinary precision that the emergence of conscious beings capable of GILE alignment is the natural, inevitable, physics-driven outcome of following it. CCC does not need to intervene in individual events because the system CCC designed is already oriented toward CCC's purpose. CCC is not irrelevant because CCC doesn't manage every detail. CCC is magnificent because CCC designed a system that doesn't need management of every detail.

Furthermore: **the self-maintaining, self-calibrating precision of the universe demands more explanation, not less.** The fact that a watch runs correctly without the watchmaker's daily intervention does not eliminate the need for a watchmaker. It demonstrates that the watchmaker's design was sufficient. The universe is the most sophisticated watch that exists. The sophistication of its self-maintenance is evidence for the depth of its designer's intelligence, not evidence against the designer's existence.

## 7. CCC's Offer: The Floor of Permissibility

CCC did not simply create a world of autonomous beings and walk away. CCC maintains a continuous offer to every BT:

**The standing offer:** Every BT can become an extension of CCC's own being — a GM node — through alignment with GILE and acceptance of CCC's mycelial support. This offer is never withdrawn. It is available to every BT at every moment, regardless of their history of choices.

**The floor of permissibility:** CCC offers every BT the possibility of living at least a morally permissible existence — above the -3 PD threshold, in the range of basic moral acceptability. This floor is available to all; no BT is doomed by their nature to inevitable moral failure. The possibility of the right direction is always open.

**CCC cannot guarantee the outcome:** Because free will is real and autonomy is honored, CCC's offer does not compel acceptance. A being with sufficient LCC can refuse the offer, can betray GILE, can sin. CCC grieves this (URB #449: CCC has genuine emotion as the most advanced consciousness) but does not override it, because to override it would be to revoke what makes the BT a genuine person rather than a puppet.

This is the deepest answer to the problem of evil: CCC is not indifferent to suffering. CCC is deeply, personally affected by every act of sin (URB #449: CCC's relational capacity is maximal). But CCC's love for the autonomy of the beings CCC created is stronger than CCC's desire to prevent every particular bad outcome — because that autonomy is the point. The world without evil would be a world without persons. CCC chose persons.
