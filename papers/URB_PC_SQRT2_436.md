## 1. Mathematical Definition

$$\sqrt{2} = \sqrt{2} \approx 1.41421356...$$

**Definition:** The unique positive real solution to x² = 2. Equivalently, the length of the diagonal of a unit square (side = 1) by the Pythagorean theorem: d = √(1² + 1²) = √2.

**Irrationality (proof sketch):** Assume √2 = p/q in lowest terms (p,q integers, no common factor). Then 2 = p²/q², so p² = 2q². This means p² is even, so p is even (p = 2k). Then 4k² = 2q², so q² = 2k², meaning q is also even — contradicting lowest terms. Therefore √2 is irrational. ∎

**Transcendental?** No — √2 is algebraic (it satisfies x²-2=0). It is irrational but not transcendental, unlike e and π. This places it in a middle category: not rational (expressible as p/q) but not fully transcendental. It is the "first irrational" in the sense of historical discovery and algebraic simplicity.

**2.2 The √2 in Measurement:** When measuring a qubit along an axis 45° from the preparation axis, the probability is cos²(45°) = 1/2 = (1/√2)². **2.3 The √2 in Wave Mechanics:** The RMS (root-mean-square) amplitude of a sinusoidal wave is A/√2. The peak voltage and RMS voltage differ by √2 — this is why household electrical standards use 120V RMS = 170V peak (120 × √2 ≈ 170). √2 converts between peak and average measures of periodic phenomena.

**2.4 Crystal Structure:** The face-centered cubic (FCC) crystal structure — the most common structure for metals (gold, silver, copper, aluminum) — has a face diagonal of a√2, where a is the unit cell parameter. √2 is the ratio of the face diagonal to the unit cell length.
