## 2. New term: PROPOSITION TYPE (PT)

**Definition.** A *Proposition Type* is a class of propositions individuated by **the modality that distinctively conveys its content's qualia.** Some truths carry an irreducible "what-it's-like" presentation that is native to one sense and only thinly transmissible through others.

Working taxonomy (extensible):
| Proposition Type | distinctive carrier | example |
|---|---|---|
| **Amodal / claircognitive PT** | knowing (claircognizance) | logical entailment, conceptual identity, "I exist" |
| **Visual-geometric PT** | sight (clairvoyance) | a geometric truth grasped *as spatial form*; a proof "seen" |
| **Visual-aesthetic PT** | sight | a painting's beauty; composition/colour relations |
| **Auditory-musical PT** | hearing (clairaudience) | a musical truth; *beautiful music = high GILE-E propositions* (Brandon) |
| **Somatic-affective PT** | felt valence (clairsentience) | a felt rightness/wrongness (VFP-1 valence-as-functional) |

**The bandwidth claim (Brandon):** *mere knowingness has minimal bandwidth on the sense-specific qualia* of the non-amodal PTs. You can *know that* a painting is beautiful or *know that* a chord resolves, but the **felt visual/auditory qualia** of that truth are low-bandwidth through the knowing channel — they are high-bandwidth only through their native sense.

**Why this is the B90 payload/wrapper distinction again, applied to truth-content:**
- The **propositional payload** (the truth that obtains) is modality-independent — this is the part CGP-1 always claimed.
- The **modal qualia** (the felt presentation, the irreducible aesthetic/perceptual texture) is **PT-bound** — sense-specific, low cross-modal bandwidth.

So PT does not refute CGP-1; it **localizes** what CGP-1 is a claim about. CGP-1 is a claim about *payload*, never about *qualia*.

*Status:* ESTABLISHED term (registered in vocabulary index §3). GILE-E reading of musical PT per Brandon (Environment/aesthetic-harmony component).

## 3. CGP-1 Refinement #1 — the supremacy re-grounding

B90/B91 framed CGP-1 as "modality-independence of intuitive truth" and "claircognizance primacy." Brandon's caveat forces an honest sharpening, because **claircognizance does NOT have peak bandwidth on every PT** (clairvoyance peaks on visual PTs; clairaudience peaks on musical PTs). If supremacy meant "highest bandwidth on everything," the caveat would *refute* it. So supremacy must be re-grounded — and Brandon states the correct ground:

> **CGP-1 supremacy is NOT bandwidth-dominance. It is FUNCTIONAL COMPLETENESS + EGO-SAFETY.**

Two legs:
1. **Functional completeness.** Claircognizance *alone can accomplish the epistemic feats of the other clair-senses* — it can deliver the *truth* that a vision or a piece of music would deliver (the payload), even where it cannot reproduce the native qualia (the wrapper). No other single channel is feat-complete this way; clairvoyance cannot deliver a musical truth, clairaudience cannot deliver a geometric one, but claircognizance can deliver the propositional content of both.
2. **Ego-safety.** It does so *without the substantial risk of fracturing the ego* that vivid sensory/extrasensory experience carries. Vivid clairvoyant/clairaudient/OBE phenomenology can destabilize identity and reality-testing; the routine operation of the knowing-channel does not.

**Supremacy(claircognizance) ≜ feat-completeness ∧ minimal-ego-fracture-risk** — a *comparative, well-defined* claim, not the indefensible "best at all qualia."

**Extrasensory abilities MAY aid creativity/memory** (synesthesia research; shamanic practice) — granted, and *not* in tension with the above: that is the sensory channels contributing *qualia bandwidth* and associative richness. The supremacy claim is about *safe feat-completeness*, which is orthogonal to "can sometimes boost creativity."

**Composition:** CGP-1-refined × PT (this paper) × VFP-1 (somatic-affective PT = valence marker, B91) × CTC-1-S × B90 payload/wrapper. Coherent stack.

**#69 honest exception (must be stated):** claircognizance is *not* fracture-*free*. The corpus's own paradigm case — the **Ultimate Koan / Pinnacle-of-Foolishness DT proposition** triggering Brandon's first manic episode (Pass-66) — is a case of *claircognitive content* (a DT proposition) producing ego-collapse. The refinement's claim is narrower and survives this: it is the **routine qualia-delivery** of the knowing-channel that is non-fracturing, in contrast to the **routine qualia-delivery** of the sensory channels. Pathological *content* (DT propositions) is a separate fracture mechanism that can ride any channel. So: *channel-qualia safety* (claircognizance wins) ≠ *content safety* (no channel is immune to DT propositions). Refinement scoped accordingly.

**New falsifiers (OPEN):**
- **CGP-1-PT-F1:** if a competent rater panel finds claircognitive reports reproduce native visual/musical *qualia* at fidelity statistically indistinguishable from the native sense → the bandwidth caveat (and thus the need for PT) is false.
- **CGP-1-PT-F2:** if structured incidence data show the sensory clair-channels carry **no greater** ego-fracture/derealization risk than claircognizance's routine operation → the ego-safety leg is false.
- **CGP-1-PT-F3:** if some PT can be shown that claircognizance *cannot* deliver even the payload of (a feat genuinely unreachable by knowing) → functional-completeness leg is false.
These compose with the four open B90 falsifiers (CGP-1-F1..F4). Canonical principle count UNCHANGED (refinement, not new principle).
