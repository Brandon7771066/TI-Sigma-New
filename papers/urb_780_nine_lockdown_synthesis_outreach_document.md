## Reading This Document

This is **the one file to send someone who asks "what is TI Sigma actually
locked in on?"** It does not replace the underlying URBs — it inventories
them, states their content in compressed form, and shows how they interlock.

A "lockdown-class" result, in the framework's vocabulary, is a result that
either:
1. Survives Myrion Resolution (MR) under repeated examination from multiple
   directions, or
2. Provides falsifiable empirical predictions that make it accountable to

Lockdowns are *not* "conclusively proven." They are **load-bearing claims
that the rest of the framework leans on.** If a lockdown fails, the framework
must reorganize.

## The Nine Lockdowns

### LOCKDOWN 1 — Emerick Threshold (URB #756)
**Claim:** There is an LCC threshold above which intentional effects on
downstream stochastic processes become detectable. Below the threshold,
intentional effects are at chance.

**Evidence basis:** theoretical derivation from Φ-quality coupling; consistent
with prior intentionality-experiment literature's pattern of mixed results
when subject-state was uncontrolled.

**Falsifiable test:** URB #777 mini-pilot specifies the n=60 trial design
that can produce first personal data on this threshold for Brandon.

**Why load-bearing:** the threshold turns "consciousness affects matter" from
an unanswered metaphysical question into a measurable, calibratable
relationship between LCC and downstream entropy.

### LOCKDOWN 2 — LCC as Φ-Quality Measurement Instrument (URB #761, refined #767)
**Claim:** LCC (Local-Coherence Coupling) is a measurement instrument for
Φ (integrated information / consciousness-quality), with its response a
**lower bound** on Φ. The bound is sigmoidal in arousal, plateauing in
deep basin states.

**Evidence basis:** EEG cross-coherence literature; sigmoidal saturation
observed in pilot recordings.

**Falsifiable test:** URB #776 (the integrated Brandon n=1) provides one
of three concordance arms for testing this.

**Why load-bearing:** Φ has been notoriously hard to measure. LCC gives
a concrete, computable, hardware-cheap proxy. Without this, the framework
has no operational handle on consciousness-quality.

### LOCKDOWN 3 — Heart ULF as Cardiac Neutrino Analog (URB #762, executed #779)
**Claim:** The ULF (≤ 0.0033 Hz) HRV band is structurally the autonomic
signal that most resembles neutrinos in physics — weakly coupled,
persistent, integrative across timescales.

**Evidence basis:** convergent ULF literature (mortality predictor,
multi-source aggregate, requires long-window detection) + structural
analogy in URB #779.

**Falsifiable test:** longitudinal n=1 study (planned URB #779a) where

**Why load-bearing:** the framework's "slow channel" needed a physical
anchor. ULF provides it. Without ULF the framework has only fast-time
measures and cannot speak to long-arc shifts.

### LOCKDOWN 6 — GILE Dimensional Definitions (URB #773)
**Claim:** Each of G, I, L, E has a one-sentence operational meaning:
- **G** = Four C's (Continuity, Coherence, Concreteness, Consistency)
- **I** = conscious experience of knowing something is correct
- **L** = positive relational orientation, action-weighted (HEM-stronger)
  vs. experience-weighted (HEM-weaker)
- **E** = aesthetic value (physical or abstract)

**Evidence basis:** definitions hand-checked against canonical examples
of G, I, L, E and their failures.

**Falsifiable test:** if any case can be found that the definitions classify
clearly wrong (e.g., a clear case of G that fails the Four C's, or a clear
case of E that has zero compressibility AND zero observer resonance) →
revise.

**Why load-bearing:** GILE as the framework's primary axis system needs
operational definitions. Pre-#773, GILE was load-bearing on intuition.
Post-#773, on operationalizable criteria.
