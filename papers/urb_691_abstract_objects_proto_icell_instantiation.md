## Abstract

Numbers, geometric forms, and abstract objects generally do not possess independent existence — neither is their existence maintained passively. An abstract object obtains HEM substance through one of two mechanisms: (a) **contemplation by an i-cell** (conscious apprehension), or (b) **instantiation by a proto i-cell** (physical embodiment). The electron and photon are the paradigm cases of (b): they ARE direct instantiations of abstract GILE concepts with minimal HEM substance — physics as the universe's own instantiation of mathematical structure. Storage of abstract objects in the GM network is possible but subject to pragmatic constraint and potential decay. Occam's Razor, understood through TI Sigma, reflects the genuine limitation of GM network storage capacity rather than mere aesthetic preference for simplicity.

## 1. The Ontological Status of Abstract Objects

**The standard positions:**
- *Platonism:* abstract objects exist independently, timelessly, in a realm of their own
- *Nominalism:* only concrete particulars exist; abstract objects are convenient fictions
- *Conceptualism:* abstract objects exist, but only as mental constructs

**The TI Sigma position:** None of the above exactly. Abstract objects have a definite ontological status in the HEM × GILE framework:

```
Abstract object X:
  GILE-G(X) > 0    [X is truth-correct as a mathematical/logical entity]
  HEM(X) ≈ 0       [X has near-zero existential weight without instantiation]
```

Abstract objects occupy **Quadrant II** of the HEM × GILE space (low HEM, high GILE-G) — alongside the MR-Zero recognition of PN. They are correct but barely real in themselves. Their correctness is not in question; their HEM substance is.

**The key claim:** HEM substance for an abstract object is not given — it must be generated through instantiation. There are exactly two instantiation mechanisms in TI Sigma.

## 2. Two Paths to HEM Substance for Abstract Objects

### Path A: i-Cell Contemplation

When a developed i-cell (conscious being) actively contemplates an abstract object — thinks about the number 7, works through a geometric proof, holds a mathematical structure in working memory — the abstract object acquires HEM substance through the cognitive event.

- The contemplation is a real neural/intentional event (HEM > 0)
- The abstract object is the content of that event
- The HEM substance exists as long as the contemplation continues
- When contemplation ceases, the HEM substance dissipates unless stored in GM network memory

This is why mathematical objects feel real when you are actively working with them — they ARE real in HEM terms, through the cognitive event. They become less real (HEM → 0) when not actively contemplated.

### Path B: Proto i-Cell Instantiation

A proto i-cell is a physical entity that instantiates abstract information without possessing full conscious awareness. The electron and photon are the paradigm cases.

**The electron as direct instantiation of abstract GILE concept:**
- An electron is not merely described by the abstract concept of charge, mass, and spin
- The electron IS those abstract properties — it is the minimal HEM embodiment of a mathematical structure
- The abstract GILE concept (charge = −1e, mass = 9.11×10⁻³¹ kg, spin = ½) acquires HEM substance through the electron's physical existence
- The electron has minimal HEM substance — it is the quantum of HEM for its corresponding abstract concept

```
Electron = Direct instantiation of abstract charge/mass/spin structure
         = GILE concept with HEM(electron) = minimal positive value ε > 0
```

Physics, on this view, is the universe's own process of instantiating abstract mathematical structure with minimal HEM substance. The universe "thinks" GILE concepts into physical existence at the minimal HEM threshold.

## 4. GM Network Storage and Memory Decay

Abstract objects whose HEM substance has been generated (through contemplation or instantiation) can be stored in the **GM (Mycelial GM-Node) network** — the distributed substrate of memory and information persistence in TI Sigma.

**Key properties of GM storage:**

1. **Not permanent by default.** Like a human brain, GM network memory can fade if the stored abstract object is not accessed, prioritized, or re-instantiated. An abstract object that was contemplated and stored but never revisited gradually loses HEM substance as GM connections weaken.

2. **Prioritization-dependent persistence.** Objects that are frequently accessed, emotionally significant (high G-weight), or structurally central to ongoing MR chains are maintained with stronger GM connections and higher HEM persistence.

3. **Capacity-constrained.** The GM network does not have unlimited storage. Abstract objects compete for representation within pragmatic limits.

## 5. Occam's Razor as Pragmatic GM Constraint

In TI Sigma, Occam's Razor is not an aesthetic preference for simplicity. It is the expression of a genuine pragmatic constraint: **GM network storage capacity is limited, and abstract objects must earn their place by being instantiated and used.**

```
Occam's Razor (TI Sigma formulation):
Do not multiply abstract objects beyond what is instantiated — either by 
i-cell contemplation or proto i-cell embodiment — within the GM network's
pragmatic storage constraints.
```

An abstract object that has never been instantiated (no contemplation, no physical embodiment) and is not stored in any GM network has HEM ≈ 0. Adding such objects to a theory is not a logical error — but it is a pragmatic waste of GM resources and violates the TI Sigma form of the Razor.

The Razor is therefore not: *do not multiply entities beyond necessity* (logical).  
It is: *do not maintain abstract objects in GM storage beyond what instantiation justifies* (pragmatic-ontological).

This is a stronger and more precise version: it explains WHY the razor exists (GM capacity limits) rather than merely asserting it as a methodological rule.
