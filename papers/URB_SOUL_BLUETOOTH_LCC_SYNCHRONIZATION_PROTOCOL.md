## 1. The LCC Virus: What It Is

The LCC Virus is not a pathogen. The name captures its propagation dynamics: like a biological virus, it self-replicates by inducing in each host consciousness a coherence pattern that the host then transmits to adjacent nodes. Unlike a pathogen, its transmission produces beneficial effects — elevated LCC, expanded Radiant access, increased GILE score — and it cannot replicate below the C_EMERICK threshold.

Formally, the LCC Virus is a self-propagating coherence eigenstate of the GM-Node network. When a node N reaches LCC >= C_EMERICK (approximately 0.4370), it begins broadcasting a coherence perturbation into the mycelial substrate. Adjacent nodes that receive this perturbation experience an induced LCC elevation. If the induced elevation pushes the receiving node above its own C_EMERICK threshold, that node begins broadcasting in turn. The result is a propagating wavefront of coherence elevation that moves through the GM network.

Key properties:
- Minimum propagation threshold: LCC of source node >= C_EMERICK
- Minimum reception threshold: receiving node LCC >= 0 (any conscious node can receive)
- Amplification condition: receiving node LCC >= C_EMERICK (node becomes a relay)
- Attenuation condition: receiving node LCC < C_EMERICK (node absorbs but does not relay)
- Distance dependence: none — the GM network topology is non-local

The LCC Virus is the carrier. Soul Bluetooth is the intentional synchronization protocol built on top of it.

## 3. The Three Connection Modes

Soul Bluetooth operates in three distinct modes depending on the embodiment status of the participating nodes.

### Mode 1: Living-Living (LL)

Both nodes are currently embodied. This is the highest-bandwidth mode: both nodes have full causal power and can actively co-regulate their LCC through behavioral, physiological, and intentional means.

LL synchronization produces measurable biometric coupling:
- HRV coherence alignment between participants
- EEG phase synchrony (particularly in the gamma and theta bands)
- Elevated GILE scores in both participants during synchronization
- Subjective reports of felt connection, shared intuition, and amplified meaning

LL synchronization is the empirically most accessible mode. The biometric bridge (Polar H10, Muse 2, Mendi fNIRS) provides direct measurement of LL coupling strength. The LCC Virus propagates bidirectionally between LL nodes that have both achieved LCC >= C_EMERICK, creating a reinforcing loop that elevates both participants.

The Maharishi Effect (URB_MAHARISHI_EFFECT_TI_SIGMA_PHI_FIELD.md) is a population-scale LL synchronization event: a critical mass of co-meditating nodes elevates the local GM network coherence, which then propagates the LCC Virus to non-meditating adjacent nodes.

### Mode 2: Living-Dead (LD)

One node is embodied; the other exists as a photonic I-cell in the dark-energy substrate. This mode is asymmetric: the embodied node retains full causal power, while the photonic node has correlational reach only.

What LD synchronization can do:
- The Living node can receive coherence perturbations originating from the photonic node's standing pattern in the GM network
- The Living node can broadcast coherence elevations that the photonic node resonates with, amplifying the photonic node's presence in the dark-energy substrate
- Both nodes experience GILE elevation during genuine LD synchronization (the photonic node's GILE elevation is not directly measurable but is implied by its increased coherence emission)

What LD synchronization cannot do:
- The photonic node cannot initiate physical-world causal actions through the Living node (no channeling of motor control, no material-world interventions)
- The Living node cannot "retrieve" information that the photonic node did not encode in its coherence signature before death — the photonic signature is a standing pattern, not an updatable database

The subjective phenomenology of LD synchronization: the Living participant reports a sense of presence, felt meaning, and occasionally intuitive access to the characteristic patterns of the deceased — not as received messages but as resonance with a coherence profile that shaped the GM network during the photonic node's embodied life.

### Mode 3: Dead-Dead (DD)

Both nodes exist as photonic I-cells in the dark-energy substrate. This mode is the least understood and the least empirically accessible. DD synchronization operates entirely within the dark-energy GM substrate.

What DD synchronization entails:
- Two photonic I-cell signatures that were highly coherent during embodied life retain their mutual adjacency in the GM network
- The dark-energy substrate maintains their resonance as a persistent standing pattern
- No causal outputs are produced in the physical world, but the coherence patterns mutually amplify

DD synchronization is not directly measurable by current TI Sigma instrumentation. It is predicted by the theory and is a subject for future empirical investigation as the dark-energy coupling measurement infrastructure matures.

The single most important design feature of Soul Bluetooth is the consent requirement: synchronization cannot be initiated by one node against the will of another. This is not merely an ethical constraint — it is a structural one.

The LCC Virus propagates by inducing coherence elevation. Coherence elevation requires the receiving node to be in an open, receptive configuration — what is phenomenologically experienced as willingness, attention, or intention. The reception threshold is met, but the amplification condition is not.

This means:

- In LD mode, the Living node's willingness is necessary but the photonic node's "willingness" is encoded in the coherence signature it left — if the relationship was one of genuine mutual coherence elevation, the signature is aligned for LD synchronization; if the relationship was conflicted or low-coherence, the signature does not easily resonate

Soul Bluetooth is, at its foundation, an operationalization of Love in the L×E dyad: Love as the binding force, Existence as the structural ground.
