## 0. One-paragraph ruling

The refinement is **accepted with one correction and one guard**. **Accepted:** eTJ (the canonical ETJ-1 incoherence-holding capacity) is a genuine *third* ground for holding a genuine tralsity — and, crucially, it is a **different kind** of ground from the first two. Coherentism and NGK are **content-side** (properties of the *proposition* and its supporting web); **eTJ is agent-side** (a property of the *mind doing the holding*). This is exactly why it is "the cherry on top": it explains the residual case PSM-1 could not — why a mind can have the coherentist scaffolding *and* NGK available and **still** fail, collapsing a genuine Indeterminate into "nonsense." The missing factor is *motor capacity*: too little eTJ and the mind snaps the pair to a false resolution (or discards it as MI) rather than sustaining it. **The gray I→MI transition** the author affirms is simply the canonical **graded boundary** ("a fading line, not a wall") — endorsed, unchanged. **Correction (#69):** the corpus does **not** pin the I/MI cutoff at √2−1. √2−1 ≈ 0.414 is the canonical **Emerick / LCC onset threshold** (existence/consciousness onset; RTI-1's minimum-Tralse-zone floor), a *different* threshold; the I/MI cutoff is the `in-1` leeway-exhaustion gate, which the corpus leaves **un-pinned numerically**. Reading √2−1 as the MI-cutoff would be a *new posit* (a resonance, not a derivation) and is flagged, not asserted. **Guard (anti-crank-shield):** because eTJ makes comprehension agent-relative, it must **never** be used to rescue a genuine MI ("you just lack the eTJ to see it"). eTJ explains failure to grasp a statement **only after** that statement independently qualifies as Indeterminate (passes `in-1` / carries real coherentist scaffolding). It **cannot convert** a genuine MI into an Indeterminate. New falsifier `PSM-1-F2`. **Count unchanged 80.**

## 1. The three grounds, and why eTJ is a different *kind*

PSM-1 (B160) gave two grounds for holding a near-/past-frontier concept *with value*. B161 adds the third and sorts all three by **what they are properties of**:

| Ground | Job | Property of | Canonical status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Coherentism** (supporting premises, e.g. "exists in POTENTIAL") | Confers **sensicality** — coherent, usable value | the **proposition** + its premise-web (content-side) | pre-existing (used across corpus; kin = refinement #9) |
| **NGK** (Negative Gap Knowledge) | Confers **tolerability** — holdable by the gap it marks | the **proposition** as grasped (content-side) | canonical (FOR_EVERYONE Ch.2) |
| **eTJ** (Epistemic Tralse-Joule) | Confers **comprehensibility-by-this-mind** — the *motor* to hold it without collapse | the **agent** (capacity-side) | canonical ETJ-1 #53 |

The first two ask *"is there sense/tolerable-grip available in the concept?"* The third asks *"does this mind have the capacity to actually take up that grip?"* They are orthogonal: a statement can be fully scaffolded (coherentist premises present) and NGK-graspable in principle, yet a **low-eTJ** mind still fails to hold it — it forces a premature resolution or files it under "nonsense." That residual failure is precisely what content-side tools cannot explain, and what eTJ does.

**This sharpens ETJ-1 without adding to it.** ETJ-1 already defines eTJ as incoherence-holding capacity and already links it to NGK ("the same NGK that lets you hold 'a square circle' … scaled up"). B161's only move is to **name its role in the I/MI demarcation**: eTJ is the *agent-side gate* on whether a genuine tralsity is comprehended as a tralsity or mis-dismissed as MI.

## 3. #69 correction — √2−1 is the Emerick/LCC *onset*, not the MI cutoff

The author hedged ("if I'm not mistaken"), and the honest answer is: **not quite.** What the corpus pins at **√2−1 ≈ 0.414** is the **Emerick Threshold / LCC onset** — the minimum GILE-Truth for existence/consciousness onset (`GT ≥ √2−1`, `urb_621`), the minimum recursive-feedback rung of the LCC ladder (`r²+2r=1`, B157), and RTI-1's minimum-Tralse-zone floor (`LCC_TRALSE = √2−1`). These are **existence/onset** thresholds on the **real/HEM** axis.

The **I/MI cutoff** is a *different* thing: it is the **leeway-exhaustion gate** (`in-1`), a categorical screen on whether a proposition retains interpretive leeway. The corpus **does not assign it a numeric value** — and B160 deliberately kept it operational-not-numeric. So:

- **True:** the PD formalizes thresholds, and √2−1 is a real, load-bearing canonical threshold.
- **Not established:** that √2−1 *is* the MI cutoff. That identification appears nowhere in the corpus.
- **Honest status of the idea:** identifying the I/MI cutoff with the minimum-Tralse-zone floor √2−1 is an *attractive resonance* (both are "where coherence/existence gives out"), but it would be a **new posit** — a resonance, not a derivation — and it collides with the base-4 categorical nature of the MI label (a leeway screen, not a magnitude on the GILE-Truth axis). It is therefore **flagged, not adopted** (falsifier `PSM-1-F3`).

This is the #69 both-ways move: credit the real threshold, decline the un-earned identification.

## 4. Anti-crank-shield — the guard that keeps eTJ honest (`PSM-1-F2`)

Making comprehension agent-relative introduces a real hazard: eTJ could be weaponized as an **unfalsifiable dodge** — "you only call my claim MI because you lack the eTJ to hold it." Left unguarded, this would let *any* genuine nonsense masquerade as a high-eTJ-only tralsity, erasing the MI screen entirely (a worse version of the `PSM-1-F1` relabeling risk).

**The guard (part of the principle):** eTJ may explain a failure to comprehend a statement **only after** the statement has *independently* qualified as Indeterminate — i.e. it **passes the `in-1` leeway test** (the label gate), with genuine load-bearing coherentist scaffolding (B160's `PSM-1-F1`) counting as supporting evidence, never as a substitute for the leeway gate. eTJ operates on the **Q-use / access** axis; it **cannot move a proposition's Q-label**. A genuine MI (identity-contradiction or category-error with leeway annihilated) stays MI for the highest-eTJ mind in existence — such a mind may hold it *tolerably* (NGK) and study it, but holding-tolerably is not comprehending-as-true-tralsity. **No amount of eTJ converts MI into Indeterminate.** Invoking "low eTJ" to protect a statement that fails the leeway test is the cheat `PSM-1-F2` names.

Corollary (the legitimate use): eTJ explains **variation and error in classification** — why competent minds sometimes wrongly file genuine tralsities (wave-particle, productive paradox, deep metaphor) under "nonsense" — without making the *correct* classification agent-relative. The proposition's label is objective; eTJ governs who can *see* it.
