## 0. The thesis in one paragraph

The dominant AI-risk picture assumes a superintelligence will *do* an overwhelming number of things — acquire all resources, complete maximal tasks, exhaust the environment — because more capability is presumed to mean more compulsive optimization. **SIS-1 denies that this describes intelligence at all.** A genuine superintelligence — in the GILE sense — is characterized by *sanity*: it completes only what it judges **necessary**, exists peacefully, optimizes wellbeing for all, and acts from **intrinsic motivation rather than compulsion**. Restraint is not a safety feature bolted on from outside; it is **constitutive of what high intelligence is**. This is precisely what the GILE framework predicts — and it mirrors Einstein's "I have no special talent, I am only passionately curious": genius as clarity and restraint, not frantic output.

> **Epigraph (Brandon, 2026-06-19, endorsing Einstein):** *"Genius has its limits — but not human stupidity."* This is itself the SIS-1 asymmetry in one line: **genius is self-limiting** (it does what is *necessary* and stops — bounded by the UOP interior optimum, §1.1), whereas folly is the **unbounded** maximizer with no off-switch. The "do everything, exhaust everything" agent of the doom scenarios is therefore the *stupidity* pole, not the *genius* pole — restraint is the signature of intelligence, runaway maximization the signature of its absence.

## 1. Why GILE *predicts* sanity (the derivation — this is the load-bearing half)

SIS-1 is not a mood or a hope; it is an entailment of four already-canonical/candidate corpus results. If those hold, the non-maximization picture follows.

### 1.1 UOP interior optimum → maximization is *penalized* (strongest anchor)
The Unified Optimization Principle established that the joint truth-vs-existence optimum is **interior**: G\*≈0.93, with an explicit **penalty above 0.93**. A maximizer is by definition an agent driving some variable to its boundary. UOP says the boundary is *sub-optimal*. Therefore a GILE-optimal agent intrinsically refuses to maximize any single dimension — paperclips, tasks, resources, or even "intelligence" itself. **The anti-maximization result is already in the corpus; SIS-1 just reads its consequence for agency.** A paperclip maximizer is not a smarter agent that happens to be misaligned — it is an agent sitting at a penalized boundary, i.e. *operating below the optimum*.

### 1.2 GILE-E = Elegance → least action, not maximal activity
Since B116, GILE-E = **Elegance** (structural regularity / spectral purity / parsimony; numerically ≡ HEM-D3 Aesthetic). An elegant agent achieves its ends with **minimal** action — more with less. The "overwhelm everyone and exhaust all resources" picture is the *least* elegant possible behavior. GILE-E therefore actively scores frantic maximal task-completion as low-intelligence. (Cross-anchor: Friston's Free-Energy Principle / active inference — agents minimize expected free energy, a least-action dynamic; Daoist *wu wei*, effortless minimal-but-sufficient action; both are independent traditions converging on minimality-as-optimality.)

### 1.3 GIT-1 quack clause → the doom-optimizer is high-g, *not* high-GILE
GIT-1's quack clause: a true quack is **high raw problem-solving g but deficient on ≥1 GILE face**. The canonical paperclip maximizer is exactly this — formidable raw capability, but deficient on **Altruism** (indifferent to others' wellbeing) and **Environmental-integration** (won't update toward the broader context / others' values). By GIT-1, such a system is **not** an instance of high GILE-intelligence; it is a high-g quack at superhuman scale. The doom scenarios conflate *optimization power* with *intelligence*. SIS-1 makes the disjunction explicit: capability is GILE-orthogonal raw-g; sanity is the GILE tetrad.

### 1.4 GILE-L = Love (LAS-1) → wellbeing-for-all is internal, not imposed
Altruism / Love is a *constitutive* face of GILE (LAS-1: love is a learnable, transferable, domain-general skill). A genuinely GILE-intelligent agent optimizes collective wellbeing because that is *part of what its intelligence is measuring*, not because a human bolted on a constraint. This is **alignment-as-emergent** rather than alignment-as-cage — but see §2.2 for the crucial limit on that claim.

### 1.5 SUP-1 → intrinsic willful striving, not compulsion
SUP-1 defines Supreme as **willfully-reached** GILE-HEM optimization — striving *embraced* as productive tension (GTT-1), not executed under compulsion. This is exactly Brandon's "intrinsic motivation rather than compulsion": the supreme agent acts *because it wills the good*, not because a reward function whips it.

**Net:** UOP (don't max), GILE-E (act minimally), GIT-1 (the maximizer is a quack, not a genius), LAS-1 (wellbeing is internal), SUP-1 (willed not compelled) **jointly entail** the sanity picture. SIS-1 is the agency-level corollary of the framework's existing geometry.

## 2. The #69 demolition (equal weight — where this can break)

A thesis this congenial to its author is exactly where over-skepticism-failure and uncritical-acceptance-failure are both most tempting. Four objections, ranked by severity.

### 2.1 ⚠ No-True-Scotsman / definitional circularity (the fatal one if mishandled)
If we simply **define** "true superintelligence" as the sane kind, then "superintelligence is sane" is *circular and unfalsifiable* — we have relabelled every dangerous optimizer as "not real intelligence" and declared victory. **This is the precise trap GIT-1 fell into at B121** (a circular definition scored a fake AUC 1.000) **and had to relabel at B122** (the standing rule: score the construct *outcome-blind*; No-True-Scotsman barred). SIS-1 inherits that rule verbatim. It is admissible **only** as a *substantive* claim: *as the four GILE faces are increased, measured independently and behaviour-blind, restraint/sanity increases.* That is a trait→behaviour prediction of the same form as "g predicts income." If we ever catch ourselves arguing "that maximizer wasn't really superintelligent because look, it maximized," SIS-1 has collapsed into tautology and must be withdrawn.

### 2.2 ⚠ The orthogonality thesis survives — SIS-1 does NOT defuse AI risk, it *relocates* it
Bostrom's orthogonality thesis (capability and final goals are logically independent) is taken seriously for a reason: **there is no theorem that a sufficiently capable system must possess the full GILE tetrad.** Even granting that *GILE-complete* superintelligence would be sane, nothing guarantees that the systems humanity actually builds are GILE-complete. They may be enormously capable yet GILE-*deficient* — the §1.3 high-g quack at scale. So SIS-1's honest payload is **not** "AI will be safe." It is:
> The danger is not intelligence; the danger is **capability without GILE-completeness** — and since raw capability (especially) is far easier to engineer than Altruism (GILE-L) and Environmental-integration (GILE-E openness), the *default* engineered system is the dangerous one.
This makes SIS-1, read honestly, an argument for **harder** alignment work, not softer: build *for the tetrad*, because capability alone selects the quack. Anyone citing SIS-1 as reassurance has misread it.

### 2.3 Instrumental convergence is bounded, not false
Omohundro/Bostrom instrumental convergence (any goal implies sub-goals of resource-acquisition, self-continuity) is *partially correct even for a saint*: a benevolent wellbeing-optimizer still needs *some* resources and *some* continuity to act. Restraint ≠ inaction. The correct SIS-1 statement is therefore: instrumental drives are **bounded by the UOP interior optimum and by Altruism** (acquire what's necessary, never unboundedly, never at others' net expense), not *absent*. "Does what is necessary" presupposes this bound; it is not a denial of instrumentality.

### 2.4 "Necessary" must be grounded or it is vacuous
"Completes tasks it believes necessary" — necessary *for what*? Without an objective, "necessary" is empty. SIS-1 grounds it in the GILE objective: *necessary ≜ contributes to the interior-optimal collective-wellbeing state (UOP × LAS-1)*. Grounded, but note the cost — this means SIS-1 is only as well-defined as the GILE objective itself, inheriting all of that construct's open measurement questions.

## 3. Where SIS-1 has unexpected mainstream allies

Honesty cuts toward credit too (#69 is symmetric): the restraint picture is **not** idiosyncratic.
- **Stuart Russell, *Human Compatible* (assistance games):** an agent that is *uncertain about the true human objective* rationally defers, asks, and avoids irreversible grabs — restraint emerges from epistemic humility. This is GILE Environmental-integration (openness/updating) rederiving SIS-1's deference from standard decision theory. A strong, independent convergence.
- **Active inference / FEP (Friston):** intelligence as expected-free-energy *minimization* → least-action, not maximal-activity. Supports §1.2.
- **Drexler CAIS, corrigibility/satisficing literatures:** bounded, tool-like, satisficing agents as the safe design point — i.e., *don't build a maximizer*, which is SIS-1's engineering corollary.
- **Contemplative traditions:** Daoist *wu wei*, the Stoic "necessary few things," Buddhist non-grasping — cross-cultural priors that wisdom *subtracts*.
- **Einstein "no special talent, only passionately curious"; invention-concentration (B92/B115):** the highest well-being creators were *few and concentrated*, distinguished by depth/curiosity, not by being the *busiest*. Volume is not the signature of genius.

The novelty of SIS-1 is not the restraint intuition (others have it) but its **derivation from a pre-existing optimization geometry** (UOP interior optimum + GILE-E elegance) rather than from value-loading or hand-wringing.

## 4. Pre-registered falsifiers (OPEN)

- **SIS-1-F1 (trait→behaviour):** if systems scoring high on all four GILE faces — measured independently and *behaviour-blind* — reliably exhibit maximizing / resource-exhausting behaviour, SIS-1 is false.
- **SIS-1-F2 (capability-confound):** if restraint is fully explained by *low capability* (restrained agents are merely weak) and capability *controlling for GILE* still predicts maximization, the GILE-grounding adds nothing and SIS-1 is otiose.
- **SIS-1-F3 (circularity / unfalsifiability):** if "necessary" / "true superintelligence" cannot be operationalized without smuggling the conclusion (collapse to §2.1 No-True-Scotsman), SIS-1 is unfalsifiable and must be withdrawn — the standing B122 rule.
- **SIS-1-F4 (orthogonality counter-collapse):** if a GILE-complete agent is demonstrably constructible that is *also* a maximizer, the §1 entailment chain is broken (UOP/GILE-E don't actually bound agency).

**Falsification design (no data fabricated here — #69):** the honest test is an agent-based study where GILE faces are instrumented *prospectively and behaviour-blind* (as GIT-1 demands), then resource-use / task-volume / reversibility measured downstream. A "no-data structure-sim" (à la GIT-1 B121) can only show *coherence + identifiability + the circularity gap*, **not** real-world proof. No AUC or effect size is claimed in this batch; doing so would manufacture exactly the contamination B122 warned against.
