## 4. The Specific Correction Applied (the Brandon directive of this curation pass)

> "Future states are NOT MI or 'pre-tralse' — they are coherently **Indeterminate** (undecided ≠ incoherent). Retrocausation may introduce minor incoherence but not enough to make time-progression MI overall."

**What was wrong:** URB #509 §7.4 listed "Pre-Tralse Undetermination" as the 4th category of Meta-Indeterminate. This conflated *coherent-but-undecided* (Indeterminate, MR1 ✅) with *incoherent* (MI, MR1 ❌).

**What is now canonical:**
- Future / undetermined states are **Indeterminate** in the base-4 truth-label sense {T, F, **I**, MI}.
- They pass MR1 (the coherence gate). The state of affairs is real and well-formed; only the *value* is not yet fixed.
- MI is reserved exclusively for τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) — formal incoherence.
- MI taxonomy has **three** categories: Maximal Incoherence, Self-Negating Nothing, Self-Refuting Meta-Statement.
- Narrow retrocausal-self-referential paradoxes can be locally MI but as a structural-self-reference failure (Category 3 territory), **not** as a property of forward-running temporal indeterminacy.
- "We don't know yet" → **Indeterminate**, not MI.
- This aligns with **FDS-1** (2026-05-17): future-state determinacy lives on a coherent PD-real spectrum across three regimes, not on a MI cliff.
