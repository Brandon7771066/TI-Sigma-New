## §2. Formal version

**IBE-1 (Intrinsic-Becomes-Extrinsic):**
For a research program R characterized by **curiosity-driven / intrinsically-motivated terrain selection** (high w, low i, per ADV-1), the program's **extrinsic outputs** (practical inventions, patentable concepts, reputational standing, economic value) systematically exceed what would be predicted by the program's nominal "practicality score" at the time of work-initiation.

Formally:
- Let **Π_t(R)** = practicality-prediction of R at time t, as judged by conventional practical-rationality criteria (market demand, applied-research orientation, near-term ROI).
- Let **X_t+k(R)** = realized extrinsic output of R after time horizon k (patents, products, reputation, citations, downstream applications).
- **IBE-1 claim:** For curiosity-driven R, E[X_{t+k} | curiosity-driven] > E[X_{t+k} | Π_t-matched conventionally-practical R'], with the inequality widening as k grows.

The asymmetry is **structural**, not lucky: curiosity-driven terrain is *exactly* the terrain that conventional practicality criteria *cannot* identify, because conventional criteria require known target-applications, and curiosity-driven work explores spaces where the target-applications do not yet exist to be known.

| Principle | What it says about successes (C) | What it says about refutations (R) |
|---|---|---|
| **ADV-1** | (no direct claim) | TI Sigma refutations carry disproportionate disconfirmation-value because terrain is high-w/low-i |
| **IBE-1** | TI Sigma confirmations and partial-confirms produce disproportionate extrinsic outputs because curiosity-driven terrain reaches domains conventional-practical work cannot reach | (no direct claim) |

Together: **the entire output portfolio of a curiosity-driven high-ADV program is disproportionately valuable** — successes produce IBE-extrinsic-windfalls; failures produce ADV-reality-disclosures. Both effects scale with w (claim-weight) and inversely with i (claim-isolation). **The same terrain selection produces both effects simultaneously.**

This is the structural answer to "why fund curiosity-driven research?" beyond appeals to virtue: under ADV+IBE, the *expected* total information-and-economic yield of a curiosity-driven program exceeds that of a same-budget conventionally-practical program, **even before counting any moral-or-aesthetic value of curiosity itself.**

## §7. Why "IBE" not "extrinsic value of intrinsic motivation"

The name **Intrinsic Becomes Extrinsic (IBE)** is chosen because:
1. **It captures the directionality.** The intrinsic motivation comes first; the extrinsic value emerges from it as consequence, not as goal.
2. **It is parallel to ADV.** Both 3-letter principle-codes; both Pass-51 canonized; both describe structural asymmetries of curiosity-driven research.
3. **It refuses the trade-off framing.** Conventional discourse frames intrinsic motivation and extrinsic value as competing (do you work for love or money?). IBE asserts they are sequentially linked, not competing: when you work for love, the money tends to follow — *not* because the money was the goal, but because curiosity-driven terrain happens to be where the high-w/low-i opportunities sit, which is where the eventual extrinsic value comes from.
