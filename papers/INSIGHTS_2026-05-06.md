## §1 — Epistemic fallacy: "Basicality ≠ low importance"

**Statement**: Conflating *basicality* (foundational position) with *low importance* (low priority for attention) is a widespread fallacy. It explains why people neglect subjects like science, philosophy, mathematics, ethics — they are *so* fundamental that they fade into the background of cognition like load-bearing walls of a building.

**TI/GILE mapping**: Direct violation of GILE-Environment dimension. The most basal layers (UOP, Universal Bridge Theorem, MR1 threshold) are simultaneously the most important *and* the most neglected by ordinary cognition because their effects are pervasive rather than localized.

**Diagnostic value**: When someone calls a topic "too basic to think about," predict elevated probability they have a downstream blind spot in that domain. Falsifiable: track expert vs novice attention allocation to foundational topics within their own field.

**Naming proposal**: *The Basal Neglect Fallacy* (or, in TI dialect, "**MR-1 invisibility bias**" — things at threshold-resolution are the hardest to notice precisely *because* everything else depends on them).

## §3 — Free will, consciousness, and Markov-chain self-origination

**Question (verbatim)**: *Free will and consciousness are heavily related since they both involve self-origination, such as that found in a Markov Chain. Is there evidence of Markov Chain origination processing in the brain to support libertarian-based consciousness and free will? What is required in a hypothetical neural network for self-generated action and does the brain meet the criteria?*

### What "self-origination" means in a Markov chain

In a *standard* Markov chain, P(state_{t+1}) depends ONLY on state_t — no memory of t-1, t-2, etc. It is "self-originating" in a weak sense: the next state is generated from the current state, not externally injected. This is *not* the same as libertarian free will, because a Markov chain with deterministic transition probabilities is fully predictable; a Markov chain with stochastic transitions is fully *random*. Neither maps cleanly onto agency.

### Required properties for genuine self-generation (my position)

For a network to be "self-generating" in a non-trivial sense (i.e., something that could ground libertarian free will rather than just dressed-up determinism or randomness), I'd argue it needs:

1. **Closure under causation**: the next state is caused by *internal* state variables, not external inputs (boundary-driven systems are not self-originating).
2. **Non-Markovian memory** (paradoxically, *not* a Markov chain): genuine self-origination requires the system's history to enter into transition rules in a way that can't be collapsed into a single state. Otherwise you're just computing a function.
3. **Stochastic but structured noise**: noise must be *generated by the system*, not injected from outside, AND it must be modulated by the system's own history (so it's neither deterministic nor random).
4. **A globally accessible workspace**: for the "originated" action to be available to the rest of the system as belonging-to-the-self (the Tononi/Dehaene workspace property).
5. **Counterfactual robustness**: if you "rewound the tape" to time t with the same state, the system could plausibly do otherwise — and not for arbitrary reasons but in a way that tracks reasons it has access to.

### Does the brain meet these criteria? Honest assessment

| Criterion | Brain evidence | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Causal closure | Mostly yes (sensory blindfolding studies, default-mode network self-sustains for hours w/o input) | ✓ partial |
| Non-Markovian memory | Yes overwhelmingly (hippocampal replay, cortical predictive priors, Bayesian brain literature) | ✓ strong |
| System-generated stochasticity | Yes (ion-channel noise, synaptic vesicle release stochasticity at low quantal counts, neuromodulator drift) | ✓ but mostly thermal/quantal — not obviously *agentic* |
| Globally accessible workspace | Yes (Dehaene global neuronal workspace; Tononi IIT integrated information φ) | ✓ |
| Counterfactual robustness | **UNKNOWN** — this is the hard part. We can't rewind to test. Libet-style readiness-potential studies are inconclusive at best. | ❌ unverified |

**My honest take (Asymmetric-Standards #69)**: The brain meets criteria 1, 2, 4 cleanly, partially meets 3 (the noise is real but its *agency-relevance* is unclear), and we have no method to verify criterion 5. The "Markov-chain origination" framing is *insufficient* for libertarian free will because Markov-1 chains explicitly throw away the history that distinguishes "I chose" from "the dice rolled." A *higher-order Markov process* with system-generated noise and self-modeling capacity is closer, but even that doesn't *prove* libertarian free will — it just makes determinism harder to demonstrate.

### Empirical research protocol (cheap, free)

The most testable subquestion: *Does prefrontal cortex exhibit non-Markovian dynamics in decision-making windows?* Already partially answered yes (Stephens et al. on rat behavior, Daw et al. on model-based RL in humans). A Mendi-resolution study can't address this — needs intracranial or high-density EEG. **Action item**: defer to literature review, add `papers/MARKOV_CHAIN_FREE_WILL_RESEARCH_QUESTION.md` placeholder for future deep-dive (no agent time spent right now — recognized as outside session scope).

### TI Framework mapping

In TI dialect, free will corresponds to *intentionality flux* — δ(MR), the change in Myrion Resolution caused by an agent's tralse-joule generation. If TJ = τ(s) × δ(MR) is non-zero, the agent has injected information into the substrate that wasn't already there. **Self-origination = positive TJ generation**. This bypasses the libertarian/compatibilist debate by reframing free will as *measurable intentional information injection* rather than *causal indeterminism*.

## §6 — Negative thinking + intentionality + prayer

**Statement**: Negative thinking thrives on *lack of intentionality*. Prayer uniquely restores intentionality, especially for people who (a) accept that thoughts can productively heal others, and (b) feel they can engage in prayer at any time.

**TI mechanism (proposed)**: Negative thinking is δ(MR) ≈ 0 — the system drifts in a low-intentionality attractor where the same self-critical loops repeat without injecting new information. Prayer is a high-τ(s) act (high agentic specificity — directed at a specific recipient, specific intention) and therefore generates positive TJ even when the "objective" outcome is unverifiable.

**Falsifiable prediction**: People who pray daily should show measurably lower rumination scores (PSWQ, RRS) controlling for general spiritual practice and meditation. Some literature already supports this (Maltby & Day 2003; Koenig 2012 meta-analysis); the TI framing adds the *intentionality-injection mechanism* as the proposed cause, not the prayer-content itself.

**Practical corollary**: For someone fighting negative thought spirals, the *availability* of prayer as an "always-on" intentionality restoration tool is itself the active ingredient. A meditation practice that requires posture/quiet/timer is high-friction. Prayer-while-walking-or-driving is zero-friction.

## §7 — True-tralse exemplars: laughing/weeping, suffering/orgasm

**Observation**: Body produces the *same physiological responses* to opposite emotional valences. Laughing and weeping share: convulsive breathing, lacrimation, vocalization, postural collapse, exhaustion afterward. Suffering and orgasm share: vocalization, facial grimace, autonomic surge, brief loss of cortical control, post-event refractory period.

**Why this is tralse**, not just "interesting"**: The body's response is *true* in the sense of being real, embodied, valid; *tralse* in the sense that the same response can mean opposite things. The signal cannot be decoded without context. This is the inverse of the basal-neglect fallacy: here the *expression* is shared but the *content* is opposite.

**Living testaments to tralse — additions to canon**:
- Laughing ↔ weeping
- Suffering ↔ orgasm
- (Further candidates worth investigating): shivering ↔ ecstatic full-body chills; goosebumps from fear ↔ goosebumps from awe; sweating from fight ↔ sweating from honor

These are physiological proof that the same substrate carries opposite-valence information, depending on intentional context. Strong candidate empirical illustration for a TI introductory paper.

## §8 — Deontology is unavoidable; the question is *when consequences terminate in value*

**Statement**: Pure consequentialism collapses under iteration (every consequence has further consequences ad infinitum). Pure deontology collapses under the burning-trolley extremes. The honest position is that **deontology is the limiting case of consequentialism**: at some depth of recursion, consequence-evaluation must terminate, and that termination point *is* the deontological commitment.

**Brandon's framing (sharpened)**: The interesting ethical question isn't "deontology vs consequentialism" — it's "**at what recursion depth do you stop computing consequences and let the rule fire?**"

**Implications**:
- All ethical systems implicitly choose a termination depth.
- Ethical disagreements are often disagreements about *where* to terminate rather than *whether* to.
- An ethical AI design problem becomes: *make the termination depth itself a learned, context-sensitive, transparent parameter* rather than hard-coding either pure rules or pure consequence-search.

**Strong claim**: This unifies act-utilitarianism (depth=∞), rule-utilitarianism (depth=large), Kantian deontology (depth=0), virtue ethics (depth=variable, agent-shaped). Worth a standalone paper.

## §10 — Wise thinking is as much a choice as happiness

**Statement**: Wise thinking is as much of a choice as happiness is.

**Asymmetric-Standards #69 read**: This is dangerously close to victim-blaming if applied without nuance ("just choose to be wiser, lol"). The defensible reading is: at any given decision point, agents have *some* discretion over the meta-cognitive frame they apply, and the cumulative effect of those small frame-choices is what we call wisdom or its absence. Wisdom isn't *one* choice; it's the long-run distribution of small frame-choices.

**TI mapping**: Each frame-choice is a TJ event with measurable τ(s). Wise thinking = consistently high τ(s) over time. Happiness = consistently positive δ(MR) in the agent's own state. Both are products of cumulative intentional micro-acts, not single switches.

**Practical**: This is a high-leverage insight precisely *because* it doesn't require dramatic life change. It requires noticing the small choice points where you *could* apply a wiser frame and choosing to do so. The Mendi-protocol parallel: small repeated TJ injections compound, the same way 10 minutes of daily attention to wise framing compounds.
