## 5. Why Three Powers Specifically (Not Two or Four)

The framework's structural reasoning:

### 5.1 Two powers would correspond to a two-generation theory

A two-generation Standard Model would have only electron and muon generations. The gravitational coupling product would have two powers. But empirically there are three generations (URB #703 confirmed), so two-power scaling is structurally wrong.

### 5.2 Four powers would require a fourth generation

A four-generation Standard Model would have a (hypothetical) fourth-generation lepton. The gravitational coupling product would have four powers. Empirically no fourth generation is observed (LEP precision data), so four-power scaling is also structurally wrong.

### 5.3 Three powers is the unique generation-count match

The three-power suppression is the **unique structural match** to the empirically observed three-generation Standard Model. This is the framework's prediction: **any change in the SM generation count would change the power scaling correspondingly**, providing a falsifiable test.
