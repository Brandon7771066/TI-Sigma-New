## 2. What the 64D GILE Matrix Is

The 64-dimensional GILE Matrix is the framework's proposed **state-space structure** for fully encoding an agent's GILE state. The "64" comes from the structural product of the framework's foundational dimensional counts.

**Original proposal (under old Love-Existence-Environment three-axis architecture)**:
- 4 GILE dimensions (G, I, L, E)
- × 4 HEM dimensions (Holistic Existence Matrix sub-dimensions)
- × 4 BOK substrate states (4+4 = 8 → 4 by Dirac decomposition)
- = 4³ = 64

This gave a 4 × 4 × 4 = 64-dimensional state space, indexed by (GILE_dim, HEM_dim, BOK_state).

**Status under old architecture**: structurally proposed but never fully derived; the 4 HEM sub-dimensions were not crisply specified; the 4 BOK states were ambiguous (4 vs 4+4=8 reduction was unclear).
