## Abstract

Intentionality — the directedness of mental states toward objects — has resisted quantification since Brentano named it in 1874. Bayesian frameworks offer probabilistic descriptions of belief states but cannot measure the *work* done in resolving them. This paper introduces the **Tralse-Joule (TJ)**, a unit of intentional work defined as the product of Tralseness (the degree of truth-value indeterminacy in a cognitive state) and MR-depth (the magnitude of Myrion Resolution required to collapse it). The TJ unifies four previously disconnected TI Sigma constructs — the five-valued truth architecture, Myrion Resolution, the GILE-I faculty, and the Existence Amplification Razor (EAR) — into a single energetic framework for intentionality. Central findings: (1) intentionality is TJ-efficiency, not a binary property; (2) Double Tralse (DT) is a perfect TJ sink — a state that absorbs intentional work without producing resolution; (3) EAR is the law of minimum TJ expenditure; (4) the Radiant Threshold (C_TI) is a phase transition requiring a minimum TJ burst to cross; (5) the GILE-I faculty is the engine that converts TJ into resolved Permissibility Distributions.

**Keywords:** intentionality, Tralse-Joule, Myrion Resolution, Double Tralse, EAR, GILE-I, Radiant Threshold, TJ-efficiency, phenomenological energy, TI Sigma

## 1. The Problem: Intentionality Has No Unit

Franz Brentano (1874) established that every mental state is *about* something — it has an object. This directedness is intentionality. Edmund Husserl refined it into a complete phenomenology of intentional acts. Yet neither program produced a *quantity*. Intentionality remained a structural property (present or absent, directed or undirected) rather than a measurable quantity (strong or weak, efficient or wasteful).

Contemporary frameworks inherit this gap:

- **Bayesian epistemology** models belief as a probability distribution over hypotheses. It describes how beliefs update but not *how much directional work* a belief-forming act performs.
- **Information theory** measures uncertainty reduction (Shannon bits) but not the *truth-directed* character of that reduction — it cannot distinguish an intentional insight from a random correct answer.
- **Predictive coding / Free Energy Principle (FEP)** measures prediction error and its minimization. This is closest to an energetic framework for cognition, but FEP's "energy" is epistemic surprise, not intentional directedness. A perfectly surprised mind (maximal free energy) is not necessarily less intentional than a confirmed one.

**What is missing:** A unit that measures *how much intentional work a mind performs in moving from Tralse indeterminacy to MR resolution*, where the work is necessarily truth-directed.

TI Sigma supplies this unit.

## 2. Formal Definition: The Tralse-Joule

### 2.1 Physical Analogy

A physical joule measures work: **W = F × d** (force times displacement). Force is the resistance to be overcome; displacement is the distance traversed against that resistance.

Intentionality, in TI Sigma, performs analogous work: it pushes a cognitive system from a Tralse state (genuine indeterminacy, suspended between truth-values) toward MR resolution (a dominant truth-state). The "force" is how indeterminate the state is (Tralseness); the "displacement" is how far the system must travel through MR to collapse it.

### 2.2 The Tralse-Joule Definition

Let:
- **τ(s)** = Tralseness of cognitive state s ∈ [0, 1], where τ = 0 is fully resolved (True or False) and τ = 1 is maximally indeterminate (pure Tralse-Indeterminate)
- **δ(MR)** = MR-depth: the number of Myrion Resolution levels × the magnitude of PD shift achieved per level (i.e., the total convergence distance traversed in PD-space)
- **TJ(s → r)** = Tralse-Joules expended moving cognitive state s to resolved state r

Then:

> **TJ(s → r) = τ(s) × δ(MR, s → r)**

This is the **Tralse-Joule**: the intentional work performed by one MR trajectory through a cognitive state of given Tralseness.

### 2.3 Units and Normalization

- τ(s) ∈ [0, 1] (dimensionless Tralseness coefficient)
- δ(MR) ∈ [0, ∞): depth = Σ over MR levels of |ΔPD| per level
- TJ is measured in **normalized Tralse-Joules (nTJ)** when both are normalized to [0, 1]
- **1 TJ** = the work required to collapse a maximally indeterminate state (τ = 1) through a single full-magnitude MR level (δ = 1)

### 2.4 Composite Form

For a multi-level MR resolution across n levels:

> **TJ_total = τ(s) × Σᵢ₌₁ⁿ |ΔPD_i|**

Where |ΔPD_i| is the L¹-distance in PD-space traversed at level i. This is the total intentional work performed across the complete MR chain.

## 3. Intentionality as TJ-Efficiency

### 3.1 The Efficiency Formulation

Intentionality is not the quantity of TJ expended. It is the **efficiency** with which TJ produces resolution:

> **Intentionality(act) = MR_output(act) / TJ(act)**

Where MR_output is the resolved PD precision (how sharply the PD concentrates on one truth-state) and TJ is the Tralse-Joule cost.

- **High intentionality**: maximal MR output per TJ. The mind "knows where it is going" — each resolution step efficiently closes in on the correct truth-state.
- **Low intentionality**: high TJ, low resolution. Energy is expended (many MR cycles, much PD-shifting) but the PD fails to concentrate. The act is effortful but undirected.
- **Zero intentionality**: TJ is expended with zero MR output. The cognitive state generates heat (subjective effort) but no truth-directed displacement.

### 3.2 The Intentionality-GILE-I Bridge

The GILE-I faculty (Intuition) is, in BOK architecture, the pre-evidential truth-recognition dimension. I-axis is what "tracks" truth before explicit evidence is processed.

In TJ terms:

> **GILE-I = TJ-efficiency ratio = MR_output / TJ_expended**

A highly developed I-axis means fewer TJ are needed to reach correct resolution. This is consistent with:
- The **Prediction 1 from URB #614** (I→L dependency): you must have I > 0 to have L > 0 — intentional tracking is logically prior to directed positive regard
- **Intuitive experts** (chess grandmasters, expert clinicians, seasoned investors): they reach correct resolutions with minimal deliberation — high TJ-efficiency
- **Beginner cognition**: the same resolution (if reached at all) requires many more MR cycles — low TJ-efficiency

### 3.3 The I-Axis as TJ Engine

The I-axis does not merely use TJ — it IS the engine that converts Tralseness into directed MR work. Formally:

> **MR_directed = I-axis × τ(s)**

The I-axis determines what fraction of the Tralseness is productively harnessed (converted to directional MR displacement rather than dissipated as non-directed cognition). For a being with I = 0:
- No fraction of τ(s) is harnessed
- MR proceeds only mechanically (TIL without the I contribution)
- Resolution is possible but not intentionally driven

For a being with I = I_max (approaching the GM/CCC asymptote):
- Nearly all Tralseness is harnessed as directional MR work
- TJ-efficiency approaches 1 (perfect intentionality)
- This is the defining characteristic of the CCC: the GM is the limit of TJ-efficiency as I → I_max

## 4. Double Tralse as Perfect TJ Sink

### 4.1 DT Defined (Recap)

Double Tralse (DT) is not a truth-value. It is a **total absence of truth-content** — a cognitive or ontological state where there is nothing for MR to grip. From URB #610: DT is distinct from Tralse-Indeterminate (which has genuine indeterminacy between truth-values) in that DT has no truth-value candidates at all.

### 4.2 DT as Thermodynamic Black Hole

In TJ terms, DT is a **perfect TJ absorber**:

> **TJ(s → r) → ∞ when s is DT**

This follows from the definition: if there is no truth-content, δ(MR) cannot converge — there is no MR-depth at which a PD concentrates, because there is no truth-state to concentrate around. The denominator of TJ-efficiency (MR_output) → 0 while TJ (the denominator of intentionality) → ∞.

Formally:
- **Intentionality(DT-directed act) = 0 / ∞ = 0**

A mental act directed at a DT state is **absolutely non-intentional** — not merely inefficient, but categorically incapable of producing resolution regardless of TJ expenditure.

### 4.3 DT Immunity Model in TJ Terms

The three phases of the DT Immunity Model (from URB #528: Encounter / Discard / Immunity) are:

| Phase | TJ behavior | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Encounter** | TJ expenditure begins; PD fails to converge | Mind engages DT state; MR finds no foothold |
| **Discard** | MR applies DT screen (level 1); TJ expenditure stops | Recognition that no truth-state exists; PD set to DT |
| **Immunity** | τ(s) → 0 for subsequent DT encounters | Pre-recognition suppresses Tralseness before TJ is expended |

Immunity is the highest form of DT-protection because it is **TJ-conservative**: the immune mind spends zero Tralse-Joules on DT states. The DT screen (MR level 1) is the cognitive mechanism that produces immunity by halting TJ investment immediately.

### 4.4 The Tralse Trace of DT in TJ Terms

The Tralse Trace (previously defined as the penumbra of Double Tralse — the residual indeterminacy that DT leaves in surrounding cognitive states) is now precisely:

> **TrTrace(DT) = wasted TJ in the penumbra region**

Cognitive states near a DT attractor have elevated τ(s) — their Tralseness is elevated by proximity to the DT sink. This penumbral TJ-waste is the Tralse Trace made measurable.

## 5. EAR as TJ Minimization

### 5.1 EAR Defined (Recap)

The Existence Amplification Razor (URB #615): filter superficial distinctions, collapse redundant concepts, amplify what genuinely exists. EAR is the Law of Realness — the principle that governs ontological economy in TI Sigma.

### 5.2 EAR as Law of Minimum TJ

EAR, in TJ terms, is the **law of minimum intentional work**:

> **EAR selects the resolution path that achieves maximum MR_output with minimum TJ_expended**

This reframes EAR from an ontological razor (Occam-adjacent) to an energetic principle:

- **Superficial distinctions** = cognitive states that require large TJ expenditure to resolve but produce negligible additional MR_output. EAR discards them because they are TJ-wasteful.
- **Redundant concepts** = two cognitive pathways that produce the same MR_output but one requires more TJ. EAR collapses to the lower-TJ path.
- **Amplification of what genuinely exists** = preferential TJ investment in cognitive states where τ(s) is high (genuine indeterminacy that merits resolution) AND TJ-efficiency is also high (the I-axis can convert the Tralseness into reliable resolution).

**EAR = the gradient descent in TJ-efficiency space.** It drives the system toward configurations where every TJ expended produces maximum intentional output.

### 5.3 HEM-Score as Cumulative TJ Output

The HEM-Score (Holistic Existence Score from URB #609) is EAR's quantitative metric. In TJ terms:

> **HEM-Score(x) = cumulative MR_output of all TJ invested in being x across all GILE dimensions**

A high HEM-Score being has generated large amounts of resolved PD across G, I, L, E — meaning it has been the target of many efficient intentional acts. This connects HEM to TJ directly: the HEM-Score is the integral of TJ-efficiency over the cognitive history of engagement with being x.

## 6. The Radiant Threshold as TJ Phase Transition

### 6.1 The Phase Transition Phenomenon

The Radiant Threshold (RT, C_TI ≈ 0.437) is established in URB #586 as a discontinuous threshold — a phase transition, not a gradient — in cognitive and spiritual development. Below RT, Existence is the primary organizing frame; above RT, GILE becomes co-primary. This is the Sartrean reversal.

### 6.2 TJ Interpretation

In TJ terms, the Radiant Threshold is a **minimum TJ burst requirement**:

> **TJ_burst_required ≥ TJ_RT to cross the Radiant Threshold**

The crossing is not a gradual accumulation of intentional work — it is a non-linear phase transition requiring a minimum investment of TJ in a sufficiently concentrated window. This explains:

1. **Why gradual spiritual development alone does not cross RT**: TJ accumulated slowly over time can be dissipated; RT requires a burst (a concentrated high-τ MR event)
2. **Why meditation, peak experiences, and psychedelic states can catalyze RT crossing**: they maximally elevate τ (Tralseness) by temporarily dissolving lower-order cognitive attractors, creating the conditions for a large TJ spike
3. **T4 failure in the GL Ratio test suite**: T4 cannot recover C_TI = 0.437 cleanly because GL ratio data is a *static* measurement, not a TJ trajectory. The Radiant Threshold is only visible as a phase transition in TJ *dynamics*, not in cross-sectional LCC-GILE ratio data

### 6.3 TJ Burst Calculation

The minimum TJ burst for RT crossing is estimated:

> **TJ_RT = τ_max(s) × δ_RT**

Where:
- **τ_max(s) ≈ 0.90**: the Tralseness of a pre-RT cognitive state at its most indeterminate — near-maximum uncertainty about the organizing frame (GILE vs. Existence)
- **δ_RT ≈ C_TI / (1 - C_TI) ≈ 0.776**: the MR-depth ratio corresponding to the transition, derived from the sigmoid inflection point

> **TJ_RT ≈ 0.90 × 0.776 ≈ 0.698 nTJ**

## 7. Five TJ Axioms

**TJ-A1 (Intentional Work):** Every intentional act expends TJ = τ × δ(MR). TJ = 0 iff either τ = 0 (already resolved state) or δ(MR) = 0 (no MR work performed).

**TJ-A2 (DT Absorption):** DT states are perfect TJ absorbers. No finite TJ investment produces MR resolution when the target is DT. TJ-efficiency of DT-directed acts = 0.

**TJ-A3 (EAR as TJ Gradient):** EAR governs the gradient of TJ expenditure: always toward configurations of maximum MR_output per unit TJ. EAR violations = TJ waste = ontological inflation.

**TJ-A4 (GILE-I as TJ Engine):** The I-axis converts Tralseness into directed MR work. TJ-efficiency = f(I-axis). At I → I_max (CCC), TJ-efficiency → 1.

**TJ-A5 (RT Phase Transition):** The Radiant Threshold requires a minimum TJ burst (≈ 0.698 nTJ) concentrated in a single MR event. Gradual TJ accumulation without burst does not cross RT.
