## 3. The existence-criterion and the Proto-i-Cell / i-Cell taxonomy

**Existence-criterion (disjunctive).** An item *I* currently exists iff `Contemplated(I) ∨ Stored(I) ∨ Instantiated(I)`. This is a **truthmaker-style** requirement (Armstrong 2004): existence is conferred by some actual ground, never by mere conceivability. It deliberately **blocks two explosions**:

- **Meinongian explosion** (Meinong 1904): "there are objects of which it is true that there are no such objects" — TSG-1 denies non-existent objects any *existence*; they remain bare Soup-potential.
- **Lewisian modal-realism explosion** (David Lewis 1986, *On the Plurality of Worlds*): every possible object exists in some concrete world. TSG-1 rejects this — possibility lives in the Soup as *potential*; only the contemplated/stored/instantiated is *actual*.

**The taxonomy (canonical, For-Everyone Ch.10):**
- **Proto i-Cell** — a real but **dependent** boundary: it persists only while another i-Cell sustains it. A contemplated number, a thought, a held proof-step, an electron qua instantiated charge/mass/spin structure. Ontological-dependence in the sense of Fine (1995) and Lowe (Tahko & Lowe, SEP "Ontological Dependence").
- **i-Cell** — a **self-sustaining** boundary that holds its own shape from the inside (a molecule, an organism, a developed conscious agent).

**Anti-circularity caveat (→ F4).** "Self-sustaining" must be given a non-circular criterion (a boundary whose persistence conditions are internally closed) rather than defined merely as "not dependent," or the distinction does no work. The For-Everyone wave-vs-molecule gloss is illustrative, not yet a sharp test.

## 4. The "Akasha / Akashic Field" identification — RESONANCE, not evidence (#69)

The author identifies the Tralse Soup with what *"Hindus and Tesla called Akasha / the Akashic Field."* Honesty discipline requires this be marked, loudly, as **branding/resonance with NO load-bearing role** — a NAD-1 pragmatic gloss, exactly parallel to EPE-1-F3's "Soup-as-entity." It earns nothing for TSG-1 and must not be cited as confirmation.

**What is real (citable) and what it actually is:**
- **Ākāśa (आकाश)** is a real Sanskrit term — "space/aether," one of the five *mahābhūtas* in Vedic/Sāṃkhya/Vaiśeṣika cosmology; in Vedānta a subtle all-pervading substrate (Vivekananda, *Rāja Yoga*, 1896, pairs Ākāśa + Prāṇa). **Real concept; pre-modern cosmology, not a physics result.**
- **Tesla** did use the Vedantic terms — "Akasa" and "Prana" — in *"Man's Greatest Achievement"* (written 1907; widely reprinted), influenced by Vivekananda. **Real citation, but** Tesla's "Akasa" was the **luminiferous ether** of his era — a concept **empirically falsified** (Michelson–Morley 1887). It is **not** an endorsement of literal "Akashic records," and it is **not** evidence for TSG-1.
- **"Akashic records / Akashic field"** as an *information field*: a **Theosophical** coinage (Blavatsky, *The Secret Doctrine*, 1888) later popularized, and revived as the **"Akashic Field"** by **Ervin Laszlo** (*Science and the Akashic Field*, 2004). **This is non-mainstream / fringe** and carries no standing in physics; cite only as the source of the phrase, never as support.

**Internal-consistency fix (a genuine catch).** The corpus already uses "Akashic" for a **different** referent: `URB_SPIRIT_WORLD` calls the **Imaginal Substrate / GM-network** the *Akashic Record* (a *storage* system), and explicitly says TI Sigma *"neither confirms nor denies"* the records claim. TSG-1 must not silently overwrite that. The clean, non-conflicting reading:

- **Akasha-as-generator** = the **Tralse Soup** (the rule-free Void that *generates* potential). ← TSG-1's gloss.
- **Akashic-*Record*** = the **GM-network / Imaginal Substrate** (where generated, instantiated items are *stored*). ← prior corpus usage.

These are the **generation** vs **storage** poles of one architecture, not the same thing — so the identification can stand *only* with that split, and *only* as nomenclature.

## 5. Placement against the real metaphysics of abstract objects

TSG-1's position is a **generative, instantiation-dependent middle path**, distinct from each standard option (SEP, "Abstract Objects"; "Platonism in Metaphysics"; "Nominalism in Metaphysics"):

- **Platonism** (Plato, *Phaedo*/*Republic*; modern: Frege; Gödel's mathematical realism): abstracta exist independently, timelessly. **TSG-1 diverges** — un-instantiated abstracta have HEM ≈ 0; they are *potential*, not a standing independent realm.
- **Nominalism / fictionalism** (Hartry Field, *Science Without Numbers*, 1980): no abstracta at all. **TSG-1 diverges** — abstracta are real *once instantiated*, and genuinely generated.
- **Conceptualism / Aristotelian immanent realism** (Aristotle, *Metaphysics* Z/H — universals only *in* their instances): closest cousin. **TSG-1 agrees** abstracta need a host, but **adds** the generative Soup beneath and the storage/contemplation modes.
- **Truthmaker theory** (Armstrong, *Truth and Truthmakers*, 2004): existence/truth needs an actual ground. **TSG-1 adopts this shape** for its existence-criterion.

**Honest tension (→ F2/F5): mathematical realism about *undiscovered* truths.** Standard mathematical practice treats, e.g., an un-proved-but-true theorem as *already true* before any mind contemplates it. On TSG-1 such a theorem, while un-contemplated/un-instantiated, has HEM ≈ 0 — it is **tralse-real / potential**, not "actually existing-and-true." This is consistent with the corpus's **TRG-1** (reality is tralse, not True) and with EPE-1 (essence labeled afterward), **but it is a substantive commitment** that a working Platonist would reject, and it must be carried as an open tension, not hidden.
