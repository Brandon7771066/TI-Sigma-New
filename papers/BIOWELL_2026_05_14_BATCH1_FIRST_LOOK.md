The two declines are smaller in magnitude than the three improvements, and Hormonal activity dropped from "Ideal" (>79%) to "Normal" rather than into "Low" territory, while Physical activity is just below the Normal threshold.

**Net read:** the lifestyle profile became substantially more *balanced* (less peaky, fewer floor-level scores) even though the unweighted mean of the six axes is roughly the same (~57 → ~67, a +10 pp improvement in mean). This pattern — three big climbs from the floor, two modest drops from elevated values — is exactly what a regression-to-balance interpretation predicts.
