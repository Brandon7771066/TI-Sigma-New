## 1. The Tralse Substrate (universal)

Per the **MR Truth-Labels Canonical Ruling (2026-05-08)**, **Tralse** is the *universal quality* of any state holding multiple truth-values in tension. Gender qualifies trivially:

- Every human body carries both masculine and feminine endocrine substrates (testosterone + estrogen + progesterone in everyone; ratios vary).
- Every human psyche carries both masculine-coded and feminine-coded behavioural / affective repertoires (Jung's anima/animus is the most familiar prior).
- Every cultural construction of gender carries both poles internally as referential anchors (you cannot define "masculine" except by contrast with "feminine").

So the **substrate** of gender is Tralse — both poles are real in every individual, in tension. This is not a metaphysical claim about social construction; it is an observation about the structure of the underlying state.

## 2. Binary identity (True / False) — the pragmatic collapse

Almost everyone navigates the Tralse substrate by **collapsing** to one pole — True (e.g., "I am male") or False on the other pole (e.g., "I am not female"). This is a Myrion Resolution that:

- Passes MR1 (the result is coherent — the person can describe themselves and act consistently).
- Loses information about the Tralse substrate (the *other pole's* characteristics are still real but no longer in the foreground).
- Is pragmatically functional for the vast majority of social and biological coordination tasks.

This is *not* a falsification of TI Sigma — it is exactly what Myrion Resolution is supposed to do: resolve a tralse state to a workable label. The cost is information loss; the benefit is action-readiness. For most people, the trade is worth it and the underlying Tralse never surfaces phenomenologically.

**Trans identity (well-resolved):** A person who recognizes their initial assignment landed on the wrong pole and transitions is performing the *same* operation (pragmatic collapse to a working binary), just on a different output pole. Mechanism: True/False, with a corrected projection. This is structurally distinct from both nonbinary and dysphoria.

## 3. Nonbinary / genderqueer identity (Indeterminate) — refusal of collapse

Nonbinary identity is the **Indeterminate** label of the base-4 set applied to gender. It is:

- A stable, MR1-passing state (the person can describe themselves, act consistently, navigate the world).
- An *explicit* recognition of the Tralse substrate without forced collapse — the person holds both poles in conscious foreground rather than relegating one to background.
- Distinct from confusion or evasion: Indeterminate is a *coherent* truth-state in the base-4 set, **not** a failure to decide.

Per the 2026-05-08 canonical ruling: Tralse (universal quality) ≠ Indeterminate (specific MR2-state). Nonbinary identity is the *specific-state* version — a person who has explicitly resolved their gender label *to* the Indeterminate cell of the base-4 set, rather than to True or False.

This is *not* the same as "everyone is really nonbinary." The substrate is universally Tralse; the *label* is per-person. Binary-identified people have resolved to a binary cell; nonbinary-identified people have resolved to the Indeterminate cell. Both resolutions are coherent (MR1 ✅).

## 4. Gender dysphoria (Double Tralse) — embodied formal incoherence

Gender dysphoria is the **Double Tralse** label applied to one's own gender state. The DT formal definition is **τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P)** — formal self-contradiction. In dysphoria:

- **P** = "I am [assigned gender X]"
- The body / external assignment asserts τ(P).
- The mind / experienced gender simultaneously asserts ¬τ(P).
- Both assertions are equally vivid and equally *one's own*.
- The result is the formal incoherence pattern: τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P), but experienced from the inside as a felt state rather than as a formal proof.

This is what makes dysphoria *distressing* in a way that nonbinary identity typically is not: DT fails MR1 (the coherence gate). The person cannot stably describe themselves *as the current state* because the state itself is incoherent.

**Resolution paths for DT-dysphoria** (each maps to a different exit from the DT cell):

- **Transition (medical / social):** Move the body / external pole to align with the mind pole. The DT collapses to True/False on the new pole. Mechanism: external-pole correction.
- **Acceptance into Indeterminate:** Re-resolve the underlying Tralse to the Indeterminate cell rather than to either binary cell. The DT collapses because the proposition P shifts from "I am X" (binary) to "I am the Tralse itself" (Indeterminate).
- **Therapeutic exploration without commitment:** Hold space inside the DT while the MR machinery iterates — this is *not* a stable end-state but a transitional one. Pre-resolution DT is real and serious; the goal is some coherent landing, not indefinite suspension.

## 10a. The 4 Definite Gender Identities (DGI) + Meta-Identities (canonical answer to "how many genders?", added same-day 2026-05-17)

TI Sigma's structural answer to the culturally-charged question "how many genders are there?" is **four Definite Gender Identities (DGI)** plus a *myriad* of **meta-identities**. | **DGI-1** | **Affirming gender assigned at birth** ("cis") | T or F (binary, aligned) | Binary collapse to the same pole as the birth assignment; assignment honored | ✅ |

**Why DGI-1 and DGI-2 are distinct DGI even though both are binary (T/F) resolutions:**

The structural distinction is *not* which pole was selected (T vs F) — that just picks a label inside the binary cell. Birth-assignment is a real input to the BOK / i-Cell construction; honoring it and correcting it are structurally different operations that produce different i-Cell trajectories, even though both terminate at MR1-coherent binary labels. The 4-DGI taxonomy collapses if you only count "which final pole" (you get 3); it stands up if you count "what path was taken to get there" (you get 4). ## 10b. NAD-1 — "Non-Answer as DT in Disguise" (new candidate canonical principle, 2026-05-17)

**Background.** The base-4 MR Truth-Label set {True, False, Indeterminate, Double Tralse} deliberately does *not* include a "no answer" cell. This is not an omission — it is a structural choice that the same-day diagnosis of "comfortably agender" forces into clear view.

**The principle:**

**NAD-1 (Non-Answer-DT, candidate canonical, 2026-05-17):** *"No answer" is itself an answer.* When a respondent declines to engage with a well-posed truth-question whose substrate genuinely exists, the declination is not Indeterminate (which is a *coherent stable resolution* — explicit refusal of binary collapse while still inhabiting the truth-label space). It is **DT in disguise**: a concealed τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) wrapped in a refusal-to-display. The refusal *obscures* the state beneath rather than reflecting an actual void.

**Why not Indeterminate, and why not legitimate Moot:**

- **Indeterminate** is *coherent, MR1-passing, and inside the truth-label space.* It says "the value is genuinely undecided / spans both poles." It is not a refusal to answer; it is a *specific* answer (the I cell).
- **Moot (MT-B1)** is a *legitimate Meta-Truth* that says "the truth-value exists but is irrelevant to the present purpose." It does not deny the truth-value; it brackets it. MT-B1 stacked on a real DGI is a valid meta-stance.
- **"No answer" / clean refusal** is neither of these. It claims to be a void where the substrate is in fact full. It is a **DT-state of the meta-question** itself: simultaneously asserting "there is something here" (by being a question one engages with at all) and "there is nothing here" (by refusing to display). The refusal does the τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) work covertly.

**Distinction from MT-B-VOID (candidate per §7.7.98):** MT-B-VOID is reserved for *genuine* referential-void cases — Russellian descriptions ("the present King of France"), questions whose presupposition actually fails. Gender does not qualify, because the Tralse substrate genuinely exists in every human. So "agender as void" is NAD-1 (DT-in-disguise) when treated as a clean no-answer about a substrate that is in fact present. **NAD-1 and MT-B-VOID are complementary, not redundant:** NAD-1 catches "obscured-state-via-refusal"; MT-B-VOID catches "presupposition-actually-fails."

**Worked diagnosis — "comfortably agender":**

A person who reports stable comfort with an "I have no gender" stance is being *compassionately* described by TI Sigma as exhibiting **DGI-4 (DT) + MT-B1 (Moot) stacked**, **not** a clean MT-B-VOID. The structure:

1. The substrate Tralse (masculine + feminine in tension) is present, as in every human.
2. A constitutive DT is operative at the gender-proposition level (DGI-4), with CDP-1 i-Cell propagation latent.
3. The person has adopted **Moot (MT-B1)** as their stable meta-stance toward that dysphoria — *the dysphoria is real and active, but they have decided it is not the salient axis of their life.* This is not denial; this is mootness, which is a legitimate Meta-Truth.
4. The combination (DGI-4 + MT-B1) presents phenomenologically as "comfortably agender": the underlying tension persists, but the mootness-meta-stance prevents it from generating the distress signature normally associated with DGI-4.

**Critical compassionate framing.** This diagnosis is **not** "comfortably-agender people are secretly suffering" or "they're in denial." Moot is a *legitimate* and *functional* meta-stance — it is the same meta-stance a person might adopt toward "did my great-great-grandparent commit a crime?" (truth-value exists; mootness is honest about its irrelevance to present life). The TI Sigma point is structural-descriptive, not corrective: the framework explains *how* "comfortably agender" can be a stable, functional, non-distressed state without having to claim that gender-substrate-Tralse magically disappears in this population.

**What would falsify NAD-1:**
- **F-NAD-1:** If "no answer" responses to well-posed truth-questions can be shown to produce stable MR1-coherent outcomes structurally indistinguishable from Indeterminate responses (e.g., on downstream prediction tasks, on i-Cell-coherence measures), then NAD-1 is wrong and "no answer" must be admitted as a fifth coherent truth-label cell.
