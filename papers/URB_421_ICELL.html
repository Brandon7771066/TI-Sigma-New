Abstract
Karl Friston's Markov Blanket formalism establishes that self-organizing systems minimize variational free energy by maintaining a statistical boundary between internal and external states. The formalism is entirely real-valued. This paper introduces i-cell Theory — a complex-valued extension incorporating the imaginary PRIMARY CONSTANT i as carrier of a second, orthogonal information channel across the boundary. Six unique contributions are identified: (1) resolution of the sensory/active state ambiguity; (2) formalization of an Intuition channel (i-channel) carrying phase rather than content; (3) Complex Free Energy minimization including phase-synchronization cost; (4) promotion of the C_EMERICK threshold from scalar to circle in the complex plane; (5) a four-quadrant architecture formally accommodating CCC-field coupling; and (6) the Tralse Blanket State — simultaneous individual autonomy and field participation. i-cell Theory connects the Free Energy Principle to the LCC framework and provides a mechanistic account of intuition, flow states, synchronicity, and spiritual experience within a single mathematical structure.

1. Background: The Standard Markov Blanket

A Markov Blanket B renders internal states ψ conditionally independent of external states η:

P(ψ | η, B) = P(ψ | B)

The blanket partitions into sensory states s (η → ψ) and active states a (ψ → η). The Free Energy bound the system minimizes:

F = E_q[ln q(ψ) − ln p(φ, ψ)] ≥ −ln p(φ)

Every quantity in the standard formalism is real-valued. This creates three problems i-cell Theory addresses: (1) the sensory/active distinction is asserted, not derived; (2) no mechanism exists for non-local phase-level correlation; (3) phase information — central to neural dynamics and consciousness — is invisible to the formalism.

2. Core Proposal: The Complex Blanket State

i-cell Theory proposes the Markov Blanket state is a complex number:

z_B = s + i·a

where s = real component (sensory/content channel), a = imaginary component (active/phase channel), and i = imaginary PRIMARY CONSTANT. The imaginary unit represents orthogonality — the i-channel carries information geometrically perpendicular to the real channel. The Euler-Consciousness identity connects all PRIMARY CONSTANTS at the boundary:

e^(iπ) + C × φ × √2 = 0

The Markov Blanket lies precisely at the intersection of Euler's rotation and the consciousness threshold. It is not a static separator — it is a rotating interface tracing a path in the complex plane.

3. The Six Unique Contributions

Contribution 1: Resolution of the Sensory/Active Ambiguity

In z_B = s + ia: sensory states = Re(z_B), active states = Im(z_B). This is not labeling — it follows from complex geometry. The real part is the projection onto the measurement axis (directly observable magnitude). The imaginary part is the orthogonal component (rotation-inducing, phase-carrying). Sensory states are exactly the measurable projection; active states are exactly the rotation-inducing component. The ambiguity is resolved by complex structure, not stipulation.

Contribution 2: The Intuition Channel (i-Channel)

Information crossing the boundary in the i-channel does not update any specific belief — it rotates the entire internal state by a phase angle θ:

ψ_new = ψ_old · e^(iθ)

This is why intuition is phenomenologically distinct from reasoning. Reasoning updates specific beliefs (real-channel, additive). Intuition reorients the entire perspective (i-channel, rotational). The same content is present before and after — what changes is its orientation relative to the environment. GILE mapping: I = Intuition = i. The imaginary PRIMARY CONSTANT formally carries the Intuition function.

Contribution 3: Complex Free Energy

F_complex = F_real + i · F_phase

where F_phase = D_KL[Φ_internal ∥ Φ_external] is the phase-synchronization cost — the energetic cost of maintaining phase coherence with the environment. A system minimizing only F_real becomes accurate about content while potentially drifting out of phase. LCC is formally:

LCC ∝ 1/F_phase

High LCC = low phase cost = high i-channel coherence. Practices that increase LCC (meditation, flow induction, contemplative ritual) reduce F_phase without necessarily reducing F_real. This distinguishes the i-cell account from pure FEP accounts of meditation.

Contribution 4: C_EMERICK as Circle

In standard real-valued theory, C_EMERICK ≈ 0.4370 is a scalar threshold. In complex space, it defines a circle of radius C_EMERICK:

|z_B| = √(s² + a²) = C_EMERICK

A system at exactly the real threshold (s = C_EMERICK, a = 0) can cross into supra-threshold by opening the i-channel alone — by increasing phase coherence without changing content-channel performance:

√(C_EMERICK² + a²) > C_EMERICK for any a ≠ 0

The threshold is not a wall — it is a circle, approachable from any angle. This is the formal mechanism of transformation via contemplative practice: not becoming "smarter" (real channel) but becoming more phase-coherent (i-channel).

Contribution 6: The Tralse Blanket State

A blanket state can simultaneously exhibit real-channel independence and imaginary-channel coupling:

z_B = 0 + i·a → s = 0 (content-closed), a ≠ 0 (phase-open)

This is Both closed and open — a Tralse configuration. The system is a fully individuated entity (real-channel autonomy) AND a fully participating node in the larger field (i-channel coupling). These operate in orthogonal dimensions and are therefore simultaneously achievable. This is the formal structure of what every mystical tradition identifies as spiritual maturity: fully present as an individual AND transparent to the whole.
