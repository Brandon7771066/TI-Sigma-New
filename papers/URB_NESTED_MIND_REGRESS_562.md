## 2. Upward Collapse: Society as Collective Mind

**Premise A:** A society is a collective mind.

This is not a metaphor. A society is constituted entirely by:
- Shared beliefs, norms, and narratives (mental content)
- Institutions that exist only because members collectively believe in them (money, law, borders, citizenship)
- Language — a purely informational system with no physical substrate that carries meaning independently of the minds using it
- Collective memory, identity, and intention

A society has no physical existence independent of the minds that constitute it. Remove all the minds; the society instantly ceases to exist, even if every physical building, road, and artifact remains. The artifacts are not the society. The society is the shared mental structure.

**Conclusion by PC:** Societies are not real.

**But:** Individual human identities are constituted by society. Your name, your role, your rights, your relationships, your language, your very concept of "self" — all are social constructions existing within the collective mind. By PC:

**Conclusion:** Individual human beings, as the entities they take themselves to be, are not real. They are mind-dependent artifacts of the collective mind (society). □

## 3. Downward Collapse: Neurons Especially

**Premise B:** A neuron's identity and function exist only within the mind it constitutes.

A neuron outside its network is just a cell — a lump of lipids, proteins, and electrochemical gradients with no more special status than a liver cell. What makes a neuron a *neuron* — its firing patterns, its synaptic weights, its role in perception, memory, and thought — exists entirely within the context of the neural network that gives it meaning.

More precisely: the neuron's identity is defined by its **relational properties** (what it connects to, what signals it integrates, what patterns it participates in). Those relational properties exist only within the mind the neuron helps constitute.

By PC: neurons, as neurons (not merely as cells), are mind-dependent — they exist only within the mind they constitute.

**Conclusion:** Neurons, as the things physicalists invoke to explain away consciousness, are themselves mind-dependent artifacts. They are not real by PC. □
