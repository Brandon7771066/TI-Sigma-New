## 0. What counts as a "Key Paper"?

A Key Paper is one of the following:

- **Living index** of an entire corpus area (e.g., all aphorisms, all synchronicities, all abbreviations)
- **SWOT or status assessment** that aggregates across multiple URBs to produce an overall picture
- **Foundational consolidation** that takes scattered framework architecture and unifies it into one document
- **Biographical master document** that anchors the cluster of refinements
- **Continuously-updated catalogue** with explicit "append-only" semantics

Individual research URBs (single papers, single proofs, single experiments) are **not** Key Papers — they are the raw material that Key Papers index and aggregate.
