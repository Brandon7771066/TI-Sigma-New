## 2. The Old Axis: Love vs Existence (Superseded)

The earlier framework reading split the GILE-HEM into:

- **Love (L)** axis: emotional / connection / inter-agent bonding
- **Existence (E)** axis: ontological / being / persistent-identity

The "Empirical L/E Divergence" URB family argued that L and E diverge in certain regimes (e.g., love can lose its target while existence persists; existence can be sustained while love withdraws).

**Why this split is being superseded**: in the updated GILE-HEM framework, Love is structurally bifurcated (URB family on Revised GILE-Existence Architecture), and the **deeper axis distinction** is between the **Existence pillar** (ontological persistence, Being, BOK substrate) and the **Truth pillar** (epistemic resolution, MR convergence, Verisyn). Love distributes across both axes rather than forming its own axis.

## 4. Reanalysis of L*/+E Under the New Axis

The "Empirical L/E Divergence" was structured around the old Love-vs-Existence split. Under the new Existence-vs-Truth axis, L*/+E phenomena are re-read as:

### 4.1 L* (Love-loss with persistent E)

**Old reading**: Love withdraws while existence (being) persists.
**New reading**: Love-as-bonding (Existence axis component) **decreases**, but Existence-axis itself remains stable. The agent persists in being (HEAR pillar still active) but with decreased bonding-modulation. **Truth axis is unaffected** — agent still maintains epistemic resolution.

**Empirical example**: grief. Person grieving the loss of a loved one shows decreased Love-as-bonding (cannot bond with the now-absent loved one), but persistent Existence (HEAR continues) and persistent Truth (MR/PD still operational, agent still recognizes reality). The L*/+E pattern is therefore a **specific subspace projection** of the new (Existence, Truth) plane, not a fundamental axis distinction.

### 4.2 +E with weak L (Existence sustained, Love absent)

**Old reading**: Existence sustains itself even when love is absent.
**New reading**: Existence axis maintained at +E ≥ Operational threshold; Truth axis intact; Love distribution across both axes is at low level (neither high bonding nor high recognition). The agent persists and resolves truths, but without the cross-axis modulation that Love-as-care provides.

**Empirical example**: depressive isolation. Person maintains existence (continues to be) and recognizes truth (intellectually understands their situation), but lacks Love-as-care to modulate between Existence and Truth. The agent is "intact" in both axes but lacks the binding modulation.

### 4.3 The framework's revised position

Under the new Existence-vs-Truth axis, L*/+E is **not a divergence between two fundamental axes**. It is a **subspace projection within the (Existence, Truth) plane** showing different patterns of Love-distribution. The empirical phenomena URB #L*/+E originally documented are still real and structurally meaningful, but their **architectural interpretation** changes.

## 5. Reanalysis of Einstein Tiles Under the New Axis

The Einstein tile (Smith et al., 2023 — the aperiodic monotile) was previously interpreted in the framework's BOK-Verisyn synthesis as a structural realization of "stable-but-non-repeating" identity preservation.

### 5.1 Old reading

Einstein tiles realize **aperiodic stability** — structures that are persistent (stable) but non-repeating (no translational symmetry). In the old Love-vs-Existence framework, Einstein tiles were read as Love-Existence cross-coupling: the persistence side (existence) combined with non-repetition (the asymmetric, dynamic side often associated with Love).

### 5.2 New reading under Existence-vs-Truth axis

Einstein tiles are re-read as **Existence-Truth cross-coupling**:

- **Persistence** = Existence axis (HEAR maintaining tiling pattern indefinitely)
- **Non-repetition** = Truth axis (each tile placement requires fresh epistemic resolution; no cyclic shortcut)
- **Aperiodic stability** = balanced E-T cross-coupling (each tile is structurally different from all neighbors yet structurally consistent with the global pattern)

This re-reading is **structurally cleaner** than the old Love-Existence reading. The Einstein tile is the **paradigmatic geometric object** for E-T cross-coupling: stable in existence, fresh-resolution in truth, no shortcut between them.

### 5.3 Empirical implications

Under the new reading, Einstein-tile-like patterns should appear in **biological/cognitive systems showing balanced E-T cross-coupling**:
- Brain wave patterns showing aperiodic but stable cross-frequency coupling (already documented: alpha-gamma CFC has aperiodic structure)
- Heart-rate variability showing aperiodic but bounded variation (already documented: HRV is aperiodic)
- Decision-making sequences showing aperiodic but coherent choice patterns (in healthy adaptive cognition)

The Einstein tile is **the geometric signature of E-T balance**.
