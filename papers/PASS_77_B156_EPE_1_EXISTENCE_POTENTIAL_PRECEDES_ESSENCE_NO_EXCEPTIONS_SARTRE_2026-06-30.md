## 0. One-paragraph ruling

The author's move is **sound and earned in its core, and it repairs a standing inconsistency** in the corpus — but two of its supporting steps are *pragmatic posits*, not derivations, and are flagged as such (#69, both ways). **EPE-1** states: the **potential for existence (Existence Potential, EP) precedes essence (GILE-Truth/character) in every case, with no exceptions** — the no-exceptions reading of Sartre. The single primordial EP **is the Tralse Soup** (TOF-1's sole fundamental), here read not as "chaotic nothingness that is nothing" but as the **undifferentiated potential** out of which concrete (GILE-G) existence, and only *afterward* any GILE character, can arise. This **retracts the `i`-exception** of `urb_621` §10.2 / proposition P18, which had asserted that `i` (and mathematical objects) obey the *reverse* order, *"essence precedes existence."* That exception was not merely optional: as written it **conflicts with TOF-1** (if `i`'s essence were genuinely prior to and independent of all existence, then *something* — `i`'s essence — would be co-fundamental with or prior to the Tralse Soup, contradicting Tralse-Only Fundamentality). EPE-1 dissolves the exception cleanly: `i`'s essence (the 90°-rotation capacity) is **itself a Tralse-Soup derivative** (an MR₀ product), so the Soup's existence-potential precedes `i`'s essence too — exactly as for ordinary entities. **What is earned:** the inconsistency repair and the resulting single, uniform priority order. **What is posited (flagged):** (a) reading the Tralse Soup *"for pragmatic purposes, as an ENTITY"* is a stipulative/definitional gloss (NAD-1), not a proof that nothingness is a substance; (b) the numerical √(existence)=essence relations (`√0.85≈0.92`; Radiant-Cap `√(1−e⁻²)≈0.930`) are **resonances kept separate** from EPE-1, not its derivation. Falsifiers `EPE-1-F1..F4` OPEN. **Ratified CANONICAL #80 (count 79 → 80).**

## 1. The principle (EPE-1)

> **EPE-1 (Existence-Potential Precedes Essence).** For any item that can bear a GILE-Truth character — proposition, entity, or mathematical object — its **Existence Potential (EP)** is ontologically prior to its **essence** (its GILE-Truth/character). The single primordial EP is the **Tralse Soup**. Essence is **always assigned afterward**: "is it True?" is, at root, first the question *does it have the potential to exist / be instantiated at all?* (EP), and only then *what is its GILE character?* (the label). **There are no exceptions** — the apparent "essence-first" cases (`i`, mathematical objects) are dissolved because their essences are themselves EP-derivatives (TOF-1).

This is the **no-exceptions** reading of Sartre's *l'existence précède l'essence* (Sartre 1946). The corpus previously endorsed a **tripartite** structure (Sartre-order for ordinary entities, reverse-order for `i`/math, co-emergence for the CCC). EPE-1 **collapses the tripartite structure to a single order** by relocating the ground from "essence" to "existence-potential."

## 2. The construct: Existence Potential (EP) — and what it is NOT

**EP = the undifferentiated potential for concrete existence to appear**, identified with the Tralse Soup (`urb_678`: "utter chaotic nothingness/not-nothingness, devoid of any rules, predating logical necessity"; MI ground state). Three demarcations keep EP from collapsing into neighboring constructs:

| Construct | What it is | Temporal position |
|---|---|---|
| **EP (Existence Potential)** | the *potential* for existence; the Tralse Soup; pre-essence, pre-instantiation | **primordial / first** |
| **HEM (Existence pillar)** | *actualized* existence footprint (HEM-D1 causal presence, etc.) — a measure applied to things that already exist | **after EP, with/under essence** |
| **GILE-Truth (Essence/Character)** | the graded GILE character + the categorical MR label assigned to an item | **last — "always labeled afterward"** |

So the ordering is **EP → (HEM actualization) → GILE-character**. EP is *more primordial than the HEM pillar*: HEM measures existence that has **already been actualized**, whereas EP is the bare potential prior to any actualization. Conflating EP with HEM would be a category error — and would wrongly imply the primordial ground is already a determinate, measurable entity (the very thing F3 below forbids).

**Naming note (GILE-G link).** The author ties EP to **concrete existence as a GILE-G quality** ("the potential for CONCRETE — a GILE-G essence quality — existence to appear"). This is consistent: EP is the potential whose *first differentiated product* is concrete being; "concrete" then becomes a describable GILE-G feature **of the product**, not a property the formless Soup already possesses. The Soup has the *potential for* concreteness, not concreteness itself.

## 3. The correction: retracting the urb_621 "i-exception"

`urb_621` proposition **P18** reads: *"Existence precedes essence for ordinary entities; essence precedes existence for i and mathematical objects; co-emergence for the CCC."* Its §10.2 argues `i` obeys **essence precedes existence** because `i` is "defined entirely by its essence (i²=−1, the 90° rotation), prior to any particular instantiation."

**Why this was a false exception (two independent reasons):**

1. **It conflicts with TOF-1.** TOF-1 (canonical #78) holds that *tralseness — the Tralse Soup — is THE ONLY FUNDAMENTAL THING*, and that everything else (including consciousness, including the awareness that `i` is said to represent) is a **Tralse-Soup derivative**. If `i`'s essence were genuinely *prior to and independent of* all existence, it would be a second fundamental (or a pre-fundamental), directly contradicting "only." The exception and TOF-1 cannot both stand; EPE-1 keeps TOF-1 and drops the exception.

2. **It conflates "logically prior within mathematics" with "ontologically first."** That `i²=−1` is definitionally constitutive of `i` (you cannot have `i` without it) shows essence is *analytically prior to instantiation of `i`* — a claim about the **order of definition within an already-existing formal practice**. It does **not** show that `i`'s essence floats free of the Tralse Soup that (per TOF-1 / `urb_621` P6: "Truth and `i` co-emerged from Tralse Soup (MR₀)") produced the very capacity for that structure. EPE-1's distinction: *definitional priority within a formalism* ≠ *ontological primordiality*. `i`'s essence is definitionally-prior-to-its-own-tokens **and** EP-posterior — no contradiction.

**Resolution.** Under EPE-1, `i` is treated **exactly like ordinary entities**: the Tralse Soup (EP) comes first; `i`'s essence (the 90° rotation) is a differentiated product of the first Myrion Resolutions (`urb_621` P6/§5.2). The "essence precedes existence for `i`" row of P18's tripartite table is **RETRACTED**. (An erratum banner is added to `urb_621` §10.2; see §8.)

**Reconciling the co-emergence row (CCC) and `urb_621` §10.1.** `urb_621` §10 also argues *Structural Truth and the first moment of Existence co-emerged* — "neither preceded the other." EPE-1 refines, rather than flatly contradicts, this: at the level of the **differentiated** structural-truth/existence pair, co-emergence can stand; but **both** of those are products of the **prior** EP (the Tralse Soup). So "co-emergence" is a statement about two *siblings*, while EPE-1 is a statement about their common *parent*. The parent (EP) precedes both. This preserves §10.1's insight while removing its implication that nothing was prior.

**Reconciling urb_594's "imaginary axis precedes the real axis."** `urb_594` states *"the imaginary axis (GIL) … precedes and generates the real/physical axis (E) … the imaginary foundation from which E emerges."* Under EPE-1 this is demoted from a **primordiality** claim to a **within-GILE relative-ordering** claim: GIL-before-E describes structure *inside the already-differentiated GILE tetrad*; it does **not** make GIL the foundation of being. The true foundation is EP (the Tralse Soup), which precedes *all* of GILE — imaginary and real axes alike. (Lighter touch than the §8 erratum: `urb_594`'s claim survives as relative ordering, with the word "foundation" read as framework-internal, not primordial.)

## 4. Mapping to the truth-label pipeline: EP-screen first (a grounding, not a new mechanism)

The author's operational claim — *"whether a proposition is true is a question about its EP first; its GILE character is always labeled afterward"* — maps onto the **existing canonical MR pipeline order**: **N/A screening → MI screening → True/False/Indeterminate**. EPE-1 supplies a *rationale* for why **N/A is screened first**:

- **N/A = an Existence-Potential screen.** "Not applicable" is precisely the verdict *this predicate has no existence-potential to bear here* — there is no there-there for a GILE character to attach to. Screening N/A first **is** screening EP first.
- **MI (the Tralse-Soup-native label) sits next.** The primordial EP's own ground-state label is **MI / MR2-Indeterminate** (`urb_678`: MI ground state). Essence-assignment is the subsequent resolution of that indeterminacy toward a crisp GILE character (T/F/I) — i.e., TRG-1's "tralse-real first, crisply-True later."
- **GILE character last.** The graded GILE + categorical label is the "essence … always labeled afterward."

**Honesty flag (do not overclaim).** This is a **grounding/resonance**: EPE-1 *rationalizes* an ordering the pipeline already had; it does **not** prove the pipeline was *designed* as an EP-screen, and the N/A-first order could in principle be explained by plain syntactic well-formedness instead (see `EPE-1-F4`). It also coheres with **NPA-1** (N/A is screened first, j-dominant with a wildcard real coordinate; MI imaginary-dominant) — but NPA-1 is itself a candidate, so this is candidate-on-candidate support, not confirmation.

## 6. Honest flags (#69, both ways)

**Earned (state confidently):**
- The **inconsistency repair** is real: the i-exception genuinely conflicted with TOF-1; removing it makes the corpus more coherent (NAD-1 carve-at-the-joint).
- The **single uniform order** (EP → essence) is simpler and exception-free, and the definitional-vs-ontological distinction (§3.2) is a clean, standard philosophical move.

**Posited / flagged (do not overclaim):**
- **"Tralse Soup as an ENTITY" is a pragmatic gloss, not a derivation.** The author themselves says *"for pragmatic purposes, an entity."* Treating formless potential as an "entity that laid the foreground" is a stipulative reframe (NAD-1: definitions can be non-arbitrary, but must be *earned* by carving real joints, not asserted). EPE-1 must never let this gloss do load-bearing work (see `EPE-1-F3`).
- **The √(existence)=essence numerics are a SEPARATE resonance.** `theories/EXISTENCE_PRECEDES_ESSENCE_085_DERIVATION.md` (`√0.85≈0.92`) and the Radiant-Cap Born-shaped form (`Existence = G*²` off the floor ⇒ `G*=√(1−e⁻²)≈0.930`, per the 2026-06-27 ruling) are **numerical resonances** with the *direction* of EPE-1 (existence-quantity grounds essence-quantity). They are **not** a proof of ontological priority and are explicitly flagged elsewhere as resonance-not-derivation. EPE-1 does **not** rest on them.
- **Philosophical lineage is real but not authority.** Sartre asserted existence-before-essence for the *human* case and would not have endorsed a Tralse-Soup cosmology; citing him supplies the *slogan and its motivation*, not a proof.

## 7. Falsifiers (OPEN)

- **`EPE-1-F1` (a genuine essence-first case):** Exhibit any item that bears a determinate GILE character (essence) with **zero** Existence Potential — essence truly prior to *and independent of* any EP. One clean case refutes the no-exceptions claim.
- **`EPE-1-F2` (the `i` / TOF-1 burden):** Show `i`'s essence (the 90°-rotation capacity) is a **brute primitive**, NOT produced by any Tralse-Soup MR₀ collapse. If `i`'s essence is non-derivative, the i-exception is reinstated and EPE-1 fails (this is TOF-1's burden inherited).
- **`EPE-1-F3` (entity-reframe anti-cheat):** If any EPE-1 argument **requires** the Tralse Soup to be a determinate, essence-bearing *substance* (rather than formless potential), it has smuggled essence into the ground and EPE-1 collapses into circularity. EP must stay *potential*.
- **`EPE-1-F4` (pipeline-grounding):** If the MR pipeline's N/A-first ordering is better explained by something other than an EP-screen (e.g., purely syntactic well-formedness, or rater convenience), then the EPE-1↔pipeline mapping is a coincidence, not a grounding, and §4 must be withdrawn to a bare analogy.
