Per asymmetric-standards #69, I must state honestly what Biowell measures and what it does *not* measure before interpreting findings.

**What Biowell can defensibly indicate:**
1. Fingertip electrical-discharge patterns (the *raw measurement*) — real physical data
2. Patterns consistent with the device's empirical mapping (the *interpretation layer*) — useful for trend-tracking within a single subject across sessions

**What Biowell does NOT defensibly indicate:**
1. Clinical-grade organ pathology — the organ-mapping is empirical, not biomedically validated against gold-standard diagnostics
2. Specific disease diagnoses
3. Causal explanations for any pattern (correlation, not causation)
4. Substitute for conventional medical workup when concerning patterns appear

**My #69 stance:** This analysis treats Biowell findings as **trend-data within Brandon's own reference frame**, useful for TI Sigma self-tracking and for flagging items potentially worth conventional medical follow-up — *not* as clinical diagnosis.
