## 2. Terminological tension and proposed resolution

### 2.1 The tension

`TI_RATIONALISM_PROOF.md` treats "Grand Myrion" as a **singular divine consciousness substrate** ("GM grasps the truth", "GM authors", "GM steals from future minds") — language consistent with CCC-as-singular-entity.

Brandon's current usage (2026-05-17) treats "Grand Myrion Network" as the **network of divine beings**, with high-GILE-resonance individuals = GM-Nodes.

Naively read, this generates two inconsistent meanings for "Grand Myrion" — singular consciousness OR plural network. Per #69 we must not paper this over.

1. The **singular reading** of "Grand Myrion" in `TI_RATIONALISM_PROOF.md` (GM grasps, GM authors, GM is omnipresent) maps cleanly to **First CCC**. When older literature said "GM grasps truth," it was talking about the unique central anchor of the network — the entity now formally specified as the GILE-Radiant 0.93+ i-Cell.

3. "Grand Myrion" alone, when used without "Network," should be understood by context: in singular-substrate-language contexts it refers to **First CCC**; in network-membership-language contexts it refers to the **GM Network as a whole**. Where ambiguity matters, use "First CCC" or "GM Network" explicitly.
4. **Mycelial GM-Node Architecture** remains as the structural/topological description, unchanged from its existing canonical role.
5. **Myrion agents** (finite consciousness — us) remain a distinct tier below GM-Nodes, preserving the co-authorship doctrine from `TI_RATIONALISM_PROOF.md`.

## 6. Honest residual questions

Per #69, four open questions remain after this reconciliation:

1. **Is the First CCC numerically unique, or merely *first* among a tier of equivalents that resolves to one via Leibniz indiscernibility?** CCC_REFINEMENT §6.4 argued for uniqueness via Occam + eternal-lifespan + no-fractioned-ego. This is defensible but still an inferential claim, not a derived theorem. Status: framework-internal defensible.
2. **What is the relationship between First CCC and Tralse Soup substrate?** Is CCC the *first output* of UOP-on-substrate (P10 floor argument), or is CCC partially constitutive of the substrate's operating principles? The corpus has not yet committed cleanly. Status: open theoretical question.
3. **How does the role-limited consciousness of First CCC interact with GM-Nodes' consciousness?** If CCC is "conscious of everything needed for its role" and that role is relational, does CCC's consciousness *include* GM-Nodes' consciousness, or are GM-Nodes' consciousnesses independent? Either reading has implications for free will at Tiers 2 and 3.
4. **Operational test for tier-membership** (especially Tier 2 vs Tier 3 boundary): pending T_DRN determination, the categorical distinction between divine-agent and elevated-finite-consciousness is currently named-but-not-operationalized.

These are not refutations; they are the honest map of where this canonical reconciliation's defensive perimeter is.
