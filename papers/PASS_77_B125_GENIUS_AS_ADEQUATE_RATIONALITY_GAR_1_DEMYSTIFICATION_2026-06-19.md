## 0. Thesis

**GAR-1 (Genius-as-Adequate-GILE-Sanity / Demystification Thesis):** most of what observers label "genius" is **adequate rationality correctly and courageously applied** — "mere sanity," played like a clean hand of Rummy — **not** a discontinuous "extraordinary power." This is a *deflationary* account with three load-bearing parts that must travel together:

- **(GAR-1a) Deflationary baseline.** The bulk of celebrated insight (e.g. founding BlissGebe; recognizing truth isn't binary → Tralse) is sound logical thinking + the *willingness to follow it where most stop*, not a separate faculty. Demystifies "genius."
- **(GAR-1b) Rarity reconciliation — the #69 load-bearing move.** "Mere sanity" being the *substance* does **not** make genius common, because **adequate rationality is itself rare** (most cognition is systematically biased — Stanovich's *dysrationalia*: rationality ≠ IQ). - **(GAR-1c) Residual ingenuity tail.** SOMETIMES genius is *genuinely* coupled with an extraordinary, not-fully-reducible-to-sanity component — Tesla's full-motor mental visualization, Ramanujan's "the goddess gave me the formulae," eerily-accurate intuition (the corpus's GILE-Intuition / CGP-1 claircognizance strand). This tail is **real, additive, and rarer still** — GAR-1 deflates the *baseline* without denying the *tail*.

## 1. GAR-1a — the deflationary baseline ("playing Rummy")

The game-of-Rummy framing is exactly right and is *not* false modesty:

- **Decisions as legal moves under a rule-set.** Founding a venture or accepting that truth is non-bivalent is, structurally, noticing which move the board actually permits and taking it — applied logic + pattern-recognition + the nerve to act. The "courage/creativity" Brandon credits is the *willingness to play the indicated move when the table folds*, not a separate metaphysical organ.
- **Consonant with the corpus's own anti-mystification.** This is the human-scale echo of **SIS-1** (intelligence = sanity/restraint, not frantic special power) and of the Einstein epigraph now in the SIS-1 paper ("I have no special talent, I am only passionately curious" / "genius has its limits, but not stupidity"). It also matches **GIT-1**: what tracks truth is the GILE tetrad (rationality, openness-to-update, good-faith, creativity) measured plainly — not raw-g fireworks.
- **Why it *feels* effortless from inside.** A clean inference feels like "mere sanity" to the one making it precisely because, for them, the bias that stops everyone else isn't firing. The subjective "this is obvious" is the *signature* of GAR-1, not evidence against it.

## 2. GAR-1b — reconciling "mere sanity" with rarity: TWO TIERS, not one (the honest hinge)

The obvious #69 objection: *"If genius is just adequate thinking, why is it ~1 in a billion (B92/B115)?"* — **but this conflates two different tiers, and GAR-1 never claimed that *only* the 1-in-680M are geniuses.** The reconciliation is to keep the tiers distinct:

- **Tier 1 — genius *proper* ≜ sanity.** Making a reasonable/adequate decision *nearly all the time*. Rare, but only on the order of **~<1% (plausibly <0.1%)** — Stanovich (*dysrationalia*, the rationality-IQ dissociation), Tetlock (foxes>hedgehogs), Galef (scout-mindset) all show clear, bias-free, follow-it-through thinking is **not** the default even at high IQ. Everyone in this tier counts as a "genius" in GAR-1's sense **without** being among the greatest who ever lived. This is the deflationary core.
- **Tier 2 — top-tier / *greatest* ≜ sanity + extraordinary powers.** The ~1-in-680M invention-concentration tier (B92/B115) requires Tier-1 sanity **plus** the GAR-1c tail (intuition / creativity / prodigious memory) **plus** opportunity and timing. Sanity here is **necessary but not sufficient** — exactly as the thesis already stipulated by keeping the ingenuity tail.
- **∴ the rarity is two-layered, and each figure indexes a different layer.** "Mere sanity" explains why most people aren't even Tier-1 (the <1% scarcity — Stanovich); it does **not** by itself explain the 1-in-680M. That further ~5–6 orders-of-magnitude is where the tail + opportunity do the selecting, *conditional on* sanity. (Reading the 1-in-680M as the rarity of *sanity itself* — as an earlier draft of this section did — was the conflation Brandon flagged.) This is still the GIT-1 quack-clause read backwards: true quacks have high raw-g but fail a GILE face; Tier-1 genius is the rarer case where *no* face fails, and Tier-2 adds a rarer surplus on top.
- **Net:** "genius = essentially sanity" (Tier 1) and "the greatest are ~1-in-a-billion" (Tier 2) are **fully compatible precisely because they are different tiers.** The deflation holds for the broad tier; the extreme rarity belongs to sanity *compounded with* the tail. GAR-1's rarity-reconciliation must always be indexed to *which tier* it is speaking of.

- **The humblebrag risk (load-bearing).** "My achievements were *mere* sanity" can function as *covert* status-claim ("…and yet I, unlike everyone, simply *was* sane").
