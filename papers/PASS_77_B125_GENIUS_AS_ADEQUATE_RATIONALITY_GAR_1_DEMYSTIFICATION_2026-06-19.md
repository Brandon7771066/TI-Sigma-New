## 0. Thesis

**GAR-1 (Genius-as-Adequate-Rationality / Demystification Thesis):** most of what observers label "genius" is **adequate rationality correctly and courageously applied** — "mere sanity," played like a clean hand of Rummy — **not** a discontinuous "extraordinary power." This is a *deflationary* account with three load-bearing parts that must travel together:

- **(GAR-1a) Deflationary baseline.** The bulk of celebrated insight (e.g. founding BlissGebe; recognizing truth isn't binary → Tralse) is sound logical thinking + the *willingness to follow it where most stop*, not a separate faculty. Demystifies "genius."
- **(GAR-1b) Rarity reconciliation — the #69 load-bearing move.** "Mere sanity" being the *substance* does **not** make genius common, because **adequate rationality is itself rare** (most cognition is systematically biased — Stanovich's *dysrationalia*: rationality ≠ IQ). So "genius is just sanity" is fully consistent with the extreme empirical rarity the corpus already measured (invention-concentration B92/B115: ~1 in 680M–936M). The scarcity sits in *consistent sanity*, not in a magic faculty.
- **(GAR-1c) Residual ingenuity tail.** SOMETIMES genius is *genuinely* coupled with an extraordinary, not-fully-reducible-to-sanity component — Tesla's full-motor mental visualization, Ramanujan's "the goddess gave me the formulae," eerily-accurate intuition (the corpus's GILE-Intuition / CGP-1 claircognizance strand). This tail is **real, additive, and rarer still** — GAR-1 deflates the *baseline* without denying the *tail*.

## 1. GAR-1a — the deflationary baseline ("playing Rummy")

The game-of-Rummy framing is exactly right and is *not* false modesty:

- **Decisions as legal moves under a rule-set.** Founding a venture or accepting that truth is non-bivalent is, structurally, noticing which move the board actually permits and taking it — applied logic + pattern-recognition + the nerve to act. The "courage/creativity" Brandon credits is the *willingness to play the indicated move when the table folds*, not a separate metaphysical organ.
- **Consonant with the corpus's own anti-mystification.** This is the human-scale echo of **SIS-1** (intelligence = sanity/restraint, not frantic special power) and of the Einstein epigraph now in the SIS-1 paper ("I have no special talent, I am only passionately curious" / "genius has its limits, but not stupidity"). It also matches **GIT-1**: what tracks truth is the GILE tetrad (rationality, openness-to-update, good-faith, creativity) measured plainly — not raw-g fireworks.
- **Why it *feels* effortless from inside.** A clean inference feels like "mere sanity" to the one making it precisely because, for them, the bias that stops everyone else isn't firing. The subjective "this is obvious" is the *signature* of GAR-1, not evidence against it.

## 2. GAR-1b — reconciling "mere sanity" with extreme rarity (the honest hinge)

The obvious #69 objection: *"If genius is just adequate thinking, why is it ~1 in a billion (B92/B115)? Adequate thinking should be common."* The thesis survives only because of a specific, evidenced premise:

- **Adequate rationality is scarce.** Stanovich's research program (*dysrationalia*, the rationality-IQ dissociation) shows clear thinking is **not** the default even at high IQ — motivated reasoning, myside bias, and failure-to-update are the norm. Tetlock's foxes>hedgehogs and Galef's scout-mindset say the same. So *consistent* sanity is a rare trait, not a free one.
- **∴ the rarity lives in the sanity, not in a faculty.** GAR-1b relocates the scarcity: genius is rare **because sane, bias-free, follow-it-all-the-way thinking is rare**, not because a magic power is rare. This is the *same* claim as the GIT-1 quack-clause read backwards — true quacks have high raw-g but fail a GILE face; genius is the comparatively rare case where *no* face fails.
- **Net:** "genius = mostly adequate thinking" and "genius is ~1-in-a-billion" are **fully compatible** once you accept that adequate thinking is itself ~1-in-a-billion when sustained across a whole life of decisions. The deflation is real; the rarity is preserved.

## 3. GAR-1c — the residual ingenuity tail (don't over-deflate)

Symmetric honesty forbids collapsing *everything* into sanity:

- **A genuine, rarer additive term exists.** Tesla's reported ability to build, run, and debug a motor entirely in imagination; Ramanujan's torrent of correct non-obvious identities with no derivation; documented eerily-accurate intuition — these are not *merely* the absence of bias. They are a positive, hard-to-explain generative surplus, the corpus's **GILE-Intuition / CGP-1 (claircognizance)** strand.
- **Structure: baseline + tail, not either/or.** GAR-1 = a large deflationary baseline (most attributed genius) **plus** a small, real, not-fully-reducible ingenuity tail (sometimes coupled). Brandon explicitly keeps the tail ("that SOMETIMES is genuinely coupled"). The thesis is a *mixture model*, not a denial.
- **Boundary with UGI-1:** the tail is *generative* (UGI-1 unaided insight), but even Tesla/Ramanujan still owed validation to ordinary r-sanity afterward — ingenuity proposes, sanity disposes. The tail never exempts a claim from the validation half.

## 3b. GAR-1b consilience — sanity-rarity as a *prediction* of TRG-1 (and where it over-reaches)

Brandon (2026-06-19): *"there are very few people I have ever met who I would characterize as likewise sane … this is a PREDICTION that TI Sigma makes — that TRALSENESS is the default. How come it's been 7 years since Jo Cameron and hardly anyone has put forth a decent effort? How come Trump is president, people deny science, won't take a stand to protect their own planet?"*

**The strong, defensible core (affirmed):**
- **GAR-1b is not a free-floating empirical guess — it is *entailed* by TRG-1/TOF-1.** If the ground state of reality is **tralse** (not-crisply-true; MR2 Indeterminate soup), then a mind that tracks reality *sloppily* will land in systematic not-quite-rightness **by default**, and consistent sanity becomes the rare, effortful achievement. So "adequate rationality is rare" is the *predicted* psychological shadow of "tralseness is the default." This is a genuine, elegant consilience and it tightens GAR-1b.
- **The default-irrationality datum is robustly replicated.** Kahneman-Tversky heuristics-and-biases, Thaler/Ariely behavioral economics, Stanovich dysrationalia, motivated reasoning and identity-protective cognition (Kahan) — the claim "humans are not reliably rational" is one of the better-supported findings in social science. Calling population-level sanity *rare* is **not arrogance**; it is the mainstream empirical picture.
- **Invention-concentration (B92/B115) is the same signal from the other end:** if a privileged few catalyzed most well-being inventions, then sustained world-class sanity is empirically concentrated, exactly as GAR-1b predicts.

**Where the argument over-reaches (#69, applied without flinching):**
- **(i) Political examples are contaminated and must be split.** "Trump is president / people deny science / won't protect the planet" fuses two very different things: **(a) genuine epistemic failures** (rejecting *replicated* evidence — a real rationality failure, fair to cite) and **(b) value, priority, and tribal-identity differences** (NOT the same as irrationality). Using "they vote/believe differently from me" as evidence of *insanity* is itself a **motivated-reasoning / myside-bias** move — the precise failure GAR-1b indicts. The defensible claim restricts to demonstrable evidence-denial, not partisan outcomes. **Climate inaction especially** is better modeled as a **collective-action / temporal-discounting / incentive-structure** failure than as millions of individually-insane minds — and **IPA-1 (Brandon's own principle)** explicitly bars the population→individual leap here.
- **(ii) The Jo Cameron "hardly anyone but me" claim is factually shaky — and #69 cuts toward Brandon here.** Jo Cameron's FAAH-OUT/FAAH genotype (reported 2019, UCL) **has** drawn serious follow-up — e.g. UCL mechanistic work on FAAH-OUT (~2023) — and FAAH/endocannabinoid modulation is an active (if hazardous, cf. the 2016 Bial BIA 10-2474 fatality) pharmacology target. So "almost no one pursued it" **overstates** the record. The honest, *still-supportive* version: it is **under-pursued relative to its apparent value** — which makes the GAR-1b point without the false "only me." Affirming the stronger version would itself be a sanity-lapse (confirmation-friendly fact-acceptance), so the thesis forbids it. *(Specific dates/labs worth re-verifying before any external use.)*
- **(iii) The self-location trap (GAR-1-F4, now triggered on the originator).** The cluster {"few I've met are sane" + invention-concentration + "only I pursued X"} conveniently places the speaker in the rational elite. That **population** claim is well-supported; the **self-location** inference ("∴ I am one of the few sane ones") is *not* discharged by it. Crucially: **being able to *perceive* others' irrationality is necessary but nowhere near sufficient for being sane oneself** — most cranks accurately diagnose *others'* errors; that's compatible with large blind spots of one's own. Per the GIT-1/LDD-1 measurement rule, "am I in the sane minority?" must be answered **outcome-blind** (track-record, calibration, prediction — e.g. the live GSA record, registered predictions), never inferred from "I can see how irrational everyone else is." The clean cut: the rarity-of-sanity claim is **defensible and not arrogant**; the *self-membership* claim is the part that still owes outcome-blind evidence, and conflating the two is exactly the arrogance Brandon is right to want to avoid.

**Net:** TRG-1 → GAR-1b is a real, strengthening consilience. The political/Jo-Cameron/self-location flourishes are where motivated reasoning can sneak back in — and the discipline that catches them is the same discipline the thesis is *about*. That self-application is the test of whether GAR-1 is science or flattery; it passes only if held to it.

## 4. Anti-cheat — the self-serving-humility trap (#69, both directions)

- **The humblebrag risk (load-bearing).** "My achievements were *mere* sanity" can function as *covert* status-claim ("…and yet I, unlike everyone, simply *was* sane"). GAR-1 must be stated so it is **not** self-flattering: per GAR-1b the claim is precisely that the speaker possessed a *rare* trait (sustained sanity), so calling it "mere" is accurate about its *kind* but **must not** smuggle in "…and therefore easy / therefore anyone-could / therefore especially-virtuous-of-me." The correct register is *demystifying*, not self-deprecating-as-flex.
- **The opposite error (mystification).** Equally barred: inflating ordinary sound judgment into "extraordinary powers" to build a personality cult (WHM-1 idolatry failure mode). GAR-1 cuts against *both*.
- **Measurement discipline (inherited from GIT-1/LDD-1).** Whether a given achievement is baseline-sanity vs tail-ingenuity must be judged **outcome-blind**, not by scoring the trait from the impressive result it produced (the hindsight-contamination trap, B122/B124).

## 5. Falsifiers / boundaries (OPEN)

- **GAR-1-F1:** if controlled study shows celebrated breakthroughs require a *discontinuous* cognitive faculty absent in matched high-rationality non-geniuses (i.e., sanity + courage is demonstrably insufficient even when present), the deflationary baseline (GAR-1a) fails.
- **GAR-1-F2 (rarity hinge):** if adequate rationality turns out to be *common* (dysrationalia overstated) while genius stays ~1-in-a-billion, GAR-1b's reconciliation collapses and a hidden faculty must be doing the work after all.
- **GAR-1-F3 (tail-reality):** if the Tesla/Ramanujan "surplus" fully reduces to ordinary rapid sanity + survivorship/biography inflation, the residual tail (GAR-1c) dissolves and GAR-1 becomes pure deflation.
- **GAR-1-F4 (anti-cheat tripwire):** if GAR-1 is deployed as covert self-elevation (or to mystify a node), it has been abused — the abuse indicts the user, not the thesis.
