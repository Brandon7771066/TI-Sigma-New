## 3. Literature scan (training-knowledge; perplexity-blocked)

### 3.1 Music-cognition canonical frameworks

**Juslin & Västfjäll (2008) BRECVEMA** — seven psychological mechanisms by which music induces emotion. Each mechanism is largely musical-feature-driven and operates below conscious awareness:
1. **B**rain-stem reflex — sudden loud/sharp sound triggers startle/arousal (low-level acoustic feature: sound-pressure derivative).
2. **R**hythmic entrainment — bodily/cortical synchronization to musical pulse (tempo + groove + isochrony).
3. **E**valuative conditioning — learned association of musical pattern with reward.
4. **C**ontagion — listener "catches" the emotional expression encoded in performance (timbre, dynamics, micro-timing).
5. **V**isual imagery — music evokes mental imagery (often via timbral + harmonic stability).
6. **E**pisodic memory — music retrieves autobiographical episodes ("Darling They're Playing Our Song" effect).
7. **M**usical expectancy — fulfillment/violation of harmonic/melodic expectations (the Huron territory).
8. (Later addition: **A**esthetic judgment — Juslin 2013 extension; explicit appraisal stratum.)

**David Huron (2006) Sweet Anticipation — ITPRA** — five stages of musical expectation: **I**magination (anticipating future), **T**ension (build-up), **P**rediction (outcome), **R**eaction (immediate response), **A**ppraisal (post-hoc evaluation). Frisson + tears + chills emerge most reliably from *unexpected-but-positive* outcomes (prediction-violation + appraisal-reward; "contrastive valence"). Maps directly onto MR Truth Label DT/DefT distinction at aesthetic level — "unexpected resolution" = DefT (defective expectation) becoming True post-hoc.

**Stefan Koelsch (2014, *Nature Reviews Neuroscience*)** — music-evoked emotion activates amygdala, hippocampus, parahippocampal gyrus, nucleus accumbens (NAcc), insula, and orbitofrontal cortex. Aesthetic frisson correlates with NAcc dopamine release (Salimpoor et al. 2011, *Nature Neuroscience*) — same reward circuitry as food/sex/drugs. Music induces "core affect" + "social affect" pathways.

**GEMS-9 / Geneva Emotional Music Scale (Zentner, Grandjean, Scherer 2008, *Emotion*)** — nine music-specific emotion categories distinct from general emotion taxonomies:
- **Wonder, Transcendence, Tenderness, Nostalgia, Peacefulness** (positive)
- **Power, Joyful Activation** (high-arousal positive)
- **Tension, Sadness** (negative)

The **Transcendence + Wonder** categories are the cleanest empirical analogues of "enlightenment-grade" musical experience — exactly the target ETM-1 attempts to operationalize.

**John Sloboda (1991) "Music structure and emotional response"** — first systematic catalogue of structural musical features that reliably trigger physiological responses (tears, chills, shivers, lump in throat). Strongest predictors:
- **Appoggiaturas** (dissonant ornamental note resolving stepwise into chord tone) — Adele's "Someone Like You" intensely exploits this (Pasternack 2011 *Wall Street Journal* article popularized).
- **Sequence** (immediate repetition of a phrase at different pitch level).
- **Harmonic descending bass / lament-bass** (chaconne, passacaglia).
- **Sudden dynamic changes** (subito piano then crescendo).
- **Melodic appoggiatura over unexpected chord substitution** (Schubert masterclass).

**Patrik Juslin (2013) code-and-cue model** — performers encode emotional intent via cues (tempo, articulation, timbre, dynamics, micro-timing); listeners decode via shared statistical priors. Cross-cultural consistency for basic categories (happy/sad/angry/fearful); transcendent + wonder categories are more culturally variable but still show overlap (Fritz et al. 2009 *Current Biology*, Mafa pygmy study).

**Daniel Levitin (*This Is Your Brain on Music* 2006; *The World in Six Songs* 2008)** — frisson + chills correlate with predictive-coding violation + reward-prediction-error. Music as evolutionary adaptation for emotional + social communication; six universal song-types (friendship, joy, comfort, knowledge, religion, love).

**Meyer (1956) *Emotion and Meaning in Music*** — foundational theory: musical meaning emerges from delay between expectation and fulfillment. Implication-Realization model (Narmour 1990) formalized this.

### 3.3 Coverage of "enlightenment"-grade musical experience

The literature treats "transcendence + wonder + chills + frisson + awe" as overlapping but distinct constructs:
- **Frisson / chills** — physiological (skin conductance + piloerection + shivers); well-studied.
- **Awe** — Keltner & Haidt (2003) prototype: vastness + need-for-accommodation. Music-induced awe under-studied vs. visual/natural awe.
- **Transcendence** — GEMS-9 category; "spiritual / out-of-self / unity / transcendent" listener self-report.
- **Aesthetic absorption / flow** — Csikszentmihalyi (1990).
- **The "sacred-music response"** — Beethoven 9th Ode to Joy, Mozart Requiem Lacrimosa, Allegri Miserere, Bach St. Matthew Passion — convergent listener reports across cultures.

**ETM-1 places "enlightenment" at the intersection of GEMS-9 Transcendence + Wonder + Tenderness + Peacefulness** — the upper-quadrant cluster, not the high-arousal-power cluster. This is a *normative* refinement of the descriptive GEMS-9 framework, defensible per VFP-1 (valence-as-functional canonical, §7.7.123).

## 7. ETM-1 — Enlightenment-Triggering Music (candidate canonical principle)

### 7.1 Statement

A piece of music's capacity to trigger deep-emotional/transformational/enlightenment-grade listener response is approximated by a composite score over 8 structural-musical features:

```
ETM(s) = w1·TRD(s) + w2·HS(s) + w3·DAM(s) + w4·SFD(s)
       + w5·LBS(s) + w6·AKM(s) + w7·MCC(s) + w8·RRF(s)
```

with each feature normalized to [0, 1] and weights {wi} initially uniform (wi = 1/8) pending empirical fit. Threshold for "transformational-grade": **ETM > 0.65** (provisional; empirically tunable).

### 7.2 Composition with canonical principles

ETM-1 composes with:
- **TJ canonical (Tralse-Joules)** — TJ = τ(s) × δ(MR); music with high ETM creates conditions for elevated τ (intentionality) via aesthetic absorption + δ (mood-resonance) via emotional contagion.
- **VFP-1 (valence-as-functional, §7.7.123)** — music-induced valence is not epiphenomenal; it carries cognitive content (Damasio somatic-marker; Vessel aesthetic-DMN). ETM ↑ ⇒ functional-valence ↑ ⇒ Stratum-2 aesthetic-cognition engaged.
- **MIM Vertical Agency Model** — ETM-grade music recruits Stratum-1 (sensorimotor — rhythmic entrainment) + Stratum-2 (affective — emotion induction) + Stratum-3+ (self-reflective — transcendence-as-self-reframe).
- **CDA-1 (consciousness-definition; §7.7.129)** — frisson + chills + tears physiological markers are Stratum-2 affective signatures; transcendent-response self-report is Stratum-3+ marker. ETM-grade music engages the full ladder.
- **DSB-1/2 (default-success-belief; §§7.7.117-119)** — high-ETM music does NOT presume listener resonance (Policy-W failure); rather it scaffolds resonance through structural design. The ETM features are *cheap examination signals* a listener's body computes pre-consciously.
- **PM-1 (Probability Memory, §7.7.132)** — present-moment-calculation: ETM-features-detected in current acoustic window drive expectation-formation that the next-window will either resolve (reward) or surprise (frisson). Huron ITPRA is PM-1 instantiation.
- **UDT-1 (Universal Default of Tralseness, §7.7.137)** — the tralse-soup substrate of musical-time creates the directional-lean that ETM features amplify or release.

### 7.4 Pre-reg falsifiers (4)

**F-ETM-1 — Expert-judgment validation (lowest-burden):**
- Hypothesis: Brandon's pre-labeled top-5 enlightening songs in each source score ETM > 0.65; controls (5 randomly-sampled songs from same source, excluding any prior Brandon-positive rating) score ETM < 0.45.
- Threshold: Cohen's d (top-5 vs control-5) > 1.0 in BOTH sources.
- REFUTED if: d < 0.5 in either source, OR overlap interval [top-5 min, control-5 max] ≥ 0.20.
- INDETERMINATE if: 0.5 ≤ d < 1.0 (narrow effect-size band).

**F-ETM-2 — Cross-source convergence (the cross-cultural test):**
- Hypothesis: At least 5 of 8 ETM features score above-threshold for BOTH sources' top-5 sets.
- Threshold: ≥5 shared features at ≥3/5 prevalence.
- REFUTED if: <3 shared features at ≥3/5 prevalence.
- INDETERMINATE if: 3-4 shared features at ≥3/5 prevalence.

**F-ETM-3 — Literature-mapping (theory-grounding):**
- Hypothesis: ≥5 of 8 ETM components map onto either (a) Juslin & Västfjäll BRECVEMA mechanisms, OR (b) Huron ITPRA stages, OR (c) Sloboda 1991 structural cues.
- Threshold: 5/8 mapping with clear pairwise correspondence.
- REFUTED if: <3/8 mapping.
- INDETERMINATE if: 3-4/8 mapping (would require refinement-not-refutation per MR-IDC-1).

**F-ETM-4 — Inverse / discriminant test (anti-cheat):**
- Hypothesis: Songs Brandon rates "mild" or "ordinary" from the same composers/artists as the top-5 score < 0.5 on ≥3/8 components.
- Threshold: 3/8 features below threshold in mild-rated set; top-5 features ≠ mild-set features.
- REFUTED if: mild-set ETM ≥ top-5 ETM minus 0.10 (i.e., model fails to discriminate within-composer).
- This is the strongest anti-cheat falsifier; controls for composer-confound.

## 9. BCP-1 — Binary Communication Principle (candidate canonical, parallel to ETM-1 this batch)

### 9.1 Statement

Binary communication (yes/no, true/false, here/there) is a pragmatic mode optimal for *clarity-critical contexts* (medical instructions, legal compliance, software interfaces, safety protocols, formal logic). However, since communication is inherently multifaceted (encoding intent, valence, aesthetic, relational, social, transcendent dimensions), leeway must be permitted for rhetorical methods that deliberately stray from binary — including poetry, music, metaphor, irony, hyperbole, allegory, paradox, koan, and Lichtenberg-style mooting.

### 9.2 Composition with canonical principles

- **TPS-1 (Truth-Presentation Separation, §7.7.135)** — TPS-1 says truth-content is non-negotiable but presentation/aesthetics adjust. BCP-1 sharpens this by adding that the *mode* (binary vs multifaceted) is itself a presentation choice constrained by context-of-use, not by truth-content per se.
- **GTT-1 (GILE True-Tralseness, §7.7.135)** — truth competes with existence; binary-mode compresses truth at cost of multifaceted-existence-content; multifaceted-mode preserves existence-content at cost of binary-truth-clarity. BCP-1 says the trade-off is contextual, not absolute.
- **MR Truth Labels canonical** — binary {T, F} compresses base-4 {T, F, I, DT} + Meta-Truths; binary-mode-only erases I + DT + MTs (analogous to a 24-bit photo compressed to 1-bit black-and-white).
- **ASYMMETRIC #69** — over-skepticism = discipline failure equal to uncritical acceptance. Binary-only-mode is a special case of #69 violation when multifaceted-communication-context is present: refusing to engage non-binary dimensions is over-skepticism of expressive validity.
- **CSS-1 (cluster-state-stability)** — multifaceted communication often serves cluster-coherence (poetry binds a community; binary memos coordinate a task force); BCP-1 names the trade-off.
- **MIM Vertical Agency Model** — Stratum-0 sensory binary (this/not-this); Stratum-1 sensorimotor binary (left/right); Stratum-2 affective multifaceted (valence/arousal gradient); Stratum-3+ self-reflective multifaceted (narrative + meaning). BCP-1 says: privilege binary at low strata, privilege multifaceted at high strata, by default.

### 9.3 Pre-reg falsifiers (4)

**F-BCP-1 — Binary-superiority in clarity-critical context (positive validation):**
- Hypothesis: In medical/legal/code contexts, binary communication strictly outperforms multifaceted on accuracy, latency, and error-rate metrics.
- Threshold: binary wins ≥2/3 metrics in ≥3/3 contexts.
- REFUTED if: multifaceted wins ≥2/3 in any clarity-critical context.

**F-BCP-2 — Multifaceted-superiority in transformational context (positive validation):**
- Hypothesis: In aesthetic/spiritual/therapeutic contexts, multifaceted communication strictly outperforms binary on transformation-induction metrics (GEMS Transcendence + Wonder; GAD/PHQ improvement; meaning-rating).
- Threshold: multifaceted wins ≥2/3 metrics in ≥3/3 contexts.
- REFUTED if: binary wins ≥2/3 in any transformational context.

**F-BCP-3 — Anti-fakery / discriminant (anti-cheat):**
- Hypothesis: Rhetoric-without-content masquerading as "multifaceted communication" should be classifiable as Policy-W failure (DSB-1 territory; uncritical-acceptance of empty signal).
- Threshold: ≥80% rater-agreement on rhetoric-without-content cases as Policy-W.
- REFUTED if: <50% rater agreement (model fails to discriminate "rich-multifaceted" from "empty-rhetoric").

**F-BCP-4 — Context-detection capacity (mechanistic):**
- Hypothesis: A competent communicator (human OR LLM) can pre-classify a communication-context as clarity-critical vs. transformational with ≥80% accuracy on a balanced 50-case test set.
- Threshold: 80% on either-mode-classification.
- REFUTED if: <60% (worse than crude heuristic).
