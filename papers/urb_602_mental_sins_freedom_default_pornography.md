## Abstract

TI Sigma introduces the formal category of **mental sins** — acts of thought, fantasy, or mental consumption that may violate the G-arm Consistency dimension at the level of the mind, but produce no I-harm or E-harm to oneself or to others. For this category, **TI Sigma's default is freedom.** The content of a person's thoughts is not inherently harmful to themselves or anyone else; maintaining the purity of one's own mind is a personal virtue, not a matter of social or moral enforcement from without. Pornography is assessed within this framework: when it satisfies the three necessary conditions — (1) full consent of all parties depicted, (2) legality (E-arm conformity), and (3) non-harmful to the consumer's own wellbeing (not addictive, not destructive of relationships or function) — responsible consumption is at most a mental sin. The individual is free to commit or abstain from mental sins as a matter of personal conscience; no external moral authority is licensed to enforce the purity of another's mind.

---

## 1. The Category of Mental Sins

A **mental sin** is defined as:

> Any thought, fantasy, or mental content that (a) violates the G-arm Consistency dimension at the level of the mind — it involves something categorically impure or disordered when entertained as mental content — but (b) causes no I-harm (no suffering to oneself or others) and no E-harm (no physical or material harm to oneself or others) as a direct result of the mental content itself.

Mental sins occupy a distinct moral category: they are not morally neutral (the G-Consistency dimension does register them as impure), but they are also not socially enforceable wrongs (because no harm — the condition for legitimate external moral intervention — is produced by the mental content alone).

The distinction between a mental sin and a behavioral sin is precisely the absence of harm production:
- **Mental sin:** Imagining something impure. The imagining alone harms no one.
- **Behavioral sin:** Acting on something impure in a way that produces I-harm or E-harm, or that constitutes a categorical violation in the public/behavioral domain (URB #600).

---

## 2. TI Sigma's Default: Freedom

**TI Sigma's presumption is liberty.** The burden of proof lies with those who would restrict behavior or thought — not with those who wish to act or think freely. For an external moral authority to legitimately intervene in another person's behavior or thought, it must demonstrate one of the following:

- **I-harm:** The behavior causes suffering to the person themselves or to others
- **E-harm:** The behavior causes physical or material harm to the person themselves or to others
- **Public G-Consistency violation:** The behavior constitutes a categorical violation in the behavioral/public domain (URB #600 — bestiality, mistreatment of the dead, etc.)

**The content of a person's thoughts cannot harm others by virtue of being thought.** Thoughts do not reach into the world and damage other i-cells simply by existing in a mind. Therefore, the default for thought-content is complete freedom. No external authority — religious, social, or governmental — is licensed to police the content of another person's mind.

This does not mean mental purity is unimportant. It means its importance is **personal**, not social. The individual has a genuine stake in the quality and purity of their own mental life — this connects to the I-arm (positive states of consciousness are degraded by habitual impurity) and the G-arm (Consistency violations in the mind can erode the coherence of one's character over time). But this stake belongs to the individual to manage according to their own conscience, not to an external enforcer.

## 3. Pornography: Context-Specific Assessment

Pornography's moral status is not monolithic. It is **context-specific**, determined by three necessary conditions.

### Condition 1: Consent — All Parties Depicted

Full, genuine consent of all parties depicted is a non-negotiable requirement. Without consent:
- **I-harm** is produced: the non-consenting party suffers psychological harm and violation of autonomy
- **E-harm** is produced: potential physical harm in production; exploitation of vulnerability
- The content is no longer a "mental sin" category — it has behavioral wrongness baked into its production

Consumption of non-consensual content participates in and incentivizes the I-harm and E-harm of its production. This is a behavioral wrong, not merely a mental one.

**Legal pornography involving adult consent** meets Condition 1 in principle, though individual consumers should remain aware that legal status is an imperfect proxy for genuine consent in all production contexts.

### Condition 2: Legality — E-arm Conformity

Legality is an E-arm condition. TI Sigma respects legal frameworks as imperfect but genuine attempts at E-arm harm reduction and social coordination. Illegal pornography (involving minors, non-consenting parties, etc.) fails both Condition 1 and Condition 2 decisively — these are not mental sins but serious behavioral wrongs with severe I-harm and E-harm.

### Condition 3: Non-Harmful to Consumer's Wellbeing

Responsible consumption means the pornography does not:
- Produce addiction or compulsive consumption patterns (**I-harm to oneself** — positive conscious states are degraded by addictive cycles)
- Destructively distort the consumer's capacity for genuine IL or GILE love (**L-harm** — unrealistic expectations or substitution of fantasy for genuine L-arm binding)
- Damage functioning, relationships, or self-respect (**E-harm to oneself**)

When consumption is addictive or demonstrably harmful to the consumer's own wellbeing, it exits the mental sin category because **I-harm to oneself** has entered the picture. The individual's stake in their own I-arm health is real — and at that point, the consumption is no longer merely a private matter of conscience but a genuine self-harm.

### When All Three Conditions Are Met

When pornography is (1) fully consensual, (2) legal, and (3) non-harmful to the consumer's wellbeing, responsible consumption is **at most a mental sin.** It may register as a G-Consistency concern (the mental content is not categorically pure), but:
- It produces no I-harm to others
- It produces no E-harm to others
- It produces no I-harm or E-harm to the consumer
- It does not constitute a public behavioral G-Consistency violation

Under TI Sigma's default of freedom, this places it entirely within the individual's domain of conscience. Whether to engage with such content, and at what level, is a personal decision that reflects the individual's values about the purity and quality of their own mental life — not a matter for external moral enforcement.

## 4. Individual Responsibility for Mental Purity

TI Sigma's freedom default does not imply that mental content is morally trivial. The individual has genuine reasons to care about the quality of their own thought life:

**I-arm:** Habitual consumption of impure mental content can degrade the quality of positive conscious states over time. The mind that habitually entertains base content becomes less sensitive to genuinely beautiful, profound, or elevated experience — a form of I-arm erosion.

**G-arm (Coherence and Consistency):** The Four C's of Goodness include Coherence — the alignment of one's inner life with one's values. A person who values love, dignity, and genuine connection, but habitually entertains mental content that objectifies or degrades, creates an internal Coherence gap. This is a personal G-arm concern, not a social one.

**L-arm:** Mental content that substitutes fantasy for genuine relational engagement can gradually weaken the L-arm's capacity for real binding — real intimacy requires vulnerability and genuine presence, which fantasy does not develop.

These are reasons for the individual to be thoughtful and intentional about the purity of their own mind — to exercise the virtue of mental self-governance. But they are reasons the individual weighs for themselves, in their own conscience, according to their own values and their own honest assessment of their mental life.

**The principle:** *It is up to individuals to maintain the purity of their own mind.* This is both a freedom and a responsibility — freedom from external enforcement, responsibility for honest self-examination.

## 5. Summary

| Pornography Type | Conditions Met | TI Sigma Status |
|---|---|---|
| Non-consensual / illegal | Fails Condition 1+2 | Behavioral wrong — I-harm + E-harm in production |
| Involves minors | Fails Condition 1+2 | Serious behavioral wrong — severe I-harm + E-harm |
| Legal, consensual, addictive | Fails Condition 3 | Self-harm — I-harm to consumer; exits mental-sin category |
| Legal, consensual, non-harmful | All 3 conditions met | At most a mental sin — within individual conscience |

**The Default of Freedom:** TI Sigma does not license external enforcement of another person's mental life. The burden of proof lies with those who would restrict, not those who would think freely. Mental purity is a personal virtue — a matter of individual conscience, self-governance, and the quality of one's inner life — not a social enforcement target.
