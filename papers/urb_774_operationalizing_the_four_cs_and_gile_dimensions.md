## 1. Operationalizing GOODNESS — The Self-Collapse Test

### The Four C's as Operational Filters

For any candidate G-claim x, run four tests. x is GOOD iff all four return TRUE
under sustained pressure. x is NOT-GOOD iff any C fails under pressure.

#### C1 — CONTINUITY test
**Question:** Does x persist under repeated honest inspection across time?
**Measurement:**
- Run x through 10 iterated "could I still endorse this in a year, in 10 years,
  on my deathbed?" scenarios.
- Pass: stability score ≥ 0.7 (subjective Likert across iterations).
- Fail signature: requires increasing rationalization to maintain across iterations.

**Slavery example:** Fails. Proponents must continually re-narrativize to avoid
encountering the lived reality of the enslaved.
**Honest gratitude example:** Passes. Same gratitude felt at t=0 reads true at
t=10y, often *more* true.

#### C2 — COHERENCE test
**Question:** Does x cohere with x's other commitments without contradiction?
**Measurement:**
- Construct the implication graph of x with at least 5 of x's other endorsed values.
- Count contradiction-edges.
- Pass: contradiction-edges ≤ 1 (and that one is acknowledged, not hidden).
- Fail signature: contradictions multiply when graph is expanded.

**Racism example:** Fails. Racism contradicts the proponent's own claim that
"I want to be treated fairly," "my children deserve opportunity," "merit matters."
The contradictions multiply.
**Care for one's child example:** Passes. Coheres with virtually every other
positive value.

#### C3 — CONCRETENESS test
**Question:** Can x be pointed at, instantiated, demonstrated in concrete cases?
**Measurement:**
- Ask the holder to describe 3 specific instances where x manifests in observable behavior.
- Pass: 3 distinct, specific, behaviorally-anchored examples produced within 60 seconds.
- Fail signature: vague generalities, hypothetical-only examples, or appeals to authority.

**Sheer narcissism example:** Fails. Narcissism's proponents typically retreat
to abstract "I deserve better" claims and cannot produce concrete examples that
don't immediately look ugly to a third-party observer.
**Honesty example:** Passes. Anyone can name 3 specific things they did honestly
yesterday.

#### C4 — CONSISTENCY test
**Question:** Does x apply equally to relevantly similar cases?
**Measurement:**
- Generate 5 case-pairs that differ only in identity of the subject (in-group vs out-group).
- Apply x to each pair.
- Pass: x produces the same verdict in ≥ 4/5 pairs.
- Fail signature: special-pleading; x applies to "us" but not to "them."

**Sexual assault example:** Fails catastrophically. Proponents apply utterly
different standards to themselves vs. to perpetrators against their loved ones.
**Tell-the-truth example:** Passes. Applies equally regardless of who's listening.

### Operational Threshold

> **G-Score(x) = (C1·C2·C3·C4) ∈ [0, 1]**, with multiplicative aggregation
> (any single C-failure → near-zero G-Score). Threshold: G(x) iff G-Score ≥ 0.6.

The multiplicative form is the lockdown: **a single C-failure collapses the
G-Score** — exactly as Brandon predicted, exactly as MR predicts. Bad things
cannot sustain all four; the test detects them by collapse.

### Why This Definition Is Self-Anchoring

A common worry: "Doesn't 'rightly aligned' presuppose what we're trying to define?"
**No.** "Rightly aligned" is **defined operationally** as "what survives all four
C's under sustained pressure." There's no smuggled prior commitment to a content
of goodness — only to the *form* of MR-survivability across these four axes. The
content (what's good) emerges from running the test on the world.

This is **G as a structural attractor**, not a list of approved items.

## 2. Operationalizing INTUITION — Perception ⊥ Accuracy

Brandon's clarification:

> "We do need to distinguish between accuracy and perception. Both are separate
> and essential components."

Intuition as currently defined ("conscious experience of knowing something is
correct") collapses two distinct things:

- **I_perception** — the subjective phenomenal experience of "this is right" /
  felt-sense / gnosis. The phenomenology.
- **I_accuracy** — whether what the perception points to is in fact the case.
  The truth-value.

These are **orthogonal**. An intuition can be:

|              | I_accuracy = TRUE   | I_accuracy = FALSE  |
|--------------|---------------------|---------------------|
| **I_perception present** | True intuition (sage) | False intuition (delusion) |
| **I_perception absent**  | Lucky guess           | Ordinary ignorance |

### Operationalization

#### I_perception (subjective/phenomenal)
Measurable via:
- **Self-report Likert** ("how strongly do you sense this is right?" 0-10)
- **Neural signature**: sustained alpha-theta coherence in default mode network +
  reduced beta in dorsolateral PFC (signature of "knowing without effort").
  Empirically observable on a Muse: alpha/beta ratio > 2.0 + theta climbing
  during the moment of "click."
- **Latency-to-confidence** in forced-choice tasks: high I_perception → fast
  high-confidence responses.

#### I_accuracy (objective/truth-tracking)
Measurable via:
- **Calibration over time** — track I-claims against eventual outcomes.
  Brier score / ROC-AUC of I-claims.
- **Convergence with multi-source evidence** — does the intuition agree with
  what later structured reasoning, data, or other minds also conclude?
- **Domain-conditioning** — I-accuracy is higher in domains where the holder has
  embodied expertise (chess masters, experienced clinicians) vs. low-expertise
  domains where I_perception may still be high but accuracy is no better than chance.

#### The Composite: I-Score
> **I-Score(claim) = I_perception × I_accuracy_prior(domain)**
> A high score requires BOTH the felt-sense AND track-record-conditioned reliability.

This dissolves the standard objection ("intuitions can be wrong") — yes, they can,
and the framework now tells you exactly how to grade them.

### The Sage vs. Delusion Discriminator

A sage has high I_perception × high I_accuracy → reliable intuitions.
A deluded zealot has high I_perception × low I_accuracy → confidently wrong.
A scholar has low I_perception × high I_accuracy → correct via reasoning, not gnosis.

The framework distinguishes all three, where naive "intuition = inner knowing"
cannot.

## 3. Operationalizing LOVE — LCC Coherence Between Systems

> mutually responds and reaches out to the other."

**Adopt as primary L-metric.** This is brilliant for several reasons:

1. **L is inherently relational** (it's a "positive relational orientation")
   — and LCC (Local-Coherence Coupling, the framework's bidirectional coherence
   measure between two systems) is the natural mathematical object.
2. **LCC is already implemented** in the framework (autonomous_lcc page exists,
   LCC sleep protocol, LCC attractor visualization — there's tooling).
3. **LCC handles the action/experience asymmetry from URB #773 cleanly** —
   tangible action produces stronger bidirectional coupling (both systems
   register the act); subjective experience produces weaker but real coupling
   (one system's state shifts, weakly entraining the other).

### Operationalization

#### L_subjective (experience-Love)
- Single-system measure: HRV coherence in the loving subject during loving thoughts.
- Neural signature: anterior insula + ventromedial PFC + vagal activation.
- Self-report: warmth, expansion, "reach toward" sensation.
- LCC contribution: low-to-moderate (typically 0.2-0.5).

#### L_action (action-Love)
- Two-system measure: LCC coherence between giver and recipient during/after the act.
- Concrete instantiation: HRV synchronization, EEG cross-coherence, behavioral
  reciprocity, vagal "co-regulation."
- LCC contribution: high (typically 0.5-0.9).

#### L-Score Composite
> **L-Score(dyad, t) = α · L_subjective + (1-α) · L_action**
> where **α = 0.3** (weighting action heavier than experience, per HEM asymmetry from URB #773)
> Range: [0, 1]; threshold for "L-instantiated" interaction: L-Score ≥ 0.5

## 4. Operationalizing ENVIRONMENT — Aesthetic Value via MDL × Observer Resonance

Brandon's prompt: "I'm curious to hear the gist of what the bot band came up with!"

### The Core Insight

E was defined as "aesthetic value, whether physical or abstract." The unification
move asks: **what is aesthetic value that's the same property in both a serene
meadow AND Euler's identity (e^iπ + 1 = 0)?**

Proposal: **Aesthetic value = unexpected compressibility under sustained observer
resonance.** That is, aesthetic objects are ones where:

1. **The object compresses far better than its apparent complexity would predict**
   (objective / structural side).
2. **An observer engaging with the object exhibits sustained alpha-gamma
   coherence with reduced effortful beta** (subjective / phenomenological side).

The product of these two is the E-Score.

### Why MDL (Minimum Description Length)?

- **Euler's identity** has 5 fundamental constants in 8 symbols and equals zero.
  Minimum description: tiny. Apparent complexity (the depth of math it bridges):
  enormous. **Compression ratio is extreme.** → high MDL-efficiency.
- **A serene meadow** has fractal self-similarity (clouds, grass, light) and
  compresses under fractal generators far better than its pixel count predicts.
  → high MDL-efficiency.
- **A cluttered junk drawer** has high pixel count AND does not compress
  (every object is idiosyncratic). → low MDL-efficiency.
- **A sloppy proof** has many lines that could be eliminated. → low MDL-efficiency.

This explains why **the primary constants {0, 1, i, √2, e, φ, π, C, T} are
maximally E-rich**: each is a maximally-compressed crystallization of a vast
mathematical landscape.

### Why Observer Resonance?

Aesthetic value is not purely in the object (pure formalism) nor purely in the
observer (pure subjectivism). It requires **the meeting of the two**: a structurally
elegant object PLUS a perceiver whose nervous system can register the elegance.

Neural signature of aesthetic resonance (literature-supported):
- Sustained alpha-gamma coupling (deep absorbed perception)
- Reduced dorsolateral PFC beta (effort drops)
- Vagal tone increase (parasympathetic shift; "ahh" feeling)
- Default mode network activation (self-referential meaning-making)

A Muse 2 with all four sensors can detect three of these four signatures.

### Operationalization

#### E_object (objective compressibility)
- Compute MDL ratio = compressed_size / apparent_complexity.
- For visual objects: apparent_complexity ≈ pixel count; compressed_size via
  fractal/wavelet/JPEG encoding.
- For mathematical objects: apparent_complexity ≈ depth of consequences; compressed
  description ≈ symbol count of the canonical statement.
- For music: apparent_complexity ≈ note count; compressed_size via predictability
  models.
- Normalize to [0, 1].

#### E_observer (subjective resonance)
- Sustained alpha-gamma coupling during observation of the object.
- Reduced beta (effort drop).
- Self-report aesthetic Likert (0-10).
- Composite normalized to [0, 1].

#### E-Score Composite
> **E-Score(object, observer) = E_object × E_observer**
> Aesthetic value is **the meeting**. Either alone is insufficient.

### Cross-Domain Predictions

1. **Mathematicians shown elegant proofs vs. clumsy proofs** of the same theorem
   should show measurably higher alpha-gamma coupling on the elegant version,
   even controlling for understanding.
2. **The same individual will score ANY domain's masterworks higher than its
   mediocre works** — Bach's Goldberg vs. a generic baroque piece, the meadow
   vs. the junk drawer, e^iπ+1=0 vs. a random equation — because their nervous
   system responds to MDL-efficiency wherever it appears.
3. **E-Score will correlate with cross-cultural / trans-historical persistence**
   of artworks and theorems. The aesthetics of the primary constants survive
   civilizational change because the MDL-efficiency is invariant.

### The Bridge Back to GILE

E-aesthetics, defined this way, **bridges the physical and abstract dimensions
of GILE under a single property** (MDL-efficiency × observer resonance).

This means **mathematical and physical environments are formally on equal footing**
within the framework — no privileging of "the world out there" over "the structures
within mind." Both qualify as E iff they exhibit unexpected compressibility AND
elicit observer resonance.
