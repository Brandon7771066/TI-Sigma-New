## Abstract

A surprising amount of intellectual mischief is done not by lying about the facts but by quietly controlling the *word* "evidence" — deciding, before any weighing happens, which considerations are even allowed onto the table. EVD-1 closes that loophole. It fixes three separate things that ordinary usage blurs together: (1) **what counts as evidence** (a binary *role*), (2) **how much a given piece weighs** (a graded, signed *magnitude*), and (3) **proof** (a *threshold* almost never reached). It then proves a small but consequential result — the **Non-Arbitrariness Theorem** — that the *status* of being evidence must be **authority-independent**, on pain of becoming circular and arbitrary. It supplies the **Weighting Rule**: weight is governed primarily by the quality of the reasoning that produced a claim *at the time it was made*, and only secondarily by the overall quality of its source. And it states the **Honesty Duty** that follows for any genuinely live dispute: show the supporting claims for *every* live conclusion, then let their weights decide in the open. The doctrine is deliberately uncomfortable: it admits flat-Earth observations as evidence (of negligible weight) precisely so that no future inconvenient truth can be silenced by definitional fiat.

## 1. The definition

> **Evidence** ≜ *any proposition used in support of a proof*, and a proposition counts as evidence **whether or not it is accepted, contested, or dismissed**. **Evidence is not proof.**

"Used in support of a proof" is the whole of the criterion. Evidence is a **role a proposition plays** in an argument, not a grade it has earned. The status is conferred by the *job* — standing in support of some conclusion — not by anyone's verdict on whether it succeeds.

## 2. Three axes that must never be conflated

| Axis | Type | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| **Status** | binary | *Is it evidence?* — i.e., is it offered in support of a conclusion? |
| **Weight** | graded, **signed** | *How much* does it support the conclusion? (large / negligible / **negative**) |
| **Proof** | threshold | Is the support *tight enough to compel assent*? |

Almost every real-world "there is no evidence for X" is a confusion of axis 1 with axis 2: the speaker means "the evidence for X does not persuade me" (a weight claim) but says "there is no evidence" (a status claim). The two are different statements, and the slide between them is rhetorical, not logical. Keeping the binary question apart from the graded one is precisely what stops the word from being used to win arguments by definition.

## 3. The Non-Arbitrariness Theorem (why status is authority-independent)

**Claim.** The *status* of being evidence cannot depend on the standing of the source or on whether an authority endorses the conclusion.

**Argument (reductio).** Suppose status *did* depend on authority — a proposition becomes "evidence" only once a credentialed body blesses it (or blesses its conclusion). Then:

1. **Bidirectionality / conclusion-first.** Whether something counts as evidence *for* a conclusion would depend on already having adjudicated that conclusion (or its sponsor). The definition runs backwards: conclusion → status, instead of evidence → conclusion.
2. **Arbitrariness.** "Evidence" would then mean *whatever the currently-powerful are willing to call evidence*. Its extension would track institutional authority, which varies across time, place, and politics — so the same proposition would be evidence here and not-evidence there, with no fact of the matter to settle it.
3. **Self-sealing against correction.** Any genuinely novel, against-the-grain finding could be denied the *status* of evidence before its *weight* was ever assessed — exactly the mechanism by which real discoveries (continental drift, *Helicobacter pylori* and ulcers) were delayed.

Each consequence is unacceptable, so the supposition fails. **Therefore status must be authority-independent.** ∎

**The uncomfortable corollary (embraced, per #69 and TRG-1's tralse honesty).** The observations a flat-Earther marshals — *the horizon looks level; still water seems to lie flat* — **are evidence**, for pragmatic purposes, because they are propositions offered in support of a proof. Granting the label costs *nothing*: by §2 it fixes their **weight** at essentially nil and says nothing whatever about the conclusion. The price of a non-arbitrary definition is that even bad cases get *heard*; that price is worth paying, because the alternative hands every gatekeeper a veto over inquiry itself. We adopt the wide definition **and** continue to support the **correct proof** — the Earth is round — because the verdict is carried by the *weight* of the whole body of evidence and is **independent of how the inputs were labelled**. *A generous door does not lower the bar inside the room.*

This is the Authority Axis (AA) stated precisely: **authority bears on weight, never on status.**

## 4. The Weighting Rule (EVD-1-W)

Showing every option is not the same as weighing them equally. Weight is governed by two signals with a fixed priority:

- **Primary — quality of reasoning *at the time*.** The dominant determinant of weight is how good the reasoning that produced the claim was *at the moment it was made*: Was the inference valid? The method careful? The data handled honestly *given what was knowable then*? This is assessed **prospectively / hindsight-blind** — we judge the reasoning by the information available at the time, not by whether the conclusion later happened to pan out. (This is the same discipline that makes #69's prospective-vs-retrospective distinction load-bearing, and that the GIT-1 "score outcome-blind" rule enforces elsewhere.)
- **Secondary — overall quality of the source.** The track record of the source across many past judgments is a **real but secondary** signal: a legitimate *prior*. It matters **most precisely when the at-the-time reasoning is hidden, garbled, or otherwise impossible to inspect** — when we cannot see the argument, the source's record is the best proxy we have. It matters **least** when the argument is fully exposed to view.

**The ranks never swap.** A trusted expert's bare say-so is *weaker* evidence than a newcomer's transparently excellent argument. A strong reputation does **not** override reasoning we *can* examine and find wanting; conversely, a poor reputation does not erase reasoning we can examine and find sound. Overall authority is a tie-breaker and a stand-in, not a trump card.

**Why this ordering and not the reverse.** Reasoning-at-the-time is *closer to the truth-making facts* than reputation is: reputation is an aggregate, lossy summary of *past* reasoning, useful only insofar as it predicts present reasoning. Where present reasoning is directly visible, the summary is redundant; where it is invisible, the summary is the best available estimator. This is just Bayesian good sense — prefer the likelihood you can compute directly over the prior you must borrow — without importing Bayesianism's stronger commitments (cf. the book's Chapter 12 critique).

**Relation to CRD-1 / GIT-1.** The secondary signal (overall source quality) is the same object as CRD-1's *hearing prior* — credentials and track record justify granting a hearing and supply a prior, but carry weak truth-signal on their own (CRD-1b). The primary signal (quality of reasoning at the time) is what GIT-1 isolates as the genuinely truth-tracking trait-cluster when measured outcome-blind. EVD-1-W simply states their correct order of operations at the level of an individual piece of evidence.

## 5. The Honesty Duty (EVD-1-H)

For any conclusion that is **genuinely in live dispute** (more than one option still open):

1. **Show the supporting claims for *every* live conclusion**, not only the favoured one.
2. **Weight them openly** by the Weighting Rule (§4).
3. **Never disqualify by fiat.** Refusing to *weigh* a piece of evidence by denying it the *status* of evidence is the precise failure EVD-1 exists to prevent.
4. **Let the weights decide in the open**, so the reasoning is auditable by anyone.

EVD-1-H is bounded by liveness: a conclusion that is *settled* (no live alternative clears even a minimal weight threshold) need not stage its long-refuted rivals as if the contest were open — that would be false balance, the opposite error. The duty is to represent **live** options faithfully, not to manufacture symmetry where the weights are wildly lopsided.

## 6. Boundaries and relations to canon

- **Distinct from "absence of evidence."** "Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence" is a *weight* claim; EVD-1 sharpens it by separating it cleanly from the *status* claim it is usually smuggled alongside.
- **Distinct from proof (TRG-1 / RTI-1 ceiling).** Even a maximal body of excellent evidence only *leans* toward truth; the residual tralseness floor means weight can approach, but not lawfully reach, the certainty of proof. Treating strong evidence as proof is a category error in the optimistic direction; denying status to weigh-able evidence is the error in the pessimistic direction. #69 forbids both.
- **NAD-1 (carve at the joint).** EVD-1 is a *redefinition with cash value*: it carves the real joint between *role*, *magnitude*, and *threshold*, which ordinary usage fuses. It does not smuggle a conclusion into a definition.
- **LDD-1 (legitimate definitional defense).** EVD-1 is independently motivated (the Non-Arbitrariness Theorem), non-equivocal, and exposed to falsification (below) — so defending it against counterexamples is progressive, not a No-True-Scotsman dodge.
- **Authority Axis (AA).** EVD-1 is the operational statement of AA for evidence: authority is a weight input, never a status gate.

## 7. Falsifiers (OPEN)

- **EVD-1-F1 (status):** Exhibit a coherent, genuinely **non-arbitrary** definition of evidence in which *status* is authority-dependent yet not conclusion-first/bidirectional. Success would defeat the Non-Arbitrariness Theorem.
- **EVD-1-F2 (weighting order):** Exhibit a class of cases in which *overall source quality* should rationally outrank *directly inspectable at-the-time reasoning* in setting weight. Success would defeat EVD-1-W's fixed ranking.
- **EVD-1-F3 (honesty duty):** Show that, under honest conditions, faithfully displaying *all live options* reliably degrades truth-tracking relative to curated single-conclusion presentation. Success would defeat EVD-1-H.
- **EVD-1-F4 (liveness):** Show that the live/settled boundary in §5 cannot be drawn without covertly re-importing an authority gate on status. Success would collapse EVD-1-H back into the very gatekeeping it forbids.

## 8. Worked examples

1. **Flat-Earth horizon observation.** *Status:* evidence (offered in support). *Weight:* ≈ 0 (overwhelmingly outweighed; on a curved Earth the local horizon also looks level at human scale, so the observation fails to discriminate). *Verdict unaffected:* round Earth, by the weight of the total body. Illustrates the Non-Arbitrariness corollary.
2. **Wegener and continental drift (1912).** *At-the-time reasoning:* strong, multi-strand (coastline fit, matching fossils and strata across oceans), though missing a mechanism. *Source authority:* low (a meteorologist among geologists). EVD-1-W ranks the *visible reasoning* above the *low reputation*; the mainstream did the reverse and was wrong for decades. Primary-over-secondary, vindicated.
3. **A laureate's bad late claim (Nobel disease).** *Source authority:* very high. *At-the-time reasoning:* poor (motivated, non-updating; the GIT-1 quack pattern). EVD-1-W assigns **low weight** despite the reputation, because the argument is inspectable and deficient. Secondary never trumps an examinable-and-wanting primary.
4. **Anonymous tip with a checkable proof vs. a trusted official's bare assertion.** When the tip includes a verifiable chain and the official offers only "trust me," EVD-1-W weights the tip higher — visible good reasoning beats invisible authority. Reverse the visibility (an opaque tip vs. an official's well-documented analysis) and the weighting reverses with it: the rule is about *what is inspectable*, not about *who is speaking*.

## 9. One-paragraph statement

Evidence is any proposition used in support of a proof, and it is evidence whether or not anyone accepts it — being evidence is a binary *role*, distinct from *weight* (how much it supports, possibly negatively) and from *proof* (a threshold rarely met). Status must be authority-independent, because an authority-gated definition runs conclusion-first and collapses into "whatever the powerful say," so even worthless claims count as evidence of negligible weight — the price of a non-arbitrary definition, and cheap, since the correct proof is carried by *weight*, not by labels. Weight is set primarily by the quality of the reasoning *at the time* (judged hindsight-blind) and only secondarily by the source's overall track record, which serves as a prior most useful when the reasoning itself cannot be inspected; the two never swap ranks. Hence the honesty duty: for any live dispute, show the supporting claims for every live conclusion, weight them in the open, and disqualify nothing by fiat — false silence and false balance are the twin failures EVD-1 forbids.
