## §1. Editorial Diagnostic — Part One (Chapters 1–5)

### §1.1 What's working

- **Chapter 1.1 (The Two-Valued Habit) opens at the right pitch.** It states the problem clearly, names the lineage (Aristotle → Stoics → scholastics → Frege/Russell/Tarski → digital computer), and calibrates the scope ("the bivalence of truth is, by now, less a thesis we defend than a habit we no longer notice"). This is publishable-quality philosophical prose.
- **Chapter 1.2 (Many-Valued Tradition) names the predecessors honestly.** Łukasiewicz 1920, Kleene, Belnap, Zadeh's fuzzy logic. Identifies what they missed (no positive logical state for stably-Indeterminate; placeholder reading of third value). This is exactly the right framing for academic readers.
- **Chapter 1.3 names the four components cleanly:** base-four truth architecture, GILE, Myrion Resolution, HEAR. Each gets a one-paragraph description.
- **Chapter 1.4 admits scope honestly** ("experimental philosophy in a precise sense"; "does not pretend to settle open questions"; the 2022 cognitive-intensity disclosure).
- **Chapter 2 (GILE) has strong concrete examples** — eating healthy, lying, deep meditation, difficult honest conversation. The numerical scoring grounds the abstract framework.
- **Chapter 2 (-3, +2) asymmetry section** sharply argues *why* asymmetry, not just *that* it exists.

### §1.2 What needs work (per #69)

**A — Voice inconsistency (most important problem in Part One).** Chapter 1 is written in academic register — careful, hedged, citation-aware. Chapter 2 switches abruptly to motivational/popularizer register — bold claims, "this is one of the most profound insights of TI", "consciousness gravity". The two voices clash; readers will feel whiplash. **Fix:** standardize Chapter 2 to Chapter 1's register OR (preferably for "Everyone" framing) standardize Chapter 1's openness to Chapter 2's accessibility — but consistently, not chapter-by-chapter.

**B — Canonical-update integration is incomplete.** The book references "Canonical Update Note (May 2026)" at line 94 and Appendix E, but Chapter 1.2 still uses the older "four base truth labels" framing without explicit cross-reference to the **2026-05-08 canonical ruling** (`MR_TRUTH_LABELS_CANONICAL_RULING_2026-05-08.md`). Specifically: the DT/DefT rename (DT = Double Tralse exclusively; DefT = Defective Truth, formerly "DT scheme B") needs an explicit footnote in Chapter 1.2 to prevent reader confusion when they hit older corpus material that uses the legacy nomenclature.

**C — GILE expansion in Chapter 2.** The "G" in the canonical TI Sigma corpus is **Goodness**, BUT Chapter 1.3 line 150 expands GILE as "Goodness, Intuition, Love, and Environment." Chapter 2 line 178+ confirms this expansion. There is **no internal inconsistency in Part One**, but earlier corpus material variably used "GILE = Goodness, Intuition, Love, Effort" or other expansions. **Fix:** add explicit footnote in Chapter 2 §2.1 noting the canonical 2026 expansion (G/I/L/E as currently presented) and pointing to Appendix E for cross-references.

**D — Chapter 2.7 ("Why -3 to +2") needs a #69 caveat.** The "consciousness gravity" claim is *evocative metaphor*, not derived theorem. The book currently presents it as if causally explained ("the asymmetry creates preference for goodness"). Per #69 this is over-claiming. **Fix:** insert one-sentence caveat: "This is heuristic motivation, not derivation; the mathematical justification for the (-3, +2) range appears in Chapter 9 / Appendix C."

**E — Chapter 3 (Indeterminate Zone — Free Will) sets up the most important argument but the Chapter 2 percentages (47% / 20% / 33%) are presented without source.** **Fix:** either derive in-text or cite the analysis paper that fixes those percentages.

**F — Reading-level inconsistency.** Spot check: Chapter 1.1 is graduate-level prose (Flesch ~30–40). Chapter 2 dimension descriptions are middle-school-accessible (Flesch ~65–75). Both are fine in isolation; together they confuse. **Fix:** decide on target audience and standardize. For "for Everyone," recommend target Flesch 50–60 (high school sophomore level, what most adult-trade nonfiction targets).

**G — Cross-references to current canonical material.** Chapter 1.2 mentions "twelve well-established Meta-Truths, with twenty-four catalogued in extended form" without footnoting `urb_608` and `urb_639` respectively. Add footnotes for serious readers who want to follow up.

**H — Five-axes review missing from foundations.** The book introduces base-four truth labels (axis 3 of 5) but doesn't mention PD-real (axis 1), PD-imaginary (axis 2), τ/δ separability (axis 4), or Authority Axis (axis 5) until much later. For pedagogical clarity, add a 1-page "preview of the five truth-axes" subsection in Chapter 1.3 with forward-references — this prevents readers from thinking the four-label system is the entire epistemology.

**I — Pass-47 principle integration is absent.** The 8 Pass-47 principles (Lazy Binary, Accurate Bluntness, Validly-Indeterminate Stance, Trend-Conditioning, Capstone, Radical Acceptance, TCAV, HPP/CSC) have not been integrated into Part One yet. They're foundational for honest reasoning under indeterminacy and naturally belong in a foundations section. **Fix:** add Chapter 5.5 or new Chapter 5 epilogue ("Eight Principles for Reasoning Honestly in the Indeterminate Zone") summarizing the 8 principles in plain language, with forward-references to anchor papers.

**J — ABC dissolution result canonized 2026-05-12 not yet in book.** This is now a corpus-architectural canonical claim (`urb_608` §9). For the foundational book, even a one-paragraph note in Chapter 5 (where i-cells and consciousness structure are introduced) is appropriate — pointing readers to the MIM-revision paper + ABC-dissolution result for the underlying ontology.
