## 2. Bengston Protocol — Summary

(Reconstructed from Bengston's published Journal of Scientific Exploration papers and *The Energy Cure*, 2010. Exact numbers below should be verified against original papers before publication.)

- **Subjects:** mice with mammary adenocarcinoma (often the BT-474 or transplanted-tumor model). Untreated base-rate survival: typically 0% by day 27 (well-documented in oncology literature).
- **Healer protocol:** 1 hour/day of "image-cycling" — healer generates ~20 positive personal-wish images, cycles through them rapidly, then "releases" while holding the cage of mice in attention. Continues for ~7-14 days.
- **Trained students:** Bengston trained skeptical graduate students with no prior healing experience. Replication across ≥10 published trials at Connecticut College, Saint Joseph's College, Arizona, etc.
- **Resonant bonding:** control-group mice sometimes also showed unexpected survival — Bengston attributes this to attention-coupling between experimenters who shared lab space (the *"resonant bonding"* hypothesis).
- **Aggregate published result:** across published trials, healed-group survival ≈ 50-90% in various studies, vs near-0% expected base rate. (Bengston & Krinsley 2000: 17/33 full remission in healed group; subsequent replications variable.)

**Open-access status:** JSE archives are open. Original papers available at scientificexploration.org/journal. Brandon can verify exact n / survival numbers without paywall.
