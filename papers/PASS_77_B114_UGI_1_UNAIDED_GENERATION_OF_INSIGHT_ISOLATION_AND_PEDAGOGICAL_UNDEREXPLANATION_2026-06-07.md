## 0. One-sentence version

Genuine insight must be **generated by the knower, not received ready-made** — so insight-production has to be protected from *premature external answers*, whether one's own (the self arm: **isolate, stop reading, free-write during generation**) or a teacher's (the pedagogical arm: **a good teacher withholds full answers** to force the student's own generative act) — with the **load-bearing guard** that this protection applies *only to the generation phase* and is worthless (indeed dangerous) without the subsequent validation phase.

---

## 1. The claim (two arms, one mechanism)

**Unifying mechanism.** Insight is a *generative* act, not a *transfer*. External input supplied *during* the generative window doesn't merely fail to help — it **pre-empts** the act: it anchors the mind on someone else's framing (functional fixedness / Einstellung), so the would-be original move is crowded out by a borrowed one. Therefore the generative act must be shielded from premature answers. Two arms apply the same mechanism at two scales:

- **UGI-1a — self arm (creative isolation).** "Isolate yourself, stop reading, simply write whenever you feel like it." During active generation, *withhold your own intake of others' framings* and write freely (free-writing lowers the editorial gate so latent connections surface). This is the **real-time operational practice** that produces the state UMA-1 names structurally: UMA-1 says *an untainted mind is the structural condition for foundational correction*; UGI-1a says *here is the do-this protocol that keeps the mind untainted at the moment of generation.*
- **UGI-1b — pedagogical arm (productive under-explanation).** "A good teacher doesn't fully explain their answers." The same shielding, done *for* a student: handing over a complete answer pre-empts the student's own generation (the **generation effect**: self-produced answers are understood and retained far better than received ones). The good teacher withholds the *answer* while still scaffolding the *search* — productive struggle, Socratic maieutics, desirable difficulty. UGI-1b is the **interpersonal mirror** of UGI-1a: you protect your own generativity by not over-reading; a teacher protects a student's by not over-explaining.

**Two-phase bound (load-bearing, NOT optional).** Both arms govern the **generation** phase only. Generation must be **followed by a validation phase** — checking the produced insight against the literature and against brutal-honesty novelty/error audit. This is exactly the Dual-Confidence two-scale structure (commitment-confidence while exploring; properly-weighted certainty in the background) and the Wallas incubation→verification sequence. **Isolation without the validation phase is not originality — it is uninformed reinvention** (the crank failure mode). Likewise under-explanation has a **floor**: "not *fully*" ≠ "not at all"; withholding must stay inside the learner's zone of proximal development, or it degrades from productive struggle into abandonment.

UGI-1 therefore **inherits UMA-1's candidate status** and its critical guard: untainted ≠ naive — broad prior modern-landscape exposure is presupposed, and the *temporary* reading-suspension is for generation, not a permanent anti-reading stance.

## 3. #69 honest accounting

**Not novel — at all.** Every component is well-established:
- *Self arm:* Schopenhauer ("reading is thinking with someone else's head"); Bloom's *anxiety of influence*; Wallas's incubation/illumination stages; functional fixedness & the Einstellung (set) effect (priming with prior framings constrains solutions); free-writing (Peter Elbow) / morning pages (Julia Cameron); solitude-and-creativity (Csikszentmihalyi, Cain's *Quiet*); default-mode-network / mind-wandering accounts of incubation. The deliberate "don't read the literature while working, check it after" practice is folklore among working mathematicians and writers.
- *Pedagogical arm:* Socratic maieutics; the **generation effect** (Slamecka & Graf, 1978); **desirable difficulties** (R. Bjork); **productive failure** (Kapur); discovery / inquiry learning (Bruner); constructivism (Piaget, Vygotsky's ZPD); the Zen koan tradition.
- *The two-phase resolution* is just Wallas (incubate → verify) and the corpus's own Dual-Confidence.

The intuition is in extremely crowded company; **no originality is claimed for either quote.**

**What is actually contributed (narrow).** (i) **Unifying** the self arm and the teaching arm under *one* mechanism (shield the generative act from premature external answers) — the two are usually theorized separately. (ii) **Wiring** the self arm in as the *operational practice* of UMA-1 (turning a standing structural condition into a do-this protocol) and the teaching arm in as UMA-1's *interpersonal mirror* + the transmission mechanism for LAS-1-style skills. (iii) Making the **anti-crank guard explicit and load-bearing** (the two-phase bound), so the principle cannot be read as "ignorance is virtue." The contribution is *integration + guarded operationalization*, not a new idea.

**Honest residues / guards:**
- **(R1) The anti-crank guard is the whole game.** Stripped of the validation phase, UGI-1a is indistinguishable from crankery — generate-in-a-vacuum-and-never-check reliably produces confident reinvention and error. UMA-1's "not naive" clause + §69 are not decoration; they are what separate UGI-1 from anti-intellectualism. This is stated as a falsifier (F2), not buried.
- **(R2) Under-explanation has a floor.** The minimally-guided-instruction critique (Kirschner, Sweller & Clark, 2006) shows that *too little* guidance harms learning. "A good teacher doesn't *fully* explain" is a claim about *calibrated* withholding inside the ZPD, **not** "explain nothing." Over-reading the quote inverts it into a known failure mode (F4).
- **(R3) Both anchors are self-report / n=1.** Brandon's own practice and "Ray as teacher" are *motivation*, not evidence (B49). They illustrate the hypothesis; they do not confirm it.
- **(R4) Apparent tension with §69's "always check the literature" is resolved by phase-separation, not by exception.** UGI-1 does **not** weaken §69; it confines reading-suspension to the generation window and *mandates* the §69 literature/novelty audit in the validation window. Generate untainted, validate ruthlessly.

## 4. Falsifiers (all OPEN — candidate)

- **UGI-1-F1 (isolation efficacy):** if controlled work shows that continuous literature engagement *during* idea-generation produces **equal or greater** originality than an isolate-then-validate protocol (no measurable anchoring/Einstellung cost), the self arm fails.
- **UGI-1-F2 (validation necessity — the anti-crank falsifier):** UGI-1 predicts that **isolation WITHOUT a subsequent validation phase raises the reinvention/error rate**. If removing the validation phase does **not** increase uninformed-reinvention, the two-phase bound is idle and the guard (hence the principle's honesty) is unsupported.
- **UGI-1-F3 (pedagogical under-explanation):** if full, complete explanation produces **equal or better durable understanding and transfer** than calibrated under-explanation (i.e. no generation-effect / desirable-difficulty benefit), the teaching arm fails.
- **UGI-1-F4 (under-explanation floor):** UGI-1 predicts calibrated withholding-within-ZPD **beats** near-zero guidance. If *minimal* guidance reliably **outperforms** calibrated withholding (less is always more), the "good teacher still scaffolds the search" guard fails.
