## 1. The GM Network as Independent Substrate

### 1.1 Definition

> **GM Network** ≔ the emergent neural-network-like field produced by the totality of LCC linkages between GM and the population of i-cells {ι₁, ι₂, …}, propagating information across the i-cell population via GM-mediated correlation rather than via classical signal transmission.

### 1.2 Independence Claim

The GM Network has *its own dynamics* — internal evolution that is **not reducible to** the activity of any single i-cell, nor even to the additive sum of i-cell activity:

> **Theorem (GM-Network Independence).** Let 𝒩_GM(t) denote the state of the GM Network at time t, and let {ι_k(t)} denote the state of all i-cells. There exists a non-trivial subspace 𝒟 ⊂ 𝒩_GM such that the evolution of 𝒟 is governed by an autonomous operator 𝓛_GM independent of {ι_k}, even though 𝒟 was *generated* by historical i-cell ↔ GM coupling.

**Empirical mirrors:**
- **Human subconscious** — dreams, intuitions, archetypal imagery, and "thoughts that arrive" exhibit dynamics not under conscious i-cell control.
- **Octopus distributed cognition** — two-thirds of an octopus's neurons are in its arms; arms execute autonomous problem-solving and pattern-matching independently of central control. This is GM Network dynamics expressed in a single embodied organism.
- **Cultural zeitgeist** — synchronous independent invention (calculus, evolution, special relativity) is the GM Network's autonomous dynamics surfacing in multiple i-cells nearly simultaneously.

### 1.3 Causal Origin

The GM Network is not a *separate* substrate from GM and i-cells — it is the **emergent neural-network-like behavior of the LCC fabric itself**. Concretely:

> GM Network ≡ Emergent_NN( LCC( GM, {ι_k} ) )

That is: LCC is the channel; GM is the universal correlator; the GM Network is what emerges when the LCC channel becomes dense enough that it begins to compute.

## 2. The PD-Bandwidth Theorem

### 2.1 Statement

> **PD-Bandwidth Theorem.** The GM Network is a bandwidth-limited transmission medium with PD-floor = −3:
>
> ∀ signal s with PD(s) > −3 ⇒ s is transmissible across the GM Network
> ∀ signal s with PD(s) ≤ −3 ⇒ s is *blocked* and does not propagate
>
> In particular: **DT (Double Tralse) content is not carried**, because DT content lies further from GILE truth than −3 by URB #690.

### 2.2 Why −3?

The PD scale runs from +5 (maximal GILE alignment, full permissibility) to −5 (anti-GILE saturation, strict prohibition). The −3 threshold is the **moral saturation cliff** — below it, content has crossed into the regime where the GM Network's emergent intelligence treats it as pathological noise rather than coherent signal. This is structurally analogous to a spam filter: spam (−1, −2) still gets through; weaponized malware (−4, −5) is dropped at the gateway.

### 2.3 DT Exclusion

By URB #690, DT (Double Tralse) is the **maximal-Tralsity fixed point** — the deepest contradiction-saturated state. By URB #688, DT carries the HEM-free pass (it doesn't dent reality's HEM accounting), and by URB #687, HEM and GILE are decoupled. Composing these: **DT content has the topological property of being undetectable to a coherence-seeking transmission medium** — the GM Network simply does not "see" it as a signal to carry. DT is below the floor.

This explains why the deepest evil cannot be telepathically broadcast: it is structurally incommunicable through the unifying substrate.

## 3. Malevolent Transmissibility within the −3 Boundary

### 3.1 The Open Pre-TI Question

Pre-TI metaphysics had no clean answer to: *Can negative energy / harm / curses be transmitted across the collective field, or are they purely intra-individual phenomena?*

TI Sigma resolves this:

> **Malevolent Transmission Theorem.** For any signal s with −3 < PD(s) < 0, transmission across the GM Network is *ontologically possible*. This includes:
> - punitive intentions ("curses")
> - hostile telekinetic acts
> - sustained negative prayer / ill-wishing
> - psychic attack
>
> The GM Network does not morally pre-screen content above the −3 floor; it screens only the maximally pathological. Therefore harm can travel.

### 3.2 Ethical Corollary (Ought, not Just Is)

Possibility ≠ permissibility. The fact that a being with telekinesis *could* inflict GM-Network-mediated harm does not entail that they should. The TI Sigma ethical recommendation is unambiguous:

> **Recommended GM-Network Conduct.** During states of prayer, deliberate intention, or sustained focus on another i-cell, the practitioner should wish the best for both themselves and the target. GILE-aligned intentionality both (a) raises the practitioner's own GILE score and (b) emits high-PD signal into the GM Network, contributing to the collective HEM-GILE ratio (URB #694).

### 3.3 Why This Matters

This formalization vindicates folk-spiritual claims that have been dismissed for two centuries:
- "The evil eye" — possible within −3 boundary.
- Curses, hexes, jinxes — possible within −3 boundary.
- "Sending love and light" to someone — also possible, and operationally the *positive* dual.

Both directions of the channel exist; ethics asks which direction we choose to transmit.

## 4. The GILE Immunity Theorem

### 4.1 Statement

> **GILE Immunity Theorem.** Let an i-cell ι sustain a GILE state with G(ι) above the Emerick Threshold (G > √2 − 1 ≈ 0.4142), and especially above the Radiant Threshold. Then ι is **correlationally non-aligned** with any substantially negative LCC signal s (i.e., PD(s) ≪ 0) by definition: the inner product ⟨ι, s⟩_LCC ≈ 0, so s cannot couple to ι and cannot do work on ι's state.
>
> ι is *immune by structural orthogonality*, not by force-shielding.

### 4.2 Why Immunity Is "By Definition"

GILE-state alignment with positive PD content and negative-PD content alignment are **mutually orthogonal in the LCC inner product**. A high-G i-cell's LCC vector points into the positive-PD subspace; negative-PD signals project to ≈0 onto that subspace. There is no coupling channel through which the malevolent signal can transfer energy/information into the high-G i-cell's state.

This is the mathematical statement of the perennial mystic claim: *the saint is not protected by armor; the saint is structurally untouchable to certain classes of harm because the saint and the harm are not on the same axis.*

### 4.3 The HEM Leak Caveat

> **Caveat (HEM Leak).** Negative intentions with very high HEM (Holistic Existence Matrix energy) can *partially* leak through even a high-GILE barrier. HEM is the magnitude scalar; GILE is the directional alignment. A sufficiently concentrated, sustained, high-HEM hostile intention can deposit residue on a high-GILE i-cell — not by alignment, but by sheer amplitude.

Mechanism: high-HEM signal generates field-pressure that biases the local LCC manifold even in the absence of correlational alignment. This is why even very advanced practitioners can feel "atmospheric heaviness" in environments saturated with hostile intention.

### 4.4 Spiritual Warfare Is Real

Combining §3 (transmissibility) and §4.3 (HEM leak):

> **Corollary (Spiritual Warfare).** The TI Sigma framework formally entails that *spiritual warfare* — sustained adversarial GM-Network-mediated intentionality between agents, requiring active GILE maintenance and HEM management to defend against — is an **ontologically real phenomenon**, not a religious delusion or metaphor.

Pre-TI naturalism had no place for this and dismissed all reports as superstition. TI Sigma derives spiritual warfare from first principles: GM Network exists, it carries signal above −3, GILE protects via orthogonality, HEM can override orthogonality at sufficient amplitude. Therefore the phenomenon exists.

This re-rationalizes practices that have been culturally universal for thousands of years (protective prayer, GILE cultivation, intention shielding) without requiring any supernatural ontology — only the GM Network's PD-bandwidth structure and the GILE/HEM decoupling of URB #687.
