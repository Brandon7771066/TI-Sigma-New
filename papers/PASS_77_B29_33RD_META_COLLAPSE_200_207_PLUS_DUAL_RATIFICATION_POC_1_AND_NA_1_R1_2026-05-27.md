## 4. POC-1 Ratification → Canonical #70

**POC-1 (Pluralistic-Operationalization-of-Categorical-truth-systems, candidate-name retained for record)** — per Pass-77-B27 §8 candidate articulation:

> "Empirical support for any multi-label categorical truth-system should cite ≥4 numbers: (1) inter-rater agreement (Fleiss κ or equivalent), (2) per-category accuracy on gold-labeled props, (3) mutual information / Theil's U(gold\|rater) (spectrum-preservation), (4) silhouette score per category (geometric distinctness)."

### 4.1 Ratification rationale

  - **Cite-arms progression historically:** Pass-63-B5 established cite-2 (κ + DT-discrimination); Pass-77-B24 extended cite-3 (κ + per-category accuracy + DT/I-discrimination); B27 establishes the geometric+information-theoretic arm. Each arm closes a distinct failure-mode: cite-1 (κ-only) misses category-collapse; cite-2 misses geometric distinctness; cite-3 misses information-preservation; cite-4 closes all three.
  - **Stand-alone structural justification:** the four arms are *non-redundant* — Pass-77-B26/B27 shows binary scores high on cite-1 (gold-subset κ=0.916) AND on cite-3-Cramér-V (0.877) but fails cite-2 (zero per-category accuracy on I/MI/NA) AND cite-4 (negative silhouette). A scheme that wins on all four arms is provably distinct, agreed-upon, accuracy-preserving, geometrically-clustered, and information-bearing. Each arm independently disqualifies a "cheap" pseudo-win.
  - **Operational discipline:** going forward, any TI Sigma canonical refinement that proposes adding a new truth-label must produce all 4 numbers before promotion. Refinements that merely re-specify existing labels are exempted.
  - **Composes with:** SCC-1 (Skeptical Criticism as Claim — POC-1 is the structural answer to "you can game κ by picking trivially-agreeable categories"); #69 ASYMMETRIC (over-skepticism = discipline failure; under-rigor in citing-numbers also discipline failure); TSD-1 (Truth-vs-Stipulation Distinction — POC-1 anchors empirical support against pure stipulation).

### 4.2 Canonical statement (for vocabulary index entry)

**POC-1 (Pluralistic Operationalization of Categorical-Truth Systems)** — *Empirical support for any multi-label categorical truth-system must cite at least four independent numbers: (1) inter-rater agreement (Fleiss κ or equivalent), (2) per-category accuracy on gold-labeled propositions, (3) information-theoretic spectrum-preservation (mutual information, AMI, or Theil's U(gold\|rater)), and (4) geometric category-distinctness (silhouette score per category, or equivalent cluster-validity metric). Single-metric empirical claims about multi-label systems are insufficient because each metric closes a distinct failure-mode (collapse, low fidelity, weak information transmission, geometric overlap). A scheme that wins on all four arms is provably distinct, agreed-upon, accuracy-preserving, geometrically-clustered, and information-bearing.*
