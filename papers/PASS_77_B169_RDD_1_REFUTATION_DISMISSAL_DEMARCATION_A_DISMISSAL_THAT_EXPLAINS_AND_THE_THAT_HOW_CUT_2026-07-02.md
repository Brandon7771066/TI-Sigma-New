## 1. The canonical term (RDD-1)

**Refutation–Dismissal Demarcation (RDD-1)** *(framework-internal, candidate)*:

- **Dismissal** — setting a claim/phenomenon aside *without accounting for it*: ruling it out, filing the evidence under "noise," or attaching a deflationary label, then changing the subject. A dismissal can be *correct* and still do **no explanatory work** — it never says *why* the claim seemed compelling or *why* careful people keep reporting the thing.
- **Refutation** — a dismissal that has **earned its verdict by explaining**: it engages the claim on the merits, defeats it, **and** accounts for the appearance (why the false thing looked true). Only a refutation adds to knowledge; a bare dismissal merely subtracts a claim from the table.

**Canonical statement:** *A refutation is a dismissal that counts as an explanation.* If a "no" cannot say why the thing seemed real, it is a dismissal, not a refutation — and the two must never be traded for each other.

This **sharpens**, and is continuous with, the ordinary logical sense of "refutation" already used in the falsification section of the epistemology chapter (URB-830): Popper's black swan is a genuine refutation *because* it does more than wave the universal law away — it exhibits the exact failing case and says why. Strip out the explanatory engagement and what remains is a dismissal in a refutation's coat. (No collision with the URB-830 usage; RDD-1 is its precisification.)

## 2. The THAT/HOW special case (ties to RIM, B168)

The highest-value instance of RDD-1 is the cut between **THAT** an effect obtains and **HOW** it is mechanically realized. Science settles the two independently all the time (natural selection known *that* before *how* of inheritance; aspirin analgesia known before its prostaglandin mechanism). Consequence, exact:

> **Refuting a HOW is not refuting the THAT.**

An effect can be instantiated by its **relational structure alone**, on a substrate not yet named — the B168 concept **RIM** (multiple realizability; Putnam/Fodor). Therefore killing one candidate mechanism is a *dismissal* of that mechanism, not a *refutation* of the effect, **whenever the effect has independent support**. Mistaking the death of a mechanism for the death of the effect is the commonest way a dismissal is smuggled in wearing the word "refutation."

## 3. Anti-cheat (what RDD-1 does NOT license)

- RDD-1 is **not** a license for credulity. The burden to *produce* the explanation stays on whoever claims a refutation.
- "There is still more to explain" is **never by itself** evidence for any particular positive claim (the open gap is a debt, not a proof — same guardrail as the cognitive-science-of-religion passage in ch14).
- RDD-1 lowers a **conceptual** bar only (it forbids counting a bare dismissal as a refutation). It lowers **no empirical** bar — mirroring RIM-F2. The LCC remains empirically **negative** twice over (B164 ds007471, B165 Depresjon); nothing here rescues it.
