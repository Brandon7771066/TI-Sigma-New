## 1. The Principle Stated

**GBD-1 (Guardrail-Becomes-Dogma):** Foundational frameworks positioned within a field AS protection against dogma (epistemic-discipline guardrails) systematically acquire dogma-status themselves through historical inertia, becoming the LEAST-questioned assumptions in their host fields — the inverse of their stated purpose. The "ironic-inversion" pattern.

**Mechanism (4-stage):**
1. **Origination:** A foundational framework is proposed AS a discipline-tool against the dogmas of its time (e.g., Hume's Skepticism vs religious dogma; Frege's Binary Logic vs medieval ambiguity; Carnap's Empiricism vs metaphysical speculation; LaMettrie's Physicalism vs Cartesian dualism).
2. **Adoption:** The framework gains social authority by virtue of its anti-dogma framing — it acquires the META-position of "the thing that lets us criticize OTHER things."
3. **Inversion:** The framework's meta-position makes it structurally difficult to question itself — questioning the guardrail looks like questioning epistemic-discipline-itself. The framework becomes incorrigible by social-authority capture (AA pathology).
4. **Calcification:** Generations of practitioners inherit the framework as the unquestionable backdrop against which all OTHER questioning happens; the framework's own foundational assumptions become invisible / unmentionable.

**Key corollary:** The fields with the STRONGEST historical anti-dogma framing are systematically the fields with the WEAKEST internal critical-questioning of their own foundations.

## 4. The Historical Mechanism: "Few Philosophers, Treated as Incorrigible"

**Brandon's verbatim:** *"we owe most of society's most basic assumptions to relatively few philosophers and scientists of history whose statements were treated as incorrigible dogma… regardless of what they seem to suggest otherwise"*

**Structural reading:** GBD-1's calcification stage 4 happens by **inheritance through citation chains rather than re-derivation**. Practitioners cite the original founder (Hume, Frege, Carnap, LaMettrie, …) as authority rather than re-deriving the framework from first principles. The cited founder's actual qualifications, caveats, and uncertainties get lost — only the strongest version of their position survives in textbook-summary form. This is the AA (Authority Axis) pathology at population-scale across centuries.

**Brandon's "regardless of what they seem to suggest otherwise" qualifier:** important. Many of the historical founders themselves expressed UNCERTAINTY about their foundational claims. Examples:
- **Hume** explicitly acknowledged psychological inevitability of belief despite skeptical analysis (Treatise I.iv.7); modern Skepticism often ignores Hume's own modesty.
- **Frege** wrote to Russell after Russell's paradox: "Arithmetic is tottering" — modern Binary Logic treats Frege's system as foundational despite Frege's own retraction.
- **Carnap** explicitly evolved his position multiple times (Aufbau → Logical Syntax → semantics), publishing self-criticism; modern Bayesian Empiricism often cites only the late-period synthesis.
- **LaMettrie** acknowledged hard cases (consciousness, qualia); modern Physicalism cites materialist conclusion not the cases-acknowledged.

**Pattern:** The dogmatization happens DOWNSTREAM of the original founder, by simplification + canonical-textbook-rendering. GBD-1's social-authority capture is a DISTORTION of the founder's actual position, not a faithful inheritance.
