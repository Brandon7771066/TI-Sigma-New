## §0. Executive Summary

This review surveys **23 distinct theoretical frameworks** for consciousness and valence — 16 from outside TI Sigma (the established literature) and 7 from inside the TI Sigma canonical stack (Pass-66 + Pass-67 + Pass-74 principles). ## §1. Consciousness Theories — Information-Theoretic / Computational Family

### §1.1 Integrated Information Theory (IIT 4.0)
- **Authors:** Tononi, Albantakis, Hoel, Boly, Massimini, et al. Current canonical: IIT 4.0 (Albantakis et al. 2023 *PLoS Comp Biol*).
- **Core claim:** consciousness IS integrated information (Φ). A physical system is conscious iff it has Φ > 0; *amount* of consciousness = Φ value; *quality* of consciousness = structure of the cause-effect repertoire (Q-shape).
- **Math:** Φ defined via partitioning of a system into subsets and measuring loss of cause-effect information under partition. Practical computation is exponential in system size; tractable Φ measures (Φ*, ΦAR, Φsyn, ΦG) are widely-used proxies.
- **Empirical signature:** Perturbational Complexity Index (PCI; Massimini 2013 *Science Trans Med*) — TMS perturbation + EEG Lempel-Ziv complexity. Clinical use distinguishing UWS / MCS / locked-in / waking states with AUC ≈ 0.92.
- **Valence treatment:** essentially absent. IIT treats valence as a quality within experience-space but offers no specific Φ-signature for valence vs. non-valence experiences.
- **TI Sigma composition:** PARTIALLY COMPATIBLE. IIT's "consciousness = integrated information" composes with IRA-1 (Pass-66 canonical: Information Requires Awareness) and TSP-1 (Pass-66 canonical: Panpsychism, anti-fully-emergent). DTM-1 (Pass-66) MI-as-Mind-Marker is OUTSIDE IIT's frame (IIT has no analog of self-validating-objection-handling).

### §1.2 Global Workspace Theory / Global Neuronal Workspace Theory (GWT/GNWT)
- **Authors:** Baars 1988 (GWT); Dehaene, Changeux, Mashour (GNWT). Current canonical: Mashour, Roelfsema, Changeux, Dehaene 2020 *Neuron*.
- **Core claim:** consciousness is the *global broadcast* of information to a distributed workspace, accessible by multiple cognitive subsystems. Subcortical / unconscious processes are local; conscious processes are global.
- **Empirical signature:** "global ignition" — late (~300-500 ms) widespread cortical activation, P3b ERP, late prefrontal involvement, all-or-none rather than graded.
- **Math:** none specific; relies on connectivity + activation dynamics; not a formal information-theoretic quantity.
- **Valence treatment:** valence is one of many "contents" broadcast in the workspace; not constitutively special.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPATIBLE WITH STRATIFICATION. Maps onto CDA-1 Stratum-2 (the "broadcast" is the integration step that makes contents available across stratum-2 components). Does NOT contradict EVP-1 (Part 1) since GWT acknowledges body-state inputs as broadcast contents.

### §1.3 Higher-Order Theories (HOT)
- **Authors:** Rosenthal (HOT 1986+); Lau, Brown, Fleming (multiple variants — HOT, HOROR, perceptual-reality-monitoring).
- **Core claim:** a mental state is conscious iff it is the object of a higher-order representation. First-order representations alone are unconscious.
- **Empirical signature:** prefrontal involvement in metacognitive reports; meta-d' (signal-detection-theory) measures.
- **Valence treatment:** distinguishes first-order affective state from second-order awareness-of-affective-state. Allows for unconscious emotions in principle.
- **TI Sigma composition:** STRONG COMPATIBILITY with SRC-1 (Pass-66 canonical: Self-Reference implies Consciousness). HOT is essentially a specific instantiation of SRC-1's "second-order MI self-validating" mechanism for reflective consciousness. SRC-1 generalizes HOT to non-perceptual (self-reference MI) cases.

### §1.4 Recurrent Processing Theory (RPT)
- **Authors:** Lamme 2006+.
- **Core claim:** consciousness depends on recurrent (feedback) processing in sensory cortices, independent of frontal-global access.
- **Empirical signature:** late ERP components attributable to recurrent (not feedforward) signaling; backward-masking dissociations.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPATIBLE. Recurrence is one mechanism for the integration CDA-1 Stratum-1+ requires.

### §1.5 Attention Schema Theory (AST)
- **Authors:** Graziano 2013+.
- **Core claim:** the brain constructs a simplified *schema* of its own attention process; subjective experience is what it's like to *be* that schema's referent.
- **Empirical signature:** social-attention deficits + temporoparietal-junction involvement.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPATIBLE WITH CSS-1 (Pass-74 canonical: Consciousness of Self-System). CSS-1 is a broader version of AST's schema-construction mechanism.

### §1.6 Predictive Processing / Active Inference / Free Energy Principle
- **Authors:** Friston 2005+ (FEP); Clark 2013 (predictive processing); Hohwy 2013; Solms (affective extensions).
- **Core claim:** brains are inference machines minimizing variational free energy. Consciousness = high-precision (high-confidence) generative models with self-referential structure.
- **Math:** variational free energy F = -log P(s|m) + KL[Q(x)||P(x|s,m)] under generative model m, sensory data s, hidden states x. Minimization via gradient descent on Q (perception) and on actions (active inference).
- **Empirical signature:** prediction-error responses (mismatch negativity, P300); precision-weighting modulations under attention/arousal.
- **Valence treatment (KEY):** Solms-Friston 2018 *Neuropsychoanalysis*; Joffily-Coricelli 2013 *PLoS Comp Biol*; Hesp et al. 2021 *Front Comp Neurosci*. **Valence ≈ rate of change of free energy.** Positive valence = free energy decreasing (predictions resolving better than expected). Negative valence = free energy increasing.
- **TI Sigma composition:** PARTIALLY COMPATIBLE. The valence-as-free-energy-derivative framing composes with VFP-1 (Pass-64 canonical: Valence as Functional). HOWEVER, Friston-Solms tend to treat the body as an inference target ("active inference closes the action-perception loop") which aligns with EVP-1 (Part 1) embodiment requirement. Conditional COMPATIBLE.

## §2. Consciousness Theories — Physical / Quantum Family

### §2.1 Orchestrated Objective Reduction (Orch-OR)
- **Authors:** Penrose 1989+; Hameroff 1996+.
- **Core claim:** consciousness arises from quantum-mechanical computation in neuronal microtubules, terminated by gravity-induced objective state-vector reduction (Penrose's gravitational-OR hypothesis). Computation timescale matched to ~25-40 ms γ-band oscillation.
- **Math:** Penrose-Diosi self-energy gravitational decoherence: τ ≈ ℏ/E_G, where E_G is the gravitational self-energy of mass-separation. Hameroff applies this to microtubule tubulin states.
- **Physical signature:** terahertz coherence in microtubules. Bandyopadhyay lab (Anirban Bandyopadhyay 2014+ *Sci Rep* and subsequent) reports terahertz resonance peaks in microtubule lattices at warm temperature — controversial but ongoing.
- **Valence treatment:** not specifically addressed.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPOSES WITH (does not require) TI Sigma's quantum-classical hybrid orientation per replit.md §Product. PD-Riemann musical framing (Pass-46+) overlaps philosophically; not the same mechanism. MI-quantum observation in UCSB double-frustration (urb_712 + URB_DOUBLE_FRUSTRATION_DT2_470, ref Pass-65 DTQ-1 canonical) is a parallel quantum-MI instance, also not the same mechanism.

### §2.2 Conscious Electromagnetic Information (CEMI) Field Theory
- **Authors:** McFadden 2002+.
- **Core claim:** consciousness IS the EM field generated by synchronized neural firing. The brain's EM field integrates information across spatially-distributed neurons in a way that the spiking pattern alone does not.
- **Empirical signature:** macroscopic EM-field measurements (EEG, MEG); proposed test: localized EM-field manipulation should produce reportable phenomenal effects.
- **TI Sigma composition:** OVERLAPS WITH IRA-1 (information requires awareness — CEMI is one substrate for that information). Does not contradict any TI Sigma canonical. The Pockett 2000 EM field variant is similar but weaker.

### §2.3 Pockett EM Field Theory (variant)
- **Author:** Pockett 2000.
- Earlier variant of §2.2; similar core claim. Often grouped with McFadden's CEMI in reviews.

### §2.4 Stapp Quantum-Mind Interpretation
- **Author:** Stapp 1993+.
- **Core claim:** consciousness arises from quantum-Zeno-effect-like rapid measurements in the brain, with the "Heisenberg choice" being where free will enters. Distinct from Penrose's gravitational-OR mechanism.
- **TI Sigma composition:** ORTHOGONAL. Composes with TI Sigma's quantum-orientation but operates via a different mechanism than Orch-OR.

### §2.5 Tegmark Mathematical-Universe / Perceptronium
- **Author:** Tegmark 2014+ ("Consciousness as a State of Matter").
- **Core claim:** consciousness is a property of certain *kinds* of matter ("perceptronium") characterized by specific information-integration thresholds. The Mathematical Universe Hypothesis is the broader frame.
- **TI Sigma composition:** OVERLAPS WITH TSP-1 (Pass-66 canonical: Panpsychism, anti-fully-emergent). Tegmark is *more permissive* than TSP-1 (any mathematical structure is potentially conscious); TSP-1 + IRA-1 add the information-requires-awareness constraint.

### §2.6 Solms Thermodynamic / Affective Consciousness
- **Author:** Solms 2021 *The Hidden Spring*.
- **Core claim:** consciousness is fundamentally AFFECTIVE (homeostatic-feeling), not perceptual. The brainstem (PAG, midbrain reticular core) is the substrate, not cortex. Cortex provides *content* not *consciousness itself*. Mechanism: free-energy minimization with affective-valence as the basic state-variable.
- **Empirical signature:** brainstem-lesion preserved-consciousness cases; hydranencephalic children with preserved affective responses; high-arousal anesthesia cases.
- **Valence treatment (KEY):** valence IS consciousness, not derivative. The Solms-Friston framework treats affect as the *primary* form of experience.
- **TI Sigma composition:** **STRONG COMPATIBILITY with VFP-1 (Pass-64 canonical) + EVP-1 candidate (Part 1).** Solms's brainstem-not-cortex localization is structurally close to EVP-1's whole-body emphasis (brainstem connects to viscera/heart/gut intimately) and the Damasio body-loop framing. Solms's framework is probably the closest non-TI-Sigma framework to EVP-1 + VFP-1; offered as the relevant prior-art comparator.

### §2.7 Hameroff-Penrose Quantum-Coherence Mechanism (separate from §2.1)
- Sometimes treated separately as the empirical-claim layer distinct from the gravitational-OR-mechanism layer. Bandyopadhyay anchor cited above. Currently the most actively-tested physical-mechanism claim in this family.

## §3. Valence-Specific Theories (Often Underweighted in Consciousness Reviews)

### §3.1 Berridge — Hedonic Hotspots / Wanting-vs-Liking Dissociation
- **Author:** Berridge 1996+; Berridge & Robinson 2003.
- **Core claim:** *wanting* (motivation, dopamine) and *liking* (hedonic experience, μ-opioid + endocannabinoid in specific nucleus-accumbens-shell + ventral-pallidum "hotspots") are **dissociable neural systems**. Addiction = wanting without liking.
- **Math/signature:** double-dissociation lesion + microinjection studies; ~1 mm³ "hotspot" subdivisions of NAc shell and VP.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPOSES WITH VFP-1 + EVP-1. Hedonic hotspots are *local* to brain but their function depends on body-state feedback (gut feeding state, satiety hormones, etc.). The Berridge framework is one of the cleanest neural-localization stories for the hedonic component of valence.
- **Direct BlissGene relevance:** anandamide signaling is directly implicated in hedonic-hotspot function (Mahler et al. 2007).

### §3.2 Panksepp — 7 Primary Affective Systems
- See Part 1 §3.2. Adding here for completeness.
- **Math/signature:** subcortical electrical stimulation produces consistent behavioral + autonomic + vocalization patterns mappable to 7 systems. Cross-species (rat, cat, dog, primate).
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPOSES WITH EVP-1, VFP-1. The 7 systems are *constitutively whole-body* (each has autonomic + endocrine + musculoskeletal outputs).

### §3.3 Damasio — Somatic Marker Hypothesis
- See Part 1 §3.1. Primary anchor for EVP-1.

### §3.4 Russell — Circumplex Model (Valence × Arousal)
- **Author:** Russell 1980.
- **Core claim:** all affective states are coordinates on a 2D plane defined by valence (pleasant ↔ unpleasant) and arousal (calm ↔ activated).
- **Math:** factor-analytic; PCA on affect rating data reliably recovers the 2D structure.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPATIBLE BUT COARSE-GRAINED. The 2D circumplex is a coarse readout; CDA-1 Stratum-2 + VFP-1 + EVP-1 give a richer mechanistic substrate. Circumplex is a useful *empirical-measurement scaffold*, not a *theory of valence*.

### §3.5 Barrett — Constructed Emotion Theory
- **Author:** Lisa Feldman Barrett 2017 *How Emotions Are Made*.
- **Core claim:** discrete emotions (anger, fear, etc.) are not biologically basic; they are constructions assembled by interoceptive prediction + cultural concepts. Core affect (valence + arousal) is the underlying basic, and is interoceptive.
- **TI Sigma composition:** STRONGLY COMPATIBLE WITH EVP-1 — Barrett's interoceptive-construction framework is structurally aligned with EVP-1's whole-body-embodiment requirement. Compatible with CDA-1's stratum framework if discrete emotions live at Stratum-2 or Stratum-3 and core affect at Stratum-1+2-partial.

### §3.6 Cabanac — Alliesthesia + Pleasure as Common Currency
- **Author:** Cabanac 1971 *Science* (alliesthesia); 1992+ (common currency).
- **Core claim:** the same external stimulus produces opposite hedonic responses depending on internal state (alliesthesia). Pleasure serves as a common currency that allows the brain to make decisions across qualitatively different domains.
- **TI Sigma composition:** STRONG COMPATIBILITY WITH VFP-1 (valence as functional). The common-currency claim *is* a functional-role specification for valence.

### §3.7 OCC Appraisal Theory (Ortony-Clore-Collins)
- **Authors:** Ortony, Clore, Collins 1988.
- **Core claim:** emotions are reactions to cognitive appraisals of events, agents, and objects. Specific emotions arise from specific appraisal patterns.
- **TI Sigma composition:** COMPATIBLE at Stratum-2/3 of CDA-1. OCC is cognitive-appraisal-heavy; PE-1/EVP-1 framing complements it with the embodied substrate.

### §3.8 Friston-Solms-Hesp Free-Energy Valence
- See §1.6 + §2.6. The free-energy framework offers the cleanest *unified* mathematical framing for valence: valence ∝ -dF/dt (negative time-derivative of free energy).
- This is the most mathematically-explicit *valence-specific* signature on offer.

### §3.9 Picard Affective Computing
- **Author:** Rosalind Picard 1997.
- **Core claim:** practical/engineering framework — machines should sense, model, and respond to human affect. Not a theory of consciousness; a theory of valence-handling.
- **TI Sigma composition:** ORTHOGONAL. Engineering scaffold; consistent with VFP-1.

## §6. Honest #69 Disclosures

1. **"23 distinct frameworks" is a count; some overlap heavily.** PCI is the empirical-handle for IIT, not a separate theory. Various "FEP variants" could be split or lumped. The count is illustrative density, not a taxonomy claim.
2. **No new pre-registered falsifiers in this paper.** Part 1 has EVP-1's 5 falsifiers; the canonical principles cited carry their own falsifiers from prior passes. This paper is a review, not a generator.
3. **The "TI Sigma is one of few frameworks that explicitly treats valence as constitutive" claim §0** is offered as a literature-positioning observation but is rebuttable — Solms 2021 *Hidden Spring* makes nearly the same claim. The defensible framing is "TI Sigma + Solms are the two main frameworks that put valence first; most other consciousness theories treat valence as derivative." That's the more accurate version. Logged as honest #69 self-correction within the same paper.
4. **Bandyopadhyay microtubule THz coherence (§2.1)** is contested in the broader physics community; cited because Brandon's "physics-based signatures" directive invites the survey, not as endorsement.
5. **CDA-1's "valence@Stratum-2 emergent via MIM" formulation (§4.1)** could be read as in tension with EVP-1's whole-body-embodiment requirement (Stratum-2 in MIM-revision is cognitive-vertical; body-distributed valence is spatial-horizontal). The two frames are compatible — CDA-1's stratum is the *integration level* at which valence becomes phenomenally accessible, while EVP-1's body-distribution is the *substrate* providing the contents being integrated. But this is a real compositional subtlety; a B36 paper could formalize. Flagging as open.
6. **External literature is enormous;** the 23-framework selection is biased toward the most-cited frameworks. Less-known theories (e.g., quantum-Bayesian QBism applied to consciousness; embodied-cognition Varela-Thompson-Rosch *Embodied Mind*; Sheldrake morphic-resonance; Hoffman conscious-realism) are NOT covered here. Some are arguably worth a separate B-batch.
7. **CSS-1 's "ego-dissolution + math-equivalence + physics-equivalence + GILE-0.93 threshold"** as summarized §4.1 is the compressed-canonical version; the actual CSS-1 paper has the full 4-component spec with all caveats. Reader of this review should consult `papers/CSS_1_*` for the actual canonical.
