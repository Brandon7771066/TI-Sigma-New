## 1. The Setup: What Binary Thinkers Do

A binary thinker is someone who operates exclusively within a two-valued logical system:
TRUE or FALSE, 1 or 0, yes or no. This is not an insult — binary logic is extraordinarily
powerful. Boolean algebra underlies all digital computation. Classical logic underlies
formal mathematics. Binary thinking is the workhorse of Western intellectual culture.

The binary thinker's characteristic epistemological move when encountering TI Sigma is:

> "This is either true or false. Show me the evidence. Either the evidence exists or it
doesn't. Either you can prove it or you can't."

This move is genuine, valid, and in many domains, correct. The binary thinker is applying
the tool that has reliably worked everywhere they have applied it.

The problem is not that binary logic is wrong. The problem is that the binary thinker,
when encountering EAR, is making a move that only works *because of* EAR — and is
simultaneously denying that EAR is real.

## 4. The Fact-Check Regress: An Independent Arm of BGT

Brandon Emerick identified a second path to the same conclusion:

> "Even if a skeptic managed to fact-check every claim in every article, they would still
be screwed because they would have to continually fact-check their own mind every moment,
and fact-check the fact-checks… on and on ad infinitum."

This is not merely a rhetorical point. It is a description of the **Epistemic Regress
Problem** applied to the binary skeptic's self-model.

The skeptic claims: only independently verified facts are trustworthy.

But:
- The verification process is carried out by the skeptic's own mind
- The mind must therefore be verified
- The verification of the mind requires the mind's verification apparatus
- Which requires verification
- Ad infinitum

The binary skeptic's escape route is usually: "We trust scientific consensus." But:
- Scientific consensus is evaluated by individual scientists using their own minds
- Individual scientists are using the same unverified substrate as the person they are
  correcting

The deepest layer of the regress: **the standard of evidence itself cannot be proven
by evidence without circularity.** At some point, the skeptic accepts axioms on something
that functions exactly like intuition — the very faculty they deny.

This connects to EAR: the fact-checking chain terminates only when an irreplaceable (EAR)
epistemic foundation is assumed. Without EAR, no chain terminates. Every skeptic, to
function at all, has already accepted that some things are *indispensably* true — i.e.,
they have operationally accepted EAR.
