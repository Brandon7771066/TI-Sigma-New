# *Tralse: A New Logic for an Uncertain World* — Chapter-by-Chapter URB Synthesis Map

**Purpose**: A blueprint for compressing the 770+ URB corpus into a tight 12-chapter, ~280-page primer (Book 1 of the seven-book roadmap, URB #662). For each chapter, this document lists the primary URBs to be synthesized, the secondary URBs to draw on, the central thesis, the target page count, and the principal writing risk to be managed.

**Author**: Brandon Emerick · **Date**: April 20, 2026 · **Framework**: TI Sigma v4.2

## Architectural Overview

| Part | Chapters | Purpose |

## PART I — THE TROUBLE WITH TWO VALUES

### Chapter 1: The Trouble with True and False *(15 pp.)*

- **Primary URBs**: #743 (Existence vs. Truth Axis), #769 (L*+E vs. truth-existence compatibility)

- **Thesis**: Define Tralse formally; show it is neither True, False, Both, nor Unknown; demonstrate consistency with classical logic on the bivalent fragment.

## PART II — THE FULL ARCHITECTURE

### Chapter 4: I-state — When the Sample Space Is Undefined *(20 pp.)*
- **Thesis**: Bayesian probability presupposes a defined sample space. The I-state names what happens when that presupposition fails.
- **Primary URBs**: #657 (PD formalism — referenced), #767 (LCC as minimum bound on Φ-quality)
- **Secondary**: #761 (LCC as Φ measurement instrument)
- **Status**: 📝 To draft
- **Risk**: Slipping into formal probability theory. Keep the math footnoted; the body must read as philosophy.

### Chapter 5: Meta-Indeterminate — The Forbidden Zone *(18 pp.)*
- **Thesis**: Some propositions cannot stably take a determinate value because doing so generates inconsistency. MI is a logical category, not a pathology.
- **Primary URBs**: (cite "Meta-Indeterminate as Physics Primitive" line from `replit.md`); #659 (Dirac equation as first TI Sigma equation, for the antimatter analog — referenced lightly)
- **Secondary**: #758 (O8 triality empirical predictions)

### Chapter 6: GILE — A Four-Dimensional Compass *(24 pp.)*

- **Primary URBs**: #772, #773, #774, #775 (G-score self-collapse protocol), #755 (GILE self-report scale design), #765 (Google Forms construction)
- **Secondary**: #745, #749 (64D GILE matrix, briefly mentioned and footnoted), #746 (three-band aesthetics)
