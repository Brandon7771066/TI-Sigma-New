## 2. The canonical refinement (formal statement)

**MR-IDC-1 (Incoherence-MI Containment Refinement to MR Truth Labels Canonical).** Incoherence and Meta-Indeterminate are not co-extensive. The relationship is **proper containment**:

```
{ Meta-Indeterminate statements }  ⊊  { Incoherent statements }
       (MR4 cell)                    (MR2 + MR4 cells jointly)
```

**(a) All MI statements are incoherent.** A MI statement satisfies "tralse AND NOT tralse" structurally, which entails incoherence.

**(b) NOT all incoherent statements are MI.** An incoherent statement that arises from **two valid-tralse statements in conflict** (rather than from a single proposition satisfying "tralse AND NOT tralse" within itself) is **MR2 Indeterminate**, not MR4 MI.

### 2.1 The structural criterion for MI (sharpened)

MI requires the **single-proposition** signature: a proposition P that, attempted as a unified mental actualization, collapses because P itself asserts and denies its tralse-status simultaneously (Russell / liar / square-circle / faulty-arithmetic class per Pass-65 canonical refinement). MI is **intra-propositional**.

### 2.2 The structural criterion for Tralse-Conflict Indeterminacy (newly canonized)

**Tralse-Conflict Indeterminacy (MR2)** arises when **two distinct propositions P₁ and P₂**, each independently valid-tralse (MR2 indeterminate), conflict with each other in such a way that their conjunction is unsupportable. Neither P₁ nor P₂ individually fails the MI structural criterion; the conflict is **inter-propositional**, not intra-propositional. The conjunction is incoherent without being MI.

### 2.3 The taxonomic refinement table

| Source of incoherence | Locus | Truth label | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| Single proposition asserting and denying its tralse-status | Intra-propositional | **MR4 MI** | "This sentence is false"; "too much truth" (Pass-67 batch-4 §3.2) |
| Two valid-tralse propositions in conflict | Inter-propositional | **MR2 Indeterminate (Tralse-Conflict)** | "more truth always better BY DEFINITION" ∧ "more truth NEITHER better nor worse" (Pass-67 batch-4 §4 conjunction) |
| Single proposition with classically-false content | Intra-propositional | **MR1 False** | "2 + 2 = 5" |
| Single proposition with classically-true content | Intra-propositional | **MR3 True** | "2 + 2 = 4" |

### 4.2 Operational test for distinguishing intra- vs inter-propositional incoherence

A 3-step diagnostic, modeled on the Pass-65 MI 3-step heuristic:

1. **Identify the unit of analysis.** Is the candidate "incoherent" thing a *single proposition P*, or a *conjunction P₁ ∧ P₂*?
2. **If single proposition:** attempt mental actualization per Pass-65 heuristic. If P collapses within itself → **MR4 MI**. If P doesn't collapse but its content is false → MR1. If P doesn't collapse and its content is true → MR3. If P is genuinely indeterminate at the propositional level → MR2.
3. **If conjunction P₁ ∧ P₂:** evaluate P₁ and P₂ individually. If each is independently valid-tralse (MR2) and their conjunction is unsupportable → **MR2 Tralse-Conflict Indeterminacy** (NOT MI). If either P₁ or P₂ is itself MI → the conjunction inherits MI-incoherence via the MI-component (under TI Sigma logical operators canonical §7.7.105 MI-absorbs-elsewhere absorption law).

### 4.3 Compatibility with the canonical logic (§7.7.105)

The three universal absorption laws (T-absorbs-in-OR, F-absorbs-in-AND, MI-absorbs-elsewhere) remain canonical. MR-IDC-1 does **not** modify them; it sharpens the **input classification** that determines which absorption-law applies.
