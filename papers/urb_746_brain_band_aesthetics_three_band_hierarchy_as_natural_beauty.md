## 2. Aesthetic Claim #1: The Three-Band Hierarchy as Natural Beauty

The brain organizes its electrical activity across three frequency bands:
- **Slow band** (~0.04-0.20 Hz): infraslow, default-mode oscillations
- **Alpha band** (~8-13 Hz): wakeful relaxation, attentional gating
- **Gamma band** (~30-100 Hz): conscious binding, working-memory coherence

**Why three, not two? Why three, not four?**

### 2.1 Two bands are insufficient
Two bands cannot stably preserve identity under environmental perturbation. With only two states, any external input pushes the system between them (binary collapse). There is no "buffer" state, no compositional reserve.

### 2.2 Four bands are wasteful
Four (or more) bands introduce redundancy and require additional metabolic cost. Empirical neuroscience consistently finds ~3 dominant bands, not 5+. The brain is **computationally minimal** at the band-count level.

### 2.3 Three is the structural sweet spot
Three bands enable:
- **Two-mode mixing** (slow ↔ alpha, alpha ↔ gamma) for compositional flexibility
- **One-mode buffering** (the middle band absorbs perturbation without collapse)
- **Stable hierarchy** (slow as ground, gamma as ceiling, alpha as bridge)

**The brain implements the simplest possible structure that preserves identity under environmental perturbation.** This is computationally optimal, metabolically efficient, and **aesthetically inevitable** — anything simpler fails; anything more complex wastes.
