## 0. Thesis

Two over-broad slogans have been doing too much work — in the wider methodological culture and, embarrassingly, inside this corpus (GIT-1/B122). LDD-1 corrects both:

- **LDD-1a — No-True-Scotsman demarcation.** Refining or defending a definition in response to a counterexample is a *fallacy* **only** when it (i) **equivocates** (silently shifts the term's sense mid-argument to dodge the counterexample) **or** (ii) is **ad hoc + totally critique-immunizing** (no independent motivation, and the move is engineered so that *nothing* could ever count against the claim). A definitional defense that is **principled, consistently applied, and still leaves the claim exposed to other tests** is legitimate conceptual work — indeed *required* by carving-at-the-joints (NAD-1).
- **LDD-1b — Falsifiability is not the demarcation of legitimacy.** A claim that cannot be *feasibly falsified* is **not** thereby illegitimate. It can still be **confirmed** (existential and framework claims are often verifiable-not-falsifiable) and can carry **nontrivial pragmatic value** (TPS-1 / NAD-1 cash value). Only the triple-empty claim — unfalsifiable **and** unconfirmable **and** pragmatically idle — is worthless.

## 1. LDD-1a — when "No True Scotsman" actually bites

The textbook NTS pattern ("no *true* Scotsman puts sugar on porridge") is fallacious because the arguer **redefines the predicate ad hoc, mid-argument, purely to neutralize a counterexample, while pretending the meaning never moved** — that's equivocation plus immunization. Strip either feature and the move is fine:

- **Defining a term is not a fallacy.** Every science narrows folk terms ("fish" excludes whales; "planet" was redefined to exclude Pluto on principled dynamical grounds). Refusing to ever refine a definition would freeze inquiry.
- **Defending a definition against a sloppy counterexample is not a fallacy.** If someone "refutes" a claim about *mammals* by pointing at a whale-as-fish, correcting them is not NTS — it is insisting on the joint that was there all along.
- **The real fault is method, named precisely by Lakatos:** an auxiliary/definitional move is illegitimate when it is **degenerating** (purely face-saving, predicts nothing new), and legitimate when it is **progressive** (independently motivated, sharpens the construct, opens new tests). *This is exactly Brandon's "unless it evades any potential critiques."* The demarcation is **progressive-vs-degenerating**, not "any redefinition whatsoever."
- **Duhem-Quine backs this:** no hypothesis meets evidence alone; a counterexample always lets you revise *somewhere*. So "you adjusted a definition" cannot by itself be the crime — the question is *whether the adjustment is principled and still falsifiable-elsewhere*.

**Test for a legitimate definitional defense (all three required):**
1. **No equivocation** — the refined sense is used consistently from here on, and prior uses are reconciled (errata, not silent swap).
2. **Independent motivation** — the refinement tracks a real joint (NAD-1) / is supported by reasons other than "it saves my thesis."
3. **Residual exposure** — after the refinement, the claim *can still be wrong* on other tests (it is not now immune to everything).

Fail any one → it has slid toward the genuine NTS fallacy.

## 2. LDD-1b — unfalsifiable ≠ illegitimate

Strict Popperian falsificationism is already **retired in this corpus (URB-830:** TIU = |log P(H|e)/P(H)|, a Bayesian/asymmetric confirmation measure). LDD-1b spells out the consequence Brandon names:

- **Confirmation is real and asymmetric to falsification.** Existential claims ("there exists a GILE-complete restrained agent") are **verifiable by a single instance, not falsifiable** in finite time — yet obviously legitimate and decidable in the confirming direction.
- **Framework / interpretive / definitional claims** ("model life as free-energy minimization"; "carve truth as a map") may not be falsifiable as stated, yet have large **pragmatic value** — they reorganize a domain, generate tractable predictions downstream, and (per B122, NAD-1, TPS-1) "even a redefinition has cash value." Their merit is *fruitfulness*, judged by what they let you do, not by a falsification test they were never meant to pass.
- **Bayesian legitimacy criterion (replaces naive falsifiability):** a claim earns its keep if it (a) shifts P(H|e) for *some* feasible e (confirmable), **or** (b) yields pragmatic leverage (better predictions, unification, decision-relevance). **Worthless ⇔ unfalsifiable ∧ unconfirmable ∧ pragmatically idle** — the *conjunction*, not falsifiability-failure alone.

## 3. The #69 residue — what the corpus was *right* to worry about (don't over-correct)

Brandon's correction is right, but symmetric honesty forbids throwing out the real constraint that GIT-1/B122 was groping toward. The legitimate worry in the GIT-1 case was **never actually a No-True-Scotsman problem** — it was a **measurement-contamination** problem:

- The GIT-1 fake "AUC 1.000" came from **scoring the predictor (GILE) from the very outcome it was predicting** (truth-vindication). That is a *methodological* defect (circular *measurement*), and B122 already half-corrected the label (circularity → "hindsight-contamination"). LDD-1 finishes the job: **the standing rule should be "no outcome-contaminated measurement," NOT "no definitional defense."** The two were conflated under the "No-True-Scotsman" slogan; they are different sins.
- So the anti-cheat survives in its *correct* form: you may define and defend GILE-intelligence freely (LDD-1a) — what you may **not** do is *measure* it using the outcome you're trying to predict (a contamination rule, satisfied equally by mainstream "g predicts income" research).
- LDD-1b similarly does **not** license vacuity: a claim that is simultaneously unfalsifiable, unconfirmable, *and* pragmatically idle is still rejected. Brandon's point widens the gate to confirmation+pragmatics; it does not remove the gate.

**Net:** the bar moves from "any definitional move / any unfalsifiable claim is suspect" → to the two precise, defensible bars: **(measurement) no outcome-contaminated scoring; (definitional) no equivocating, all-critique-immunizing redefinition.**

## 4. ERRATA / refinement applied to existing principles

- **GIT-1 (B121/B122) standing rule — RESTATED:** ~~"No-True-Scotsman barred"~~ → **"(i) score GILE outcome-blind (no measurement contamination); (ii) any definitional refinement must be progressive, not degenerating (LDD-1a)."** The original phrasing wrongly implied *defining* GILE-intelligence was itself suspect; per LDD-1a it is not — only contaminated *measurement* and ad-hoc *immunization* are.
- **SIS-1 (B123) #69-limit #1 — RESTATED:** ~~"admissible ONLY as substantive, never definitional, else No-True-Scotsman"~~ → **"admissible as a substantive trait→behaviour claim; a definitional component is *also* permitted provided it is progressive (independently motivated, still exposed to SIS-1-F1..F4) and is measured behaviour-blind."** SIS-1's actual safeguard is its open falsifiers + behaviour-blind GILE measurement, not a blanket ban on definition.

These are refinements (relabelings/sharpenings), **not** new principles and **not** retractions of GIT-1/SIS-1's substance. Count unchanged.

## 5. Falsifiers / boundaries (OPEN)

- **LDD-1-F1:** exhibit a definitional defense that meets all three §1 criteria (no equivocation, independent motivation, residual exposure) yet is uncontroversially fallacious → §1 test is insufficient.
- **LDD-1-F2:** exhibit a claim that is unfalsifiable, *confirmable*, and yet contributes *negative* epistemic value (confirmation misleads) → LDD-1b's confirmation criterion needs a quality qualifier.
- **LDD-1-F3 (anti-cheat tripwire):** if LDD-1 is ever used to wave through a claim that is in fact triple-empty (unfalsifiable ∧ unconfirmable ∧ idle) or to relicense outcome-contaminated measurement, it has been abused — LDD-1 then indicts the user, not the standard.
