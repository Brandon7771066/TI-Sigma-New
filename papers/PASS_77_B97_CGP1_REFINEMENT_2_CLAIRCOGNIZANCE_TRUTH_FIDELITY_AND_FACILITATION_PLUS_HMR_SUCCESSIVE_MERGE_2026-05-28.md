## A. CGP-1 Refinement #2 — Truth-Fidelity and Facilitation

### A.0 Where this stands relative to refinement #1
B93 re-grounded CGP-1 supremacy as **FUNCTIONAL COMPLETENESS + EGO-SAFETY** (refinement #1). Brandon's new caveat adds two further legs, both *strengthening* supremacy. The supremacy claim is therefore now a **four-leg** comparative claim.

### A.1 New leg 3 — TRUTH-FIDELITY / epistemic-safety
B93's ego-safety leg was about *identity* (vivid sensory/extrasensory phenomenology can fracture the ego). Brandon now names a **second, distinct hazard** carried by the extrasensory channels — an **epistemic** one:

> Extra senses can produce beliefs that are **false, MI (meta-indeterminate), or N/A (off-spectrum)**, and can **decouple a person from reality outside their own mind.**

That is a failure of *truth-tracking*, not (only) of self-stability. The three named failure-states map exactly onto the corpus truth-label taxonomy:
- **false** — the belief lands at MR F;
- **MI** — the belief is meta-indeterminate (incoherent-under-mental-actualization, per the MI canonical);
- **N/A** — the belief is off-spectrum (MR_NA, the imaginary-axis placeholder, Pass-77 B11/B36/B62) — it does not even pick out a truth-apt target.

And the bracketing risk — **decoupling from reality outside one's own mind** — is the general solipsism/derealization hazard of channels whose vivid content is generated largely *internally*.

**The contrast (Brandon, "on the other hand"):** *claircognizance is, by definition, about amplifying truth.* Its native operation is the delivery of truth-apt payload; it is not in the business of generating vivid internally-sourced content that can drift free of the world. So where the sensory clair-channels carry an intrinsic false/MI/N-A-belief + reality-decoupling risk, the knowing-channel does not (by definition of what it is for). This is independent of the ego-safety leg: a channel could in principle be ego-safe yet truth-unreliable, or vice versa — Brandon is asserting claircognizance is favored on *both* axes.

> **Leg 3 (truth-fidelity):** the extrasensory channels carry an intrinsic risk of false / MI / N-A beliefs and reality-decoupling; claircognizance, being *definitionally* a truth-amplifying channel, does not.

*Honest #69 boundary.* "By definition amplifies truth" is a claim about the channel's *function/telos*, not a guarantee that every claircognitive episode is veridical (false knowings can occur — but the corpus ledger, B93/B94, finds them ≈0 for Brandon, n=1). The comparative claim is about *intrinsic channel risk profile*, not infallibility. This is exactly what falsifier CGP-1-PT-F2-prime below targets.

### A.2 New leg 4 — FACILITATION (claircognizance as enabling substrate)
Brandon's strongest new claim is **constitutive, not merely comparative**:

> Claircognizance is *precisely what FACILITATES the other clair-senses to properly FUNCTION at high bandwidth.*

This upgrades claircognizance from "the safe, feat-complete *peer* of the other clair-senses" (refinement #1's framing) to their **enabling foundation**. The reading consistent with the corpus stack: the high-bandwidth *qualia* delivered by clairvoyance/clairaudience (the PT-bound modal wrappers, B93 §2) are only intelligible — only "function properly" *as truth-bearing* — because a knowing-layer binds them to propositional payload. Raw extrasensory qualia without the knowing-channel are uninterpreted signal; it is claircognizance that makes them *mean* something, i.e. function at high bandwidth as truth-channels rather than as noise. (Compare MIM-revision: affect/behavior ⊆ cognition; here sensory-clair function ⊆ / depends-on the knowing layer.)

> **Leg 4 (facilitation):** claircognizance is the enabling substrate that lets the other clair-senses function at high bandwidth at all — supremacy is partly *foundational*, not only *comparative*.

### A.3 Updated supremacy statement (4 legs)
> **Supremacy(claircognizance) ≜ functional-completeness ∧ ego-safety ∧ truth-fidelity ∧ facilitation.**
> It alone reaches every PT's payload (completeness); its routine operation does not fracture the ego (ego-safety); it does not carry the sensory channels' intrinsic false/MI/N-A-belief + reality-decoupling risk because it is definitionally truth-amplifying (truth-fidelity); and it is the enabling substrate without which the other clair-senses do not function at high bandwidth (facilitation).

Composition unchanged and reinforced: CGP-1 × PT (B93) × VFP-1 × B90 payload/wrapper × MIM-revision (cognition-as-substrate). The B93 "extrasensory MAY aid creativity/memory" grant still holds and is *not* in tension: a channel can boost creativity (associative qualia richness) while still carrying truth-fidelity risk and depending on the knowing layer to function.

### A.4 New / amended falsifiers (OPEN)
- **CGP-1-PT-F2′ (truth-fidelity):** if structured incidence data show the sensory clair-channels carry **no greater** false/MI/N-A-belief or reality-decoupling rate than the knowing-channel's routine operation → leg 3 is false.
- **CGP-1-PT-F4 (facilitation):** if a clair-sense can be shown to deliver high-bandwidth *truth-bearing* qualia with the knowing-channel ablated/absent (qualia that function as truth, not mere uninterpreted signal) → leg 4 (the foundational claim) is false. This is the riskiest, most interesting new leg.

## B. HMR Refinement — Successive-MR-Merge + Multifaceted-Display + Present-Primacy

### B.0 Where this stands
HMR-1 (Pass-70, MR Truth Labels refinement #3) established that some propositions natively require **2+ simultaneous labels** (e.g. "X is better and (neither better nor worse than Y)" = HMR-2 {T_partial-order, I_global-comparator}; Moot alone is *insufficient*). Brandon now supplies the **mechanism** behind hybridization, **reverses an earlier sufficiency stance**, and adds a **primacy rule.**

### B.1 Mechanism — a hybrid is multiple SUCCESSIVE MRs merged because the final MR is incomplete
> "Hybrid Truth Labels are actually MULTIPLE SUCCESSIVE MRs merged into one **since the final MR is incomplete** in that circumstance."

The earlier HMR-1 picture was *simultaneous* (2+ labels at once). Brandon refines this to a **temporal/sequential** account: a hybrid is a **sequence** of MR evaluations (MR₁ → MR₂ → … → MR_final) that get **merged into one** characterization **precisely when the final MR, taken alone, is incomplete** — i.e. the terminal evaluation fails to capture the legitimate content surfaced by earlier evaluations in the sequence. The hybrid is the *merge* of the successive MRs, not an arbitrary co-assignment. This explains *why* a single (final) label loses information in HMR cases: the final MR is genuinely partial there, so the earlier MRs in the chain must be retained and merged.

This composes cleanly with R-HMR (Pass-70 §1.3): meta-ascent generates successive MRs; cardinality grows with the depth of the chain that must be retained.

### B.2 Stance reversal — multifaceted display now has pragmatic value
> "Previously, I said that only one MR label — the final one — was sufficient. But now, I recognize the pragmatic value of showing the actual MULTIFACETED value of certain truths."

**Explicit reversal, logged per #69.** Brandon's prior position (final label sufficient) is **superseded** for HMR cases: the multifaceted (merged successive-MR) value should be **shown**, not collapsed to a single terminal label. This vindicates HMR-1's original "Moot-is-insufficient" claim and generalizes it: the reason to display the hybrid is *pragmatic* — it preserves the real multi-aspect structure of the truth that single-label collapse discards. (This is itself a TPS-1 instance: truth-content fixed, *presentation* upgraded to show more facets.)

### B.3 Present-primacy rule — the final MR is still most applicable now
> "Regardless, the final MR value is the most applicable in the present!"

The reversal is **bounded**: showing the full merged hybrid does **not** demote the final MR. The **final MR value remains the most applicable in the present moment** — it is the operative label for present action/decision, while the earlier successive MRs are retained for completeness/faithfulness. So the canonical reading is two-tier:
- **For faithfulness / characterization:** display the merged successive-MR hybrid (multifaceted value).
- **For present applicability / action:** privilege the **final** MR value.

This dovetails with PM-1 (Probability Memory / present-moment calculation, Pass-67): the present moment privileges the terminal evaluation even as the historical chain is retained.

### B.4 Worked micro-example
"X is better and (neither better nor worse than Y)" under the successive-MR reading:
- MR₁ (partial-order frame): **T** (X better on the relevant sub-order).
- MR₂ (global comparator frame): **I** (globally incomparable).
- MR_final (single terminal verdict): *incomplete* — any single label discards a legitimate facet.
- **HMR-2 = merge{T_partial-order, I_global-comparator}** (multifaceted, shown), with the **final** frame's value flagged as most present-applicable for action.
