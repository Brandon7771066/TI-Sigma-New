## 3. The Identification with General Relativity

In conventional GR, spacetime is a 4-manifold with a metric g_μν that determines distances, angles, and curvatures. The Einstein equations relate the metric to the stress-energy tensor:

> R_μν − (1/2) R g_μν = (8πG/c⁴) T_μν

- **Spacetime points** = "snapshots" of the multi-BOK at single instants → points in a slice of ℳ

- **Einstein equations** = the consistency condition that the metric on ℳ be compatible with the local multi-BOK structure

This identification has the same form as the Wheeler-DeWitt approach to quantum gravity (where superspace is the moduli space of 3-geometries), but with a richer underlying object (multi-BOK rather than just 3-geometry).
