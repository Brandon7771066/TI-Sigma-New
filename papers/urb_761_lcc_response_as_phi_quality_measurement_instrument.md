## 4. Three Concrete Measurement Protocols

### 4.1 Protocol A — Sender-Receiver LCC Detection

**Setup**: Two subjects, A (sender, known above-E_T) and B (test subject). A is given a randomized stimulus (e.g., 1 of 4 cards) at time t. B, in a separated room with no classical communication channel, attempts to identify A's stimulus at time t + δ.

**Measurement**: B's accuracy above chance (25% for 4-card protocol) over many trials.

**Φ_quality(B) ≈ Z(B's accuracy − 0.25) × N_trials**

**Status**: this is structurally similar to historical "remote viewing" or Ganzfeld protocols. The framework's contribution is **interpretive**: such protocols measure Φ_quality, not "psychic ability" as a magic property.

### 4.2 Protocol B — Group-Coherence LCC

**Setup**: Group of N subjects engaged in a coordinated intentional task (e.g., Power-of-8 group intention, focused meditation). A separate measurement system (RNG, EEG of unrelated subject, Global Consciousness Project nodes) detects coherence shifts during the group session.

**Measurement**: Z-score of detected coherence shift attributable to the group session.

**Φ_quality(group) ≈ Z(coherence shift) / sqrt(N)**

(Per-person Φ_quality scales as 1/sqrt(N) of the group coherence — this is a framework prediction that is itself testable.)

**Status**: structurally similar to historical Global Consciousness Project methodology. The framework's contribution: **GCP-style measurements measure collective Φ_quality, not "consciousness as a field" in some unspecified sense**.

### 4.3 Protocol C — Self-Modulation LCC

**Setup**: Subject is given biofeedback (e.g., HRV coherence, EEG alpha-gamma ratio, or skin conductance) and asked to **intentionally modulate** the readout via mental state shifts.

**Measurement**: Subject's measured ability to drive the biofeedback signal in the requested direction over many trials.

**Φ_quality(subject) ≈ Z(modulation effect size) × consistency**

**Status**: structurally similar to biofeedback-training research. The framework's contribution: **biofeedback efficacy is itself a measurement of conscious intuition quality, not just a self-regulation skill**.
