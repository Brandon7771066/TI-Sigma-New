## 1. The paradox B99 left open
B99's SUP-1 and its consciousness gloss assert that the reality-conferring ground is an "illusion." Taken in ordinary (bivalent) terms this is self-undermining: if the foundation of reality is *unreal*, the whole edifice it founds should collapse. Calling the **grounding** of reality "unreal" looks like sawing off the branch you sit on. **Why is it not?**

## 2. The answer — TRG-1
> **TRG-1 (candidate).** The grounding of reality is called "unreal/illusion" **because reality itself isn't TRUE — it is TRALSE.** Per UDT-1, the ground state of reality is **MR2 Indeterminate** (tralse-soup substrate), *not* classically-True and *not* classically-False. "Illusion" is the **binary-collapse misname** that a bivalent mind reaches for when it correctly senses the ground is *not crisply True* — but, lacking a third primitive, it can only file "not-True" under "false / unreal / illusion." The accurate label is **tralse-real**: real enough to ground, yet not classically-True. The "illusion that makes things real" is therefore not a contradiction but a **literal tralse object — real ∧ not-true.**

Three moves:
1. **"Illusion" is a category error of bivalence, not a metaphysical verdict.** Binary logic offers only {real=True, unreal=False}. A tralse ground is *neither*, so under bivalence it gets mislabeled "illusion" by default. The word reports the *observer's missing category*, not the ground's nonexistence.
2. **Tralse-real dissolves the regress/eliminativism fork.** Bivalent metaphysics faces a forced dilemma about any proposed ground: call it **True/real** (then it demands its own further ground — regress — and cannot be the "illusion" mystics and physicists keep reporting) or call it **False/unreal** (eliminativism/nihilism — reality has no ground at all). TRG-1 takes the third branch: the ground is **tralse**, so it can be *simultaneously* reality-conferring **and** "illusory" (not-True) without contradiction. The fork was an artifact of having only two values.
3. **Consciousness-as-reality-conferring-illusion (B99) is the inside view of the tralse ground.** What B99 described phenomenologically — consciousness as "the illusion that makes things real" — is exactly TRG-1 apprehended from within: the i-cell *is* a locus of the tralse ground, so its own self-presentation carries the same real∧not-true signature. SUP-1's "embrace the Existence/Truth illusion" is then the *practical* stance whose *metaphysical ground* is TRG-1.
