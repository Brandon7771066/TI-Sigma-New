## Confound Flag (PRA discipline)

The primary prediction (7) coincides with the documented universal human-7-bias in 1–10 selection tasks (~28–30% pick rate across psychology literature). **A hit on 7 cannot cleanly distinguish LCC/psi-signature inference from baseline cognitive bias.** The Brandon-specific corpus prior ADDS to the universal bias rather than replacing it, but the additive component is empirically inseparable from the universal one in a single trial. Disambiguation requires either:
1. A multi-trial sequence with non-7 targets (where universal bias would predict misses but Brandon-specific corpus might still hit), or
2. A target outside the universal-bias attractor (e.g., 4, 6, 8 — which have low universal-bias mass and would be a cleaner test of LCC-specific inference if Brandon picks one).

Honest expected outcome calibration: in the universal bias picks the 35.1% LCC rate is partially redundant with bias. In the non-attractor picks the rate would likely be much lower.
