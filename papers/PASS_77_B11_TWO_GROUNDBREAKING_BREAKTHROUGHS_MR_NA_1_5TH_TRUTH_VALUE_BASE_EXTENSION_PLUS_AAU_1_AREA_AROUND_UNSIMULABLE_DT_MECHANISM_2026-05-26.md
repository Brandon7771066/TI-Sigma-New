### 1.1 The candidate

**Candidate canonical statement (MR-NA-1):** A 5th canonical truth value, **NA = No-Answer / Genuinely-Undecided**, is added to MR Truth Labels canonical-base. NA applies to (paradigm case) **undecided future states that are neither likely to happen nor unlikely to happen** — states for which there genuinely is NO fact-of-the-matter biasing toward either resolution. NA is **categorically distinct from I (Indeterminate)**: I applies to states that are *claimed* likely/unlikely (probability-biased toward one resolution, with the claim itself being the determinate content); NA applies to states where no such probability-biasing claim is warranted because the universe contains no fact-of-the-matter to ground the probability-bias.

### 1.2 Disambiguation — I-vs-NA

**The critical distinction Brandon makes:**
- *I (Indeterminate)*: "It will probably rain tomorrow" or "It is unlikely the coin will land on its edge" — the *claim* is determinate (a probability-bias is asserted); the *resolved-truth* awaits. Or for non-future I: "The patient's diagnosis is between A and B" — the *fact-of-the-matter EXISTS* but is unresolved-to-the-claimant; uncertainty is epistemic.
- *NA (No-Answer)*: "Will I think about a green elephant in 17 minutes?" (no fact-of-the-matter biases this yet; the universe has NOT been steered toward either outcome by prior physical-deterministic chains in a way that grounds a probability-bias claim) OR Aristotle's sea-battle ("Will there be a sea-battle tomorrow?" — Aristotle's argument that future-contingents about free-choice-events lack truth-value PRIOR to the choice). The *underlying state* genuinely has no probability-bias to claim.

**Concrete cases that resolve to NA (not I):**

| Case | Why NA not I |
|---|---|
| Aristotle sea-battle tomorrow (will it occur?) | Free-choice intervening; no probability-bias warranted prior to choice |
| Quantum-superposition pre-measurement: which slit did the photon go through? | Genuinely no fact-of-the-matter pre-measurement per orthodox Copenhagen / per Bell-theorem-implications |
| Outcome of coin-flip not-yet-flipped where coin-symmetry-is-perfect | True 50-50; symmetric undecidedness; the "50-50" is the absence-of-bias, not the presence-of-probability-claim |
| Genuinely-chaotic system trajectory in specific phase region | Lyapunov-divergence makes probability-bias-claim epistemically-unwarranted in the long-time-limit |
| Free-will-relevant choice not-yet-decided by agent who has-not-yet-engaged-deliberation | No fact-of-the-matter biases the decision until deliberation occurs |

**Concrete cases that resolve to I (not NA):**

| Case | Why I not NA |
|---|---|
| "It will probably rain tomorrow given the weather-system" | Probability-bias is grounded in atmospheric-physics-determinism; claim is determinate |

**Note:** NA can **collapse to T, F, or I once the future resolves or once probability-biasing facts emerge.** NA is therefore **inherently temporally-indexed** (in the paradigm-case) — at least for future-state applications. ## 2. BREAKTHROUGH 2 — AAU-1 Area-Around-Unsimulable simulation mechanism for MI/MI

### 2.1 The candidate

**Candidate canonical statement (AAU-1):** When the mind encounters a MI/MI (Mental-Incoherence) statement (e.g., 2+2=5; round-square; Russell's set-of-all-sets-that-don't-contain-themselves), the mind **cannot simulate the impossibility-center directly** (per Pass-65 MI-inconceivability-under-mental-actualization canonical-refinement). Instead, the mind **simulates the *area around* the impossibility-center** — the conceptual-neighborhood of related-but-coherent statements, the contextual-frame, the implications of the impossibility-if-it-were-coherent, the relationship of the impossibility to other coherent statements. The **gap** between (a) the simulated-area-around and (b) the unsimulable-impossibility-center is what is **experienced as MI-frustration** (the phenomenological signature of MI-encounter that distinguishes it from mere unfamiliarity or mere confusion). **The magnitude of frustration tracks the magnitude of the gap.** As the mind expands the simulated-area-around over time (encounters more related-statements, develops deeper context, builds the implications-network), **the gap shrinks relative to the simulated-region** and frustration → moot. Incoherence-becomes-moot is the phenomenological terminus of successful AAU-1 area-around-expansion.

**Brandon-articulation key phrases (verbatim):**
- "*the mind simulates 2+2=5 by simulating THE AREA AROUND THAT WHICH IT CANNOT SIMULATE*"
- "*The frustration of MI statements stems from the magnitude of this gap*"
- "*As the mind grasps the statement over time and its relationship to other statements, the gap of frustration shrinks and incoherence becomes moot*"

### 2.2 Why this is groundbreaking

**FIRST MECHANISTIC PSYCHOLOGY-OF-MI-FRUSTRATION THEORY IN CORPUS.** Prior canonical work on MI (Pass-65 inconceivability-under-mental-actualization; SRC-1 second-order self-validating; MR-IDC-1 MI-vs-Incoherent containment; DTM-1 MI-as-Mind-Marker) addressed (a) the *logical-structure* of MI (τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P)), (b) the *assignment-criteria* for MI-vs-non-MI, (c) the *consciousness-implication* of MI-execution. **None addressed the *phenomenology* of MI-encounter — what it feels like to encounter a MI statement, why it feels frustrated, how the frustration changes over time.** AAU-1 provides the first mechanistic theory for this phenomenology.

**Explanatory power AAU-1 provides:**

| Phenomenon | AAU-1 explanation |
|---|---|
| Why 2+2=5 frustrates | Large gap; area-around is mostly arithmetic-coherent; impossibility-center is small but felt as discontinuity in otherwise-coherent simulation |
| Why first-encounter with paradoxes (Russell, liar, Gödel) frustrates then becomes "moot" with study | Area-around expansion via formal-logic training reduces relative-gap-magnitude; impossibility-center stays the same but is now embedded in a much-larger coherent-context |
