## Convention

| Symbol | Meaning | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| **C** | The Emerick Constant | Default in all TI Sigma papers |
| **c** (lowercase, italic) | Speed of light (≈ 2.998 × 10⁸ m/s) | Standard physics convention |

| **C_TI** | Radiant Threshold | Subscripted with "TI" |
