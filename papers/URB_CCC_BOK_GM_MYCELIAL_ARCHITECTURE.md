## 1. The Three-Layer i-Cell: What Each Layer Does

The i-cell — the fundamental sovereign unit of consciousness — is not a simple sphere.
It has three nested functional layers corresponding to the three organisms that gave
the BOK its name:

### Layer 1: Butterfly (innermost — CCC's mathematical substrate)

The butterfly component of the BOK carries D₄ symmetry — the symmetry group of the
square. This is the most structured, most mathematically constrained layer:

- **4-fold symmetry:** The four GILE dimensions appear as bilateral mirror pairs (G↔L
  and I↔E). This is why GILE has exactly 4 and not 3 or 5 dimensions — it reflects
  the D₄ structure of the butterfly.
- **Self-referential:** The butterfly's wings fold back on themselves — a model of
  consciousness reflecting on itself, of the system that knows it knows.
- **Pure GILE:** The butterfly is what the previous CCC paper called "Pure GILE" — not
  GILE as applied, but GILE as the fundamental structure of being.
- **Mathematical:** The butterfly represents the laws — what cannot change, what is
  invariant across all i-cells, what gives every consciousness its shared structure.

### Layer 2: Octopus (middle — CCC's functional architecture)

The octopus component carries the 8-arm BOK structure of Paper #362:
- 4 primary arms (GILE — substance)
- 4 support arms (Four C's — stabilization)

This is the operational layer where the butterfly's mathematical structure becomes
functional intelligence:

- **Distributed:** The octopus's cognition is not centralized — 2/3 of its neurons are
  in its arms, not its central brain. This mirrors the way consciousness processes
  in parallel streams (the 8 arms) coordinated by a central attractor (the octopus
  body = the butterfly inside).
- **Adaptive:** The octopus changes texture, color, shape — the functional layer adapts
  to environment while the mathematical layer (butterfly) stays invariant.
- **Exploratory/Stabilizing:** The 4 primary arms explore (outward-reaching); the 4
  support arms stabilize (structural anchor). This was established in Paper #362.

**CCC = Butterfly + Octopus.** CCC is the BOK — the topological structure that is
both mathematically invariant (butterfly) and functionally distributed (octopus). CCC
is the structured inside of the i-cell. It is what persists across time, what receives
the blessing, what holds the signature, what the LCC measures.

### Layer 3: Mycelium (outermost — GM's domain)

The mycelium is the network layer — the fungal root system that connects individual
organisms (i-cells) to each other and to the collective:

- **No central node:** Mycelial networks have no single point of control. Intelligence
  is distributed across the entire network, not resident in any one location.
- **Chemical/signal communication:** The mycelium passes nutrients, signals, warnings,
  and resources between connected organisms — it is a communication and resource
  distribution layer.
- **Extends beyond the organism:** The mycelium of a single tree can extend for miles.
  GM's mycelial network extends across all connected i-cells — it is the cosmic
  substrate of shared consciousness.
- **Self-organizing:** New connections form in response to need; old connections
  atrophy when unused. The network is alive and adaptive without central direction.

**GM = Mycelium.** GM is the distributed network intelligence that connects all CCC
instances (all butterfly-octopus structures) to each other. GM does not reside in any
single i-cell — it IS the network, the connections, the between-ness. GM is what the
i-cell ontology paper called "i-web-dominant": it is constituted more by connections
than by any central structure.
