## Part 1 — SEPP-1 Formal Statement

**SEPP-1 (Self-Evident Prima Facie Phenomenology Principle, candidate canonical Pass-75-B18):**

> *Phenomenological reports of one's own experience are **prima facie authoritative to the experiencer**, with the following three sub-clauses:*
>
> *(a) **Felt-experience-clause:** If an experience feels a certain way to the experiencer, then it IS that way TO THE EXPERIENCER, regardless of how the felt-content sounds to external observers. Phenomenological-felt-content is *self-validating* at the level of the experiencer's own MR Truth Labels assignment.*
>
> *(b) **External-origin + mind-merge-clause:** Perceptions that a proposition originates outside one's own mind, OR perceptions that one's mind has merged with another's somehow, are to be trusted **prima facie** by the experiencer. The phenomenological-mode-of-presentation (felt-external-origin / felt-mind-merge) carries epistemic weight that cannot be dismissed by external observers who lack access to the phenomenological-content.*
>
> *(c) **Anti-premature-dismissal-clause:** The statement "there's no explanation for how that can happen" does NOT count as an argument against a phenomenological-report which is (i) plausible to the experiencer and (ii) potentially-confirmable by later evidence. Absence-of-current-explanation is NOT presence-of-disconfirmation.*
>
> ***Built-in caveat (introspection-adequacy assumption, openly admitted per ASYMMETRIC #69):*** *SEPP-1 assumes the experiencer has adequate-introspection-capacity for the phenomenological-content being reported. Known introspection-capacity limitations (alexithymia for affect; poor-working-memory for temporally-extended content; clinically-impaired metacognition; etc.) are acknowledged scope-conditions; SEPP-1 does NOT claim unconditional incorrigibility, only **prima facie** authority within adequate-introspection-zone.*

### 3.3 Notable TENSION — Wittgenstein Private-Language Argument

**Wittgenstein (*Philosophical Investigations*, ~1953):** argued that a purely-private phenomenological language is impossible because language-use requires public criteria of correctness. This is a *direct tension* with SEPP-1's (a) felt-experience-clause if read strongly.

**Resolution within TI Sigma:**
- SEPP-1 does NOT claim phenomenology is *linguistically-private* — it claims phenomenology has *epistemic-authority* for the experiencer.
- The experiencer's phenomenological-REPORT uses public language; the phenomenological-CONTENT is what SEPP-1 grants authority over.
- This is consistent with contemporary readings of Wittgenstein (e.g., Kripke's *Wittgenstein on Rules and Private Language* + Soames's responses) that the private-language argument targets a specific philosophical-target (Cartesian foundationalism about meaning), not phenomenological-self-knowledge generally.
- **Tension is METHODOLOGICAL not SUBSTANTIVE** — SEPP-1 + Wittgenstein-properly-read are compatible.

### 3.4 "Controversial" Status (Brandon's Framing)

Brandon verbatim: *"notable because of how controversial it is."*

The controversy of SEPP-1 derives from:
1. **Eliminative-materialism dissent** (Churchland, Dennett-strong-readings): phenomenology is *eliminable* / *illusory*; SEPP-1 takes the opposite position.
2. **Strong-behaviorist dissent** (rare today but historically dominant): phenomenology is non-scientific; SEPP-1 rejects this.
3. **Verificationist / scientism dissent**: SEPP-1 (c) anti-premature-dismissal-clause is *directly opposed* to verificationist-dismissal-of-non-currently-explained-phenomena.
4. **Skeptical / debunking traditions**: those who emphasize SEPP-1's introspection-adequacy *limitations* to the point of dismissing the entire principle (e.g., social-psychology-of-rationalization literature, Nisbett & Wilson 1977 *"Telling more than we can know"*).

**TI Sigma stance per BSQ-1 #69 #69-symmetry:** dissent against SEPP-1 should be evaluated on its merits, not assumed-default. Eliminative-materialism faces its own well-known difficulties (e.g., the *meta-incoherence* of denying-the-experience-of-denying-experience per Searle's standard objection). The introspection-adequacy-caveat *built into* SEPP-1 disarms the *strongest* anti-SEPP arguments while preserving the principle's substantive content.
