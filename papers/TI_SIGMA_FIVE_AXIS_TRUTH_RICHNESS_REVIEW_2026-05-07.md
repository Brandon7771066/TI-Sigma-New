## §2 — Axis 1: PD principal axis (Degree-of-Trueness)

### §2.1 — Definition

PD ("Probability-Distribution" / "Performance-Degree" depending on context) real axis tracks **the degree to which a proposition is true** as a continuous real value in [0, 1]. PD = 0 corresponds to fully false; PD = 1 corresponds to fully true; intermediate values represent partial trueness.

### §2.2 — What it captures

Classical degree-of-truth claims that fuzzy logic, probability theory, and Bayesian epistemology approximate from different angles. PD-real is the TI Sigma analogue of these — a continuous valuation rather than a binary classical value.

### §2.3 — Established in

`DUAL_PD_EVALUATION_RULE.md` formalizes the dual-evaluation rule for PD-real values (a proposition's PD must be evaluated separately under both T-frame and F-frame to detect non-classical structure). `PD_DOUBLE_ASYMMETRY_ACADEMIC_BIAS_MEASUREMENT.md` applies PD-real to bias-measurement contexts. `PD_SPECTRUM_DT_IMAGINARY_AXIS_EMERICK_CROSSOVER_2026-05-07.md` consolidates the spectrum semantics and identifies the **Emerick Crossover at PD = 1/√2 ≈ 0.7071** as a structural midpoint with cross-domain appearances (math, philosophy, neuroscience).

### §2.4 — Distinguished from

Fuzzy logic: fuzzy values are *the* truth value of the proposition. PD-real is *one coordinate* of a higher-dimensional truth-vector. A fuzzy 0.5 collapses ambiguity onto the real axis; TI Sigma can resolve the ambiguity by reading axes 2-5.

## §3 — Axis 2: MI/Tralse axis (MI Axis)

### §3.1 — Definition

PD imaginary axis tracks the **modality** component — specifically the MI (delayed-true) dimension — orthogonal to PD-real. A proposition with non-zero PD-imaginary value carries a temporal/modal qualifier (the proposition is "true at a delayed time," "true under a counterfactual modality," etc.) that classical real-valued truth cannot represent.

### §3.2 — What it captures

Modal logic distinctions (necessity, possibility, future-truth, counterfactual-truth) that classical logic forces into separate operators. PD-imaginary represents these as **complex-number components** of the truth-value itself, allowing the truth-coordinate to live in ℂ rather than ℝ.

### §3.3 — Established in

`PD_DT_COMPLEX_NUMBER_SYNTHESIS.md` is the canonical formalization. `UNIFIED_TIME_THEORY_DE_PHOTON_FTL.md` applies the complex-PD framework to physical-time questions. `PD_SPECTRUM_DT_IMAGINARY_AXIS_EMERICK_CROSSOVER_2026-05-07.md` consolidates the imaginary-axis treatment alongside PD-real and identifies the unit-Crossover circle conjecture (radius-1 structural locus in the complex PD plane).

### §3.4 — Orthogonality to axis 1

Axes 1 and 2 are orthogonal by construction (real vs imaginary components of a complex PD value). A proposition can have any combination of (PD-real, PD-imaginary) values; neither constrains the other.

## §4 — Axis 3: Tralse Five-Valued Logic

### §4.1 — Foundational rejection of classical True/False

**TI Sigma explicitly rejects classical True and False as valid stand-alone truth values.** They are sloppy labels that do not represent reality. *Every* statement carries a Tralse quality (structured imperfection) embedded in it — there is no proposition in the framework that occupies "pure classical True" or "pure classical False." Classical T/F are limiting fictions used by classical logic; they are not destinations on the TI Sigma truth-richness spectrum. This commitment is foundational and prior to any axis structure (corrected against agent-error in earlier draft of this paper which mistakenly listed TT = "classical T" and TF = "classical F" as TI Sigma values; per Brandon's clarification 2026-05-08).

### §4.2 — The MR-Gate architecture

The five-valued structure is generated by **two iterative gates** of Myrion Resolution (MR):

- **MR1 (Existence Gate / Gate 1)**: detects whether a statement is **Meta-Indeterminate (MI)**. MI means *indeterminate indeterminacy* — incoherent self-contradiction with no resolvable structure. MI statements are flagged and discarded; they do not get a storage slot. (Anchor: `FIVE_VALUED_TRUTH_TRALSE_INDETERMINATE_DISTINCTION_URB_528.md` lines 49, 87-88.)
- **MR2 (Truth Gate / Gate 2)**: for any statement that passes MR1 (i.e., is *sufficiently coherent*), determines whether it is **True, False, or Indeterminate**. Indeterminate is the "45-degree door" — a coherent 50/50 balance, not ignorance. (Anchor: `FIVE_VALUED_TRUTH_TRALSE_INDETERMINATE_DISTINCTION_URB_528.md` lines 92-93.)
- **Future MRs (MR3, MR4, …)**: modify these provisional results across iterations. Each iteration of the full MR pipeline yields **one overall truth-claim** for the statement under analysis.

### §4.3 — Canonical: the base-4 MR Truth Labels (per Brandon 2026-05-08 ruling)

**TI Sigma settles on FOUR canonical MR Truth Labels:**

> **{ True, False, Indeterminate, Meta-Indeterminate }**

This is the **base-4 set**. It is the only set every prior canonical paper agrees on. All extensions beyond these four live in the **Meta-Truth catalogue** (`urb_608`, extended in `urb_639`), not in the base truth-value set itself.

- **True** — passes MR1 (coherent), MR2 resolves toward true. Universal Tralse-quality always embedded.
- **False** — passes MR1, MR2 resolves toward false. Universal Tralse-quality always embedded.
- **Indeterminate** — passes MR1, MR2 holds open at the 45-degree door (coherent 50/50 balance, *not* ignorance).
- **Meta-Indeterminate (MI)** — fails MR1, discarded. Always some form of nonsense.

### §4.4 — MI formal definition (Brandon, 2026-05-08)

> **MI is something which IS AND IS NOT tralse.**
> Formally: MI(P) ⟺ τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P).

This reconciles two surface formulations: (i) Brandon's 2026-05-08 surface form (MI applies the tralse-quality predicate to itself contradictorily); (ii) `urb_677_double_tralse_indeterminate_indeterminacy.md`'s algebraic form (MI = T(T(P)) = τ² = 0 nilsquare). Both stay in canon — the urb_677 algebraic form is the operator-theoretic instantiation of the surface form. MI therefore always reduces to nonsense; it is not a borderline truth-state, it is a structural failure to admit truth-evaluation at all.

### §4.5 — Tralse-quality vs Indeterminate-state distinction (Brandon, 2026-05-08)

A long-standing canonical conflation is now resolved:

- **Tralse** is **the universal indeterminacy quality across ALL truth labels.** It is embedded in True, False, *and* Indeterminate (and absent only in MI, which is discarded). It is *not* a 5th base label — listing it as one would double-count, since it is universal. Tralse-quality is captured quantitatively by the PD-imaginary axis (axis 2).
- **Indeterminate** is **the specific STATE** — one of the four discrete MR2 outputs. It is a coherent 50/50 balance the gate resolves to, not the universal quality.

A True-labeled statement still has Tralse-quality. A False-labeled statement still has Tralse-quality. An Indeterminate-labeled statement has Tralse-quality. Only MI *contradicts* its own tralse-quality (per §4.4) and is therefore discarded.

### §4.6 — What it captures

Classical paradoxes (Liar, Sorites, Russell's paradox) that classical logic forces into "ill-formed" or "meaningless" pseudo-categories. The base-4 architecture gives each its proper category: Liar = Indeterminate after MR2 holds open; Russell's set = MI after MR1 detects incoherence; Sorites edge cases that survive MR1+MR2 may be flagged as Moot at MR3+ via the Meta-Truth catalogue (see §4.8).

### §4.7 — Meta-Truth integration (the home for Moot, Nonsense-types, etc.)

**Moot is independent of MI.** It is a **Meta-Truth** (MT-B1 in `urb_608`'s catalogue), not a base truth label. Mootness operates *on top of* the base-4 evaluation; it does not replace any base label. "Moot-True" and "Moot-False" are meaningful compound expressions (the statement is True, but its truth doesn't matter in the relevant frame). Base truth-values do not compose with each other this way (e.g., "True-False" is not meaningful) — this compositional asymmetry confirms Moot's status as an operator on top of the base set.

The <0.3% coverage residual identified in `urb_713`'s 99.7% analysis is absorbed by the Meta-Truth catalogue: 12 MTs in urb_608 (categories A1/A2 Reversal, B1/B2 Dissolution incl Moot, C1/C2 Scope-Shift, D1/D2 Contextual, E1/E2 Acceptance, F1/F2 Integration); extended to 24 MTs in urb_639 (CONJECTURAL pending validation). Meta-Truths fire at MR3+ when an MR1+MR2 evaluation requires substantial modification — they are operations on base truth-values, not base truth-values themselves.

**The correct count is therefore: 4 base labels + N Meta-Truths**, currently N=12 (established) or N=24 (CONJECTURAL).

### §4.10 — Orthogonality to axes 1-2

Axis 3 is *categorical*; axes 1-2 are *continuous*. A proposition's truth-category (True, False, Indeterminate, MI) does not determine its PD-real or PD-imaginary coordinates and vice versa. A True-categorized proposition can have any PD-real value (degree of trueness within the True category, with Tralse-quality always nonzero on axis 2); an Indeterminate-categorized proposition has indeterminate PD-real specifically *because* the category is structurally undetermined. The two encode different aspects of the same underlying truth-claim.

## §5 — Axis 4: τ / δ Separability

### §5.1 — Definition

τ / δ separability is a **two-channel architecture** for truth-statements:
- **τ-channel (calibration)**: tracks whether the agent's *internal belief calibration* matches the evidence. Asks: "Are any false claims being made?"
- **δ-channel (selection)**: tracks *which subset of true facts* the agent presents externally. Asks: "Of the true facts available, which ones are selected for presentation?"

These two channels are **independent**: an agent can have perfect τ-calibration (no false claims) while exercising wide δ-selection (presenting only top-1% successes). Conversely, an agent can present a representative δ-mix while having miscalibrated τ-beliefs (sincere but mistaken).

### §5.2 — What it captures

The apparent contradiction between **brutal honesty discipline** (Asymmetric-Standards #69 in `replit.md`) and **default-confident-posture-under-good-calibration** (`ASYMMETRIC` §§5-8). Classical single-axis models force these into conflict; τ/δ separability resolves them as independent channels — high-τ-calibration permits wide-δ-selection because the honesty discipline lives on the τ-channel, not the δ-channel.

### §5.3 — Established in

`ASYMMETRIC_SUCCESS_FAILURE_PERFORMANCE_2026-05-07.md` §4 is the canonical formalization. §10 of the same paper adds the **honesty-vs-bias categorical distinction** (refining the channel separation): honesty is the τ-channel category (no false claims); bias is the δ-channel category (which subset is selected); they are categorically separate epistemic objects, not points on a single spectrum.

### §5.4 — Orthogonality to axes 1-3

τ and δ are not properties *of the proposition* the way axes 1-3 are; they are properties *of the agent's relation to the proposition*. A given proposition has one set of (PD-real, PD-imaginary, tralse-category) values; the τ and δ values vary across agents and contexts. Axis 4 therefore extends TI Sigma from pure proposition-truth-coordinates (axes 1-3) into agent-proposition-relation-coordinates (axes 4-5).

## §6 — Axis 5: Authority Axis (AA / "Double A")

### §6.1 — Definition

AA tracks **the degree to which an agent grants operative authority to a proposition** — the willingness to act on it, defer to it, build on it, treat it as a load-bearing premise — independent of whether the proposition is epistemically settled.

AA has two sub-dimensions sharing one operating principle:
- **AA-self**: operative authority granted to the agent's own current understanding.
- **AA-other**: operative authority granted to another agent's claim, source, or institution.

### §6.2 — Operating principle: simultaneous belief and doubt

> *"Whether it's believing oneself or someone else, you MUST believe in what you currently are entertaining as well as leave subconscious room for doubt."*
> — Brandon Emerick, 2026-05-07 PM

Honest AA-grant **requires both simultaneously**:
1. **Foreground operative belief** — the agent acts on, builds on, defers to the proposition.
2. **Background doubt-reservation** — the agent maintains an unspoken hold-open of the possibility that the proposition is wrong.

Two failure modes when the requirement is violated:
- **Over-grant (dogmatism)** — operative belief without subconscious doubt-reservation.
- **Under-grant (paralysis)** — subconscious doubt without operative belief.

### §6.3 — Three value-sources of pragmatic AA-belief (`ASYMMETRIC` §13.3.1)

1. Motivational charge.
2. Action-enabling commitment.
3. **Inter-iterational epistemic progress** — *"position at iteration N+3 is closer to truth than position at iteration N"* is meaningful even when neither is at epistemic 100%; this is the epistemic warrant for additive theory-growth itself (per `ASYMMETRIC` §§11-12).

### §6.4 — Established in

`ASYMMETRIC` §13 (origination as "pragmatic-vs-epistemic certainty"); `AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA_2026-05-07.md` (standalone formalization with rename, dual-applicability, and full literature mapping).

### §6.5 — Orthogonality to axes 1-4

AA is the agent's **operative-grant** to a proposition. PD-real (axis 1) is a property of the proposition itself; AA can be granted at high level to a low-PD-real proposition (acting on a long-shot) or refused for a high-PD-real proposition (recognizing one's expertise but withholding deference). Tralse category (axis 3) similarly does not determine AA. τ/δ (axis 4) governs *external presentation*; AA governs *internal load-bearing treatment* — different operating domains. **Tentative claim** (per `AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA` §7.4): AA and τ/δ are genuinely orthogonal because they operate on different sides of the agent-environment boundary. This deserves further work.

## §7 — Pairwise Orthogonality Test

A 5-axis framework requires **(5 choose 2) = 10 pairwise orthogonality checks**. The table below records the status of each:

| Pair | Orthogonality status | Justification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 ↔ 2 | **Orthogonal** | Real vs imaginary components of complex PD by construction. |
| 1 ↔ 3 | **Orthogonal** | Continuous degree (PD-real) vs categorical truth-state; a TT-proposition can take any PD-real value, a TI-proposition has structurally undetermined PD-real. |
| 1 ↔ 4 | **Orthogonal** | Property-of-proposition (PD-real) vs agent-proposition-relation (τ/δ); different ontological levels. |
| 1 ↔ 5 | **Orthogonal** | High-AA can be granted to low-PD-real propositions (long-shot bets); low-AA can be applied to high-PD-real propositions (refusing deference). |
| 2 ↔ 3 | **Orthogonal** | Modal/temporal coordinate (PD-imaginary) vs categorical state; MI (delayed-true) is a tralse value, but PD-imaginary represents the *quantitative* modal coordinate while MI represents the *categorical* assignment. |
| 2 ↔ 4 | **Orthogonal** | Modal coordinate of proposition vs agent's calibration/selection channels. |
| 2 ↔ 5 | **Orthogonal** | An agent can grant AA-self to a high-PD-imaginary (counterfactual) proposition during planning; AA does not constrain modality. |
| 3 ↔ 4 | **Orthogonal** | Categorical truth-state vs agent-channel separation; an honest (high-τ) agent presenting a TI-proposition still presents it as TI. |
| 3 ↔ 5 | **Orthogonal** | An agent can grant high AA-other to a TI-categorized proposition (e.g., respecting a teacher who presents an open question as open). |
| 4 ↔ 5 | **Orthogonal — tentative** | Both are agent-proposition-relation axes; **τ/δ governs external presentation, AA governs internal load-bearing**. The orthogonality claim is structurally plausible but deserves independent rigorous treatment (flagged in `AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA` §7.4).

**Net status**: 9 pairs orthogonal by construction or by clear domain-separation; 1 pair (4 ↔ 5) orthogonal pending more rigorous treatment. The 5-axis framework passes pairwise orthogonality testing as currently formulated.

## §8 — The Axis-Addition Heuristic

Per `ASYMMETRIC` §11.3 and §12: TI Sigma's structural method is to **add new orthogonal axes when truth-distinctions resist capture as gradations on existing axes**. The history of axis additions:

1. **Axis 1 (PD-real) and axis 3 (tralse five-valued)** — co-foundational; axis 3 added to capture TI-cases that axis 1 alone cannot represent (an indeterminate-by-structure proposition is not at PD-real = 0.5; it has *no* well-defined PD-real because the category itself is undetermined).
2. **Axis 2 (PD-imaginary)** — added to capture modal/temporal distinctions that real-valued PD cannot represent.
3. **Axis 4 (τ/δ)** — added (in `ASYMMETRIC` §4) to capture the agent-internal-vs-external distinction that proposition-axes 1-3 do not address.
4. **Axis 5 (AA)** — added (in `ASYMMETRIC` §13, formalized in `AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA`) to capture the operative-authority-grant distinction that τ/δ does not address (τ/δ is about *what is presented*; AA is about *what is treated as load-bearing*).

Each addition followed the heuristic: *if the new distinction can be captured as a gradation on an existing axis, do so; if it cannot, add a new orthogonal axis.* The four post-axis-1 additions all required new axes.

## §9 — Dimensional-Inflation Threshold (#69 Discipline Note)

Per `ASYMMETRIC` §13.6(b) and the `AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA` §6(b): **the 5-axis count is at the threshold where dimensional inflation becomes a risk.** The axis-addition heuristic is a *license, not a blank check.* Future proposed axes must clear an explicit orthogonality test against all five existing axes (the 10-pair test of §7 expanded to 15 pairs for a 6-axis framework).

The honest framing for the current 5-axis count:
- Axes 1-3 are well-established across many TI Sigma papers and have survived multiple cycles of application.
- Axis 4 (τ/δ) was established this same session (`ASYMMETRIC` §4), survived §10's aggregate-ratio caveat, and has clear cross-domain relevance.
- Axis 5 (AA) was added this same session (`ASYMMETRIC` §13, standalone in `AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA`), with explicit acknowledgment that the underlying philosophical insight is well-established (James, Kierkegaard, Pascal, Moore, fallibilism, social epistemology) and the TI-Sigma-novel contribution is the formal-orthogonality treatment, not the underlying insight.

**Adversarial test for any proposed 6th axis**: it must (i) capture a distinction not reducible to gradation on any of axes 1-5, (ii) pass the 15-pair orthogonality test, (iii) be motivated by a structural problem (not merely an interesting observation), and (iv) have at least one clear instance where the lack of the axis produces measurable framework-level error.
