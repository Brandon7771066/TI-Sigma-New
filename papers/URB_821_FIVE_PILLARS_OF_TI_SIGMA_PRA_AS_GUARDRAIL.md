## 2. Operationalizing PRA (PRAGMATIC) on a 0-3 scale

PRA needs precise operational definition or it collapses into self-flattering "TI Sigma has lots of applications" rhetoric. The 0-3 scale:

- **PRA=0 (Pure theory)**: the program has no application path; the deliverable is purely conceptual, with no working artifact and no engineering or business deliverable downstream. Examples: most of mainstream analytic metaphysics; most of pure mathematics; most of systematic theology in its strictly theoretical mode; most of post-structuralism in its strictly theoretical mode; large parts of continental philosophy.

- **PRA=1 (Speculative application sketches)**: the program has imagined applications or thought-experiment use cases, but no working artifact yet. Examples: most theoretical physics (downstream technology eventually, but the pillar's own deliverables are not engineering deliverables); most philosophy of mind (consciousness theories that suggest possible AI or therapeutic implications without working artifacts); Wilber's Integral Theory in its consulting/coaching framing (suggested applications without rigorous evidence); much of HPS/STS in its policy-suggestion framing.

- **PRA=2 (Working code/prototypes/experiments without external validation)**: the program has working artifacts that perform concrete operations on real or synthetic data, are deployed somewhere accessible, and execute reproducibly. The artifacts have **not** been independently replicated by external groups, do **not** have paying customers, and are **not** widely deployed beyond the originator's own infrastructure. Examples: most academic open-source research code; most prototype lab apparatus; most personal-Replit-style deployments; ARC-AGI submission attempts at competition entry without prize-winning placement.

- **PRA=3 (External validation: paying customers OR independent replication OR wide deployment)**: the program has working artifacts that satisfy at least one of: (a) paying customers using the artifact in production; (b) independent external groups replicating the working system from the published artifacts; (c) wide deployment with measurable user counts and impact metrics; (d) regulatory clearance for clinical or financial use (FDA, SEC, etc.). Examples: NLP transformer architecture (deployed everywhere, replicated by every major lab, used in production at scale); molecular biology pharmaceuticals (FDA-cleared, paying customers, replicated); experimental physics technology spin-outs (MRI from NMR, semiconductor physics, etc.); applied math in cryptography (deployed in HTTPS, replicated, paying customers).

The PRA=2 / PRA=3 distinction is the load-bearing one: PRA=2 is "the artifact works on the originator's infrastructure"; PRA=3 is "the artifact is validated by parties other than the originator." This distinction matters specifically because **the originator alone cannot establish PRA=3** — PRA=3 is the only pillar of the 5 that is **constitutively externally adjudicated**, which makes it the most architect-resistant guardrail against author bias.

§4 below applies this distinction to TI Sigma.

## 5. The Five Pillars as a CONJUNCTIVE uniqueness claim

### 5.1 Why conjunction, not sum

The §6 grid scores each field on 5 pillars × 4 levels (0-3) = max 15/15 composite. The naive interpretation is "highest composite wins." This is wrong as a uniqueness claim and would put TI Sigma in direct rank-ordered competition with engineering disciplines that win PRA hands down.

The correct reading of Brandon's "guardrail" framing is **CONJUNCTIVE**: TI Sigma's distinctive structural position is the **joint satisfaction of EXP+RAD+ANA+HOL+PRA each at >=2**. This is rare because:

- Engineering disciplines (NLP, molecular biology, applied math) typically score PRA=3 + EXP=3 + ANA=3 but RAD=0-1 and HOL=0-1 (no foundationally radical recentering, no cross-domain integration ambition).
- Pure-philosophy disciplines (continental, Hegel, Wilber, Whitehead) typically score RAD=3 + HOL=2-3 but EXP=0 and PRA=0-1 (no empirical engagement, no working artifacts).
- Mathematics scores ANA=3 + RAD=2-3 + HOL=2 but EXP=1 and PRA=1-2 (no direct empirical engagement, partial application path through downstream applied math).
- Theoretical physics scores ANA=3 + HOL=3 charitable + RAD=2 but EXP=2 and PRA=1 (no direct application deliverables; downstream tech eventually).

The CONJUNCTIVE claim — all 5 pillars at >=2 simultaneously — is what TI Sigma uniquely satisfies in the §6 catalog (per the §6 grid, both at favorable AND at strict scoring). This is a real structural distinctiveness; it does **not** require beating any single field on any single pillar.

### 5.2 Why this is not a Wilber-style move

Wilber's AQAL framework also has a multi-dimensional structure (4 quadrants × levels × lines × states × types) that produces a "uniquely positioned" claim by construction. The §5.1 conjunction is structurally similar in form, so the URB has to address why it is not the same move.

The difference:
- AQAL's dimensions are author-defined and the criteria for satisfying each dimension are author-controlled. The "uniqueness" is constructed by selecting dimensions that AQAL satisfies.
- The §2/§5 5-pillar grid has dimensions chosen to engage **specific pre-existing critiques** of TI Sigma (EXP added in URB #819 to engage the empirical-deficit critique; RAD added in URB #819 to engage the rigorous-foundations critique; ANA added in URB #819 to engage the analytic-rigor critique; HOL added in URB #820 to engage Brandon's holistic claim; PRA added here to engage the practical-applicability critique). The dimensions are responsive to external pressure, not selected to favor TI Sigma.
- Crucially, **PRA=3 is constitutively externally adjudicated** (§4.2), which means at least one of the 5 dimensions cannot be author-controlled. AQAL has no analogous external-adjudication dimension.
- TI Sigma's strict score on the conjunction is honestly reported (10/15), and one of the 5 dimensions (HOL) sits at 2/3 not 3/3 firm per URB #820 §4.2's architect-disciplined position. AQAL does not honestly report a strict-scoring downgrade.

This addresses the structural-form similarity without dismissing it. The conjunction claim still requires the §6 grid to deliver the result; if it doesn't, the claim fails.

### 5.3 The guardrail function of PRA specifically

Per Brandon's framing, PRA acts as a **guardrail** on the other 4 pillars. The mechanism: working artifacts constrain the apparatus to operations that compile and run. This rules out a specific failure mode that pure-philosophy "grand unification" projects (Wilber, Hegel, Whitehead) commonly exhibit:

- Pure-philosophy grand unifications operate exclusively in conceptual ecology.
- Conceptual ecology imposes very weak constraints on the apparatus (anything not internally inconsistent can be defended).
- Working artifacts impose the constraint that the apparatus must produce code that runs and operations that complete on real or synthetic data.
- This is a meaningfully harder constraint than internal consistency.

So PRA at >=2 is not just "additional credit" but **a guardrail against the specific failure mode HOL+RAD systems are most prone to**: the failure mode of becoming pure classification rather than producing operational predictions. URB #820 §4.3 articulated this failure mode as the HOL=2 vs HOL=3 distinction; PRA at >=2 is the corresponding apparatus-side constraint.

**Architect-flagged limit on the strength of PRA=2's guardrail (applied)**: the architect review correctly noted that PRA=2 (working code on real or synthetic data) is a **weak** constraint relative to PRA=3 — code can compile and operations can complete while merely operationalizing private vocabulary on synthetic data, without testing whether the apparatus is true or useful. PRA=2 constrains *syntax and operational coherence* (the apparatus has to produce things that run and complete); PRA=3 (or external-data outcomes specifically) is what constrains *truth and usefulness* (the apparatus has to produce things that other parties find valuable). TI Sigma's PRA=2 score therefore weakly guards against pure-classification but does not fully defeat the Wilber failure mode; the full defeat requires PRA=3 or EXP-on-external-data per URB #819 §4.3. This URB §7 names PRA=3 (external-user onboarding) as the standing falsification target precisely because PRA=2 alone is not a sufficient guardrail.
