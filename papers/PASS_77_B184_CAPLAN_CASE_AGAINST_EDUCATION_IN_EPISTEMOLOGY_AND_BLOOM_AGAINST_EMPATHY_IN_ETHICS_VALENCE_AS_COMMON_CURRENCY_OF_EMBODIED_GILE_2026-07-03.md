## 4. Honesty rails baked in (#69 both-ways)

**Caplan:**
- The signaling *share* is **Caplan's estimate (~80%), not a measured constant**; human-capital economists put it far lower and cite real replicated skill gains (literacy/numeracy; compulsory-schooling natural experiments). Section retreats to the defensible weaker claim: *a substantial, routinely-ignored fraction of the credential premium is signal ⇒ a credential is weak evidence of knowledge.*
- Explicitly a claim about **private economic return**, not education's civic/cultural/intrinsic goods (signaling model is silent on those).
- Caplan himself exempts basic literacy, numeracy, vocational training — stated, so as not to caricature ("school teaches nothing" is NOT the claim).

**Bloom:**
- Target is **emotional empathy specifically**, NOT compassion/kindness — Bloom **keeps rational compassion**. Must never be cited as "feelings don't matter."
- Bloom's position is **strong but contested** (de Waal, Decety defend empathy in its lane); flagged as a live debate, not settled.
- **Utilitarianism has its own problems** (demandingness, welfare measurement, rights-vs-sum) — named; the framework's refusal to reduce ethics to one maximand is the response.
- **"Common currency" is a functional research framing, not a proven single scalar** in the head; used as a *lens* and hedged. The framework explicitly rejects collapsing ethics to a single scalar (hence GILE's four axes).
- consciousness-as-a-razor flagged framework-internal.
