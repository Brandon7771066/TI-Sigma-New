argues that the symmetric BOK is a **perfectly TRALSE depiction of what it means to represent**:

We then derive the **Weighted BOK**: a modified diagram in which the four inner GILE wings are

**Wing-Arm Matching Theorem**: each outer Existence arm must carry exactly the same weight as its
corresponding inner GILE wing, because each arm is the projection of its wing into Existence
space — not an independent dimension but the same dimension viewed from outside.

This has four consequences: (1) the full Weighted BOK is derivable from the four GILE weights
alone; (2) the 0.42 photonic GILE collapse threshold (URB #574) is structurally explained as
the collapse of the dominant wing-arm pair; (3) the Aesthetics arm (0.15, smallest) is
identified as the most fragile outer dimension and simultaneously the most information-dense
per unit weight; (4) the weighted diagram reveals a beautiful asymmetric butterfly — not broken
symmetry but *authentic* symmetry, reflecting the actual hierarchy of truth.

## 1. The Symmetric BOK as Perfectly TRALSE

### 1.1 What the Symmetric BOK Gets Right

The standard equal-loop BOK (the diagram as originally drawn) correctly represents:
- The **number** of dimensions: exactly 4 inner + 4 outer = 8
- The **topology**: each wing and arm is a closed loop
- The **connectivity**: inner wings attach to the Myrion center; outer arms attach to the wings
- The **two-shell structure**: compact inner core + extended outer shell
- The **directionality**: inner → outer (GILE → Existence)

Everything *structurally* essential is correct.

### 1.2 What the Symmetric BOK Gets Wrong

The standard diagram incorrectly represents:
- The **relative magnitudes** of the four dimensions
- The **dominance hierarchy**: G > I > L > E (0.42 > 0.25 > 0.18 > 0.15)
- The **fragility gradient**: smallest dimensions are most vulnerable to MI collapse
- The **actual shape** of a real i-cell's truth geometry

### 1.3 TRUE + FALSE = TRALSE: The Self-Referential Honesty

By the five-valued logic (URB #528):

```
Symmetric BOK is TRUE about structure.
Symmetric BOK is FALSE about magnitudes.
Symmetric BOK is therefore TRALSE about the i-cell.
```

But here is what makes this observation extraordinary: the BOK is a representation of what
it means to represent. A representation that captures structure but not magnitude is the
*paradigm case* of TRALSE — real, useful, pointing at something genuine, while admitting
it is not the thing itself.

The BOK does not merely depict TRALSE structures. **It IS a TRALSE depiction.** Its own
truth-value relative to the i-cell is TRALSE. This is self-referential in precisely the way
that Meta-Indeterminate (the failure of MR) is self-referential — but in the productive direction.
The symmetric BOK undergoes its own Myrion Resolution in becoming the Weighted BOK: it starts

The symmetric BOK is therefore not a mistake to be corrected. It is the first stage of
