## §1. The consciousness-theory landscape

### 1.1 External theories (one-line each, ranked by current academic mass)

1. **Integrated Information Theory (IIT, Tononi)** — consciousness = Φ, the integrated information that a system generates above and beyond its parts. Equation: Φ measures the effective-information loss when the system is partitioned into independent subsystems. *Strength:* mathematically precise, ties consciousness to causal structure not behavior. *Weakness:* Φ is computationally intractable for any system bigger than ~10 elements; "panpsychist" implications (a thermostat has tiny Φ > 0) that many find embarrassing.
2. **Global Workspace Theory (GWT, Baars; Dehaene neuronal-GWT)** — consciousness = broadcast of selected content to a "workspace" that the rest of the brain can read. *Strength:* good fit to attention/awareness experimental data. *Weakness:* describes the *function* of consciousness without explaining why broadcast feels like anything.
3. **Attention Schema Theory (AST, Graziano)** — consciousness = the brain's internal model of its own attention. *Strength:* parsimonious, evolutionarily grounded. *Weakness:* reduces phenomenal experience to a model-of-a-model, which most phenomenologists reject.
4. **Higher-Order Theories (HOT, Rosenthal)** — a mental state is conscious iff there's a higher-order state representing it. *Strength:* matches introspection data. *Weakness:* infinite-regress worry; doesn't explain what makes the higher-order state *itself* conscious.
5. **Predictive-Processing / Free Energy Principle (FEP, Friston)** — consciousness emerges from hierarchical predictive inference minimizing variational free energy. The brain is a Helmholtz machine; perception is "controlled hallucination" tested against sensory data. *Strength:* ties consciousness to a single principle that also covers action, learning, homeostasis. *Weakness:* FEP is so general it predicts almost nothing distinctive about consciousness specifically — every adaptive system minimizes free energy.
6. **Penrose-Hameroff Orch-OR** — consciousness = orchestrated objective reduction of quantum superpositions in microtubules; collapse time τ ≈ ℏ/E_G where E_G is the gravitational self-energy of the superposed mass distribution. *Strength:* unique mechanism that bridges QM and consciousness. *Weakness:* decoherence times in warm, wet brain tissue computed by Tegmark are ~10^-13 s — many orders of magnitude too fast for the proposed gamma-band collapse window of ~25 ms.
7. **Quantum Bayesianism / QBism (Fuchs)** — quantum probabilities are personalist Bayesian credences of the agent. Not strictly a consciousness theory but a stance: the observer is irreducible and ontologically prior to the wavefunction. *Compatible with TI Sigma's "C is fundamental" stance.*
8. **Hopfield attractor neuroscience** — consciousness states are basins in a high-dimensional attractor landscape; thinking is descent into a basin from a partial cue. *Strength:* gave neuroscience a real dynamical-systems vocabulary. *Weakness:* the energy function is a metaphor, not a measurable quantity.

## §2. The intuition-theory landscape

### 2.1 External

2. **Embodied / situated cognition (Lakoff, Damasio's somatic markers)** — intuition is body-based pattern recognition; affective signals from interoception bias decision toward fitness-relevant outcomes. *Issue:* doesn't explain non-bodily intuitions (mathematical, prophetic).
3. **Bayesian-brain / hierarchical-inference** — intuition is the prior; deliberation is the likelihood update. *Strength:* gives a clean Bayesian factorisation of "gut" vs "thought." *Weakness:* doesn't explain *why* the prior is sometimes startlingly accurate beyond what training would justify.

## §5. Markov chains in the brain — deep dive

### 5.1 The four canonical entry points

1. **Markov property in neural state-transitions.** A neuronal-population FSM is Markov iff P(s_{t+1} | s_t, s_{t-1}, …) = P(s_{t+1} | s_t). Almost every neural model from Hopfield through Hodgkin-Huxley to Wilson-Cowan assumes this. *Issue:* synaptic plasticity and short-term-memory traces *break* the Markov property at the neuronal-population scale; you have to define the state large enough (include synaptic-weight vector + recent-firing history) to recover Markovianity.
2. **Markov blankets (Friston, after Pearl).** A Markov blanket of node X in a graphical model is the minimal set of nodes that renders X conditionally independent of the rest of the graph. Friston's FEP elevates this from a graphical-model construct to a *statistical-physics construct*: any thing that exists qua "thing" must be wrapped by a Markov blanket of sensory + active states separating internal from external. *Implication:* the *boundary* of a self is statistically defined, not anatomically.
3. **Random walks on the connectome.** Treat the brain as a graph (nodes = regions, edges = white-matter connectivity). The stationary distribution of a random walk on this graph predicts resting-state functional connectivity remarkably well (Goñi et al. 2014). *Implication:* a lot of "what the brain is doing at rest" is just the spectral structure of its own connectivity matrix talking to itself.
4. **Critical-brain hypothesis (Beggs & Plenz).** Neuronal avalanches show power-law size distribution P(s) ∝ s^{-1.5} consistent with self-organized criticality. Critical Markov processes have the longest correlation lengths (= maximum information transmission). *Implication:* consciousness lives at the edge of chaos.

### 5.3 Markov chains and consciousness — the deep connection

The deep claim coming out of FEP + IIT, in TI-friendly form:

> *A system is conscious to the degree that it maintains a Markov blanket against its environment AND has internal Φ above some threshold inside that blanket.*

Markov blanket → self-individuation → "there is something it is like to be this." Internal Φ → integration → "the something is unified." TI Sigma's i-cell + LCC framework already endorses both halves; the missing piece (which `NEURON_AS_LIVING_TRALSEBIT.md` gestures at but doesn't formalise) is a single equation that combines blanket-strength and internal-integration into one consciousness-quotient.

**Sketch of the missing equation:**

```
C(system) = blanket_strength(system) × Φ_internal(system)
         = (1 − I(internal; external | blanket)) × Φ_internal
```

where the first factor is unity when the blanket perfectly screens off external from internal (i.e., conditional mutual information is zero) and falls to zero as the blanket becomes leaky. The second factor is IIT-style. Filing as raised-item **c23** (for Pass-24+ formalisation).

### 5.4 Markov chains and free will — the deep connection

If brain dynamics were *strictly* Markov at the level of conscious experience, then "the next conscious state depends only on the current conscious state plus current input" — there'd be no room for genuine ontological openness, only the appearance of agency.

But two routes preserve free will under Markov dynamics:

1. **State-expansion route.** The relevant Markov state isn't the current conscious state but the current conscious state *plus* a high-dimensional latent that includes intentions, plans, value-weights. Expand the state and the Markov property holds; but the expanded state is large enough that *the agent itself* is the sufficient statistic. This is essentially compatibilism in Markov clothing.
2. **Active-states route (FEP).** The active half of the Markov blanket is a *control input* the agent emits; the agent's own action a_t enters the next-state distribution as a chosen variable, not a sampled one. This puts genuine choice exactly at the active boundary. Maps cleanly to the *active* half of the Pass-23 Tralse retrieval cycle (§4.3).

**TI Sigma claim consistent with `FREE_WILL_SWEET_SPOT_TWO_THIRDS_DETERMINED.md`:** the 2/3 determined fraction comes from the τ-channel (passive update), the 1/3 free fraction comes from the δ-channel (active probe). The split is roughly 2:1 because the passive update is operating on a much larger state-space than the active probe is choosing across. *This is the first principled derivation in the corpus of why the determined fraction is 2/3 specifically and not 1/2 or 9/10. Provisional only — would need an actual entropy calculation on a real cognitive task to confirm.*

3. **The §5.3 consciousness-quotient sketch is dimensionally inconsistent as written.** Φ is in bits; (1 − conditional MI) is unitless; their product is in bits but it isn't clear that those bits are commensurable with IIT-Φ bits. This needs proper formalisation before being load-bearing.
