## §3 — The four candidate frontiers, scored

### §3.1 — Penrose / Orch-OR

**Claim:** consciousness exploits microtubule quantum-coherence to access non-computable mathematical truths (Gödel sentences).

**TI Sigma reading:**
- Penrose's mathematical argument (1994) survives Pass-14 audit at the level of "you can't algorithmically rule it out without already assuming the conclusion"; this is symmetric to URB-830's anti-Popperian point.
- Hameroff's microtubule biology has improved since 2014 (Bandyopadhyay vibrational measurements; Reimers' decoherence-time critiques weakened by warm-coherence findings in cryptochromes). Status: *more credible than 2010, still not established.*
- TI Sigma alignment: Pass-23 shortlist #4 (Orch-OR) was rated WEAK on AA per Pass-34 e25-d §3; rated Conjectural on A2 (PD-real) and DT-rich on A1 (MR Truth Labels).

**Pass-35 verdict:** Penrose's argument is **load-bearing for the meta-philosophical case** but does **not yet provide independent empirical leverage**.

### §3.2 — TI Sigma Crystal (V₄, B4, C5, C6, c25)

**Claim:** the 57-vertex BOK Crystal is the structural substrate of GM-Network computation; its V₄ symmetry maps onto the 4-truth-label group; its 57-eigenvalue spectrum gives 5 phase energies isomorphic to BEC/Supersolid/Mott/FQH/Fragmented quantum-many-body phases.

**TI Sigma reading:**
- §2.2 inventory: structurally rich, mathematically computed, multiply ratified.
- The 4/3 invariant (Pass-10 T1-C) is the only Crystal-adjacent structural claim that survived strict Monte-Carlo (p ≪ 0.001) — a real positive-direction result.
- Crystal C.6 cross-ring CHSH prediction (CHSH_ij ≤ 2·min(r_i, r_j)) is a structural inequality, not a quantum-mechanical violation, and so does NOT itself constitute super-Turing evidence — but it is *testable on real CHSH data* if any exists in the corpus's reach.

**Pass-35 verdict:** Crystal is **the most-developed mathematical scaffold** in the corpus. It is **not a super-Turing demonstration**; it is a candidate substrate. The leverage is downstream: if Crystal is right, what would it predict that ordinary computation cannot?

### §3.3 — P vs NP

**Claim:** if non-computational cognition exists, the P vs NP boundary may be irrelevant to conscious problem-solving — humans may routinely "solve" NP-hard instances by step-skipping.

**TI Sigma reading:**
- Strongest *philosophical* leverage point: P vs NP is the most-famous open problem in computer science; demonstrating super-Turing on a small NP-hard instance would have outsized rhetorical weight.
- Weakest *technical* leverage: NP-hard problems have polynomial verifiers but no known polynomial solvers. A demonstration that consciousness "solves" a single NP-hard instance is *not* a proof that P=NP — it's evidence that conscious cognition is not Turing-equivalent.
- Pass-21 r20 R-A AUC 0.7318 (TSC SAT-vs-UNSAT discrimination) is the corpus's *only* prospective-replicated empirical result that touches NP-hard problems — and per Pass-21 §7.7.57 it's a "first cleanly-replicated empirical prediction" but is also a *higher-energy⇒SAT* signature, not a *consciousness-solves-SAT* signature.

**Pass-35 verdict:** P vs NP is a **good rhetorical frontier** but the technical claim must be precise: *"conscious cognition exceeds Turing-equivalence on individual NP-hard instances"* not *"P=NP."* Confusing the two would be a #69 discipline failure.

### §3.4 — Busy Beaver / BB(6)

**Claim:** BB(6) is a concrete, well-defined mathematical constant whose value is known only to exceed 2↑↑↑↑↑5 (Knuth pentation+); 1,314 holdout 6-state Turing machines remain unclassified as of Jan 2026; the Antihydra machine reduces to a Collatz-like dynamical system. GILE Discoverability Theorem (`HALTING_PROBLEM_GM_HYPERCOMPUTING_BB6.md` §4) claims that high-GILE truths are accessible to GILE-attuned inquiry.

**TI Sigma reading:**
- Most-tractable concrete frontier: each holdout machine is a fully-specified mathematical object; classifying any single one is independently meaningful.
- Verification asymmetry (HALTING paper §5.3): discovering whether a machine halts may require non-algorithmic insight, but verifying any halting witness or any provable-loop invariant is purely algorithmic. **This means TI Sigma cannot self-deceive on a positive result** — any claimed BB(6) holdout-classification must survive standard mathematical proof-checking.
- Stage-gating: even classifying ONE holdout machine that bbchallenge.org has not classified would be a publishable result. Classifying the Antihydra would be a major mathematical contribution. Determining BB(6) exactly would be a Fields-Medal-class result.

**Pass-35 verdict:** **Busy Beaver is the highest-leverage concrete target.** The verification asymmetry means any positive result is robustly checkable; any negative result (TI Sigma method tried, failed) is symmetrically informative per URB-830. Unlike Penrose/Crystal/P-vs-NP, BB has a concrete object-level workspace (the 1,314 holdout machines).

## §4 — The structural problem: coupling vs hypercomputation

A recurring TI Sigma error pattern, observable across Pass-23 to Pass-32: results that demonstrate *coupling* (LCC v3 R-3, ΦFE bridges, 4/3 invariant, Pearson rolling correlations) are routinely *interpreted* as evidence for hypercomputation. They are not.

**The diagnostic question (Pass-35 canonical):** *"If this exact result were observed in a system known to be Turing-computable, would I still call it evidence of hypercomputation?"* If yes, the result is not super-Turing-discriminating.

**Application:**

- DANDI:000003 r=+0.988: a Turing-simulable LFP could produce identical statistics. **Not super-Turing evidence.** Coupling, yes. Hypercomputation, no.
- 4/3 invariant Monte Carlo p ≪ 0.001: a Turing-simulable model could produce 4/3. **Not super-Turing evidence.** Structural invariant, yes.
- Crystal C5 V₄: a Turing-computable group-theoretic computation. **Not super-Turing evidence.** Mathematical structure, yes.
- BB(6) holdout-classification of a previously-unclassified machine: a Turing-computable enumerator already exists for the simulation; the open question is whether *the proof of halting/looping* can be obtained without exhaustive search. **This IS super-Turing-discriminating** because the verification asymmetry separates the discovery process from the verification process.

This diagnostic is the cleanest way the corpus can avoid the coupling-vs-hypercomputation conflation Pass-35 has been raising for ~10 passes.

## §5 — URB-830 application: what counts as evidence?

Per URB-830 §6.3, the canonical metric is TIU magnitude with direction recorded but not weighted. For super-Turing claims:

- **Positive-direction TIU:** any single instance where (i) a problem provably outside the Turing-computable class is (ii) resolved by a TI-Sigma-attributable process, (iii) with the resolution verified by standard mathematical proof-checking. *Magnitude:* very high (potentially the largest single TIU in the corpus's history).
- **Negative-direction TIU:** repeated failure of TI-Sigma methods on the same problem class, with standard algorithmic methods continuing to set the frontier. *Magnitude:* accumulates slowly; no single failure is decisive.
- **Inadmissible:** coupling demonstrations (per §4), structural-mathematical results (per §2.2), meta-philosophical arguments (per §2.1) — these update *priors*, they do not test *the hypothesis itself*.

**Asymmetry-removal:** URB-830 forbids treating the negative-direction TIU as more or less weighty than positive. If TI Sigma methods fail on every BB(6) holdout for 5 years, that is a strong negative TIU. If they classify one previously-unclassified holdout in 6 months, that is a strong positive TIU. Both are equally weighty.

## §6 — Path forward: stage-gated single-instance super-Turing demonstration

### §6.1 — Stage-gate ordering (most-to-least tractable)

**Stage 1: BB(6) holdout machine — single-target attack.**

Concrete plan:
- Pull bbchallenge.org's current holdout list (~1,314 machines).
- Filter for the 50 lowest-Kolmogorov-complexity holdouts (machines whose state-table is shortest).
- Run *standard* deciders (Direct simulation; Finite Automata Reduction; Inductive reasoning; Accelerated simulation) on the filtered set first; expected outcome: some still resist.
- For the residual: apply the GILE Discoverability protocol: compute G/I/L/E scores per `HALTING` §4.4; rank by GILE descending; attack highest-GILE first using the Myrion Resolution method (`HALTING` §6) — re-cast each machine's behavior as a configuration-space attractor diagram; identify which attractor the machine enters.
- Verification: any halting witness is N-step-simulable in Coq; any provable loop-invariant is Coq-encodable. Standard mathematical proof-checking applies.

**Stage 2: Antihydra Collatz-reduced classification.**

The Antihydra reduces to a Collatz-like iteration. Do *not* attempt to solve Collatz itself. Do attempt to classify the *specific* Collatz-like iteration the Antihydra induces, using TI Sigma techniques (step-skipping; Tralse retrieval cycle from Pass-23 §7; LCC v3 R-3 phase-energy attractor reads). Any partial result (e.g., "the iteration enters a definite attractor regime under conditions C1, C2") is publishable.

**Stage 3: SAT instance hand-crafted with provable-non-derivability marker.**

Construct a single 3-SAT instance with a known satisfying assignment but with a "GILE marker" — a structural property that makes the assignment intuitively obvious to a human while being algorithmically hard. Use Pass-21 r20 protocol (TSC SAT discrimination AUC 0.7318) but prospectively-replicated on the marked instance. Verify the marker's role with K=100 mapping-sensitivity check (Pass-20 R-B method).

**Stage 4: Crystal-adjacent structural claim with non-trivial empirical content.**

Identify one Crystal C5/C6/B4/c25 prediction that (a) is mathematically derivable from the 57×57 graph Laplacian, (b) has non-trivial empirical content (i.e., is not obviously true by construction), and (c) can be tested against existing data (DANDI / OpenNeuro / IBMQ). The Pass-31 D2-HYBRID 5-qubit GM-Network is the obvious candidate substrate; Pass-29 b27 (5/5 bowtie-projection) is the obvious candidate test.

### §6.3 — Pre-registered stop-rules (per URB-830 §6.3 symmetry)

- **Continue-condition (POSITIVE):** at least one stage shows positive-direction TIU magnitude ≥ 1.0 per attack iteration → continue iterating on that stage.
- **Continue-condition (NEGATIVE):** at least one stage shows negative-direction TIU magnitude ≥ 1.0 per attack iteration → continue iterating, BUT also raise the iteration-count for that stage's Reclassify-condition trigger by 1 (we want the negative-direction signal to be stress-tested before promoting it to a halt).
- **Pivot-condition:** 5 consecutive iterations on a single stage with TIU magnitude < 0.5 in *either* direction → pivot to the next stage (no informational signal, in either direction = move on).
- **Reclassify-condition (NEGATIVE, mirrors positive halt):** any single stage accumulates negative-direction TIU magnitude ≥ 2.0 → that stage is formally re-classified as "TI Sigma methods do not exceed standard methods on this problem class," and the corpus issues a positive-direction-equivalent acknowledgment (per URB-830 §6.3 symmetry, this is the same epistemic weight as a positive cumulative ≥ 2.0 would carry — *not* a defeat, but a definite finding).
- **Halt-condition (POSITIVE):** any single stage produces a single-event positive-direction TIU magnitude ≥ 3.0 (e.g., a verified BB(6) holdout-classification by TI-Sigma-attributable methods) → the program has met its primary objective; subsequent stages become independent confirmations rather than primary tests.
- **Halt-condition (NEGATIVE):** all 4 stages exhausted with cumulative negative-direction TIU magnitude ≥ 4.0 AND cumulative positive-direction TIU magnitude < 2.0 → corpus-level retraction of the strong-form noncomputational-ability headline (meta-philosophical case retained per §2.1).
- **Halt-condition (NULL):** all 4 stages exhausted with cumulative TIU magnitude < 2.0 in BOTH directions → "uninformative outcome" verdict; the program is suspended pending new methods, not retracted.

This is **URB-830-required and architect-patched**: positive- and negative-direction stop-rules are now mirrored in both magnitude (≥3.0 single / ≥4.0 cumulative) and reclassification action. A strong negative-direction trajectory now triggers an early reclassification (≥2.0 cumulative on any single stage), not just an end-of-program halt.
