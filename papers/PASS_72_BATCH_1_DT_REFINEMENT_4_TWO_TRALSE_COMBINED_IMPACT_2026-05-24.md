## 2. The Three Operational Sharpenings

### 2.1 From single-proposition to two-proposition MI structure

**Old:** MI = τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) for single proposition P.
**New:** MI(P₁, P₂) for two tralse propositions whose combination is inconceivable.

**Impact:** every previously-flagged MI case must be DECOMPOSED into its two-proposition components. Quick-tested in §5 of the refinement paper: liar, Russell, square-circle all decompose cleanly.

### 2.2 From "inconceivability" (broad) to "two-tralse-combination-inconceivability" (sharpened)

**Old (Pass-65):** MI = inconceivability under mental actualization attempt.
**New (Pass-72):** MI = two tralse propositions whose combination is inconceivable under mental actualization attempt.

**Impact:** Pass-65's inconceivability heuristic is operationally sharper — the agent now knows EXACTLY what to attempt to mentally actualize (the combination, not the proposition).

### 2.3 From "incoherence ⊊ MI" containment claim to operational discriminator

**Old (Pass-67 MR-IDC-1):** {MI} ⊊ {Incoherent}; discriminator unspecified.
**New (Pass-72):** Discriminator = CONCEIVABILITY. Incoherent-conceivable pairs (bachelor-married) are NOT MI; incoherent-inconceivable pairs (square-circle) ARE MI.

**Impact:** the {Incoherent}\{MI} difference set is now well-defined and populated with concrete examples.

## 4. HMR-1 Compatibility Re-Verification

The new MI framing requires re-verification that HMR-1 is fully compatible:

### 4.1 The HMR-vs-MI distinction is SHARPER under refinement #4

**Old (Pass-70/71):** HMR is multi-label per proposition; MI is "both-truth-values" per proposition. Distinction was clear but not deeply principled.

**New (Pass-72):** HMR is multi-label-and-CONCEIVABLE per proposition or proposition-set; MI is two-propositions-COMBINED-inconceivable. Distinction is now principled on the **conceivability axis**.

### 4.2 HMR examples re-checked under refinement #4

All 15 HMR examples in corpus (5 from Pass-70 + 10 from Pass-71 B0) require the SINGLE-MODEL-CONCEIVABILITY check. Re-verifying the 5 main examples:

| Ex | Labels | Conceivable as single multi-framing mental model? | MI under refinement #4? |
|---|---|---|---|
| HMR-3.1 (Consciousness) | {T_panpsychist, T_emergentist, I_malformed} | YES — can hold all 3 framings at their respective levels | NO |
| HMR-3.2 (Liar) | {MI, T, MT-B1} | YES — the MI-IN-THE-LABEL-SET is itself a holdable meta-claim | NO (the proposition contains a MI label but the multi-label set is conceivable) |
| HMR-3.3 (Ego-dissolution) | {T, F, I} | YES — anchored in Brandon ketamine lived experience | NO |
| HMR-4.1 (Free will) | {T, F, MI, MT-B1} | YES — conceivable as a four-framing analysis target | NO |
| HMR-5.1 (God exists) | {T, F, MI, I, MT-B1} | YES — conceivable as a five-framing analysis target | NO |

**All 5 HMR main examples PASS the HMR-vs-MI distinction under refinement #4.** HMR-1 is FULLY COMPATIBLE with the new MI framing.

**Critical clarification — HMR-3.2 (liar sentence) contains a MI label in its multi-label set BUT is not itself MI.** The proposition is HMR-3 {DT_self-reference-paradox, T_informative-about-paradoxes, MT-B1_moot-for-decisions}. The MI label captures the self-referential paradox component (which IS MI in the refinement-#4 sense: "this sentence" + "is false" combined yields no actualizable mental object). But the FULL multi-label HMR characterization is conceivable as a stable mental model. **MI can appear as a component of an HMR, but HMR is not MI.**
