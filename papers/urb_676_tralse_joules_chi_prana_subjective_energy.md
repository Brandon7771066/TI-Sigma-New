## 1. The Two-Component Structure of Chi

Vital force traditions across cultures describe chi/prana as somehow both physical and experiential — simultaneously the energy that powers biological processes and the felt sense of aliveness, vitality, and intentional capacity. ### 1.2 Subjective Chi: The Tralse-Joules Rate

**Subjective chi** is the felt sense of energy, intentional capacity, and vital aliveness. This is the component that materialist frameworks cannot account for: the experience of feeling energized or depleted that does not always correlate with ATP levels or physical measures.

Low subjective chi = low Tralsity (system defaulting to Existence loop) AND low MR rate (stagnation).

The two components are **causally coupled but not reducible to one another:**

The complete chi experience requires BOTH components. This is why traditions universally combine physical practices (diet, exercise, herbs, acupuncture — E-dimension) with contemplative practices (meditation, pranayama, metta — I-dimension).
