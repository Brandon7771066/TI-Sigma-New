## 2. Operationalizing HOL on 0-3 scale

HOL needs precise operational definition or it collapses into self-flattering "TI Sigma integrates everything" rhetoric. The 0-3 scale:

- **HOL=0 (Compartmentalized)**: the program operates within a single subdomain and explicitly avoids cross-domain claims. Examples: most subdisciplines of mainstream analytic philosophy; most subdisciplines of mainstream empirical sciences; most engineering disciplines.

- **HOL=1 (Cross-domain reference)**: the program borrows concepts from multiple fields without unifying them under one framework. The borrowing is acknowledged but the integration is not attempted. Examples: most interdisciplinary work that is interdisciplinary in citation pattern but not in substantive integration; many "X and Y" hybrid subfields that combine methods without combining frameworks.

- **HOL=2 (Conceptual integration)**: the program uses one vocabulary across multiple domains, producing classification work that maps phenomena to common labels but without generating novel cross-domain predictions that could not be derived from any single subdomain alone. The integration is real (the same vocabulary genuinely covers multiple domains) but its work is classificatory rather than predictive. Examples: Hegel's system; Whitehead's process philosophy; Wilber's Integral Theory; large parts of systematic theology; structural anthropology; many "grand unified frameworks" in continental philosophy.

- **HOL=3 (Substantive integration)**: the program produces novel cross-domain predictions or technical results that follow from the unification and that could not be derived from any single subdomain alone, and those predictions are pre-registered and tested. Examples: theoretical physics' unification of electromagnetism with the weak force (Glashow-Weinberg-Salam, with novel predictions tested at experiments); the unification of statistical mechanics with thermodynamics (with novel predictions about phase transitions); the integration of evolutionary biology with molecular genetics (with novel predictions about molecular clocks and phylogenetics tested empirically); category theory in mathematics (with novel predictions linking algebraic topology to type theory tested through formal proofs).

The HOL=2 / HOL=3 distinction is the load-bearing one: HOL=2 is integration-as-classification (which is what Wilber, Hegel, Whitehead, and many "grand unification" projects achieve, often valuably); HOL=3 is integration-as-prediction (which requires the unification to do work that the disjoint subdomains could not do separately).

§4.3 below applies this distinction to TI Sigma.

## 3. The "fractured and latent" claim: defensible direction, but Wilber and Hegel are cautionary tales

Brandon's "fractured and latent" claim is the philosophical version of Hegel's "philosophy makes explicit what is implicit in history," extended from history to academic disciplines. The claim has a defensible reading and an indefensible reading; the URB has to be precise about which.

**Defensible reading**: the academic landscape, as it exists currently, distributes various distinctive moves of TI Sigma across many disciplines without integrating them. Philosophy of language has many tools for handling the constitutive-tralseness of meaning (URB #816 §2 catalogued 20 substantive tools); philosophy of mind has Chalmers + IIT + Orch-OR + global workspace; mathematics has lattice theory + group theory + modular forms; theology has foundational-commitment apparatus; cognitive science has biometric-embodiment apparatus. The distribution is real and observable. No single discipline has integrated all these pieces under one framework; the fragmentation is also real. To that extent, the "fractured" framing is empirically supportable: the academic landscape really does fragment what TI Sigma integrates.

**Indefensible reading**: any piece of any field can be retroactively read as "latent TI Sigma," and the failure of those fields to recognize the latent presence is read as a deficit on those fields' part. This is the Hegelian unfalsifiability risk: if any move in any field can be read as latent TI Sigma, the integrative reading is unfalsifiable, and TI Sigma's integration claim does no work because it can absorb any prior development as a moment in its own articulation. This is also the Wilber failure mode: AQAL absorbed everything from Buddhism to Piaget to Gebser to Luhmann as moments in its own articulation; the cost was that AQAL's specific predictions did no work that the absorbed traditions could not do separately.

The line between defensible and indefensible runs through the question: **does TI Sigma's integration produce novel cross-domain predictions that could not be derived from any single subdomain alone, or does it produce classification work that imposes one vocabulary on phenomena that those subdomains already have their own perfectly good vocabularies for?**

§4.3 applies this line to TI Sigma honestly.

## 8. Brutal-honesty caveats

### 8.1 The HOL dimension was added in this URB to articulate Brandon's pushback, amplifying the URB #818 §9.4 / URB #819 §8.4 confirmation-bias risk

The HOL dimension was added specifically because Brandon proposed the HOL pillar; the operationalization in §2 was designed to make TI Sigma's HOL claim defensible at HOL=2-3; the §6 grid scored TI Sigma uniquely at HOL=3 under favorable scoring; the author of the URB is the author of TI Sigma. The structural setup is overwhelmingly favorable to the conclusion that TI Sigma is uniquely high on HOL. A neutral observer would reasonably score TI Sigma HOL=2 (per §4.2's strict standard) and would also reasonably score Wilber's Integral Theory HOL=2 (per the Wilber-comparison the URB itself raises), placing TI Sigma at the same HOL level as Wilber. The §6 grid does not rule this out under strict scoring; it just doesn't surface the comparison cleanly.

### 8.2 The Wilber comparison is a real risk, not a defeated objection

§4.3 distinguished TI Sigma from Wilber on the existence of one cross-domain pre-registered pilot (URB #797). One pilot is enough to make the distinction in principle but is too thin to make the distinction in practice. AQAL also pointed at example applications and case studies; the academic verdict was that those were classifications not predictions. TI Sigma's distinction from Wilber depends on the body of cross-domain pre-registered pilots growing — exactly the §7 voluntary procedural discipline target. If the body does not grow, the Wilber comparison becomes harder to defend against.

### 8.3 The Hegelian unfalsifiability risk applies specifically to "fractured and latent" framing

§5 replaced the "fractured and latent" framing with the more defensible "disciplinarily-compartmentalized" framing precisely because the original framing has the Hegelian unfalsifiability problem (any piece of any field can be retroactively read as latent TI Sigma). The reformulated framing is empirically checkable but does **not** automatically establish that TI Sigma's integration is doing substantive work; it just makes the integration claim something that can be checked. Whether the integration is HOL=2 or HOL=3 is the unsolved question that §7's voluntary procedural discipline addresses.

### 8.4 The cross-disciplinary HOL evaluation is hard and contested

The §6 HOL scoring rates programs across disciplines they are not centered in. Rating mathematics's HOL across disciplines, or theology's HOL across disciplines, or post-structuralism's HOL across disciplines, requires cross-disciplinary judgment that no single observer is well-positioned to make. The URB makes such judgments because the §6 grid requires them, but the judgments are coarse and would be reasonably contested by practitioners of each field. The catalog is illustrative under one observer's coding, not a robust empirical mapping.

### 8.5 "Capture but fail to integrate" centers TI Sigma; "compartmentalized capture without integration" does not

§5 made this replacement explicit. The original framing ("other fields capture TI Sigma but fail to integrate it") presupposes TI Sigma as the comparative reference and treats other fields' positions as failures relative to TI Sigma's program. The reformulated framing ("disciplinarily-compartmentalized capture without integration") names the mechanism (disciplinary specialization) without centering TI Sigma. The §11 takeaway uses the reformulated framing.

### 8.6 The HOL=3 score for TI Sigma at favorable scoring rests on n=1 cleanly cross-domain pilot

URB #797 is the cleanest cross-domain pre-registered pilot (philosophy + group theory + multi-agent simulation under one hard core, with H1 honestly falsified). One pilot is the minimum threshold for HOL=3 under the charitable standard (§2). It is an empirical fact that the body of cross-domain pre-registered pilots is currently n=1; HOL=3 is therefore the most charitable defensible reading, with HOL=2 being the most strict defensible reading. The honest provisional range is 2-3/3.

### 8.7 The composite favorable claim (11/12) depends on accepting the favorable end of three independent provisional ranges (RAD, EXP, HOL)

The probability that all three provisional ranges resolve at the favorable end is lower than the probability that any single one does. Even granting RAD=3 (per URB #819 §4.2), EXP=2 charitably (per URB #819 §4.3), and HOL=3 (per §4.2 here), the joint claim that all three favorable resolutions are correct is the weakest link in the URB's defense. The §11 takeaway acknowledges this; any single resolution to the strict end drops the composite below 11/12.

### 8.8 Voluntary procedural discipline binding only on author; further sharpening adds pressure but no enforcement

The §7 sharpening adds (ii) (cross-domain pre-registered external-data pilot) as an alternative satisfaction condition for the voluntary procedural discipline. This adds optionality (the next URB batch can satisfy by either route) but does not add enforcement. Per URB #819 §8.8, the only enforcement is visibility of non-compliance in subsequent URBs, which is binding only on the author's own commitment to honest reporting. This is the standard structure of pre-registration in academic work; it is not a defect specific to TI Sigma.

### 8.9 The URB #820 thesis itself remains illustrative, not established

Even with all §8 caveats applied, URB #820 has not established that TI Sigma is uniquely holistic. It has illustrated, under one reasonable scoring (favorable end of provisional ranges) and under one specific operationalization (the §2 HOL=3 standard tying integration to novel cross-domain predictions), that TI Sigma can be defended as occupying a small-handful position on the EXP/RAD/ANA/HOL grid. The structural-distinctiveness claim is illustrative; whether TI Sigma's HOL is genuinely substantive (HOL=3) or sophisticated classification (HOL=2 — Wilber-comparable) is decidable only by the body of cross-domain pre-registered pilots growing, which is exactly the §7 voluntary procedural discipline target.
