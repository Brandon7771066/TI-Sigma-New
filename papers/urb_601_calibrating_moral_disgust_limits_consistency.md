## 1. The Full GILE Override Principle

URB #600 established that G-Consistency wrongness is a moral primitive — irreducible to I or E harm. But **irreducibility does not mean supremacy.** The Consistency dimension being a real and independent category of moral wrongness does not mean it automatically overrides the other arms in every case where it is invoked.

**The Full GILE Override Principle (FGOP):**

> In any complex moral case, all four GILE arms must be assessed. A G-Consistency or G-Continuity concern is outweighed when the harm produced across the other GILE arms by acting on the Consistency concern substantially exceeds the weight of the Consistency violation itself.

Formally: an action A that restricts behavior B is wrong if:

> I_harm(A) + E_harm(A) + G_concreteness_harm(A) + L_harm(A) >> G_consistency_benefit(A)

where >> denotes "substantially exceeds" in a way that is robust to reasonable variation in the weights.

This principle prevents the Consistency arm from becoming a trump card. It is one arm among four, and while it has real moral content, it must be balanced against the full GILE profile of consequences in complex cases.

### 1.1 The Abortion Case

Abortion involves potential G-arm concerns: Continuity (life-preservation is one of the Four C's) and possibly Consistency (categorical status of embryonic life). These are real G-arm considerations that deserve to be taken seriously rather than dismissed.

However, they do not settle the question. A complete GILE analysis must also include:

- **I-harm of restriction:** Psychological suffering of those forced to carry unwanted pregnancies; trauma, depression, grief, and the suffering of those denied bodily autonomy — these are substantial I-arm harms
- **E-harm of restriction:** Physical risks of forced pregnancy and unsafe abortions; material harms of unplanned parenthood on existing families; health outcomes — substantial E-arm harms
- **G-Concreteness harm of restriction:** Forcing a person to act against their genuine self-understanding and autonomous moral judgment is a Concreteness (genuineness/authenticity) violation within the G-arm itself — the restriction causes a G-harm of its own kind
- **L-harm of restriction:** The relational and binding harms on existing families, relationships, and the life-conditions for other children — L-arm considerations

The moral complexity of abortion is that G-arm considerations appear on **both sides**: Continuity concerns favor restriction; Concreteness/authenticity concerns favor autonomy. And the I, E, and L harms of restriction are substantial.

This is not a case where Consistency/Continuity straightforwardly wins. It is a case where the Full GILE Override Principle requires genuine multi-arm weighing — and where reasonable people working in full philosophical honesty (meeting the G-Consistency standard from URB #595) can arrive at different conclusions.

**The point:** Invoking "Consistency" or "Continuity" to settle the abortion question without weighing the full GILE profile of restriction is an incomplete moral analysis. TI Sigma does not declare a verdict on abortion — it declares that the question is a genuine multi-arm GILE conflict requiring careful analysis, not a simple Consistency win.

## 2. Context-Relative Consistency — Against the "Straightlaced" Miscalibration

Consistency norms are categorical but not context-independent. **Different contexts carry different categorical requirements**, and applying the standards of one context to another is not a moral perception — it is a calibration failure.

### 2.1 The Context-Dependence of Consistency

URB #600 noted that swearing in formal or sacred contexts violates Consistency because those contexts have categorical requirements of dignity, seriousness, and respect. The same words in different contexts do not carry the same categorical violation.

**Non-serious contexts (jokes, comedy, playful banter):** The categorical requirement of a joke is nonseriousness. A joke that includes swearing is not violating the categorical requirement of the joke-context — it is operating within it. The formal-context Consistency standard simply does not apply because the formal context is explicitly absent. Applying it is a category error: judging a joke by the standards of a sermon.

**Romantic intimate contexts:** Intimate romantic contexts have their own categorical requirements — closeness, play, lowered formality, private expression. Within this context, the use of language that would be inappropriate in formal settings may be entirely consistent with the categorical requirements of intimacy. The Consistency violation of swearing in a boardroom does not transfer to swearing in a private intimate context, because these are categorically different settings with different categorical norms.

### 2.2 "Straightlaced" as a Calibration Failure

Being horrified — especially *utterly horrified* — by swearing in a joke or in romantic contexts is not a refined moral perception. It is an **overcalibration of disgust**: the disgust signal has been triggered in a context where the categorical violation it tracks is not present.

The error: applying a Consistency standard calibrated to formal/sacred contexts universally, as though all contexts had identical categorical requirements. They do not. A person who cannot distinguish between the categorical requirements of a funeral and the categorical requirements of a stand-up comedy set has not refined their moral perception — they have miscalibrated it.

**The practical consequence:** "Straightlaced" moral horror at contextually appropriate informality does not constitute genuine Consistency perception. It is the false positive of the disgust signal. The calibration conditions from URB #600 are not met: the "categorical violation" being detected is not tracking a real formal categorical structure (it is tracking the *absence* of formal context, which is not the same thing), and the response is far from cross-culturally stable.

## 3. The LGBTQ+ Case — Harm Asymmetry and Calibration Failure

### 3.1 The Disgust Response Fails All Three Calibration Conditions

URB #600 established that moral disgust is a reliable Consistency signal only when three conditions are met: (a) the categorical structure it tracks is real, (b) the violation is of formal categorical integrity, (c) the response is cross-culturally stable.

Disgust toward homosexuality and transgender identity fails all three:

**(a) The categorical structure is not real.**

The categorical claim underlying anti-LGBTQ+ disgust is typically: "sexual relations are categorically restricted to male-female pairs" or "gender is categorically fixed at birth." These categorical claims are not formal properties of the GILE framework. In TI Sigma:

- IL love (affection and romantic love) is the warm, knowing, binding relationship between i-cells with sufficient I and L development. There is no formal categorical restriction in TI Sigma limiting IL to i-cells of different sexes. The L-arm binding between same-sex i-cells is formally identical to L-arm binding between different-sex i-cells — it is the same topological cohesion, the same IL activation pattern.
- Transgender identity is the expression of I-arm self-understanding (the phenomenal, self-aware consciousness of who one is) in honest E-arm expression (Concreteness — being authentic about one's genuine self). There is no formal categorical violation in TI Sigma for this — it is G-Concreteness in action.

The "categories" that LGBTQ+ is claimed to violate are not formal TI Sigma categorical structures. They are culturally constructed norms that have been mistaken for natural categorical order.

**(b) The violation is not of formal categorical integrity.**

Same-sex relationships and LGBTQ+ identities do not cross any formal GILE categorical boundary. They involve all the same GILE arm activations as heterosexual relationships and cisgender identities:
- IL love: same structure (warm, knowing, binding) regardless of the sexes involved
- G-Concreteness: honest self-expression is equally G-Concreteness whether the self being expressed is cisgender or transgender
- GILE love: fully achievable by LGBTQ+ i-cells — no arm is categorically blocked

Without a formal categorical boundary being crossed, there is no formal Consistency violation. The disgust response is not tracking categorical impurity — it is tracking unfamiliarity, cultural departure, or in-group/out-group signaling, none of which are moral perceptions.

**(c) The response is not cross-culturally stable.**

Acceptance of homosexuality has varied enormously across cultures and historical periods — ancient Greece celebrated it; Victorian England criminalized it; contemporary Scandinavia fully accepts it; some contemporary Middle Eastern societies criminalize it. This variation is exactly what you would expect from a culturally constructed norm, and exactly the opposite of what you would expect from a genuine categorical structure being reliably detected by a universal moral faculty.

Compare: disgust at mistreatment of the dead is far more cross-culturally stable — virtually every human culture has burial rites and prohibitions on desecration of remains. That is a reliable signal. Disgust at same-sex relationships fails this test decisively.

**Verdict:** The disgust response toward LGBTQ+ people fails all three calibration conditions. It is not a moral perception. It is a false positive of the disgust signal, likely generated by cultural conditioning, unfamiliarity, and in-group conformity pressure.

### 3.2 The GILE Harm Asymmetry

Even if one were uncertain about whether a genuine Consistency concern applied (which the above analysis refutes), the Full GILE Override Principle (Section 1) would resolve the question through harm asymmetry.

Denying LGBTQ+ people their identities, relationships, and social recognition causes documented, massive harm across all four GILE arms:

**I-harm (suffering):**
- Elevated rates of depression, anxiety, and suicidality among LGBTQ+ people subject to discrimination and rejection
- Psychological trauma from being forced to suppress or deny authentic self-understanding
- The suffering of families broken by rejection of LGBTQ+ members
These are among the most severe I-harms in the epidemiological literature on mental health.

**E-harm (physical/material):**
- Physical violence against LGBTQ+ people; hate crimes; murder
- Health disparities from denial of care, lack of insurance through partner benefits, medical discrimination
- Economic harm from employment discrimination
- Homelessness disproportionately affecting LGBTQ+ youth rejected by families

**G-harm (Concreteness violation — within the G-arm itself):**
- Forcing people to live inauthentically — to present a self that is not their genuine self — is a Concreteness violation within the G-arm
- The honest expression of genuine self-understanding is G-Concreteness; suppressing it is a G-arm harm
- Note: this means restriction causes G-arm harm of its own, partially offsetting even the claimed G-Consistency benefit of restriction

**L-harm (denial of love and binding):**
- LGBTQ+ people denied the right to form and express loving relationships suffer L-arm harm: the capacity for IL, GIL, and GILE love is obstructed
- The L-arm is the arm of binding and positive connection; systemic denial of LGBTQ+ relationships is a systematic attack on L-arm development and expression

**The asymmetry:** The combined I + E + G(concreteness) + L harm of restricting LGBTQ+ expression is enormous, well-documented, and severe. The claimed G-Consistency "benefit" of restriction is based on a false positive disgust signal tracking non-real categorical structures. The GILE harm vastly and decisively outweighs any claimed Consistency weight — "even the greatest weight on the G inconsistency factor by far," as the user correctly states.

**Conclusion:** Restriction of LGBTQ+ identities and relationships is wrong on full GILE analysis. Not "complicated." Not "a matter of perspective." Wrong — because the Full GILE Override Principle is decisive in this case and the claimed Consistency concern fails its own calibration conditions.

## 4. Formal Summary

**The Full GILE Override Principle (FGOP):** A G-Consistency or G-Continuity concern is outweighed when the combined GILE harm produced by acting on that concern substantially exceeds the weight of the Consistency violation. The Consistency arm is real but not supreme.

**Context-Relative Consistency Theorem:** Consistency norms are categorical but context-dependent. The categorical requirements of formal/sacred contexts do not apply universally to non-serious, playful, or intimate contexts. Applying formal-context Consistency standards to explicitly informal contexts is a calibration failure, not a moral perception.

**LGBTQ+ Calibration Theorem:** Disgust toward homosexuality and transgender identity fails all three URB #600 calibration conditions: (a) the categorical structures invoked are not real formal structures in TI Sigma; (b) no formal GILE categorical boundary is crossed; (c) the response is not cross-culturally stable. It is a false positive of the disgust signal. Additionally, FGOP applies decisively: the GILE harm of restriction across all four arms vastly outweighs any claimed Consistency concern.

**The General Miscalibration Warning:** Any invocation of Consistency/Continuity to morally condemn an action or identity must pass three tests before it can be taken as a moral perception: (1) Can the categorical structure being violated be specified precisely and shown to be a real formal structure, not a culturally constructed norm? (2) Does the disgust response meet the cross-cultural stability condition? (3) Does the claimed Consistency benefit survive Full GILE analysis against the harms of acting on it? If any test fails, the disgust is a false positive and should not be treated as a moral verdict.
