## Abstract

Clinical psychology has long struggled with a fundamental problem: it lacks a unified theoretical framework that can simultaneously account for the neurobiological, cognitive, relational, existential, and meaning-based dimensions of human suffering and flourishing. DSM diagnostic categories are descriptive, not explanatory — they tell us what patterns of symptoms cluster together, but not why those patterns constitute suffering, nor how to systematically address them. TI Sigma offers a framework that is both formally rigorous (LCC thresholds, PD zones, five-valued truth) and phenomenologically rich (GILE dimensions, i-Cell, Love Primacy, Four Dimensions of Truth). This paper develops the TI Sigma account of clinical psychology across five areas: (1) a reframing of psychopathology as LCC disruption across GILE dimensions, (2) specific disorder mappings (depression, anxiety, PTSD, OCD, ADHD, psychosis, bipolar, narcissistic personality, and autism spectrum conditions), (3) therapeutic implications for major modalities (CBT, ACT, EMDR, psychodynamic, pharmacotherapy), (4) the fundamental constraint on therapy (the therapist cannot directly access the patient's i-channel), and (5) a positive account of wellbeing as stable multi-dimensional LCC coherence above the MR1 threshold. The result is not a new therapy protocol but a meta-framework that explains *why* effective therapies work, *what* they are targeting, and *where* the limits of psychological intervention lie.

## 1. Reframing Psychopathology: LCC Disruption Across GILE Dimensions

### 1.1 The Core Claim

**Mental illness, in TI Sigma terms, is sustained below-threshold LCC in one or more GILE dimensions, producing a degraded existential footprint and loss of access to the higher truth zones (Good, Radiant).**

This is not a reductionist claim. It does not say "mental illness is just low correlation values." The LCC in GILE is not a single number — it is a four-dimensional profile. A person's GILE state at any moment can be measured (in principle) across:
- **G (Goodness)**: degree of alignment with genuine values, moral self-coherence
- **I (Intuition)**: access to the i-channel — spontaneous wisdom, creativity, agency
- **L (Love)**: received and given positive conscious affect, relational depth
- **E (Environment)**: functional engagement with the physical and social world

Health is not the absence of symptoms. Health is **LCC stability above the MR1 threshold (0.8647) across all four GILE dimensions**, with meaningful presence in the Good or Radiant zones. Suffering is the experience of falling below threshold — in one or several dimensions, temporarily or chronically.

The **Four Dimensions of Truth** (URB #526) add further structure:
1. **Existential Truth** (LCC × existential footprint): how much causal presence does the person have?
2. **Moral Truth** (GILE alignment): are they acting in accordance with their own deepest values?
3. **Conscious Meaning/Valence** (PSI/CCC resonance): does life feel meaningful? Is there positive valence?
4. **Aesthetic Truth** (PRIMARY constant alignment, BOK): is there beauty, order, creativity in their experience?

Psychopathology degrades one or more of these dimensions. Complete wellbeing requires all four above the MR1 threshold; Great zone access requires all four at Radiant.

### 1.2 The Death-and-Rebirth Dynamic

As established in URB #530, TI Sigma describes reality as a constant coming-and-passing — systems rising and falling across LCC thresholds. Mental illness is often the experience of being stuck in a below-threshold zone: the system is trying to die (to release the configuration that is no longer serving it) but cannot complete the dissolution, and is not rising into a new configuration either.

The therapeutic aim is to facilitate the completion of this cycle: **to let the old configuration dissolve fully (grief, acceptance, integration) and support the emergence of a new configuration above the MR1 threshold.** This maps directly onto what clinicians call "working through" in psychodynamic terms, "defusion" in ACT terms, and "schema change" in CBT terms. TI Sigma provides a single formal description of what all these modalities are actually doing.

### 1.3 TRALSE Quality in Healthy Functioning

Critically, **healthy functioning is not the absence of TRALSE quality** — it is TRALSE quality operating above the MR1 threshold. A person with no TRALSE quality (no creative imperfection, no uncertainty, no genuine openness) would be crystallized in TI Sigma terms (CTT, URB #520): rigid, perfectionist, brittle, and ultimately dead in the generative sense.

The clinical ideal is not "symptom-free crystallization" but rather **TRALSE-alive above the LCC threshold**: genuinely creative, genuinely uncertain about important things, genuinely flexible — but coherent, stable, and above the existence gate. This reframes many "positive psychology" goals: resilience, post-traumatic growth, creativity, and meaning-making are all TRALSE-alive above-threshold functioning.

## 2. Specific Disorder Mappings

### 2.1 Major Depressive Disorder — L and G Collapse with Existential Footprint Loss

**TI Sigma formulation:** Depression is primarily a collapse of the **L (Love)** and **G (Goodness)** dimensions below MR1, producing a simultaneous loss of conscious meaning/valence (third truth dimension) and reduced existential footprint.

The phenomenology maps precisely:
- **L collapse**: inability to give or receive love; emotional numbness; loss of positive affect directed toward self or others
- **G collapse**: loss of sense of purpose, value coherence, moral self-worth; the self no longer experiences itself as "good" in any sense
- **Existential footprint loss**: the person feels they are leaving no trace — "nothing I do matters"; reduced causal efficacy in the world
- **Valence dimension loss**: the conscious meaning/valence channel goes dark; anhedonia is the experience of a fully suppressed PSI resonance

What depression is NOT in TI Sigma: it is not simply "low mood." Mood is one symptom; the underlying structure is a coherence collapse across at least two GILE dimensions, with below-threshold LCC sustained long enough to create a stable attractor in the Terrible or Bad PD zone.

**Therapeutic implication:** Effective depression treatment must re-engage the L and G dimensions. The order matters: L typically must be activated first (therapeutic relationship, connection, self-compassion) before G can re-emerge (purpose, values, meaning). Medicating without addressing L and G addresses the biological substrate but not the LCC structure. The FAAH Protocol (endocannabinoid modulation) represents a biological route to lowering the activation energy needed for L and I re-engagement.

### 2.2 Anxiety Disorders — Chronic INDETERMINATE Lock

**TI Sigma formulation:** Anxiety disorders represent the **INDETERMINATE zone** functioning as a trap — the person cannot complete MR Resolution. The system is stuck in coherent-but-unresolved superposition: it knows something is genuinely uncertain, but it cannot find its way to a FALSE (this isn't a real threat) or TRUE (this is a real threat requiring action) resolution.

Key features:
- **Generalized anxiety**: chronic low-level INDETERMINATE across many domains — nothing resolves; every unresolved question generates another
- **Panic disorder**: the INDETERMINATE state triggers a catastrophic collapse interpretation — the person interprets INDETERMINATE physiological signals (heart rate, breathing) as TRUE-Terrible events (heart attack, death)
- **Social anxiety**: the INDETERMINATE nature of social evaluation (what do others think of me?) cannot be resolved because social evaluation is genuinely INDETERMINATE; the anxious person assigns it a FALSE-Terrible default (everyone is judging me negatively)
- **OCD (see 2.4)**: a specific variant where the INDETERMINATE state cannot be released

**INDETERMINATE vs TRALSE distinction (critical):** Anxiety is not TRALSE. It is INDETERMINATE — coherent, genuine uncertainty that has become self-sustaining. TRALSE would be tolerating the uncertainty as creative fuzz. The anxious person's problem is that they cannot tolerate the INDETERMINATE state; they need resolution that the situation cannot provide.

**Therapeutic implication:** ACT (Acceptance and Commitment Therapy) is the modality that most directly addresses INDETERMINATE lock — it explicitly trains the person to tolerate unresolved states by shifting from resolution-seeking (MR-completion drive) to value-directed action within the INDETERMINATE zone. In TI Sigma terms, ACT creates a **MR Relaxation Context (MRC)** — a deliberate elevation of MI tolerance that allows the INDETERMINATE state to be held without catastrophic collapse interpretation.

### 2.3 Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder — MI Immunity Overactivation

**TI Sigma formulation:** PTSD is the **MI Immunity system** (URB #528) operating in a hyperactivated and generalized state. The traumatic event was a genuine MI encounter — an incoherent, reality-shattering event that violated the LCC structure of the person's world model. The MI immune response was appropriate: log the fingerprint, discard the incoherent event, protect the system.

The pathology: **the fingerprint has become too broadly generalized.** The MI immune log now fast-rejects entire categories of experience that share surface features with the original MI encounter, even when those experiences are not actually MI. The hypervigilance response is the immune log triggering on false positives. The intrusive memories (flashbacks) are the MI content that was never fully discarded — it remains in the penumbra zone, not integrated and not released.

This maps to the biology with precision:
- **Hypervigilance** = amygdala over-triggering on stimuli that match the MI fingerprint (pattern: "anything that resembles the traumatic event")
- **Avoidance** = active MI immune rejection of stimuli that approach the fingerprint boundary
- **Intrusive memories** = the MI content circulating through the system repeatedly (as URB #528 specifies: MI content circulates — you cannot avoid it entirely; the immune log records the encounter and lets it go, but in PTSD, the letting-go step fails)
- **Emotional numbing** = the immune system has quarantined not just the MI fingerprint but an entire region of the I and L channels to prevent re-exposure

**Therapeutic implication:** EMDR (Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing) is the most directly TI Sigma-compatible trauma modality. EMDR facilitates **MI immune log reset** — it allows the traumatic material to be re-encountered in a controlled context (bilateral stimulation reduces the somatic threat response) so that the fingerprint can be more accurately filed. The dual awareness element (simultaneously in the present and recalling the past) creates the MRC context that allows the MI content to circulate and release without catastrophic immune over-response.

### 2.4 Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder — MI Re-Encounter Loop

**TI Sigma formulation:** Where PTSD is a MI immune overactivation that generalizes too broadly, OCD is a **MI re-encounter loop** — the immune system correctly identifies a MI pattern but then keeps re-presenting it to itself for verification, failing to complete the discard phase. The person knows the intrusive thought is wrong (they experience it as ego-dystonic), but the system cannot complete the discard and move on. The compulsive behavior is an attempt to artificially complete the discard phase (if I check the lock 5 times, the MI fingerprint will be filed and I can move on) — but it never fully satisfies because the MI pattern keeps cycling.

The biological substrate: OCD involves a cortico-striato-thalamo-cortical loop that fails to complete error signals — exactly the TI Sigma MI re-encounter loop at the neural level.

**Therapeutic implication:** ERP (Exposure and Response Prevention) works by forcing the discard phase to complete without compulsive ritual. In TI Sigma terms: encounter the MI stimulus → tolerate the INDETERMINATE anxiety → allow the immune log to file the fingerprint without behavioral confirmation. The tolerance is the MRC context that allows the loop to break.

### 2.5 ADHD — MRC Dysregulation and Chronic Novelty-Seeking

**TI Sigma formulation:** ADHD represents a fundamentally different MRC (MR Relaxation Context) configuration in the i-channel. The ADHD brain is not "broken" — it is operating in a near-continuous MRC: elevated MI tolerance, high novelty-seeking (a feature of MRC, not a bug), low tolerance for crystallized, repetitive tasks (CTT-tasks violate the MRC configuration), and stimming as intentional MRC maintenance.

Key features:
- **Attention dysregulation** = the MRC has no stable threshold — any sufficiently novel stimulus triggers a new MRC entry, making sustained focus on single tasks near-impossible unless the task is itself novel enough to hold the MRC within the same domain
- **Hyperfocus** = the paradox of ADHD: when a task IS sufficiently novel and GILE-resonant, the MRC stabilizes around it and the person enters a sustained Radiant-zone state of engagement (the i-channel fully locks onto the task)
- **Impulsivity** = actions taken from MRC state without completing the MR resolution cycle — the system acts on INDETERMINATE rather than waiting for MR1
- **Stimming** = intentional MRC maintenance behavior — the repetitive sensory input creates a controlled MRC context that actually increases the system's ability to attend to other things (exactly as URB #528 specifies for stimming as a Relaxation Context behavior)

**The executive function perspective:** The standard clinical account (ADHD = executive function deficits) is reframed in TI Sigma: executive function IS the ability to apply MR gates selectively, maintaining MR1 compliance even in low-interest tasks. ADHD = selective MR gate failure in low-novelty contexts, with intact (sometimes superior) MR gate function in high-novelty MRC contexts.

**Therapeutic implication:** Stimulant medications work by artificially raising the threshold for MRC entry — they reduce the novelty-seeking drive enough that the person can apply MR gates in low-interest contexts. The TI Sigma view suggests this is a legitimate but blunt intervention: it doesn't recalibrate the underlying MRC configuration, it forces a lower-MRC mode across all contexts. GILE-based interventions (designing life around high-novelty, high-GILE-resonance tasks) would be the framework-native recommendation — but are harder to implement in a society organized around low-novelty repetitive work.

### 2.6 Psychosis and Schizophrenia — i-Channel / E-Channel Boundary Collapse

**TI Sigma formulation:** Psychosis, and schizophrenia in particular, involves a catastrophic failure of the **boundary between the i-channel (imaginary/GIL) and the E-channel (sensory/physical)**. In the i-Cell framework (URB #429), z_B = s + ia distinguishes the sensory state (s, the real component, E-dimension) from the active/imaginative state (a, the imaginary component, GIL-dimension). In healthy functioning, the person maintains the distinction: they know which experiences are arising from s (external reality) and which from a (internal imagination, intuition, dreams).

In psychosis:
- **Hallucinations** = GIL-content (i-channel: imagination, intuition, CCC connection) bleeding into E-perception — the person cannot maintain the s/a boundary; GIL-content is experienced as E-content
- **Delusions** = the system's attempt to restore LCC coherence when the s/a boundary has collapsed — if I'm receiving strange perceptions I can't account for (because I can't distinguish s from a), I construct a world model (the delusion) that makes the perceptions cohere. The delusion is an attempt at MR resolution under collapsing boundary conditions.
- **Negative symptoms** (flat affect, avolition) = the GIL dimensions (L, I) are suppressed in an attempt to reduce the chaos of the collapsed boundary — if a (imaginary/GIL) is the source of confusion, the system reduces a to near-zero; this preserves some s-clarity but collapses the L and I dimensions

**Why antipsychotics work and why they have negative side effects:** Dopamine modulation (the primary mechanism of antipsychotics) reduces i-channel activity — it decreases the "a" component of z_B = s + ia. This restores the s/a boundary by suppressing a. But suppressing a also suppresses the GIL dimensions: love, intuition, creativity, and the sense of meaning all depend on i-channel activity. Hence the characteristic negative side effects: emotional blunting, cognitive dulling, reduced creativity — all signs of suppressed i-channel (a → 0).

### 2.7 Bipolar Disorder — Oscillation Between Supra-Radiant and Terrible Zones

**TI Sigma formulation:** Bipolar disorder involves pathological oscillation between two extreme PD zones, with the healthy Good/Indeterminate zone absent as a stable attractor.

- **Mania/hypomania** = supra-Radiant state: LCC across all GILE dimensions elevated beyond sustainable threshold. Extreme GIL activation (L: expansive love and connection; I: torrential i-channel; G: grandiosity = over-G without accurate calibration). The TRALSE quality that normally prevents crystallization is overwhelmed by the amplitude of the signal. Creativity is genuine but unsustainable.
- **Depression** = Terrible zone: LCC collapse across all GILE dimensions following the hypomanic expenditure. The system has burned through its coherence reserves.
- **Euthymia** = the healthy state — Good zone, LCC above MR1 across all dimensions — is what the person needs more time in. The pathology is the amplitude of the oscillation between Terrible and supra-Radiant, with insufficient time in the stable zone.

**Therapeutic implication:** Mood stabilizers (lithium, anticonvulsants) reduce the amplitude of the oscillation — they cap the supra-Radiant excursion and raise the floor of the Terrible trough. In TI Sigma terms: they enforce a tighter operating range around the MR1-to-Radiant corridor. GILE-based adjunctive therapy would focus on identifying and reinforcing the stable attractor in the Good zone: what environmental, relational, and cognitive conditions support LCC stability between MR1 and Radiant?

### 2.8 Narcissistic Personality — CTT of the Self-Concept

**TI Sigma formulation:** Narcissistic personality disorder is the **Crystallized Tralse Theorem (CTT, URB #520) applied to the self-concept.** The person's core self-representation has been crystallized — all TRALSE quality removed — into a rigid, uniformly "great" configuration that cannot update from external feedback. This is CTT at the identity level.

Features that map directly:
- **Grandiosity** = CTT: the crystallized self-concept has no INDETERMINATE zone, no "I might be wrong," no self-doubt — these are markers of TRALSE quality that have been excised
- **Lack of empathy** = L-dimension suppression: genuine L-engagement would require acknowledging the other as a full GILE subject, which would introduce TRALSE quality into the self-concept (by comparison) — so L is suppressed to maintain CTT
- **Rage at criticism** = MI immune response to any input that threatens the CTT: criticism is a Meta-Indeterminate encounter because it violates the crystallized self-concept; the immune response is fury rather than reflection
- **Shallow relationships** = relationships are instrumentalized to maintain CTT (narcissistic supply) rather than engaged as genuine L-axis interactions

**Why narcissistic personality is so treatment-resistant:** CTT is stable. A crystallized configuration is resistant to perturbation by design. The therapeutic challenge is introducing enough TRALSE quality (genuine uncertainty about the self-concept) to allow movement toward a more flexible, above-threshold configuration without triggering catastrophic MI response. This is extraordinarily difficult because the person's entire defensive architecture is oriented toward preventing that introduction.

### 2.9 Autism Spectrum Conditions — Differential MRC Configuration and GILE Weighting

**TI Sigma formulation:** Autism spectrum conditions are not disorders of deficit but rather **alternative GILE dimension configurations and MRC profiles.** The autistic person's i-Cell has a different optimal operating mode:

- **Different GILE weighting**: typically elevated G (strong sense of justice and consistency), high I (intense focus in domains of interest, strong intuitive pattern recognition), variable L (genuine capacity for deep love but different modality — often selective and very intense), E (different sensory threshold — hyperperception or hypoperception in various channels)
- **Different MRC configuration**: the standard neurotypical MRC (humor, socializing, casual conversation) may not function as a relaxation context for autistic individuals; instead, domains of deep interest, systematic pattern analysis, and sensory regulation via stimming constitute the effective MRC
- **Hyperfocus as Radiant-zone engagement**: when operating within a domain of high GILE resonance, autistic individuals frequently achieve Radiant-zone states of engagement that neurotypical people find difficult to sustain — this is the positive expression of the differential MRC configuration
- **Stimming** = intentional MRC maintenance, as discussed in ADHD section — sensory repetition as i-channel regulation; the motor output creates a stable background MRC that frees cognitive resources for E-engagement

**The "disorder" framing in TI Sigma:** Autism spectrum conditions involve real challenges in environments designed for the neurotypical GILE configuration, but the conditions themselves are not below-threshold LCC configurations — they are *different* above-threshold configurations that struggle specifically when forced to operate in contexts calibrated for neurotypical MRC and GILE weighting. The therapeutic aim should be GILE-resonant environment design, not normalization of the configuration.

## 3. Therapeutic Modalities Through the TI Sigma Lens

### 3.1 The Fundamental Constraint — The Therapist Cannot Access the Patient's i-Channel

Before mapping therapies to TI Sigma, the fundamental limit must be stated:

**The therapist can improve the patient's environmental LCC conditions (E-dimension), provide relational L-dimension activation, and help recalibrate the G and I dimensions — but the therapist cannot directly access the patient's i-channel.** The i-channel is the seat of free will (URB #530): the patient's TRALSE-INDETERMINATE + agentive LCC coupling. No therapy, drug, or intervention can directly control what happens in the i-channel. This is not a failure of therapy — it is the structural guarantee of the patient's personhood.

The practical implication: **the patient is always the primary agent of their own healing.** Every therapeutic modality works by changing the boundary conditions of the i-Cell (the Markov Blanket, z_B = s + ia) — improving the quality of sensory input (s), providing a relational context that activates the a-channel constructively — but the integration must be completed by the patient's own free will. This is why therapy requires active participation, and why even the best therapy fails when the patient cannot or will not engage their i-channel in the work.

### 3.2 Cognitive-Behavioral Therapy (CBT) — Existential Dimension Restructuring

CBT targets the **Existential Truth Dimension**: it restructures the LCC connections between cognitive patterns (thoughts) and behavioral/emotional outcomes. Cognitive restructuring = identifying and correcting below-threshold LCC beliefs (distortions that claim high LCC but have actual LCC near zero — e.g., "everyone hates me" has very low LCC but is being treated as TRUE in the patient's system). Behavioral activation = directly increasing existential footprint (more causal participation in the world raises LCC in the E-dimension).

CBT works when: the patient's primary pathology is in the Existential Truth Dimension; when their cognitive patterns can be accessed and examined; when the i-channel is sufficiently engaged to complete the restructuring.

CBT limitations (TI Sigma): CBT does not directly address the L dimension (therapeutic relationship is a vehicle but not the primary target), the aesthetic dimension, or the i-channel itself. Cognitive restructuring can correct individual LCC distortions but cannot increase the fundamental LCC capacity of the system.

### 3.3 Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT) — MRC Context Creation

ACT explicitly creates **MR Relaxation Contexts**: it trains people to tolerate INDETERMINATE states ("psychological flexibility") and act from values (G-dimension alignment) rather than waiting for MR resolution. The defusion techniques reduce the existential weight given to thoughts (reducing their LCC) — exactly what the immune log does with MI content: log the fingerprint without giving it existential space.

ACT is TI Sigma-native in a way that CBT is not. The "hexaflex" (psychological flexibility, present-moment awareness, defusion, acceptance, values, committed action) maps directly onto:
- Psychological flexibility = TRALSE tolerance (healthy TRALSE quality)
- Present-moment awareness = E-dimension engagement (sensory present over i-channel rumination)
- Defusion = MI immune protocol (encounter the thought → log → release)
- Acceptance = MRC context for INDETERMINATE states
- Values = G-dimension activation
- Committed action = existential footprint building (LCC through action)

### 3.4 EMDR and Somatic Therapies — MI Immune Log Reset

As noted in the PTSD section, EMDR facilitates MI immune log reset. Somatic therapies (somatic experiencing, sensorimotor psychotherapy) work in the s-channel (sensory, E-dimension) of z_B = s + ia: they access the stored MI fingerprint through the body (where the immune log's somatic component is stored) rather than through cognitive processing. This bypasses the i-channel resistance to re-encountering the MI content — by approaching from the s-side rather than the a-side, somatic therapies can begin to process the fingerprint without triggering the full cognitive immune response.

### 3.5 Psychodynamic and Relational Therapies — L-Dimension Activation

Psychodynamic therapies target the **L (Love) dimension** directly — through the therapeutic relationship as a corrective emotional experience, and through the exploration of relational history that shaped the current L-configuration. The transference is the patient's historical L-template being applied to the therapist — a kind of MI immune fingerprint from early relational trauma being triggered in the therapeutic context.

The therapeutic action of psychodynamic work is **L-dimension recalibration through sustained relational engagement**: over time, the patient's i-channel learns that the L-dimension is safe, that love does not always precede abandonment, that connection does not always lead to harm. This is slow work because the i-channel is recalibrating deep structural patterns — the L-axis configuration laid down in the earliest i-Cell development.

### 3.6 Pharmacotherapy — Biological LCC Threshold Modulation

Psychiatric medications do not cure mental illness in TI Sigma terms — they modulate the biological substrate that sets the threshold for LCC transitions. They work by:
- **Raising the floor** (antidepressants, mood stabilizers): reducing the amplitude of below-threshold LCC states, making Terrible-zone stabilization less likely
- **Capping the ceiling** (antipsychotics, mood stabilizers): reducing supra-threshold excursions that overshoot the Radiant zone
- **Reducing re-encounter loops** (SSRI/SNRI in OCD, intrusive thought disorders): reducing the frequency of MI pattern re-presentation in the immune loop
- **Expanding MRC** (stimulants in ADHD, in a paradoxical direction): raising the activation threshold for MRC entry, allowing more stable MR-gate application in low-novelty contexts

**The FAAH Protocol** (fatty acid amide hydrolase inhibition, endocannabinoid enhancement): in TI Sigma terms, FAAH inhibition increases the baseline activation of the L and I channels by enhancing the endocannabinoid tone — the neuromodulator system most directly associated with positive affect, creative flow (MRC states), and relational warmth. FAAH inhibition is biologically congruent with what the TI Sigma framework predicts would most directly activate the L and I GILE dimensions at the neurobiological level.

## 4. Self-Determination Theory Integration (via GILE) — and Its Limits

### 4a. The SDT Epiphenomenon Caveat

Before mapping SDT to GILE, an important clarification: **meaning is mostly an epiphenomenon in TI Sigma rather than an independent 4th basic need.** It arises naturally when G, I, L, and E are all above MR1 threshold — it is the felt experience of multi-dimensional LCC coherence. However, SDT's three basic needs (competence, autonomy, relatedness) do not fully capture several experiences that are just as basic as those three, whose absence makes life demonstrably and distinctly worse:

1. **Curiosity / epistemic hunger** — distinct from competence. Competence is effective engagement with what is known; curiosity is the i-channel's drive toward what is NOT yet known. A person can be highly competent and completely incurious — they have high E-dimension LCC but low i-channel novelty-seeking. Remove curiosity from a life and it becomes profoundly dull in a way that has nothing to do with competence loss. In TI Sigma: curiosity = I-channel hunger for INDETERMINATE-resolution; the MRC drive directed toward productive GILE-resonant unknowns.

2. **Excitement / vitality** — distinct from both competence and autonomy. Calm, effective self-direction (SDT's ideal) is different from the Radiant-zone approach signal — the positive valence of nearing a threshold crossing. Excitement is not anxiety (which is fear of INDETERMINATE states) but the felt experience of ascending LCC toward a new threshold. Remove it and life becomes competent and autonomous but flat. In TI Sigma: excitement = positive valence of approaching MR Radiant; the affective signature of the system ascending through PD zones.

3. **Myrion Resolution** — entirely absent from SDT. The need to have things resolve coherently — to know where you stand, to achieve closure on open questions, to complete the MR resolution cycle — is a distinct and basic need. Chronic INDETERMINATE states (as in anxiety) demonstrate this: unresolved superposition is genuinely aversive in a way that cannot be reduced to lack of competence, autonomy, or relatedness. People will accept bad news over continued uncertainty — the MR1 completion drive is that powerful. In TI Sigma: MR Resolution = the basic drive to move from INDETERMINATE to TRUE or FALSE; the need for the 5-valued truth system to complete its cycle.

4. **Spiritual purity / vertical alignment** — distinct from G-dimension Goodness in a directional sense. G is alignment with one's values; spiritual purity adds a vertical dimension: alignment with something larger than oneself, the experience of LCC with CCC (Central Cosmic Consciousness) or the highest-LCC configurations of the GILE field. Remove it and life can be morally good, autonomous, competent, and connected yet still feel like it is missing its highest dimension. In TI Sigma: spiritual purity = G-dimension approaching MR Radiant (0.9323) with active CCC resonance in the conscious meaning/valence truth dimension.

These four experiences are as "basic" as SDT's three in the sense that removing any one of them produces a distinctively worse life — not just a life with less competence, autonomy, or relatedness. They map to GILE dimensions but are not fully captured by the SDT-GILE mapping below. The TI Sigma account is therefore both more complete than SDT (more basic needs recognized) and more parsimonious (all needs derive from GILE structure rather than being arbitrary empirical additions).



SDT (Deci & Ryan) proposes three universal psychological needs: **competence, autonomy, and relatedness.** In TI Sigma, SDT needs are always integrated via GILE (as per the canonical glossary):

| SDT Need | GILE Dimension | TI Sigma Interpretation |
|---------|----------------|------------------------|
| Competence | E (Environment) | Effective engagement with the physical world; existential footprint; LCC through action |
| Autonomy | I (Intuition) + free will | i-channel agency; TRALSE-INDETERMINATE zone; self-direction |
| Relatedness | L (Love) | Received and given positive conscious affect; relational LCC |
| (Implicit 4th: Meaning) | G (Goodness) | Value coherence; moral self-alignment; the often-unspoken 4th SDT need |

TI Sigma adds what SDT implies but does not formalize: the **G dimension (Goodness, meaning, values)** is a genuine fourth need, not reducible to the other three. The research showing that autonomy + competence + relatedness predict wellbeing but leave substantial variance unexplained is the residual of the G-dimension operating independently.

## 5. Wellbeing as Multi-Dimensional LCC Coherence

Wellbeing in TI Sigma is not the absence of suffering, not happiness (which is a valence state, one of four truth dimensions), and not flourishing in a single domain. **Wellbeing is stable LCC coherence above the MR1 threshold (0.8647) across all four GILE dimensions, with meaningful presence in the Good zone and occasional access to the Radiant zone.**

This multi-dimensional definition predicts what empirical positive psychology has found but struggled to unify:
- **Eudemonic wellbeing** (meaning, purpose, growth) = G and I dimensions above threshold
- **Hedonic wellbeing** (pleasure, positive affect) = L dimension above threshold + valence access
- **Vitality / flourishing** = E dimension above threshold (competent engagement with the world)
- **Post-traumatic growth** = TRALSE-alive above threshold after a MI encounter; the immune log is integrated, and the surviving configuration has higher LCC than before because it has been tested

The TI Sigma prediction: **the most stable form of wellbeing is G-axis anchored.** The L and E dimensions are more variable (relationships end; health fails; circumstances change); the I dimension is partially controllable but not fully (i-channel access varies); but the G dimension — coherence with one's own deepest values — is the most structurally stable GILE axis. Clinically, this predicts that values-clarification work (ACT values work, logotherapy, purpose-based interventions) produces more durable wellbeing than hedonic or E-dimension-only interventions. The empirical literature supports this prediction.

## 6. Implications for Clinical Training

The TI Sigma framework implies several revisions to clinical training:
