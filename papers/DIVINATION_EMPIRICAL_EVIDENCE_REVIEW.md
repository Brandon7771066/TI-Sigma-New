### 3.1 Ganzfeld ESP — The Crown Jewel

**Protocol:** The Ganzfeld protocol places a receiver in mild sensory isolation (halved ping-pong balls over eyes, white noise in ears) and has them describe mental imagery. **Chance baseline:** 25% (1 in 4)

**Meta-analytic results (Honorton & Ferrari, 1989):**
- 309 experiments
- 50,000+ participants
- Overall hit rate: **34%** (vs. 25% chance)
- z = 6.02
- **p = 1.1 × 10⁻⁹**
- Odds against chance: approximately 10 million to 1

**Results (Bem, Palmer & Broughton, 2001 update):**
- 88 Ganzfeld studies reviewed
- Hit rate: **32-34%** consistently
- Effect size consistent across labs

**TI Sigma interpretation:** The 36% above-baseline improvement corresponds to a consistent but modest LCC elevation. Most participants are not in peak Q-states during laboratory conditions (unfamiliar environment, artificial protocol). The effect should be substantially larger in natural conditions with high-GILE practitioners — which the stock market PSI data (Section 3.3) confirms.

**Moderator findings (critical):**
- Creative individuals (artists, musicians): **41-50% hit rate** (vs. 25% baseline) — a 64-100% above-chance improvement
- Immediate feedback condition: consistently improves results
- Relationship between sender/receiver: stronger connection = higher hit rate
- All three moderators are predicted by TI Sigma's coherence model (L×E)
