## 1. Canonical Statement

**HMR-1 (Hybrid MR Truth Labels):** Some propositions are NOT correctly characterized by assignment to a single base-4 MR truth label (T / F / I / DT) plus optionally a single Meta-Truth (MT). They natively require **simultaneous assignment of 2 or more labels** as a *Hybrid-MR* (HMR) characterization. The base-4 taxonomy + MT catalogue (urb_608 / urb_639) must therefore be extended to admit Hybrid-MR labels as **first-class outputs** of the MR resolution process, not as artifacts of underspecification or as failures-to-resolve.

**Cardinality:** HMR-k denotes a k-fold hybrid (k ≥ 2). The cardinality is **bounded above** only by the number of distinct MR-label categories applicable to the proposition in question. With base-4 (T, F, I, DT) plus 12 + 24 = 36 MTs from urb_608 + urb_639, the theoretical maximum cardinality for a single proposition is 40. Practically observed cardinalities range 2 ≤ k ≤ 5 in the examples constructed below.

## 2. The Brandon Seed Example (2-Hybrid)

> *"X is better and (neither better nor worse than Y)"*

**Decomposition:**
- **T_partial-order:** X IS better-than-Y on dimension D1 (e.g., subjective preference, specific feature comparison) → label T at the dimension-restricted level
- **I_global-comparator:** X is neither-better-nor-worse-than-Y at the GLOBAL level because no total ordering exists → label I (MR2 Indeterminate) at the global level

**Why MT-B1 Moot is insufficient:** A Moot characterization ("the question of which is better is irrelevant") would **lose information** — it would erase BOTH the legitimate dimension-D1 betterness AND the legitimate global indeterminacy. The proposition's truth content REQUIRES preservation of BOTH labels simultaneously. The Moot meta-truth, if applied, is a *third* label, not a replacement for either: **HMR-3 with {T, I, MT-B1}.**

**HMR characterization: {T_partial-order, I_global-comparator}** at minimum; **{T, I, MT-B1} when Moot is included.**

> *"Free will exists, doesn't exist, the proposition is paradoxical, and is empirically moot."*

**HMR-4:** {T_libertarian, F_determinist, DT_compatibilist, MT-B1_practical} — four labels, each anchored at a different framing-axis. The proposition CANNOT be reduced to fewer labels without information loss. This is the canonical HMR-4 example.

## 4. The Buffalo / Police Analog — Unbounded Recursive HMR

Brandon's analogy: in English, the sentences

> *"Buffalo buffalo Buffalo buffalo buffalo buffalo Buffalo buffalo"*

and

> *"Police police police police police police police police"*

are grammatically well-formed at arbitrary length because each instance of the lexical item is multi-functional (noun / verb / adjective / proper-noun), and successive applications can be chained. The sentences become parseable through layered grammatical role-assignment.

**HMR analog (Recursive-Hybrid-MR, R-HMR):** A meta-proposition about an HMR proposition is itself a hybrid, often with a HIGHER cardinality than the object-level proposition. Iterating produces unbounded HMR-cardinality.

**Construction (R-HMR-1 candidate):**
- **Level 0 (object):** "X is better and (neither better nor worse than Y)" → HMR-2 {T, I}
- **Level 1 (meta-1):** "The statement 'X is better and (neither better nor worse than Y)' is HMR-2" → adds T (the meta-claim is true; it correctly classifies the object-level proposition) AND adds I (whether HMR-2 is the *complete* characterization is itself MR2-Indeterminate without enumerating all dimensions) → HMR-4 {T, I, T_meta, I_meta}; the two T's and two I's are at different framing-levels per HMR composition rule §5
- **Level 2 (meta-2):** "The statement that 'the statement [Level 0] is HMR-2' is HMR-4" → adds T_meta-meta + I_meta-meta + DT_self-reference-emerging → HMR-7
- **Level k (recursive):** add 2 labels per ascent (one T_meta-k + one I_meta-k); for sufficiently high k, DT_self-reference becomes unavoidable; further ascent adds MT-L1 (MR Saturation) and MT-L2 (Recursive Self-Reference) from urb_639

**Theorem (R-HMR cardinality, INFORMAL):** For meta-ascent k ≥ K\*, HMR-cardinality grows at least linearly in k; for k ≥ K\*\* > K\*, HMR-cardinality is unbounded (saturates the full base-4 + 36-MT taxonomy = 40 labels).

**This is the corpus's first formal characterization of an unbounded HMR construction.** The R-HMR construction is structurally analogous to the buffalo/police recursion — each meta-ascent adds labels the same way each lexical-recursion adds grammatical roles.

## 5. Composition Rules

**R1 (Cross-level preservation):** When labels at different framing-levels both apply, BOTH are preserved in the HMR characterization. Example: HMR-3.1 has T at panpsychist-level AND T at emergentist-level — these are NOT merged into a single T per MT-F2 (Both True at Different Levels), because MT-F2 resolves to a single T-at-the-higher-frame; HMR preserves both T's distinguished by framing.

**R2 (DT preservation under hybridization):** If a proposition is DT under Pass-65 canonical, the DT label is PRESERVED in any HMR that includes that proposition. DT is not "diluted" by hybridization. HMR-3.2 and HMR-4.1 and HMR-5.1 all preserve DT alongside other labels.

**R3 (Moot as meta-label, not base):** MT-B1 Moot remains a META-truth in HMR; it does not become a 5th base label. When Moot is part of an HMR, the cardinality count INCLUDES Moot (HMR-3 with {T, I, MT-B1} not HMR-2 with Moot-on-top). This preserves MR Truth Labels canonical structure.

**R4 (HMR composes with MR-IDC-1):** MR-IDC-1 proper containment {DT} ⊊ {Incoherent} is preserved in HMR. A hybrid containing DT is still "DT-containing"; a hybrid containing only I (no DT) is incoherent-but-not-DT.

**R5 (HMR composes with MT-F2 vs MT-E2 distinction):** MT-F2 resolves apparent-contradictions to single-T-at-higher-frame; MT-E2 accepts genuine DT-Paradox-Stable; HMR does NEITHER — it preserves the multi-label structure as the final output. HMR is the **third option** alongside MT-F2 (resolve) and MT-E2 (accept-DT). This is a substantive expansion of urb_608's resolution-mode taxonomy.
