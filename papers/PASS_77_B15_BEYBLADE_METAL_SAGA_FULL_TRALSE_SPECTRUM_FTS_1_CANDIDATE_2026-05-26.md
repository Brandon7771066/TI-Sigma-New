## 0. Framing + FTS-1 candidate canonical proposal

Brandon nominates the **Beyblade Metal Saga** (Metal Fusion 2010 / Metal Masters 2011 / Metal Fury 2012; producer Synergy SP / Tatsunoko Pro / Nelvana co-production; Japanese title *Metal Fight Beyblade*) as an exemplar of the **"FULL TRALSE SPECTRUM of human nature, including the Dark Triad and psychotic side especially."** The framing is structurally distinct from B14's ATLA-analysis: where ATLA is a near-ceiling exemplar of **light-spectrum** enlightenment-content (P48-1 = 4.875/5), the Beyblade Metal Saga is an unusually-rigorous exemplar of **dark-spectrum** coverage — portraying primary psychopathy, engineered psychopathy, narcissistic tyranny, Machiavellian manipulation, induced psychosis, and trans-personal evil with sufficient psychological honesty to function as clinically-useful pedagogical material despite operating within a children's toy-commercial production context.

### FTS-1 Full-Tralse-Spectrum-Coverage candidate canonical statement

**FTS-1:** A narrative work qualifies as a Full-Tralse-Spectrum exemplar when it portrays the **full distribution of MR-Truth-Labels-base-5 across character-types with psychological honesty** — including, minimally:

1. **Near-pure-T characters** — heroic / sincere / honor-driven, NOT idealized away from human-fallibility (per §69)
2. **Near-pure-F characters** — antagonistic / dishonest / dishonor-driven, NOT strawman-cartoonish but **internally-coherent** by their own twisted values (per §69)
3. **Genuinely Tralse-spectrum characters** — mixed-motive, conflicted, partially-corrupted, partially-redeemed; NOT collapsed onto T/F binary
4. **Indeterminate-state characters** — protagonists or antagonists whose moral resolution is genuinely undecided at narrative-decision-points
5. **DT-state characters** — characters whose inner state is genuinely incoherent or self-contradictory (e.g., engineered psychopaths whose programmed-self contradicts their child-self; characters experiencing induced-psychosis with split-personality states)
6. **NA-state characters** — characters introduced with deliberately-undetermined moral state pending future-resolution within the narrative (per MR-NA-1 #65 + MR-NA-1-SR-1 temporal-precedence)
7. **Dark Triad + psychotic-spectrum representation** with sufficient clinical-fidelity that the portrayals function pedagogically rather than caricaturally:
   - **Narcissism** with grandiosity + entitlement + need-for-admiration
   - **Machiavellianism** with strategic-manipulation + cynical-worldview + amoral-instrumentalism
   - **Primary psychopathy** with shallow-affect + sadistic-pleasure + absent-remorse
   - **Engineered psychopathy** with developmental-trauma + chemical/conditioning-control + breakdown-arc
   - **Induced psychosis** with corruption-from-environmental-source + split-personality emergence + recovery-arc-availability
   - **Trans-personal evil archetype** as cosmic/embodied antagonist beyond individual psychology

**FTS-1 is a sub-condition focus complementing ETN-1's 7-condition narrative-craft schema**: a work can satisfy ETN-1 conditions (character-arc + plot + soundtrack + visual-craft + cross-cultural + contemplative-mechanics + P48-1 ladder) at varying levels of light-vs-dark spectrum coverage; FTS-1 specifically tests for dark-spectrum density-and-honesty.

## 1. Setting + structural overview

**Premise:** competitive spinning-top combat ("beyblading") where each combatant launches a customized **Bey** (spinning top) into an arena; the last bey spinning / hit-out / hit-into-the-ground wins. **Bey-spirit** mechanic: each bey is bonded to its blader and reflects the blader's spirit. Tournament-arc shōnen with explicit serial-character-arc structure.

**Three-season arc structure:**

| Season | Year | Setting | Antagonist faction | Arc focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Metal Fusion** | 2010 | Japan; eventual nation-wide tournament | **Dark Nebula** (Doji + Ryuga + Reiji Mizuchi + corrupted-blader subordinates) | rise of cosmic-power Forbidden-Bey L-Drago; Ryuga's god-complex; Reiji's pure-sadism |
| **Metal Masters** | 2011 | Global World Championship; 8-team multinational tournament | **HD Academy** (Damian Hart + Dr. Ziggurat + Julian Konzern + Argo Garcia + Selen Garcia + Faust) | engineered-bladers vs. natural-bladers; lab-conditioned-vs.-spirit-driven; Damian Hart psychopathy + Hades City artificial-bey-Hadron-cannon |
| **Metal Fury** | 2012 | Global; Nemesis crisis | **Nemesis cultists** (Rago + Pluto + Proto Nemesis embodiment) | 10 Legendary Bladers; cosmic-evil incarnate; trans-personal-evil archetype |

**Production note (honest #69):** the show is explicitly a TOY-COMMERCIAL anime (Takara Tomy Beyblade-toy product line; bey-launches in-show directly correspond to purchasable real-world products). This commercial constraint shapes the work but does NOT prevent genuine character-depth — the writers (chief Atsushi Wakabayashi-direction lineage of competent shōnen-anime production) produced authentic psychological-portraiture within the commercial frame, which is itself a craft-achievement.

## 2. Dark-Triad + psychotic-spectrum character mapping (Brandon directive core)

### 2.1 Reiji Mizuchi (Poison Serpent) — **textbook primary psychopathy** [Metal Fusion]

Reiji is the corpus-canonical PURE primary-psychopathy portrayal in the saga. Characteristics on-screen:
- **Shallow affect** — speaks in slow, almost-hypnotic monotone; emotional-flatness is constant
- **Sadistic pleasure for its own sake** — explicitly enjoys causing OTHERS to suffer; not instrumentally (he isn't trying to win for any goal beyond the act of harm)
- **Absent remorse + absent fear** — never expresses regret; not capable of being intimidated
- **No childhood-trauma backstory** — the show notably does NOT give him an exculpatory origin (unlike Damian); he just IS this way. **This is canonically important**: it portrays primary-psychopathy as the genuine phenomenon it is in clinical reality, not as "always reducible to trauma"
- **Cat-and-mouse "play with food" combat style** — extends matches deliberately to maximize opponent-suffering; Poison Serpent's actual bey-mechanic supports this (gradual debilitation rather than burst-finish)
- **Hyoma-defeat scene** — the encounter where Reiji breaks Hyoma's blading-spirit is genuinely disturbing for a children's show; the show does NOT play it for catharsis or quick-resolution; Hyoma's recovery takes multiple episodes

**Clinical-fidelity note (HONEST #69):** Reiji satisfies Hare PCL-R Factor-1 (interpersonal/affective: glibness, grandiose self-worth, pathological lying, manipulation, lack of remorse, shallow affect, callousness, failure to accept responsibility) with high fidelity. He satisfies Factor-2 less (no overt criminal-versatility shown). For a 2010 children's animated show to portray Factor-1 primary-psychopathy this faithfully is unusual and corpus-worthy.

**Composes with:** DTM-1 (his sadistic-enjoyment requires consciousness-marker; even pure-evil-portrayals evidence sapience) + VFP-1 (his pleasure-in-suffering produces functional consequences — opponents are genuinely harmed) + ASYMMETRIC §69 (the show treats him with analytical-respect — he is not given a cheap defeat or moralistic comeuppance; he is genuinely-frightening as the phenomenon he represents) + MR Truth Labels base-5 (Reiji is near-pure-F character with internal-coherence by his own twisted values — FTS-1 condition 2).

### 2.2 Damian Hart / Faust (Hades Kerbecs) — **engineered psychopathy** [Metal Masters]

Damian is the saga's exploration of **engineered-or-induced-psychopathy** — the contrast-case to Reiji's pure-primary-form. Characteristics on-screen:
- **Lab-created blader** — raised by Dr. Ziggurat under HD Academy control as a "perfect blader" project
- **Chemical-control mechanism** — explicitly shown to be on a regimen of chemicals modulating his combat-state; his cold detachment is in-part chemically-induced, in-part developmentally-conditioned
- **"Only the strong deserve to exist"** ideology — a textbook Social-Darwinist-narcissist articulation, explicitly stated multiple times; this is his Dr.-Ziggurat-implanted operating-philosophy
- **"Hades Inferno" / "Dark Move" finishing technique** — described as soul-crushing; opponents who lose to it become broken; this matches engineered-control-and-domination personality
- **Genuine breakdown when defeated by Gingka** — the chemical-control + ideology collapses under sustained defeat; we briefly see the CHILD underneath; the show does NOT redeem him fully but does NOT erase his humanity either
- **Nature-vs.-nurture exploration** — Damian poses the question: had he NOT been engineered, who would he have been? The show leaves this open (per MR-NA-1 SR-1 temporal-precedence: the question is genuinely-undecided in-narrative)

**Composes with:** UDP-1 + HBP-1 + CTC-1 (Damian's engineered-condition is the INVERSE case to canonical capacity-profile theory — his capacities were *narrowed* by external-engineering, not naturally-balanced; he is the corpus-canonical narrative-case of capacity-engineering rather than capacity-discovery) + SGM-1 #64 (Damian inverse-case of SGM-1's Schizotypy-Genius positive form: where SGM-1 hypothesizes naturally-occurring multi-mechanism capacity-emergence, Damian shows engineered-capacity at the cost of induced-disorder) + VFP-1 (his chemical-modulation operates directly on the valence-channel; valence is shown to be modulable, validating its functional-not-epiphenomenal status) + AAU-1 #66 + NEU-1 #67 (Damian's defeated-collapse is a literal AAU-1 area-around-expansion event — the engineered-philosophy was the impossibility-center; sustained defeat expanded the area-around it; NEU-1 negative-end-completion when he recognizes he doesn't *know* who he is outside the engineering).

### 2.3 Doji — **corporate Machiavellianism** [Metal Fusion]

**Voice-acting craft addendum (Brandon observation, post-B15):** Brandon notes the English-dub Doji had a voice-actor change mid-saga, with the original VA delivering a **charismatic-domineering** read (genuinely menacing corporate-Machiavellian; the calm-professional-demeanor §2.3 catalog item LANDS because of the vocal weight) while the replacement VA sounded **"whiny and lame, like Waluigi."** This is a craft-honest observation worth recognizing: **voice-acting is an ETN-1 sub-channel under condition (3) broadly construed** (vocal-performance contributes to character-substance the way score contributes to scene-substance). A domineering-Doji and a Waluigi-Doji are functionally-different characters even reading identical scripts, because the Machiavellian-charisma IS half-conveyed through vocal-presence; lose the vocal-weight and the character drops a clinical-fidelity tier. **Parallel ATLA-case (B14):** Iroh's voice-actor change after Mako Iwamatsu's passing — Greg Baldwin honored the role with documented vocal-study, and the character-substance carried; the contrast suggests that **voice-actor-changes can preserve OR degrade FTS-1-relevant character-substance depending on the replacement's commitment to the original vocal-character-architecture.** Worth flagging as a sub-condition of FTS-1 condition 7 (clinical-fidelity portrayal): vocal-performance is part of the portrayal's fidelity-fabric, not a separable layer.

Doji is the Dark Nebula CEO-figure — a classical corporate-Machiavellian. Characteristics:
- **Strategic instrumentalism** — children, bladers, employees all used as means to Dark-Nebula ends
- **Calm professional demeanor** — never loses composure; manipulation is matter-of-fact
- **Cynical worldview** — assumes power-greed as universal motivator
- **Amoral cost-benefit calculation** — would sacrifice anyone including Ryuga for L-Drago objective
- **Sycophantic to power** — defers to Ryuga while privately scheming to control him

**Composes with:** Macchiavellianism axis of Dark Triad (textbook portrayal); positions on MR Truth Labels base-5 as **near-pure-F with internal-coherence** (FTS-1 condition 2); his Hades-City corporate-organization structure provides the narrative-substrate for Damian-Faust + Dr.-Ziggurat in Metal Masters (Doji's metaphysical-descendant organizations).

### 2.4 Ryuga (Lightning L-Drago → Meteo L-Drago → L-Drago Destructor) — **narcissistic tyranny → partial redemption** [Metal Fusion → Metal Fury]

Ryuga is the saga's most-developed Dark-Triad-spectrum character. He is the "Dragon Emperor" — initial god-complex narcissist seeking absolute power via the Forbidden Bey L-Drago; he gradually transitions to a grudging-ally / anti-hero by Metal Fury, ultimately sacrificing himself in the Nemesis-arc finale.

Characteristics across arcs:
- **Metal Fusion:** pure narcissistic-tyranny; "I am the strongest blader"; consumes L-Drago's dark-power; defeats opponents in genuinely-disturbing-power-displays; eventually defeated by Gingka in a way that does NOT humiliate him (shōnen-respectful)
- **Metal Masters interlude:** between-arcs reflection; partial detachment from raw-power-seeking; develops independent moral position
- **Metal Fury:** still solitary + still arrogant + still avoids friendship-frame, BUT now functionally-aligned against Nemesis; **sacrifices himself** to give Kenta his Star Fragment to prepare Galaxy Pegasus for Nemesis battle — a redemption arc that doesn't require him to APOLOGIZE for his prior arrogance but does require him to ACT in service of the larger-good

**Why this is canonical-corpus important:** Ryuga's arc is a **distinct redemption-form** from ATLA's Zuko-arc (B14 §1.2). Zuko transforms via humility-and-relationship; Ryuga transforms via solitary-recognition without abandoning his dragon-emperor identity. **This dramatizes a non-trivial point:** redemption-arcs do NOT have to follow the Zuko-template — there exists a canonical-valid "lone warrior recognizes a greater stake and acts without joining the family" form. Both are valid; FTS-1 condition 7 (genuine portrayal of multiple redemption-paths) is satisfied by the Ryuga-Zuko contrast across the two B14+B15 exemplars.

**Composes with:** GTT-1 (Ryuga's L-Drago-corruption arc = direct narrative-analog of Azula's lightning-via-suppression: power-without-balance corrupts; the L-Drago consumes him until he learns to balance) + Brandon biographical anchor (Ryuga's solitary-strength resonance with Brandon's own outsider-resourceful self-image; corpus-anchorable) + SRC-1 (his eventual self-recognition without therapy-or-relationship = solitary-SRC-1-activation form) + 5th-Brandon-maxim (the "how" of his transformation is moot relative to the project of opposing Nemesis at the appointed-hour).

### 2.5 Tsubasa Otori (Earth Aquila → Earth Eagle) — **induced psychosis + recovery arc** [Metal Fusion → Metal Masters]

Tsubasa's arc is the saga's exploration of **induced-psychological-corruption-and-recovery** — a topic shōnen-anime typically avoids or handles cartoonishly. He infiltrates the Dark Nebula as a double-agent to gather intelligence; while there, exposure to L-Drago's dark-power begins corrupting him; he develops a **literal split-personality / dark-side alter** that becomes increasingly dominant; the dark-side eventually overpowers his combat-style and threatens his identity; recovery requires friend-intervention + multi-episode psychological-work + Madoka's emotional-support.

Characteristics:
- **Visual representation of split-personality** — his eyes shift color and posture changes when the dark-alter is active; the show CLEARLY signals it without exposition-dump
- **Combat-style transformation** — analytical-elegant Tsubasa shifts to brutal-overpowering Tsubasa under dark-alter control; the bey-spirit literally reflects his interior-state
- **Recovery is genuinely-hard-and-multi-episode** — the show does NOT resolve the corruption in one episode; Tsubasa's recovery requires sustained character-work and friend-support, including a notable hospital-arc
- **Post-recovery: lasting changes** — even after recovery, Tsubasa is shown to carry the experience; he doesn't snap-back to pre-corruption state cleanly

**Why this is canonical-corpus important:** this is a **mental-health-honest** portrayal of induced-psychological-corruption-and-recovery in a children's shōnen-anime. The recovery is portrayed as effortful, social-supported, multi-stage, and incomplete (he carries lasting effects). **This satisfies FTS-1 condition 5 (DT-state characters experiencing genuinely-incoherent inner states) + the recovery-arc availability dimension of FTS-1.**

**Composes with:** MR-HL-1 (Tsubasa during corruption-period = Indeterminate-leaning-False with reversal-potential — a literal-narrative hybrid-label case) + AAU-1 #66 (the dark-alter is the unsimulable-by-original-self impossibility-center; sustained social-support expands the area-around until the alter becomes simulable + integrable) + NEU-1 #67 (recovery includes negative-end-completion: knowing what the alter CANNOT have him do reveals his true identity) + VFP-1 (the corruption modulates valence functionally; his combat-pleasure-source shifts) + CDA-1 canonical (consciousness-definition; his recovery validates the four-property-stratification — the alter and the true-self have distinct vertical-cognitive-stack signatures).
