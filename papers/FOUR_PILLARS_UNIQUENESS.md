Therefore: Abstract-dimension necessary. ✓

- No implementation capacity

- Disconnected from reality

Therefore: Concrete-dimension necessary. ✓

These span the complete space of intelligence requirements.

Any additional dimension would be reducible to combinations of these four.

**Conclusion:** Exactly four dimensions necessary and sufficient. ∎
