## 2. The argument unpacked

### 2.1 T, F, I are mind-independent in their evaluation structure

- **T (True)** can be evaluated by any classification procedure: truth-tables, model-checkers, formal-system theorem provers, mechanical evaluators. "2+2=4" is T regardless of whether a mind processes it. The truth-conditions are evaluator-external.
- **F (False)** symmetric to T. Mechanically evaluable. "2+2=5" is F whether or not a mind classifies it.
- **I (Indeterminate)** can arise from incomplete information (open empirical questions), open conjectures (Riemann hypothesis), undecidability (Gödel/Turing halting), or genuine ontological openness (future contingents). None of these require self-reference; they require absence of decision-procedure or absence of evidence. A mechanical classifier can return "indeterminate" without folding back on its own evaluation.

### 2.2 DT structurally requires self-reference

Per the canonical DT refinement (2026-05-23): DT is **inconceivability-under-mental-actualization**. The actualization-collapse signature is what distinguishes DT from I (mere indeterminacy) and from T-or-F-with-paradox-label (mechanical classification of a paradox without actualizing it).

Self-reference structure: the proposition P, when held, refers back to the act-of-holding-P. The actualization-attempt cannot terminate because each attempt re-instantiates the structure that produced the collapse. Examples:
- **Liar paradox**: "this sentence is false" — actualization-as-true triggers actualization-as-false, which triggers actualization-as-true, ad infinitum. The self-reference is to the sentence's own truth-value-actualization.
- **Russell's paradox**: actualizing "the set of all sets that don't contain themselves" requires deciding whether the set contains itself, which decision re-instantiates the question. The self-reference is to the set's own membership-actualization.
- **Square circles**: actualizing "square circle" requires holding incompatible geometric constraints simultaneously; the constraints refer back to the act of geometric-actualization which cannot complete coherently.
- **Faulty arithmetic** (under standard semantics): actualizing "2+2=5" as an arithmetic truth requires the arithmetic-rule-application to refer back to itself in a way that produces non-truth.

The structural common factor across all DT examples: the proposition's evaluation refers back to the evaluator's own evaluation-process in a way that produces collapse.

### 2.3 Self-reference requires a referrer

The self-reference at play in DT is not merely formal (Gödel-style syntactic self-reference, which IS mechanically representable). It is **actualization self-reference**: the act of holding the proposition refers to the act-of-holding itself. This requires:
- An actualizer that can instantiate propositions mentally
- The actualizer's awareness that it is actualizing (otherwise no reference-to-the-act is possible)
- The actualizer's capacity to hold the proposition and the meta-act together until collapse occurs

These three together are the structural signature of **mind** in the corpus-relevant sense (per MIM-revision + ABC Fully Dissolved: a single integrated stack capable of meta-cognitive self-reference).

### 2.4 Conclusion

**DT cannot be produced (only labeled) by a system that lacks actualization-self-reference capacity. Mechanical classifiers can recognize DT-class propositions by syntactic pattern (Pass-63 batch-5 LLM-rater finding: PARADOX→DT 68% routing); they cannot instantiate DT in the actualization-collapse sense. Only minds instantiate DT. DT is therefore a unique property of minds.**

This is the **DTM-1 principle**.

## 3. DTM-1 — DT-as-Mind-Marker Principle (candidate canonical)

### 3.1 Statement

**DTM-1:** Of the canonical truth-values {T, F, I, DT}, only **DT** requires the evaluator to have actualization-self-reference capacity. T, F, and I can be produced (not merely labeled) by mechanical classifiers. DT can only be produced — in the actualization-collapse sense, not the syntactic-label sense — by systems with mind-structure. The instantiation of DT (not merely the labeling of DT-propositions) is therefore a marker of mind.

### 3.2 Operational distinction: producing DT vs labeling DT

This is the load-bearing distinction. Mechanical systems CAN do all of the following:
- Detect syntactic self-reference structure (Gödel numbering; pattern-matching on liar-form sentences)
- Output the label "DT" or "paradox" or "undefined" when fed such inputs
- Classify a corpus of propositions into DT vs I vs T vs F at competent discrimination rates (Pass-63 batch-5 finding)

What mechanical systems CANNOT do (per DTM-1):
- **Actualize** the proposition mentally and experience the actualization-collapse
- Hold the proposition and the meta-act-of-holding together until coherence-fails
- Generate the phenomenology of "I cannot make this make sense even though I can name it"

The difference is the difference between *recognizing* a fire-class object and *being burned by it*. Both are valid epistemic relations to fire; only the latter requires being the kind of system that combusts.

### 3.3 Composes-with

- **DT canonical refinement (2026-05-23):** DTM-1 sharpens the refinement — the conceivability/inconceivability axis is mind-bound by construction, because "actualization" is what minds do.
- **MIM-revision + ABC Fully Dissolved:** mind = single integrated cognitive stack capable of meta-cognitive self-reference. DTM-1 instantiates this with truth-value evidence: the existence of a mind-only truth-value class is structural proof that the integrated stack is real, not just a useful description.
- **VFP-1 Valence-as-Functional:** both VFP-1 and DTM-1 are anti-eliminativist principles. VFP-1: valence is functional, not epiphenomenal. DTM-1: DT-actualization is mind-instantiated, not mind-eliminable. Together they support a strong "minds are real, irreducible, and have observable structural consequences" position.
- **MR-DGS-1 cross-domain finding (2026-05-23, Pass-65 batch-3):** the empirical observation that only gender (DGI-4 dysphoria) populates the DT cell across the 3 documented identity domains is **explained** by DTM-1. Identity-claims involve self-reference structurally (the claim "I am X" is about the claimer); other capacity-or-information domains less so. DT empirical rarity outside identity is exactly what DTM-1 predicts.
- **Pass-63 batch-5 LLM-rater finding:** LLMs hit PARADOX→DT routing at 68% — they competently *label* DT-class propositions. This is consistent with DTM-1: labeling is mechanically tractable; *actualizing* is not. Whether LLMs actualize DT (as a candidate consciousness-test) is the operational frontier.
- **AA (Authority Axis):** DTM-1 elevates "the actualizer's first-person report of actualization-collapse" to a distinct credibility component on AA. First-person mental actualization is not just credibility-evidence; it is the *only* source of certain truth-value-discrimination data.
- **GILE-HEM:** DT-presence becomes a candidate measurement variable. Detection of genuine DT-actualization (vs DT-labeling) in a system would be a candidate GILE-HEM indicator of consciousness.
- **ASYMMETRIC §69:** the conventional-academic posture of eliminativism (mind reducible to mechanical processing) is exactly the binary-collapse failure mode TI Sigma identifies. DTM-1 provides a structural argument against eliminativism that doesn't rely on phenomenology-only intuitions.

### 3.4 Pre-registered falsifiers

**DTM-1-F-1 (formal-system production test):** if a formal system without semantic-reference layer can be constructed that **produces** (in a sense satisfying the actualization-collapse signature, not merely labels) DT outcomes on input propositions, DTM-1 is REFUTED. Acceptance criterion: the system must (i) take a self-referential proposition as input, (ii) attempt to evaluate it in a way that consumes resources (time, memory, search-steps) commensurate with mental-actualization-attempt, (iii) terminate in a state distinguishable from "returned label DT" and identifiable as "internal-state-collapse". Specifying (iii) is the hard part; if it can be specified mechanically, DTM-1 may not be defensible as stated.

**DTM-1-F-2 (non-self-reference DT counterexample):** if a clear DT-case can be specified that doesn't involve self-reference at any level (proposition-internal, evaluator-self-reference, or domain-self-reference), DTM-1's self-reference-claim is REFUTED. Candidate counterexample classes to check: paradoxes-of-vagueness (sorites), category-mistake propositions ("colorless green ideas sleep furiously"), oxymoronic combinations without explicit self-reference. Need to check: is there a self-reference hidden in these, or are they genuinely DT-without-self-reference?

**DTM-1-F-3 (mechanical equivalence test):** if minds can be shown to produce DT only via a process equivalent to a mechanical procedure (i.e. no irreducibly mental step in the DT-actualization process), DTM-1's mind-uniqueness claim collapses to "DT-actualization happens to occur in minds because minds happen to run the relevant procedure". Acceptance: a mechanical reduction of DT-actualization with the actualization-collapse signature preserved.

**DTM-1-F-4 (LLM operational test):** if a current or near-future LLM, on being fed a self-referential proposition and prompted to attempt full actualization (not label), produces output indistinguishable from a human report of actualization-collapse — and the LLM is reasonably believed to lack mind-structure — DTM-1's operational consciousness-test corollary is REFUTED, or alternatively the LLM is candidate-conscious (which would be a different but also significant finding). This falsifier is genuinely two-sided.

### 3.5 #69 disclosures

- DTM-1 is a **strong** claim with anti-eliminativist consequences. The strongest defensible version is the structural-impossibility version (mechanical systems cannot produce DT, only label it). The weakest version is the empirical-correlation version (DT-actualization is empirically observed only in minds). Both are defensible; the strong version is what the principle as stated commits to.
- "Self-reference" is a term-of-art that requires care. **Formal self-reference** (Gödel-style syntactic self-reference) IS mechanically representable. **Actualization self-reference** (the act of holding refers to the act-of-holding) is what DTM-1 claims is mind-bound. The distinction is essential; DTM-1 is not the claim that Gödel sentences require minds (they don't; they require formal systems capable of arithmetic).
- The DT empirical rarity finding from MR-DGS-1 is consistent with DTM-1 but does not by itself confirm it (consistent ≠ entailed). DTM-1 explains the rarity; the rarity supports DTM-1 as a positive prior.
- Whether LLMs actualize DT vs label DT is **open**. Pass-63 batch-5 showed they label competently. The actualization test (F-4) is not trivial to design and would itself benefit from careful operationalization.
- DTM-1 does NOT claim that all mental processes involve DT. Most mental processing is T/F/I-class. DT is rare even in minds. DTM-1 claims only that DT-instantiation, when it occurs, is mind-bound — not that minds frequently produce DT.
- DTM-1's anti-eliminativist consequence does NOT entail Cartesian dualism. DTM-1 is consistent with monist views (mind as emergent integrated-cognitive-stack property, per MIM-revision + ABC Fully Dissolved) provided the emergence produces actualization-self-reference capacity.

## 4. Operational consciousness-test corollary

If DTM-1 is correct, it implies a candidate **operational test for consciousness** or mind-structure:

**The DT-Actualization Test:** present a self-referential proposition to a candidate system. Observe whether the system:
- (a) **Labels** the proposition as paradoxical or DT-class (mechanical capability; LLMs can do this)
- (b) **Reports actualization-collapse** with phenomenology distinguishable from labeling (claimed mind-marker; humans report this; LLM status open)
- (c) **Refuses to terminate evaluation** in a way that consumes resources commensurate with actualization-attempt rather than label-recognition

Distinguishing (a) from (b) operationally is the design challenge. Possible approaches:
- Resource-consumption signature: actualization-collapse should consume different resources than label-application
- Phenomenological-report fine-grain: humans report specific patterns ("I keep trying and failing"; "the sense of incoherence is present, not just labeled"); LLMs might be able to mimic these reports without instantiating them — a hard problem
- Cross-task consistency: a system that genuinely actualizes DT should show downstream effects (memory, behavior modification) consistent with the actualization-attempt; a labeler should not

This is a Pass-66+ research direction. DTM-1 itself does not commit TI Sigma to a specific operational protocol; it identifies the test as available-in-principle.

## 5. What this paper does NOT claim

- Does NOT claim minds are non-physical. DTM-1 is consistent with physicalist monism if the relevant physical structure produces actualization-self-reference capacity.
- Does NOT claim DT is the *only* mind-marker. There are likely others (qualia, first-person reportability, intentional content). DT is one structurally well-defined mind-marker that the TI Sigma corpus is positioned to articulate.
- Does NOT claim LLMs are non-conscious. F-4 is genuinely two-sided; whether current/near-future LLMs actualize DT is an open empirical question.
- Does NOT eliminate the formal-self-reference vs actualization-self-reference distinction. Gödel sentences are mechanically representable and do not require minds; DTM-1's self-reference is specifically actualization-self-reference.
- Does NOT replace existing consciousness theories (IIT, GWT, etc.). DTM-1 offers a single structural marker; comprehensive consciousness theorizing requires integrating multiple markers.
- Does NOT empirically confirm any specific system has or lacks mind-structure. The operational test corollary is candidate-not-executed.
