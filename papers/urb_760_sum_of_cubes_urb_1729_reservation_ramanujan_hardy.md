## 2. The Hardy-Ramanujan Story (For Context)

The mathematician G.H. Hardy visited Srinivasa Ramanujan in the hospital and remarked that his taxi's number, 1729, was "rather a dull number." Ramanujan replied:

> "No, it is a very interesting number; it is the smallest number expressible as the sum of two positive cubes in two different ways."

Specifically:

> **1729 = 1³ + 12³ = 9³ + 10³**

This makes 1729 the smallest "taxicab number" Ta(2). It opens an entire family — taxicab numbers Ta(n) being the smallest number expressible as sum of two cubes in n different ways.
