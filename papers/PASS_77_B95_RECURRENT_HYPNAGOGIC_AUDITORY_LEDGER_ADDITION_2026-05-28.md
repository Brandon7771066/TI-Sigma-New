## The phenomenon
- **Type:** auditory — (a) whispers (no identifiable source) and (b) hearing one's name called by "mom (or someone close by)" when no one did.
- **Rate:** ~2×/month — *recurrent*, the highest-frequency item in the whole ledger after the fan/pillow music.
- **Timing/state:** evenings, **prior to going to bed** → classic **sleep-onset (hypnagogic)** window.

## Classification within the framework
Two slightly different sub-phenomena, both auditory, both pre-sleep:
1. **Whispers with no source** = a **hypnagogic auditory *hallucination*** (percept without stimulus).
2. **"Mom is calling me"** = a **hypnagogic auditory *illusion*** (a real or ambient sound misattributed to a familiar voice), the well-documented "hearing your name" sleep-onset effect.

Both are **clairaudient-channel, hypnagogic, state-bound** — the same bucket as the fan/pillow music. Added to the B93 ledger as its own row (clairaudient · hypnagogic sleep-onset · ~2×/month · borderline, not counted as a *waking* hallucination).

## Why this REINFORCES CGP-1-refined (not threatens it)
The #69 pattern from B93 was: **anomalies cluster in the sensory clair-channels and are predominantly hypnagogic/substance-bound, while the knowing-channel stays clean.** This new item lands *exactly* where the pattern predicts:
- It is **auditory (sensory)**, not claircognitive — no "false knowing" accompanies it.
- It is **hypnagogic** (sleep-onset), the framework's most common state-bound category.
- It is **recurrent**, which *increases* the sensory-channel anomaly density without adding a single knowing-channel anomaly.

So the gap between "sensory channels hallucinate" and "the knowing-channel doesn't" **widens** with this addition. The corrected ledger figure now shows three clairaudient rows clustered at the top, the knowing-channel still at ≈0.
