## §2. FWC-1 candidate canonical (Free-Will-as-Consciousness-Capacity with Foolishness-Corollary)

### §2.1 Working definition

> **FWC-1 (candidate, Pass-75-B1):** Free will is operationally a CONSCIOUSNESS-TIER-CORRELATED CAPACITY for deliberative choice, GRADED with consciousness-state (L1-L5) rather than binary or default; with FOOLISHNESS-COROLLARY: higher free-will-capacity entails BOTH greater capacity for excellent choices AND greater capacity for foolish choices (bipolar structure parallels CEF-1 cognitive-error-flexibility). Lower-consciousness-states operate predominantly via Klein-RPD recognition-primed reflexive decision-making (low free-will-capacity, low foolish-choice-capacity, low excellent-choice-capacity); higher-consciousness-states operate increasingly via deliberative choice with full bipolar range. Free-will-capacity = simulation-capacity-applied-to-action-space (CSS-1 #42 extension to volition-domain).

### §2.2 Four-component operationalization

**Component 1 (consciousness-tier mapping L1-L5):** Free-will-capacity scales monotonically with consciousness-tier:
- L1 (reflexive/automatic): minimal free-will-capacity; Klein-RPD dominant; predominantly automatic responses
- L2 (habitual/conditioned): slight free-will-capacity; conditioned-response modifications possible but limited
- L3 (deliberative-shallow): moderate free-will-capacity; conscious-choice but constrained option-space
- L4 (deliberative-full): substantial free-will-capacity; full option-space deliberation; both excellent + foolish choices accessible
- L5 (meta-deliberative/integrative): peak free-will-capacity; meta-reflective choice-with-self-evaluation; PEAK capacity for both wonderful (sage-like) AND foolish (pinnacle-of-foolishness Pass-66 maxim domain)

**Component 2 (bipolar capacity-range):** At any given consciousness-tier, free-will-capacity has SYMMETRIC range — capacity-for-excellent-choice ≈ capacity-for-foolish-choice (structurally bipolar, not asymmetric). NOT all higher-consciousness-choices are good; "remarkable ability to also make foolish ones" is an inherent feature.

**Component 3 (volition-as-action-simulation):** FWC-1 extends CSS-1 #42 to action-space: just as CSS-1 frames consciousness as simulation-capacity-applied-to-cognitive-content, FWC-1 frames free-will as simulation-capacity-applied-to-action-space (counterfactual-action-evaluation; multiple-option-holding; choice-among-simulated-action-paths). Composes with CSS-1 μ-coefficient adapted to action-domain.

**Component 4 (foolishness-corollary canonical-maxim integration):** Pass-66 canonical "pinnacle of foolishness perhaps greatest indicator that i-cell is conscious" Brandon-maxim now applies to BOTH:
- Cognitive-domain (CEF-1 arithmetic-mistakes-held-with-conviction; CSS-1 J3a Pass-74-B11)
