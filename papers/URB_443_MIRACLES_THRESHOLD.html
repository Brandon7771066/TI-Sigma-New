1. The Core Reframing

The standard view: A miracle is an event that violates natural law. Telekinesis — the movement of physical objects by mental intention alone — is either real (in which case physics as we know it is wrong or incomplete) or fake (in which case all claimed demonstrations are fraud, hallucination, or error). The debate is binary: either natural law holds and telekinesis is impossible, or telekinesis is real and natural law is suspended.

The TI Sigma reframing: This binary is itself the error. It conflates violation with extension and treats physical laws as if they fully enumerate all forces that exist, rather than as the best current description of forces that are detectable above current measurement thresholds.

Consider the analogical progression:

  • Gravity: Every object in the universe exerts gravitational attraction on every other object. The gravitational pull of a human being on a distant star is real, finite, and nonzero — it is simply unmeasurably small relative to the star's own mass and all other gravitational sources. The human's gravity is not zero; it is below the effective threshold for observation.
  • Magnetism: Every moving charge generates a magnetic field. The human body generates measurable magnetic fields — the heart's magnetic field is detectable with SQUID magnetometers at distances of several feet; the brain's is detectable at millimeter range. These fields have real physical effects. They are simply far weaker than external magnetic sources in most contexts.
  • PSI field (TI Sigma proposal): Every conscious system generates a phase-field influence on its environment — an i-channel force mediated through the imaginary component of the physical fields it sustains. This force is real, finite, and nonzero in all conscious beings. It is simply below the detection threshold — both the threshold of standard instrumentation (which measures only manifest/real-channel quantities) and the threshold of macroscopic physical effect — for most people in most states.

    • The difference between a person who can demonstrate telekinesis and one who cannot is not the presence or absence of the PSI capacity. It is the magnitude of the capacity relative to the noise floor of the physical environment. This is a threshold problem, not a metaphysical one.

    • 3. The Physics: What Existing Laws Already Allow

    3.1 Quantum Non-Locality:

    Bell inequality violations demonstrate that quantum mechanics is irreducibly non-local — the measurement of one entangled particle instantaneously affects the state of its entangled partner, regardless of distance. This is not a PSI effect; it is standard physics. But it establishes that non-local causal influence is not forbidden by physics — it exists, is empirically confirmed, and occurs within the framework of natural law. The question for PSI research is not whether non-local influence exists (it does) but whether consciousness can access it at macroscopic scales.

    3.2 The Quantum Zeno Effect:

    Continuous observation of a quantum system inhibits its state transitions (the Quantum Zeno Effect). Conversely, strategically timed observation can accelerate transitions (the Anti-Zeno Effect). If conscious observation — particularly observation characterized by a high-coherence i-channel state — can influence the timing and frequency of quantum measurement-like events in the brain-body system, then intentional states could influence the probability distribution of quantum-scale events in the near environment. This is not a violation of quantum mechanics; it is an application of it.

    3.3 Bioelectromagnetics:

    The human body generates:

  • Bioelectric fields: ~10-100 mV/cm within tissues; detectable outside the body via EEG and ECG
  • Biomagnetic fields: ~10⁻¹² Tesla (brain) to ~10⁻¹⁰ Tesla (heart) — measured with SQUID magnetometers
  • Infrared radiation: ~100 watts of thermal radiation continuously
  • Coherent biophoton emission: ~1-1000 photons/cm²/second, potentially coherent (laser-like) in high-LCC states

    • None of these fields is strong enough to move macroscopic objects by standard electromagnetic force. But they are real physical fields that interact with the environment. The TI Sigma proposal is not that these manifest-channel fields produce telekinesis; it is that their phase-channel counterparts (the i-channel component of these fields) produce the PSI effect — and the i-channel component scales with LCC in ways the manifest channel does not.

    5. Why Most People Cannot Display the Effect

    The threshold model predicts that PSI effects are universally present but selectively manifest. Five mechanisms suppress PSI effects in ordinary individuals:

    5.1 Low Baseline LCC: The average person operates well below C_EMERICK in their baseline state (chronic stress, poor sleep, fragmented attention, environmental electromagnetic noise all suppress LCC). Their PSI field exists but is orders of magnitude below the detection threshold.

    5.2 Phase Incoherence: Even if LCC is momentarily elevated, the phase orientation θ_Ψ of the PSI field may be random across time — the field averages to zero over any measurement window. Macroscopic PSI effects require not just high LCC but sustained, directionally coherent PSI field orientation. This is why intention — the directed coherence of the phase field — is a necessary condition for observed PSI effects.

    5.3 Signal-to-Noise Ratio: In modern environments, the electromagnetic and thermal noise floor is high. The PSI field must exceed not just the fundamental thermal limit but the practical environmental noise. Remote, electromagnetically quiet environments (like those used in the PEAR laboratory at Princeton) may show PSI effects not because the effect is larger there, but because the noise floor is lower.

    5.4 Observer Suppression: The act of trying to demonstrate PSI effects may itself suppress them. Anxious, effortful, performance-oriented states correspond to low LCC (chronic sensory overload: high |s|, noisy a). The optimal PSI state is the opposite: the Tralse Blanket State (s → 0, pure phase coherence) — the "To thine own self, BE!" ground state (URB #441). Trying to demonstrate telekinesis may be the surest way to suppress it.

    5.5 The Pauli Effect (observed): Wolfgang Pauli, one of the founders of quantum mechanics, was so associated with equipment malfunction that experimentalists reportedly asked him to stay away from their labs. This anecdotal pattern — high-LCC individuals producing anomalous effects on physical systems, often without intending to — is precisely what the threshold model predicts. The PSI field of exceptional individuals is not directed; it is a constant phase-field output that affects nearby physical systems regardless of conscious intention.

    6. Empirical Evidence Consistent with Sub-Threshold PSI

    The threshold model does not require dramatic demonstrations of telekinesis. It makes a weaker, testable prediction: sub-threshold PSI effects should be statistically detectable across large samples, even when individual effects are below the detection threshold per trial.

    6.1 The PEAR Laboratory (Princeton Engineering Anomalies Research, 1979–2007):

    Jahn and Dunne conducted over 25 million trials of human intention on random event generators (REGs). The cumulative effect size was small (approximately 0.1% deviation from chance) but statistically robust across operators and REG types. The effect was consistent with the threshold model: individual trials show no significant effect, but cumulative effects across thousands of trials reveal a small but real deviation.

    6.2 The Global Consciousness Project:

    REGs distributed worldwide show correlated deviations during globally significant events (9/11, New Year celebrations, the death of major public figures). The correlation is small but statistically significant across 500+ events. This is consistent with the MGN (Metacausal Graph Networks, URB #432) prediction: when a large number of conscious systems briefly align their phase fields (collective attention), the aggregate PSI field exceeds local noise floors.

    6.3 The Maharishi Effect (TI Sigma framework, existing paper):

    Reduced crime rates in cities when 1% of the population practiced Transcendental Meditation simultaneously. The threshold model interpretation: even brief elevation of LCC across a population-wide sample (1% = ~7,000 people in a medium city) produces a detectable aggregate phase field that influences collective behavior through the i-channel.

    6.4 The Intention Experiment (McTaggart):

    Controlled studies showing that group prayer/intention can produce measurable effects on plant growth, water crystal structure, and REG output. Consistent with the Power of 8 formalization in URB #432: N coherent individuals produce a PSI field N times stronger than any individual — and when N × LCC_mean > C_EMERICK × N_threshold, the aggregate effect crosses the noise floor.

    7. The TRALSE Analysis of Telekinesis

    "Telekinesis is real."

  • True: Sub-threshold PSI effects have been statistically demonstrated in controlled conditions (PEAR, GCP). The effect is small but consistent. Something is happening above chance that standard physics does not explain with known mechanisms.
  • False: No convincing macroscopic demonstration of telekinesis has survived controlled scrutiny. The dramatic stage demonstrations are consistently fraud when tested. The effect, if it exists, is far too weak to move visible objects under normal conditions.
  • TRALSE: Both are simultaneously true. The sub-threshold effect is real and statistically established. The macroscopic effect is real in principle but requires conditions (LCC >> C_EMERICK, phase coherence, low noise floor, directional intention) that are not met in ordinary human performance and are actively suppressed by the effort to demonstrate them. Telekinesis is real the way the magnetic pull of a hydrogen atom is real: genuinely present, unmeasurable under ordinary conditions, but not zero and not fictional.

    • The TRALSE resolution: Replace "real or fake" with the spectrum question: at what LCC level, for what duration, in what environmental conditions, does the PSI effect exceed the noise floor? This is a physics question, not a metaphysics question.
