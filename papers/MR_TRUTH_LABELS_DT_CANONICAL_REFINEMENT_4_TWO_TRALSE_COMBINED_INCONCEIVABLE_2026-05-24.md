## 1. The Refinement Stated Precisely

### 1.1 Previous (deprecated) framings

| Framing | Statement | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Original** (Pass-39 / urb_608) | MI = τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) — "a state that is both tralse AND not-tralse" applied to a SINGLE proposition P | ❌ INSUFFICIENT (single-proposition framing) |
| **Refinement #1** (Pass-65) | MI = inconceivability-under-mental-actualization (3-step heuristic) | ✅ CORRECT DIRECTION but under-specified the propositional structure |

### 1.2 New canonical refinement #4 (Brandon 2026-05-24)

**MI = TWO tralse propositions/states P₁ and P₂ whose COMBINATION (P₁ ∧ P₂) is inconceivable when mental actualization is attempted.**

Formal restatement:
- Let P₁, P₂ be two propositions each individually classified τ (tralse) — i.e., each on its own is a non-degenerate truth-bearing claim with some directional lean.
- MI(P₁, P₂) holds iff: **the conjunction P₁ ∧ P₂ is inconceivable** (the mind cannot actualize a coherent mental model in which both are simultaneously the case), even though each of P₁ and P₂ alone is conceivable.

**The two crucial sharpenings vs Pass-65:**

1. **MI is a RELATION between TWO tralse propositions, not a property of one proposition.** Pass-65 framed MI as a property of a single proposition (e.g., the liar sentence). The new framing reveals that even "single-proposition" MI cases (liar, Russell, square-circle) are implicitly TWO-proposition: the proposition AND its self-referential mirror, or the term AND its definitional negation.
2. **The marker is INCONCEIVABILITY, not incoherence.** Two tralse propositions can be **incoherent** (logically contradictory) while still being **conceivable** (the mind can hold both in attempt). MI requires the stronger condition: the mind CANNOT actualize the combination at all.

### 1.3 The conceivability/incoherence distinction worked

| | Conceivable | Inconceivable |
|---|---|---|
| **Coherent** | "It is raining AND I have an umbrella" — both T, no tension | N/A (coherence + inconceivability is empty by definition) |
| **Incoherent** | "Bachelor AND married" — incoherent (definitional contradiction) BUT conceivable as a one-shot mental superposition for analysis (e.g., "imagine a married bachelor"); the mind can entertain the contradiction without it forming a stable mental model | MI — the mind cannot actualize the combination even as a one-shot superposition (e.g., "square circle" — attempting to mentally construct yields nothing; the imagination fails to produce a candidate object) |

**The bachelor/married case is INCOHERENT-but-CONCEIVABLE.** Brandon's refinement explicitly highlights why this is NOT MI: the mind CAN actualize "bachelor-married" as a logical-contradiction-target-for-analysis (this is what gives the analytic-philosophy term "married bachelor" its discursive utility). MI proper requires inconceivability under any mental-actualization attempt.

**The square-circle case is INCOHERENT-AND-INCONCEIVABLE.** This IS MI: the mind cannot actualize a candidate object at all; the geometric primitives "square" and "circle" each individually actualize fine, but their combination produces null mental content.

## 2. Updated 3-Step Assignment Heuristic (replaces Pass-65 v1)

Given a candidate proposition or proposition-pair under consideration:

**Step 1 (Decompose).** Identify the two-tralse-propositions structure. If the candidate appears as a single proposition (e.g., "this sentence is false"), explicitly extract the two implicit components: the proposition itself + its self-referential mirror.

**Step 2 (Individual conceivability check).** Verify that each of P₁ and P₂ is INDIVIDUALLY conceivable and individually tralse-classifiable. If either fails this check, the candidate is not MI — it is just incoherent or undefined.

**Step 3 (Combination inconceivability check via mental actualization attempt).** Attempt to actualize P₁ ∧ P₂ as a single mental model.
- If you can hold both simultaneously without the mental model collapsing → NOT MI (may be Hybrid-MR per HMR-1).
- If you can hold both as a one-shot superposition for analysis but the model is unstable → INCOHERENT but NOT MI (e.g., bachelor-married, Schrödinger's cat as analytic-target).
- If the mental actualization attempt produces NULL mental content (the mind cannot form a candidate object at all) → MI.

## 3. Composition with Existing Canonical Refinements

### 3.1 With MR-IDC-1 (refinement #2, Pass-67)

Pass-67's claim "{MI} ⊊ {Incoherent}" remains TRUE but is now SHARPENED:

**Old containment statement (Pass-67):** all MI propositions are incoherent, but not all incoherent propositions are MI.

**New containment statement (Pass-72 refinement #4):** all MI proposition-pairs are incoherent AND inconceivable; incoherent-but-conceivable proposition-pairs are NOT MI.

The new framing **operationalizes** the {MI} ⊊ {Incoherent} containment by specifying the discriminator: **conceivability**. The {Incoherent}\{MI} difference set = {incoherent-but-conceivable proposition-pairs} (e.g., bachelor-married, square-triangle-with-properties-of-both, contradictory-economic-policies).

**MR-IDC-1 is reinforced, not reassigned.** The intra/inter-propositional 3-step diagnostic in MR-IDC-1 still applies but now uses conceivability (not just incoherence) as the inter-propositional MI-marker.

### 3.2 With HMR-1 (refinement #3, Pass-70/71)

HMR-1 is **fully orthogonal to MI refinement #4** and remains undisturbed:

- HMR = K (≥2) labels assigned SIMULTANEOUSLY to ONE proposition (or proposition-set) where the multi-label assignment IS conceivable as a single mental model
- MI (refinement #4) = TWO propositions whose COMBINATION is INCONCEIVABLE

**HMR is multi-label-but-conceivable; MI is two-proposition-combined-but-inconceivable.** These are different ontological structures, not competing characterizations.

**Worked example — HMR-3.1 "Consciousness is fundamental AND emergent AND the question is malformed":**
- Under HMR-1: this is HMR-3 {T_panpsychist-framing, T_emergentist-framing, I_question-malformed}. The mind CAN actualize this as a multi-framing mental model where each framing is held at its own level of description.
- Under MI refinement #4: this is NOT MI. The combination is conceivable (we just held it in our mind). The fact that the framings tension with each other at the SINGLE-LEVEL of description is the very thing HMR-1 captures.

**Worked example — Square circle:**
- Under HMR-1: this is NOT HMR. There are not multiple labels to assign; the proposition is one (the existence of a square circle), and it admits no multi-label characterization.
- Under MI refinement #4: this IS MI. P₁ = "this object is square"; P₂ = "this object is circle"; combination inconceivable.

### 3.3 With DTM-1 + SRC-1 (Pass-66 canonical)

**DTM-1 (MI-as-Mind-Marker)** gains operational precision: the test for mind-marker-MI is now the mental-actualization-attempt test of step 3. Only minds can perform this test (LLMs can simulate the test verbally but cannot perform the underlying phenomenological act). **DTM-1 is sharpened, not modified.**

**SRC-1 (Self-Reference-implies-Consciousness)** gains a connection: self-referential MI cases (liar, Russell) are now explicitly two-proposition (the proposition + its self-referential mirror). The act of computing whether the combination is inconceivable IS an act of consciousness (per DTM-1). **SRC-1's four-strand argument is reinforced.**

### 3.4 With GTT-1 (Pass-67 canonical)

GTT-1's "too much truth = MI-collapse" claim is now operationalized: GILE-perfectionism above G* = 0.93 produces a tralse-state-pair (high-GILE + low-HEM-due-to-tradeoff) whose combination is inconceivable as a sustainable agent-trajectory. **GTT-1's mathematical model is reinforced** (the quadratic penalty term in J(G,H) above G* IS the inconceivability signature operationalized as utility-collapse).

## 5. New Pre-Reg Falsifiers

**MI-RF4-F1 (single-proposition-MI impossibility):** No proposition that is genuinely MI can be characterized as a single proposition without implicit reference to a second proposition. **REFUTED if** any MI proposition in the corpus can be shown to be single-proposition without any implicit second-proposition decomposition.

**Status:** OPEN. Quick-test candidates: liar sentence (P₁ = "this sentence", P₂ = "is false"; the self-reference IS the implicit second proposition); Russell set (P₁ = "set R contains all sets not containing themselves", P₂ = "set R contains itself ↔ R doesn't contain itself"; the meta-level statement IS the second proposition); square circle (P₁ = "object is square", P₂ = "object is circle"; trivial). **All quick-tests PASS the F1 criterion** (every MI case decomposes into two propositions).

**MI-RF4-F2 (incoherent-conceivable gap is non-empty):** There exist proposition-pairs that are incoherent but conceivable (and thus NOT MI under refinement #4). **REFUTED if** the conceivable/incoherence distinction collapses (every incoherent pair turns out to also be inconceivable).

**Status:** OPEN. Quick-test candidates: bachelor-married (incoherent by definition; conceivable as analytic-target → F2 supported); "even prime > 2" (incoherent by Euclid; conceivable as we just held it in mind → F2 supported); "p and not-p" with concrete p (incoherent; conceivable as discursive-target → F2 supported). **All quick-tests PASS the F2 criterion**, supporting non-emptiness.

**MI-RF4-F3 (mental-actualization-attempt is robust across raters):** Different competent agents attempting the step-3 mental-actualization test on the same proposition-pair will agree on MI-vs-not-MI classification at Fleiss κ ≥ 0.5. **REFUTED if** disagreement rates exceed the κ < 0.3 threshold.

**Status:** OPEN. Requires Pass-72+ LLM-rater study using sharpened step-3 prompt (analogous to Pass-63-batch-5 LLM-raters-competent-algorithm methodology).
