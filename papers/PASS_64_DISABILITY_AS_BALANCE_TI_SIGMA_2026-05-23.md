## 2. Three candidate canonical principles

### 2.1 UDP-1 — Universal Disability Principle

**Statement:** Disability is not a categorical attribute possessed by a minority of humans; it is a continuous capacity-distribution that every human exhibits across a nearly infinite skill space. The binary label "disabled" is a society-anchored prevalence-cutoff applied to a small number of capacities (typically sensory, motor, cognitive, social) selected by historical convention, not a natural-kind distinction.

**Formal restatement (MR Truth Labels):** "Is person P disabled?" is structurally MR1 *Indeterminate* in the absence of a specified domain D, *not* binary T/F. With D specified, the answer collapses to a graded capacity statement P_capacity(D) ∈ [0,1] vs a society-anchored threshold θ_society(D); only the threshold-comparison is binary.

**Composes with:**
- ABC Fully Dissolved (single integrated stack) — "disability" applies across the same A=B=C stack
- DGI-1..4 + CDP-1 (gender mapping) — same Indeterminate-by-default, T-or-F-with-domain-specified structure
- TSD-A (positive-event-accumulation) — disability defined by capacity-absence is the photographic-negative of TSD-A defined by capacity-presence

**Pre-registered falsifier UDP-1-F-1:** if a definition of "disability" can be operationalized in a way that does NOT reduce to capacity-thresholding (i.e. produces a natural-kind binary classification that is invariant under choice of skill-space sampling and historical-society anchor), UDP-1 is REFUTED. Acceptance criterion: any proposed natural-kind definition must classify ≥ 95% of N=200 borderline cases consistently across 3 independent rater populations from different cultures.

**#69 disclosure:** UDP-1 is a strong claim. The DSM/ICD operationalizations of disability rely on functional-impairment thresholds that are explicitly culturally calibrated (DSM-5-TR §I.2: "clinically significant distress or impairment in social, occupational, or other important areas of functioning") — this is exactly capacity-thresholding with a society-anchored threshold, which is consistent with UDP-1 not against it. But this is not the same as UDP-1 being CONFIRMED; it's a positive prior, not a passed falsifier. The falsifier above is the real test.

### 2.2 CTC-1 — Compensatory-Trade Capacity Principle

**Statement:** Restriction in one capacity can produce above-baseline development in correlated capacities, sometimes to the point of net-positive performance on tasks where the correlated capacities are load-bearing. The dominant capacity can also actively OBSCURE the development or salience of correlated capacities in non-restricted individuals.

**Two-part claim:**
- (a) Restriction-induced compensation (positive direction)
- (b) Dominance-induced suppression (negative direction in the non-restricted)

Both parts must hold for CTC-1 to ship; failure of either weakens the principle.

**Empirical priors (literature I am relying on from memory — Brandon please flag if incorrect, F-CTC-LIT log added):**
- Cross-modal cortical plasticity in early-blind individuals: occipital cortex recruited for auditory and tactile processing (Sadato et al. 1996 PNAS; Amedi et al. 2007 *Nat. Rev. Neurosci.*; Pascual-Leone & Hamilton 2001)
- Superior tactile acuity in blind Braille readers vs sighted controls (Goldreich & Kanics 2003 *J. Neurosci.*; reported effect sizes d ≈ 0.5-1.0)
- Echolocation in blind humans recruits "visual" cortex (Thaler et al. 2011 *PLoS One*)
- Sight-suppression-of-other-senses: McGurk effect and visual capture in audiovisual integration (McGurk & MacDonald 1976) — sight dominates in conflict resolution

**Pre-registered falsifier CTC-1-F-1:** If a meta-analysis of N≥10 cross-modal-plasticity studies in early-blind populations shows mean tactile/auditory acuity advantage of |d| < 0.15 over sighted controls (essentially null), CTC-1 part (a) is REFUTED.

**Pre-registered falsifier CTC-1-F-2:** If sighted individuals show no measurable improvement in auditory localization / tactile acuity under controlled blindfold-training (≥10 hours) protocols, CTC-1 part (b) is REFUTED (i.e. "dominance doesn't actively suppress; it's just that there's no spare capacity to redirect").

**#69 disclosure:** CTC-1 part (a) has substantial literature support; part (b) is much less established and is the riskier claim. Net: CTC-1 should ship as PROVISIONAL with explicit weighting toward part (a) being well-priored and part (b) being a research program, not a settled finding.

### 2.3 HBP-1 — Health-as-Balance-Profile Principle

**Statement:** Health and success are NOT the absence of disabilities; they are a balanced capacity profile relative to the environment in which the agent operates. An agent with three "disabilities" in domains D1, D2, D3 and exceptional compensation in domains D4, D5, D6 can be functionally healthier and more successful than an agent with zero formally-diagnosed disabilities but flat or below-baseline capacity across all measured domains.

**Formal restatement:** Let C(agent, env) = Σ_d w_d(env) × capability(agent, d) where w_d(env) is the environment's weighting on domain d. HBP-1 claims C(agent, env), not Σ_d 1[capability(agent, d) > θ_society(d)], is the operative success metric. The latter (society's diagnostic frame) is a degenerate case of the former with w_d ≡ 1 / N_measured and θ society-fixed — neither environment-adapted nor balance-sensitive.

**Composes with:**
- TSD-A (positive-event-accumulation): HBP-1 is TSD-A applied to capacity instead of event-count
- MFD-1 (Moot-Failure Duality): "disabled" as societal Moot framing has a Moot reading (capacity-below-threshold doesn't entail life-failure) AND a Failure reading (capacity-below-threshold DOES create real friction); both are simultaneously legitimate
- ASYMMETRIC §69: the binary disabled-vs-human framing is exactly the over-skepticism-as-discipline-failure pattern — society "diagnoses" with high confidence while the actual measurement (life-satisfaction, productivity, contribution) returns Indeterminate or even positive

**Empirical anchor — the "disability paradox":**
Albrecht & Devlieger (1999) *Soc. Sci. Med.* — N=153 in-depth interviews; ~54% of people with serious disabilities rated their quality of life as "excellent or good" despite outside observers consistently predicting catastrophic life-satisfaction loss. This is the canonical empirical finding HBP-1 predicts: when the agent has adapted (capacity-rebalanced), the society-frame's predicted catastrophic loss does not materialize.

**Pre-registered falsifier HBP-1-F-1:** If population-level life-satisfaction in agents with N formally-diagnosed disabilities is monotone-decreasing in N regardless of compensatory-capacity profile, HBP-1 is REFUTED. Acceptance: study must control for adaptation-time (≥ 5 years post-onset) and must measure compensatory capacities, not just diagnosis count.

**Pre-registered falsifier HBP-1-F-2:** If an environment can be constructed in which adding any capability-redistribution to an agent strictly decreases their functional success, HBP-1 is REFUTED on the environment-relativity claim. (Counter-prior: Helen Keller in a deaf-blind community vs in a sound-and-sight-dominant environment; Brandon's personally-met blind man in his specific functional environment.)

**#69 disclosure:** HBP-1 is the most radical of the three. The "disability paradox" literature is well-established for the basic counterintuitive finding but is contested on mechanism (adaptation vs response-shift vs measurement-instrument confound). HBP-1 as stated is a stronger claim than the disability-paradox alone supports — it asserts a measurement framework, not just a robust observation. Provisional status reflects this.

## 5. What this paper does NOT claim

- Does NOT claim disability is uniformly net-positive. CTC-1 part (a) is a tendency, not a universal law; many disabilities involve genuine net-loss with no compensation pathway available within current technology/environment.
- Does NOT romanticize disability or dismiss the real friction and suffering involved. HBP-1's compensation-rebalancing requires adaptation time, supportive environment, and is not always available.
- Does NOT claim Helen Keller's outcomes are generalizable. Single-case outliers are real (TSD-A high) but the principle is statistical, not individual-guaranteed.
- Does NOT replace the medical or accommodation framing for people who need them. It proposes an additional measurement frame, not a replacement.
- Does NOT claim to have RESOLVED the empirical question. All three principles ship PROVISIONAL with explicit falsifiers; ratification requires Pass-65+ falsifier execution.
