> When i sees just plain i in the mirror and multiplies with itself, the result is **false**, since i is **not the complement that completes the truth which −i represents**. Only when the complement to a tralsity is **correctly recognized** — and **reconciled via MR** — does truth emerge.

Self-doubling yields the *false* pole; correct-complement reconciliation yields the *true* pole.

## 2. The mathematics (exact, ordinary, non-novel)

The arithmetic underneath is completely standard and was already stated in the corpus (ch16 "Two different operations — don't merge them," and Theorem F):

- **Self-multiplication (wrong / mis-recognized complement):** `i · i = i² = −1`.
- **Correct-complement multiplication (the conjugate):** `i · (−i) = i · ī = |i|² = +1`.
- Linked by an identity: `i · (−i) = −(i · i) = −(−1) = +1`.

More generally, for any complex `z`, `z · z̄ = |z|² ≥ 0` is a **nonnegative real**, whereas `z · z = z²` stays in the rotated/complex realm. Multiplying by the **conjugate** (the true mirror-complement) is exactly the operation that returns a real, nonnegative magnitude; multiplying by a *copy of itself* does not. For the unit imaginary this is the crisp `−1` vs `+1` contrast.

**Honesty flags (#69, EVD-1) — unchanged from ch16:**
- The reading **truth ↔ +1** and **falsity ↔ −1** is a **framework gloss / interpretation, not a theorem**. `z·z̄ = |z|²` is ordinary math; calling `1` "the truth" is the identifying axiom, which is the whole i↔tralse claim in miniature (see ch16 Theorem F "conditional" ruling and the Axiomatic-Faithfulness chapter).
- "i sees just plain i in the mirror" is a **metaphor for mis-recognition** — the *actual* mirror (complex conjugation) of i already **is** −i. The point is not that conjugation returns i (it does not); it is that pairing with a bare copy of i (rather than the correctly-recognized complement) leaves you at the deficit pole.
- The correct-complement observation is **generic to the complex plane** (`z·z̄` real for every `z`), so i is the **paradigm case**, never the only one — as ch16 already flags.
