## 1. The Problem That Has No Standard-Model Answer

The Standard Model has **exactly three generations** of fermions:

| Generation | Charged lepton | Neutrino | Up-type quark | Down-type quark |
|------------|----------------|----------|---------------|-----------------|
| 1          | electron (e)   | ν_e      | up (u)        | down (d)        |
| 2          | muon (μ)       | ν_μ      | charm (c)     | strange (s)     |
| 3          | tau (τ)        | ν_τ      | top (t)       | bottom (b)      |

Across generations, the *electric charge, color charge, and weak isospin* are identical.
