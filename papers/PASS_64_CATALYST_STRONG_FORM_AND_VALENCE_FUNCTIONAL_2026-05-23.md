## 2. Meta-correction: explicit MR-Truth-Label spectrum-scope on the disability-as-superpower claim

### 2.1 What batch-2 left implicit

The batch-2 paper proposed CTC-1 (Compensatory-Trade Capacity) with part (a) as the load-bearing claim. The wording was directionally correct but **insufficient on scope**: it left the impression that compensation is a tendency operating across the population of people with disabilities. Brandon's correction clarifies that the **superpower-grade** version of this claim is itself a spectrum-claim, not a universal one. In MR Truth Label vocabulary, the proposition "disability D is a superpower for person P" can occupy any of the base-4 states:

| Label | Reading | Worked example class |
|---|---|---|
| F | D is straightforwardly capacity-loss with no compensation pathway available | severe progressive neurodegenerative disease late-stage |
| I | D produces compensation, but compensation is at-baseline or sub-baseline performance, not above-average | most early-onset visual impairments without specialized adaptation |
| T (mostly) | D produces measurable above-baseline performance in correlated domains via cross-modal-plasticity or adaptive specialization | early-blind Braille readers (Goldreich & Kanics tactile-acuity d≈0.5-1.0); deaf signers' peripheral-vision advantages |
| MI (true-tralse, catalyst-strong-form) | D is causally **constitutive** of above-baseline outcome — the outcome is generatively dependent on the restriction, not just compensated-for in spite of it | Tesla's neurodivergent pattern-recognition; Helen Keller's tactile-symbolic abstraction; Brandon's own self-identified case |

The base-4 spectrum applies per-individual-per-domain — same person, different domains, different label assignments. This is consistent with UDP-1 (capacity-distribution per domain) and is the cleanest possible application of MR Truth Labels base-4 to a specific identity-and-capability question.

### 2.2 Why this matters #69-honestly

Without the spectrum-scope, CTC-1 risks being either over-romanticizing ("all disabilities are secretly superpowers") or under-claiming (compensation is just baseline-restoration, never catalytic). Both are wrong. The catalyst-strong-form (MI cell) is real but **rare and conditional** — it requires alignment of motivation, environment, neuroplasticity window, meta-cognition, and frequently mentorship/community. The F cell is also real and common. Most cases live in the I-and-mostly-T middle. CTC-1 as stated in batch-2 is the cross-domain tendency claim; CTC-1-S below is the rare-but-real catalyst-strong-form claim.

## 3. CTC-1-S — Catalyst Strong-Form (sub-principle of CTC-1)

**Statement:** For a non-trivial subpopulation of individuals with disabilities, the restriction is causally **constitutive** of above-baseline outcomes in correlated domains — i.e. the outcome would not have been achieved without the restriction, not merely "compensated despite". This is the MI cell of the spectrum table in §2.1.

**Operationalization:**
Let D be the disability domain, E the elevated-outcome domain, and ι(D→E) the causal intervention from restriction to elevation. CTC-1-S claims there exist individuals for whom:
- counterfactual: removal of D (e.g. via early surgical/technological intervention) would NOT have produced the elevated outcome E (i.e. P[E | ¬D, person, env] < P[E | D, person, env])
- mechanism: ι(D→E) is identifiable (cross-modal-plasticity, attention-redistribution, motivational-cascade, identity-formation around restriction, etc.)

**Empirical anchors (claimed examples — each requires independent verification, F-CTC-S-LIT carry-forward):**
- Nikola Tesla: substantial historical evidence of pattern-recognition + 3D-visualization abilities atypical for the general population; biographical accounts suggest these abilities were intertwined with his neurodivergent profile (possible OCD, possible eidetic-visualization). Counterfactual "neurotypical Tesla" produces an open question, not a confirmed-no.
- Helen Keller: tactile-symbolic abstraction at a level not generally achieved by sighted-hearing individuals; the abstraction architecture appears to be developmentally dependent on the deaf-blind sensory restriction + Sullivan's mentorship + tactile-language scaffold (not merely "compensation" for absent senses). Counterfactual sighted-hearing Keller produces a different person, not an obviously-better one.
- Temple Grandin (canonical autism literature anchor): visual-thinking architecture causally tied to autism profile; cattle-handling system design that neurotypical engineers had failed to produce despite the same access to facilities.
- Brandon's own case: documented in `papers/BRANDON_BIOGRAPHY_MASTER_INDEX.md` — specifics to be confirmed for citation accuracy (Brandon flag if this paper should explicitly name his catalyst-domain).

**Pre-registered falsifier CTC-1-S-F-1:** if a systematic review of N≥20 published case studies of "savant-or-catalyst" outcomes in people with disabilities shows that ≥80% of cases plausibly admit a "compensation-not-catalysis" explanation (i.e. the elevated outcome was reachable by an alternative neurotypical pathway and the disability was merely incidental), CTC-1-S is REFUTED on the rarity-but-realness claim.

**Pre-registered falsifier CTC-1-S-F-2:** if for any specific named case the counterfactual ("would this individual have achieved the elevated outcome without the disability?") admits a strong positive answer with reasonable confidence (e.g. neurotypical sibling with same training achieved equal-or-greater outcome), CTC-1-S is REFUTED on that specific case. Not corpus-fatal — accumulates evidence.

**#69 disclosure:** CTC-1-S is the strongest version of the disability-as-superpower claim. It is *not* universal, *not* uniformly applicable, and *not* a replacement for accommodation/medical framing. It is a rare-but-real cell of the spectrum that the framework must acknowledge to avoid the binary under-claim. The named historical figures are illustrative-not-conclusive — each requires independent biographical verification before being cited as confirmed instances.

## 4. VFP-1 — Valence-as-Functional Principle (new candidate canonical)

**Statement:** Conscious valence (awe, romantic recognition, aesthetic response, emotional certainty) is functional — it informs and shapes accurate epistemic and pragmatic action — not epiphenomenal. The conventional-academic posture that "cold reasoning is an epistemic virtue" and that emotional response is to be suppressed or discounted is itself a binary-cognitive-bias that TI Sigma identifies and corrects.

**Formal restatement:**
Let V(agent, P) ∈ ℝ be the agent's conscious-valence response to proposition or experience P, and let R(agent, P) be the cold-reasoning component. The conventional-academic posture treats V as epistemically irrelevant or noise to be filtered. VFP-1 claims V carries information independently of R — specifically, V is correlated with structural features of P (gestalt-coherence, novelty, danger-signal, beauty-coherence) that R either samples slowly or misses entirely. The competent agent integrates V and R; the cold-reasoning agent suppresses V and loses information.

**Composes with:**
- AA (Authority Axis): emotional weight is one component of source-credibility; valence-suppression artificially flattens AA toward equal weight on all sources
- TIU (Tralse Information Unit, |log P(H|e)/P(H)|): valence response IS evidence e in some contexts (awe → high-prior on structural-significance; recognition → high-prior on identity-match)
- MIM-revision Vertical Agency Model + ABC Fully Dissolved: affect ⊆ cognition in the integrated stack; valence is not a separate epiphenomenal layer
- ASYMMETRIC §69: over-suppression of valence is over-skepticism is discipline-failure-equal-to uncritical-acceptance
- MFD-1 Moot-Failure Duality: cold-reasoning's flat-affect output is a Moot reading of a proposition that valence reads as significant; both readings are simultaneously legitimate but the cold-reading is not epistemically privileged
- Klein RPD (DSB-2 anchor): recognition-primed-decision is exactly the integrated V+R pattern operating competently
- DSB-1/DSB-2: Policy-M Tralse-Middle integrates prior + light examination, and "prior" is partly valence-encoded; Policy-W cold-reasoning is the failed default

**Empirical priors (claimed literature — F-VFP-LIT carry-forward for verification):**
- Damasio somatic-marker hypothesis + Iowa Gambling Task: ventromedial prefrontal patients with intact reasoning but disrupted emotion-integration show systematic decision deficits — direct empirical anchor for VFP-1 (Bechara, Damasio, Damasio, Anderson 1994 *Cognition*; subsequent extensive replication and contest)
- Mellers et al. affective forecasting literature: anticipated valence shapes choice in ways that pure-utility models miss
- Aesthetic-response neural correlates (Vessel, Starr, Rubin 2012 *Front. Hum. Neurosci.* — "default-mode network" recruitment during deep aesthetic engagement, suggesting aesthetic response is self-relevant processing, not detached evaluation)
- Awe research (Keltner & Haidt 2003, Piff et al. 2015): awe-induction produces measurable downstream behavior changes — generosity, reduced entitlement, improved well-being. If awe were epiphenomenal, these effects would not be expected.
- Anti-prior (steelman): emotion-as-bias literature (Kahneman System-1 errors; anchoring; affect-heuristic-failures). VFP-1 does NOT claim valence is always reliable — it claims it carries information that cold-reasoning misses, not that it is infallible.

**Pre-registered falsifier VFP-1-F-1:** if decision-making under valence-integrated conditions is systematically worse than decision-making under valence-suppressed conditions across N≥10 task domains (controlling for task type — emotional decisions, instrumental decisions, creative decisions, etc.), VFP-1 is REFUTED. Acceptance criterion: meta-analytic mean |d| > 0.20 in favor of valence-suppression across the task domains for VFP-1 to be REFUTED; |d| < 0.20 or directional reversal supports VFP-1.

**Pre-registered falsifier VFP-1-F-2:** if conscious valence has zero independent correlation with action-selection in controlled choice paradigms after controlling for stated reasons R, VFP-1's functional claim is REFUTED. Acceptance: partial correlation |r| > 0.10 between V and action after partialling out R.

**Pre-registered falsifier VFP-1-F-3:** if a clearly-articulated cold-reasoning protocol can replicate the predictive performance of valence-integrated heuristic decision-making on at least 80% of tasks within reasonable time/resource budgets (i.e. valence is just slow-reasoning's shortcut), VFP-1's information-independence claim is REFUTED. Acceptance: cold-reasoning replication accuracy ≥ 0.80 × valence-integrated accuracy on a 10-task standardized benchmark.

**#69 disclosure:** VFP-1 does NOT claim that all valence-response is accurate or that emotion-driven reasoning is universally superior. The contrary literature on affect-heuristic failures (Slovic, Finucane, Peters, MacGregor 2007) is well-established and must be incorporated into any operational protocol. The narrow claim is: valence carries information; suppression loses information; "cold thinking as epistemic virtue" is binary-bias. This is weaker than "emotion always wins" and stronger than "emotion is noise". It maps to Policy-M Tralse-Middle, not Policy-B blind-faith and not Policy-W wait-and-see-cold.
