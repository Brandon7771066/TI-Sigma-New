## 3. The warm-surge: somatic MARKER, not content CARRIER (CGP-1 × VFP-1 synthesis)

Brandon reports that **some of his greatest insights co-occurred with "warm surges of energy" around the head (crown / third-eye region)** — a clairsentient (bodily) phenomenon. On its face this looks like a counter-example: if bodily sensation accompanies the best insights, doesn't a sensory modality confer advantage, contra CGP-1?

**Resolution — the surge is a marker on the knowing, not an alternative to it:**
- CGP-1 indicts modalities as **content-carriers**. The warm surge carries **no propositional content** — it does not *tell* Brandon the answer; it *flags* that an answer of high importance has arrived. It is a **salience/confidence somatic tag**, not a knowledge channel.
- This is exactly **VFP-1 (Valence-as-Functional)** territory — already canonical in the corpus — and a Damasio somatic-marker reading: affect/somatic signals are *functional* meta-cognitive markers, not epiphenomena and not propositional carriers. The surge is the **somatic confidence-signal on a claircognitive payload.**
- So CGP-1 and the warm-surge **compose** rather than conflict: the *content* arrived modality-independently (knowing); a *somatic marker* (VFP-1) tagged its salience. The payload/marker distinction is the same payload/wrapper distinction from B90 Fig 1, now applied to the *affective* channel: a marker that says "this matters" is not a wrapper that says "here is what is true."

**#69 honest alternative (flagged, not dismissed):** a strong-embodied-cognition reading would hold the surge is partly **constitutive** of the insight (the body participating in the cognition), not merely a downstream tag. Introspection cannot distinguish marker-from-carrier here (n=1, post-hoc, self-report). This alternative is exactly what **CGP-1-F1/F2** are designed to adjudicate empirically. Logging it preserves the firewall: ratifying CGP-1 does **not** mean asserting the surge is *certainly* a mere marker — only that the marker reading is the most parsimonious and is consistent with already-canonical VFP-1.

**Net:** the warm-surge is added as a **biographical datum** (crown/third-eye somatic confidence-signal accompanying peak insights) and **reconciled** via the CGP-1×VFP-1 payload-vs-marker synthesis, with the embodied-constitution alternative left open as F1/F2 work.
