## 1. FTE-1 stated

> **FTE-1 (Fundamental Tension Between Truth and Existence — candidate, framework-internal, NOT ratified).**
> For anything that exists:
> (a) **Stable indeterminacy of truth.** Its GILE-essence (its Truth) is a settled, mind-independent fact ("things are what they are"), yet it is **unknowable with certainty (τ=1) by anyone, including itself** — permanently, not for lack of data (TRG-1 + TLT).
> (b) **Even the best instrument is tralse.** Incorrigible conscious experience is the single highest-warrant route to truth and *still* falls short of τ=1: consciousness is a Tralse-Soup derivative (TOF-1) subject to fallacies, alexithymia, and working-memory limits (RTI-1).
> (c) **The veil is coherent (no MI).** It is consistent for a thing to **be** without being **perfectly known**; "existence hiding behind a veil" is **not** a Meta-Indeterminate self-refutation, because MI requires the *same* THAT to both be and not-be tralse, whereas here two *different* THATs are in play — the object's existence (settled) and the knowledge of its essence (incomplete) (B179 object-specificity).
> (d) **Knownness-floor.** Whatever exists must nonetheless be known **to some degree** (if only by itself); total unknownness is nonexistence (existence criterion, TSG-1/EPE-1). Hence **knowledge of an existing thing ∈ (0,1)** — strictly above zero, strictly below one.
> **The Tension** is the conjunction (a)–(d): to exist is to be *partially* known — never fully, never not-at-all. **The Compromise** (UOP/SUP-1) is the agent's response to it, and is a **distinct** object.

## 2. The four legs, defended

### 2.1 Stable indeterminacy (a)
Not skepticism ("we can't know") and not anti-realism ("there's no fact"). The fact is settled; the *certainty of grasping it* is capped below 1 by the TLT (which itself rests on real results: Mertens' conjecture disproven, retracted "theorems," Gödel-2 self-consistency limit, CH/AC independence). "Stable" = the indeterminacy is the permanent ground state (TRG-1), not a transient gap. This is the metaphysical reading of the operating stance in Part 1: aim at the settled fact (realism as regulative ideal, Peirce/Putnam), bank only pragmatic warrant (POD-1).

### 2.2 Incorrigible experience is still tralse (b)
The classic foundationalist redoubt is the incorrigible report ("I am in pain"). FTE-1 grants it **best-shot** status and denies **certainty**:
- **Metaphysical route:** consciousness is Soup-derived (TOF-1) ⇒ inherits tralse character ⇒ no conscious act reaches τ=1.
- **Empirical route (real phenomena, no fabrication):** **alexithymia** (Sifneos 1973 — difficulty identifying/describing one's own affect) is a documented failure of privileged self-access; **working-memory capacity limits** (Miller 1956; Cowan 2001) cap how much of one's own state is simultaneously reportable; ordinary **logical fallacies** corrupt introspective inference.
- **Philosophical ally (real cite):** Sellars, *Empiricism and the Philosophy of Mind* (1956) — the **Myth of the Given**: no experience is self-certifying. FTE-1(b) is Sellars's conclusion re-expressed in tralse terms.

### 2.3 The veil is not MI (c)
The apparent self-refutation — "X exists but its truth is hidden" reads like "is and is not" — dissolves under **B179 object-specificity**. MI(P) ⟺ τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) on **one** referent. The veil scenario predicates over **two** referents: existence(object) is settled; knowledge(essence-of-object) is incomplete. Different THATs ⇒ no identity-clash ⇒ no MI. This is a **new application** of B179, not a new principle. (Falsifier FTE-1-F3: exhibit a veil case that genuinely pins one THAT to both poles ⇒ it would be real MI and (c) fails.)

### 2.4 The knownness-floor (d) — the honest soft spot
The floor is the paper's most load-bearing new posit and its weakest. Read the existence criterion (exists ⇔ contemplated ∨ stored ∨ instantiated) epistemically: each disjunct is a mode of *being-known-to-some-degree* (contemplation = known by a mind; storage = known by a record; instantiation = at minimum self-registered). Then existence ⇒ knowledge > 0.
- **Comparative anchor (real):** Berkeley, *Principles of Human Knowledge* (1710), *esse est percipi*. FTE-1(d) is **deliberately weaker**: (i) the knower can be the thing **itself** (self-knowledge counts — no external perceiver required, dodging Berkeley's God-as-universal-perceiver move), and (ii) knowledge is **graded**, not binary.
- **Honest risk (FTE-1-F2):** if "known to some degree" is *defined* via the existence criterion, the floor is near-analytic and cannot be independently falsified — a CCL-TAUT-style concern. The floor earns empirical content only if an **independent** measure of "degree of being-known" is specified that could, in principle, read zero for a putatively existing thing. Until then, (d) is flagged **framework-internal, possibly definitional**.

The distinction blocks a category error: treating the 0.93 cap / UOP balance as if it *were* the metaphysical fact. It is not — it is the best *response* to the fact. This mirrors, one level up, the LCC-topology/UOP-attractor split (B164).
