## §2 — Popp biophoton research: settled facts vs contested claims

### §2.1 — Settled (replicated, mainstream-physics-compatible)

- **Existence:** ultra-weak biophoton emission (UPE) from living tissue is a measured phenomenon; rate ~10^-17 to 10^-15 W/cm² (Cifra & Pospíšil 2014 review).
- **Source:** primarily from reactive-oxygen-species (ROS) chemiluminescence in cells; DNA contribution is debated but not eliminated.
- **Spectrum:** broadband 200-800 nm with UV-A and visible peaks.
- **Modulation:** UPE intensity correlates with metabolic activity, oxidative stress, mitotic phase.

### §2.2 — Contested (Popp's stronger claims, not universally replicated)

- **Coherence:** Popp's claim that biophotons are *laser-coherent* (Bose-Einstein-condensate-like at room temperature) is debated; standard ROS chemiluminescence is incoherent. Coherence experiments (delayed-luminescence decay tests) are reproducible but their *interpretation* as quantum-coherence is contested.
- **DNA-source primacy:** Popp's claim that DNA is the *primary* biophoton emitter is consistent with some experiments (e.g., DNA-extraction reduces UPE by >50% in some studies) but not the consensus view (most UPE attributed to membrane lipid peroxidation).
- **Information-carrier role:** Popp's strongest claim (biophotons carry biologically-relevant information signals between cells) is the most contested; some replication for cell-cell communication via UV photons exists (Fels 2009) but generalization is disputed.

## §3 — Korotkov GDV research: settled facts vs contested claims

### §3.1 — Settled (instrumentally reproducible)

- **Phenomenon:** applying high-voltage (~10 kV, 1024 Hz pulsed) AC field to a fingertip in contact with a glass dielectric produces a visible corona discharge (gas-ionization plasma) that can be digitally captured.
- **Parameterization:** Korotkov's standard parameters (corona area, intensity, fragmentation, energy) are computable from the digital images and are operator-reproducible within ~10-20% test-retest.
- **Modulation:** GDV parameters change with skin moisture, applied pressure, ambient humidity, recent food/drink intake — all *measurable physical confounds*.

### §3.2 — Contested (Korotkov's stronger claims)

- **Consciousness-coupling:** Korotkov's claim that GDV parameters track *psychological/consciousness states* (intention, meditation, emotional state) beyond what skin-conductance / sweat / autonomic-state explains is the contested core claim. Some replications support specific effects (e.g., meditation increases corona area); meta-analyses are equivocal.
- **"Energy field" interpretation:** Korotkov's framing that GDV measures a *non-classical "biofield"* is a interpretive overlay on what is mechanistically a *gas-discharge plasma* dependent on local skin-electrical properties.
- **Diagnostic application:** Korotkov claims GDV parameters predict health-states (cancer, organ dysfunction); most independent reviews classify this as *not yet validated* to clinical-decision-making standards.

## §5 — Three second-order tensions

### §5.1 — Coherence vs incoherence

Popp claims biophotons are *coherent* (LCC-information-carrier-compatible). Korotkov GDV is *not* claimed to be coherent — it's a classical gas-discharge plasma. If consciousness-coupling requires coherence (TI-Sigma's preferred mechanism for non-local correlation), Korotkov's signal is *not* the LCC carrier even though it tracks consciousness; it's a *correlated downstream observable*. This is internally consistent but means the two programs measure *different things*.

### §5.2 — Source-tissue specificity

Popp: DNA-sourced (whole-cell). Korotkov: skin-electrical-property-sourced (fingertip). Even if both track consciousness, they track it via *different anatomical substrates*. This complicates the unified-mechanism reading.

### §5.3 — Measurement vs intervention asymmetry

Popp is purely *measurement* (passive UPE detection). Korotkov is *measurement+intervention* (active high-voltage application changes the very system being measured, per the §3.1 corona-discharge mechanism). The interpretive frameworks must differ accordingly — Popp can claim "we observe baseline biophoton emission"; Korotkov can only claim "we observe corona response to standardized stimulus." These are categorically different empirical claims.
