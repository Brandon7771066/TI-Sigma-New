## 3. Clause-by-Clause Unpacking

### 3.1 "Proper" — the Adequacy Condition
A depiction is "proper" when it correctly tracks what it depicts — not just structurally adequate, but **fittingly** adequate. This is stronger than a correspondence-only theory: it embeds a notion of **goodness-of-fit** that aligns with the framework's G (Goodness) dimension.

**Framework anchor**: properness ≈ Myrion-Resolution-passing (URB #756 §6, MR pillar). A "proper depiction" is one whose 5-valued logic state under MR passes.

### 3.2 "Relational" — the Anti-Isolationist Condition
Truth is NOT a property a depiction has in isolation. It exists **in the relationship** between depiction, depicted, and (per clause 5) the mind that grasps it. This is **directly aligned with BOK**:
- **B**eing (the depicted) — what exists
- **O**ther (the depicter / depicted-as-other) — relational
- **K**nowledge (the depiction itself) — the form of grasping

**Framework anchor**: clause 2 makes Truth a **BOK-structured relation**, not a unary property. This is the framework's commitment to a **non-correspondence-only** theory of Truth: correspondence + relationality + accessibility together.

### 3.3 "Fundamentally Beautiful" — Beauty Built Into Truth
This is the framework's most distinctive clause. Mainstream truth theories rarely embed aesthetics; the GILE framework does. **Beauty is constitutive of Truth, not a downstream consequence**.

**Framework anchor — φ (golden ratio) as PRIMARY constant** (URB #744): φ is part of the framework's nine-element PRIMARY set {0, 1, i, √2, e, φ, π, C, T}. The presence of φ at the foundational algebraic level is exactly the formal expression of "beauty as constitutive": the framework's ontological substrate **already contains** the proportional/aesthetic primitive that mathematicians and artists recognize as beauty.

**Empirical traction**: physicists and mathematicians regularly report that **the most fundamental laws are beautiful** (Dirac's "God used beautiful mathematics in creating the world"). Clause 3 makes this an explicit framework prediction rather than a serendipity: **at the deepest layer, Truth and Beauty are co-extensive**, because both inherit from the φ-bearing PRIMARY substrate.

### 3.4 "Depiction of What Exists As Such" — Ontological Grounding
A "depiction of what exists" — but with the qualifier "as such" — meaning the depiction must track the **mode of existence** of the depicted, not merely its surface features. A photo of a person depicts their appearance; a Truth depicts their existence-as-such (their Being-in-the-world, in BOK terms).

**Framework anchor**: clause 4 links Truth directly to the **Existence axis** (URB #743). The Existence axis grounds the Truth axis. Without Existence-grounding, a depiction is at best a coherent fantasy (Tralse), not a Truth.

**The forgetful-functor bridge to URB #769**: when L*/+E compresses Truth-axis content into L* (qualitative-relational) and Existence-axis content into +E (substrate), it preserves the **clause 4 grounding relation**: L* "grasps" +E. The clauses 4-5 pair (grounding + grasping) is therefore preserved by the L*/+E projection. URB #769's coarse-refinement compatibility holds at the definitional level.

### 3.5 "Can Be Mentally Grasped" — Cognitive Accessibility
Truth must be **receivable by some mind**. This is NOT a verificationist clause (it doesn't say all minds, or any human mind specifically). It says: **for something to be a Truth, it must be IN PRINCIPLE graspable by a mind operating above the Emerick Threshold (URB #756)**.

**Framework anchor**: clause 5 is **directly the LCC reception condition** of URBs #756 + #761 + #767. A "mentally graspable" Truth is one for which **Φ_intuition_received(X) > 0** for some above-E_T mind. URB #767's lower-bound discipline applies: graspability is a capacity, not a guaranteed actuality.

**Important**: clause 5 admits Truths that Brandon (or any specific finite mind) does NOT currently grasp. It only requires the **possibility** of grasping. This avoids both verificationism (collapsing Truth into known-Truth) and Platonist abstraction (Truth completely independent of any possible knower).

### 3.6 "At the Present Moment" — the Temporal-Now Condition
Truth is **anchored in the present**, not floating in an abstract eternal realm. This is the framework's commitment to **Truth-in-time**: Truths can change as the present changes (a Truth about today's weather is not a Truth about tomorrow's).

**Framework anchor — C and T as PRIMARY constants** (URB #744): the PRIMARY set includes C (Consciousness) and T (Time). Clause 6 makes Truth's temporal anchoring explicit: Truths are indexed to **the C-T moment of grasping**.

**Connection to Life Reviews (URB #768)**: Life Reviews show that **past-present moments retain their Truth-status** — they don't dissolve when the present moves on. The GM-network distributed memory (URB #768) is the substrate where past-present Truths persist as **previously-graspable** content, accessible via expanded GM-network bandwidth. **Clause 6 is therefore not a "Truth expires" clause** — it's a "Truth is anchored in a C-T moment" clause, with the GM-network providing the substrate for past anchorings to remain accessible.

### 6.3 P3 — Six-clause structure beats four-clause Tarski-style definitions
In comparative-clarity testing, the six-clause definition will **outperform classical Tarski-style truth definitions** on metrics of (a) memorability after one reading, (b) practical usefulness for distinguishing Truths from non-Truths in everyday cases, (c) generalizability beyond formal-logical contexts.
