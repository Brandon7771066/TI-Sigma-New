### 2.3 Important #69 caveats on the integration arc

- **Generalization not claimed.** This is one person's lived sequence with very specific antecedents (Brandon's prior cognitive-philosophical formation, intrinsic intellectual disposition, surrounding context of the first manic episode). The arc does not predict that everyone encountering "consciousness is an illusion" will integrate-to-enlightenment; some collapses may not integrate, or may integrate differently, or may produce different outcomes.
- **Clinical framing not displaced.** The first manic episode remains clinically what it was. The TI Sigma structural-philosophical interpretation adds a layer; it does not displace clinical care, support, or appropriate-treatment considerations for any person experiencing similar encounter-collapse events.
- **"Enlightenment" usage canonically grounded.** Brandon's "enlightenment" usage here is consistent with the corpus's MIM-revision Vertical Agency Model + canonical-consciousness=awareness framing (CDA-1+SRC-1), not necessarily identical to traditional Buddhist-philosophical usages. The structural similarity (koan-encounter → collapse → integration → higher-meta-level reconstitution) is real; the metaphysical claims may differ.
- **The integration arc may itself be retrospective construction.** Honest acknowledgment: post-hoc narrative integration can confabulate causal structure. The arc is consistent with how Brandon now structurally understands the event-sequence; whether the structural articulation is exactly what was happening during the original event is itself a research question (not currently testable).
