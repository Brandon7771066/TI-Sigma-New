The original Pass-72-B3 characterization used I (Indeterminate) for the theory-completeness aspect on the rationale "true approximation in many domains, not strictly true vs MR canonical." Brandon's correction is sharper:

- **I (Indeterminate)** = the question's answer is genuinely undetermined (no decisive lean toward T or F)
- **F (False, established-by-counterexample)** = the question's answer IS decisively F because counterexamples have been demonstrated

Binary's claim to completeness is NOT genuinely undetermined — it is DECISIVELY FALSE, because we have demonstrable counterexamples (the MI cases themselves). The MI cases are not merely "uncertainty" about binary's completeness; they are POSITIVE PROOF that binary is incomplete.

**Therefore:** F is the correct theory-completeness aspect label, with the F-MI alignment structurally noting that F is established VIA MI demonstration.

The PROOF of F **IS** the demonstration of MI. The MI cases are not a separate aspect from F; they are the EVIDENCE for F at a different level of description.

## 5. Worked Examples — When Does F Precisely Align With MI?

### 5.1 Binary logic as complete theory — F-MI ALIGNED (Brandon's case)

- F = binary is FALSE as a complete logical framework
- MI = liar/Russell/Gödel paradoxes are inconceivable in 2-valued logic
- Alignment: the MI cases PROVE the F status
- **FMA-1 APPLIES** ✓

### 5.2 Geocentric astronomy as complete theory — F-MI does NOT align

- F = geocentric model is FALSE as physical cosmology
- Counterexamples = observational data (parallax, retrograde motion at non-aligned scales) — these are NOT MI cases (they are I cases at most, with eventual T resolution under heliocentric reframing)
- Alignment: F is established via I/T evidence, NOT MI
- **FMA-1 does NOT apply** — this is plain F-via-empirical-counterexample, not F-via-MI

### 5.3 Naive set theory (pre-Russell) as complete foundational system — F-MI ALIGNED

- F = naive set theory is FALSE as a complete foundation
- MI = Russell's paradox is the MI counterexample
- Alignment: Russell's MI proves the F status
- **FMA-1 APPLIES** ✓

### 5.4 Hilbert's program for complete-and-consistent formalization — F-MI ALIGNED

- F = Hilbert's program FAILS (we cannot have both completeness and consistency in sufficiently strong systems)
- MI = Gödel's incompleteness sentences (and the diagonal-lemma-construction underlying them) are MI cases
- Alignment: Gödel's MI sentences prove the F status of Hilbert's program
- **FMA-1 APPLIES** ✓

### 5.5 Newtonian mechanics as complete physics — F-MI does NOT align

- F = Newtonian mechanics is FALSE as complete physics (relativity + QM)
- Counterexamples = perihelion of Mercury, photoelectric effect, etc. — these are T-under-better-theory cases, NOT MI
- Alignment: F is via better-empirical-theory, NOT via MI demonstration
- **FMA-1 does NOT apply** — this is plain F-via-empirical-revision

### 5.6 Discrimination pattern

**FMA-1 applies SPECIFICALLY to foundational/meta-theoretical claims about completeness or consistency** where the counterexamples ARE inconceivable-within-the-theory-itself (MI cases). It does NOT apply to ordinary empirical-revision F-status claims where counterexamples are I or T-under-better-theory cases.

**Estimated FMA-1 applicability domain:** foundational logic + foundational mathematics + foundational metaphysics where self-referential or paradox-generating cases are decisive. Roughly: anywhere Gödel-style results apply, FMA-1 is potentially active.
