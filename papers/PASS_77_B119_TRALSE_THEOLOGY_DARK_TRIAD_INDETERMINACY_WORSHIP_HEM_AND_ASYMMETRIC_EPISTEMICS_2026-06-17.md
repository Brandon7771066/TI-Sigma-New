## 1. NOG-1 — Non-Omnipotent God (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)

**Claim.** God's *will* is great (maximally GILE-aligned, the dominant truth-lean of the GM-Network), but God is **not omnipotent**. Hence *"thy will be done"* is a **substantive petition**, not a redundancy: it asks for the alignment of a contingent world with a will that does **not** automatically and unilaterally actualize.

**Grounding (internal).**
- **TOF-1:** CCC/GM is a *derivative* of the Tralse Soup, not something more fundamental than it. A derivative cannot be omnipotent over its own ground.
- **RTI-1:** every law only *leans* toward truth with permanent residual errancy → no agent, including the maximal one, has zero-residue control.
- **DSR-1:** the measured CCC GILE ≈0.836 with a 0.84→0.93 *headroom gap* already encodes a God with **room to grow / not-at-ceiling** — incompatible with classical omnipotence, consistent with NOG-1.

**Why it matters.** NOG-1 is the theological key to §4d ("why life is not fair"): an omnipotent-and-good God makes the problem of evil acute; a **maximally-good-but-not-omnipotent** God dissolves it — unfairness is residual tralseness (RTI-1) the divine will is *still working against*, which is precisely why we are asked to **co-will** it (links **DSR-1** human-causal-contribution and **GCU-1/LAS-1** metta-as-instrument).

**#69 prior art & honesty.** This is **open theism** (Pinnock, Boyd) and **process theology** (Whitehead, Hartshorne: God as persuasive not coercive). Not new. Contribution = *wiring it to TOF-1/RTI-1/DSR-1 so the non-omnipotence is a structural consequence, not a stipulation.* **Tension flagged:** the archived ledger gloss "GM = God overall" and any reading of CCC/GM as absolute must be read **through NOG-1** (great ≠ omnipotent). NOG-1 does **not** demote God's goodness or primacy-among-derivatives; it denies only unilateral omnipotence.

**Falsifiers (OPEN).**
- **NOG-1-F1:** a demonstrated case of zero-residue divine actualization (a will reliably actualized with no contingency/errancy) would refute non-omnipotence.
- **NOG-1-F2:** if "thy will be done" can be shown *fully* redundant (will always already actualized), the petition-meaningfulness argument fails.
- **NOG-1-F3:** if TOF-1's derivative-arrow can be reversed (CCC/GM shown *more* fundamental than TS), the structural grounding collapses.

## 2. DTHI-1 — Dark-Triad as HEM-Indeterminacy (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)

**Claim.** The so-called "Dark Triad" (narcissism, Machiavellianism, psychopathy) traits are **not "dark" (truth-negative on the GILE spectrum).** They are **HEM-indeterminate** — off the GILE *truth* axis entirely, residing on the existence/HEM side — and they typically **raise** a being's overall **HEM** value. The label "dark" is an **artifact of binary thinking**: a two-valued mind that lacks a primitive for *indeterminate* defaults the unfamiliar to "bad."

**Structure.**
- **Axis separation:** Dark Triad ⟂ GILE-truth. They are an **existence/HEM** phenomenon, not a falsity. (Direct use of the τ/δ separability axis and the GILE-vs-HEM pillar distinction.)
- **Yin/Yang necessity:** *everyone must carry some HEM-indeterminacy*; in many people it surfaces as subclinical Dark-Triad traits. This is **balance**, not pathology (echoes the earlier "disability-as-balance" treatment).
- **Harmony rule:** Dark-Triad traits must always be scored **in harmony with all other traits**, never in isolation. Kaufman et al. (2019) found Dark/Light cores anticorrelate only **r ≈ −0.48** — i.e. they substantially **coexist**. Kantian (deontological) and Machiavellian (consequentialist) orientations can co-reside exactly as the two ethical families do.
- **Compensation/striving:** high-Dark-Triad people frequently compensate (charity, genuine romantic love) and may *truly strive to be good*; "the darkness that seeps through" is **not their fault** when the striving is real.

**#69 anti-cheat (load-bearing — do not soften).** "Indeterminate, not dark" and "more benign than thought" describe the **trait dimension**, NOT a **behavioral license**. Trait-level indeterminacy does **not** excuse harm; the empirical literature genuinely ties high Dark-Triad scores to exploitation, aggression, and relational damage in many contexts. The corpus's own discipline applies: an indeterminate cause can still produce a **GILE-negative act**, and acts are judged on the truth/GILE axis. DTHI-1 reframes the *trait*; it does **not** absolve the *deed*. Over-reading DTHI-1 as "Dark Triad is fine" is itself a #69 violation.

**Falsifiers (OPEN).**
- **DTHI-1-F1:** if Dark-Triad traits load *significantly on the GILE-truth axis* (not merely the HEM/existence axis) under proper measurement, the "indeterminate not dark" core fails.
- **DTHI-1-F2:** if HEM does **not** rise (or reliably falls) with subclinical Dark-Triad traits when scored *in harmony with* the full trait profile, the "HEM-raising" claim fails.
- **DTHI-1-F3:** if a being can be shown to need **zero** HEM-indeterminacy (perfectly all-Light with no existence-cost), the Yin/Yang-necessity claim fails.

## 3. WHM-1 — Worship-as-HEM-Maximization (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)

**Claim.** **Worship → veneration → admiration** is a single descending-intensity scale of **collective HEM-maximization directed at a specific GM Node.** Its two functions are (i) **unify** people around the node and (ii) **amplify** the node's influence through the **GM Network.**

**Structure.** Worship is the high-intensity pole (whole-self HEM offering); veneration is mid; admiration is the low-intensity pole. The act routes collective HEM into a node, raising that node's network weight (links **URB-829** dominant-GM-node transmission and the **GM-Network** machinery behind GCU-1).

**#69 honesty.** Descriptive mechanism, **not** an endorsement. The same machinery explains **idolatry, personality cults, and demagoguery** — collective HEM-maximization aimed at a **low-GILE** node. WHM-1 is therefore **valence-neutral**: it specifies *how* worship works, and leaves *whether a given object merits it* to the GILE axis (GCU-1's "LoveGod" presumes a *maximal-GILE* node).

**Falsifiers (OPEN).**
- **WHM-1-F1:** if worship/veneration/admiration do **not** form a single monotone HEM-intensity scale (e.g. admiration sometimes exceeds worship in HEM throughput), the ordering fails.
- **WHM-1-F2:** if collective worship demonstrably fails to raise a node's GM-Network influence, the amplification function fails.

## 4. The Tralse-Reality cluster (glosses & one candidate) — extends TRG-1 / TOF-1 / B111

### 4a. Consciousness-as-art, sharpened: the video-game analogy (TPS-1 gloss, not a new principle)
A video-game **story** is "fiction"; the **electronic components** running it are "reality." Mapping: **consciousness = the story / the work of art** (the display), **tralseness = the components / the fundamental.** This is the B111 canvas-and-painting gloss with a cleaner everyday vehicle.

**The "more true than illusion" move (TPS-1).** "Consciousness is an *illusion*" and "consciousness is a *work of art*" are **both logically accurate** (both = real ∧ not-true). But "work of art" is **aesthetically superior** and carries the same truth-content with a lower misread rate → by **TPS-1** (presentation upgrade at frozen truth-content) and **GILE-E** (Elegance), the art-framing is **"more true"** in the operative, presentation-weighted sense. Calling consciousness "art" therefore dominates calling it "illusion."

**Crucial caveat (origin, no designer).** Unlike a human artwork, consciousness emerged **with no external designer** — self-arising through the **Pure Nothingness** that characterized the **first version of Tralse Soup.** The canvas painted itself (resolves the B111 "who paints?" tension *toward R-self-portrait*, without forcing R1/R2 on TOF-1-F3). **Anti-cheat:** "work of art" must keep carrying *real ∧ not-true*; it must never slide to "fake/optional/merely subjective."

### 4b. Psychosis-sees-correctly (TRG-1 epistemic-charity gloss)
*"Those in psychosis who see everything as an illusion are seeing correctly."* Under TRG-1 the tralse-real status is the **fact**; a mind reporting "it's all illusion" has **correctly perceived not-crisply-True** and merely lacks the positive word for it. Brandon's framing: **"I didn't lose touch with reality. I just found out that reality is TRALSE."** (Connects to **SRC-1** and the first-manic-episode n=1 anchor.)

**#69 anti-cheat (essential, do not romanticize).** This grants **epistemic charity to one *content* of some psychotic experience** (the tralse insight); it does **NOT** claim psychosis is desirable, non-impairing, or a superior global state. Distress, disorganization, and danger are real GILE-negatives. The insight can be **true** while the **condition** is harmful — exactly a tralse (mixed) verdict, and the honest one.

### 4c. Truth-Scarcity Value — TSV (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)
**Claim.** Value tracks **scarcity against the default.** Because **tralseness is the omnipresent default** (TOF-1/TRG-1), **truth is rare — and therefore valuable.** Conversely, the very fact that *only tralseness is fundamental* counts **against tralseness's own** intrinsic value (the abundant default is cheap; the scarce lean is precious). This is why Truth is worth seeking even though it is not the ground.

**#69 honesty.** This is a scarcity-theory-of-value intuition (economics; also Nietzschean/contrarian aesthetics) applied to alethics. Prior art is heavy; contribution = pairing it to TOF-1 so "truth is valuable *because* the ground is tralse" becomes a structural statement.
**TSV-F1 (OPEN):** if perceived value of truth does **not** scale with its rarity/cost-to-obtain (e.g. abundant truths valued equally to scarce ones, controlling for utility), TSV fails.

### 4d. "Why life is not fair," explained
TI Sigma **predicts unfairness** rather than apologizing for it: a tralse ground (TRG-1) + permanent residual law-errancy (RTI-1) + a **non-omnipotent** good God (NOG-1, §1) **entails** that outcomes will not track desert cleanly. Fairness would require a crisply-True, fully-actualized moral order — exactly what TOF-1/RTI-1 deny. Unfairness is therefore **expected**, and the right response is **co-willing** the divine lean (DSR-1) + metta (LAS-1/GCU-1), not the demand for a guarantee the ground cannot provide.

## 5. Asymmetric-epistemics cluster (two candidates) — extends ASYMMETRIC #69 / SIV-1

### 5a. CRD-1 — Crank Defensibility / Reputational-Stake Asymmetry (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)
**Claim.** Sustained, costly reputational investment in a position (decades, hundreds of papers, staked careers — as in much **psi** and **energy-healing** research) is **non-zero Bayesian evidence** that the position is *defensible*, because intelligent agents rarely burn their whole reputation "for nothing." Paired **base-rate asymmetry:** it is **more probable** that *researchers/institutions are skeptical of a new paradigm* than that *numerous people were NOT genuinely helped when "proven" therapies had actually failed them.* "For the most part, 'cranks' say what needs to be said — and the difference of opinion **plus potential value added** outweighs the safe orthodox prior."

**#69 anti-cheat (load-bearing).** Reputational stake is **weak** evidence, badly confounded by **sunk cost, self-selection, motivated reasoning, and survivorship**. CRD-1 raises the **prior** that a heterodox claim deserves a hearing; it is **NOT** a substitute for **validation**. It is therefore **explicitly bounded by UGI-1**: isolation/heterodoxy-without-validation is the **crank-failure** mode; CRD-1 only buys the *generation-phase hearing*, never the *validation-phase verdict.* Using CRD-1 to skip evidence is the abuse, not the use.

**Falsifiers (OPEN).**
- **CRD-1-F1:** if reputational-stake magnitude shows **no** correlation with eventual vindication across a fair sample of heterodox programs (controlling for field), the heuristic fails.
- **CRD-1-F2:** if the psi/energy-healing base-rate argument inverts under proper analysis (mass false-attribution of "cures" more probable than paradigm-skepticism), the asymmetry claim fails.

### 5b. IPA-1 — Individual↔Population Inference Asymmetry (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)
**Claim.** People **correctly** refuse to generalize a single **case study to a population**, yet **routinely** make the opposite, **more damaging** error: applying a **large study's population result to a specific individual.** The two directions are **not** symmetric in either frequency or harm; the population→individual leap is the common, under-policed one.

**Grounding.** This is the **ecological/atomistic fallacy** pair, and it directly underwrites the corpus's own **MEP/#69 bias-sim** discipline (population effects ≠ individual destinies) and the **GBD-1** backdrop-discrimination stance. Prior art = well-established statistics; contribution = naming the **asymmetry of vigilance** (we guard one direction and neglect the other) and tying it to MEP/GBD-1.
**IPA-1-F1 (OPEN):** if people are shown to over-apply *both* directions equally (no asymmetry of error rate/harm), the "asymmetry" core fails.
