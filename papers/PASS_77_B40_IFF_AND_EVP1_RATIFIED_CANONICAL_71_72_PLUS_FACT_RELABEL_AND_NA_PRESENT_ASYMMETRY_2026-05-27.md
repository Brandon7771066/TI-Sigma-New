## §2. The "Future" → "Fact" Relabel (Brandon consistency request)

Brandon: *"make it consistent by changing Future to Fact! Except it's not a fact yet since it's in the imagination so far (captured by the I and the fact that it's FORESIGHT about a FUTURE TRUTH STATE)."*

### §2.1 The consistency it achieves

In the B38 triad, the three modes were named by **tense** (Future / Past / Present). But the *target* of all three is a **truth-state — a fact** — held as an imaginary placeholder because the fact is not presently latch-able on the real axis. The relabel renames the future mode by its **target (a fact)** rather than its tense, making all three modes consistent as **"imaginary placeholders for facts (truth-states), differing in the fact's temporal/epistemic status."**

### §2.2 The nuance Brandon pre-empts (it's not a fact *yet*)

The future-mode target is a **fact-to-be**, not a present fact. Its not-yet-actual status is exactly what the **imaginary component I encodes**: a high I = "this is foresight about a future truth-state, not a settled real value." So calling the mode "Fact" does **not** smuggle in premature actuality — the I marks the pending status, and the cognitive operation is **foresight**. The relabel and the caveat are the same point: *the mode points at a fact; the I says the fact hasn't arrived.*

### §2.3 The ratified (relabeled) triad

| Mode (relabeled) | Target (fact / truth-state) | Cognitive operation | Why no real value now | Resolution trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Fact** *(was "Future")* | **future** truth-state (fact-to-be) | **Foresight** | real value does **not yet exist** (ontological-not-yet) | **actualization** (the event happens) |
| **Past** | **past** truth-state (fact-that-was) | Hindsight / retrodiction | real value **exists but is inaccessible** (epistemic-not-recovered) | **recovery** (evidence/memory supplies it) |
| **Present** | **present** truth-state (fact-being-constituted) | Insight / interpretation | real value **is being constituted** (constructive-not-yet-settled) | **interpretation** (construal/decision collapses it) |

One mechanism, three entry conditions, three exit triggers — all targeting facts (truth-states), all marked by I until resolution.

**Naming note:** this is a canonical **relabel of a component of a ratified principle**, not a new principle and not a numbered MR-Truth-Labels refinement; per Pass-65 precedent, relabels/refinements do not change the canonical principle count (held at the post-ratification value). ## §4. N/A-Present First-/Third-Person Asymmetry (Brandon's self-reference insight)

Brandon: *"A mind cannot describe its own state as N/A without stopping its thought process for the pending proposition. Thus, the N/A label for the PRESENT is mainly for another i-cell's working memory. You were right to point out the present's limited applicability for N/A. It's just ontologically important for completeness' sake."*

### §4.1 The asymmetry

The **NA-PRE-DECISION** sub-mode (from NA-1-R1, refinement #11) — "P is pragmatically N/A in working memory *until* a truth-evaluation is computed" — has a **first-person/third-person asymmetry**:

- **First-person (self-attribution): performatively unstable.** A mind cannot stably assert "P is N/A *for me, right now*" about its own currently-pending P, because the act of forming and asserting that N/A-judgment *is itself* an evaluative engagement with P — it **halts the pending/unevaluated state** the label was meant to describe. The label collapses the very condition it asserts. (This is **distinct from MI/inconceivability**: it is *performative self-undermining*, not inconceivability-under-mental-actualization. The proposition is fine; the *self-applied present-tense N/A speech-act* is what's unstable.)

- **Third-person (other-attribution): stable and valid.** i-cell A *can* attribute NA-PRE-DECISION to i-cell B's working memory — "P is currently un-evaluated in B" — without contradiction, because A is not the one holding P pending. **This is the primary home of present-tense N/A.**

### §4.2 Consequences

1. **Vindicates the B38 "weakest leg" disclosure.** I flagged present-tense as IFF's weakest temporal leg (present is partly observed; present cases may be mixed z = a + bi). Brandon's asymmetry explains *why*: first-person present N/A is performatively self-limiting, so the present mode's clean near-imaginary placeholder is the *third-person* case; the first-person present is typically a **mixed** state (partly interpreted while interpreting).
2. **Why it's retained anyway.** Present-tense N/A is **ontologically required for completeness** of the temporal-mode taxonomy (NA-FUT / NA-PST-FORGOTTEN / **NA-PRE-DECISION** / NA-CAT) — dropping it would leave a gap, even though its first-person applicability is narrow.
3. **Compositions:** **SRC-1 (Pass-65/66, self-reference ⇒ consciousness)** — the asymmetry is itself a self-reference phenomenon: the i-cell's inability to N/A-label its own live state is a marker that it *is* the locus of the pending evaluation. **TPS-1, PM-1, CDA-1, UDT-1, NAO-1, NA-1-R1** all compose.
