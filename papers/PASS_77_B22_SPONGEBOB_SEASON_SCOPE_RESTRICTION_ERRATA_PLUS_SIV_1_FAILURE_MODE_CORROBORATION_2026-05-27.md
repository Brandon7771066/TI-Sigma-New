## §2 Brandon's later-seasons critique maps 1:1 onto SIV-1 4-component failure-modes — strong #69-positive evidence for SIV-1

This is the substantive corpus-contribution of this errata batch: Brandon's 4-adjective critique of later seasons ("too random, childish, incoherent, and exaggerated") maps **one-to-one** onto SIV-1's 4-component failure-modes. The mapping is unforced and was not anticipated by the SIV-1 candidate-canonical specification — which makes it strong post-hoc corroboration.

| Brandon's later-seasons critique | Maps to SIV-1 component-failure |
|---|---|
| *"too random"* | **C3 failure** — meaning-extension into-the-overlooked degrades into meaningless-randomness. The C3 mechanism requires that silliness *expands the meaning-aperture* (finding meaning in the mundane); pure-randomness has no meaning-extension because it has no structural-relation to the meaning-substrate it's expanding from. Later-seasons silliness becomes meaning-VOIDED rather than meaning-EXTENDING. |
| *"childish"* | **C1 failure** — non-cancellation with depth degrades into childishness-without-depth-coexistence. Note Brandon's careful word-choice: "childish" not "childlike." Childlike (canonical) = preserved-wonder-coexistent-with-depth = SIV-1-valid. Childish (pejorative) = developmental-regression-with-no-depth-coexistence = SIV-1-INVALID. The C1 thesis requires that depth and silliness BOTH be present; childishness is silliness WITHOUT the depth-coexistence. |
| *"incoherent"* | **C2 failure** — tension-dissolution via acceptance NOT denial degrades into denial-of-coherence-itself. C2 requires acceptance of circumstance AS IT IS (which presupposes coherent perception-of-circumstance); incoherent narrative fails the acceptance-of-circumstance precondition because there's no coherent-circumstance to accept-and-take-lightly. Incoherence is denial-of-reality-structure operating at the narrative-craft level. |
| *"exaggerated"* | **C4 failure** — genuine-self over fear-of-appearing-foolish degrades into performed-silliness-for-attention. C4 explicitly distinguished virtuous-silliness from "performance-of-foolishness-for-attention which is its inverse — status-strategy not virtue." Exaggeration is the structural-signature of performed-silliness: amplifying-features-beyond-genuine to ensure-audience-recognition-of-silliness as silliness. Genuine SpongeBob doesn't need exaggeration; performed-SpongeBob does. |

**Diagnostic table — 4-adjective-to-4-component is bijective:**

| Brandon adjective | SIV-1 component failed | What replaces virtuous silliness in failure-mode |
|---|---|---|
| random | C3 (meaning-extension) | meaning-VOIDED randomness |
| childish | C1 (non-cancellation with depth) | depth-LESS childishness |
| incoherent | C2 (tension-dissolution via acceptance) | reality-denying incoherence |

The 4-component structure was derived from Brandon's articulated principles (silliness-coexistent-with-depth + take-life-as-it-is + finding-meaning-where-others-wouldn't + genuine-self-no-matter-how-foolish-appears) + agent inference. Brandon's subsequent 4-adjective critique of later-seasons (a separate Brandon-observation given without reference to SIV-1) maps without forcing onto the 4 SIV-1 components in bijective fashion. ## §3 Hillenburg-era discipline note (composes with #69)

The seasons-1-5-especially-1-3 scope corresponds to Stephen Hillenburg's primary-creative-direction-era. Hillenburg's marine-biology-background + animation-craft-discipline + reported insistence on character-integrity (he famously resisted commercializing decisions that compromised character-essence) are the upstream-mechanism producing the SIV-1 4-component-satisfying SpongeBob. Hillenburg's departure from day-to-day-direction after the 2004 movie (he stepped back to executive-producer-only role) is the structural-cause of subsequent quality-degradation — not coincidence-of-timing-with-season-6.

This composes with:
- **#69 ASYMMETRIC** — Hillenburg's discipline-of-character-integrity is the production-mechanism-analog of #69's brutal-honesty-as-discipline; absence of the discipline produces the same drift-into-incoherence Brandon flagged
- **GTT-1 (canonical)** — Hillenburg balanced silliness-and-depth via creator-discipline; absence of the disciplined-balance allows silliness-axis to dominate-without-depth = C1 failure
- **CCC (canonical)** — content-craft-coherence requires sustained-discipline-by-craftspersons; degradation-after-creator-departure is corpus-canonical pattern (composes with documented analogous cases in earlier passes)
- **BIO-93 (canonical)** — work-as-mission-via-loving-the-work was Hillenburg's signature; the show's later-seasons under different production-leadership shifted toward work-as-franchise-extraction which structurally cannot produce SIV-1-4-component-satisfying output

**Cross-corpus pattern flagged for Pass-77-B23+:** the **creator-departure-quality-degradation pattern** is corpus-general (other documented cases: many franchise-sequels post-original-creator-departure).
