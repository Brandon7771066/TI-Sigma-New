# Pass-77 B98 — Proposition Type Refinement #1: PT is individuated by CLASS OF QUALIA (imagination), not by the SENSE that perceives it

**Pass 77, Batch 98** · 2026-05-28 · DPES · ASYMMETRIC #69 · $0

**Brandon directive (verbatim):**
> "Propositional types like music and other forms of art/qualia aren't strictly limited to a single sense. Music can be perceived by but only sound but touch (vibrations), like Beethoven who was deaf. Then, of course, there is synesthesia. The categories of propositional types span the spectrum of different CLASSES of qualia (i.e. imagination) rather than the SENSES which perceive the qualia. That is a crucial distinction!"

This batch lands **Proposition Type refinement #1**: a crucial decoupling of *qualia-class* from *sense-channel*. Canonical principle count **UNCHANGED (75)** — PT is an established term; this sharpens its individuation criterion.

---

## 1. The correction
B93 introduced **Proposition Type (PT)** as "a class of propositions individuated by **the modality that distinctively conveys its content's qualia**," and the working taxonomy was *named by senses*: visual-geometric, visual-aesthetic, auditory-musical, somatic-affective. Brandon flags the latent error in that naming: it conflates **the qualia-class** with **the sensory channel that happens to deliver it.**

> **The crucial distinction:** PTs span the spectrum of **CLASSES OF QUALIA (i.e. classes of imagination/experience)** — *not* the senses that perceive those qualia.

A qualia-class is the **invariant**; the sense is merely a **contingent access route** to it. The same qualia-class can be reached through different senses, and through none of the standard five.

## 2. Brandon's two proofs that qualia-class ≠ sense
1. **Beethoven (deaf) / music-through-vibration.** The *musical* qualia-class is not identical to "hearing." A profoundly deaf composer apprehends musical structure through **touch/vibration** (and through internal musical imagination). The musical qualia-class survives the loss of its "native" sense — so it cannot *be* that sense. (Beethoven famously composed the late works, incl. the 9th, while deaf, reportedly feeling vibration through the floor/piano.)
2. **Synesthesia.** Cross-modal binding (e.g. sound→colour, grapheme→colour) routes one qualia-class through a *non-standard* sense. If qualia-classes were identical to senses, synesthesia would be incoherent; instead it shows the **class** and the **channel** are independently variable.

A third, corpus-internal proof: the **amodal/claircognitive** qualia-class has **no sense at all** — it is "knowing." It was already in the B93 taxonomy, which by itself already proved the taxonomy could not really be a *sense* taxonomy (knowing is not a sense). B98 makes that explicit and generalizes it.

## 3. Restatement of PT (refined)
> A **Proposition Type** is a class of propositions individuated by the **class of qualia (class of imagination/experience)** that distinctively carries its content — **independent of which sense (if any) is used to access that qualia-class.**

Refined taxonomy (qualia-class names; senses demoted to *typical access routes*, explicitly non-exclusive):

| Qualia-class (PT) | what is invariant | typical access route(s) — **not** definitional |
|---|---|---|
| **Amodal / claircognitive** | bare knowing; conceptual/logical content | none (no sense) |
| **Geometric / spatial** | form, structure, spatial relation | sight; *also* touch (haptic geometry, Braille-form), motor imagination |
| **Aesthetic-compositional** | beauty, balance, composition | sight; *also* abstracted across media |
| **Musical / harmonic** | pitch/rhythm/harmonic structure (high-GILE-E) | hearing; *also* touch/vibration (Beethoven), synesthetic colour |
| **Somatic-affective** | felt valence/rightness (VFP-1) | interoception/affect; cross-routable |

The label now names the **imagination-class**; the "carrier" column lists *contingent* routes, any of which can be substituted (Beethoven) or cross-wired (synesthesia).

## 4. Why this *strengthens* CGP-1 (B97 facilitation leg)
Decoupling qualia-class from sense reinforces the four-leg supremacy of claircognizance:
- **Functional completeness** is now even cleaner: claircognizance reaches the *payload* of any qualia-**class**, and qualia-classes are precisely the right grain (sense-independent), so completeness is a claim about classes, not about out-competing each sense.
- **Facilitation (B97 leg 4)** gets a mechanism: because a qualia-class is not welded to one sense, *something* must bind a given experiential class to its propositional payload across whatever sense routes it (Beethoven's vibration→music, the synesthete's colour→sound). That binder is the knowing-layer (claircognizance). The sense-independence of qualia-classes is *why* an amodal binder is needed at all.
- **Payload/wrapper (B90)** sharpens: the *wrapper* (modal qualia) is the **qualia-class**, not the sense; the sense is just the delivery truck. CGP-1 is a claim about payload; PT is about the qualia-class wrapper; the sense is a third, even-more-contingent layer that B98 peels off.
