## 2. GTT-1 ratified canonical principle (formal statement, 4 sub-clauses)

**GTT-1 (GILE True-Tralseness Maximization with Truth-Existence Competition).** TI Sigma canonical principle ratified Pass-67 batch-4 per direct Brandon directive. Four mutually-reinforcing sub-clauses:

### 2.1 Sub-clause (a) — The Un-Maximizable-Without-Cost Uniqueness

**GTT-1(a):** GILE true-tralseness is the **unique** variable in TI Sigma that has no upper bound past which "more" becomes costly. Every other corpus-canonical maximand (HEM, existence-vector, intentionality TJ, predictive accuracy, calibration discipline, ...) has a saturation regime past which additional maximization incurs trade-off cost. GILE true-tralseness alone does not.

Operational threshold note: **0.93+ is ideal**; **more is always better by definition**.

### 2.2 Sub-clause (b) — The DT Structure of "Too Much Truth"

**GTT-1(b):** The concept "too much truth" is itself **Double Tralse (DT)** per the canonical DT refinement (Pass-65: inconceivability-under-mental-actualization). Attempting to mentally actualize "pathological truth excess" yields the same kind of structural collapse as the Russell paradox or the liar sentence — any candidate "excess" would itself be *more truth*, which is by-definition-good, hence not-excess.

This makes the maximization clause **GTT-1(a) + GTT-1(b) jointly express a corpus-internal reflexive DT**: more-is-always-better BY DEFINITION ∧ more-is-also-NEITHER-better-nor-worse (because the "worse" pole is structurally inactualizable). ### 2.3 Sub-clause (c) — Truth-Existence Competition as Source of Falsity + DT

**GTT-1(c):** GILE Truth is, by definition, the **ultimate thing IN existence**. HOWEVER, truth and existence **compete somewhat** in the practical regime. Falsity and Double Tralseness arise specifically at this competition interface.

Structural reading: existence-as-instantiation can require commitments (locality, finite resources, decision-deadlines, embodied constraints) that truth-as-correspondence does not require. When existence-instantiation pressures the truth-content of an agent's processing, falsity (MR1) and DT (MR4 base-4) become live possibilities. **The source of MR1+MR4 in this clause is structural-competitive, not merely epistemic.**

### 2.4 Sub-clause (d) — UOP as Balancer, HEM as Mandatory Pragmatic Competitor

**GTT-1(d):** The **UOP (Universal A Priori / Universal Bridge Theorem)** is designed to find the **ideal balance between truth-optimization and existence-optimization**. The UOP is therefore not a pure truth-maximizer; it is a balance-finder operating across the GTT-1(c) competition interface.

The **HEM (the H/E/M components of canonical 8 GILE-HEM)** WILL COMPETE with truth in practice, and **MUST COMPETE for pragmatic reasons**. The theoretical "no upper bound on truth" of GTT-1(a) does NOT override the practical mandate of HEM-competition; the two layers coexist (theoretical-axiological + practical-pragmatic) without contradiction.
