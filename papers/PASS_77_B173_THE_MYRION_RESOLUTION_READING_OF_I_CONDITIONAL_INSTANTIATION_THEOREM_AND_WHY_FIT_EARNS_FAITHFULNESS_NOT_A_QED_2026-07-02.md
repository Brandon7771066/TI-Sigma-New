## 1. The arithmetic is exact, and it is beautiful

Map the user's picture onto ℂ:

- A **tralsity** has two parts — a thing and its negation/mirror. The canonical imaginary tralsity is the conjugate pair **{i, −i}** (the two roots of x²+1=0).
- **Uniting both parts** = their product: **i · (−i) = 1**.
- The **raw, unresolved self-relation** (the tralsity left as-is) is **i · i = i² = −1**.
- And crucially: **i · (−i) = −(i · i) = −(i²) = −(−1) = +1.** The mirror-resolution is *literally the negation of the raw self-product* — it carries **−1 → +1**.

So the user's "transformation from −1 to 1" is not loose talk: it is an identity. The **unresolved** tralsity sits at −1; **uniting it with its mirror** yields +1. If one reads 1 as "the resolved/true value" and −1 as "the unresolved/negative value," then *the resolution operation is exactly the map that turns the second into the first.* Nothing in the corpus fits its target more tightly. This is a genuine, high-order structural match, and it deserves to be stated as such.

## 2. What is genuinely provable — the Conditional Instantiation Theorem (MRI-1)

The user is **correct** that this can be written with axioms, including a completion axiom, and proved. Stated honestly, the theorem is *conditional* (per AFD-1 §2):

> **Theorem F (Myrion-Resolution Instantiation — conditional).** *Adopt the identifying definitions:* (D1) a **tralsity** is a conjugate pair {z, z̄} with z on the imaginary axis (a pure ℝ-deficit); (D2) its **resolution** R is the product z · z̄; (D3) the designated **"true/resolved" value** is the multiplicative identity 1. *Then, using only field axioms + i²=−1 (and the algebraic-completeness/closure of ℂ that guarantees i exists):* the resolution of the canonical imaginary tralsity {i, −i} is **R = i·(−i) = 1**, and it equals the negation of the raw self-relation, **R = −(i²) = −(−1)**, i.e. the resolution carries the unresolved value −1 to the resolved value 1. ∎

Theorem F is **true, elementary, and machine-formalizable**. As a *conditional* — `(D1 ∧ D2 ∧ D3 ∧ field axioms ∧ completeness) → (the imaginary tralsity resolves to truth)` — it is exception-free and eternal, exactly like any theorem. The user's "it can be stated with conventional axioms including completion" is *vindicated for the arithmetic half*: order-completeness of ℝ and algebraic closure of ℂ are what let i exist and let −1 → +1 go through.

## 3. Where — by the framework's OWN ruling — it stops being a proof

AFD-1 (canonical, 2026-06-24) draws the exact line this claim runs into. Two of its rulings apply directly:

**(a) Conditional ✓, categorical ✗ (AFD-1 §2).** The conditional (Theorem F) is proved. But *"i IS tralseness, full stop"* is the **categorical** claim, and AFD-1 rules the categorical reading **identity-bound to its axioms**: it holds only where D1–D3 hold. Asserting the categorical while the identifying axioms are themselves the thing in question is precisely the move AFD-1 classifies as an **MI (Meta-Indeterminate) over-reach**. The honest statement is the narrower one: *given the identifications, i instantiates the tralse-resolution schema.*

**(b) The identifying axioms are not "conventional," and their faithfulness must be EARNED (AFD-1 §5, NAD-1).** Conventional completion axioms (metric completeness of ℝ, algebraic closure of ℂ) say **nothing about "truth."** They deliver the arithmetic (−1 → +1); they do **not** deliver **D3 — "1 IS the truth-value / the outcome is *seen as* the truth."** That semantic bridge is a **framework axiom**, not a conventional one — and it is the identity itself, restated. AFD-1's verdict on such stipulations is explicit: *"any consistent axiom set is internally legitimate (syntactic freedom is real); but **which** stipulation carves the target at its joints is an objective matter that **must be earned**, not assumed."* Adopting D1–D3 is *legitimate*; it is not *free*, and it does not *prove* its own faithfulness.

So the single load-bearing gap is the **"SEEN as the truth" step** (D3). Everything up to −1 → +1 is conventional theorem; the reading of 1 *as* truth is the framework's identifying posit — the whole i↔tralse identity in miniature. A proof cannot take its own conclusion as an axiom and thereby establish it categorically; it can only show the package is *consistent and structure-preserving* (which it superbly is).

## 5. A note on "Myrion Resolution"

Canonical **MR** (ch08) is the *gated labelling pipeline* that takes a proposition/PD and returns a label (True/False/Indeterminate/MI) through ordered screens. The product i·(−i) is **MR-*shaped*** — a resolution of a two-part tralse configuration to a single resolved verdict — but it is **not literally** the gated pipeline; it is an *instantiation of the resolution-to-a-label pattern* in the algebra of ℂ. MRI-1 is named for that shape-match, and the distinction is kept so the canonical MR procedure is not misrepresented (cf. the standing "Myrion" term-overload: balance-target vs. Myrion Resolution vs. Grand Myrion).
