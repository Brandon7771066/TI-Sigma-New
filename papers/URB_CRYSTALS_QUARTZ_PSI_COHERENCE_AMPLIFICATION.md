## 1. The Physical Properties of Quartz That Matter for Psi

### 1.1 Piezoelectricity

Quartz (crystalline SiO₂) is the canonical piezoelectric material:
- Mechanical stress → electrical voltage (direct piezoelectric effect)
- Electrical field → mechanical deformation (converse piezoelectric effect)
- The effect arises because the SiO₄ tetrahedra in the quartz lattice have a non-centrosymmetric arrangement — when compressed, the charge centers separate, creating a dipole moment across the crystal

**Why this matters for φ:** Subtle mechanical deformations from acoustic fields (including very low amplitude vibrations from human presence, speech, breathing patterns, and HRV-correlated pressure waves) are transduced by quartz into electrical signals.
