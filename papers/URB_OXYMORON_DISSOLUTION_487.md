## Abstract

There are no true oxymorons. Every expression we call an oxymoron — "deafening silence," "living death," "cruel kindness," "jumbo shrimp," "necessary contingency" — is only *perceived* as self-contradictory from within a particular formal system or conceptual framework. By the Tralse Limit Theorem, τ(P) > 0 strictly for all P — nothing is perfectly, absolutely contradictory. By the Mathematical Subordination (URB #486), logical contradiction is a feature of specific formal systems, not of CCC-level reality. By Myrion Resolution, every apparent contradiction has a level at which both poles are simultaneously coherent without remainder. **An oxymoron is a Myrion Resolution candidate that has not yet been elevated to its resolving level.** Nothing more.

## 1. The Claim

> Any oxymoron is only something we THINK is an oxymoron.

Formally: for any expression E judged to be self-contradictory within formal system F:

> ∃ level L > F : at L, both poles of E are simultaneously coherent

The contradiction is a property of F. Not of E. Not of reality. Not of CCC.

## 2. Why — The Three-Part Proof

### 2.1 Tralse Limit Theorem (URB #485)

τ(P) ∈ (0,1) strictly for all P. This means: no proposition has τ = 0 — perfect, absolute falsity or contradiction is unreachable. An oxymoron O claims τ(O) = 0: "this expression is perfectly self-canceling, with no coherent content." But by TLT, τ(O) > 0. The oxymoron has non-zero truth content. The contradiction is not absolute.

### 2.2 Mathematical Subordination (URB #486)

Logical contradiction — the Law of Non-Contradiction (¬(P ∧ ¬P)) — is a feature of specific formal systems. It holds within classical logic. It fails in:
- **Paraconsistent logic** — formal systems that tolerate contradictions without explosion
- **Dialethism** — the philosophical position that some contradictions are genuinely true
- **Quantum superposition** — wave AND particle, simultaneously, without contradiction, within quantum mechanics
- **The Tralse itself** — Pole A and Pole B simultaneously, at different depths

Since formal contradiction is context-dependent (URB #486), what appears contradictory within F may be perfectly coherent within a broader or different F'. CCC, operating above all formal systems, instantiates whatever F' is required to make any apparent oxymoron coherent.

### 2.3 Myrion Resolution

The Myrion Resolution (MR) is TI Sigma's standard tool for apparent contradictions: when two poles appear mutually exclusive, MR finds the level at which both are simultaneously true. Every oxymoron is a pair of poles that appear to exclude each other within a given framework. MR guarantees the existence of a resolving level for every such pair — because CCC contains all levels, and CCC does not recognize ultimate contradiction.
