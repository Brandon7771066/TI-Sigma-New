## 1. URR-1 — UOP Ratio-Reframing & the Thirds Regime *(CANDIDATE)*

### 1.1 Statement
Specify the UOP domain dial as the field's own HEM-GILE (Truth/Existence) ratio:

> **ρ = T_d / (1 − T_d)**, with `T_d` a FIXED property of the domain (its truth-importance), **distinct** from the action-truth `T` being optimized.

Then the Myrion objective and its optimum are

> **Myrion\*  =  max₀≤T≤1 [ ρ · ln(1+T) + ln(2−T) ]**,  ρ = T_d/(1−T_d)
> **cap  =  T\*  =  (2ρ−1)/(1+ρ)  =  3·T_d − 1.**

### 1.2 The three regimes (split at the thirds)
| Regime | Condition | Cap | Balanced-ternary tag |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existence-only | `T_d ≤ 1/3` | `T* = 0` | **−** |
| Balanced (Myrion) | `1/3 < T_d < 2/3` | `0 < T* < 1` | **0** |
| Truth-saturated | `T_d ≥ 2/3` | `T* = 1` (SAC-1) | **+** |

Mathematics sits at `T_d ≈ 0.644 → cap ≈ 0.932`, recovering the classic Radiant cap with nothing about "0.93" written anywhere. Verified numerically: max |numeric − closed-form| over the interior band = **1.4 × 10⁻⁷**; boundaries land at exactly cap(1/3)=0 and cap(2/3)=1.

### 1.3 Where the "3" comes from (honest provenance)
The balance condition is `ρ·(2−T) = (1+T)`. The **2** is the existence side (`1 + Existence = 1 + (1−T) = 2 − T`); the **1** is the truth side (`1 + T`). Substituting ρ and solving, `2·T_d + 1·T_d = 3·T_d`. So **3 = 2 (two-sided existence floor) + 1 (truth pole)** — the third thing is *born from one-against-two*. This is elegant **given the log forms**, not a law of nature.

### 1.4 Contingencies (the explicit ledger the author requested)
1. **Log shapes** `ln(1+T)`, `ln(1+H)` — the "3" and the thirds are artifacts of these curvatures.
2. **Unit linear tradeoff** `H = 1 − T` (HRR-1 residual identity); other rates move the boundaries off 1/3, 2/3.
3. **T_d fixed and distinct from T** — using one symbol collapses the cap (it runs to T=1). [Established B144 thread.]
4. **Holistic aggregate** — one scalar T, not per-dimension GILE.
5. **Trades one posit for another** — the original cap came from `λ=2` (the `e⁻²` floor); this route needs `T_d ≈ 0.644`. They agree only because `3·0.644−1 ≈ 1−½e⁻²`. **Consistency if T_d is justified independently; circular if T_d was picked to hit 0.93.** (Most important honesty flag.)
6. **Validity window** `T_d ∈ (1/3, 2/3)` — outside it the "cap" is a boundary, not interior.
7. **Candidate status** — count stays 79.

**Falsifier URR-1-F1:** exhibit a domain whose Truth/Existence weighting is independently measurable, whose truth-utility is genuinely non-logarithmic, and whose optimal truth-cap nonetheless equals `3·T_d − 1` — that would show the relation is *not* form-contingent (current claim: it IS).
