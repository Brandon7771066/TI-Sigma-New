## 2. The Two Architectures Briefly

### 2.1 L*/+E (earlier model)

**Structure**: Splits the framework into two macro-clusters:
- **L*** — encompasses Goodness, Intuition, Love (GIL) treated as one composite — the "qualitative-relational-experiential" cluster
- **+E** — Environment treated separately as the substrate-contextual layer
- **Compresses BOK** — Being, Other, Knowledge are not individuated; they're folded into the L* cluster

**Strengths**: simple binary partition; intuitive for non-specialists; matches everyday language ("the qualitative" vs "the contextual").

**Weaknesses**: loses internal structure of GIL (Goodness ≠ Intuition ≠ Love operationally); compresses BOK's three-fold structure; does not align with the framework's later 64D GILE Matrix.

### 2.2 Truth vs Existence axis (URB #743)

**Structure**: Splits framework operations into two orthogonal axes:
- **Truth axis** — MR (Myrion Resolution) + PD (Permissibility Distribution) pillars; the **logical-resolution** dimension
- **Existence axis** — HEAR (Holistic Existence Amplification Razor) pillar + HEM (Holistic Existence Matrix) substrate; the **ontological-substrate** dimension

**Strengths**: aligns with the three operational pillars (MR, PD, HEAR); maps onto BOK individually (Being↔Existence, Other↔Truth/Existence interface, Knowledge↔Truth); operationalizes the Emerick Threshold gating (URB #756 §6.1: E_T is the Truth-axis activation threshold).

**Weaknesses**: more abstract than L*/+E; requires familiarity with the framework's pillar structure; not as intuitive for first encounters.

## 3. The Compatibility Mapping

### 3.1 Direct mapping

| L*/+E component | Truth/Existence equivalent | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **L*** (GIL composite) | **Primarily Truth axis** + secondarily Existence | GIL operates on the Truth axis (intuition, value, relational-meaning) but is GROUNDED in Existence (substrate). |
| **+E** (Environment) | **Existence axis** | Environment IS the substrate-contextual layer the Existence axis names. |

### 3.2 The coarse-refinement structure

The L*/+E model is a **coarsening** of Truth/Existence in two ways:

1. **It projects the Truth axis onto an "L*" composite** without separating G/I/L individually, while the Truth/Existence architecture preserves the four GILE sub-dimensions.

2. **It identifies E narrowly with environmental substrate** while the Truth/Existence architecture distinguishes E (Environment, a GILE sub-dimension) from the broader Existence axis (HEM substrate generally).

In category-theoretic terms: **there exists a forgetful functor F: (Truth/Existence) → (L*/+E)** that loses GIL internal structure and conflates two notions of E. The functor is well-defined; therefore the two architectures are **compatible in the coarsening direction**.

**The reverse direction is NOT well-defined** — recovering the full Truth/Existence structure from L*/+E requires the additional information that L*/+E throws away. So the architectures are NOT equivalent — they're nested.

## 4. Where L*/+E Retains Independent Value

### 4.1 As a teaching/onboarding tool
L*/+E's binary partition is **far easier for first-time encounters with the framework**. Many practitioners-in-training will find L*/+E digestible where Truth/Existence is initially overwhelming.

### 4.2 As a quick-decision heuristic
For rapid practitioner decisions ("is this an L* concern or an +E concern?"), L*/+E is faster than running the full Truth/Existence pillar analysis. It's a **first-cut filter** in clinical or applied settings.

### 4.3 As a diagnostic for over-Truth-axis or under-Existence-axis bias
Some framework users may **over-emphasize L*** (qualitative-relational analysis) and **under-engage +E** (substrate-grounding). L*/+E's binary structure makes this imbalance visible faster than Truth/Existence's more nuanced architecture.

### 4.4 In specific applied contexts
In **Mood Amplifier safety analysis** — the framework's central application — the L*/+E decomposition may map cleanly onto:
- L* = the user's experiential/emotional dimensions to amplify
- +E = the substrate (biometric, environmental) safety constraints

This applied-domain match preserves L*/+E's practical utility.
