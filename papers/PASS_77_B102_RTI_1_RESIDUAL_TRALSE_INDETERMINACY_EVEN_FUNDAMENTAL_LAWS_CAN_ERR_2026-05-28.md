## 1. The claim
> **RTI-1 (candidate).** Because every law and regularity is a *derivative* of the Tralse Soup (TOF-1) and reality only *leans* toward truth (UDT-1) rather than ever reaching classical-True necessity, **every law retains a nonzero residual tralseness.** Consequently (a) **anything can in principle happen or "err"** — including the most fundamental laws; (b) **stability is real but contingent** — it exists because the truth-lean is *far enough* to make deviations vanishingly rare, not because deviation is impossible; and (c) **the residue is permanent** — the imperfection/unpredictability can be minimized but never driven to zero, and **always will remain**.

Three parts, stated precisely:
- **(a) In-principle possibility ≠ in-practice expectation.** "Anything can happen" means *nonzero tralse-probability*, not "expect it." The residue is tiny by construction (see §2). RTI-1 is emphatically **not** a license for routine miracles (see the #69 guard, §4).
- **(b) Stability = sufficient lean.** A law is a strong directional lean toward MR3-True, reliable enough to ground a stable universe — but it never becomes MR3-True-*without-residue*. The lean buys stability; it does not buy necessity.
- **(c) Permanence.** Per the canonical **minimum Tralse zone**, the residue has a floor it cannot cross. "Always will" is the irreducibility clause.

## 3. What is *distinctively* claimed (#69 honesty about novelty)
Mainstream physics **already** grants ontological indeterminacy of *states* (quantum mechanics). RTI-1 must not pretend to have discovered that. Its distinctive, non-trivial content is two extensions:
1. **From states to laws.** Standard QM keeps the *laws* (e.g. the Schrödinger equation, conservation laws) exact while states are probabilistic. RTI-1 claims the **laws themselves** carry residual tralseness — they are extremely-strong leans, not exceptionless necessities. This is a stronger, riskier claim than textbook indeterminacy.
2. **Permanence as a theorem-shaped clause, not a mood.** "Always will" is grounded in the minimum-Tralse-zone *floor*: the residue is bounded *below* away from zero, so no future theory, however refined, removes it. Determinism is therefore not merely unknown-to-be-true but **structurally precluded**.

## 4. The #69 anti-cheat guard (the load-bearing discipline)
"Anything can happen" is one sloppy step from unfalsifiable miracle-talk. RTI-1 is only admissible **with** the following guard, which is part of the principle, not a footnote:
- **Residue must stay vanishingly rare.** The same lean that *permits* deviation *requires* it to be near-zero-frequency — otherwise the stability RTI-1(b) depends on collapses. **A framing that predicts frequent macroscopic law-violation refutes itself** (it destroys the stability premise).
- **No post-hoc wildcard use.** RTI-1 may **not** be invoked to "explain" ordinary or merely-surprising anomalies (those have ordinary causes). Reaching for residual tralseness to cover a common event is a misuse that *counts as a refutation of that application*, not a confirmation of RTI-1.
- **Directional commitment required.** To stay distinct from strict determinism, RTI-1 must point at *something*: consistency with observed quantum indeterminacy + fat-tailed (non-Gaussian) extreme-event statistics, and the prediction that no exceptionless zero-residue law will ever be exhibited. A version of RTI-1 that forbids no observation and predicts no tendency is **vacuous and therefore failed** (LITERAL anti-cheat, per Pass-45 §11 precedent).
