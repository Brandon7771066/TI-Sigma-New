## 1. The Tetrad–Truth Identity (TTI-1, CANDIDATE)

**Claim (author's).** The four powers of `i` — the C4 i-Cell `{1, i, −1, −i}` — carry a second, independent interpretation as the canonical base-4 truth labels:

| Power of `i` | Truth label | Axis role |
|---|---|---|
| `+1` | **True** | real, determinate |
| `−1` | **False** | real, determinate (negation of True) |
| `+i` | **Indeterminate** | imaginary, indeterminate (leeway *present*) |
| `−i` | **Meta-Indeterminate (MI)** | imaginary, indeterminate (leeway *annihilated*) |

**Why this is NOT post-hoc numerology (the NAD-1 defence).** The mapping is *forced* by two facts the corpus already ratified independently, before this batch:

1. **Real axis = determinate truth.** `papers/QUANTUM_LOGIC_DIMENSIONAL_RECONCILIATION.md` §4 already fixes `1 = TRUE`, `−1 = FALSE` (negation = ×(−1)). Established prior art.
2. **Imaginary axis = the indeterminacy / MI-Tralse modality.** This is canonical refinement #8 ("PD as ONE 1-D indeterminate spectrum with real + imaginary aspects") and PDR-1's Complex representation ("imaginary = MI/Tralse"), where the imaginary axis was *the decisive upgrade* in the 500-prop rater benchmark (NA-blind 0.746 → NA-holding 0.918–0.922).

Given (1)+(2), the only open question is *how the two indeterminate labels sit on the imaginary axis* — and the natural assignment is `+i = Indeterminate` / `−i = MI`, because they are **complex conjugates** and the corpus already demarcates the pair by *leeway* (refinement #17): leeway present → Indeterminate; leeway annihilated ("fully…fully") → MI. Conjugation `z ↦ z̄` (which swaps `+i ↔ −i` and fixes `1, −1`) is then read as **"flip the indeterminacy modality, leave determinate truth untouched."** That is a genuinely meaningful operator, not a coincidence.

**This SUPERSEDES (refines) the old coarse mapping.** The QUANTUM_LOGIC paper predates base-4 ratification and used `i = MI` with the whole imaginary axis labelled "MI ↔ −MI" — i.e. it lumped *all* indeterminacy under "MI" in a 3-valued (True/False/Tralse) scheme. TTI-1's contribution is precisely to **split that axis** to match the ratified base-4 set `{True, False, Indeterminate, MI}`. **ERRATA flag:** the QUANTUM_LOGIC paper's `i = MI` should be read as legacy/coarse; the current label for `+i` is **Indeterminate**, with MI demoted to `−i`.

**Operator readings (structural; honesty-tagged).**
- `×(−1)` (negation): `T↔F` and `I↔MI`. The `T↔F` half is intuitive (standard negation). The `I↔MI` half is **algebra-driven, not yet semantically argued** — flagged as a structural artifact pending a reason why ¬Indeterminate should equal MI.
- conjugation: `I↔MI`, fixes `T,F` — semantically clean (modality flip).
- `×i` (the i-Cell generator): `T→I→F→MI→T`, a 4-cycle "truth rotation." The `T→I→F` arc (truth degrading through indeterminacy to falsity) is suggestive; the `F→MI→T` return arc is **not** independently motivated and is flagged as just-the-cycle.

**Relation to PD thresholds (author: "independent but related").** Correct and worth stating precisely: the truth **labels** live on the *categorical* MR axis (base-4), whereas PD thresholds live on the *graded* PD axis (the indeterminate spectrum). TTI-1 links the **four categorical corners** to **four fixed points in the complex plane**; it does **not** claim the continuous PD threshold values are powers of `i`. They are different axes (per the 4-axis architecture) that happen to share the complex plane as a drawing surface. Independent, related, not identical — exactly the author's framing.

**Falsifier TTI-1-F1 (anti-numerology, OPEN).** TTI-1 must **predict a new, independently-checkable fact** — e.g. a truth-label *transition law* derived from the `×i` / conjugation dynamics that an outcome-blind rater study confirms (does real labelling traffic actually move `T→I→F` more than `T→F` directly?). Until it predicts rather than relabels, TTI-1 is an **interpretive overlay**, like the B135 8-constant map that failed its permutation test — coherent, grounded, but not yet evidence.

**Demarcation note (term-overload guard).** The SAME C4 already carries the i-Cell ↔ **GILE** `{G,I,L,E}` reading (URB_371/URB_670). So `{1,i,−1,−i}` now hosts (at least) two coordinate systems: **GILE tetrad** and **truth-label tetrad**. These must be kept demarcated and never conflated (cf. the Myrion-overload hazard). They are two *charts* on one group, not one claim.

## 2. Concession + correction on Hume — Consciousness Instantiates the Ought (CIO-1, CANDIDATE)

**First, a concession (EVD-1).** B135's *technical* result was narrow and stands: a **value-free** simulation cannot bootstrap value (MORAL-F1 failed; the gap is *relocated* into the injected objective). But to the extent B135 carried the *spirit* "math therefore cannot dissolve Hume," **that spirit was over-strong, and the author is right to reject it.** "A value-free dynamics did not spontaneously select Myrion in one sim" does **not** entail "no mathematical account can dissolve the is→ought gap." Those are different claims; B135 earned only the first. This batch withdraws the stronger reading.

**The author's route (CIO-1).** *Consciousness instantiates the ought from objectively existing GILE values.* Intentionality — the felt sense of ought and of free will — is a (the?) central explanandum of consciousness; and since no phenomenon has been shown to be *in-principle* un-modelable mathematically, consciousness — and with it intentionality, and with it normativity — is a legitimate target for mathematical modelling. On this route the is→ought gap is not jumped by deriving value from description; it is **dissolved at the seam where conscious agents convert objectively-existing GILE values into experienced oughts.**

**Honest structure of the argument (what it does and doesn't earn).**
- CIO-1 **dissolves Hume *conditional on* a bridge premise**: that GILE values *objectively exist* and that *consciousness instantiates* them as oughts. Granting that premise, the derivation of oughts is legitimate and Hume's guillotine simply doesn't bite (you are no longer inferring ought from *pure* is — you have a normative primitive in the ontology).
- CIO-1 **presupposes rather than proves moral realism.** The objective existence of GILE values is the premise, not the conclusion. A strict Humean re-aims the original challenge at exactly this premise ("you smuggled the ought in as an axiom"). That is fair — but note it is *no longer Hume's guillotine* (an inference-rule objection); it has become a *metaphysics* objection (do these values exist?). **Relocating the dispute from logic to ontology is itself progress**, and is fully consistent with B135's "gap relocated into the objective." CIO-1 names the relocation target and argues it is principled, not arbitrary.
- Therefore the accurate headline is **"dissolved given the bridge premise," not "proven from nothing."** The author's "I ALREADY dissolved the razor" is defensible *under its premise*; it is not a premise-free proof, and the paper does not assert one.

**Correction of one over-claim (EVD-1, not preachy — just the weaker true version).** "LITERALLY EVERYTHING HAS BEEN EXPLAINED BY MATH" is false as stated — the hard problem of consciousness is the standing counter-example, and plenty is unexplained. The **defensible** version, which fully supports CIO-1's prior, is Wigner's *unreasonable effectiveness of mathematics*: math has succeeded everywhere it has been seriously tried, and **no domain has been shown to be in-principle mathematically un-modelable.** That yields a *reasonable prior* that consciousness/ethics are modelable — which is all CIO-1 needs. It does not need (and should not claim) that the modelling is already done.

**Falsifier CIO-1-F1 (OPEN).** CIO-1 must yield a consequence that distinguishes "consciousness *instantiates* objective GILE values" from "the modeller *stipulated* GILE values as an axiom." Candidate: a model in which the GILE-value primitive does *explanatory work* the modeller did not hand-tune — e.g. predicts an agent's ought-judgements (or their breakdown under specific consciousness-impairments) better than a model with the values free-fit. If the value primitive earns no out-of-sample keep, CIO-1 is indistinguishable from axiom-smuggling.

## 3. Cross-Normativity — one bridge, three abutments (NB-1, CANDIDATE)

**Claim (author's).** There is a **bidirectional** case for normativity spanning the physical and the ethical: (i) physics carries "oughts" (scientific laws constrain what happens); (ii) mathematics itself "has preferences" (φ and consonance in music, extremal structure); so (iii) the ground is set for **mathematical oughts for ethics**. If physical-ought and math-preference are real, ethical-ought is not a different *kind* of thing.

**The real engine (sharpening "math has preferences").** The precise, non-metaphorical core is **variational / extremal structure**: physical law is overwhelmingly stated as "nature *extremizes* a quantity" — least action, geodesics, maximum entropy, free-energy minimization. That IS the formal shape of a "preference": a functional, and a configuration that optimizes it. The corpus's **UOP is exactly this shape** — `argmax_x [ρ·f_cap(G(x)) + g(H(x))]` with a proven Interior-Optimum Theorem (B133). So **ethics-as-UOP-optimization inherits the *form* of physical law**: same variational template, different functional. That is the substantive, defensible version of "impossible bridge between physical and ethical normativity" — a *shared mathematical form*, not a pun.

**The honest seam (where the bridge actually needs CIO-1).** A variational "preference" in physics is **descriptive**: systems *do* extremize action; it does not follow that they *ought* to. The is→ought gap reappears here as the gap between "the system extremizes" and "the agent ought to maximize GILE+HEM." **What converts the descriptive extremum into a felt prescription is precisely consciousness/intentionality — i.e. CIO-1.** So the three pieces lock together coherently:
- **math supplies the FORM** (variational/extremal template, NB-1),
- **objective GILE values supply the CONTENT** (the functional being extremized),
- **consciousness supplies the FORCE** (turns "does extremize" into "ought to," CIO-1).

This is a genuinely tidy synthesis — and stating the seam openly is what keeps it honest rather than triumphalist.

**Correction of one over-claim (anti-numerology).** "Math has preferences… like in music and φ especially" — φ and consonance are **real structural facts** (small-integer frequency ratios sound consonant; φ appears in extremal packing). But "preference" is a metaphor: math exhibits *extremal/structural distinguished points*, it does not *desire* them. Keep φ-in-music **illustrative**, never evidentiary; do not let "math prefers φ" do argumentative work (that is the exact trap B135's failed 8-constant permutation test was built to catch).

**Falsifier NB-1-F1 (OPEN, two-sided).** (a) *Over-proving guard:* if the variational *form* alone (no consciousness premise, no value content) sufficed to derive an ethical ought, NB-1 would prove too much (every rock "ought" to fall) — so a valid NB-1 must show the form needs CIO-1 to yield a *prescription*, not a mere description. (b) *Earning guard:* NB-1 must exhibit at least one ethical claim whose justification genuinely *uses* the variational structure (e.g. a UOP-derived "ought" with empirical bite), beyond redescribing a known ethical intuition in variational vocabulary.

The author proposes "building impossible bridges" as the TI Sigma tagline. **Adopted as an official slogan** — it is apt: the program repeatedly bridges domains usually held apart (Truth↔Existence via the UOP; physical↔ethical normativity via NB-1; math↔consciousness via CIO-1; determinate↔indeterminate truth via TTI-1). **Honesty rider:** the word that makes the slogan *true rather than grandiose* is the discipline behind it — every bridge in this corpus is meant to be **earned via an open falsifier**, not asserted.
