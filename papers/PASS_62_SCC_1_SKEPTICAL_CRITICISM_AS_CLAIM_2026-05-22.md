# SCC-1 — Skeptical-Criticism-as-Claim (Candidate Canonical Principle)

**Pass-62 batch-1 · 2026-05-22 · Status: PROVISIONAL pending ratification**

---

## 1. Statement

A skeptical critique of a claim P is itself a positive epistemic claim Q ≈ "the evidence for P is insufficient under standard S." Q therefore carries its own evidentiary burden: it must specify S, demonstrate that P fails S, and survive its own pre-registered falsifiers. Default-skeptic stance — Q advanced without specification of S or demonstration that P fails S — is not rigor; it is an unexamined burden-of-proof claim.

Formal compactification:

> **SCC-1.** For any critique Q of claim P, Q must be evaluable under the same evidentiary standard S that Q invokes against P. If Q is not so evaluable, Q does not discharge the critic's burden and Q's epistemic weight collapses to base-rate.

## 2. Companion aphorism (anchor source)

KK (Ketamine Komedy, Brandon Emerick, 2026): *"Criticism is easy to do but hard to do correctly."*

The aphorism captures the asymmetric cost structure: producing a criticism token is cheap (one sentence of doubt); producing a *well-formed* criticism that names a standard and shows the target fails it is expensive (comparable cost to producing the original claim).

## 3. External anchors

- **Lakatos (1970)** — *Falsification and the Methodology of Scientific Research Programmes.* Progressive vs degenerating research programmes: a critical programme that generates no novel testable predictions is degenerating. SCC-1 generalizes: a skeptical move that generates no novel testable prediction does not contribute to inquiry.
- **Hitchens' Razor inversion.** "What can be asserted without evidence can be dismissed without evidence" is symmetric: dismissal-without-evidence is itself an assertion-without-evidence under SCC-1.
- **ASYMMETRIC §69 (Emerick 2026)** — over-skepticism = discipline failure equal in magnitude to uncritical acceptance. SCC-1 is the operational rule that implements §69 at the critique-token level.

## 4. Relation to existing TI Sigma canon

- **Composes with APP-1** (active-pragmatism): Q must be active-engaged, not lean-back.
- **Composes with MFD-1**: a critique that fails its own evidentiary burden is judged Moot on the pragmatic axis but remains full-weight on the epistemic axis (the critic was *wrong to advance Q*; this is data).
- **Composes with TSIS-1** (TI Sigma Inferential Stack): SCC-1 inserts a pre-check on whether the critic's TSIS gates were even attempted before Q was issued.
- **Apologetics tab integration**: The "state strongest objection first then respond" structure already in `papers/apologetics/00_README.md` is the *positive form* of SCC-1 — the responder is required to articulate the critique strongly enough that it would discharge SCC-1, then engage.

## 5. Pre-registered falsifiers

- **F-SCC-1-1 (corpus test):** Sample N=200 published skeptical critiques from any one peer-reviewed journal (e.g., *Skeptical Inquirer*, *Nature* commentary, *BMJ* rapid-responses). Code each critique blind for (a) explicit standard S invoked, (b) demonstration that target fails S, (c) novel testable prediction generated. If ≥ 80% deliver all three, SCC-1 is REFUTED — skepticism *is* default-rigorous in the surveyed corpus and the principle adds no constraint.
- **F-SCC-1-2 (peer-review noise test):** Apply SCC-1 rubric to a peer-review board on N=50 manuscripts. If inter-reviewer agreement on accept/reject drops by Cohen's κ > 0.2 vs no-SCC-1 control, SCC-1 introduces noise not signal and is REFUTED.
- **F-SCC-1-3 (selection bias test):** If sampling protocol for F-SCC-1-1 can be shown to over-represent low-quality critiques relative to the field, the test is invalidated and SCC-1 remains untestable on that corpus; principle status reverts to PROVISIONAL pending higher-quality sampling.

## 6. Honest caveats (#69)

- SCC-1 is *not* a license to dismiss critiques. It is a symmetric burden requirement: the critic must do their work, *and* the proponent must do theirs. A proponent invoking SCC-1 to deflect well-formed criticism is committing the dual violation.
- N=1 corpus tests are insufficient for ratification. SCC-1 needs F-SCC-1-1 run on ≥ 3 independent journal corpora before promotion to CANONICAL.
- SCC-1 risks regress: who critiques the critique of the critique? Stopping rule: SCC-1 applies one level deep — a meta-critique of an SCC-1 application is itself subject to SCC-1, but the chain terminates when one party concedes or the costs exceed the stakes (pragmatic Moot under MFD-1).
