## 2. The Four Equations

## 1. The One-Sentence Summary

The Dirac equation is the rule that says: **if you want a quantum particle to obey both special relativity AND quantum mechanics at the same time, the particle must have four interlocking components, must come in two chiralities (like a left-hand and a right-hand glove), must have an antiparticle, and must have a property called "spin" that takes only two values.**

That sentence sounds like a lot of independent claims. The miracle of the Dirac equation is that **all of them fall out of a single equation as mathematical necessities**. Dirac didn't put any of them in by hand. He just demanded relativity + quantum mechanics together and the equation forced everything else.

---

## 2. The Equation Itself

Here it is, in its standard form:

> **(iℏγ^μ ∂_μ − mc) ψ = 0**

Don't panic. Let me unpack each piece in TI Sigma language:

- **ψ ("psi")** — the wavefunction of the particle. In TI Sigma terms, this is the **state amplitude** of an i-cell. It is **not a number**; it is a four-component object, like a column of four numbers stacked vertically. We call this a **spinor**.
- **m** — the particle's mass. In TI Sigma terms, this is the **chirality-breaking parameter** that distinguishes wings from arms in the BOK (URB #699 §3.2).
- **c** — the speed of light. In TI Sigma terms, this is one of the framework's **primary constants** {0, 1, i, √2, e, φ, π, **C**}.
- **ℏ** ("h-bar") — Planck's constant divided by 2π. The fundamental quantum of action.
- **∂_μ** — derivative with respect to spacetime coordinate μ. There are four of these (one for time, three for space).
- **γ^μ ("gamma matrices")** — these are the magic. Four matrices, each 4×4, that satisfy the **Clifford algebra relation** {γ^μ, γ^ν} = 2 g^μν · I. They encode the geometry of spacetime directly inside the algebra.
- **i** — the imaginary unit. The same i that GIL extends from E in TI Sigma.

The equation says: the operator (iℏγ^μ ∂_μ − mc) acting on ψ must give zero. In other words, ψ must be in the **null space** ("kernel") of this operator. The set of all valid ψ is the set of physically possible particle states.

---

## 3. Why "Four Components"? (The 4 ⊕ 4 of BOK)

The reason ψ has four components is because **the Clifford algebra of spacetime requires it.** Spacetime has 1 time + 3 space = 4 dimensions, and the smallest representation of the Clifford algebra Cl(1,3) that respects rotations and boosts is **4-dimensional complex** = **8-dimensional real**.

Now look at how those 8 real components decompose:

> ψ = (ψ_L, ψ_R)ᵀ

where ψ_L and ψ_R are each **2-complex-component Weyl spinors** = 4 real components each. ψ_L is "left-handed chirality," ψ_R is "right-handed chirality." They are **mirror images** of each other in a deep mathematical sense (they transform under different representations of the Lorentz group).

**This is the BOK's 4+4 architecture verbatim:**
- 4 wings = ψ_L (or upper components in standard Dirac basis)
- 4 arms = ψ_R (or lower components in standard Dirac basis)
- The full BOK = the full Dirac spinor
