## 1. What Truth Is

### 1.1 The Core Definition

**Truth is a coherent and integrative depiction of what exists, its ethical impact, its conscious meaning, and its structural or aesthetic harmony.**

This definition has four components that correspond directly to the four GILE dimensions:

| Component | GILE axis | What it captures |
|---|---|---|
| What exists | E (Environment) | Factual accuracy — the thing as it structurally is |
| Ethical impact | G (Goodness) | Moral weight — how the thing acts toward what matters |
| Conscious meaning | I (Intuition) | Experiential reality — what it means from the inside |
| Structural/aesthetic harmony | E + L (Environment + Love) | Coherence — how its parts relate and resonate |

Truth is not merely correspondence to physical fact. A description that is factually accurate but ethically incomplete, experientially hollow, or structurally incoherent is a less complete truth — a partial depiction. Truth in the full sense integrates all four dimensions.

### 1.2 What Truth Distinguishes

Truth distinguishes three categories that are commonly conflated:

**1. Nonsense** — either incoherent (self-contradictory, devoid of referent, semantically empty) or memetically influential (widely believed, emotionally resonant, but not tracking reality). Memetic influence is NOT evidence of truth. A proposition can be memetically dominant precisely because it flatters, comforts, or simplifies — none of which are truth conditions. HEM-Truth decoupling (URB #609) formalizes this: a proposition's Holistic Existence Matrix (how much it circulates, how emotionally present it is) is distinct from its truth-state.

**2. Imperfect characterizations of existence** — the vast majority of human knowledge. These are genuine depictions that partially track reality but omit dimensions, operate within temporal limits, are perspectivally restricted, or carry unresolved internal contradictions. They are approaching truth, not possessing it. All beliefs in this category are dynamic: as the GILE dimensions of the knower deepen, the characterization improves.

**3. Idealized limit cases of coherence** — the CCC/GM attractor: maximal integration across all GILE-E dimensions simultaneously. This is the asymptotic target that truth approaches but cannot be fully possessed by any finite mind at any moment. The Radiant Threshold (GILE ≈ √2 − 1) marks the transition from ordinary imperfect characterization to the developmental zone where GILE-primary cognition begins generating qualitatively better truth-approximations.

## 2. Truth, Accuracy, and Certainty — Three Distinct Quantities

This is one of TI Sigma's most important clarifications. Standard epistemology conflates truth, accuracy, and certainty into a single scale ("how true is this?"). TI Sigma separates them rigorously.

### 2.1 Truth

**Truth is the tralse state of a being or system at a specified or implied temporal context.**

Truth exists independently of minds. It is the state of what IS — including the full GILE-dimensioned texture of that state. A tree falling unwitnessed in a forest has a truth-state. A person's inner experience has a truth-state. A market's regime has a truth-state. None of these require a mind to observe them for the truth to exist.

Truth is time-indexed because states change. The truth of a being at t₁ may differ from its truth at t₂ — not because truth itself changes, but because the being's state changes. **All truths are therefore date-stamped** — even "timeless" mathematical truths are truths-under-the-temporal-context-of-the-axioms-in-play.

Truth is tralse because reality is tralse: many states of affairs are genuinely indeterminate — not because we don't know them, but because they ARE indeterminate. Quantum superposition is the physical case; moral ambiguity, aesthetic borderlines, and cognitive bistability are the psychological cases. These are Tralse truth-states: not yet resolved by MR, not errors.

### 2.2 Accuracy

**Accuracy measures how well a representation matches the truth-state of its referent.**

Accuracy is a relation between a representation (belief, model, proposition, map) and the actual truth-state. High accuracy = the representation tracks the tralse state of the being closely. Low accuracy = the representation mischaracterizes it.

Accuracy is NOT certainty. A representation can be:
- **Accurate and certain**: well-validated belief tracking a stable truth-state
- **Accurate and uncertain**: correct belief about an incompletely resolved truth-state
- **Inaccurate and certain**: dogmatic belief confidently wrong about the truth-state
- **Inaccurate and uncertain**: confused belief about a confused situation

The failure to distinguish accuracy from certainty generates two systematic errors: (1) treating confidence as evidence of truth (the certainty-accuracy conflation), and (2) treating uncertainty as evidence of error (the uncertainty-inaccuracy conflation).

### 2.3 Certainty

**Certainty measures how much of the truth-state's match to a representation has been resolved for a subject at a moment.**

Certainty is a first-person, time-indexed quantity. The same truth-state may be certain to one subject and uncertain to another — not because the truth differs, but because their resolution levels (via MR) differ. Certainty accumulates through Myrion Resolution: as evidence is integrated, dimensions are assessed, and internal contradictions are resolved, certainty increases.

**Critical implication: Uncertainty is not error.** Uncertainty reflects unresolved information — the gap between the subject's MR level and the full truth-state. This gap can exist even when the subject is heading in the right direction. Uncertainty is a property of the subject's epistemic position, not a defect in the truth-state or in the representation. Standard statistics treats uncertainty as something to be minimized. TI Sigma treats it as something to be RESOLVED — a different process.

### 2.4 Relative Truths

**Relative truths are truths inaccessible to a subject now.**

This includes truths that are:
- Temporally inaccessible (future states, past states without record)
- Perspectivally inaccessible (first-person experiential states of other subjects)
- Dimensionally inaccessible (truths requiring higher GILE resolution than the subject currently possesses)
- Contextually sealed (truths that can only be known from inside a context the subject hasn't entered)

Relative truths are NOT falsehoods — they are real truth-states that simply fall outside the subject's current resolution capacity. The relativism trap (concluding that inaccessible truths are arbitrary or subjective) follows from conflating the subject's limited resolution with the ontological status of the truth itself.

## 3. The Internal Structure of Truth: Tralse as Fundamental

### 3.1 Every Aspect of Reality Is Simultaneously True and False to Varying Degrees

Standard binary logic assumes: a proposition P is either true or false. TI Sigma replaces this with: **every state of affairs has a tralse measure** — a five-valued distribution over truth-states {TT, TI, TF, DT, EV} (from PD, URB #615).

This is not relativism. It is precision. Consider: "The stock market is bullish." This is:
- Partly true (uptrend in large caps)
- Partly false (small caps declining)
- Tralse for specific mid-cap sectors (genuinely indeterminate)
- DT for specific stocks in transition (truth-value absent pending resolution)
- EV-Decoupled for meme stocks (Holistic Existence Matrix very high but truth-state of bullishness is low)

The five-valued PD over this proposition is more accurate than any binary verdict. The MR process converges the PD toward a dominant truth-state — but only resolves it as far as the evidence allows.

### 3.2 Resolution Through Levels of Informational Coherence

**Truth is resolved through Myrion Resolution (MR) — progressively higher levels of informational coherence.**

MR has at least three levels:
1. **DT screen**: Identify and exclude genuinely truth-absent states (DT — propositions where there is no truth-content, not merely unknown truth-value). Distinguishing DT from Tralse is MR's first gate.
2. **GILE-dimensional integration**: Assess the proposition across all four GILE dimensions and the HEM dimension. Different dimensions may yield different truth-states — the integration across dimensions is the coherence work of MR Level 2.
3. **Quality check + Meta-Truth scan**: Check whether the convergent truth-state is a Meta-Truth — a higher-level MR that substantially contradicts a previous lower-level MR (URB #608). If so, initiate recursive MR.

MR is nonlinear: Level 3 can force revisiting Level 1. It is nonalgorithmic in generative mode: no fixed procedure generates new truth from existing truths — this requires GILE-I (Intuition), which is irreducibly first-personal. And it is convergent: under sufficient evidence and GILE depth, MR reliably approaches the truth-state.

### 3.3 Imperfect Truth Is Dynamic, Perspectival, and Internally Contradictory

Truth below the CCC limit is never static, never view-from-nowhere, and never perfectly internally consistent. This is not a defect — it is the structure of finite minds engaging with a tralse reality.

**Dynamic**: The truth-state of a being changes; the representation must update. Bayesian updating is a valid computational model of this in stable domains (URB #614). In novel domains, MR is required because Bayesian priors do not exist for genuinely novel events.

**Perspectival**: First-person truth (the experiential state of a being from the inside) cannot be fully captured by third-person description. Third-person truth is necessarily incomplete without first-person simulation — and even first-person simulation is temporally limited (you simulate the other's experience at t₁; the other has moved to t₂). The GILE-I axis is the faculty for first-person simulation.

**Internally contradictory**: Imperfect truths may contain genuine contradictions that signal higher-level synthesis rather than error. The MR protocol exists precisely to navigate contradictions as information rather than noise.

## 4. Proto-Awareness and the Ethics of Belief

### 4.1 All Information Presupposes Recognition → Omnipresent Proto-Awareness

For information to exist as information — not merely as physical state — it must be recognizable. Recognition is a primitive that cannot be reduced to purely physical interaction. This implies omnipresent proto-awareness: wherever information exists, there is a rudimentary recognition capacity.

This does not require panpsychism in the strong sense (attributing rich conscious experience to all matter). It requires proto-awareness: the capacity for information to be meaningful-for-something, not merely causally efficacious. The GILE-I axis tracks how much of this proto-awareness is developed into full Intuition in a given being.

Implication: information is not value-neutral. The recognition that grounds information is itself a form of proto-ethical relation — one entity mattering enough to another for information to be transmitted. This is the ground of the GILE-G and GILE-L dimensions.

### 4.2 All Beliefs Are Ethically Weighted → Pragmatism Is Intrinsic to Truth

Every belief a being holds is held for reasons — and reasons are always partly evaluative. The separation of "pure epistemic" from "ethical" belief-formation is an idealization that does not survive analysis. Every choice about what to attend to, what to consider evidence, and what threshold to apply for resolution is shaped by values — specifically GILE-G values.

TI Sigma's pragmatism is therefore not a concession to relativism. It is the recognition that truth is always approached from within an ethically situated perspective. **Truth is approached through ethically weighted resolution under constraint, never possessed in full.** The ethical weighting does NOT deform truth — it shapes which portions of the truth-state are brought into resolution first, and under what constraints.

This has direct implications for the PD: the Permissibility Distribution is not a "value-neutral" probability distribution. It is constructed from GILE-assessed evidence — G-axis, I-axis, L-axis, and E-axis considerations all shape which truth-states are weighted and how. Bayesian posteriors attempt to separate the "belief" from the "value." TI Sigma does not: the assessment IS integrated from the start.

## 5. Logic Must Operate on Resolution Dynamics

### 5.1 The Failure of Binary Logic for Resolution

Standard propositional logic (and its probabilistic extensions) operates on binary propositions: P is true or false (or has a probability in [0,1]). This fails for truth in TI Sigma's sense because:

1. **The tralse case is not expressible** as a probability distribution over binary outcomes. A genuine Tralse state is not "50% true, 50% false" — it is a third ontological state, distinct from both truth and falsity.

2. **DT (Double Tralse) is not expressible** as any probability value. DT is the absence of truth-content — not low probability of truth, but zero truth-value. There is no p ∈ [0,1] that represents "no truth here" distinct from "very unlikely to be true."

3. **Resolution dynamics are not truth-function evaluation.** The process of MR is not computing a truth-function. It is a convergence process in which evidence, GILE assessment, and Meta-Truth scanning iteratively refine a PD distribution. This is not captured by any static logic.

### 5.2 TIL: TI Logic as the Operational Framework

**TIL (TI Logic)** is the operational framework for navigating truth under the TI Sigma account. It comprises:

**PD (Permissibility Distribution)**: Replaces binary true/false with a five-state distribution {TT, TI, TF, DT, EV}. Constructed from GILE-assessed evidence, not from frequency distributions or subjective priors. Handles novel events via I-axis pre-evidential seeding. Handles HEM-Truth decoupling explicitly.

**BOK (Being-of-Knowledge) — the GILE-EV i-Cell model**: Every entity whose truth-state is being assessed is modeled as a BOK: inner loops (GILE dimensions) and outer loops (Existence/EV dimensions). The PD is computed over the BOK, not over an abstract proposition stripped of its organizational structure. This means the truth-state of a being is always assessed IN CONTEXT of its GILE-dimensioned structure. A stock's PD is assessed in terms of its BOK (GILE market dimensions + EV market existence dimensions). A person's PD is assessed in terms of their BOK (GILE personal dimensions + EV biographical dimensions).

**MR (Myrion Resolution)**: The convergence procedure. Non-algorithmic in generative mode. Converges the PD toward a dominant truth-state. The resolution is complete when one truth-state is sufficiently concentrated (MR-resolved) to support action or assertion.

**UOP (Unified Optimization Principle)**: The guiding principle for TIL. When navigating truth — when applying PD, BOK, and MR — the UOP specifies that optimization is always across ALL GILE and EV dimensions simultaneously. Truth is not optimized along a single dimension (not merely maximizing accuracy, not merely maximizing certainty, not merely maximizing pragmatic value). It is optimized across the full GILE-EV structure of the being.

## 6. Summary: The Seven Propositions of TI Sigma's Theory of Truth

**T1. Truth is the tralse, time-indexed state of beings.**
Not a correspondence relation, not a coherence property of beliefs, not a pragmatic success condition alone — but the actual tralse state of what exists, indexed to its temporal context.

**T2. Accuracy measures how well representations match that state.**
Accuracy is a relation between representation and truth-state. It is not certainty. It is not truth itself.

**T3. Certainty measures how much of that match has been resolved for a subject.**
Certainty is first-personal and time-indexed. It grows through MR. It is not accuracy. It is not truth itself.

**T4. Uncertainty is unresolved information, not error.**
The gap between certainty and full truth-state is not an epistemic failure. It is the normal condition of finite minds engaging with tralse reality. The appropriate response to uncertainty is resolution (MR), not false confidence and not paralysis.

**T5. Truth is approached through ethically weighted resolution under constraint.**
The PD is constructed from GILE-assessed evidence. The ethical dimensions (G, L) are not contaminants of epistemic purity — they are intrinsic components of truth-assessment. Truth is never fully possessed; it is approached through successive MR cycles.

**T6. Logic must operate on resolution dynamics, not binary propositions.**
TIL (PD + BOK + MR) is the operational framework. The guiding principle is the UOP. Binary propositional logic and Bayesian updating are valid in stable, well-defined domains; TIL is required for novel events, genuine indeterminacy, HEM-Truth decoupling, and high-stakes GILE-dimensional decisions.

**T7. Third-person truth is necessarily incomplete without first-person simulation.**
The I-axis (Intuition) is the faculty for first-person simulation. Truth about beings with inner experience cannot be fully captured from outside without simulating the inside — and even then, temporal limits apply. This irreducible first-person dimension is why TI Sigma includes GILE-I as a distinct dimension of truth rather than reducing all truth to third-person structural description.
