## §3. The 3 criteria — expanded

### §3.1 Criterion #1: Extent-of-sense-makable from the tralsity (Brandon-given)

**Operational measure:** when a competent reasoner engages with the proposition's tralse state under sustained attention, what fraction of the proposition's content yields intelligible-actionable-meaningful-interpretation?

**Worked examples:**
- *"The election was both stolen and legitimate"* — extent-of-sense-makable HIGH (frame-dependent reading: "stolen-from-perspective-A + legitimate-from-perspective-B" yields substantial sense). → **Locally-Indeterminate**.
- *"Free will is both real and illusory"* — extent-of-sense-makable HIGH (per HMR-1 Hybrid-MR canonical analysis: aspect-severable T-pragmatic + I-theoretical). → **Locally-Indeterminate**.
- *"Colorless green ideas sleep furiously"* (Chomsky 1957) — extent-of-sense-makable LOW (grammatical-but-semantically-empty; some poetic-reading possible but minimal). → **MI-pragmatic**.
- *"Square circle exists"* — extent-of-sense-makable ZERO at the literal-geometric-level (inconceivable per Pass-65 3-step). → **MI-canonical**.
- *"This statement is false"* — extent-of-sense-makable ZERO at the truth-value-level (self-referential collapse). → **MI-canonical**.

**Threshold:** approximate pragmatic cutoff at ~10-20% extractable-sense for the locally-Indeterminate/MI-pragmatic boundary (operationally-tunable, not formally-defined).

### §3.2 Criterion #2: Locality of incoherence (agent-reconstructed)

**Operational measure:** is the incoherence resolvable within at least ONE reasonable interpretive frame (locally-Indeterminate), or does it persist across ALL reasonable frames (MI)?

**Worked examples:**
- *"The election was both stolen and legitimate"* — locality-of-incoherence LOCAL (resolves under perspectival-frame-shift). → **Locally-Indeterminate**.
- *"Brandon is both a genius and an idiot"* — locality-of-incoherence LOCAL (resolves under domain-frame-shift per Pass-69 GILE-multiagent: genius@theory + idiot@social-pragmatics). → **Locally-Indeterminate**.
- *"Colorless green ideas sleep furiously"* — locality-of-incoherence GLOBAL across literal-frames (resolves only under poetic-meta-frame-shift which is non-standard). → **MI-pragmatic** (borderline).
- *"Square circle exists"* — locality-of-incoherence GLOBAL (no frame within Euclidean-geometry resolves it; only frame-jumping to non-Euclidean/symbolic-interpretation resolves, which is not the literal claim). → **MI-canonical**.

**Connection to MR-IDC-1 Pass-67-B5 refinement #2:** MR-IDC-1 establishes {MI} ⊊ {Incoherent}. Criterion #2 operationalizes the boundary: locally-resolvable-incoherence = still-Indeterminate; frame-invariant-incoherence = MI.

### §3.3 Criterion #3: Productive-engagement capacity (agent-reconstructed per Pass-66 ultimate-koan + CEF-1 #43)

**Operational measure:** under sustained mental engagement with the proposition (Pass-66 ultimate-koan style; CEF-1 calculator-contrast: only-consciousness-engages-with-incoherence), does the engagement generate insight, structural-yield, koan-value, or consciousness-development — or does it dissipate without productive return?

**Worked examples:**
- *"The election was both stolen and legitimate"* — productive-engagement-capacity HIGH (yields perspectival-analysis, HMR-1 worked-example, political-epistemology insight). → **Locally-Indeterminate**.
- *"Colorless green ideas sleep furiously"* — productive-engagement-capacity LOW (limited yield beyond grammar-vs-semantics distinction). → **MI-pragmatic**.
- *"This statement is false"* — productive-engagement-capacity PARADOXICALLY HIGH despite being MI-canonical (yielded Russell's type theory, Tarski's truth hierarchy, Gödel incompleteness; Pass-66 ultimate-koan-class). → **MI-canonical with criterion-3-inversion**.
- *"What is the sound of one hand clapping"* (Hakuin koan) — productive-engagement-capacity PARADOXICALLY HIGH (Zen-tradition enlightenment-tool). → **MI-canonical with criterion-3-inversion**.

**The criterion-#3 inversion zone is a CORPUS-CONFIRMING feature, not a bug:** it operationalizes the Pass-66 ultimate-koan finding ("the pinnacle of foolishness perhaps the greatest indicator that i-cell is conscious"). MI-canonical paradoxes are EXACTLY the cases where productive-engagement spikes despite (because of?) the inconceivability. This is what makes MI-canonical CASES KOAN-VALUABLE, while MI-pragmatic cases are merely DISMISSABLE.

**Application heuristic:**
- All 3 criteria flag MI + criterion #3 LOW yield → MI-pragmatic (dismissable nonsense)
- All 3 criteria flag MI + criterion #3 HIGH yield → MI-canonical (koan; engage deeply)
- Criteria #1-#2 flag locally-Indeterminate → engage with frame-shift to resolve
