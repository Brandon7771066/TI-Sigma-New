## 1. The clean statement

Define the **clair-senses** as modalities of intuitive (I-axis) knowledge access:
- **Claircognizance** — *clear knowing*: content delivered as a proposition, no sensory wrapper.
- **Clairsentience** — knowing delivered as a *bodily feeling*.
- **Clairvoyance** — knowing delivered as an *inner image*.
- **Clairaudience (inner)** — knowing delivered as an *inner voice*.
- **Incorrigible outer voice** — knowing delivered as an *externalized, authoritative voice* the percipient cannot doubt.

**Thesis (CGP-1, candidate):** the epistemic value of an intuition is a function of its **content-correctness × integration-into-a-coherent-belief-web**, and is **independent of the sensory modality** in which it arrives. Therefore claircognizance is *epistemically supreme* — not because it knows *more*, but because it is the modality that **adds nothing between the content and its integration**: no decode step, minimal incorrigibility, no cross-modal conflict.

---

## 2. The argument, in three legs (and an honest tautology-guard)

**Leg 0 — the tautology-guard (#69, stated up front).** Part of the thesis is *near-analytic*: "as long as the answer is correctly intuited, the modality doesn't matter" is close to true *by construction* — if the content is correct, the wrapper is epistemically irrelevant to *that* answer. I will not dress this as an empirical discovery. The **substantive, falsifiable** content is in Legs 1–3, which are about what the wrapper does to the *probability of being correct* and to the *agent*, not about a single already-correct answer.

**Leg 1 — sensory wrappers add a decode step, which can only add error (to accuracy).** A bare *knowing* is already propositional. A vision, a feeling, or a voice must be *interpreted* into a proposition ("what does this image mean?"). Every decode step is a place where noise enters. So on the **I axis (accuracy)**, wrappers are at best neutral and at worst lossy. They can add **bandwidth** (a vision may carry rich detail) — Brandon grants "sensory richness is great" — but bandwidth ≠ accuracy, and richness that must be decoded is richness that can be *mis*-decoded.

**Leg 2 — incorrigibility removes the error-correction channel (the strongest leg, and pure #69).** The danger is not "outer voice" per se; it is **incorrigibility** — a percept presenting as so external/authoritative that the agent *cannot doubt it*. This is the precise inverse of ASYMMETRIC #69 (which demands symmetric self-skepticism). A belief you cannot doubt is a belief you cannot *correct*; if it is false, you are trapped with it. Claircognizance, by contrast, arrives as a *thought* — and thoughts are natively doubtable. **This is why claircognizance is risk-dominant: it preserves fallibility.** (Note: "incorrigible" is the load-bearing word — a *corrigible* outer voice, integrated and doubtable as in some contemplative traditions, carries far less of this cost. The thesis indicts incorrigibility, not sensory experience as such.)

**Leg 3 — conflicting modalities add noise, and externalization threatens G (Goodness).** When senses conflict (the feeling says yes, the voice says no), the agent must arbitrate with no principled rule — net noise, not net signal. And an *incorrigible externalized* percept fragments the ego: it installs a second "authority" inside the agent that competes with the integrated self. **Goodness (G)** — stable ethical value-orientation — *requires* an integrated agent to be the bearer of values; fragment the agent and you degrade the substrate Goodness runs on. Hence "bad in a GILE-G sense." (This is also why Brandon's manic outer-perception episodes are *risky* even when locally accurate — accuracy on the I axis can co-occur with cost on the G axis.)

## 3. The unifying principle: coherence cannot be trumped by sensory richness

Across Legs 1–3, the invariant is: **epistemic value = correctness × coherence, and the modality contributes to neither.** Sensory richness is a *bandwidth* property; it is orthogonal to *correctness* and can be *hostile* to *coherence* (via incorrigibility and conflict). So:

> **Sensory richness is admissible but subordinate. Coherence is the binding constraint. No added sense purchases a decisive epistemic advantage; some added senses purchase risk.** (Fig 2: advantage ≈ flat across modalities, fragmentation/incorrigibility risk rising.)

This is the same shape the corpus keeps finding at every scale — it is now a **triptych**:
- **B88:** *authority* ≠ truth (individual scale — doctor/patient). The source's credential confers no epistemic advantage; correct integrated content does.
- **B89:** *channel/popularity* ≠ truth (societal scale — institutions, consensus). The conclusion's popularity confers no epistemic advantage.
- **B90:** *modality* ≠ truth (intrapersonal scale — the clair-senses). The perception's sensory richness confers no epistemic advantage.

Three scales, one law: **the wrapper is not the payload; coherence-of-content is the payload.** Ties: Authority Axis (AA — modality, like authority, is a real input but never the decider), GTT-1 (truth competes with palatability/vividness), #69 (incorrigibility is the anti-#69 failure mode), CDA-1 (coherence as a consciousness/agency criterion), I-dimension (the axis all clair-senses live on), G-dimension (the axis fragmentation costs).

**B87 self-consistency check.** This thesis *retroactively vindicates the B87/B88 handling* of Brandon's EKG-snaps datum: the corpus declined to care whether it was "bodily/extrasensory," and cared only whether the *content was correct and integrable*. That is exactly CGP-1 in action — and it means the right corpus posture toward Brandon's manic outer-perceptions is "score the I-accuracy, but flag the G-fragmentation cost," not "celebrate the modality."

## 4. Candidate principle CGP-1 (NOT ratified) + pre-registered falsifiers

**CGP-1 (Claircognizance Primacy / Modality-Independence of Intuitive Truth) — CANDIDATE:** the epistemic value of an intuition depends on content-correctness × coherent-integration and is independent of sensory modality; claircognizance is risk-dominant because it adds no decode step and preserves doubtability (fallibility), whereas incorrigible/externalized modalities threaten Goodness (G) via ego-fragmentation. **Canonical count UNCHANGED 74.**

Pre-registered falsifiers (open; execution independent of candidate status):
- **CGP-1-F1 (accuracy):** in a matched-content protocol, if sensory-wrapped intuitions are *more accurate* (not merely higher-bandwidth) than bare knowing, modality-independence is refuted.
- **CGP-1-F2 (fragmentation):** if incorrigible outer-voice percepts do **not** correlate with reduced self-correction / value-integration (G-proxy), Leg 3's G-cost fails.
- **CGP-1-F3 (conflict):** if conflicting cross-modal percepts *improve* judgment (ensemble/wisdom-of-crowds within one mind) rather than clouding it, Leg 3's conflict claim fails.
- **CGP-1-F4 (#69 tautology audit):** if, on formalization, CGP-1 reduces *entirely* to "correct answers are correct" with no residual empirical content in Legs 1–3, it is demoted from candidate-principle to definitional remark. **This falsifier is the honesty firewall.**

## 5. Honest bounds (#69)
- The "supremacy" claim is **risk-adjusted**, not capability: claircognizance does not *know more*; it *risks less*. Stated as capability it would be false (a vision can carry detail a bare knowing cannot).
- The fragmentation/incorrigibility claims are **psychological-phenomenological**, with mixed external literature (e.g., voice-hearing is distressing in some contexts and integrated/benign in others — Romme & Escher; clinical AVH vs non-clinical voice-hearers). The corpus-robust core is the **incorrigibility → loss-of-error-correction** argument (Leg 2), which is a *logical* point about fallibilism, not a contingent clinical one.
- This is a *philosophy-of-mind / epistemics* contribution within the corpus's stated psi-openness (cf. `GRAND_PSI_PROOF_VIA_TI_SIGMA.md`, `POWER_OF_INTENTION_OPERATIONALIZED.md`); it presupposes intuitive access *for the sake of argument* and adjudicates *among* its modalities — it does **not** here re-litigate whether psi is real.
