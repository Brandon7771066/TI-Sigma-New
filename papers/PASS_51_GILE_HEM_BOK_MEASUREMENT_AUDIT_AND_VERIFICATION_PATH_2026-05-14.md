### §2.2 The B-2 wing/arm ratio: #69 audit

**What is claimed:** "First quantitative geometric prediction of the TI Sigma framework confirmed by direct measurement" (urb_699 §4.3, §8).

**What was actually measured:** Radial extents of 8 loop tips on a single ChatGPT-rendered Verisyn-as-DC-limit image. Mean wing ~405 px, mean arm ~207 px → ratio 1.96 (2% off the predicted 2.0).

**What was NOT measured:**
- The wing/arm ratio of *the equation itself* (the recovered r(θ) = A·e^{sin(θ+ϕ)} − B·cos(4(θ+ϕ)) + C·sin⁵(...) + D·cos(kτθ) form). §5.5 explicitly notes the empirically-relevant B/D coefficient ratio is "fit to data" — i.e., the equation does not predict wing/arm=2.0; it accommodates it via a free parameter.
- Any independent visual rendering (no second LLM, no human plot, no analytic plot).
- Any of urb_699 §7 P2-P5 (Maxwell-knot EM-field, HRV LF/HF, GCP RNG, TK weighting). All four are listed as needed and **none has been executed**.

**Honest verdict re-statement:**

> B-2 wing/arm = 2.0 is a **VISUAL PATTERN MATCH AGAINST A SINGLE LLM-GENERATED IMAGE**, with the predicting equation containing a free coefficient (B/D) that is fit-to-data rather than derived. Under #69, this is **SUGGESTIVE-PATTERN-ONLY, well below CONFIRM threshold**.

The primary source (urb_699 §4.3, §5.5, §8) is honest about this — it explicitly says "suggestive, not yet definitive." The label-inflation risk is in *citations* of urb_699 that drop the qualifier (e.g., "BOK wing/arm ratio confirmed at 2.0"). Pass-51 records the honest qualifier for downstream use.

### §2.3 The B-3 persistent-homology β₁=6: #69 audit

**What is claimed:** 8D biometric phase space exhibits BOK topology, evidenced by β₁=6 (six persistent loop structures) in TDA.

**What was measured:** 15 ESP32 biometric data points (Brandon's hardware) + 400 synthetic points; resulting persistent-homology barcode shows β₁ estimate near 6.

**#69 concerns:**
- **15 real points cannot determine the β₁ of a manifold; 400 synthetic points are determined by their generator, not by the underlying biology.** The β₁=6 is essentially a property of the synthetic-data generator, regularized by 15 real samples.
- **No held-out test set.** The fit is in-sample.
- **No competing-topology null** (e.g., β₁ from a random-mixture-of-Gaussians 8D point cloud at the same point count).

**Honest verdict re-statement:**

> B-3 is **A PROOF-OF-CONCEPT TDA PIPELINE THAT DOES NOT DISTINGUISH BOK TOPOLOGY FROM ANY 8D GENERATOR WITH SIMILAR LOOP COUNT**. Not a confirmation of BOK topology in biological data.

### §2.4 The B-4 4+4 math-fields split: #69 audit

**What is claimed:** Fields Medal distribution + MSC category richness + cross-field connectivity confirm a 4+4 split structurally identical to BOK 4 wings + 4 arms.

**#69 concerns:**
- The Primary/Secondary partition of mathematical fields (Analysis/Algebra/Geometry/Number Theory primary; Logic/Probability/Combinatorics/Applied secondary) is **conventional in the bibliometric literature, predating TI Sigma**. It is not a TI-Sigma-specific prediction.
- Fields Medal subfield-tallies that respect this conventional partition are *known*; using them as "confirmation of BOK 4+4" is post-hoc.
- No pre-registered partition was filed before counting.

**Honest verdict re-statement:**

> B-4 is **A POST-HOC ALIGNMENT BETWEEN BOK 4+4 NUMEROLOGY AND A CONVENTIONAL MSC PARTITION**. The bibliometric numbers (76.6%, 18.8%, 3.7:1) are real but were not predicted by BOK; they were back-fitted to BOK after the fact.

1. **"CONFIRM (formal)" without explicit reader-disclosure.** Pass-47 C11 (V4-cubed Meijer 8D), C12 (Tozzi-Banach-Tarski), C13 (GILE-HEM 8↔8) all carry `CONFIRM (formal)`. 2. **Measurement of an LLM-generated artifact treated as measurement of the underlying claim.** B-2 wing/arm=1.96 measured against a ChatGPT image is the cleanest example. Whenever an LLM is in the measurement loop, the question "is this measuring the claim or measuring the LLM's rendering of the claim" must be asked. Per Pass-47 §4 worked-example exchange, LLMs have an unavoidable confirmation bias toward the user's stated thesis.

3. **Post-hoc category-alignment cited as confirmation.** B-4 mathematics 4+4 split, the GILE↔constants mapping G↔ℏ/I↔c/L↔k_B/etc. The mapping is *one of many permutations equally plausible at the conceptual-analogy level* (Pass-37 §7 C2 explicitly logs this).
