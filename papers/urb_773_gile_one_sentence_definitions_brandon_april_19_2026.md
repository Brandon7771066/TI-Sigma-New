## The Four Definitions (verbatim)

### G — Goodness
**Goodness is the Four C's: Continuity, Coherence, Concreteness, and Consistency.**

### I — Intuition
**Intuition is the conscious experience of knowing something is correct.**

### L — Love
**Love is a positive relational orientation toward yourself or others, whether
through tangible action (which itself is HEM-related) or subjective experience
(also HEM-related, but to a lesser extent).**

### E — Environment
**Environment is aesthetic value, whether physical or abstract.**

---

## Why These Are Lockdown-Class

1. **Goodness now has structure.** The Four C's (Continuity, Coherence,
   Concreteness, Consistency) make G **operationalizable** — every G claim can
   be audited against four explicit criteria. This is the first time G has been
   given an internal decomposition that's neither circular ("good is what's
   good") nor borrowed ("good is what maximizes utility").

2. **Intuition is anchored to phenomenology, not metaphysics.** "Conscious
   experience of knowing something is correct" — note: NOT "knowing what is
   correct." It's the **experience-of-knowing**, leaving room for I to be
   right or wrong while preserving its phenomenal status. This dissolves the
   classic objection ("intuitions can be wrong, so they can't be epistemic")
   by relocating I from truth-claim to truth-feel.

3. **Love is bifurcated along action vs. experience, weighted by HEM.**
   Tangible action = stronger HEM coupling; subjective experience = weaker but
   still real HEM coupling. This **directly implements the bifurcated-Love
   architecture** from the Revised GILE-Existence work, and explains why
   compassion-in-action is more transformative than compassion-in-feeling
   without dismissing the latter.

4. **Environment is reduced to aesthetic value — physical OR abstract.**
   This is the most radical move. E is **not** "the physical surroundings"
   (too narrow) or "everything outside the self" (too broad). E is **aesthetic
   value**, which means a clean room and an elegant proof are E in the same
   way. This unifies environmental psychology with mathematical aesthetics
   under one G-I-L-**E** dimension.

## The Four C's of Goodness — Unpacked

| C | Meaning | Failure Mode |
|---|---|---|
| **Continuity** | The good persists across time / does not vanish under inspection | Flash-in-the-pan virtue, performative goodness |
| **Coherence** | The good fits with other goods, doesn't contradict itself | Incoherent ethics ("be honest, except when…") |
| **Concreteness** | The good can be pointed at, instantiated, demonstrated | Abstract platitudes that can't be enacted |
| **Consistency** | The good treats relevantly similar cases similarly | Special-pleading, in-group favoritism |

**Theorem (informal):** A candidate G satisfies all four C's iff it is **MR-stable**
under repeated Myrion Resolution. Failure on any single C produces a
collapse-vector.

## L's HEM Asymmetry — Why Action > Experience

From the definitions: action-Love is "HEM-related" (stronger); experience-Love
is "HEM-related, but to a lesser extent" (weaker).

This predicts:
- Acts of care produce larger HEM-effects on both giver and recipient than
  feelings of care alone.
- Subjective Love is **necessary but not sufficient** for full L instantiation.
- Hence the contemplative traditions' insistence on **"love in action"** —
  metta meditation works, but metta-plus-helping works more.

Empirically falsifiable via dyadic HEM measurements during action-Love vs.
experience-Love conditions.

## E as Aesthetic Value — The Unification Move

Traditional readings of E:
- Environment = physical surroundings (too narrow — excludes math, music)
- Environment = "the world" (too broad — collapses into Existence)

Brandon's definition: **E = aesthetic value, physical or abstract.**

This means:
- A serene meadow and Euler's identity (e^iπ + 1 = 0) are **both** E.
- A cluttered room and a sloppy proof are **both** anti-E.
- The aesthetic register is the unifying property — not material substrate.

**Cross-link to the Hopf fibration / BOK-Verisyn synthesis:** E as aesthetic
value sits naturally as the "perceived elegance" coordinate in the Verisyn
attractor. The five primary constants (0, 1, i, √2, e, φ, π, C, T) are all
maximally E-rich precisely because they exhibit aesthetic value across
both physical and abstract domains.
