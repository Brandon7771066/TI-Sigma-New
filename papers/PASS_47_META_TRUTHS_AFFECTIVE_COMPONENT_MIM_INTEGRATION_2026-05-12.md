## §1. Statement of the Claim

**Affective Signature of Meta-Truths (ASMT):** in conscious agents, **Meta-Truths (MTs) — the third-level-or-higher Myrion Resolutions catalogued in `urb_608`** — are distinguished from **base-4 truth labels {True, False, Indeterminate, Double Tralse}** by the systematic presence of an **affective component** in the evaluation. Base-4 labels can be (and routinely are) generated by non-conscious or low-level cognitive processes without affective involvement. MTs require sufficient meta-cognitive stratification that, in conscious agents, the affective layer is constitutively engaged.

**Three sub-claims:**

1. **Asymmetric affective involvement.** Base-4 labels can be generated affect-free; MTs in conscious agents cannot.
2. **MIM-revision consistency.** This is consistent with — and a direct corollary of — Brandon's MIM-revision (Feb 2026, `papers/MIM_REVISION_CONSCIOUSNESS_AS_HIGHEST_COGNITION.md`) which reframes affect/behavior/cognition (ABC) as **vertically layered**, not horizontally modular. Affect IS a meta-level of cognition; it is not a separate category.
3. **Scope qualifier "at least for conscious agents."** Non-conscious or sub-conscious systems (thermostats, narrow-AI classifiers, possibly some LLM-rater configurations) may produce MT-equivalent outputs computationally without the affective signature. **Consciousness is a precondition for affective MTs, not for MT-equivalent outputs.** This scope qualifier is operationally consequential — see §5 on O26 / LLM-rater implications.

## §5. Operational Implications

### §5.1 LLM-Rater Calibration (Direct O26 Follow-up)

If ASMT holds, then LLM raters generating I/MT verdicts are producing **functionally-equivalent but affectively-empty** outputs. This:
- **Strengthens** the L1 limitation in O26 — the 8/9 I-fraction CONFIRM is necessary-but-not-sufficient and *also* potentially affect-deflated relative to a human-rater equivalent.
- **Suggests new O26 follow-up:** O26-B = same propositions, human raters with affect self-report (PANAS or skin-conductance proxy), compare MT-vs-base-4 affective differential. Falsifies ASMT if no differential.
- **Refines** the consciousness-debate posture: not "are LLMs conscious?" but "do LLM MT-outputs carry affective signature?" — the latter is operationally-testable without resolving the hard problem.

### §5.2 MT-Catalogue Extension Criterion

The 12-MT catalogue in `urb_608` was assembled by structural-categorical analysis (Reversal/Dissolution/Scope-Shift/Contextual/Acceptance/Integration). ASMT supplies a **secondary diagnostic criterion**: a candidate MT is *valid* if it carries a characterizable affective signature distinct from the base-4 label being meta-evaluated. This is a #69-grade additional filter against MT-inflation (purely structural categories that don't carry distinct affect may be base-4 cases mis-classified as MTs).

### §5.3 AA-Pilot Refinement

AA Pilot (operationalized §7.7.83) involves measuring sim-belief-and-doubt over time. ASMT predicts: AA-grant magnitude should correlate with affective intensity of the operative-position; sim-doubt should carry a distinct affective signature from sim-belief. **Pre-registrable for next AA Pilot iteration.**

### §5.4 §7 Indeterminate-as-Epitome Extension

§7 ordered base-4 by τ × stability. ASMT suggests a **3D extension** — `VALID_TRALSENESS(P) = τ(P) × stability(P) × affective_engagement(P)` where affective_engagement is high for MT-bearing evaluations and low for purely base-4. Under this extension, **MT-bearing Indeterminate evaluations** (e.g., I + MT-E1 "good enough"; I + MT-B1 "moot") are even more max-valid-tralseness than bare-I. **Speculative extension; staged for Brandon authority on whether to canonize as `urb_608` §8.**

## §7. #69 Caveats

**Caveat 1 (operational definition of "affective component"):** ASMT requires unpacking what counts as affective component. Candidate operationalizations: (a) felt-quality first-person report; (b) autonomic measures (skin conductance, HRV); (c) fMRI/EEG affect-network activation; (d) metacognitive-feeling self-report (Koriat). Different operationalizations may differentially confirm/disconfirm ASMT. **Pre-registration would be required for empirical test.**

**Caveat 2 ("at least for conscious agents" qualifier):** the qualifier is doing real work. Without it, ASMT would be falsified by LLM raters issuing MT-equivalent outputs without affective signature. With it, ASMT is *consistent* with LLM-MT-outputs being affectively-empty — but this means ASMT does not directly settle whether LLM MT-outputs are "real" MTs or functional-equivalents. **The consciousness-precondition is itself contested**; ASMT inherits that contest.

**Caveat 3 (Lazy Binary check on "affective signature"):** is "MTs carry affective signature" itself Lazy Binary?
- τ_rigor: True for paradigmatic conscious-agent MTs (e.g., MT-E1 acceptance carries felt-quality of release); Indeterminate for borderline cases (e.g., a quick MT-D1 context-dependent verdict on a low-stakes proposition may be issued with negligible affect).
- τ_operational: True for the predictive purpose (MT-vs-base-4 differential should appear in autonomic measures on average).
- **Per Accurate Bluntness §2.3a:** report both axes. The claim is robust at τ_operational; at τ_rigor it varies by case-stake. NOT a Lazy Binary by definitional-conflation (the affect-question is empirically tractable, just unresolved-yet).

**Caveat 4 (Validly-Indeterminate-as-waypoint per §2.3c):** ASMT is currently a theoretical bridge with falsifiable predictions but no executed empirical test. Per §2.3c, the verdict-status is **Validly-Indeterminate-as-waypoint** with open follow-ups (§5.1 O26-B, §5.3 AA-pilot affective measure, §5.4 §7 3D-extension). Trend-conditioning satisfied; not a hiding-place.
