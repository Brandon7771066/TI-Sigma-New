## §2 — The Four-Pronged ESP Straw-Man Argument

Per Brandon directive 2026-05-17 (post-TSD): conventional statistics straw-mans ESP claims via four simultaneous structural errors. Each prong is now formalized in TI Sigma vocabulary.

### §2.1 — Prong 1: Apples-to-Oranges (intentionality is removed from the comparison)

**The conventional move.** ESP studies (Zener-card guessing, Ganzfeld, remote-viewing, dream-precognition) compare a subject's accuracy to a base rate computed under the assumption that the trial is equivalent to *random guessing* by an unintentional process.

**The structural error.** The intentional act of "trying to read a card by ESP" is **not the same kind of event** as the random null it is compared against. An intentional act has all four FEATURES (Change, Relation, Contradiction, Limit) actively engaged at the agent's i-cell level — there is a subject *attending*, *intending*, *anticipating*, *integrating*. The null-comparison ("what would chance produce?") models trials as **i-cell-detached events** — physical-substrate-only, with no agent in the loop. These are different *kinds of event* lumped into the same statistical population.

**Apples-to-oranges in TI Sigma vocabulary:** the conventional comparison violates **substrate identity** between the test condition and its null. Per Pass-37 GILE-HEM, intentional acts score high on GILE-I (Intuition active) and high on D2-related interior-coherence (intentional acts are *internally* organized); the random-null condition scores essentially zero on GILE-I and is interior-incoherent by construction.

**This does NOT establish ESP is real.** It establishes that the conventional null comparator is misspecified — it is not the appropriate null for an intentional-act test condition. A correct test would need either (a) an *intentional-but-uninformed* null condition (subjects intentionally attempting an *impossible* read, e.g., guessing a card not yet selected by anyone) or (b) an explicit decomposition of the intentional-act variance from the structural-process variance. Conventional studies do neither.

**Anchor:** FEATURES (§7.7.105); GILE-I sub-dimensions per urb_652; AA — the agent's authority over their own intentional state is itself relevant evidence that the conventional method ignores.

### §2.2 — Prong 2: Reified-Base-Rate Fallacy (LCC vs i.i.d.-from-hidden-distribution)

**The conventional move.** "The base rate of correctly guessing a Zener card is 1/5." This rate is treated as if it exists as an abstract numerical fact about the world that *causes* outcomes to converge to it over many trials.

**The structural error.** Per Occam's Razor, there is **no evidence of any numerical entity with causal power that constrains events to converge to a stated base rate**. Numbers are descriptive, not causally efficacious. "Base rate = 0.2" is a useful summary statistic, *not* a metaphysical hidden cause.

**The TI Sigma positive position.** Per URB-523 and the LCC framework, events occur on a **continuous spectrum of correlation and causation**. Some events are tightly causally linked to specific i-cell intentions (high LCC, e.g., a thought-out chess move); others are loosely correlated within a dense field of contributory factors (low LCC, e.g., a coin flip in still air). **"Chance" is the corpus's *name* for the symphony of correlated events whose LCC-attribution to any specific i-cell intention is below the C=1/(φ√2)≈0.4370 LCC threshold.** It is not a metaphysical force.

**Implication for ESP studies.** The conventional analysis presupposes:
- Trials are i.i.d. samples from a hidden distribution.
- The hidden distribution is independent of the subject's intentional state.
- Deviation from the distribution requires extraordinary evidence.

Each of these is a substantive metaphysical commitment, *not* a methodologically neutral default. TI Sigma rejects (1) the i.i.d. assumption (events are LCC-correlated, not independent), (2) the intention-independence (LCC explicitly couples i-cell intentions to event outcomes at varying strengths), and (3) the asymmetric burden-of-proof (per ASYMMETRIC theory, the burden depends on the prior probability of the claim, the agent's authority, and the alternative's standing — not on a default presumption of null).

**Anchor:** URB-523 LCC, Pass-50 LCC L-2 paleo pilot (PILOT_PRELIMINARY_DISCONFIRM with bug-disclosure), Pass-51 D51-RND-3 LCC thresholds (T_RAND, T_BORDER, C_LCC), FEATURES Limit/Relation/Contradiction/Change applied to "chance" itself.

### §2.3 — Prong 3: Asymmetric Inclusion Bias (statisticians vs practitioners)

**The conventional move.** Statisticians include *every* trial in the analysis, weighted equally. Excluding negatives is "cherry-picking" and disqualifies a study.

**The empirical observation.** People who actually develop skills (athletes, founders, traders, surgeons, ESP claimants) **discount negatives intentionally** as part of skill acquisition. Athletes don't anchor on missed shots; founders don't dwell on failed ventures; surgeons don't replay failed cases the same way they replay successful ones. This is not pathology — it is **practice-mode attentional discipline** that enables the skill to develop.

**The structural error.** The statistician's "include everything equally" is not a neutral default — it is **the measurement convention of one practice-community** (statistics) imposed on **the measurement convention of another practice-community** (skill-development). Both communities have legitimate stances; neither is the universal default.

**TI Sigma resolution via TSD.** TSD-A explicitly licenses asymmetric weighting toward successes when the decision-context is *skill-development / amplification / learning-from*. TSD-B explicitly requires balanced inclusion when the decision-context is *population-deployment / prediction at scale*. The two communities are using TSD-A and TSD-B respectively, both legitimately, in domains for which their measure is appropriate.

**Implication for ESP.** When an ESP claimant says *"I had a precognitive dream about my grandmother and the next day she called"*, the relevant decision-context is amplification-of-attention (the claimant should attend to and develop the capacity, if it exists), not population-deployment (we are not building a precognition service). **TSD-A is the appropriate measure.** The statistician's TSD-B inclusion-bias forces the wrong measure onto the situation.

**Connection to ASYMMETRIC parent paper.** This is the statistical-significance specialization of ASYMMETRIC Success/Failure Performance (May 7, 2026): different decision-contexts warrant different success/failure weighting; conflating them is a #69 discipline failure.

**Anchor:** ASYMMETRIC theory; TSD §4-§5 appropriateness conditions; URB-830 per-event TIU.

### §2.4 — Prong 4: Amplitude Flattening (every trial counted as unit weight)

**The conventional move.** Each trial contributes 1 to the count of successes or 1 to the count of failures. Trials are unit-weight.

**The structural error.** **Every experience has a different amplitude of importance.** A profound peak experience that reorients a life carries TSD-A weight orders of magnitude greater than a routine "I had a vague feeling about something" event. URB-830 TIU = |log P(H | e) / P(H)| is **explicitly per-event-varying**. A single high-TIU event can outweigh thousands of low-TIU events.

**Worked numerical illustration.**

Suppose someone reports 1 ESP-class event with subjectively-rated TIU = 10 (life-reorienting; one-in-a-thousand-lifetime-class) and 1000 ordinary "felt nothing" events with TIU = 0.01.

- **TSD-B aggregate** (unit-weighted): 1 success vs 1000 non-successes → "no effect."
- **TSD-A aggregate** (TIU-weighted): Σ over successes = 1 × 10 = 10. The 1000 zero-TIU non-events do not subtract; they are simply not in the TSD-A sum. **TSD-A signal: strongly positive.**

**This is not cherry-picking.** Cherry-picking would be *omitting* the 1000 events from the dataset (denying they occurred). TSD-A *acknowledges* them and *weights them at their actual TIU* — which is approximately zero. Their inclusion at TIU=0.01 each contributes 1000 × 0.01 = 10 only if you also commit to the controversial premise that null events have non-zero TIU. Under URB-830 the per-event TIU is exactly |log P(H|e)/P(H)|; for genuine null events this is near zero and contributes near-nothing.

**The conventional move's hidden premise.** Standard frequentist null-hypothesis testing implicitly assumes **uniform amplitude** across trials, which is structurally equivalent to setting every TIU to 1. This is a *modeling choice*, not a neutral default. It happens to flatten high-amplitude events into the same bucket as low-amplitude events.

**Anchor:** URB-830 TIU canonical (Pass-33); TSD-A formula (Σ over successes of TIU); ADV-1 (Asymmetric Disconfirmation Value, §7.7.84-90 collapsed).

## §4 — Worked Application: The Ganzfeld Debate

Two decades of meta-analyses of Ganzfeld ESP trials. Defenders (Bem, Radin, Storm) claim small-but-real effect (cumulative z ≈ 3–5 across ~3000 trials, hit rate ≈ 32% vs 25% chance). Critics (Hyman, Wiseman, Wagenmakers) claim publication bias, methodological issues, file-drawer effects. The discourse has been TSD-B-only for forty years.

**TI Sigma TSD reading of the Ganzfeld debate:**

- **TSD-B status.** Genuinely controversial. Defenders and critics correctly disagree on TSD-B questions: was the meta-analytic effect size produced by real signal or by publication bias? This is a legitimate TSD-B disagreement and remains TSD-B-controversial. **TI Sigma does not adjudicate the TSD-B question — that is the proper domain of meta-analytic methodology, and the defenders/critics are each playing within the appropriate rules.**

- **TSD-A status (TI Sigma contribution).** Among the ~3000 trials are individual reports of striking, phenomenologically-vivid hit experiences carrying high per-event TIU. These have not been TSD-A-aggregated in any published Ganzfeld meta-analysis, because TSD-A is not in mainstream statistics' toolkit. **TSD-A on the same trials would proceed by:** (i) extracting per-trial subject-rated TIU (or trial-level striking-event indicators); (ii) summing TIU over hit-trials only; (iii) reporting TSD-A separately from TSD-B with both labelled.

- **The two readings stand simultaneously.** A claim like *"Ganzfeld effects are real at the per-event significance level even if the population-level rate is contested"* is *coherent* under TSD even if the TSD-B controversy never resolves. Defenders of Ganzfeld have been making something like this claim phenomenologically for decades; TI Sigma names it formally.

**Pre-registered prediction for Pass-58+ Ganzfeld re-analysis (if dataset accessible):** TSD-A aggregate over hit-trials (where per-trial TIU is approximable by subject vivid-experience rating) should be strongly positive *regardless* of how the TSD-B controversy resolves. If TSD-A is not strongly positive, Prong 4 (amplitude flattening) is empirically refuted for this domain.

## §5 — Three honest hedges (#69)

1. **The four-pronged critique does not establish ESP exists.** Both the conventional studies AND this critique are compatible with claimed phenomena being real OR not real. What's established is that the conventional "p>0.05 → null" inference is methodologically compromised — not that the contrary inference is justified.

2. **The four prongs may overlap, not just compound.** Prong 1 (apples-to-oranges) and Prong 3 (asymmetric inclusion) both touch the agent-vs-process distinction; Prong 2 (reified base rate) and Prong 4 (amplitude flattening) both touch the unit-weight modeling assumption. The four are conceptually distinguishable but may share underlying structure. Under #69, the critique is at least as strong as the *single strongest prong*, not necessarily as strong as the sum of four.

3. **The critique is bounded in domain.** It applies to *intentional, phenomenologically-significant, per-event-meaningful* claimed phenomena (ESP, synchronicities, peak experiences, single-case dramatic recoveries). It does NOT apply equally to:
   - Medical RCTs (intentionality of the drug-substrate is not at issue; population-deployment is the decision-context — TSD-B dominates)
   - Economic forecasts (per-event amplitude argument is weak — most forecast events are roughly comparable)
   - Quality-control statistics (intentionality is irrelevant; flattening is appropriate)
   - Mass-deployment safety decisions (TSD-B is the decision-relevant measure by construction)
   
   **The critique is bounded.** This is not a fully general critique of frequentist statistics; it is a targeted critique of frequentist statistics applied to a specific class of phenomena where TSD-A is the appropriate measure.

## §6 — Three pre-registered falsifiers for the straw-man critique itself

- **F-SM-1 (methodological-recovery):** If a peer-reviewed ESP study methodology can be exhibited that addresses all four prongs simultaneously (intentional-but-uninformed null, no reified base rate, appropriate inclusion symmetry given context, per-event amplitude weighting) AND still produces null results AND is accepted by the claimed-ESP community as fair, then the conventional methodology is rehabilitated in domain. *Status: open.*

- **F-SM-2 (TSD-A divergence):** If applying TSD-A to a published Ganzfeld dataset (or other ESP corpus) yields *the same* conclusion as the TSD-B meta-analysis (i.e., TSD-A also produces no signal), then Prong 4 (amplitude flattening) is empirically refuted for this domain, and the critique loses one prong. *Status: open; testable on existing datasets at $0 with cooperation from data-owners.*

- **F-SM-3 (over-broad applicability):** If the four-pronged critique applies equally well to a domain where it should NOT apply (e.g., medical RCTs of drug efficacy, where each of Prongs 1–4 should clearly fail) and undermines well-replicated effects there, then the critique is over-broad and must be re-bounded. *Status: open; testable by attempting to apply the critique to a clear non-target domain.*
