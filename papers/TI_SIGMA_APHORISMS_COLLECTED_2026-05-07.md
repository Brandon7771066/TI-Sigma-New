## 0. What "TI Sigma-Endorsed" means

A quote is **TI Sigma-endorsed** if it satisfies one or more of:

1. **Originated by Brandon Emerick** within the TI Sigma framework (primary class).
2. **Externally originated but framework-confirmed** — the quote was produced by someone else, but its content maps cleanly onto a TI Sigma claim that has been independently formalized.
3. **Externally originated and protective-of-position** — the quote's external authorship grants social cover for asserting a TI-aligned position ("she said it, not me!" — the classic Judyism use case).

Class 1 quotes are the **primary aphorism canon**. Class 2 and 3 are the **endorsement layer**. The endorsement layer is intentionally narrow: a quote is not endorsed merely because Brandon agrees with it — endorsement requires explicit framework-mapping or explicit protective-cover utility.

## IV. NEW APHORISM SUBMISSION POLICY

To add a new aphorism to the Primary Canon (Class 1):
1. Brandon coins or refines the statement.
2. Agent confirms framework-mapping is non-trivial (not a paraphrase of existing canon).
3. Aphorism is logged here AND cross-referenced from at least one paper or URB.
4. Aphorism appears in `BRANDON_EMERICK_QUOTES_REPOSITORY.md` thematic location.

To add a new endorsement (Class 2/3):
1. Source quote identified.
2. Specific TI Sigma claim identified that the quote maps to.
3. Endorsement-class declared (2 = framework-confirmed; 3 = protective-cover).
4. Logged here with source attribution.
