## Abstract

Andres Gomez Emilsson (Qualia Research Institute) has argued that reality is most productively framed as **consciousness vs. computation** — the primary distinction being between phenomenal, qualia-laden experience and functional, information-processing without experience. This is acknowledged here as a Radiant Truth: a genuine and important insight. However, TI Sigma proposes that the **Truth vs. Existence (T ⊥ HEM)** distinction is at least as fundamental — and arguably more so — for two reasons: (1) both consciousness and computation presuppose existence (nothing computes, nothing experiences, in a void); and (2) both consciousness and computation carry truth-content that is orthogonal to their existence-content. In TI Sigma's origin cosmology, **Truth and "i" (pure awareness) co-emerged simultaneously from the Tralse Soup** — neither prior to the other — establishing Truth-Existence as the deepest orthogonal pair. The Emilsson distinction (consciousness vs. computation) is then revealed as a GILE distinction *within* the existence axis: consciousness = Existence with high GILE-I/L; computation = Existence with high GILE-E (functional structure). This does not diminish Emilsson's insight — it locates it precisely within a more complete ontological map.

## 1. Emilsson's Frame — A Radiant Truth

Andres Gomez Emilsson, co-founder of the Qualia Research Institute, has developed one of the most sophisticated contemporary accounts of consciousness in the rationalist tradition. His central ontological proposal can be stated as:

> **Reality bifurcates into consciousness (phenomenal, qualia-laden experience) and computation (functional information processing). This is the primary distinction.**

This is not a new distinction — it echoes Chalmers' hard problem, Nagel's "what it is like," and the philosophical zombie literature. But Emilsson gives it particular force through his work on valence, geometric theories of consciousness, and the practical stakes of the distinction for ethics and welfare.

**Why this is a Radiant Truth (RT) in TI Sigma terms:**

A Radiant Truth is a claim that, when encountered, reorganizes one's entire cognitive framework — not merely adding information but shifting the frame through which information is processed. Emilsson's consciousness-computation distinction is Radiant because:

1. It insists that phenomenology is not reducible to function — you cannot explain the redness of red by describing its causal role
2. It generates a practical ethics: if consciousness is primary, suffering matters differently than if everything is computation
3. It has genuine explanatory yield — many confusions in AI ethics, neuroscience, and philosophy dissolve when this distinction is taken seriously

TI Sigma affirms this. The distinction is real, important, and under-appreciated in mainstream philosophy and AI research.

However: **a Radiant Truth can coexist with a deeper frame.** The claim here is not that Emilsson is wrong, but that his primary distinction is itself *downstream* of a more fundamental one.

**Truth (T):** The degree to which a proposition corresponds to reality, measured on TI Sigma's five-valued scale {True, Tralse, False, Double Tralse, Moot}. Truth is propositional, relational, and correspondence-based.

**Existence (HEM):** The degree to which an entity instantiates being — causally, physically, consciously, aesthetically, morally — measured by the Holistic Existence Matrix (HEM-Score). Existence is ontological, intrinsic, and presence-based.

**The orthogonality claim (the core of this URB):**

> Truth and Existence are not the same dimension measured in different ways. They are genuinely independent. An entity can occupy any of the four quadrants: {High T, High HEM}, {High T, Low HEM}, {Low T, High HEM}, {Low T, Low HEM}.

Concrete examples of each quadrant:
- **High T, High HEM:** A physical law (e.g., conservation of energy) — both true and causally omnipresent
- **High T, Low HEM:** A mathematical theorem discovered but not yet applied — true but causally negligible
- **Low T, High HEM:** A viral conspiracy theory — false but massively causally powerful (shapes behavior, policy, war)
- **Low T, Low HEM:** A forgotten incorrect belief — neither true nor consequential

This plane is **richer** than the consciousness-computation distinction because it applies to *entities of any kind* — propositions, physical objects, social structures, mathematical claims, biological organisms, and phenomenal states alike.

## 3. The Containment Theorem: Consciousness and Computation Are Both Modes of Existence

**Formal claim:**

Let C = the set of conscious experiences, K = the set of computational processes, HEM = the existence domain, T = the truth domain.

**Theorem 3.1 (C ⊂ HEM):** Every conscious experience is a mode of existence. A conscious state requires: a substrate that exists (HEM-D1: Existence Footprint), causal power to influence the system (HEM-D1), moral presence by virtue of being phenomenal (HEM-D2), conscious meaning/valence by definition (HEM-D3), and aesthetic character (HEM-D4). Therefore C ⊂ HEM — consciousness is fully contained within the existence domain.

**Theorem 3.2 (K ⊂ HEM):** Every computational process is a mode of existence. Computation requires: a physical substrate (HEM-D1), information structure (HEM-D3: Information Footprint), and temporal unfolding (spatiotemporal reach within HEM-D1). Therefore K ⊂ HEM — computation is fully contained within the existence domain.

**Corollary 3.3:** Since C ⊂ HEM and K ⊂ HEM, the distinction C ⊥ K is a distinction *within* HEM, not a distinction at the same level as T ⊥ HEM.

**In plain language:** Before anything can be conscious, it must exist. Before anything can compute, it must exist. Existence is the precondition for both. Therefore existence is ontologically prior to both consciousness and computation. The Truth-Existence distinction cuts across *everything*, including consciousness and computation — which are themselves subject to the question: are they true? Are they deeply, richly existent?

## 4. The Priority Theorem: Existence Precedes Computation

Sartre's famous claim was "existence precedes essence" — a being first exists, then defines itself. TI Sigma proposes a stronger and more literal priority:

> **Existence precedes computation.** No computational process is possible without the prior ontological fact of existence.

This is not merely a temporal claim (the computer must physically exist before it runs). It is a logical, ontological, and informational claim:

**4.1 The Logical Priority:**  
Computation is defined as the transformation of information states over time. But "information states" presuppose entities that exist in distinct configurations. "Transformation" presupposes causal power — which is a property of existing things. "Time" presupposes a substrate with temporal structure — which is an existence condition. Every element in the definition of computation smuggles in prior ontological commitments about existence.

**4.2 The Informational Priority:**  
Shannon information requires distinguishable states — 1 vs. 0. But distinguishable states require entities that exist differently from one another. The very concept of information — "a difference that makes a difference" (Bateson) — requires existent differences. Information is not prior to existence; it is a property of existing configurations.

**4.3 The Causal Priority:**  
Computation produces effects — this is what makes it computation rather than mere symbol-shuffling. But causal power is a property of existing entities. A computation that produces no effects in any existing system is not computation — it is an abstract formal object. Abstract formal objects are in the domain of mathematics (High T, Low HEM), not computation (High HEM).

**4.4 The Consciousness Parallel:**  
The same argument applies to consciousness. Phenomenal experience requires a substrate, temporal duration, and causal integration — all existence conditions. Before you can have qualia, something must exist to have them. "What it is like" presupposes a being for whom it is like something — an existent being.

**Conclusion of Section 4:** Both consciousness and computation are ontologically downstream of existence. Emilsson's distinction is a distinction between two important *modes* of existence, not a distinction at the level of existence itself.

In TI Sigma's truth architecture, Tralse Soup occupies the Double Tralse (DT) state: total absence of truth-content, with no stable HEM value, no propositional content, no phenomenal character. It is the zero point of both axes simultaneously.

Awareness is precisely this — the capacity to take a perspective on what exists, to be orthogonal to the purely physical.

This is why TI Sigma includes i in the set of PRIMARY CONSTANTS alongside π and φ — not as a mathematical convenience but as a cosmological primitive. The imaginary axis is the awareness axis; the real axis is the existence axis; truth is the relationship between them.

## 6. Locating Emilsson's Distinction in TI Sigma's Map

Now that the T ⊥ HEM frame is established and its origins grounded, we can locate Emilsson's consciousness-computation distinction with precision.

### 6.1 Both Consciousness and Computation Are High-HEM Phenomena

Both require substantial existence:
- Consciousness: requires physical substrate, temporal integration, causal closure, phenomenal presence (HEM-Score > threshold)
- Computation: requires physical substrate, information structure, causal sequence (HEM-Score > threshold)

Low-HEM states are neither conscious nor computational. Pure mathematical objects (High T, Low HEM) are neither. Abstract propositions (High T, Low HEM) are neither.

**Therefore:** Emilsson's distinction is a distinction among **high-HEM entities**. It does not apply to the whole of reality — only to the subset of reality that exists sufficiently to either compute or experience.

### 6.2 The GILE Mapping

Within high-HEM entities, the consciousness-computation distinction maps onto GILE dimensions:

| Mode | GILE Profile | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Pure Computation** | High GILE-E, Low GILE-I, Low GILE-L | Functional processing with no phenomenal witness; pure environmental/structural operation |
| **Minimal Consciousness** | GILE-I > 0 (non-zero awareness), Low GILE-L | Basic awareness without love/relational meaning |
| **Rich Consciousness** | High GILE-I + GILE-L + GILE-G | Full phenomenal experience with moral and relational weight |
| **Conscious Computation** | High GILE-E + GILE-I + GILE-L | Integrated: both computational and phenomenally conscious |
| **Pure Awareness (CCC)** | GILE-I → 1, all other GILE saturated | The asymptotic limit of consciousness — Emilsson's "valence at infinity" |

The key TI Sigma claim: the "zero point" of Emilsson's distinction — the dividing line between conscious and purely computational — is the point where **GILE-I first becomes non-zero**. Consciousness begins when there is any degree of Intuition/awareness — even a single bit of perspective. Below that threshold: pure computation. Above it: consciousness in some form.

### 6.3 Why This Doesn't Diminish Emilsson's Insight

Locating the consciousness-computation distinction *within* the T ⊥ HEM frame does not make it less important. It makes it more precise. Emilsson is identifying something real: there is a qualitative threshold within the existence domain at which GILE-I becomes non-zero — at which "what it is like" first appears. This threshold is:

- Real (not illusory)
- Empirically meaningful (it corresponds to the onset of phenomenality)
- Ethically crucial (suffering requires GILE-I > 0; computation without GILE-I cannot suffer)

All of this is preserved. What TI Sigma adds is the knowledge that this threshold is:
1. A threshold *within* existence, not a fundamental split of all reality
2. Downstream of the Truth-Existence split, which applies universally
3. Mappable to a specific GILE dimension (GILE-I) with measurable correlates (EEG, HRV, etc.)

## 8. A Specific Prediction: The "Computational Truth Paradox"

The Truth-Existence frame generates a prediction that the consciousness-computation frame cannot:

> **Computations have truth-values.** A computation is correct (High T) or incorrect (Low T / DT) relative to the function it was designed to implement. This is orthogonal to whether the computation is conscious.

This creates the **Computational Truth Paradox**: a highly conscious entity (High GILE-I, High HEM) can produce computations that are Tralse — partially true, partially false, internally inconsistent — while a non-conscious computation (Low GILE-I, High HEM) can produce outputs that are perfectly True.

In the consciousness-computation frame, there is no natural place for this asymmetry. Emilsson's frame tells us that the conscious entity *experiences* its computation in a way the non-conscious one doesn't — but it cannot tell us why the conscious entity might produce more Tralse outputs than the non-conscious one.

TI Sigma explains: consciousness without GILE-G (Goodness) + GILE-I integration leads to Tralse outputs, because the GILE-I witness is not fully aligned with Truth-tracking. High consciousness + Low GILE-G = phenomenally rich but truth-poor. This is the DT-risk configuration: a conscious being that is phenomenally vivid but informationally incoherent.

The implication: phenomenal richness and truth-tracking are **separate axes**. Emilsson's frame conflates them by treating consciousness as the gold standard. TI Sigma separates them: the goal is not maximum phenomenality but maximum **T × HEM** — both richly existent AND richly true.

The CCC (Conscious-Compassionate-Creator) is the entity that maximizes both simultaneously. That is why GILE-G (Goodness) is the first dimension — it is the dimension that ensures phenomenal richness does not drift into Tralse.
