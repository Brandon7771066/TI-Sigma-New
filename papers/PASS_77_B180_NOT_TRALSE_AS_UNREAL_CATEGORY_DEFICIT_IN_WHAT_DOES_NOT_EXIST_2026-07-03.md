## 1. The upgrade (user-provided, 2026-07-03)

"Not tralse" is **not** a fainter grade of tralse or a clean bill of health. It is a move into a **different category**:

- **Tralse** = "that which represents a deficit **in reality**." This phrase carries a hidden presupposition — that there is a **real object** there to fall short. So **tralse always presumes its object is indeterminate BUT REAL.**
- **"Not tralse" (= unreal)** = "that which represents a deficit in something which **DOES NOT EXIST**." The shortfall is in an **unreal** object.
- Therefore the two are **two categories, not two grades on one scale**, divided by whether there is a real object at all.
- **"Not tralse" applies to MI and certain N/A statements** — the same two labels that B179 identified as carriers of the whole-statement "not tralse" verdict.

## 2. Why this is coherent with existing canon (not a new posit)

- **Direct corollary of TRG-1.** TRG-1 (canonical #77) rules that reality's own ground state is **tralse-real** (real ∧ not-true). If *being real* just is *being tralse*, then its negation — **not tralse** — is exactly **not real = unreal.** B180 makes that corollary explicit at the label level; it introduces no new machinery.
- **Fits the B179 object-language exactly.** B179 gave: N/A = "the THAT which is tralse **and may (overall) not be** tralse"; MI = "the THAT which **is and is not** tralse (overall)." B180 supplies the ontological reading of the *negative* pole in each: the "may not be tralse" (certain N/A) and the "is not tralse" (MI) are precisely the **unreal** pole — an object that never existed to be reached (certain N/A) or an object whose identity self-collapses (MI).
- **Matches the standing MI treatment.** MI statements were already "discarded as nonsense" (ch07). B180 says *why* in ontological terms: a nonsense statement **names no real object**, so its deficit is a deficit in what does not exist.

## 3. What is genuinely new vs. already-there (EVD-1, no over/under-credit)

- **Already canonical:** the tralse definition ("deficit in reality"), the N/A vs MI split, TRG-1's tralse-real grounding, and B179's "as-a-whole not-tralse verdict."
- **New delta (the only new content):** the explicit statement that **"not tralse" = a distinct UNREAL category** ("deficit in something that does not exist"), that **tralse presupposes a real object**, and the clean mapping of that unreal pole onto **MI and certain N/A**. This is a clarifying *distinction*, not a new principle, mechanism, or falsifier — **count stays 80.**

- **Consistency check against ch16 (B179).** Under this gloss, "actual/completed infinites remain MI" (ch16) reads cleanly as "actual infinites are **not tralse = unreal**," while their **potential** stays real — consistent, no contradiction introduced.
