```
NORMAL STIMULANT RESPONSE:
Stimulant → ↑DA release → ↑D1/D2 activation → Focus/Motivation
                         ↓
                    Reuptake via DAT
                         ↓
                    Recycled for next release

BRANDON'S DEPLETED RESPONSE:
Stimulant → ↑DA release (from depleted stores)
                ↓
        Less DA available to release
                ↓
        Shorter duration of effect
                ↓
        D2 receptors DOWNREGULATED from chronic use
                ↓
        DAT UPREGULATED (faster reuptake)
                ↓
        COMT rapidly metabolizes remaining DA
                ↓
        NET EFFECT: DIMINISHED RESPONSE

MATHEMATICAL MODEL:
Effective_DA = (Baseline_Stores × Release_Factor) - (Reuptake_Rate × Time) - (COMT_Metabolism)

For Brandon:
- Baseline_Stores: LOW (chronic depletion)
- Release_Factor: HIGH (stimulant-induced)
- Reuptake_Rate: HIGH (DAT upregulation)
- COMT_Metabolism: HIGH (genetic + substrate availability)

Result: Brief spike, rapid crash, net deficit
```

### 2.2 The Five Bottlenecks

```
BOTTLENECK 1: PRECURSOR DEPLETION
├─ Tyrosine → L-DOPA → Dopamine
├─ Rate-limiting: Tyrosine hydroxylase (TH)
├─ Status: Substrate insufficient for demand
├─ Evidence: Stimulant tolerance pattern
└─ Solution: L-Tyrosine, Mucuna (already doing, but not enough)

BOTTLENECK 2: VESICULAR STORAGE
├─ VMAT2 packages DA into vesicles for release
├─ Stimulants cause massive vesicular release
├─ Refill rate slower than demand
├─ Result: "Empty gun syndrome" - trigger pulled, no bullets
└─ Solution: Reduce stimulant dosing, add recovery periods

BOTTLENECK 3: RECEPTOR DOWNREGULATION
├─ D2 receptors internalized after chronic stimulation
├─ Fewer receptors = reduced signal even with DA present
├─ Evidence: Need higher doses for same effect
└─ Solution: Receptor upregulators (TAU, Uridine), drug holidays

BOTTLENECK 4: TRANSPORTER UPREGULATION
├─ DAT increases to compensate for excess DA
├─ Faster reuptake = shorter effect duration
├─ Creates "rebound" as DA cleared too quickly
└─ Solution: Not more stimulants (worsens problem)

BOTTLENECK 5: COMT METABOLISM
├─ COMT enzyme breaks down DA in PFC
├─ You have 45 COMT SNPs (genetic variants)
├─ Likely FAST metabolizer = rapid DA clearance
├─ Result: PFC DA levels drop quickly
└─ Solution: COMT support (SAMe, Catechins - but careful)
```
