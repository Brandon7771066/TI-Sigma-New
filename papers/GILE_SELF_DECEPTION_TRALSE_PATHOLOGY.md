This view has become the operating assumption of modern cognitive science: humans are fundamentally irrational, and truth-seeking is a thin veneer over deeper self-serving processes.

**The error in this view:** It confuses a *pathological state* with a *design specification*. A doctor observing a population with widespread vitamin D deficiency does not conclude that humans are "designed to have weak bones." A doctor observing widespread depression in an industrial society does not conclude that sadness is the human baseline. The error is mistaking the statistical prevalence of a condition for its necessity.
