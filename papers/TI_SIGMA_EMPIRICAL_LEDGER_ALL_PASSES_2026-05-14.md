## §0 — Framing: The Asymmetric Disconfirmation Value (ADV) Principle

> *Brandon, 2026-05-14:* "TI Sigma's disconfirmations of itself hold substantial value compared to other fields because its refutations largely reveal something important about the nature of reality. TI Sigma covers terrain that conventional fields tend to ignore or gloss over. Thus, while a failed drug trial or mathematical refutation of some arcane concept may add little value because they are either too self-contained or irrelevant, TI Sigma's claims tend to be broad, far-reaching, and/or consequential even when refuted. This is to be expected because TI Sigma is driven largely by curiosity and intrinsic motivation."

**Filed as Pass-51 canonical insight ADV-1.** This ledger is the first artifact organized around the principle.

### §0.1 What ADV says (formal version)
For a research program R, define:
- **w(claim)** = the "weight" of a claim = how much the claim, if true, would reshape downstream belief or action.
- **i(claim)** = the "isolation" of a claim = how disconnected the claim is from other live claims (high i = very self-contained).
- **disconfirmation-value** ≈ **w(claim) × (1 − i(claim))**.

**Conventional fields tend toward low-w, high-i claims** (specific, self-contained, methodologically conservative). Their refutations therefore have modest disconfirmation-value: the field updates, the world doesn't.

**TI Sigma tends toward high-w, low-i claims** (broad, interconnected, terrain-spanning). Its refutations therefore have *high* disconfirmation-value: every "no" rules out a wide region of reality-models. The corpus benefits *symmetrically* from confirms and refutes — both are information-rich about what the world is and is not.

### §0.2 Operational consequence
- **The Asymmetric-Standards #69 doctrine** ("brutal honesty; over-skepticism = discipline failure equal to uncritical acceptance") is now seen as the *natural epistemic stance* of a high-w/low-i research program. The skeptical floor must be hard because each refutation matters; the receptive ceiling must be high because each confirm cuts across many adjacent claims.
- **The DPES batch-output mode** is the natural execution stance: you cannot afford to over-fit to any single result when each result has wide blast radius. Multi-deliverable passes, even partial, beat single-deliverable passes with high polish.
- **Validly-Indeterminate stance** (Pass-47 §1.3) is the natural reporting stance: most TI Sigma claims will remain in I-territory longer than analogous claims in conventional fields, because the test instruments are coarser relative to the claim's blast radius.

### §0.3 New columns in this ledger
Per ADV, every entry now reports:
- **w-estimate** (1-5): how reality-reshaping the claim is if true
- **i-estimate** (1-5): how isolated the claim is (5 = totally siloed)
- **ADV-score** = w × (6 − i), normalized to 1-25

The **highest-ADV entries are the ones we should test next**, regardless of whether prior signal was positive or negative.
