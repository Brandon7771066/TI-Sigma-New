## 1. The Insight

One of life's greatest lies that seems to be true: **"Nobody cares."**

The truth is that passionate and good-natured people do exist but lack the **Capital, Competence, and Connections** to make a difference.

### The Three C's of Social Connections

| C | Definition | What it enables |
|---|---|---|
| **Capital** | Financial, social, and temporal resources | The autonomy to act on good intentions |
| **Competence** | Skill, knowledge, and domain expertise | The ability to translate intentions into effective action |
| **Connections** | Relationships, networks, and access | The reach to deliver action to where it matters |

Without all three, good-natured people are structurally prevented from making the difference they want to make. The cynical conclusion ("nobody cares") is a misattribution — it confuses structural incapacity with moral indifference.
