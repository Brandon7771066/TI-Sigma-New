## §1 — What is a meta-collapse?

A regular off-rhythm-collapse condenses 7-12 individual `replit.md` Pass-entries into one paragraph (Pass-22, Pass-27, Pass-31, Pass-34, Pass-38 = 5 precedents). A **meta-collapse** condenses *multiple already-collapsed paragraphs* into a single line that delegates to:

1. The per-pass anchor papers (already linked in original entries).
2. The per-collapse meta-papers (Pass-34 collapse paper for §7.7.55-66; Pass-38 collapse paper for §7.7.67-73).

The meta-collapse is *display-only*; no content is erased. Anyone reading `replit.md` with the meta-collapse line can recover full detail by following the chain: meta-collapse line → per-collapse paper → per-pass paper.
