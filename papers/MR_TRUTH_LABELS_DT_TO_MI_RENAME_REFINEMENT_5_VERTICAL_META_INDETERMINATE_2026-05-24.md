## 1. The Two Components of Refinement #5

Refinement #5 packages TWO substantive canonical changes that landed together:

### 1.1 Component A — Canonical Rename: DT → MI (Meta-Indeterminate)

**Old canonical name:** DT (Double Tralse)
**New canonical name:** MI (Meta-Indeterminate)
**Status of old name:** LEGACY-EQUIVALENT — "DT" remains valid in all historical corpus references; "MI" is the new preferred canonical form going forward; the two are interchangeable canonical labels for the same MR Truth Labels base-4 cell.

**Brandon's justification (3 reasons):**
1. **"Double" is structurally ambiguous** — could mean vertical (meta-level) or horizontal (two-in-conflict). The actual canonical content requires vertical reading; the word "Double" does not enforce this.
2. **MT is already taken** as a canonical abbreviation (Meta-Truths, 12 in urb_608 + 24 conjectural in urb_639).
3. **"Meta-Indeterminate" resonates with academics** — the prefix "Meta-" is well-understood in analytic philosophy + logic + cognitive science; "Indeterminate" is already the canonical name for the base-4 cell I (Pass-65 refinement #1 specified Tralse = universal quality, Indeterminate = specific MR2 state).

### 1.2 Component B — Vertical/Horizontal Axis Sharpening

**Brandon's structural insight:** the relationship between the two tralsities in an MI proposition-pair is **VERTICAL (meta-level: tralsity-of-tralsity)**, not **HORIZONTAL (object-level: two-in-conflict)**.

**Operationalization:**

| Relation type | Structure | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **Horizontal conflict** | Two predicates P₁, P₂ that DIRECTLY NEGATE each other when applied to the same subject (e.g., bachelor-married, even-odd, alive-dead) | INCOHERENT (per MR-IDC-1) — NOT MI |
| **Vertical meta-tralsity** | Two tralse propositions P₁, P₂ each individually conceivable, whose JUXTAPOSITION creates a meta-level tralsity that cannot itself be mentally actualized (e.g., square-circle, liar self-reference, Russell-set membership) | MI (Meta-Indeterminate) |

**The vertical/horizontal axis is the missing operational hinge** that refinement #4 pointed toward but did not explicitly name. Refinement #4 said "combination is inconceivable"; refinement #5 specifies WHY some combinations are inconceivable while others are merely incoherent: **inconceivability arises from vertical meta-structure; incoherence arises from horizontal predicate conflict.**

## 2. Worked Examples Under Refinement #5

### 2.1 Bachelor-married = HORIZONTAL conflict = INCOHERENT, not MI

- P₁ = "X is a bachelor (unmarried man)"
- P₂ = "X is married"
- Relation: P₂ directly negates the "unmarried" component of P₁'s definition
- Structure: HORIZONTAL (both predicates apply to same subject X at same object-level)
- Mental actualization: the mind CAN entertain "married bachelor" as an analytic-target (incoherent-but-conceivable)
- Verdict: **INCOHERENT, NOT MI**

### 2.2 Square-circle = VERTICAL meta-tralsity = MI

- P₁ = "object O is a square (has 4 equal straight sides and 4 right angles)"
- P₂ = "object O is a circle (set of points equidistant from a center)"
- Relation: P₁ and P₂ do NOT directly negate each other definitionally (a triangle satisfies neither and is consistent); they create a meta-level tralsity about whether such an object can exist
- Structure: VERTICAL (the conflict is at the meta-level of "what kind of object could satisfy both?", not at the predicate level of "do these predicates negate each other?")
- Mental actualization: the mind CANNOT actualize even a candidate object — the imagination returns NULL
- Verdict: **MI (Meta-Indeterminate)**

### 2.3 Liar sentence = VERTICAL meta-tralsity = MI

- P₁ = "this sentence asserts proposition S"
- P₂ = "proposition S is false"
- Relation: P₁ and P₂ are not direct negations; the meta-tralsity arises from the SELF-REFERENCE — at the meta-level, asking "is the sentence true?" yields tralsity-of-tralsity (if T then F, if F then T, neither stable)
- Structure: VERTICAL (the conflict requires going up to a meta-level of evaluation)
- Mental actualization: the mind oscillates without stabilization (the meta-evaluation produces NULL stable content)
- Verdict: **MI (Meta-Indeterminate)**

### 2.4 Russell set = VERTICAL meta-tralsity = MI

- P₁ = "R is the set of all sets that do not contain themselves"
- P₂ = "R ∈ R ↔ R ∉ R"
- Relation: not direct negations; the meta-tralsity emerges at the set-membership-meta-level
- Structure: VERTICAL
- Verdict: **MI (Meta-Indeterminate)**

### 2.5 Schrödinger's cat (analytic-target reading) = HORIZONTAL = INCOHERENT, not MI

- P₁ = "cat is alive"
- P₂ = "cat is dead"
- Relation: direct horizontal predicate conflict
- Structure: HORIZONTAL (at the object-level, alive/dead are mutually exclusive states)
- Mental actualization: the mind CAN entertain the superposition as analytic-target (this is what makes the thought experiment discursively useful)
- Verdict: **INCOHERENT (when read object-level), NOT MI** — note that if read as a QUANTUM superposition (different ontology), the predicate "alive XOR dead" is replaced by a quantum-state predicate that IS coherent at its own ontological level; this is HMR not MI

## 3. Updated 3-Step Assignment Heuristic (replaces refinement #4 v1)

Given a candidate proposition or proposition-pair under consideration:

**Step 1 (Decompose).** Identify the two-tralse-propositions structure. If the candidate appears as a single proposition, extract the two implicit components.

**Step 2 (Individual conceivability + tralseness check).** Verify each P₁, P₂ is individually conceivable AND individually tralse-classifiable.

**Step 3 (Vertical-vs-horizontal axis check).** Determine the structural relationship between P₁ and P₂:
- **Horizontal** (predicates directly negate each other when applied to the same subject) → INCOHERENT, not MI
- **Vertical** (relation requires going up to a meta-level of evaluation; the combination creates tralsity-of-tralsity) → check step 4
- **Multi-framing-conceivable** (multiple labels apply at different levels of description) → HMR (per HMR-1 refinement #3)

**Step 4 (Mental-actualization-attempt at the meta-level).** For vertical-axis candidates, attempt to mentally actualize the meta-level object/state the combination requires:
- NULL mental content (imagination returns nothing) → **MI** (canonical Meta-Indeterminate)
- Oscillating-without-stabilization (mind cycles through readings without settling) → **MI** (vertical-recursive subtype)
- Stable meta-frame achievable → NOT MI (likely HMR)
