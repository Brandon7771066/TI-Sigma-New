### 2.2 C₁ — Coherence (Internal Non-Contradiction)

**Definition:** The degree to which the entity's internal propositions, states, or actions are mutually consistent — no subset implies a contradiction.

**Measurement scale:** 0 to 1

| Score | Anchor |
|---|---|
| 0.0 | Every claim/action directly contradicts another — systematic self-refutation |
| 0.2 | Frequent contradictions that undermine core claims |
| ET (≈ 0.42) | Enough internal consistency to be truth-assessable; contradictions present but not fundamental |
| 0.65 | Mostly coherent; isolated inconsistencies that can be resolved |
| 0.92 | Virtually self-consistent; minor edge cases only |
| 1.0 | Perfect internal coherence — no logical tension anywhere |

**Measurement procedure:**
1. Identify the N core propositions/claims/actions of the entity.
2. For each pair (pᵢ, pⱼ), score: 0 if they contradict, 0.5 if they are neutral, 1 if they support each other.
3. C₁ = (mean pairwise consistency score), normalized to [0,1].

**Automated proxy (text/argument):** Contradiction-detection NLP; logical consistency checking.
**Behavioral proxy (person/organization):** Self-reported belief–action alignment; behavioral consistency across contexts.

### 2.3 C₂ — Concreteness / Genuineness

**Measurement scale:** 0 to 1

| Score | Anchor |

| 0.65 | Mostly genuine; specific domains of performance that do not reach the core |
| 0.92 | Authentically present; minor social performance within norms |
| 1.0 | Perfect transparency — no gap between inner and outer |

**Measurement procedure:**
1. Collect self-report, observed behavior, and independent assessment.
2. Calculate discrepancy score between self-report and external observation.
3. C₂ = 1 − normalized_discrepancy.

**Proxies:** Self-discrepancy index (clinical psychology); Linguistic Inquiry and Word Count (LIVC) authenticity markers; third-party behavioral rating.

### 2.4 C₃ — Continuity / Life-Preservation

**Definition:** The degree to which the entity's actions and existence sustain, amplify, and extend the being of self and others — rather than depleting, destroying, or truncating existence.

**Measurement scale:** 0 to 1

| Score | Anchor |

| 1.0 | Maximal life-amplification — every action extends and enriches existence |

**Measurement procedure:**
1. Identify the entity's primary actions/outputs over a reference period.
2. For each action: score +1 if existence-amplifying, 0 if neutral, −1 if existence-depleting.
3. C₃ = (sum of scores + N) / (2N), normalized to [0,1].

**Proxies:** Mortality/morbidity outcomes (medical); ecosystem net productivity (environmental); organizational sustainability metrics (organizational).

$$\text{HEM} = w_{D1} \cdot \hat{D1} + w_{D2} \cdot D2 + w_{D3} \cdot D3 + w_{D4} \cdot D4$$

where $\hat{D1}$ is the log-normalized EF (per URB #625), and default weights are currently equal pending empirical calibration.

### 6.2 HEM-D1 — Existence Footprint (Operationalized)

**Formula (URB #609, #619, #625):**
$$\text{EF} = f \cdot A \cdot R_{ST} \cdot \text{AMI}$$

Where:
- **f** = frequency of manifestation (oscillation rate, occurrence rate, or recurrence interval — domain-specific)
- **A** = amplitude / intensity of causal effect when manifest
- **R_ST** = normalized spatiotemporal reach (spatial extent × temporal duration, normalized to LCC level)
- **AMI** = Average Mutual Information (informational coupling to the environment, from URB #619)

**LCC-relative normalization (URB #625):**
$$\hat{D1} = \sigma\left(\frac{\log_\phi(\text{EF}) - \mu_{LCC}}{\sigma_{LCC}}\right)$$
where σ is the logistic function, and μ_LCC and σ_LCC are the mean and standard deviation of log_φ(EF) within the entity's LCC level.

**Domain-specific f, A, R_ST measures:**

| Domain | f | A | R_ST |
