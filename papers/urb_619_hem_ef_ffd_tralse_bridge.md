### 2.3 The Contradiction Language: Inconsistency, Opposition, Discrepancy

The contradiction analysis from the Tralseness measurement framework identified three types of contradiction:

| Type | Definition | Example |
|------|-----------|---------|
| **Inconsistency** | Two claims cannot both be true in the same logical space | "The door is open" + "The door is locked shut" |
| **Opposition** | Two claims are at opposite ends of the same axis | "Life is beautiful" + "Life is suffering" |
| **Discrepancy** | Two claims partially overlap but diverge in their implications | "She loves him" + "She chose to leave" |

**Theorem (Contradiction–Tralse Taxonomy):** These three contradiction types are the *interpersonal/linguistic face* of the three FFD indeterminate states, and all three resolve to Tralseness:

| Contradiction Type | FFD Descriptor | Truth Architecture State | HEM Measurement |

| Opposition | Indeterminacy (genuine open outcome — both poles are real) | Tralse (pure midpoint — equally supported extremes) | D2 ↑ + D1 ↑ (complex system with high contradiction) |
| Discrepancy | Constraint (the limiting structure generates the gap between partial truths) | Tralse-True or Tralse-False (closer to resolution on one side) | D2 moderate (partial coherence remains) |

**All three** are manifestations of Tralseness at different intensities and resolvability levels:

- **Inconsistency** produces *Tralse-Indeterminate*: the most stable form of Tralseness (the two claims keep bouncing without collapse)
- **Opposition** produces *pure Tralse* (midpoint): highly unstable, requires MR to collapse toward one pole
- **Discrepancy** produces *partial Tralse*: one side is stronger; MR will typically collapse toward Tralse-True or Tralse-False

The key unification: **all three contradiction types describe the same fundamental situation** — a system that has not undergone complete Myrion Resolution. The "contradiction" is not an error or defect in the system; it *is* the Tralse state, awaiting MR.
