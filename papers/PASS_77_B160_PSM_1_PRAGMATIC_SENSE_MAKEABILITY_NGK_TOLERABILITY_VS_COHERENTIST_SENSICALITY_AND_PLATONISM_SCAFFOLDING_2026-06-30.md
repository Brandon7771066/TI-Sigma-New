## 2. PSM-1 — the tolerability / sensicality split (the genuine delta)

**PSM-1 (Pragmatic Sense-Makeability, CANDIDATE).** Whether a concept near or past the I/MI frontier can be *held with value* is decided by **sense-makeability**, which decomposes into two scaffolds doing two different jobs:

| Scaffold | What it does | What it does **not** do |
|---|---|---|
| **Negative Gap Knowledge (NGK)** — grasp the item by the gap it marks | Makes the item **TOLERABLE**: holdable in mind without breaking, a real cognitive object known by its edges | Does **NOT** make it **SENSICAL** / true / coherently-valued on its own |
| **Coherentist support-premises** — embed the item in a web of genuine additional premises | Can make the item **SENSICAL**: confers *coherent value*, a usable meaning | Does not by itself confer *content-truth* (still subject to MR labels) |

Two consequences, both the author's:
- **NGK alone ≠ sense.** NGK is the *tolerability* channel. This sharpens the existing NGK doctrine: NGK was already "you can grip a nonsense claim by its gap"; B160 adds the **explicit limit** that gripping ≠ making-sense — sense is a *further* achievement requiring coherentist premises.
- **Coherentism is the *sensicality* channel.** "Making pragmatic sense … via other premises" = classical **coherentism** (a belief/concept earns standing by cohering with a supporting web), here used not as a theory of *truth* but as a theory of *usable sense*. Kin already in canon: POD-1's pragmatic register ("best answer given present limits") and CAD's value-floor ("pragmatic value OR substantial intrinsic meaning").

**Anti-cheat rail (part of the principle, #69).** Coherentist scaffolding must add **genuine supporting premises that change the item's usable status** — it must not be mere *verbal relabeling* of nonsense as sense. If *any* MI claim could be trivially "scaffolded" into sense by renaming, PSM-1 would erode the MI screen and be vacuous. Guarded by `PSM-1-F1`. (Parallels RTI-1's "no post-hoc wildcard" and TSG-1-F5's "concrete checkable token" rails.)

## 3. Application — B159's Platonism "does-and-does-not-exist" is scaffolded, not bare MI

The worry PSM-1 answers: *isn't "everything potential already exists AND does not" just a flat contradiction — bare MI (identity-contradiction kind)?* No — it clears PSM-1 because it is genuinely scaffolded:

- **Coherentist premise (sensicality): "exists in POTENTIAL."** This is a *real* supporting premise, not a relabel: potential existence is an independently-motivated notion (EPE-1's Existence-Potential; the potentialist tradition in philosophy of mathematics). It splits the apparent contradiction into two *different* predicates — *potential* subsistence (affirmed) vs *official/instantiated* existence (denied) — so the "AND" is no longer `P ∧ ¬P` over one predicate. That is exactly what lifts it out of the identity-contradiction MI kind.
- **"Official" as an explicit scaffolding qualifier.** "Officially exists" is a *stipulated standing predicate* (instantiation-conferred), flagged as scaffolding so the reader does not read it as bare ontological existence. This is the same move as B159's "official-existence" clause, now named as scaffolding.
- **NGK (tolerability): the gap we know by its edges.** We understand that we can only *positively* know the **instantiated**, and we know the **non-instantiated** *negatively* — by the precisely-shaped hole it leaves (NGK). So even the residue that resists positive grasp is *tolerably held*, not gibberish.

**Result:** Platonism-as-tralse is a **coherently-scaffolded tralse** (MR2-Indeterminate with substantial leeway under `in-1`), **not** an MI collapse. This is a *stronger* defense of B159 than B159 gave itself: B159 asserted the both-and; B160 shows *why it is sense-making and not nonsense*.

## 5. Falsifier (new; OPEN)

- **PSM-1-F1 (scaffolding must be load-bearing, not verbal).** If an arbitrary MI claim (a genuine identity-contradiction or category-error, e.g. "a square circle exists officially") can be "scaffolded into pragmatic sense" by PSM-1's machinery **without** adding any genuine predicate-splitting or standing-conferring premise — i.e. if the coherentist channel relabels nonsense as sense — then PSM-1 erodes the MI screen and is vacuous, and the tolerability/sensicality split collapses (NGK-tolerability would be doing all the work, with "sensicality" an empty honorific). Anti-cheat: a putative scaffold must be shown to *change what the concept licenses in use*, not just its wording.
