## 2. What L\*/+E Means

The GILE framework is not a flat list of four equally-weighted dimensions. It has a hierarchical algebraic structure:

**L\* (the GIL core):** Goodness, Intuition, and Love form a unified algebraic core. They are not independent of each other — high G constrains I and L (Goodness sets the conditions under which Intuition and Love operate authentically); high I enables L (accurate other-perception enables genuine other-orientation); high L amplifies G (genuine care for others deepens moral commitment). The three GIL dimensions are mutually conditioning constituents of a single coherent unit. This unit is designated L\*.

**+E (the appended Environmental layer):** The Environment dimension is *added* to L\* as an outer layer, not integrated into it as a constituent. The +E notation means E is appended — structurally exterior to the core — rather than derived from or coupled to the GIL core.

**The structural implication:** In a system where E were derived from L\* (where E was a function of GIL), then knowing GIL would determine E, and knowing E would constrain GIL. The system would show reliable correlation in both directions. The Inverse Metric Problem would be self-correcting, because high GIL would eventually produce observable high-E effects, and the physicalist measurement system would eventually detect them.

The empirical observation is the opposite: E and GIL can vary independently across the full range of both quantities. This independence is precisely what L\*/+E predicts. It is not what a tightly coupled or derived structure would predict.
