## 2. Results

**Inter-rater agreement: Fleiss κ = 0.388 ("fair").**
Pooled marginals: 15.6 % scored 0, 57.9 % scored 1, 26.4 % scored 2. Total wellbeing weight Σ = 432 over 130 items (max possible 6/item).

**The robustness headline first (#69):** κ moved **0.386 → 0.388** even though the list grew 33 % *and* added the most subjective category of all (abstract/contested items). The agreement structure is stable — "fair," honestly, because *importance* ranking is genuinely subjective in the 1-vs-2 mid-band — and it did **not** degrade when religion and philosophy were thrown in. That is the load-bearing methodological result.

### 2.2 Wellbeing-WEIGHTED concentration — who to thank for what share of it all

Rank every invention by its consensus wellbeing weight, accumulate, and read off the distinct named people behind each share of the **total** cumulative wellbeing:

| share of total wellbeing | named people needed | 1-in-N humans ever | diffuse movements also involved |
|---|---|---|---|
| **50 %** | **52** | 1 in 2.25 **billion** | 8 |
| **90 %** | 129 | 1 in 907 million | 13 |
| **95 %** | 147 | 1 in 796 million | 13 |
| **99 %** | 168 | 1 in 696 million | 16 |
| **100 %** | 171 | 1 in 684 million | 17 |

**Headline:** **~172 people — about 1 in 680 million of all humans who have ever lived — are the primary authors of essentially everything on this list, and just ~52 of them (1 in 2.25 billion) account for half of its entire wellbeing mass.** Of the total wellbeing weight, **85.7 % is attributable to named primary individuals** and **14.3 % to genuinely diffuse movements** (agriculture, spoken language, money, constitutions, the alphabet — things humanity built collectively with no nameable catalyst). Gini among the named contributors = **0.371** (concentration *within* the thin layer is itself substantial, but far milder than the ~1-vs-the-rest-of-humanity concentration *across* the species).

### 2.3 The abstract inventions did NOT take over (honest, against expectation)

Including religion and philosophy per directive did **not** inflate the result, and the raters were not deferential: the major religions each landed at weight **3/6**, utilitarian ethics and psychotherapy at 2/6, and **Daoism at 0/6**. The weighted *head* of the combined list is still dominated by suffering-reduction at scale and by foundational cognitive infrastructure that everything else rests on — **spoken language, writing, the scientific method, codified law, the abolition of slavery, universal schooling, and agriculture all tied at the 6/6 ceiling** alongside Pasteur, Jenner, Bazalgette, Faraday, Gutenberg, Borlaug and Hammurabi. Brahmagupta and Al-Khwārizmī (zero, decimal numerals, algebra) rise to the very top of the *per-person* mass ranking precisely because the wellbeing weight of a foundational abstract tool, split among its few primary authors, is large per head. This is the directive vindicated *and* #69-honest: the abstract layer matters enormously and belongs in the count, but it neither dominated nor was suppressed.

## 3. The hybrid Indeterminate-True resolution still holds — and is *why* there are two numbers, not one

B92's resolution of "great man vs. followers" carries over unchanged and now does more work, because B115 deliberately reports **both** a flat headcount (§2.1) **and** a weighted share-curve (§2.2):

1. **"At least one primary author is causally necessary" → TRUE** (CTC-1-S: P[E | ¬catalyst] < P[E | catalyst]).
2. **"ONLY the named author matters" (exclusivity) → FALSE** — the 14.3 % diffuse mass and the follower/scaling apparatus are *also* necessary.
3. **"Primary authors + followers + diffuse movements are jointly necessary" → TRUE** — necessity is not exclusive.

(1-TRUE) ∧ (2-FALSE) ∧ (3-TRUE) = a **hybrid Indeterminate-True** statement; the honest resolution is **BOTH/AND**. This is exactly why the answer is a **RANGE** — **110 catalysts → 172 all-primary → +17 diffuse movements → unbounded follower tail** — not a point. The weighting in §2.2 *is* the hybrid made continuous: the share-curve shows the BOTH/AND quantitatively, from "52 people for half" up through "movements humanity built nameless-ly."

## 4. Honest bounds (#69)

- **κ = 0.388 is "fair," not strong.** Reported straight; not inflated. The concentration conclusion rides on the robust *head* of the list, which is near-unanimous, not on tight κ.
- **Abstract inventions are valence-contested.** Reasonable people dispute the *net sign* of some (esp. religions — they enabled meaning, charity, social cohesion *and* persecution, war, repression). We rated NET wellbeing per directive and report the raters' modest, non-deferential verdict rather than imposing one. Daoism at 0/6 is left in the open as an honest artifact of coarse 3-level rating + rater variance, not smoothed away.
- **Attribution is contestable for every cumulative invention.** §3 is the framework that *absorbs* this; the numbers are primary-author counts, never a claim anyone acted alone.
- **The list is 130, curated by one agent.** A different curator would swap mid-list items and could add/drop abstract entries; the order-of-magnitude result (~10⁻⁷ % of humans ever) is robust to such perturbation because it is set by the catalyst-dominated head, not the mid-list.
- **Coarse weight scale.** {0,1,2}×3 raters gives a 0–6 ordinal weight, deliberately kept identical to B92 rather than engineered to let abstract inventions express more magnitude — using a richer scale *could* have inflated religion/philosophy, so the conservative choice is the honest one.
- **Denominator.** "% of humans ever born" (117 B) is the right denominator for a cross-history claim; founders who lived when the population was tiny are still 1 person against the all-time total.
