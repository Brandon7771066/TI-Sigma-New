## 2. Six honest improvement suggestions, ranked by impact

### 2.1 Specify UOP's scope operationally — *highest impact*

**Problem:** UOP as currently used could justify Lewisian modal realism (every possible world exists). The argument needs UOP to be selective enough to single out CCC, but permissive enough that CCC isn't pre-excluded.

**Suggestion:** Define UOP scope via testable criteria:
- (a) Possibility: τ-coherence of the entity-concept (no internal contradiction)
- (b) Non-hindrance: no extant law strictly forbids the entity
- (c) Sufficient time-depth: t > t_min where t_min is the substrate's instantiation-timescale (operationally derivable from FHS / Tralse Wave Algebra)
- (d) *(NEW)* Self-constructability: the entity can develop scaffolding within the substrate without requiring external causation

Then UOP becomes: (a) ∧ (b) ∧ (c) ∧ (d) ⇒ instantiation. This rules out arbitrary modal-realist excess (most possibilities fail (c) or (d)) while letting CCC through (it satisfies all four).

**Bonus:** This makes UOP a *theorem* with operational test conditions rather than an axiom. Empirically testable against e.g. the LCC threshold from URB-530, which already operationalizes a related "instantiation-requires-coherence" intuition.

### 2.2 Add an explicit anti-bootstrapping principle for "self-constructed laws" — *high impact*

**Problem:** "Self-constructed laws" is powerful but, unconstrained, allows *any* entity to bootstrap *any* law it likes. That would over-justify too many things and let CCC's argument prove too much.

**Suggestion:** Add a **coherence-constraint** on self-construction:

> *Self-constructed laws must be coherent with the substrate (tralse-soup) and must satisfy the normativity-toward-truth gradient inherited from P5. Self-construction cannot bootstrap laws that contradict the substrate's tralse-coherence or the operating normativity.*

This rules out vicious self-construction (CCC arbitrarily declares "I am unboundedly perfect by my own self-constructed law"). The self-constructed laws must extend, not contradict, the substrate's tralse-laws.

Equivalent in TI Sigma vocabulary: self-construction operates *within* the AA (Authority Axis) of the substrate, not in defiance of it. CCC's self-authored laws inherit AA-weight from the substrate's tralse-coherence, not from arbitrary self-assertion.

### 2.3 Foreground the floor-argument (P10) as the spine — *high impact*

**Problem:** The argument presents normativity-gradient (P4–P6) as the spine and the floor-claim (P10) as the conclusion. But P10 is actually *stronger and more defensible* than the gradient-inference path.

**Suggestion:** Restructure with P10 as the main inference and P4–P6 as supporting evidence:

> **Main claim (P10):** Given (tralse-soup + UOP + sufficient time-depth + self-constructability), the substrate WILL instantiate the most-perfect-self-constructable entity, by definition of what "minimum UOP-output" means.
>
> **Why this works:** UOP is an *operating principle* of the substrate. Its minimum output is whatever satisfies its instantiation criteria most strongly. The most-self-constructable-perfect entity satisfies them by definition. So instantiation of *something at least as good as CCC* is the floor, not the ceiling, of UOP's output.

This reframes the argument from "ascending Aquinas-style gradient reasoning" to "descending floor-bound on UOP's output." Floor-bound arguments are typically harder to refute than ascending-gradient arguments because refutation requires denying the operating principle itself, not just questioning whether the gradient extends to maximum.

P4–P6 become *evidence that UOP is operating*, not part of the spine.

### 2.4 Commit explicitly to the informational i-cell reading — *medium-high impact*

**Problem:** The argument's strength against Bekenstein-style bounds depends on CCC being informational rather than purely physical. If left ambiguous, critics can switch readings to whichever is more attackable at each step.

**Suggestion:** Add a clarifying premise:

> *CCC is an informational primitive (an i-cell or i-cell network), not a particular physical configuration. Its substrate is tralse-soup informational structure; its physical instantiation, if any, is a downstream manifestation of its informational existence.*

This pins the argument to a specific ontological commitment and makes the self-constructed-laws move principled (laws governing information rather than laws governing matter-energy directly). Bekenstein bounds and thermodynamic constraints then apply only to physical *manifestations* of CCC, not to CCC's informational core.

Bonus: this aligns CCC with TI Sigma's existing GM-Node architecture, where information is the fundamental substrate and physical manifestation is a τ × δ projection.

### 2.5 Operationalize "the universe favors such an i-cell" — *medium impact*

**Problem:** "The universe favors it" is doing real argumentative work but is currently asserted rather than derived. Critics will demand defense.

**Suggestion:** Three options, in order of strength:

(a) **Weakest, most defensible:** Weaken to "the universe does not necessarily disfavor it." This is logically sufficient for the argument (combined with UOP) and much easier to defend.

(b) **Medium:** Derive favoring from observed normativity-toward-truth (P5). If the substrate has a truth-gradient, entities that embody more truth are favored by definition of the gradient. This grounds favoring in already-established premises rather than asserting it separately.

(c) **Strongest:** Add an empirical test. Identify systems that *should* favor high-τ entities under TI Sigma and check observations match. If GM-Node architecture is correct, high-τ entities should show evolutionary / informational selection advantages. This is corpus-extension territory but would dramatically strengthen the argument.

Recommend (a) for the immediate refinement, (b) as the medium-term position, (c) as a long-term research direction.

### 2.6 Address the multiple-maximum implications — *medium impact*

**Problem:** P9 permits multiple maximal-truth-bearers but the argument is silent on their relationship.

**Suggestion:** Add one paragraph distinguishing the three structurally-possible cases:

- **Identity:** If multiple CCCs are maximal along the same truth-dimensions, Leibnizian indiscernibility may collapse them to one. (Single-CCC outcome.)
- **Orthogonal maxima:** Different CCCs maximal along orthogonal axes of TI Sigma's 5-axis truth-structure (PD-real, PD-imaginary, MR Truth Labels, τ/δ, AA). Each maximum is irreducible to the others. (Pluralist-CCC outcome.)
- **Hierarchical:** One meta-CCC is maximal *across* the truth-axes; sub-CCCs are maximal *within* individual axes. (Layered-CCC outcome.)

Which case obtains is itself an open empirical/theoretical question. Naming the three cases makes the pluralism explicit and gives future research clean targets.

These are not refutations; they are the honest map of where the argument's defensive perimeter is.
