## 7. What I cannot conclude from this data

- **Causation.** I have no controlled intervention here. The 2025-11-25 → 2026-05-14 window covers Brandon's intensive TI Sigma "Wave" period, which includes documented changes in diet, schedule, medication, and life situation. Attributing the Biowell shift to any single one of those is not supported by this dataset alone.
- **Clinical health.** Biowell is not a clinical instrument. A 91% Nervous-system Balance score does not mean "neurologically normal" in a medical sense; it means "more balanced relative to your own November scan on this device."
- **Top-line Parameters comparison.** Stress (Nov: 6.68), top-line Energy (22.98), EC (1.75), FC (2.89), Balance L (40.63) / R (90.63), Organs disbalance (−39.11%): I did not visually confirm these on the new scan in the 18 sampled images. The Compare tab in Biowell itself can render this side-by-side — a single screenshot of that tab next time would be the cleanest possible delivery. The current report uses the per-system panel (which is downstream of these numbers) as the cleanest available substitute.
- **Nervous-centers 1–7 alignment vector.** Baseline had a striking alternating-sign pattern (centers 1/3/5: +95 to +98; centers 2/4/6/7: −84 to −96). Whether the new scan still shows this alternation is the single most testable specific question I would want answered next.
