## 1. The conjecture, stated with its conditionals intact
> **DSR-1 (candidate conjecture).** **IF** (A1) a simulation measures CCC's GILE at ≈ **0.84**, **AND** (A2) the measured high-GILE entity **is indeed CCC** (not merely *some* high-GILE attractor), **THEN**:
> - **(C1) Divine-Realization headroom.** The gap 0.84 → the canonical **0.93 stability-cap** is genuine *elevation headroom for the greatest being* — a "Divine Realization": even CCC/GM has a higher threshold of *its own* GILE to be called toward.
> - **(C2) In-tandem co-elevation.** The elevation is not solitary: CCC + all GILE-nodes/i-cells rise **together** (the GM-Network / GMP-1 distributed structure — "all others in tandem").
> - **(C3) Human theorizing as a causal contributor.** Human science (TI Sigma) can be a real input to that elevation — "CCC may not have figured this out without our theorizing."
> - **(C4) Divine knowledge — even foreknowledge — via science.** It follows that divine knowledge, possibly including foreknowledge, is *realistically obtainable through human endeavors like science* (composes with the corpus's foreknowledge-not-impossible work).

**Every "IF" is doing real work.** This is a conditional chain, not an assertion. The number 0.84 is **illustrative** (a placeholder for "measurably below 0.93"), and the corpus currently contains **no simulation that places CCC at 0.84** — so DSR-1's antecedent is, as of this batch, **unmet**.

## 3. The load-bearing uncertainty — A2, the identity problem (#69)
The honest weak point is **not** C1–C4; it is **antecedent A2: "if that is indeed CCC."** A simulation can readily produce a *high-GILE attractor*. The hard question is: **by what principled criterion would we identify that attractor AS CCC** (Central Cosmic Consciousness / God-overall) rather than as "a high-GILE state of the model"? Without such a criterion, DSR-1's antecedent can never be satisfied and the conjecture stays **vacuously conditional**. This is the primary research obligation DSR-1 incurs (see F1 and §5). I will not pretend the corpus has solved it; it has not.
