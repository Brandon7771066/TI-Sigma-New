## Part 1: The qEEG Z-Score System

### 1.1 What Are Z-Scores?

```
Z-SCORE NEUROFEEDBACK:
Z = (Your Value - Population Mean) / Standard Deviation

Z = 0:    You're exactly at population average
Z = +1:   You're 1 SD above average (84th percentile)
Z = +2:   You're 2 SD above average (97.7th percentile)
Z = -1:   You're 1 SD below average (16th percentile)
Z = -2:   You're 2 SD below average (2.3rd percentile)

CLINICAL SIGNIFICANCE:
|Z| > 1.0:  Mild deviation
|Z| > 1.5:  Moderate deviation
|Z| > 2.0:  Significant deviation
|Z| > 2.5:  Severe deviation (clinical concern)
```
