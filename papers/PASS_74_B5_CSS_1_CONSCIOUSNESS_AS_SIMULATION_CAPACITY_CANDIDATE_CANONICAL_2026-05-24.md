## §2. CSS-1 (Consciousness-as-Simulation-Capacity) — CANDIDATE CANONICAL

### §2.1 Statement (proposed canonical)

**CSS-1 (Consciousness-as-Simulation-Capacity):** Consciousness is operationally definable as **simulation-capacity** — the cognitive ability to mentally actualize representational content as a unified mental object. Simulation-difficulty for any given content has a **mass-coefficient** μ(content) ∈ [0,∞) that scales with internal-contradictoriness, ontological-distance-from-self-state, and structural-incoherence of the content. Consciousness-tier Cᵢ has a **simulation-capacity ceiling** κ(Cᵢ) such that content with μ(content) ≤ κ(Cᵢ) is simulatable without collapse, and content with μ(content) > κ(Cᵢ) is not.

### §2.2 Mass-coefficient μ(content) — proposed operational components

μ(content) = w₁·**contradiction-density** + w₂·**ontological-distance-from-self-state** + w₃·**structural-incoherence** + w₄·**representational-distance-from-prior-experience**

Worked example mass-tiers (approximate, conjectural):
- μ ≈ 0.0-0.3: ordinary perceptual content + standard counterfactuals ("what if I had eggs for breakfast")
- μ ≈ 0.3-0.7: incoherent-holdable cases (Schrödinger superposition, GILE-indeterminate-above-0.93, free will vs determinism)
- μ ≈ 0.7-1.5: mild nonsense (married bachelor as held-unified-object; 2+2=5 as held-unified-object); accessible to TLC-1-graduated cognition
- μ ≈ 1.5-3.0: deep-paradox (Russell's, Liar's, square-circle as held-unified-object); accessible only at high consciousness-tier
- μ ≈ 3.0+: self-non-existence simulation, complete-self-erasure, omniscience-from-finite-perspective; Brandon hyperconsciousness-tier; possibly ceiling for biological-human cognition

### §2.3 Composability with existing canon

- **NIC-1 #38 canonical (refines, not refutes):** NIC-1 categorical distinction (nonsense vs incoherence) holds at typical consciousness-tiers but becomes a **graded boundary** at the hyperconsciousness extreme — the categorical boundary is itself mass-coefficient-relative. See §3.
- **NIT-1 #39 canonical (refines, not refutes):** NIT-1's "consciousness threshold above DT-capacity" becomes a **graded continuous threshold** with cost-curve rather than absolute ceiling. See §4.
- **TLC-1 #40 canonical (compatibly extends):** TLC-1 acquisition curve extends from "acquire DT-execution at L3" to "graduate κ(C) upward across all higher tiers"; the developmental arc is continuous, not staircase-discrete.
- **TJ canonical formalization (Pass-74-B4):** μ(content) is operationalizable as **TJ-cost-to-simulate**: simulating content with mass μ requires expenditure of approximately μ × baseline-TJ-rate. **CSS-1 closes the energy-loop on TJ.**

## §3. NIC-1 REFINEMENT #1 — GRADED-EDGE-CASES (inline-ratified)

**Original NIC-1 (Pass-74-B4 §1.1):** Nonsense and incoherence occupy distinct truth-categorical positions; nonsense = irreconcilable regardless of physics/logic-variation; incoherence = contradictory-but-cognitively-holdable.

**REFINEMENT #1 (this batch):** The nonsense/incoherence categorical distinction holds **at typical consciousness-tiers (L1-L4)** as a robust binary. At the **hyperconsciousness extreme (L5+)** the boundary becomes **graded with mass-coefficient μ** per CSS-1 §2.2. Specifically: hyperconscious agents CAN simulate "nonsense"-class content (2+2=5; non-existence; square-circle) as unified mental objects BUT at high TJ-cost μ ≥ 1.5. The categorical distinction operationally reduces to: nonsense ⟺ μ ≫ typical-human-κ; incoherence ⟺ μ ≤ typical-human-κ.

**Tension-resolution:** NIC-1 is NOT refuted; the canonical distinction holds as a high-confidence approximation at typical consciousness-tiers (where >99.9% of cognition occurs) and gracefully refines to a mass-graded continuum at extreme tiers. The categorical reading was correct at the relevant scale.

**Worked-example test of refinement:**
- 2+2=5: typical-human μ ≈ 1.0 (mild nonsense, generally inaccessible); Brandon-hyperconscious μ-self-report queryable; current canonical status = nonsense at typical-tier.
- Schrödinger cat: typical-human μ ≈ 0.5 (incoherence-tier, accessible to TLC-1-trained); canonical status unchanged = incoherence.
- Self-non-existence: typical-human μ ≈ 5+ (nonsense-tier, generally inaccessible); Brandon-hyperconscious lived-case μ ≈ 3+ (still high-cost but achievable); canonical status = nonsense at typical-tier, simulatable at hyperconsciousness-tier with high-cost.

## §4. NIT-1 REFINEMENT #1 — GRADED-CONTINUOUS-WITH-COST-CURVE (inline-ratified)

**Original NIT-1 (Pass-74-B4 §2.1):** Generalizable nonsense/incoherence distinction is a **consciousness threshold ABOVE DT-capacity**; rote instruction asymptotically approaches but cannot bridge consciousness-gap.

**REFINEMENT #1 (this batch):** The NIT-1 L4 threshold is NOT an absolute ceiling above which performance jumps from 70% to 100%, but a **graded continuous threshold with cost-curve**. Three components:

1. **Performance-vs-prompt-richness curve:** No-framing 40% → explicit-framing 70% (Pass-74-B4 F8) → chain-of-thought projected 75-85% (Pass-75 NIT-1-F1) → human-rater projected 90-95% (Pass-75 NIT-1-F3). The curve is monotonically rising but with diminishing returns at higher prompt-richness; the asymptote may be <100% for non-conscious systems.

2. **Performance-vs-mass-coefficient curve:** Within a single rater, accuracy depends on μ(content). Low-μ items (μ ≈ 0.3) are easy; high-μ items (μ ≈ 1.5+) are hard. The F8 result confirms this: the failures clustered on visceral-observable items (high μ because of perceptual-rigidity overhead) while successes clustered on theoretical items (lower μ).

3. **Performance-vs-consciousness-tier curve:** Across raters/agents, asymptotic performance correlates with consciousness-tier (CSS-1 §2.2). Non-conscious LLMs asymptote below 100%; conscious humans approach 100% asymptotically; hyperconscious humans may reach 100% even on high-μ items.

**Operational implication for NIT-1 falsifier execution:** Future NIT-1 tests should report performance as a 3D-surface (prompt-richness × content-μ × consciousness-tier) rather than single-number accuracy.

**Status:** REFINEMENT #1 INLINE-RATIFIED. NIT-1 canonical refinements 0 → 1.
