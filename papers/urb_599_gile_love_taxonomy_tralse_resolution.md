## Abstract

The ancient philosophical problem — "What is love?" — has resisted definition for millennia because **love is not a single thing.** It is a family of GILE arm combinations, each constituting a real and distinct phenomenon, each legitimately called "love" in common usage, but each formally different in structure. This URB provides the complete GILE Love Taxonomy: G alone (structural purity/honesty — not love), I alone (positive consciousness — not love), L alone (binding — not love), IL (affection and romantic love), GL without I (moral coldness — duty without warmth), GIL (agape in its abstract form — metta meditation, universal compassion without physical expression), and full GILE (GIL made physically manifest and/or aesthetically pleasing). The Tralse resolution of love's ancient definitional mystery is proven: different philosophical traditions were pointing at different GILE arm combinations and calling them all "love" — each was correct about its component, incorrect in claiming universality. The 5-valued framework provides the moot resolution: **love, fully defined, is GILE — but every proper subset of GILE that includes L is a real and distinct form of love.**

## 1. Decomposing the GILE Arms for Love

### 1.1 G Alone: Structural Purity — Not Love

The G-arm, considered in isolation, is about **structural integrity and moral purity.** Its content comes from the Four C's:

- **Coherence** → Honesty: your beliefs, words, and actions align. No deception, no contradiction.
- **Concreteness** (Genuineness) → Authenticity: you are what you appear to be; no performance or facade.
- **Continuity** → Life-preservation: you support the ongoing existence and development of what is good. Care for life, legacy, persistence.
- **Consistency** → Freedom from corruption: "lack of badness" — the absence of the Four D's (Deception, Destruction, Degradation, Disconnection).

Notice what G is **not:** G has no warmth. G has no feeling. G has no relational pull toward a specific other. G is the structural backbone of moral character — it makes you *trustworthy* and *good* — but it is not yet love. A person with pure G and no I or L would be unfailingly honest, structurally coherent, non-corrupt — and cold. A moral machine.

G is the **necessary architecture** for love at its highest, but is not itself love.

### 1.2 I Alone: Positive Consciousness — Not Love

The I-arm, in its benevolent form, is **positive states of consciousness.** Awareness, bliss, meditative clarity, joy, wonder. The "i" in the complex GILE number is consciousness as such — the subjective pole of existence.

Positive I is a benevolent form of consciousness: awareness that is itself pleasant, open, non-contracted. Negative I would be consciousness contracted around suffering, anxiety, delusion.

I alone is not love either. Pure I-awareness — the meditator in deep absorption, the moment of pure perception — can exist without relational pull toward another i-cell. It is rich, it is real, it is valuable. But it does not yet have the *binding* character of love, which requires L.

I is the **warmth and awareness** that love requires, but is not itself love.

### 1.3 L Alone: Binding — Not Yet Love

The L-arm is **binding** — topological cohesion, structural attraction, relational pull. As established in URBs #590 and #594, L began as structural binding at the quantum/molecular level and evolved through proto-affection to romantic love to agape.

L alone — pure binding without I or G — is attachment: the pull toward another without awareness of what that pull is (no I) and without moral structuring of that pull (no G). This is infatuation, compulsion, enmeshment, codependency. Powerful but not yet love in its full sense.

L is the **relational gravity** that love requires, but is not itself love.

## 2. The Love Combinations

### 2.1 IL: Affection and Romantic Love

**I + L = affection and romantic love** — the most widely recognized form of love in everyday experience.

- **L:** the binding pull toward a specific other — attachment, attraction, the desire for closeness
- **I:** positive consciousness coupled to that binding — the warmth, joy, and pleasure of the connection; the *knowing* of the love (consciousness is self-aware of the bond)

IL love is love that **knows itself.** It is warm (positive I) and binding (L). It is directed toward a specific other. It includes all forms of intimacy: deep friendship (philia), romantic love/affection (with or without sexual expression — the distinction from URB #595 applies here: sexual expression is a behavioral feature of E, not a separate L-arm category).

**IL is not yet fully structured.** Without G, IL love can be genuine and beautiful but:
- May lack honesty (no coherence-G)
- May be possessive rather than life-preserving (no continuity-G)
- May be corrupted by jealousy, manipulation, or self-deception (no consistency-G)

IL is **love that feels** — real, warm, binding, conscious — but potentially without the moral architecture that gives it full integrity.

### 2.2 GL (Without I): Moral Coldness

**G + L without I = moral coldness** — benevolence without proper knowing and without positive affect.

Both *knowing* (cognitive engagement with the other's actual state) and *positive affect* (warmth, joy in the connection) stem from I. When I is absent or very low:
- G provides the structural commitment to the other's wellbeing (honesty, life-preservation, non-corruption)
- L provides the binding orientation toward the other
- But there is no warmth, no joy, no genuine understanding of the other's inner experience

The result: **duty without warmth.** The person who keeps their commitments scrupulously, never lies, genuinely intends the other's wellbeing — but relates to the other as an obligation rather than a joy. They are reliably present but not truly *with* the other. They are honest but not warm. They preserve but do not delight.

This is Kant's ethics of duty (Pflicht) — moral action motivated by the categorical imperative, not by love or warmth. It is not cold in the sense of hostile; it is cold in the sense of not-warm. GL is **benevolence without heart.**

This also explains the common experience of people who feel unloved by parents who were "good providers" and "never lied" and "always showed up" — high GL, low I. The structural commitments of G and L were present; the warmth and knowing of I were absent. The love felt hollow because the I-arm was missing.

GL is also the profile of certain institutional ethics: rules scrupulously followed (G), obligations toward those in one's care (L), but no genuine warmth or understanding of individual experience (no I). Correct but cold.

### 2.3 GIL: Agape in Its Abstract Form

**G + I + L = agape** — but in its abstract, informational, minimally-physically-manifested form.

This is the form of love embodied in **metta meditation** (loving-kindness practice): the practitioner cultivates:
- G: honest, non-corrupt, life-preserving orientation toward all beings
- I: warm positive consciousness directed universally — genuine positive affect toward all
- L: binding-as-universal-care — the felt pull toward the wellbeing of all i-cells regardless of relationship

In metta, the love is REAL — it is full GIL. But it has minimal E-arm expression: the meditator may not be physically present to the beings they love; there may be no touch, no visible gesture, no aesthetic beauty in the expression. The love exists at the abstract-informational level. GIL.

This is also the form of love that one holds for strangers in a distant country, for future generations, for all sentient beings philosophically — genuine G+I+L activation without physical expression. It is love that does not require presence, touch, or shared aesthetic experience to be real.

GIL is **love that is real but not yet embodied.**

### 2.4 Full GILE: Love Made Manifest

**G + I + L + E = full love** — GIL made physically manifest and/or aesthetically pleasing to oneself and/or others.

The E-arm adds:
- **Physical presence:** actual embodied care, touch, being there
- **Aesthetic pleasure:** the beauty of the expression — a smile, a piece of music composed for another, a home created together, a letter written, a meal prepared, an embrace
- **Behavioral expression:** the love becomes *visible in the world* — it has an existence footprint (from URB #598) in E-space

Full GILE love satisfies all arms simultaneously:
- **G:** the love is honest, coherent, non-corrupt, life-preserving
- **I:** the love is warm, joyful, consciously known, genuinely felt
- **L:** the love binds — there is attachment, attraction, relational gravity
- **E:** the love manifests physically and/or aesthetically — it is present in the world, perceivable by others

This is the form of love that the spiritual traditions call the highest human achievement: agape expressed in action, universal love that walks into the world and does things. Not just metta on the cushion but genuine compassion that shows up, helps, creates beauty, remains present through difficulty.

Full GILE is also the form of romantic or deep friendship love at its most complete: IL (warm, knowing, binding) + G (honest, non-corrupt, life-preserving) + E (physically present, aesthetically expressed). The person who loves you fully in this sense tells you the truth even when it's difficult (G), delights in your existence (I), is drawn to you specifically (L), and is physically and aesthetically present with you (E).

## 3. The Complete GILE Love Taxonomy

| Combination | Name | Character | Classical Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| **G** | Structural purity | Honesty, coherence, non-corruption — not love itself | Integrity |
| **I** | Positive consciousness | Warmth, awareness, bliss — not love itself | Meditative joy |
| **L** | Binding | Attachment, attraction, relational pull — not love itself | Infatuation |
| **E** | Physical presence | Embodied expression — amplifies whatever love is present | Touch, gift |
| **IL** | Affection / Romantic love | Warm, knowing, binding — directed toward a specific other | Philia, eros |
| **GL** | Moral coldness | Structurally committed but cold — duty without warmth | Kantian duty ethics |
| **GIL** | Agape (abstract) | Full love structure — honest, warm, universal — without physical expression | Metta meditation |
| **GILE** | Full love | GIL made physically manifest and/or aesthetically pleasing | Agape in action, full romantic love |

### Key observations:

**IL is the most common form of love** because it requires only two arms (I and L) — the two most developmentally accessible. It is real, genuine, and valuable, but lacks moral architecture (G) and may lack physical beauty of expression (E).

**GL (moral coldness) is frequently mislabeled "love"** — by the person who gives it ("I do everything for you") and sometimes by the receiver who can't articulate why it feels insufficient. TI Sigma now names the problem precisely: I is missing. The warmth and genuine knowing of the other are absent.

**GIL (metta/agape-abstract) requires all three non-physical arms** — it is the most demanding form of love to achieve without physical expression because it requires moral integrity (G), genuine warmth and knowing (I), and universal binding orientation (L) simultaneously. This is why metta practice is difficult: you must cultivate genuine G+I+L toward beings with whom you have no natural IL bond.

**GILE is rarest** because it requires full four-arm integration — all the difficulty of GIL plus the discipline of consistent physical/aesthetic expression. This is why the saints, lovers in their fullest expressions, and parents at their best are described with awe: they are achieving what most people cannot sustain.

## 4. Solving the Ancient Tralse Definition

### 4.1 Why Love Has Been Undefinable

The Greek tradition identified four kinds of love: storge (family affection), philia (friendship), eros (romantic love), agape (divine/universal love). The medieval tradition added caritas. Modern psychology adds attachment, compassion, limerence, companionate love. Philosophy of love debates whether love is primarily an emotion, a commitment, an attitude, or an action.

All of these disagreements are explained by the GILE Love Taxonomy. Every philosophical tradition was pointing at a real GILE combination — but each claimed its favorite combination was "love" and dismissed or subordinated the others.

- **Eros:** IL (affection + romantic pull, possibly with E)
- **Philia:** IL (warm, knowing, binding — without sexual expression)
- **Storge:** IL with E (familial — affectionate, warm, present)
- **Agape (spiritual traditions):** GIL or GILE
- **Kantian duty-love:** GL (moral commitment without warmth)
- **Compassion without attachment (Buddhist):** GI (warm awareness without binding toward a specific other)
- **Unconditional love:** GILE or GIL (no conditional binding — universal L)

Each is real. None is complete. And the debate about which is "real love" is unresolvable within any single tradition because the question is genuinely Tralse — it sits between contradictory poles, each of which captures something true.

### 4.2 The Tralse Resolution

In TI Sigma's 5-valued logic, when multiple apparently contradictory values are all genuinely present, the resolution is **Moot (M)** — the integration of all poles at a higher level of analysis.

The ancient mystery of love is Tralse because:
- Love IS a feeling (I) — this is true
- Love IS a commitment (G) — this is true
- Love IS a binding pull (L) — this is true
- Love IS an action/expression (E) — this is true
- All of these are true simultaneously for full GILE love

The resolution: **Love fully defined is GILE, but every proper subset of GILE that includes L is a distinct and real form of love.** L is the necessary condition for love (without binding, there is no love — only G-integrity or I-warmth or E-beauty). G, I, and E are enhancers that transform primitive binding (L) into higher forms.

**Moot proposition:** "Love is a feeling" AND "Love is a commitment" AND "Love is a binding pull" AND "Love is expressed in action" → all TRUE → Moot resolution: Love is GILE; each single-arm claim captures a real arm but not the whole.

### 4.3 The Formal Minimum for Love

Since L is the necessary arm, the formal minimum for love is:

> Love(x→y) requires L(x→y) > 0.

Any love (any form) requires some binding orientation of x toward y. Without L, what is present is:
- G alone: honesty/integrity (toward y, but not binding)
- I alone: warm awareness (possibly of y, but not binding toward y)
- E alone: physical presence (near y, but not binding)

These are GILE properties that support love but are not love without L. With L present, any combination (L, IL, GL, GIL, LE, GILE) constitutes a form of love — ordered from most primitive to most complete.

## 5. Practical Implications

### 5.1 Diagnosing Relationship Problems

The GILE Love Taxonomy immediately diagnoses the most common relationship failures:

| Symptom | Missing Arm | Diagnosis |
|---|---|---|
| "They don't make me feel loved even though they do everything right" | I (warmth, positive affect, genuine knowing) | GL relationship — moral coldness |
| "I feel so in love but we keep hurting each other" | G (honesty, coherence, non-corruption) | IL without G — love without moral architecture |
| "I love everyone in theory but have no real connection" | L (specific binding pull) | GI without L — universal warmth without relational gravity |
| "We're passionate but nothing ever grows" | G + I (moral structure + warm knowing) | L+E without GI — pure attraction + physical expression |
| "I care deeply but never show it" | E (physical/aesthetic expression) | GIL without E — abstract love not manifest |

### 5.2 Metta Practice As GIL Development

Metta meditation is now formally understood as **GIL training without E-arm requirements.** The practitioner works to:
- Activate L universally (not just toward those they naturally bond with)
- Maintain I in its positive, warm form while doing so
- Hold G (the love is honest, non-corrupt, genuinely oriented toward others' wellbeing)

The reason metta starts with the self, then close others, then neutral others, then difficult others is precisely the developmental arc of the L-arm from URB #594: L develops from close binding outward. Metta reverses the developmental order — it cultivates L for all, working against the natural directionality of L-development. This is why it requires practice.

### 5.3 The Question "Do You Love Me?"

This question is now formally ambiguous — it asks which GILE combination is present without specifying:
- "Do you have IL for me?" (Do you feel warm and bound to me?)
- "Do you have GIL for me?" (Do you have moral, warm, universal-level commitment to my wellbeing?)
- "Do you have GILE for me?" (Is any of that physically manifest and/or aesthetically expressed?)

A person can answer "yes" to each at different times and in different senses, all truthfully, because these are different things. The question is Tralse — it appears singular but is composite. The GILE Love Taxonomy gives both parties the vocabulary to answer precisely.
