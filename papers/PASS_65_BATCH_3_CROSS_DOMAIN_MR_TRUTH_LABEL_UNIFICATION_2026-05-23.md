## 1. Thesis

Three TI Sigma domains — **gender**, **disability**, and **conscious valence** — that were developed independently over Passes 53-65 all follow the *same* MR-Truth-Label playbook. The playbook is general enough that it likely extends to additional domains (race, sexuality, neurodivergence, intelligence, "talent") with appropriate domain-specific empirical priors. This paper extracts the playbook as a meta-principle (**MR-DGS-1**) so future domain extensions can be executed systematically rather than re-invented per domain.

The unification is non-trivial: it demonstrates that TI Sigma's MR Truth Labels are not a one-off technical apparatus for paradox-classification but a general framework for **moving categorical-identity claims out of binary collapse into spectrum-with-structure**.

### 2.2 Notes on the table

- **Substrate** row: each domain has a corresponding "universal-quality" claim that mirrors urb_607's Tralse-quality. The substrate is what makes the binary frame fail: it is impossible for X to be entirely outside the spectrum because the spectrum is the substrate, not a sub-region.
- **DT cell** row (post-refinement): the canonical DT refinement (2026-05-23, inconceivability-under-actualization) means the DT cell is reserved for actualization-collapse cases. Across the three domains, only **dysphoria** has the well-established actualization-collapse signature; disability and valence DT-cells exist in principle but are rare and require specific paradox-structured claims to instantiate.
- **Universal substrate test** row: all three domains pass the structural test — the question "Is P [in category]?" without context-specification is genuinely MR1 Indeterminate, not deficiently-specified-T-or-F. This is the strongest single-line argument for the unified playbook.

## 3. The unified playbook (MR-DGS-1 candidate canonical meta-principle)

### 3.1 Statement

**MR-DGS-1 — MR-Truth-Label Domain Generalization Schema.** For any categorical-identity domain D in which society applies a binary classification, the TI Sigma extension proceeds via the following five-step schema:

1. **Substrate identification.** Articulate the continuous quality or distribution that underlies D. (gender = identity-coherence-distribution across self/social/embodiment axes; disability = capacity-distribution across domains; valence = information-signal-distribution across stimuli)
2. **Universal-substrate question.** Demonstrate that "Is P [in category]?" without context-specification is genuinely MR1 Indeterminate, not a deficient T/F that needs more measurement. This is the structural argument against the binary frame.
3. **Base-4 spectrum mapping.** Assign F, I, T, T-strong-with-mechanism, and (rare) DT cells to recognizable cases within D, per the conceivability/inconceivability axis from the DT canonical refinement. The DT cell is reserved for actualization-collapse cases; do not over-assign it.
4. **Society's binary-collapse failure mode.** Identify the specific binary frame society uses, what it collapses, and what information is lost. (gender = cis/trans exhaustive; disability = disabled/abled binary; valence = cold-vs-emotional binary)
5. **#69 disclosure structure.** State explicitly what the framework does NOT claim, what it does NOT replace (medical/legal/practical apparatuses), and where it would be falsified. The disclosure is not a hedge; it is the framework's #69 brutal-honesty guarantee that distinguishes TI Sigma from rhetorical-only critique.

### 3.2 Composes-with claims

MR-DGS-1 composes with:
- **MR Truth Labels base-4** (provides the cells)
- **DT canonical refinement** (constrains DT-cell assignment)
- **TSD-A** (provides the "count positive instances additively" success-metric for any domain D)
- **MFD-1 Moot-Failure Duality** (provides the dual-reading scaffold so that "category-membership" in the society-binary-frame and "category-presence" in the TI Sigma frame can both be acknowledged)
- **ASYMMETRIC §69** (provides the brutal-honesty discipline that prevents the framework from becoming over-claiming)

### 3.3 Pre-registered falsifiers

**MR-DGS-1-F-1:** if there exists a categorical-identity domain D in which the binary classification is provably-correct (in the sense that the binary genuinely exhausts the substrate, not just society's measurement), MR-DGS-1 is REFUTED on that domain. Candidate test domains: (a) discrete chromosomal sex (XX vs XY) — but this is already known to NOT exhaust gender even though it might exhaust a narrow biological-classification; (b) discrete logical truth of P given fully-specified semantics — but TI Sigma's whole point is that "fully-specified semantics" doesn't characterize lived propositions.

**MR-DGS-1-F-2:** if applying MR-DGS-1 to a new domain D produces strictly worse predictions than the binary-frame on a real-world prediction task, MR-DGS-1 is REFUTED on that domain. This is the operational, not just-conceptual, test.

**MR-DGS-1-F-3:** if the DT cell is empty across all surveyed identity domains (i.e. no domain genuinely has actualization-collapse cases), then the base-4 structure is reducible to base-3 (T/F/I) plus the strong-T-with-mechanism label, and MR-DGS-1 is partially REFUTED (the base-4 part). Per DT canonical refinement, DT is empirically rare; if it turns out to be empty in all identity-domains, the schema should be modified to acknowledge that.

### 3.4 #69 disclosures

- MR-DGS-1 is a **meta-principle**, generalizing across already-canonical domain principles. Its CANONICAL ratification (if Brandon directs) would be elevation, not first-introduction.
- The DT cell is empirically rare. Across the three documented domains, only gender (via DGI-4 dysphoria) has a strong DT-cell signature. Disability and valence DT-cells are theoretically valid (inconceivable-actualization claims can be constructed) but empirically uncommon. MR-DGS-1 acknowledges this; do not over-claim DT in new domains.
- The schema is descriptive of a successful pattern across three domains, not a derivation from first principles. It could be wrong about base-4 being the right cell count, wrong about substrate-identification being the right first step, or wrong about generalizing beyond the three documented domains. Falsifiers above test these specific risks.

## 5. What this paper does NOT claim

- Does NOT replace the individual domain principles. UDP-1, CTC-1, HBP-1, VFP-1, DGI-1..4, CDP-1, NAD-1 retain their independent canonical status and their domain-specific empirical content. MR-DGS-1 is the *pattern* observed across them, not their *replacement*.
- Does NOT claim base-4 is provably the right cell count for all domains. Per MR-DGS-1-F-3, the DT cell empirical rarity might justify a modified schema in some domains.
- Does NOT extend automatically to non-identity-categorical domains. The schema is articulated for *identity* and *capacity* claims. Domains like "is this number prime" or "did event E occur at time t" are different categories of question and have their own MR Truth Label treatments already (per urb_608 / urb_639).
- Does NOT claim the binary-collapse framing is unique to Western or modern society. The framing is descriptive of the failure mode the schema corrects; whether different cultures collapse the same domains differently is an empirical question (and an interesting Pass-66+ extension).
