## Abstract

TI Sigma distinguishes two irreducible questions about any entity: (1) *Is it true?* and (2) *Does it exist?* Prior work established the GILE truth-score and Myrion Resolution (MR) for handling (1). This paper formalizes (2) via the **Holistic Existence Matrix (HEM)** — a four-dimensional scalar derived from the **HEM Dimensions**: Existence Footprint (EF), Moral presence, Conscious meaning/valence, and Aesthetics. HEM is calculated during the *first MR* in **parallel** with the GILE truth-score, producing two distinct but interacting values. The **Privation Theory** is formalized: negative existence states (immorality, suffering, aesthetic poverty) are ontological *holes* — absences of positive existence — not positive entities in a negative direction. Accordingly, negative states receive **double existential weight (2×)** in EV. The key philosophical implication: Meta-Indeterminate and False entities **exist** (can have high HEM) while lacking truth entirely. This explains why the world systematically gravitates toward high-EV, low-truth configurations — and why Goodness (G) is the critical corrective.

## 1. Motivation: Truth and Existence Are Orthogonal

The most persistent confusion in philosophy of mind and social epistemology conflates *truth* with *existence*. Common errors:

- "It's just a belief" → implies the belief lacks causal power. But beliefs exist forcefully regardless of their truth.
- "Immorality is real" → treated as a positive entity in a negative direction. TI Sigma holds it is an ontological hole.
- "False information is harmless" → ignores that false information can have enormous Existence Footprint, L-binding (social reinforcement), and aesthetic appeal.

**Formal distinction:**

> **Truth** is the correspondence of a proposition's content with reality, measured on the five-valued TI Sigma scale {True, Tralse, False, MI, Moot}.
>
> **Existence** is the degree to which an entity instantiates being — causally, physically, consciously, aesthetically, and morally — measured by the Holistic Existence Matrix (HEM).

These are orthogonal axes. An entity can occupy any quadrant:
- High HEM, True: factually correct and causally powerful (e.g., a law of physics)
- High HEM, False: causally powerful but untrue (e.g., a viral conspiracy theory; a cult belief)
- High HEM, MI: causally powerful but truth-incoherent (e.g., a sensory hallucination with full phenomenal force)
- Low HEM, True: correct but causally negligible (e.g., a mathematical truth nobody has discovered)
- Low HEM, False: wrong and negligible

The HEM–Truth plane is the **ontological map** of TI Sigma.

## 2. The HEM Dimensions

HEM is computed from four dimensions, listed by ontological priority:

### 2.1 Dimension 1: Existence Footprint (EF) — *Primary*

**Definition:** The physical causal impact of an entity — its frequency, amplitude, and spatiotemporal extent.

**Measurement:** EF is pluralistic and empirical. It includes:
- **Frequency (f):** How often the entity manifests in a given system (oscillation frequency, occurrence rate, recurrence interval)
- **Amplitude (A):** Intensity or magnitude of causal effect when manifest
- **Spatiotemporal reach (R):** Extent of physical influence in space and time
- **HEM contribution:** Any additional causal dimensions identified by the Holistic Energetic Model (pending integration)

**Formula (base):**
$$\text{EF} = f \cdot A \cdot R_{\text{ST}}$$
where R_ST is the normalized spatiotemporal reach. HEM may add further multiplicative factors.

**Interpretation:** EF captures *how much of the physical world* the entity touches. A pandemic virus has enormous EF. A whispered truth has small EF. EF is value-neutral — it measures presence, not goodness.

### 2.2 Dimension 2: Moral Presence — *Second*

**Definition:** The degree to which the entity participates in the G-axis of GILE — the orientation toward genuine good.

**Key clarification (Privation Theory, §4):** Moral presence has a lower bound at zero (absence of goodness), not at a negative value. Immorality is the *privation* of moral presence — the ontological hole left by the absence of G — not a positive "evil force" in the negative direction.

**Measurement:** Derived from the GILE truth-score's G-component after first MR. The G-axis contribution to HEM is:
$$\text{EV}_{\text{Moral}} = \max(0, G_{\text{GILE}})$$
Negative G scores (moral absence or harm) reduce Moral HEM to zero, not below — reflecting the privation structure.

### 2.3 Dimension 3: Conscious Meaning / Valence — *Third*

**Definition:** The degree of conscious significance the entity carries — the phenomenal weight of experience, meaning, and valence (positive or negative affect) it generates in conscious systems.

**Measurement:** Derived from the I and L components of the GILE truth-score:
$$\text{EV}_{\text{Meaning}} = f(I_{\text{GILE}}, L_{\text{GILE}})$$
Positive valence (meaning, joy, insight): additive to EV.
Negative valence (suffering, meaninglessness): weighted 2× per the Privation Asymmetry (§5).

**Key observation:** An entity can be **high in conscious meaning/valence** while simultaneously **low in truth**. Cult membership can produce profound felt meaning (high EV_Meaning) from entirely MI or False doctrines. This is a structural prediction of TI Sigma about why false beliefs persist.

### 2.4 Dimension 4: Aesthetics — *Fourth*

**Definition:** The aesthetic value or beauty of the entity — its capacity to generate E-axis resonance in conscious systems.

**Key property (Privation Theory):** Aesthetic poverty is a privation. There is no "negative beauty" in a positive direction — only absence of beauty. However, aesthetically *compelling* false or MI entities are especially dangerous: high E + low GIL = maximum existential lure with no truth guidance.

**Measurement:** Derived from the E-component of the GILE truth-score:
$$\text{EV}_{\text{Aesthetic}} = \max(0, E_{\text{GILE}})$$

## 4. Privation Theory — Formal Statement

**Theorem (Privation):** In TI Sigma, negative existence states — immorality, suffering, aesthetic poverty — are *ontological holes* (privations, lacunae) rather than positive entities in a negative direction.

**Justification:**

1. **Phenomenological:** Suffering is experienced as the *absence of wellbeing*, not the presence of an anti-wellbeing entity. Psychophysical research confirms: pain activates nociceptive systems (absence of threat-neutral signal), not a separate positive "anti-pleasant" system.

2. **Moral:** Immorality is the absence of G-orientation (Goodness) — the hole left where moral presence should be. This is consistent with classical privation theory (Augustine, Aquinas) but given a new formal grounding in GILE.

3. **Mathematical:** Negative GILE-truth scores represent *truth pointing in the negative direction* (False has truth content). But negative EV states represent the *removal of positive existence* — they don't contribute negative EV, they reduce positive EV asymmetrically.

4. **Physical analogy:** The 0°C freezing point and 100°C boiling point are not negative temperatures — they are lower bounds in a phase structure. Similarly, zero EV on each dimension is a phase boundary, not a negative value.

**Consequence:** All four FDE dimensions have a floor at zero. No dimension of existence has a natural negative value. Negative states are absences of positive values, not positive negatives.

## 5. Privation Asymmetry — 2× Existential Weight for Negative States

Although negative EV states cannot be less than zero on the dimension itself, they exert **disproportionate existential pressure** on the overall system. This is the **Privation Asymmetry**:

> **A unit of negative existence state (suffering, moral absence, aesthetic poverty) reduces the total HEM of the containing system by 2× relative to a unit of positive increase.**

**Justification:**
- Evolutionary loss aversion: negative outcomes require 2-2.5× the positive payoff to restore motivational equilibrium (Kahneman–Tversky). Loss aversion is an empirically confirmed asymmetry in human (and likely general conscious) value systems.
- Thermodynamic: disorder (ontological hole) in a system typically requires more energy to repair than was present before the disorder.
- GILE prediction: suffering is a strong attractor in the L-axis (binding to pain is stronger than binding to pleasure of equal magnitude). This means negative meaning/valence has greater L-persistence than equivalent positive valence.

**Formal implementation:**
$$\text{EV}_{\text{corrected}} = \text{EV}_{\text{base}} - 2 \times \sum_k |\min(0, D_k)|$$
where D_k are the dimensional scores before the zero-floor is applied. This ensures the 2× asymmetry is applied in computing the total EV, even though individual dimensions don't go below zero.

## 6. The Parallel MR Protocol

**Critical structural claim:** EV and GILE truth-score must be computed **in parallel** during the first Myrion Resolution. They are not sequential.

**Why parallel:** The first MR involves:
1. Identifying which i-cells (propositions, entities, concepts) are relevant to the tralsity
2. Assessing the overall Permissibility Distribution

EV computation (specifically EF) can begin immediately from physical/causal data without waiting for GILE truth-score completion. The GILE score requires logical/semantic analysis. Running them in parallel:
- Allows early flagging: "this entity has high EF — even if MI, it has existential force we must account for"
- Prevents the error of ignoring high-EV entities because they're false or MI
- Produces the correct dual-output: (EV_total, GILE_score) — one measure of being, one of truth

**Protocol:**

```
MR Level 1 (parallel tracks):
  Track A (GILE Truth):
    → Identify i-cells
    → Assess G, I, L, E dimensions
    → Compute GILE score
    → Produce initial PD value

  Track B (Holistic Existence Matrix):
    → Measure EF (frequency, amplitude, spatiotemporal reach)
    → Apply Privation Theory floors
    → Apply Privation Asymmetry (2× correction)
    → Compute EV_total

Output of MR1: (PD_value, EV_total)
```

MR levels 2+ refine the GILE score. EV may also be updated if new causal/physical information emerges, but EF is generally stable after MR1.

## 11. Nature vs. Nurture as EV/PD Example

The nature vs. nurture debate illustrates the failure of percentages for complex nonlinear systems and the superiority of the PD.

**The problem with percentages:** "Intelligence is 50% genetic" commits two errors:
1. Assumes the components are additive and sum to 100%
2. Ignores bidirectional causation: genes influence environment (gene expression shapes niche selection); environment influences gene expression (epigenetics); the interaction term is not separable

In nonlinear systems with **bidirectional causation** and **LCC activation** (emergence from interaction), percentages are ontologically unfit. They model a linear, additive world that doesn't exist.

**The PD approach:** Instead of percentages, the PD:
1. Identifies all relevant i-cells: genetic factors, epigenetic factors, social environment, developmental timing, etc.
2. Scores each i-cell independently on EV and GILE dimensions
3. Models interaction effects as metacausal edges (URB #567)
4. Produces a Permissibility Distribution over outcomes — not a percentage attribution

**The result:** Instead of "50% genetic," you get: "Given the i-cell configuration {genetics: G-score 0.7, EV 0.6; developmental environment: G-score 0.5, EV 0.8; interaction effects: metacausal amplitude 0.3}, the PD for intelligence outcome X is [distribution with mean at Y, spread reflecting interaction uncertainty]."

This captures the emergence and bidirectionality that percentages cannot.
