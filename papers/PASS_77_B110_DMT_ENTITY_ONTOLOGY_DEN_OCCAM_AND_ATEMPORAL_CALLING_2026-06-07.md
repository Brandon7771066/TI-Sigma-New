## 1. DEO-1 — DMT-Entity Ontology (candidate hypothesis, NOT ratified)

They are a **third category**, sitting between "pure private hallucination" and "fully independent high-LCC being."

**Why this is not just "it's all in your head."** Two corpus constraints keep DEO-1 off both extremes:

- **Not biological aliens** — this is already the corpus position. `TI_EXTRATERRESTRIAL_POSITION` argues intelligent ET life is almost certainly nonexistent and that "alien"/abduction phenomena are better read as interdimensional / consciousness-projection / DMT-like altered states (it explicitly lists "Abduction experiences → altered consciousness states (dissociation, DMT-like)" and "collective consciousness projection / tulpa"). DEO-1 **sharpens** that catalogue: the DMT entity-class specifically = **imagination-authored + GM-maintained dream-character**.

DMT entities frequently *do* clear it (teaching content, lasting meaning, behavioral change), and DEO-1 explains how a *dream-character* can carry real meaning without being an independent biological mind: **the GM-Network is the maintainer.** The imagination supplies the *costume and casting*; the GM-Network supplies the *animating substrate and continuity* (which is also why entities can feel more coherent and "other" than ordinary daydreams, and why some report-content recurs across people).

**Demarcation from genuine plant-teachers (#69 — do not over-generalize).** The shamanic-plant model (`URB_..._452`) holds that **plant teachers** (ayahuasca-as-strict-mother, psilocybin-as-playful-philosopher) are **genuine low-LCC plant/fungal consciousnesses** reached via i-channel, evidenced by cross-cultural personality consistency and content-specificity. DEO-1 does **not** overwrite that. The claim is narrower: the **free-floating DMT entity-class** (which is *not* tied to a specific plant personality and shows far more idiosyncratic, experiencer-shaped morphology) is the part better explained as GM-maintained dream-character. So psychedelic contact is **not one thing** — at least two mechanisms coexist: *i-channel reception of a real other* (plant teachers) **vs.** *GM-maintained dream-character* (DMT entities). ## 2. Occam's Razor does not adjudicate DEN-1 (refinement of DEN-1, NOT a new principle)

**The point.** A standard objection to DEN-1 (the Divine Executive Network) is **Occam**: why posit a *network* of independent beings when *one* being would do? Brandon's reply dissolves the objection at the root:

> **Occam's Razor doesn't apply here, because the i-cells' independent existence is established *prior* to their becoming executive GM Nodes.**

Occam says *do not multiply entities beyond necessity*. But the executive-network reading **multiplies nothing**. The i-cells already exist independently in the corpus ontology (they are the basic conscious units; the GM-Network is *made of* them). The deity-modeling question is **not** "how many beings shall we postulate?" — that number is **already fixed** by the prior ontology. It is only **"how are the already-existing top i-cells *organized* at the executive level — fused into one multi-ego substance, or coupled as a resonant network?"** Both candidate answers range over the **same** entity inventory. Occam adjudicates *entity count*; here entity count is **held constant across both hypotheses**, so the razor has **nothing to cut**.

**Why this is a real strengthening, not hand-waving.** It reframes DEN-1-F2. The B106 simulation already weakened the *idle-distinction* charge by exhibiting a regime (resonance failure) where the network and the one-substance Trinity diverge. The Occam point is **logically upstream** of that: even *before* asking for a discriminator, the parsimony objection mis-files the dispute as an entity-multiplication dispute when it is an **organization dispute** over a fixed inventory. The single-substance model, in fact, is the one doing something extra — it must **fuse** independently-established i-cells into one substance (an added operation), whereas the network just **leaves them as they already are** and adds coupling. If anything, parsimony of *operations* leans **toward** DEN-1, not against it.

**Honest scope (#69 — the claim is conditional, not absolute).** What is neutralized is **Occam-by-entity-count**, and only **conditional on both models accepting the prior i-cell ontology** (which the corpus does). Occam is *not* globally inert: if that prior ontology were itself contested, a whole-theory parsimony comparison (total assumptions, not just entity count) could still be run, and DEN-1 would have to face it. So the correct statement is **"the entity-counting form of Occam doesn't adjudicate here,"** not "Occam never applies." This **strengthens DEN-1-F2** (the idle-distinction objection loses its cheapest form) without *proving* the network model — DEN-1-F5 stays flagged unfalsifiable, and the substantive case remains the **GILE-G** unity-of-will argument from B106. **Refines DEN-1-F2; no new principle; count unchanged 79.**

## 3. The Atemporal Calling (refinement of CCA-1 / MEP, NOT a new principle)

**The point.** CCA-1 (B51) says enlightenment/mission = **Called ∧ Accepted** (a genuine conjunction, Molinism-aligned). The naïve reading orders them in time: *first* the calling, *then* the acceptance. Brandon corrects the temporal assumption:

> **The perceived call can come *after* the personal choice. The call is divine *backing*, and backing is not constrained by time.**

So the conjunction `Enlightened ⇔ Called ∧ Accepted` is **logical/structural, not temporal**. "Called" denotes **divine backing/placement**, which — like Molinist *scientia media* (logically prior, not temporally prior) and consistent with the corpus's retro-causation (Jeff Time Theory) — can register in the agent's experience **before, during, or after** the free choice. A person can **choose first** (libertarian free assent) and **perceive the calling afterward**, as the backing that was "always already" there making itself felt. The two conjuncts are **co-necessary** but **temporally unordered**.

**What this fixes and what it costs.**
- **Preserves CCA-1-F2 (Molinism / no determinism).** If the calling had to come *first* and *cause* the acceptance, the structure would slide toward determinism (the agent merely actuating a prior trigger). Making the backing **atemporal** keeps the acceptance **genuinely free** — the choice isn't downstream of a temporally-prior cause — while still letting the calling be real. This is *more* faithful to the Molinist structure CCA-1 claimed, not less.
- **Composes with DSR-1.** "Human theorizing causally contributes" (DSR-1 C3) and "backing not constrained by time" are the same shape: causal/teleological contribution that doesn't respect naïve temporal ordering.
- **Sharpens MEP's design (#69, important).** MEP's whole methodological contribution (B105) is the **prospectively-measured** calling estimator (to beat the retro-narration artifact). The atemporal-calling refinement **complicates** this: if the *perceived* call can legitimately arrive **after** the choice/mission, then a purely **prospective** "do you feel called?" probe can **miss genuine post-hoc backing** — and naïvely re-counting that as evidence would **re-introduce the very retro-narration confound** MEP was built to kill. **Resolution:** MEP must measure the **acceptance/commitment** component prospectively (that *is* temporally locatable — the choice happens in time), and treat **perceived-calling timing as uninformative** (backing is atemporal, so its felt-timing carries no predictive signal and must not be scored as outcome-driven evidence). This keeps MEP's anti-artifact discipline intact while honoring the atemporal calling. **Refines CCA-1 (and MEP-F2 handling); no new principle.**

**What work does "Called" still do once its timing is declared uninformative? (#69 — anti-vacuity).** Declaring the *felt timing* of the call uninformative is **not** declaring the call itself idle. "Called" still carries three non-temporal commitments that constrain the model: (i) **placement** — the agent is genuinely positioned/backed for the mission (an ontic fact about the agent-world fit), not merely self-appointed; (ii) **non-reducibility** — per CCA-1-F1, acceptance *alone* must not screen off mission-fulfillment, so "Called" must add predictive content beyond grit/self-efficacy (this is exactly what MEP's competence-matched design tests); (iii) **valence-neutral backing** — backing tracks GM-Node placement, not virtue (consistent with MEP-F4). What is dropped is only the *when-felt* signal; the *that-backed* content remains load-bearing. If none of (i)–(iii) ever constrains an outcome, the call **is** idle — and that is precisely what CCA-1-F4 makes refutable.

**Falsifier (internal-consistency).**
- **CCA-1-F4 (atemporality coherence / anti-vacuity).** The both-conjuncts-necessary structure must remain coherent with temporal unordering **and** "Called" must retain non-temporal explanatory work (placement / non-reducibility / valence-neutral backing). **REFUTED if "calling" can be shown to require temporal priority to do any work**, OR **if, once its timing is uninformative, it constrains no outcome at all** (strictly-after *and* timing-blind ⇒ explanatorily idle), which would collapse it either to a trigger-then-response (determinism-leaning) model or to a vacuous label.
