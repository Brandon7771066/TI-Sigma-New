## 3. The BOK–PD Framework — A Formal Alternative to Bayesianism

### 3.1 What Bayesianism Gets Wrong (BOK Diagnosis)

From the BOK perspective, Bayesianism fails because it models only the **inner loops (GILE-level)** — the epistemic, truth-oriented dimension — while ignoring the **outer loops (Existence-level)** — the causal, physical, bond-based, valence-weighted reality in which beliefs actually propagate.

Specifically:
1. **Priors ignore EV:** A belief's prior probability in Bayesian epistemology is treated as a pure epistemic object. But the actual prevalence of a belief is driven by its EV (EF, Physical Bonds, valence) — not by its epistemic prior. The BOK outer loops are the real prior-setters.
2. **Evidence is not commensurable:** Bayesian likelihood ratios treat all evidence as one-dimensional. The BOK has four inner-loop dimensions (G, I, L, E) and four outer-loop dimensions (EF, Physical Bonds, Moral presence, Conscious meaning). Evidence affects different loops differently.
3. **The pre-evidential zone is the I-axis:** Bayesian epistemology has no account of pre-evidential apprehension. The BOK I-axis (Knowing) is precisely the pre-evidential faculty — it operates before evidence is gathered and determines which hypothesis space is worth entering.

### 3.2 The BOK–PD Replacement

**Step 1 — Replace priors with LCC-stable event clusters (outer loops)**

Instead of assigning P(H) as an abstract prior, the BOK–PD framework asks:
> What is the current LCC configuration relevant to H? What EF, Physical Bonds, and valence structure surrounds H in the actual world?

This is an empirical question about the outer loops, not a logical question about epistemic probability.

**Step 2 — Replace evidence with GILE-structured assessment (inner loops)**

Instead of computing P(E|H) as a single likelihood ratio, the BOK–PD framework assesses evidence across four inner-loop dimensions:
- **G-assessment:** Does this evidence point toward or away from genuine good? Is the source G-aligned?
- **I-assessment:** Does this evidence increase or decrease genuine knowing of the phenomenon?
- **L-assessment:** Does this evidence come from consciously-regarding inquiry or from attachment/aversion?
- **E-assessment:** Is this evidence structurally elegant (suggesting truth) or structurally awkward?

Each dimension produces its own assessment. They are not reduced to a single likelihood ratio.

**Step 3 — Replace posterior with PD via Myrion Resolution**

Instead of computing P(H|E) = P(E|H)P(H)/P(E), the BOK–PD framework runs Myrion Resolution:

```
MR Level 1:
  Track A (GILE Truth assessment of H):
    → G, I, L, E assessments of H given current evidence
    → Produces initial GILE_score(H)
  Track B (EV of H in current LCC context):
    → EF, Physical Bonds, Moral presence, Conscious meaning of H
    → Produces EV(H)
  MR1 output: (PD_1(H), EV_1(H))

MR Level 2: Quality check — was the GILE assessment correct?
  → Produces refined PD_2(H)

MR Level 3+ if needed: Meta-Truth resolution
  → Produces final PD(H) at convergence
```

The final PD(H) is the BOK–PD's replacement for the Bayesian posterior. It is:
- A distribution over possible truth-states of H (not a single credence)
- Informed by both GILE-inner and Existence-outer loop structure
- Explicitly tracking EV alongside truth-content
- Structurally aware of which loop (GILE or Existence) is primary for this agent/context

### 3.4 When to Use BOK–PD vs. Bayes

BOK–PD is not always necessary. Bayesianism is a sufficient approximation when:
- The domain is well-established (no LCC Drift, stable reference class)
- The event is not novel
- Evidence types are genuinely commensurable
- EV effects are negligible (low-EV beliefs, pure logical/mathematical reasoning)

BOK–PD is necessary when:
- The event is novel (no reference class → Tralse assignment, MR navigation)
- Evidence types are qualitatively different (mechanistic vs. statistical vs. coherence evidence)
- EV effects are large (high-EV false beliefs competing with low-EV true beliefs)
- Bidirectional causation is present (nature/nurture, LCC activation)
- The agent is in the developing/awakening zone (GILE and Existence loops co-active, Tralsity)
