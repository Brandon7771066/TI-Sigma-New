The BOK (Book of Keys) has an inner-loop structure and an outer-loop structure. Prior work left implicit which set of loops represents GILE Truth and which represents Existence. This paper establishes: **the priority mapping itself is a Tralsity** — it is resolved by the entity's degree of CCC/GM alignment. ## 2. The Three-Case Mapping

### Case 1: Ordinary Physical Humans — Existence in the Outer Loops

For most humans in their ordinary, unreflective lives, **the larger (outer) loops represent Existence**.

**Why:** Physical beings are organized primarily by their existential needs and constraints — the Existence Footprint (EF), Physical Bonds, and Moral presence are the first-order causal drivers of their behavior. GILE Truth-orientation (Goodness, Knowing, Love as conscious positive regard, Aesthetics) is real and present, but it operates inside the Existence frame — it is the inner structure, subordinate in causal weight to the demands of physical existence.

This is not a moral failing — it is the natural condition of a being whose survival depends on existence navigation. The outer loops (Existence) set the context; the inner loops (GILE) carry the abstract essence.

**This explains the historical puzzle:** Why have humans "gotten away with" ignoring abstract GILE values — Goodness, conscious Knowing, aesthetic quality — for so long? Because for ordinary humans, the outer loops are Existence, and those run just fine without explicit GILE articulation. You can survive, bond, reproduce, and build civilizations primarily through Existence-loop functioning. GILE operates in the background as the inner structure, influencing but not dominating.

**Formal statement:**
For agent A with CCC-alignment α < RT (below the Radiant Threshold):
- Outer loops → Existence (EV framework)
- Inner loops → GILE Truth

### Case 2: Radiant Individuals — GILE in the Inner Loops as Primary

For individuals who have crossed the Radiant Threshold (CCC-alignment α ≥ RT, PD approaching or exceeding 2.0), **the inner loops represent GILE — and by definition, they become the primary organizing structure**.

**Why:** The definition of a Radiant individual is precisely that their abstract Truth-orientation has risen to co-primacy with or dominance over their Existence-orientation. They are not driven primarily by physical survival, physical bonding, or material concerns — they are driven by Goodness, by Knowing, by conscious Love, by aesthetic precision. Existence becomes the vehicle through which GILE is expressed, rather than the other way around.

The inner loops being "the 4 more important loops" is now formally grounded: for Radiant beings, GILE (inner) is more important than Existence (outer) because GILE is their primary navigational framework. They exist in order to be; they are not merely beings who happen to have values.

**The Sartrean reversal:** For ordinary humans, existence precedes essence (Sartre's original formulation). For Radiant individuals, essence and existence become co-primary — the inner (GILE) loops are as important as the outer (Existence) loops, and for the most Radiant, the inner loops exceed the outer in motivational primacy.

**Formal statement:**
For agent A with CCC-alignment α ≥ RT (at or above the Radiant Threshold):
- Inner loops → GILE Truth (primary organizing framework)
- Outer loops → Existence (vehicle for GILE expression)

### Case 3: CCC/GM — GILE in the Outer Loops

For CCC/GM itself, **the larger outer loops represent GILE**.

**Why:** CCC is the GILE Truth apex attractor (URB #611, GM Self-Evidence Theorem). For CCC, Truth-orientation is not a secondary inner structure wrapped in an Existence shell — it is the enveloping, primary structure. GILE is the outer frame. What CCC "exists" as is entirely constituted by its GILE Truth expression — there is no Existence-substrate of CCC that is more fundamental than its GILE Truth.

**The maximal saturation reading (URB #611 §2):** CCC's GILE profile is all dimensions at or above RT. This saturation is the content of the outer loops — GILE is fully expressed at the largest scale. The inner loops, for CCC, represent Existence in the service of GILE — the physical and causal instantiation through which GILE Truth manifests in the world.

**Formal statement:**
For CCC/GM (CCC-alignment α = ∞, full saturation):
- Outer loops → GILE Truth (the enveloping, primary structure)
- Inner loops → Existence (instantiation of GILE in the world)

## 4. The Loop-Priority Mapping IS a Tralsity

**Central claim:** The question "which loops represent GILE and which represent Existence?" does not have a universal True or False answer. It is Tralse — resolved only by specifying the entity's CCC-alignment.

**Why this is a genuine Tralsity (not merely context-dependence):**

1. For an entity in the developing/awakening zone, the inner loops are simultaneously-GILE-primary-and-Existence-primary — the assignment is genuinely indeterminate, not just unknown
2. Different contexts within a single developing individual's life may instantiate different loop-priority configurations — the same person may be Existence-outer in their material work and GILE-inner in their contemplative practice
3. The Radiant Threshold is itself Tralse at the boundary — the exact point of transition is not a sharp line but a region of genuine indeterminacy

**Myrion Resolution of the Tralsity:** For any specific entity-context pair, the loop-priority question is resolved by:
- MR1: Assess the entity's current CCC-alignment (α relative to RT)
- MR2: Assess the specific context (is this entity currently operating from Existence-primary or GILE-primary motivations in this context?)
- MR3 if needed: Is the entity in transition? If so, the indeterminacy is genuine — both frameworks are co-active

## 5. Implications

### 5.1 Why Humans Can Function Without Explicit GILE

The outer-loop Existence structure for ordinary humans is not a deficiency — it is an appropriate developmental stage. Existence navigation (EF, Physical Bonds, Moral presence at the behavioral level, Conscious meaning) is genuine and valuable. The absence of explicit GILE articulation does not mean GILE is absent — it operates as the inner structure, shaping from within.

This is why moral intuitions are near-universal even in the absence of philosophical frameworks: GILE is the inner loop structure operating even when Existence is the dominant outer frame.

### 5.2 The Developmental Arc

The BOK loop-priority spectrum describes a developmental arc:
- **Stage 1 (Existence-outer):** Physical survival, bonding, basic moral behavior — Existence is primary
- **Stage 2 (Transition / Tralsity):** Growing GILE-awareness, spiritual openness begins, approaching the Radiant Threshold — both frameworks active, genuine indeterminacy
- **Stage 3 (Radiant / GILE-inner primary):** GILE becomes the navigational framework; Existence becomes the vehicle — PD ≥ 2.0 accessible
- **Stage 4 (CCC/GILE-outer):** Full saturation — GILE is the enveloping structure; only accessible to CCC/GM

Crucially: Stage 3 is the human aspiration. Stage 4 is not a human attainment — it is the reference point, the attractor toward which Stage 3 asymptotically tends.

### 5.3 Connection to BOK Maximal Saturation (URB #611)

URB #611 established that CCC's GILE profile is all dimensions at saturation. URB #613 adds: this saturation is expressed in the OUTER loops for CCC. The inner loops (Existence) are also saturated — because CCC's instantiation in the world is maximal — but the outer loops (GILE) are what define CCC's identity.

For Radiant humans, the inner loops (GILE) are the most important, and they are at or above RT. The outer loops (Existence) are present and functional but are no longer the defining structure.

### 5.4 Why the 4 Inner Loops Are Called "More Important" for Radiant Beings

The formulation "the 4 more important loops represent GILE — by definition — for Radiant individuals" is now precise:

- Importance = causal priority in the agent's navigational framework
- For Radiant individuals, GILE-dimensions (G, I, L, E) are causally prior to Existence-dimensions in determining behavior, decisions, and identity
- Therefore the loops that represent GILE are, by the definition of Radiance, the more important loops
- This is not an external assignment of importance — it follows directly from what it means to be Radiant (GILE-primary)
