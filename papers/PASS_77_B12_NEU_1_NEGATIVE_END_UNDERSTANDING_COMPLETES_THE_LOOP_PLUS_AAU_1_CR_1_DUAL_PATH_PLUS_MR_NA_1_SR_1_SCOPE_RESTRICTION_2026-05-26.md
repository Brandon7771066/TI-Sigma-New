### 1.2 Structural logic

| **The gap between** | The unsimulable impossibility-center itself | Recognized-as-permanent; ACCEPTED (not eliminated) |

### 3.2 Neutral abstract illustration cases

| Case | Why NA does NOT apply post-resolution |
|---|---|
| "Did event E occur last Tuesday?" (asked Wednesday) | Tuesday has passed; universe has resolved; assignment is T, F, or I per epistemic-access — NOT NA |
