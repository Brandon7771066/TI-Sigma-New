## 2. Background: Aperiodic Tilings from Penrose to Einstein {#2-background}

### 2.1 What Is Aperiodic Tiling?

A **tiling** covers a plane with shapes (tiles) leaving no gaps and no overlaps. A tiling is:
- **Periodic** if it has translational symmetry — you can slide the entire pattern some distance and it looks identical
- **Non-periodic** if a particular arrangement happens not to repeat
- **Aperiodic** if the tiles *force* non-periodicity — no arrangement of these tiles can ever produce a repeating pattern

This **dual nature** — simultaneously multiplicative and additive — is a deep structural feature of Penrose tilings specifically.
