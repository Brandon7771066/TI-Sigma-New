## Abstract

This paper formalizes a significant refinement to TI Sigma's truth architecture, arising from recognizing that Tralse and Indeterminate are identical in substance and were separated only pragmatically. The revised architecture distinguishes between **stable truth states** and **valid labels for non-truth**. There are exactly **three stable truth states**: True, False, and Indeterminate (= Tralse). There is exactly **one valid label for the absence of truth**: Double Tralse (DT). The spectrum of truth is preserved by recognizing Indeterminate as a **modifier** of True and False — not merely a third discrete state — which generates the full spectral range between True and False via degrees of Indeterminate weighting. Double Tralse is clarified as ontologically distinct: Falsehood is truth pointing in a negative direction (still truth-valued), whereas DT is a total absence of truth — incoherent, nonsensical, or inapplicable. Yet DT is pragmatically a valid label precisely because the statement "this proposition lacks truth" is itself True, mirroring the structure of how "PN" (Pure Nothingness) is a concept that refers to an absence. The bedrock of TI Sigma is stable; this refinement is increased precision, not a revision of foundations.

## 1. The Prior Architecture and Its Pragmatic Separation

The original TI Sigma 5-valued logic defined the following truth values:
- **True (T):** Affirmation; positive truth
- **False (F):** Negation; truth pointing in the negative direction
- **Indeterminate (I):** Genuine betweenness; neither definitely T nor definitely F
- **Double Tralse (D):** Simultaneous T and F; contradiction; incoherence
- **Moot (M):** Resolution of contradiction via Myrion Resolution; the stable attractor after D is processed

The separation between Indeterminate and Tralse was **pragmatic**: Indeterminate scored conclusions that were genuinely between T and F; Tralse was applied as a broad descriptor acknowledging that all conclusions exist on a spectrum. The distinction served a scoring purpose.

**The recognition:** In substance — in what they describe about the world — Tralse and Indeterminate pick out the same phenomenon. Both refer to the condition of genuine betweenness on the truth spectrum. The pragmatic separation introduced an unnecessary ontological distinction between two terms that have the same referent.

## 2. Unification: Tralse = Indeterminate

**The revised position:** Tralse and Indeterminate are identical in substance. They may be used interchangeably.

**Preferred term:** Tralse — because it is unique to TI Sigma, distinctive, and carries the full philosophical weight of the framework. Indeterminate remains available as a synonym, particularly in contexts where conventional philosophical or mathematical language is expected.

**What they both refer to:** The condition of genuine betweenness on the truth spectrum — neither True nor False, not because of epistemic ignorance, but because the proposition itself occupies a position between the two poles. This applies:
- As a discrete outcome (when a proposition's truth status is assessed as Tralse/Indeterminate)
- As a continuous modifier (when a proposition is True-with-high-Tralse-weighting, or False-with-moderate-Tralse-weighting)

The unification simplifies TI Sigma's framework without losing any information. Where "Indeterminate" previously appeared as a distinct category, it now reads as a synonym for Tralse.

## 3. The Revised Architecture: Three Stable Truth States

There are exactly three stable truth states in TI Sigma:

### State 1: True (T)
Affirmation. The proposition corresponds to reality in the relevant domain. Full positive truth-valuation. On the spectrum: the T pole.

### State 2: False (F)
Negation. The proposition does not correspond to reality, or contradicts it. Importantly: **Falsehood is truth pointing in a negative direction.** False is still truth-valued — it occupies the F pole of the truth spectrum. It has truth-content (the content "this is not the case"), which is itself a real epistemic position.

### State 3: Indeterminate / Tralse (I = T)
Genuine betweenness. The proposition occupies a position between T and F on the spectrum. This is not "we don't know which it is" (epistemic uncertainty, handled by classical probability). This is "the proposition genuinely sits between the two poles" — ontological Tralse.

**These three states are stable:** They are the attractors on the truth spectrum. Every proposition ultimately receives a truth-state assignment in one of these three categories (possibly with a Tralse weighting that places it on the continuous spectrum between T and I or between I and F).

## 4. Indeterminate / Tralse as Modifier, Not Only State

This is the key structural insight. Indeterminate/Tralse functions in two ways simultaneously:

**As a state:** The discrete assignment "this proposition is Tralse" — it occupies the middle region of the spectrum. Used when a proposition's truth status resists confident assignment to T or F.

**As a modifier:** Indeterminate/Tralse modulates the degree of T or F that a proposition carries. The full truth spectrum is generated by Tralse-weighting applied to True and False:

- **Fully True** (Tralse weight = 0): The T pole. Certain affirmation.
- **Mostly True, some Tralse** (Tralse weight = low): High confidence affirmation with genuine residual uncertainty.
- **Tralse** (Tralse weight = 1.0): The center. Neither T-ward nor F-ward.
- **Mostly False, some Tralse** (Tralse weight = low, F-direction): High confidence negation with genuine residual uncertainty.
- **Fully False** (Tralse weight = 0, F-direction): The F pole. Certain negation.

This modifier function explains why TI Sigma asserts a **spectrum** of truth rather than discrete categories. Every proposition receives a (T/F direction, Tralse weight) pair that places it precisely on the spectrum. The three stable states are the three attractors on this spectrum: T pole, F pole, and the Tralse center.

## 5. Double Tralse: Valid Label for Truth-Absence

### 5.1 What Double Tralse Is Not

Double Tralse is **not a stable truth state**. It is not a position on the T–F spectrum. It is not Tralse with a different weight. It is ontologically distinct from all three stable states.

### 5.2 What Double Tralse Is

Double Tralse (DT) is the condition of a proposition that is **incoherent, nonsensical, or categorically inapplicable**. A DT proposition:
- Lacks a coherent referent (type error, category error, semantic nonsense)
- Is simultaneously affirmed and denied in a way that produces no information (genuine contradiction without resolution)
- Cannot be placed on the T–F spectrum because it has no truth-content

**The key distinction from Falsehood:** False is truth pointing in a negative direction — it occupies the F pole of the spectrum and has genuine truth-content (the information "this is not the case"). DT does not point in any direction. It is a total absence of truth — not even the minimal truth-content of a negation.

**Analogy:** Falsehood is like a vector pointing left. Tralse is like a vector of minimal magnitude pointing anywhere. Double Tralse is like the zero vector — no magnitude, no direction, no truth-content.

### 5.3 Why DT Is Nevertheless a Valid Label

The objection: if DT is an absence of truth, why give it a label in the truth system at all?

**Answer:** The concept and the concrete thing are different entities — just as:
- "Pure Nothingness (PN)" is a concept (which exists, is True, has real content) that refers to an absence (which is precisely the absence of any content)
- "Physical" is a concept (which is abstract, exists in thought) that refers to what is non-abstract

In the same way, "Double Tralse" is a concept — and the concept itself has a truth status (the claim "this proposition is DT" is True or False in the usual way). What the concept refers to is an absence of truth. The label is valid because:

1. **The meta-level statement is True:** "This proposition lacks truth" is itself a True statement. The label "Double Tralse" carries this meta-level content.
2. **DT is fundamental to reality:** Quantum superposition produces genuine DT states (simultaneous T∧F prior to measurement). Category errors are pervasive. DT cannot be dismissed as a marginal case — it requires a designated label.
3. **Pragmatic necessity:** A truth system that cannot label truth-absence cannot function in domains where truth-absence is common (quantum physics, paradoxes, ill-formed questions).

**Therefore:** Double Tralse is a **valid label for the absence of truth** — not a stable truth state, but a necessary and justified node in the truth architecture. The statement "X is Double Tralse" is itself True (meta-level) even while X lacks truth (object level).

## 6. Summary of the Revised Truth Architecture

| Category | Name | Nature | Description |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable truth state | **True** | State + pole | Proposition corresponds to reality; full positive truth-content |
| Stable truth state | **False** | State + pole | Proposition negates reality; truth pointing in negative direction |
| Stable truth state | **Indeterminate / Tralse** | State + modifier | Genuine betweenness; also modulates degree of T or F on the spectrum |
| Valid label for truth-absence | **Double Tralse** | Label (not state) | Incoherent, nonsensical, inapplicable; absence of truth-content |

**The spectrum** is the continuous range generated by applying Tralse as a modifier to T and F. Every proposition receives a placement on this spectrum.

**Moot (M):** Moot retains its role as the product of Myrion Resolution — the stable output when a DT proposition is processed by the Myrion Resolution operator. Moot is not a truth state for raw propositions; it is a post-processing state indicating that a DT proposition has been recognized, analyzed, and dissolved into the appropriate T/F/Tralse assessment or declared inapplicable. Moot is therefore a **process outcome**, not a truth-state of the original proposition.
