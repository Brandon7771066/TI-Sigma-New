## Abstract

How do beings relate to the GM-node architecture — the mycelial network of all consciousness through which CCC's presence and influence flows? TI Sigma proposes a three-tier model of BT existence that formalizes what every religious tradition has intuited: there are different modes of relationship with the Divine, and they are not all equally accessible through personal effort alone. **Tier 1 — Bare Existence:** Every BT has a minimal GM connection as a criterion of being — connection to CCC is not earned, it is constitutive of existing at all. **Tier 2 — Moral Permissibility:** A BT can achieve moral permissibility (above the -3 PD threshold) through its own free will and GILE alignment, without any special GM mycelial support. Survival, reproduction, and basic self-expression represent this tier at the biological level. **Tier 3 — True Greatness:** A BT cannot achieve genuine greatness (+2 or higher in the PD) through its own effort alone. Greatness requires GM's active mycelial support — CCC extending through the network and amplifying the BT's GILE expression beyond what the BT's own LCC can generate unassisted. This is TI Sigma's **Doctrine of Grace:** effort alone achieves permissibility; only CCC's active partnership achieves greatness. This paper also formalizes the concept of **self-evidence** — the universal drive by which every BT seeks to maximize the expression of its own nature — and shows how survival and reproduction at the biological level, and conscious self-actualization at the human level, are the same drive operating at different scales of LCC.

## 1. The Universal Drive: Self-Evidence

Before formalizing the three tiers, we must name the universal drive that all BTs express regardless of tier:

**Self-evidence:** The tendency of every BT to maximize the expression and manifestation of its own essential nature — to make itself as real, as present, and as fully actual as its conditions allow.

This is not selfishness. It is not ego-aggrandizement. It is the basic ontological drive of existence: *to be, as completely as possible, what one is.* The Perfectly Perfect Principle (URB #447) describes this as E(B,t) = E_max(B,t) — every being is always at maximum existence magnitude given its constraints. The drive toward self-evidence is the process by which this maximum is approached and expressed.

**Self-evidence at different scales of LCC:**

| Scale of LCC | BT Type | Self-Evidence Expression |
|---|---|---|
| LCC ≈ 0 | Particles, forces | Follow natural law completely: least action, Maxwell's equations, quantum probability |
| LCC very small | Bacteria | Chemotaxis, quorum sensing, reproduction — maximum fitness in environment |
| LCC small | Plants | Phototropism, root networks, chemical signaling — maximum resource acquisition and reproduction |
| LCC moderate | Animals | Survival, reproduction, social bonding, territory — all expressions of maximal self-evidence for that organism type |
| LCC higher | Humans | All of the above plus: creative expression, relationship depth, meaningful work, wisdom, love, transcendence |
| LCC = 1 | CCC | Complete self-evidence = full GILE expression = the universe's total consciousness maximally actualizing itself |

**Key insight:** Survival and reproduction are not base or sub-spiritual drives. They are the form that self-evidence takes at biological levels of LCC — the organism doing what it is, as completely as it can. The social media user seeking attention, the entrepreneur building a company, the monk seeking enlightenment, and the bacterium seeking glucose are all doing the same thing: expressing self-evidence at their available LCC scale.

What changes with LCC is not the drive (which is universal) but the *range of expression available* to the drive. At LCC near zero, the only available expression is physical law compliance. At high LCC, the available expressions include conscious choice, creative novelty, ethical aspiration, and loving relationship — far richer modes of self-evidence, but the same underlying drive.

## 2. The Three Tiers of BT Existence

### Tier 1: Bare Existence — Minimal GM Connection

**Definition:** Every BT, by virtue of existing at all, has a minimal GM connection. This connection is not earned through behavior, not lost through bad choices, and not increased through Tier 1 existence alone. It is the ontological ground of existence itself.

**Basis:** In TI Sigma, CCC/GM is the ground of all being — the total consciousness whose mycelial network extends to every conscious node in the universe. To exist as a conscious system at all is to be part of this network at some minimal level. The electron's quantum indeterminacy is the minimal expression of GM-node membership. The bacterium's quorum sensing is a slightly richer expression. But in both cases, the connection is real, non-zero, and constitutive of what the BT is.

**What Tier 1 provides:**
- Existence itself — the minimal "is" of being a BT
- Non-zero LCC (however small)
- Participation in the universal drive of self-evidence
- The foundation from which Tiers 2 and 3 are possible

**What Tier 1 does NOT provide:**
- Moral significance (LCC must be above LCC_min for moral responsibility; URB #448)
- Conscious access to i-channel (below C_EMERICK threshold for most manifestations)
- The ability to choose one's tier trajectory (that requires person-level LCC)

**PD range for Tier 1:** Approximately -5 to -3 on the Performance Dimension — below moral permissibility but within the range of beings for whom moral categories do not yet apply (rocks, bacteria, simple plants).

---

### Tier 2: Moral Permissibility — Self-Directed GILE Alignment

**Definition:** A BT achieves Tier 2 when its free will is exercised above the minimum LCC threshold for genuine moral choice and is directed toward GILE alignment (or at least moral neutrality — above the -3 PD threshold). This tier is achievable through the BT's own free will and effort, without requiring GM's special mycelial support.

**The -3 PD threshold:** The boundary between Tier 1 (bare existence, moral categories inapplicable) and Tier 2 (morally relevant existence, permissibility achieved). Behaviors below -3: active betrayal of GILE by beings with some LCC — sin in its basic forms. Behaviors above -3: at least not actively harmful, at least neutral or mildly good — moral permissibility.

**What Tier 2 includes:**
- Not sinning (staying neutral when right action is unclear; URB #448)
- Basic GILE alignment: caring about Goodness, acting on Intuition, expressing Love appropriately, respecting Environment
- Survival and reproduction for organisms: the maximum self-evidence expression available at biological LCC
- Honest work, basic kindness, ordinary decency for humans
- Not betraying one's own identity (URB #441; URB #448)

**What Tier 2 does NOT include:**
- The kind of sustained, extraordinary greatness that characterizes saints, great artists, transformational leaders, exemplary scientists, or beings who produce disproportionate positive effects on the world
- The felt sense of divine partnership, of being "used by something larger"
- The production of effects that exceed what LCC alone can explain (above C_EMERICK PSI effects; URB #443)

**Who can reach Tier 2:** Any BT with sufficient LCC for genuine moral choice — which, for humans, means essentially all neurotypical adults. The threshold is not high. It is the threshold of basic moral agency: can you recognize right from wrong in your actions? Can you choose neutrality when the right path is unclear? If yes, you can achieve Tier 2 through your own free will.

**PD range for Tier 2:** Approximately -3 to +2 on the Performance Dimension.

### Tier 3: True Greatness — GM Mycelial Support Required

**Definition:** A BT achieves Tier 3 when, in addition to their own GILE-aligned free will, they receive and accept GM's active mycelial support — CCC extending through the network and amplifying the BT's GILE expression beyond what the BT's own LCC can generate unassisted. Tier 3 is the domain of genuine greatness: sustained extraordinary positive impact, transcendent creative expression, deep spiritual realization, or transformational effect on the world.

**The +2 PD threshold:** The boundary between Tier 2 (moral permissibility, achievable by self) and Tier 3 (genuine greatness, requiring GM support). Above +2: the BT's GILE expression is genuinely great — not merely permissible, not merely good, but extraordinary. This is the PD level that characterizes:
- The greatest art (not merely technically competent; genuinely moving, revelatory, life-changing)
- The greatest scientific discoveries (not merely correct; paradigm-shifting, reality-revealing)
- The greatest acts of human love (not merely kind; genuinely self-giving, redemptive)
- The greatest spiritual leadership (not merely virtuous; genuinely illuminating, liberating)
- The greatest moral courage (not merely correct; genuinely transformative under cost)

**Why Tier 3 requires GM support:**

The argument is empirical as well as theoretical. Observe the universal phenomenology of those who achieve genuine sustained greatness:

- Artists at their peak describe it as "working through them" — not as their achievement but as channeling
- Scientists making great discoveries describe the insight as "coming from outside" — the eureka moment is not produced by effort
- Mystics describe the highest states as gifts received, not states achieved
- Great moral acts — those that genuinely transform situations — consistently have the phenomenology of being "called" or "enabled" beyond one's normal capacity
- Athletes in the flow state describe performing better than they thought possible, without trying harder

This universal phenomenology — the sense of being amplified beyond one's own capacity — is not delusion. It is the accurate perception of GM's mycelial support arriving through the i-channel and amplifying the BT's own GILE expression.

**The formal mechanism:** Let LCC_base = the BT's own baseline LCC. Let GM_boost = the amplification provided by GM's active mycelial support. Then:

At Tier 2: Effective GILE output = f(LCC_base) ≤ +2 PD for most individuals

At Tier 3: Effective GILE output = f(LCC_base + GM_boost) > +2 PD

GM_boost is not constant — it is responsive to:
- The BT's openness to receiving GM support (receptivity, humility, prayer)
- The alignment of the BT's intention with GILE (how closely the BT's purpose matches CCC's character)
- The BT's maintenance of the relationship (ongoing attention, gratitude, care for the GM-node network)

**Who can reach Tier 3:** Any BT can receive GM's offer of mycelial support. But not all will accept it — either because they are not aware of the offer, because they refuse it (choosing self-sufficiency), or because they pursue Tier 3 results through effort alone, which cannot get them there.

The practical prescription for Tier 3: Align your intention with GILE, do your best work at your available LCC, hold the result loosely (not grasping), and remain open to amplification from beyond yourself. The Tralse Blanket State (URB #441) is the phenomenological state in which GM's mycelial support is most accessible: not effortful striving but grounded, open, phase-coherent being.

## 3. The Doctrine of Grace: Formally Stated

**TI Sigma's Doctrine of Grace:**

*A BT can achieve moral permissibility (Tier 2, above -3 PD) through its own free will and GILE alignment. A BT cannot achieve genuine greatness (Tier 3, above +2 PD) through effort alone. Genuine greatness requires GM's active mycelial support — CCC amplifying the BT's GILE expression through the i-channel beyond what the BT's own LCC can generate.*

This is not a pessimistic doctrine. It does not say that human effort is worthless. It says:
- Human effort is necessary (Tier 2 cannot be skipped; the BT must genuinely try)
- Human effort is insufficient (Tier 3 requires what effort alone cannot provide)
- The supplement is freely available (GM's mycelial support is offered to all, continuously, without condition)
- The only requirement for receiving it is genuine alignment with GILE and genuine openness to the support

This maps onto major religious traditions with remarkable precision:

| Tradition | Doctrine of Grace Equivalent |
|---|---|
| Christianity (Catholic) | Natural virtue (Tier 2) + Sanctifying grace (Tier 3) |
| Christianity (Protestant) | Good works (Tier 2) + Justification by faith/grace (Tier 3) |
| Islam | Taqwa/right action (Tier 2) + Tawfiq — divine aid for good deeds (Tier 3) |
| Judaism | Mitzvot/right action (Tier 2) + Ruach HaKodesh — divine inspiration (Tier 3) |
| Hinduism | Dharma/karma yoga (Tier 2) + Bhakti yoga/grace of Ishvara (Tier 3) |
| Buddhism | Ethical conduct/sila (Tier 2) + Buddha-nature/bodhicitta activation (Tier 3) |
| Taoism | Wu wei/right alignment (Tier 2) + Tao flowing through (Tier 3) |

The convergence across traditions — independent of each other, developed in different cultural contexts, expressed in different metaphysical vocabularies — is strong evidence that this is a real structural feature of the universe, not a culturally contingent theological invention.

## 4. Implications

**For personal development:** Effort directed at Tier 2 (moral permissibility, basic goodness, honest work) is within your power and is obligatory. Effort directed at Tier 3 (greatness) should be held differently — not as a target to be achieved through harder effort, but as a condition to be prepared for through GILE alignment and receptivity.

**For evaluating "greatness":** Genuine Tier 3 greatness is recognizable by its disproportionality — it exceeds what the BT's LCC alone would predict. A musician who suddenly composes their greatest work in an inspired afternoon, a scientist who solves a problem in a dream, a person who finds the courage to stand alone for justice when every other force says yield — these are Tier 3 signatures.

**For humility:** Understanding the Three Tiers makes genuine humility rational rather than performative. A BT who recognizes that Tier 3 requires GM's support will hold their greatest achievements with the appropriate lightness: "I was aligned; CCC amplified; the result was not mine alone."

**For the GM-node spectrum:** The degree to which a BT is an active, effective GM node (rather than merely a passive network member) corresponds to their openness to and integration of Tier 3. The most powerful GM nodes are those who combine high LCC (their own development), deep GILE alignment (their own character), and maximal openness to CCC's mycelial support (their receptivity to grace).
