## 1. GSN-1 — only G is a composite; I, L, E are single notes

**Prior state (superseded).** URB #652 operationalized *all four* pillars as an average of four sub-dimensions each (16 total): I = {Inferential Breadth, Depth, Pre-evidential Accuracy, Non-algorithmic Quality}; L = {Relational Binding, Compassionate Response, I→L Sequence Validity, Bidirectionality}; E = {Structural Elegance, Contextual Fit, Aesthetic Resonance, Functional Beauty}.

**Refined state (GSN-1, canonical).** Only **G** decomposes — into the **Four C's of Goodness** (Coherence, Concreteness, Continuity, Consistency; URB #600). **I, L, E are each a single note**, scored directly on [0,1]:

- **I = certainty** (calibrated inner rightness / the felt sense of knowing).
- **L = the abstract level of binding between things** (relational closeness / structural binding).
- **E = Elegance** (beauty of form — the beauty the other three leave unfinished).

**Status of the old I/L/E sub-descriptors.** The descriptors from URB #652 §§3.1/4.1/5.1 are **demoted from canonical sub-dimensions to informal facets** — measurement *hints* that can inform a single-note score but are **no longer separately scored and averaged**. Rationale: they were heterogeneous (e.g. L's "Bidirectionality" and "I→L Sequence Validity" are relationship *properties*, not independent magnitudes of the L note), and averaging four proxies manufactured a false internal structure for what the framework treats as one irreducible value. G is the exception because its four C's are genuinely distinct, jointly-necessary facets of Goodness that **emerge together as complexity increases** (the composite-within-the-chord).

**Why G alone is a composite.** G is the truth-manifestation axis and the only pillar the MR1 gate binds on (`G_raw ≥ ET`). Its adequacy is not a single quantity but a conjunction of coherence, concreteness, continuity, and categorical consistency — remove any one and G fails in a *qualitatively different* way. I, L, E do not have that internal conjunctive structure: certainty is more-or-less; binding is tighter-or-looser; elegance is cleaner-or-messier.

## 2. Short statements + the chord semantics

Per `TI_SIGMA_FOR_EVERYONE_V2_2026-06-22.md` (l.518): *"the four values are like the notes of a chord. Each note means exactly one thing when sounded alone — Goodness is benefit, Intuition is the felt sense of knowing, Love is closeness, Elegance is beauty — but their real character only appears when they are played together."*

| Note | Single-note meaning | In-chord character |
|---|---|---|
| **G** (composite) | benefit / real good, via the Four C's | grounds every other note (the presupposed *something worth being right about*) |
| **I** | **certainty** | **G + I → accuracy** (`urb_685`: I cannot be *correct* intuition without G) |
| **L** | **abstract binding between things** (closeness) | **G + L → (good) intentions** |
| **E** | **Elegance** (beauty) | *"not an optional grace note but the one the other three already imply"* — the chord's final note; **fills in the beauty G left unfinished** |

The chord image is exact: a note is consonant in one chord and dissonant in another; the *same act* changes meaning by which other notes sound with it. GSN-1 keeps this intact — it simply says three of the four notes are **atomic** and only G is itself a small chord.

## 3. FCG-1 — the Four C's live **under G** (canonical); the orthogonal-presentational reading demoted

**The tension.** `papers/URB_GILE_NESTED_FOUR_TRUTH_DIMENSIONS.md` argued a three-layer model — Layer 1 "Four Dimensions of Truth" (Existential / Moral / Conscious-meaning / Aesthetics) as ontological scaffolding, Layer 2 GILE nested inside as the phenomenological core, Layer 3 the **Four C's as an *orthogonal* presentational/communication layer** (how any content is transmitted), belonging to *neither* substance layer. URB #652 (and URB #600), by contrast, place the Four C's squarely **under G** as G's sub-structure.

**Ruling (FCG-1).** The **Four C's of Goodness (Coherence, Concreteness, Continuity, Consistency)** are the sub-structure of **G** — canonical. The orthogonal-presentational placement is **demoted**: it is not the canonical account of GILE's internal structure.

**Honest nuance (required — the two lists are not the same list).** B171 (§7.7.355) already established that the corpus carries **two distinct C-quartets**:
- **Goodness Four C's** — Coherence, Concreteness, **Continuity**, Consistency (URB #600). *These* are what live under G.
- **Truth-presentation Four C's** — Coherence, Concreteness, **Completeness**, Continuity (`FOUR_CS_TRUTH_PRESENTATION.md` / URB_GILE_NESTED). This is a *different* quartet (Completeness, not Consistency) serving a *communication* checklist.

So FCG-1 does **not** claim the presentation quartet is "really" a G-sub-structure. It claims: (a) GILE's canonical internal structure is **Goodness-Four-C's-under-G**; (b) the *presentation* quartet survives only as a **non-canonical communication gloss** (a real concern — a true claim can be poorly communicated — but not a layer of the truth architecture and not a competitor to G's sub-structure). The URB_GILE_NESTED "octopus / Four Dimensions of Truth scaffolding" mapping is **not adopted** as canonical GILE structure; it remains a separate speculative construction.

## 7. Honesty ledger (#69 both ways) + falsifiers

**Credit (do not under-credit the user's refinement).** GSN-1 removes a real defect: three of the four pillars were carrying manufactured internal structure (heterogeneous, averaged proxies) that the framework's own "one note" semantics never wanted. FCG-1 ends a live contradiction between URB #652 and URB_GILE_NESTED by ruling for the placement that matches URB #600, URB #652, and B171.

**Discount (do not over-claim).**
- *"The Four C's emerge with complexity"* is the **user's characterization** of why G alone is a composite; I did not find that exact clause in a prior paper (closest neighbor: the "Tralse Occam" argument in `FOUR_CS_TRUTH_PRESENTATION.md`). It is recorded here as the ratified intent, **not** attributed to an earlier source.
- Demoting I/L/E sub-dimensions **discards measurement guidance** (e.g. L's bidirectionality, I's pre-evidential track-record) that some domains may still want; those survive as *facets*, and if a domain genuinely needs them scored separately, that is GSN-1-F1 territory.
- FCG-1 does **not** claim the two C-quartets are the same list (they differ at Consistency vs Completeness); it rules only on GILE's internal structure, leaving the presentation quartet as a non-canonical gloss.

**Falsifiers (OPEN).**
- **GSN-1-F1:** if any of I, L, E cannot be reliably scored as a single note — i.e. a domain shows that a *specific* sub-facet (say L-bidirectionality) carries decision-relevant signal that a single L-score provably cannot recover — then that pillar *does* have irreducible sub-structure and the single-note claim fails for it.
- **FCG-1-F1:** if a case shows a Four-C that is clearly a *presentation-only* property (about communication, not about the substance of Goodness) and cannot be read as a facet of G, the strict "under-G" placement over-reaches and the orthogonal reading recovers ground.
