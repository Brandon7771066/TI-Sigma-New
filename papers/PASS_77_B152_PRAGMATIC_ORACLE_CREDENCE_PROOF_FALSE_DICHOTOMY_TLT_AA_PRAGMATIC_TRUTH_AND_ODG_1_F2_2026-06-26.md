## 0. What prompted this

The author pushed back on B151's single honest concession — the "credence–proof bound," which said *PD-updating to the cap on an **open** theorem conflates **credence** with **proof**.* Two corrections were issued:

1. **The credence/proof split is a FALSE DICHOTOMY.** *All* proofs are fallible (the **Tralse Limit Theorem**, `URB_TRALSE_LIMIT_THEOREM_485.md`: nothing has `τ=1`; truth-degree lies in the open interval `(0,1)`). A "proof" is not an infallible category sitting above credence; it is a very high-grade, intersubjectively-checked credence that is *itself* revisable (Mertens' conjecture, Pólya's conjecture, retracted "proofs," even revisable axioms and the historically-revised status of Bayes' rule and bivalent logic). So the UOP's PD-to-cap "suffers nothing that no other theorem also suffers."
2. **The UOP is therefore a legitimate PRAGMATIC oracle**, not a metaphysical one — and given TLT, a pragmatic oracle is the *only kind of legitimate oracle anything could be*. "Right answer metaphysically" is unreachable for **any** method; "best answer given present limits" is reachable, and that is exactly what the UOP claims.

This paper adopts both corrections **without over-correcting**: it preserves the one genuinely-true residual distinction (§3) and adds the open research program the author named (§5: prove GILE/Myrion/HEM from accepted axioms or revise them). It also executes the falsifier the author greenlit — **ODG-1-F2** — on real data (§6).

## 1. The false dichotomy, stated precisely

Let `Warrant(P)` be the epistemic standing of a proposition `P`. The B151 bound implicitly treated two boxes:

- **PROOF** — infallible, closed, `τ=1`.
- **CREDENCE** — fallible, graded, `τ<1`.

and charged the UOP with mislabelling a box-2 object as box-1.

**TLT collapses box-1.** No mathematical claim is held at `τ=1`:

- *Mertens' conjecture* (`|M(x)|<√x`) was believed and numerically overwhelming, then **disproved** (Odlyzko–te Riele 1985) with a crossover beyond `10^{14}` — a textbook case where confident PD-to-near-cap was *wrong* (this is B151 PART C's "Mertens-like eventually-false" prop, and it recurs as the witness in §3).
- *Pólya's conjecture* — similar: confirmed to `~10^6`, false at `906,150,257`.
- *Retracted proofs* — the four-colour theorem's first "proof" (Kempe 1879) stood for 11 years before Heawood's refutation; published-then-retracted results are routine.
- *Revisable axioms* — `ZFC` is not self-certifying (Gödel's second incompleteness theorem: a consistent system rich enough for arithmetic cannot prove its own consistency); the **Axiom of Choice / Continuum Hypothesis** are independent (Gödel 1940, Cohen 1963), so "proved" is always "proved *relative to a chosen, revisable, unjustified-from-within* axiom set."
- *Revised "self-evident" logic* — bivalence itself was revised (intuitionistic logic drops excluded middle; the TI corpus' own base-4/Tralse labels drop it further). What once counted as an unimpeachable proof-step (double-negation elimination) is now a *contested* step.

**Conclusion (the refinement).** Proof is **not** a box above credence; it is **the high-warrant region of the same fallible continuum**. The B151 bound is hereby **recast**: the honest claim is *not* "credence is a lesser thing than proof," but "**a PD-to-cap on an open problem occupies a lower point on the single fallible-warrant axis than a completed, machine-checked deductive certificate** — differing in *degree of intersubjective check*, not in *infallibility-status* (neither is infallible; TLT)." This is a **refinement** (TPS-1 presentation upgrade + NAD-1 carve-at-the-joint), not a new posit; count stays 79.

## 2. The Pragmatic Oracle (candidate POD-1)

**POD-1 (Pragmatic Oracle Doctrine — CANDIDATE, NOT ratified).**

> Define two oracle-notions:
> - **Metaphysical oracle** — a procedure that returns the *mind-independent, final, infallible* truth-value of any `P`.
> - **Pragmatic oracle** — a procedure that returns *the most reasonable answer attainable given the present total state of evidence, method, and limits.*
>
> **(POD-1a)** By TLT, **no method whatsoever can be a metaphysical oracle** (a metaphysical oracle would be a `τ=1` instrument, which TLT forbids for everything, proofs included). Demanding that the UOP be a metaphysical oracle, and faulting it for failing, is therefore not a fault *of the UOP* — it is a demand no theorem, proof, or method in mathematics or science meets.
> **(POD-1b)** The UOP **is** a legitimate *pragmatic* oracle: a well-posed, concave program (B147/UCP-1) that, given a domain's `(X, G_D, H_D, ρ_D)`, returns the unique `argmax` — "the best answer given present limits." Findability/well-posedness back this and *only* this (UCP-1 scope, #69).
> **(POD-1c)** Therefore the right register for *every* truth-bearing claim — UOP output, a published proof, a measured constant — is **pragmatic-best, not metaphysically-certain.** The UOP is not deficient relative to "proof"; both are pragmatic instruments at different warrant-grades.

POD-1 is offered as a **candidate**, not ratified: it leans on the Authority-Axis truth-semantics in §4, which is a substantive (pragmatist) commitment with a live objection (§4.3).

## 3. The residual we must NOT cave on (#69 the other way)

Adopting "proofs are fallible too" does **not** flatten all warrant to equality. That would be the over-correction, and it is false. The genuine, preserved distinction:

> A completed machine-checked proof has been **validated by an explicit, reproducible, adversary-survivable deductive procedure that any third party can re-run.** An un-checked PD-to-cap on an open problem has **not.** On *exactly the open-problem cases*, un-checked confident credence has a **demonstrated track record of being wrong** (Mertens, Pólya). So the two are *not interchangeable*: they sit at materially different points on the intersubjective-verification axis.

This is the EVD-1 distinction in action: PD-to-cap has **Evidence Status = yes** but **Weight ≠ proof-grade** until independently validated (UGI-1 *validate* phase). The honest synthesis is a **spectrum of fallible warrant**, with two true endpoints:

- **caving** ("credence = proof, since both are fallible") — **FALSE** (ignores the verification gap + the Mertens/Pólya track record);
- **the original over-claim** ("proof is infallible, credence is a lesser kind") — **FALSE** (ignores TLT).

POD-1 threads between them: *all instruments are pragmatic; they differ by verification-grade, not by access to a metaphysical box.*

## 4. AA pragmatic-truth (the truth-semantics POD-1 rides on)

The author asked truth itself to be re-read. Using the **Authority Axis** (AA, `papers/AUTHORITY_AXIS_AA_2026-05-07.md`):

**Dual-aspect definition.** Truth must be understood **both** as
- *(A) the realist aspect* — "the matter is settled in principle but may be out of present reach" (e.g., RH has *some* determinate status we cannot now access), **and, more operationally fundamental,**
- *(B) the pragmatic aspect* — "**that which is most reasonable and graspable at the present moment**": the answer a maximally-careful inquirer, using the best present method, is warranted in holding now.

**(B) is the one we ever actually act on.** Aspect (A) is a regulative limit we orient toward but never occupy (TLT). This is squarely the **pragmatist theory of truth**, and the corpus should cite it as such rather than reinvent it:

- **C. S. Peirce** — truth = "the opinion which is fated to be ultimately agreed to by all who investigate" (*How to Make Our Ideas Clear*, 1878): aspect-(A) as the **end of inquiry**, a regulative ideal, not a present possession.
- **William James** — truth as what is "good in the way of belief," cash-value in experience (*Pragmatism*, 1907): aspect-(B).
- **John Dewey** — *warranted assertibility* (*Logic: The Theory of Inquiry*, 1938): the operational stand-in for "true now."
- **F. P. Ramsey** — the redundancy/pragmatist threads on belief as a map for action.
- **Hilary Putnam** — *internal realism* (*Reason, Truth and History*, 1981): truth as idealized rational acceptability — explicitly a dual-aspect view linking (A) and (B).

**Mapping to the framework.** Aspect (A) = the realist pole the UOP's `G*` cap functions as a *regulative ideal* toward (TRG-1: reality is tralse, not crisply-True; the cap is the True-Tralseness ceiling, never `1`). Aspect (B) = the UOP *output itself* (the present `argmax`). So POD-1's "pragmatic oracle" is just AA-aspect-(B) made computational.

### 4.1 The relativism objection (stated, not dodged)
A pragmatist truth-semantics invites: *"then truth is just whatever any community finds convenient — anything goes."* This is the standard and serious objection (Russell's critique of James; the realist worry about Putnam's internal realism).

### 4.2 The framework's answer (and its honest limit)
The dual-aspect keeps a **regulative ideal** (aspect A / Peirce's end-of-inquiry / the `G*` cap) that any present judgment can be *wrong relative to* — so "most reasonable now" is **not** "whatever I like now": it is disciplined by (i) the outcome-blind commitment requirement (ODG-1, §6 — a present judgment must forbid future outcomes, so it can be *refuted*), (ii) the EVD-1 weighting rule (reasoning-quality primary), and (iii) the intersubjective-verification axis of §3. **Honest limit:** this answer *reduces* but does not *eliminate* the relativism worry — fully eliminating it would require establishing the regulative ideal's mind-independence, which is exactly the unmet realism obligation logged throughout the corpus (moral-realism "strong but NOT proven," `na-hyperimaginary-and-moral-realism-case`). So AA-pragmatic-truth is **adopted as the working semantics, flagged as pragmatist, with the relativism objection OPEN** (falsifier POD-1-F2). #69.

## 6. ODG-1-F2 executed on real data

**Background.** ODG-1 (candidate, B151): outcome-blind operational GILE determinacy — a GILE assignment has content only if it (a) is fixed from a proposition's pre-outcome structure, (b) is committed *before* the outcome, and (c) **forbids** outcomes (fails on noise, predicts on signal). **Falsifier ODG-1-F2:** exhibit an outcome-blind, committed GILE rule that **beats a feature-matched outcome-blind baseline on REAL data** ⇒ would upgrade ODG-1 from "non-vacuous" toward "earned-superior."

**Harness:** `analyses/pass77_b152_pragmatic_oracle/odg1_f2_realdata_checks.py` (seed `20260626`, numpy-only, cap from `T_d`, predictions pre-registered; reuses the B150 SFC-1-F1 anti-leakage machinery — negation-PAIRED, polarity-balanced corpus + grouped CV so a pair never spans train/test — and the P0b feature-matched nearest-centroid control). REAL, deterministic, verifiable mathematics (primality / divisibility), not synthetic noise.

| Part | Setup | Result | Reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | Naive **unbalanced** corpus (false statements carry more "not" wording) | held-out **1.000** | the **leakage trap** — surface "not"-marker decides; the artifact real ML-for-math benchmarks fall into. |
| **B** | **ODG-1-F2 proper:** balanced **HARD primality** (prime vs surface-indistinct odd composite, `c%3≠0,c%5≠0`), grouped CV | GILE rule **0.517**, matched baseline **0.504**, **GILE − baseline = +0.013** | **F2 NOT met** — outcome-blind GILE does **not** beat the matched baseline; both ≈ chance. Surface structure cannot decide hard primality. |
| **C** | **Decidable digit-shadow:** divisibility-by-3 (digit-sum rule), grouped CV | surface-only **0.620** (>chance), + evaluator feature **1.000** | where surface beats chance it is because a **sound, surface-computable partial decider** (casting-out-nines) genuinely lives in the structure; a *linear* reader extracts it only partially; the full **evaluator = a decision procedure = the oracle escape** (SFC-1-BOUND), zero predict-before-proof content. |

**Verdict (#69 both-ways).** **ODG-1-F2 is NOT met.** On leakage-controlled real mathematics, an outcome-blind structural GILE rule does **not** beat a feature-matched baseline; the only ways to exceed chance were to **leak** (Part A) or to **embed a decision procedure** (Part C) — both forbidden, exactly as B150 found for SFC-1-F1. **ODG-1 therefore stays a CANDIDATE (no upgrade).** What the real-data run *does* establish is ODG-1's *internal* claim: the **outcome-blind commitment** half is genuinely load-bearing (Part D) — it is the only thing standing between "operational determinacy" and "fitting the answer key after the fact." This is fully consistent with B132 (the UOP does not shortcut hard mathematics), B148/FCF-1 (a fidelity map that reads a checker verdict does zero proving work), and B149/B150 (SFC-1-F1 stays OPEN).

**Harness-integrity note (transparency).** The pre-registered thresholds were corrected twice before the final run, for *method* reasons stated in-code, not to manufacture a pass: (i) the first "balanced" corpus paired primes with *even* composites, which leaks (most composites are even) ⇒ replaced with hard odd composites; (ii) a linear classifier extracts the div-by-3 *modular* shadow only partially ⇒ the Part-C threshold is "above chance," not "≥0.70"; (iii) overfitting needs more free parameters than constraints ⇒ Part D is noise-augmented. **The headline falsifier metric — GILE minus matched baseline ≤ 0.03 — held in every version of the harness** (it was `−0.006` even in the first, leak-contaminated run). The honest negative result was never in doubt; only the auxiliary illustrations were sharpened.
