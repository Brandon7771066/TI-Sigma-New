### 3.1 UDP-1 Universal Disability Principle
"Disabled" is not a natural-kind binary but a society-anchored prevalence-cutoff on continuous capacity-distribution. MR Truth Labels mapping: "Is P disabled?" without domain = MR1 *Indeterminate*; with domain+threshold = T/F. Falsifier F-1: any disability definition not reducing to capacity-thresholding must classify ≥95% borderline cases consistently across 3 cross-cultural rater pools (N=200).

### 3.2 CTC-1 Compensatory-Trade Capacity Principle
- Part (a): restriction in one capacity → above-baseline development in correlated capacities (strong literature prior: Sadato 1996 cross-modal cortical plasticity; Amedi 2007; Goldreich & Kanics 2003 tactile-acuity d≈0.5-1.0 in early-blind)
- Part (b): dominant capacity actively suppresses correlated capacity development in non-restricted (riskier; McGurk prior)
- Falsifiers F-1 (meta-analysis |d|<0.15 → part-a REFUTED), F-2 (blindfold-training sighted no acuity gain → part-b REFUTED)

### 3.3 HBP-1 Health-as-Balance-Profile Principle
Most radical. Success = C(agent, env) = Σ_d w_d(env) × capability(agent, d), NOT Σ_d 𝟙[capability > θ_society(d)]. Society's diagnostic frame is the degenerate special case. Empirical anchor: Albrecht & Devlieger 1999 "disability paradox" — ~54% serious-disability participants rate QOL excellent-or-good despite observer-predicted catastrophic loss. Falsifiers F-1 (life-sat monotone-decreasing w/ adaptation control → REFUTED), F-2 (constructable environment where redistribution strictly hurts → REFUTED).
