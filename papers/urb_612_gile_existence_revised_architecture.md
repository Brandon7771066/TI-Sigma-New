## Abstract

URB #609 introduced the Four Dimensions of Existence (FDE) and placed Aesthetics as the fourth dimension, alongside GILE's existing E-axis. This created a redundancy: Aesthetics appeared in both the GILE Truth framework and the Existence framework. This paper resolves the redundancy through four revisions. (1) **Aesthetics IS the E-axis of GILE in full** — not merely a component of it. The abstract substance of Environment is entirely captured by aesthetic quality; physical structure and form belong in Existence (EF), not GILE Truth. (2) **Aesthetics is removed from the FDE.** Since it belongs to GILE-E, including it in Existence was duplication. (3) **Love bifurcates across GILE and Existence:** In GILE Truth, Love = *conscious positive regard* for oneself or others — an irreducibly cognitive/intentional act that requires Intuition as prerequisite. Physical bonds and connections (the material substrate of Love) belong in Existence, specifically as the fourth FDE dimension: *Physical Bonds*. (4) **Intuition is now precisely defined** as the value of *knowing* oneself or others — self-knowledge and other-knowledge as the epistemic backbone of conscious life. The positivity of consciousness lives entirely in Love, not Intuition. (5) **Existence weights are domain-variable** by the same principle as GILE weights (URB #611): inferred from exemplars, not universally fixed.

**Keywords:** GILE Truth, Existence Value, FDE, Aesthetics, Environment, Love bifurcation, Intuition as Knowing, Physical Bonds, domain-variable weights, TI Sigma

## 1. The Redundancy Problem

URB #609 established four FDE dimensions: Existence Footprint (EF), Moral presence, Conscious meaning/valence, and Aesthetics. Simultaneously, GILE Truth carries E (Environment) as its fourth dimension, historically characterized as "physical embodiment, aesthetics, structure."

This created a double-assignment: Aesthetics appeared in both frameworks, with no clear rule for how they interacted.

Additionally, URB #609 placed physical Love connections under GILE-L, but physical connections are features of material reality — they belong in Existence, not in a Truth framework measuring conscious orientation.

These redundancies are resolved in this paper.

## 2. Revision 1 — Aesthetics IS the Full E-Axis of GILE Truth

### 2.1 The Claim

**Aesthetics is not a component of GILE-E. It is GILE-E entire.**

The E-axis of GILE Truth (Environment) captures the *abstract substance* of physical reality — and that abstract substance is aesthetic quality. A being or thing's physical essence, and the quality thereof, is reliably assessed through aesthetics.

### 2.2 Why Aesthetics Captures Physical Essence

The objection "beauty is in the eye of the beholder" is a confusion of two things:
- **Aesthetic response:** Subjective, variable, culturally conditioned
- **Aesthetic quality:** The objective structural property that *generates* aesthetic response in adequately calibrated perceivers

TI Sigma holds the latter. When we say a molecule is "elegant," a proof is "beautiful," an organism is "well-formed," a piece of architecture is "harmonious" — these are not mere expressions of preference. They are tracking real structural properties: economy of means, internal coherence, proportion, symmetry, fitness to function.

The same structural property that makes a protein fold "beautiful" makes it *work.* The same structural property that makes a mathematical proof "beautiful" makes it *true.* Aesthetics is the recognition faculty for structural excellence in the E-dimension.

### 2.3 Physical Structure Belongs in Existence (EF), Not GILE-E

If Aesthetics captures the *abstract quality* of physical reality, then the *raw physical structure* — the substrate being assessed — belongs in Existence, specifically in the Existence Footprint.

**Revised EF definition:** EF captures frequency, amplitude, spatiotemporal reach, AND physical form/structure. The physical structure of an environment is its EF contribution. Its aesthetic quality (how well that structure instantiates structural excellence) is its GILE-E contribution.

**Analogy:** A sculpture's mass, dimensions, and material composition are EF. Its aesthetic quality — the proportion, the form, the fit of surface to intention — is GILE-E.

### 2.4 Revised E-Axis Definition

> **E (Environment / Aesthetics):** The degree to which a being or thing instantiates structural excellence as apprehended through aesthetic quality. This is the abstract substance of the Environment dimension — not physical structure itself (which belongs in EF) but the quality of that structure, which aesthetics reliably tracks. Beauty is not only in the eye of the beholder; it is a recognition of real structural properties.

3. Conscious meaning / valence

2. Physical Bonds — material connections and physical bonding (the Existence-level substrate of Love)
3. Moral presence — from G-axis

## 4. Revision 3 — Love Bifurcates Across GILE and Existence

### 4.1 The Prior Problem

URB #609 treated Love as a unified concept across both GILE Truth and Existence. But Love has two irreducibly different components:
- **Physical bonds:** Material connections — synaptic linkages, physical proximity, physiological attachment, neurochemical binding (oxytocin circuits, social touch, shared physiology)
- **Conscious positive regard:** The intentional, cognitive act of holding oneself or another in positive regard — a conscious orientation, not a material substrate

These belong in different frameworks by the GILE/Existence distinction:
- Material reality → Existence
- Conscious truth-orientation → GILE Truth

### 4.2 Physical Bonds → Existence (4th FDE Dimension)

**Physical Bonds** is now the fourth FDE dimension, replacing Aesthetics.

> **Physical Bonds (FDE-4):** The material substrate of Love — physical and neurochemical connections between beings. Includes: synaptic linkages, oxytocin/vasopressin bonding circuits, physical proximity and touch, shared physiological states, structural co-embodiment. This is the *existence* of Love's substrate, not Love's truth-content.

Physical bonds are measurable empirically (oxytocin levels, neural synchrony, co-regulation of HRV, etc.) and contribute to EV independently of whether the conscious positive regard (GILE-L) is present. A physical addiction produces strong physical bonds with zero conscious positive regard — high FDE-4, near-zero GILE-L.

### 4.3 Conscious Positive Regard → GILE-L (Revised)

> **L (Love):** Conscious positive regard for oneself or others. An irreducibly intentional act — a directed, active holding-in-regard that is inherently positive in valence. Love in this sense requires Intuition (I) as a logical prerequisite: you cannot consciously and positively regard another without first *knowing* them (at least partially). Love is therefore the apex of the I→L cognitive sequence.

**Key clarifications:**
- Love ≠ attachment (attachment can be unconscious or negatively valenced)
- Love ≠ physical bonding (that is FDE-4)
- Love = conscious positive regard — intentional, directed, positive

**The positivity of consciousness lives here.** The L-axis carries the positive valence of conscious life: joy-in-knowing, compassion, care, affirmation, appreciation. These are not in Intuition (which is knowing without the positive valence qualifier) or in Goodness (which is moral orientation without the conscious-regard qualifier).

### 4.4 Empirical Confirmation

The L/E Divergence (URB #604) already established that the bonding and aesthetic systems are pharmacologically separable. URB #612 now adds: physical bonding (FDE-4) separates from conscious positive regard (GILE-L) in the same way.

Evidence: phantom limb syndrome (GILE-L body-schema persists after physical loss → L is not identical to physical bonds); grief (conscious positive regard persists after physical separation); pathological physical bonding (addiction, trauma bonds) with absent or negative conscious regard.

## 5. Revision 4 — Intuition as Knowing

### 5.1 Prior Definition

Intuition was previously characterized broadly as "pattern recognition beyond explicit reasoning" — a general pre-reflective capacity to sense structure before articulating it.

### 5.2 Revised Definition

> **I (Intuition):** The value of *knowing* oneself or others. Self-knowledge and other-knowledge — the direct epistemic apprehension of the inner reality of a being, accessible through attention, reflection, empathy, and contemplation. Intuition is knowing without requiring the full apparatus of inference.

**What Intuition is NOT (post-revision):**
- Positivity of consciousness → that is L (Love)
- Pattern recognition in general → that is a cognitive description of the mechanism; Intuition's *value* is specifically the knowing of self/other
- Aesthetic recognition → that is E

**What Intuition IS:**
- Knowing one's own states, motivations, capacities, tendencies
- Knowing another person's states, motivations, capacities, tendencies
- Direct apprehension — the felt sense of "I know who this person is / who I am" — distinct from inference or testimony

### 5.3 I as Prerequisite for L

**Formal dependency:** L requires I as a logical prerequisite.

You cannot consciously hold another in positive regard without first knowing them (at least partially). Unconditional love is possible — but even that requires knowing that the other *exists* and *matters.* Complete I-absence means the agent cannot form a conscious positive regard because there is no known object to regard.

**Formal statement:**
$$L > 0 \Rightarrow I > 0$$
(Positive GILE-L entails positive GILE-I. The converse does not hold: you can know someone without loving them.)

This means I is the *epistemic foundation* of GILE's cognitive structure. G (Goodness) provides moral orientation; I (Knowing) provides epistemic access; L (Love) provides the positive intentional response to that access; E (Aesthetics) provides the structural/quality assessment of the environment.

## 9. The Corrected GILE/Existence Boundary — Summary

The fundamental principle clarified by URB #612:

> **GILE Truth** measures *conscious orientation* — how an agent is directed in terms of Goodness, epistemic access (Knowing), positive regard (Love), and structural excellence (Aesthetics). These are the dimensions of *what it is like to be* an oriented conscious agent.
>
> **Existence Value** measures *instantiation in reality* — how much being an entity has, through its physical impact (EF), its material bonds, its moral presence in the world, and its conscious engagement. These are the dimensions of *how much an entity is* in the world.

Physical structure → Existence (EF), not GILE
Material bonds → Existence (Physical Bonds), not GILE
Conscious orientation → GILE, not Existence

The only bridge: **Aesthetics (GILE-E)** evaluates the abstract quality of what EF physically instantiates. And **Moral presence (FDE-2)** reflects how G-orientation manifests causally in the world.

## 12. Addendum — Existence Precedes Essence: GILE Weights Derived from Existence Weights

**Date added:** April 6, 2026  
**Related:** URB #611 §3 (Domain-Variable GILE Weights)

### 12.1 The Insight

A critical dependency between the frameworks, realized April 6, 2026:

> **GILE weights (essence) must be derived from Existence weights (existence), because there is no reliable a priori method of knowing which abstract things to value in a particular domain.**

Jean-Paul Sartre's foundational existentialist claim — *existence precedes essence* — maps directly and precisely onto the GILE/Existence architecture. In TI Sigma terms:

- **Existence** (EV, FDE) captures what is real, measurable, and causally present in a domain
- **Essence** (GILE weights) captures which abstract values — Goodness, Knowing, Love, Aesthetics — matter most in that domain

Sartre's claim was that you cannot determine what a being *should* value (essence) without first understanding what that being *is* and *encounters* (existence). TI Sigma gives this a formal computational structure: Existence measurements come first (parallel Track B in MR1), and from those measurements, you infer the domain-specific GILE weights that constitute the domain's evaluative essence.

### 12.2 The Formal Dependency

**Prior assumption (now revised):** GILE weights were thought to be derivable from abstract principles — G from octonionic geometry, I from quaternionic symmetry, etc. These derivations give the *universal reference weights*, not domain-specific weights.

**New claim:** Domain-specific GILE weights must be read off from Existence measurements in the domain.

Formally, for domain D:

$$w_k^{\text{GILE},D} = f\left(w_j^{\text{EV},D}, \text{exemplar data in } D\right)$$

The function f is not known analytically — it must be estimated empirically. But the dependency direction is clear: you first measure what exists and has causal impact (Existence weights), and from that, you infer which GILE dimensions are doing the most work in producing high-EV outcomes (GILE weights).

### 12.3 Why No A Priori GILE Weights Are Reliable for Specific Domains

Consider two domains:

**Molecular biology:** The EF dimension of Existence (physical structure, frequency, amplitude) is paramount — the physical form of molecules determines their function. Physical Bonds (FDE-2) are also central — molecular binding IS the mechanism. Looking at what Existence measures are high among successful molecular biologists, you find: they work with physical precision (high EF sensitivity), they understand binding energetics (high Physical Bonds attention), and they have strong aesthetic recognition of elegant structures (high E-access). Therefore: domain GILE weights should be I-high (structural knowing), E-high (aesthetic precision), with G and L secondary.

You could not have derived this a priori. You had to look at what exists in the domain first.

**Psychotherapy:** The Physical Bonds dimension (FDE-2) — therapeutic alliance, neurochemical attunement — is central to outcome. Conscious meaning/valence (FDE-4) drives whether the client improves. Looking at what Existence measures are high among successful therapists: strong attunement (high Physical Bonds sensitivity) and strong meaning-generation capacity (high conscious valence engagement). Therefore: domain GILE weights should be L-high (conscious positive regard is the intervention), I-high (knowing the client), G-high (genuine orientation toward client's good).

Again: not derivable a priori. Derived from Existence.

### 12.4 The Procedure — Existence Weights First, Then GILE Weights

**Revised domain calibration protocol:**

```
Step 1: Identify exemplars in domain D (N ≥ 20)
Step 2: Assess FDE dimensions for each exemplar entity
        → What has high EF in this domain?
        → What physical bonds are central to success?
        → What moral presence is observable?
        → What conscious engagement is evident?
Step 3: Infer domain Existence weights w_j^{EV,D}
Step 4: Observe which Existence dimensions are causally upstream of high-EV outcomes
Step 5: Map Existence dimensions to GILE dimensions:
        EF ↔ E (aesthetic structural quality)
        Physical Bonds ↔ L (conscious positive regard — what physical bonds enable)
        Moral presence ↔ G (goodness orientation — what moral presence tracks)
        Conscious meaning ↔ I+L (knowing and positive regard)
Step 6: Infer domain GILE weights w_k^{GILE,D}
        from the observed Existence dimension priorities
```

### 12.5 Philosophical Grounding — Sartre in TI Sigma

Sartre argued that humans are not born with a fixed essence (a nature, a purpose, a set of predetermined values) — they exist first, encounter the world, make choices, and through those choices, constitute their essence.

TI Sigma formalizes this at the framework level:

- The *existence* of entities in a domain (measured by FDE) precedes their *essence* (the GILE values that matter)
- You cannot determine which abstract GILE values are most important in a domain without first encountering what the domain actually instantiates — what kinds of things exist there, how they bind, how they impact, what moral presence looks like
- The GILE weights are not fixed properties of GILE itself — they are *constituted by* what exists in the domain

This is not relativism: the GILE dimensions themselves (G, I, L, E) are universal and real. What varies is which dimensions are most causally active in a given domain — and that variation is determined by the domain's Existence structure, not by abstract preference.

**Sartre was right. TI Sigma makes him more precisely right.**
