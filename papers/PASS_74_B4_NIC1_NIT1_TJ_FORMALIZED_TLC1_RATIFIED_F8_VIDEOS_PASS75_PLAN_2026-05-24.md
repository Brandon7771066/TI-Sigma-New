### §3.1 Canonical definition (refines pre-existing replit.md TJ entry)

**TJ (Tralse-Joule) — the canonical unit of intentionality.** Pre-existing definition: `TJ = τ(s) × δ(MR)` where τ(s) = tralseness-quality at situation s, δ(MR) = MR-degree of resolution toward T. **Pass-74-B4 canonical refinement:** TJ has DUAL operationalization tracks for empirical anchoring.

### §3.2 DUAL operationalization (canonical)

**Track A — Internal-cognitive measurement (intentionality-side):**
- τ(s) ∈ [0,1]: tralseness-quality coefficient at situation s; measured via MR Truth Label assignment density (count of T+I+DT+MI assignments / total propositions in s's mental representation)
- δ(MR) ∈ [-1,+1]: MR-degree of resolution direction; +1 = full T-resolution; 0 = full DT/MI; -1 = full F-resolution
- TJ = τ(s) × δ(MR) ∈ [-1,+1]: signed intentionality-magnitude at s

**Track B — External-environmental measurement (Rosetta-stone, NEW):** Bidirectional translation between TJ (consciousness-energy) and Joules (physics-energy) via empirical correlates:
- **Brain electrical activity:** EEG power spectral density (PSD) integrated across consciousness-correlate bands (gamma 30-100 Hz + frontal theta 4-8 Hz); measured in μV²/Hz → translatable to Watts via tissue-impedance; cumulative integration → Joules
- **Brain biophoton emission:** ultraweak photon emission from neural tissue (10⁻¹⁶ to 10⁻¹⁷ W/cm² baseline; reported modulation under meditation/altered-states; controversial but published evidence base e.g. Van Wijk 2014, Esposito et al.); photons × frequency × Planck constant → direct Joules conversion

**Rosetta-stone relation (canonical hypothesis):** 1 TJ ≈ K × Joules, where K is the **consciousness-physics translation constant**. K's empirical estimation is RESERVED (5 falsifier-anchored experiments below).
