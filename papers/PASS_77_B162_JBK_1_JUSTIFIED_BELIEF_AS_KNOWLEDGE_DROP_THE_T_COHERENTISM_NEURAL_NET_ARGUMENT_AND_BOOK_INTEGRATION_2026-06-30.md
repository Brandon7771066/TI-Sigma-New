## 1. JBK-1 — Justified-Belief-as-Knowledge (CANDIDATE, NOT ratified)

### 1.1 The thesis

The classical analysis of knowledge is **Justified True Belief (JTB)**: S knows p iff (i) S believes p, (ii) S is justified in believing p, (iii) p is true. JBK-1 claims the **T is the defective condition** and should be dropped: knowledge, as *anything an agent can aim for or claim to possess*, just **is justified belief**.

Two-part argument:

1. **"Truth" in JTB conflates two different notions.**
   - **Justified truth** — well-supportedness in the accessible web of reasons/evidence. Inspectable, arguable, possessable.
   - **Metaphysical truth** — correspondence to reality-in-itself, obtaining whether or not anyone could tell. By its own definition **inaccessible**: we never step outside all our beliefs to compare one against bare reality.
2. **The T is (a) unreachable and (b) redundant.**
   - *(a) Unreachable:* every operation we actually run to *certify* knowledge — check reasons, weigh evidence, test coherence — certifies **justification**. Metaphysical truth is never a further box we tick.
   - *(b) Redundant/idle relative to J:* where metaphysical truth holds, it holds **regardless of justification** (a lucky unjustified guess that corresponds is still metaphysically true; a true belief is true whether or not you had reasons). So T floats free of J and adds nothing an agent can wield.

Conjoining an accessible-but-non-correspondence condition (J) with a correspondence-but-inaccessible condition (T) **muddles two theories of truth** — pragmatic/epistemic vs metaphysical/correspondence — and demands their conjunction as if it were one property. JBK-1's diagnosis: this conflation is the deep engine of the Gettier regress.

### 1.2 Tralse knowledge (embraced consequence)

A properly justified belief that turns out metaphysically false was still **knowledge in the only sense we ever had** — **tralse knowledge**: real knowing (epistemically proper) that is not metaphysically true. Newtonian mechanics = paradigm case: extraordinary knowledge, strictly metaphysically false. This is the corpus's **tralse-real** move (TRG-1: real ∧ not-classically-true) applied to *knowing*.

### 1.3 #69 — strongest objection, stated before the defense

- **Factivity** ("S knows p" ⊨ p true) is deeply entrenched; we retract "knew" when p fails. **Reply:** ordinary retraction is (i) discovering the belief was *not in fact well-justified*, or (ii) *upgrading* to a stricter later standard — both moves *inside justification*, not evidence we ever independently checked correspondence. The genuinely maximally-justified-yet-false residue is bitten as tralse knowledge, not papered over.
- **Williamson knowledge-first (2000)** *also rejects* JTB — from the **opposite** direction (keeps truth central; denies knowledge = belief+justification, makes knowledge primitive). Honest placement: JBK-1 is in respectable company abandoning JTB; it parts from Williamson over *which* term to drop. This is a genuine rival, flagged not defeated.

### 1.4 Real antecedents (no fabrication)

- **Plato**, *Theaetetus* (JTB's origin gesture).
- **Gettier (1963)**, "Is Justified True Belief Knowledge?" (the counterexamples).
- **Williamson (2000)**, *Knowledge and Its Limits* (knowledge-first; the principled rival).
- **Pragmatists** — Peirce, James, Dewey (truth as the ideal limit of inquiry, not inaccessible correspondence).
- **Anti-realists** — Dummett; **Putnam** internal realism (the "no God's-eye view" pressure).

Corpus ties: **POD-1** (pragmatic oracle), **TLT** (all proofs fallible), **AA/Authority Axis** (JBK-1 makes the AA's background reservation *permanent by construction* — the reserved-about T is out of reach), **TRG-1** (tralse-real).

### 1.5 Honest novelty (EVD-1)

The lineage (pragmatism, anti-realism, fallibilism, Gettier, knowledge-first) is **all pre-existing**. JBK-1's **delta** = the sharp corpus-native form: (i) name the J/T conflation as *two theories of truth welded together*; (ii) make **justified belief** the operative currency of knowledge outright; (iii) route the false-but-justified residue through the corpus's own **tralse-real** as **"tralse knowledge."** No claim that the general position is novel.

### 1.6 Falsifiers (OPEN)

- **`JBK-1-F1`:** exhibit a genuine case of knowing whose warrant *essentially* requires inaccessible correspondence — i.e. a certification we actually run that is **not**, on inspection, a justification-check in disguise. (If such exists, T is not idle and JBK-1 fails.)
- **`JBK-1-F2`:** show that "tralse knowledge" collapses a distinction we need — i.e. that treating justified-false belief as knowledge licenses crank immunity (any responsibly-held falsehood claims the honorific). Guard: tralse knowledge requires *genuine* justification by the same coherentist standards; it is not a shelter for the unjustified.
- **`JBK-1-F3`:** if factivity turns out to be defensible on grounds *independent* of justification-upgrades (a real access to correspondence), the "unreachable" premise (1.2a) breaks.

## 2. Coherentism as (near-)self-evident — the two supports (NOT new principles)

### 2.1 The sentence↔novel analogy (author's)

A single sentence in isolation ("*He finally understood.*") is nearly meaningless; the identical sentence on a novel's last page is precise and weighted, because the whole book fixes its sense. **The sentence did not change; its web did.** Meaning and warrant are **holistic** — a claim earns sense and justification from its place in the whole. Presented as an intuition-pump for coherentism's *self-evidence*, not a proof.

### 2.2 The neural-network argument (author's; honestly bounded)

- **Mathematically exact part (verified):** a trained neural network never consults metaphysical truth; it minimizes a **divergence** (cross-entropy / KL) between its internal model and the data it is coupled to. Training **is** coherence-maximization under an input constraint — this is *what the loss function computes*, not a metaphor.
- **Empirically strong part (verified):** such systems acquire broad, generalizing, knowledge-*like* competence with **coherence-plus-coupling as the entire mechanism** — no correspondence-to-reality module present or needed.
- **Answers BonJour's isolation objection (1985):** coherence *alone* could be a self-consistent fantasy; but real knowing-webs are coherent **and coupled to input**. Empirical clincher: cut the coupling (ungrounded generation) → **confident hallucination** = the isolation worry appearing on schedule, *confirming* the input-coupled refinement.
- **#69 bound (the hard hedge):** what is exact is *what nets optimize* (a coherence objective, provably). What stays an **analogy** is the leap from "how artificial knowers work" to "what human justification *is*." Powerful existence-proof, **not** a deductive proof that coherentism is the correct epistemology. Full-strength resonance, not a closed theorem. The author's "empirically and mathematically strong" is honored **for the ML mathematics and the empirical success**, and explicitly *not* extended to a proof of the epistemology.

> This is the correction the author's framing needed: "mathematically strong" is TRUE of the optimization fact and FALSE as a claim that coherentism-the-epistemology has been mathematically derived. Both directions stated (#69).

## 3. The three grounds, at reader register (integration of B160–B161)

Distinguishing a live **Indeterminate** (keep) from **Meta-Indeterminate / MI** (nonsense, screened) uses three grounds doing different jobs:

1. **Coherentism → sense** (content-side: load-bearing supporting premises give the in-between a coherent place).
2. **NGK (Negative Gap Knowledge) → tolerability** (content-side: grasp by the shape of the gap; makes it *holdable* before it fully makes sense).
3. **eTJ (Epistemic Tralse-Joule = canonical ETJ-1 #53) → capacity-to-hold** (agent-side: the "motor" to hold contradiction-in-tension without snapping/dismissing).

**Guardrail (`PSM-1-F2`, carried in):** eTJ governs who can *see* a genuine in-between; it **never creates one**. A claim earns Indeterminate on the content side *first* (coherent scaffolding + passes the `in-1` nonsense screen); only then does eTJ explain access-failure. "You lack the eTJ to see it" can **never** convert genuine MI into Indeterminate. Label objective; eTJ = access only.

No new principle here — this is the PSM-1 line (candidate) rendered for the book.
