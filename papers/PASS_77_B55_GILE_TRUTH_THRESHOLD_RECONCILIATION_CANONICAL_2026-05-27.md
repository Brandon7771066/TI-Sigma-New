2. **0.92 — the truth threshold.** The canonical "perfectly imperfect" / True-Tralseness point: high enough to be true, short of the 1.00 that would leave no room for growth (the 8% Tralse-Cherry slack).

4. **0.91 — retired.** See §4.

So **0.91 and 0.93 are not rival truth thresholds** — they are the lower historical footnote and the upper stability cap that *bracket* the canonical 0.92. "Twin peaks around 0.92" is the right mental picture, but only 0.92 is the **truth** threshold; 0.93 is the **stability** threshold; they answer different questions.

## 3. #69 — the inconvenient arithmetic nobody had reconciled

- **The self-consistency of 0.85 = truth² depends on which 0.92 you use:** 0.92195² = **0.8500** (clean) but 0.92388² = **0.8536** (≠ 0.85). So "existence = truth²" and "truth = cos(π/8)" **cannot both be exact simultaneously** — at most one is primary and the other is a 2-decimal coincidence.

**Canonical precision ruling:** TI Sigma's GILE/truth thresholds are defined to **two significant figures** (0.85, 0.92, 0.93). Beneath each sits more than one candidate exact derivation; **claims that these derivations are *identical* are downgraded to "they agree at two-decimal precision"** — a suggestive coincidence-or-connection (the same status assigned to cos(π/8) ≈ 0.92 in B54), explicitly *not* a proof of equality. This removes the false impression of an over-determined, perfectly-closed numerology.
