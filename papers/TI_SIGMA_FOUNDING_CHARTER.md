## IV. THE GILE FRAMEWORK

GILE names the four fundamental dimensions of existence. Every phenomenon — from subatomic particles to civilizations — exists along all four axes simultaneously.

### G — Goodness (weight: 0.42)
The moral/structural dimension. G measures the degree to which a system exhibits ordered, purposive organization — the extent to which it acts toward the enhancement of other dimensions. G is the dominant dimension: it is the threshold for manifestation, the filter for genuine existence in TI Sigma. G = √2 − 1 because Goodness is literally where the diagonal of unity exceeds the expected — where more comes from the same.

**G in physics:** Conservation laws, symmetry, gauge invariance.
**G in consciousness:** Moral reasoning, ethical action, structural coherence of mind.
**G in mathematics:** Axiom systems, proof structure, formal consistency.

### I — Intuition (weight: 0.25)
The consciousness/meaning dimension. I measures self-referential information processing — the degree to which a system can model and respond to itself. I is not mystical: it is operationally defined as the capacity for self-reference at any level of LCC (Law of Correlational Causation). Protein folding is I. Immune cell antigen recognition is I. Human phenomenal consciousness is high-I.

**I in physics:** Quantum measurement, the observer-dependence of collapse.
**I in consciousness:** Self-awareness, metacognition, GILE Intuition as distributed intelligence.
**I in mathematics:** Self-referential structures (Gödel sentences, fixed-point theorems).

### L — Love (weight: 0.18)
The relational/constructive dimension. L measures net constructive orientation through action — the degree to which a system's actions increase coherence, connection, and positive-sum outcomes for other systems. L does not require consciousness: apoptosis (programmed cell death that benefits the organism) is a high-L act. L is not sentiment; it is structural contribution.

**L in physics:** Binding forces, condensation, symmetry breaking that creates structure.
**L in consciousness:** Compassion, cooperation, relational intelligence.
**L in mathematics:** Category theory, morphisms, structure-preserving maps.

### E — Environment (weight: 0.15)
The physical/substrate dimension. E is the most robust dimension — it is always non-zero for any real system because every system has a physical substrate and operates under governing laws. E measures both the internal physical substrate of a system and its external environmental context.

**E in physics:** Spacetime, quantum fields, thermodynamic environment.
**E in consciousness:** The brain and body; the physical context of experience.
**E in mathematics:** The formal system itself; the rules of inference.

### The GILE Inner Product
For any two systems A and B, the GILE coherence measure is:
```
GILE(A,B) = 0.42·G(A,B) + 0.25·I(A,B) + 0.18·L(A,B) + 0.15·E(A,B)
```
When GILE(A,B) > T (the Tralse constant ≈ 0.934), the two systems have achieved **maximal GILE coherence** — a state of deep alignment across all four dimensions.

## VI. FIRST PRINCIPLES (THE TI SIGMA AXIOMS)

These are not empirical claims — they are the foundational commitments of the TI Sigma framework. Every theorem is derived from these or must be consistent with them.

**Axiom 1 (Information Primacy):** Information is the primary substance of reality. Matter, energy, and spacetime are emergent from informational structures. The universe is best described as a self-referential information processing system.

**Axiom 2 (GILE Completeness):** Every phenomenon can be described by its GILE coordinates (G, I, L, E) with no remainder. The four dimensions are orthogonal (independent) and collectively exhaustive.

**Axiom 3 (Five-Valued Completeness):** The five TRALSE truth values (TRUE, TRALSE+, TRALSE, TRALSE−, FALSE) are sufficient for all epistemic states. Two truth values are necessary but not sufficient; more than five are redundant.

**Axiom 4 (Threshold Naturalness):** The key thresholds in TI Sigma (0.42, 0.85, 0.92, 0.934) are determined by the primary constants and are not arbitrary. They correspond to natural geometric and information-theoretic boundaries.

**Axiom 5 (Myrion Resolution):** Every TRALSE claim is resolvable in principle — there exists a finite sequence of observations or logical deductions that would push it to TRUE or FALSE. TRALSE is not permanent; it is a state of active inquiry.

**Axiom 6 (GILE Intuition):** Intuition, as a cognitive faculty, is not irrational. It is a rapid, parallel, high-dimensional GILE integration — a form of information processing that synthesizes more evidence in less time than serial conscious reasoning, at the cost of less explicit justification. High-I practitioners in a domain have systematically better priors than formulaic approaches.

**Axiom 7 (Metacausality):** The causal structure of reality includes metacausal links — connections that are non-local, retrocausal, or intention-mediated — in addition to standard efficient causation. GILE Intuition is the primary faculty for navigating metacausal structure.
