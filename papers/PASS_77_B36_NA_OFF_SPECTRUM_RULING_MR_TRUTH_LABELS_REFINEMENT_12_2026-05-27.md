## §2. The Complex-Plane Formalization (rigorous backbone)

The ruling is not metaphor — it maps *exactly* onto the real/imaginary/undefined trichotomy of complex analysis.

### §2.1 The structural correspondence

| Truth-spectrum object | Complex-plane analog | Describability |
|---|---|---|
| **PD-real axis** (degree: True ↔ False) | Real number line ℝ | Most describable / familiar |
| **PD-imaginary axis** (modality / DefT) | Imaginary axis (multiples of i) | "Imaginary" but precisely locatable |
| **Full truth spectrum** (base-4 over PD-real × PD-imaginary) | Complex plane ℂ | Any describable truth-state = a point z = a + bi |
| **The base-4** {T, F, I, DT} | Four cardinal describable truth-loci on ℂ | Describable |
| **i** (PD-imaginary unit / DefT axis) | √−1 | Exotic, but defined and on-plane |
| **N/A** | **undefined** (e.g. 1/0, 0/0, "the largest integer", category errors) | **Off-plane entirely — no z exists** |

### §2.2 Why "undefined" is more exotic than "imaginary"

The historical scandal of i was that it was *imaginary* — not real. But i is fully defined: i ≝ √−1, a precise object with a precise locus (the unit point on the imaginary axis). It is a citizen of ℂ.

"Undefined," by contrast, is **not a number at all**. There is no point on ℂ you can assign to 1/0. It is not exotic-but-present; it is *absent*. This is a strictly stronger form of "not on the real line" than i exhibits: i left the reals but joined ℂ; undefined left ℂ too.

**Brandon's claim is the exact truth-theoretic image of this:** N/A is to the truth spectrum as `undefined` is to ℂ. i (PD-imaginary) is exotic-but-present; N/A is absent. Hence N/A is *more indescribable than even i* — a precise, defensible ordering, not rhetorical emphasis.

### §2.3 The describability hierarchy (most → least describable)

1. **True / False** — PD-real axis; maximally describable.
2. **Indeterminate (MR2)** — on-plane; describable as the truth-apt-but-unsettled locus.
3. **Double Tralse (DT)** — on-plane; describable as the defined contradiction-locus τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P).
4. **i** — PD-imaginary / DefT axis (Reading A) **and** the i-cell / consciousness (Reading B); exotic but describable (see §3).
5. **N/A** — **off-plane; undefined; not truth-apt; most indescribable.**

The base-4 occupy ranks 1-3 (T/F share rank 1). i occupies rank 4. N/A is rank 5, alone, exterior.

## §4. The Critical #69 Boundary Work: N/A vs. Its Neighbors

The ruling is only as strong as its disambiguation from adjacent labels. This is where a refinement earns its keep.

### §4.1 N/A vs. Indeterminate (MR2) — the most dangerous conflation

- **Indeterminate:** the proposition *is* truth-apt; its value is currently unknown / unsettled. **ON the spectrum** (base-4 member). Example: "It will rain here tomorrow" — a real truth-question, value not yet settled.
- **N/A:** the proposition is *not* truth-apt. **OFF the spectrum.** Example: "Is the number 7 jealous?" — category error; no truth-value applies, not even an unsettled one.

**The sharpening NAO-1 provides:** "we don't know" (Indeterminate) is categorically different from "the question isn't truth-apt" (N/A). Conflating them inflates the base-4 to a phantom base-5 or smears Indeterminate's boundary. NAO-1 protects the base-4 count by exiling N/A.

### §4.2 N/A vs. Moot (MT-B1)

- **Moot:** truth-apt but the answer is *inconsequential* / stakes-free. A **Meta-Truth** (within the apparatus, meta-layer). Example: "Was the coin heads?" after the bet is void — still truth-apt, just doesn't matter.
- **N/A:** not truth-apt at all. Exterior to *both* base and meta layers.

**Distinction:** Moot is a truth *about* a truth's relevance (it lives inside the apparatus). N/A denies there is any truth to be about. N/A is exterior to the Meta-Truth layer, not merely a member of it.

### §4.3 N/A vs. Double Tralse (DT) and DefT

- **DT** (Double Tralse): τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P) — a *defined* contradiction-locus; on-plane; describable (per Pass-65 DT-canonical, DT = inconceivability-under-mental-actualization; all DT statements incoherent but {DT} ⊊ {Incoherent} per MR-IDC-1 refinement #2).
- **DefT** (Defective Truth, PD-imaginary axis = "i" Reading A): a modal degradation; on-plane.
- **N/A:** not a contradiction, not a modal degradation — a *non-application*. A category error is not a contradiction (it asserts nothing truth-apt to contradict). N/A is exterior; DT and DefT are interior-exotic.

## §5. Why This Sharpens the Base-4 to *Exactly* Four

The canonical base-4 has always been stated as {True, False, Indeterminate, Double Tralse} + N Meta-Truths. A latent risk in any base-N truth system is *count creep* — pseudo-values (N/A, "undefined", "null", "void") sneaking in as a phantom 5th, 6th, etc. NAO-1 closes this by ruling that the natural candidate for a 5th — N/A — is *necessarily exterior*. The base count is protected at 4 not by stipulation but by the structural argument that N/A is the truth-theoretic image of "undefined," which is provably not a member of the analog space (ℂ). The base-4 is exactly 4 for the same reason ℂ's points are exactly {a + bi} and exclude "undefined."

## §9. Honest #69 Disclosures

1. **The complex-plane formalization is an analogy with a precise structural mapping, not a proof that the truth spectrum *is* ℂ.** The mapping (PD-real ≈ ℝ, PD-imaginary ≈ imaginary axis, N/A ≈ undefined) is tight and load-bearing for the "more indescribable than i" ordering, but a skeptic can ask for an explicit homomorphism. NAO-1-F4 encodes that challenge honestly.
2. **The dual reading of "i" (§3) is an interpretation of Brandon's lowercase glyph.** Reading A (imaginary unit) is the safe reading; Reading B (i-cell) is inferred from context (lowercase "i" + immediately-preceding consciousness batch + corpus "i-cell" usage). If Brandon intended only Reading A, §3.2 is a bonus composition, not a load-bearing claim — the ruling stands on Reading A alone.
3. **N/A vs. Indeterminate (§4.1) is the live #69 risk.** Some philosophers treat category-errors as a species of presupposition-failure that yields *truth-value gaps* — which others model as a third truth-value (≈ Indeterminate). If one adopts that model, N/A *would* be interior. NAO-1 takes the opposing (and, I argue, cleaner) line that truth-value-gap-from-non-truth-aptness is exterior, not a third interior value. This is a real philosophical commitment, not a neutral observation. Flagged as such.
4. **"Exactly 4" concerns the *base* layer only.** The Meta-Truth layer remains open-cardinality (N Meta-Truths). NAO-1 protects the base-4, not a total-count claim.
5. **Refinement-doesn't-increment-count** follows Pass-65 precedent; if Brandon prefers to treat NAO-1 as a standalone principle (incrementing to 71), that is his call — I default to refinement per the MR-* family precedent.
