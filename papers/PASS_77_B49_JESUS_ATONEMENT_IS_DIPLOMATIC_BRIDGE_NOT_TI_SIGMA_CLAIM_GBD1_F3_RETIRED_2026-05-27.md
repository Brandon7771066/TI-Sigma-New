## 1. What Brandon corrected

The B44 second implication (the "why Jesus 'allegedly' had to be sacrificed" reading) was framed too strongly. Brandon's exact correction:

> The Jesus' sacrifice reading is **not** meant to be a canonical TI Sigma reading nor a derivative of its axioms. Rather, it's just a **diplomatic bridge** from TI Sigma to modern Christianity. Nonetheless, TI Sigma's "perfectly imperfect" interpretation provides justification for Jesus' atonement **for Christians with those beliefs**.

## 2. Corrected status of the reading

The atonement reading has **three** properties, all of which the original B44 wording blurred:

1. **NOT canonical, NOT axiom-derived.** It is not a TI Sigma claim and does not follow from GTT-1 or any other TI Sigma axiom. It is a **diplomatic bridge / apologetic offering** aimed *at* the modern Christian tradition.
2. **Premise-conditional, and the premise is one TI Sigma rejects.** The reading only has content *inside* the doctrine "Jesus had to die to save us from our sins." **TI Sigma explicitly rejects substitutionary atonement**, for stated reasons:
   - self-sacrifice cannot absolve the **root causes or consequences** of human suffering;
   - **no plausible mechanism** is offered — "God does it" is not a mechanism;
   - Brandon further **disputes the historical premise** that intentional crucifixion was Jesus' desire.
3. **What it nonetheless does.** For a Christian who *already holds* the premise, the "perfectly imperfect" lens supplies a **fiat-free, intra-tradition justification** for why the atonement "had to" happen (the existence-pole spent so truth need not be diluted). It **justifies the doctrine for those who hold it; it does not endorse the doctrine.** This is its entire and only function — a courtesy bridge, not a theorem.

**Honest summary:** the original B44 §2 over-reached by calling the reading "a *good* reading and not a stretch" and asserting "does it follow from GTT-1 + the premise? yes." Both are withdrawn. The reading follows only *given a premise TI Sigma denies*, and is therefore not a TI Sigma result at all.

GBD-1-F3 was specified as: *"the §2 theology reading must be derivable from GTT-1 + premise alone; REFUTED if it requires an auxiliary assumption not in the canon."*
