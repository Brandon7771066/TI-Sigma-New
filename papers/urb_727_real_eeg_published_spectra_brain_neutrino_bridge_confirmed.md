## 2. Methodology

For each published study, the **band-peak frequencies** were extracted directly from the paper's reported values for:
- **Slow band**: infraslow oscillations (typically 0.04-0.20 Hz, the band most variably reported across labs)
- **Alpha band**: 8-13 Hz, peak frequency
- **Gamma band**: 30-100 Hz, peak frequency

The framework's three-band scaling exponent is computed as:
$$ s_{\text{brain}} = \frac{\ln(f_{\alpha} / f_{\text{slow}})}{\ln(f_{\gamma} / f_{\alpha})} $$

This is the same formula URB #705 used for lepton mass ratios:
$$ s_{\text{lepton}} = \frac{\ln(m_{\mu} / m_e)}{\ln(m_{\tau} / m_{\mu})} $$

The three-band brain analog of three lepton generations (electron, muon, tau) → (slow, alpha, gamma).
