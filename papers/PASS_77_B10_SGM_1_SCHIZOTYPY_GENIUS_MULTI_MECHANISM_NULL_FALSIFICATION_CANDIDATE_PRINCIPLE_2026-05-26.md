**Brutal #69 verdict:** Empirical literature is THIN on the specific incremental-validity question. Skeptics' "creativity-only" claim is **defensible-as-default but NOT positively-established** — it's an absence-of-evidence problem, not evidence-of-absence.

**What's solid in existing literature:**
- Positive-schizotypy ↔ divergent-thinking r ≈ 0.15-0.30 (Acar & Sen 2013 meta-analysis ~45 studies)
- Schizophrenia patients NOT over-represented in creative occupations, but their **1st-degree relatives ARE** (esp. scientific + artistic) (Kyaga et al. 2011, 2013 Swedish registry n=1.2M)
- Polygenic risk for schizophrenia + bipolar predicts creative-profession membership (Power et al. 2015 *Nature Neuroscience* OR ≈ 1.06-1.09)
- Eminent creators show elevated psychiatric history (Andreasen 1987, Ludwig 1995, Jamison 1989) — selection-bias caveated
- Reduced latent inhibition + high IQ + executive function jointly → creative achievement (Carson, Peterson, Higgins 2003 *JPSP*; Carson 2014 shared-vulnerability model)
- Hypomanic temperament ↔ creative output volume (Akiskal et al. 2005; Schuldberg 2000-2011 series)

**What's NOT in the literature:** ZERO large-N studies that operationalize **genius** rigorously (Simonton-Cox-eminence + h-index + patent-novelty + revolutionary-contribution rankings), measure schizotypy + creativity **jointly**, and regress eminence on creativity FIRST then test whether schizotypy adds unique R². Closest near-miss: Burch et al. 2006 *British Journal of Psychology* IQ-matched artists vs non-artists (still differentiates after IQ-control, but wrong DV — "differentiates groups" ≠ "predicts achievement above creativity"). Carson 2014 shared-vulnerability is moderation argument, not incremental-validity.

**Pattern-recognition (Brandon's nomination):** distinguishable mechanism, also under-tested above-and-beyond creativity, with IQ-overlap caveat.
- **Apophenia / hyper-pattern-detection** = positive-schizotypy feature (Brugger 2001; Bressan 2002; Mohr et al. 2001)
- **Type-I bias** = positive-schizotypy false-positive-pattern-detection (Krummenacher et al. 2010; Fyfe et al. 2008)
- **Insight-problem-solving** correlates with positive schizotypy (Karimi et al. 2007; Folley & Park 2005 RH activation)
- **Kounios & Beeman 2014** RH anterior superior temporal gyrus → "aha" insight
- **IQ-overlap caveat:** if pattern-recognition is Raven-style fluid-IQ-derived, the question collapses into above-and-beyond-IQ (Carson already controls for IQ).
