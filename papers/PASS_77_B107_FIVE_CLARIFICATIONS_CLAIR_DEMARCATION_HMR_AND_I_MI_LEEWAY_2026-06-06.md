## 1. CGP-1 refinement #3 — Clair-Ability Demarcation (the value-floor)

**Brandon:** *"Having hallucinations doesn't itself count as having a 'clair' ability in that area. It all depends on whether the hallucinations contribute pragmatic value or substantial intrinsic meaning at a bare minimum."*

**The criterion.** A perceptual stream in some modality (visual, auditory, somatic, amodal/claircognitive, …) qualifies as a genuine **clair-ability** in that modality **only if** it clears a minimum **value floor**:

> **CAD floor:** the perceptions must contribute **pragmatic value** (they do useful predictive/decisional work in the world) **OR** carry **substantial intrinsic meaning** (they are non-arbitrary, structured, semantically load-bearing) — at a **bare minimum**.

Raw hallucination — perceptual content that does *neither* — is **not** a clair ability; it is noise wearing the costume of perception. The presence of vivid extrasensory-like content is **necessary-but-not-sufficient**; the value floor is the sufficiency gate.

**Why this is a CGP-1 refinement, not a new principle.** CGP-1's **truth-fidelity leg** (refinement #2, falsifier CGP-1-PT-F2′) already states that extrasensory channels *risk* false / MI / N-A beliefs and reality-decoupling. CAD sharpens that leg from the *input* side: before we even rank a channel's fidelity, we must ask whether the channel is **delivering perception at all** vs. **manufacturing hallucination**. The value floor is the demarcation that lets the truth-fidelity comparison be about *channels that actually perceive*, not about noise generators. It also coheres with the **B93 hallucination ledger** (Brandon's own honest catalogue of his hallucinations) — that ledger is *not* a claim of clair-abilities; CAD is exactly why those entries are logged as hallucinations rather than as clair-perceptions.

**#69 honesty.** This raises the bar on Brandon's *own* claims: any clair-ability he or anyone else asserts must be accompanied by either a demonstrable pragmatic payoff or a defensible intrinsic-meaning structure. Mere subjective vividness earns nothing. This is the asymmetric-standards ethos applied inward.

**New OPEN falsifier — CGP-1-CAD-F1.** Exhibit a perceptual stream that contributes **neither** pragmatic value **nor** substantial intrinsic meaning yet is nonetheless, on principled grounds, a genuine clair-ability → would refute the value-floor demarcation. (Conversely, if *every* hallucination turns out to clear the floor under a sufficiently loose reading of "meaning," the criterion is vacuous — anti-cheat: "intrinsic meaning" must be non-trivial and not retro-fitted.)

**Counter:** CGP-1 refinements 2 → **3**; canonical count unchanged **79**.

## 3. HMR refinement (MR Truth Labels #16) — second-label inclusion is governed by present-resolution

**Brandon:** *"Whether or not to include a second (or more) truth label depends on the extent to which the final label(s) is resolved in the present."*

**Background.** HMR (Hybrid MR) already established the two-tier rule: **display the hybrid** (merged successive-MR value) **for faithfulness**, but **privilege the final MR value for present action**. What HMR did *not* yet specify is **when** to actually surface the extra label(s). B107 supplies that criterion:

> **HMR second-label-inclusion criterion:** surface a second (or further) truth label to the **extent that the final label is *un*resolved in the present**. The dial runs:
> - **Final label well-resolved in the present** → the final label **alone** suffices (privilege-final; suppress the hybrid as redundant).
> - **Final label poorly/partially resolved in the present** → **surface the additional label(s)** (display-hybrid; the present genuinely needs the multifaceted value because the final label is not yet doing the whole job).

This makes the display-vs-privilege choice a **graded dial**, not a binary — and grounds the dial in a single quantity: **present-resolution of the final label**. It composes cleanly with HMR-1-F7 (present-primacy): present-primacy is the *limit case* where present-resolution is high; the new criterion fills in the intermediate regime.

**New OPEN falsifier — HMR-1-F8 (resolution monotonicity).** If well-justified second-label inclusion turns out to be **independent of**, or **inversely related to**, present-resolution of the final label (e.g., cases where a *fully* present-resolved final label *still* requires the hybrid for action), the monotone criterion is refuted.

**Counter:** MR Truth Labels refinements 15 → **16**.

## 5. MR Truth Labels refinement #17 — I-vs-MI leeway demarcation ("fully…fully" is the MI marker)

**Brandon:** *"'Alive and dead' provides a lot of leeway compared to 'fully alive and fully dead.'"*

**The demarcation.** The very same surface predicate-pair lands on **different truth-labels** depending on a **degree-qualifier**:

| Phrase | Truth label | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **"alive and dead"** | **Indeterminate (MR2)** — coherent | Carries substantial **leeway**: compatible with superposition, partial/transitional states, perspectival both-lean, metaphor. The conjunction does **not** force both poles to maximum simultaneously. |
| **"fully alive and fully dead"** | **Meta-Indeterminate (MI)** — incoherent | The "**fully…fully**" qualifier **removes all leeway** — it asserts **both poles at maximal degree at once** = τ(P) ∧ ¬τ(P), a genuine contradiction with no interpretive slack. |

**The load-bearing insight:** the word **"fully"** (or any maximal-degree intensifier applied to *both* poles) is a **linguistic marker that pushes a coherent-Indeterminate reading across the boundary into MI**. Coherent Indeterminacy lives precisely in the **leeway** — the room for the two leans to be partial, perspectival, or temporally staged. Strip the leeway with a double "fully," and you get incoherence.

This sharpens the **MR-IDC-1 guard** (the principle that the ground/various states are *coherent Indeterminate, not MI*): MR-IDC-1 can now point to a crisp **diagnostic** — *is there interpretive leeway?* Leeway present → Indeterminate; leeway annihilated by maximal-degree double-assertion → MI. It also dovetails with the **PD-real degree axis** (the "fully" is a degree-claim) and the **HMR successive-merge** machinery (a phrase may *start* Indeterminate and only collapse to MI when over-specified).

**#69 honesty.** This does **not** make "alive and dead" *true* — it remains Indeterminate. And it does not rescue MI from incoherence — "fully alive and fully dead" stays genuinely contradictory. The refinement is purely a **demarcation sharpening**: it tells you *which side of the I/MI line a natural-language both-pole phrase falls on*, via the leeway/degree test.

**New OPEN falsifier — MR-IDC-1-F-leeway.** Exhibit a both-pole phrase that (a) retains full interpretive leeway yet is genuinely MI/incoherent, or (b) carries a maximal-degree double-"fully" assertion yet is coherently Indeterminate → would refute the leeway-as-diagnostic claim.

**Counter:** MR Truth Labels refinements 16 → **17** (this batch contributes **two** MR-label refinements: #16 HMR second-label criterion + #17 I/MI leeway).
