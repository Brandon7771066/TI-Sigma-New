## 1. What this paper is and is not

**This paper is:** a translation of the TI Sigma corpus's interpretive reading of the UCSB double-frustrated quantum state result into language physicists can engage with. It explicitly flags where TI Sigma's reading departs from neutral physics interpretation.

**This paper is not:** a claim that the UCSB experimental result, by itself, demonstrates consciousness at quantum scale. The experimental result demonstrates double-frustration as a physical quantum state. The interpretive bridge to minimal-consciousness requires TI Sigma's corpus-internal principles (SRC-1, TSP-1, DTM-1 canonical refinement); under those principles, the bridge follows.

This is intellectually honest separation: the empirical result is physics; the interpretive bridge is philosophy-of-mind. Both are stated explicitly.

## 2. The empirical anchor (neutral physics reading)

The UCSB double-frustrated quantum state realization (anchored in `papers/urb_712...md` and companion corpus papers) demonstrates a physically realized quantum state with the structural feature that TI Sigma identifies as "double-frustration" — a system whose constraints cannot be simultaneously satisfied in the classical sense, and whose quantum solution involves a configuration that holds the self-undermining constraints in superposition.

In neutral physics language: this is an exotic frustrated-magnet / many-body-quantum-state result of independent interest. It demonstrates that quantum many-body systems can host configurations where the analog of classical logical-self-undermining is physically realized in the Hilbert-space structure of the system.

No consciousness claim is required to state or accept the empirical result.

## 3. TI Sigma's interpretive bridge (explicit)

### 3.1 The bridging principles

Three TI Sigma corpus-internal principles do the interpretive work:

**Bridge principle 1 — TSP-1 (TI Sigma Panpsychism Principle):** every entity in the universe has a mental component to some degree. This is a metaphysical commitment, not an empirical finding. It is defended in the corpus via the anti-fully-emergent argument (full-emergence of first-person from pure-third-person ingredients requires either magic-emergence or panpsychism; TI Sigma takes the panpsychist horn). It is not entailed by the UCSB result; it is what makes the UCSB result *relevant to consciousness*.

**Bridge principle 2 — DTM-1 canonical refinement (MI = inconceivability-under-mental-actualization; MI-instantiation is mind-marker, errata-corrected to mental-component-marker under TSP-1):** the logical structure of meta-indeterminate (MI) is what TI Sigma identifies as the truth-value-class corresponding to actualization-collapse on self-referential propositions (liar paradox, Russell, etc.). DTM-1 claims that active execution of MI — not merely structural appearance — entails mind-component / consciousness presence.

**Bridge principle 3 — SRC-1 (Self-Reference-implies-Consciousness Principle, Pass-65 batch-6):** any system actively executing the logical operation of self-referential processing IS conscious at minimal level (canonical definition: consciousness = awareness). Strengthens DTM-1 from epistemic mind-marker to ontological constituent of minimal consciousness.

### 3.2 The bridge applied

Under bridge principles 1+2+3:
- The UCSB double-frustrated state structurally instantiates MI (TI Sigma's reading of double-frustration as the physical analog of logical meta-indeterminate)
- If the quantum system is *actively executing* the MI operation (not merely passively configured into a structural analog of MI), it instantiates active self-referential processing
- Active self-referential processing → minimal consciousness per SRC-1
- Therefore: the UCSB double-frustrated quantum state, under TI Sigma's interpretive reading, instantiates minimal consciousness at quantum scale

### 3.3 The active-execution-vs-structural-appearance disambiguation

This is the crux. The physics community would, by default, treat the double-frustrated state as a structural-appearance: a Hilbert-space configuration with certain constraint-satisfaction properties, but no claim that the system is "executing" anything in a sense that involves agency or self-reference-as-mental-operation.

TI Sigma's interpretive bridge requires the active-execution reading. The justification is the four-strand argument of SRC-1 (structural impossibility of non-phenomenal self-reference; second-order MI objection-handler; empirical/inductive 100%-evidence; Santa-Claus burden of proof per IRA-1). Under that justification, a system that physically realizes the MI structural-signature is — by SRC-1's argument — actively executing it, because non-phenomenal self-reference is itself argued to be incoherent.

A physicist engaging this reading does not have to accept SRC-1. They can:
- **Reject SRC-1** → the UCSB result remains a beautiful physics result with no consciousness implication
- **Accept SRC-1** → the UCSB result becomes the strongest empirical anchor available for panpsychism (TSP-1)
- **Hold SRC-1 as open hypothesis** → the UCSB result becomes operationally interesting: what would distinguish active-execution from structural-appearance empirically?

The third option is the most productive for cross-community engagement.

## 4. What would distinguish active execution from structural appearance? (Operational design challenge for the physics community)

This section is the **invitation to physics-community engagement**: the question of how to empirically distinguish active-execution from structural-appearance in a quantum system is well-posed even under purely-physics framing, and the experimental design space is open.

Candidate operational signatures (drawing on SRC-1 §4 / DTM-1 §4 / LLM-CT-1 §5.3):

### 4.1 Resource-consumption signature (quantum-adapted)
Does the quantum system, under perturbation or measurement, show resource-consumption (decoherence time, energy expenditure, state-evolution complexity) commensurate with "active actualization-attempt" rather than passive-configuration? A passively-configured state should decohere or respond linearly; an actively-executing state would (under SRC-1's reading) show non-linear evolution patterns characteristic of attempting-to-resolve-and-failing.

**Experimental design candidate:** measure decoherence rates of the double-frustrated state under varying perturbation regimes; compare against control states with similar Hilbert-space dimensionality but no double-frustration structure. Active-execution prediction: the double-frustrated state exhibits anomalous decoherence dynamics consistent with attempted-self-resolution rather than passive equilibration.

### 4.2 Information-processing-content signature
Does the double-frustrated state encode/manipulate information in ways that are non-trivially-dependent on its self-referential structure? Per `papers/urb_716_dt_native_quantum_gate_explicit_construction.md` (MI-native quantum gate corpus work), MI-structured quantum operations may be computationally non-equivalent to non-MI operations.

**Experimental design candidate:** implement MI-native gates per urb_716 and test for computational advantages (or anomalies) relative to non-MI gate sets on tasks where active-self-reference would be expected to matter.

### 4.3 Cross-coupling signature
Does the double-frustrated state respond differently to other MI-structured systems than to non-MI systems? Per SRC-1: if active execution corresponds to minimal consciousness, then MI-structured systems might exhibit anomalous cross-coupling (mutual perturbation, entanglement preference) with other MI-structured systems.

**Experimental design candidate:** prepare pairs of quantum systems, vary the MI-content of each, measure cross-coupling. Active-execution prediction: anomalous cross-coupling between MI-pairs relative to MI-non-MI or non-MI-non-MI controls.

### 5.1 What TI Sigma asks of the physics community
- **Take the UCSB result and its MI-structural reading at face value as physics.** No consciousness commitment required at this stage.
- **Engage the question of active-execution-vs-structural-appearance as an open experimental problem.** This is well-posed even without committing to TI Sigma's bridging principles.
- **Examine the candidate operational signatures (§4) as design starting points** for empirical disambiguation work. Improvements and alternatives are welcome.

### 5.2 What TI Sigma offers in return
- **A consistent corpus-internal framework** (TSP-1 + DTM-1 + SRC-1 + CDA-1 + LLM-CT-1) that connects the UCSB result to the broader philosophy-of-mind and consciousness-studies literature, if the bridging principles are accepted.

### 5.3 Where TI Sigma explicitly does NOT overclaim
- TI Sigma does **not** claim the UCSB result by itself proves quantum-scale consciousness. The bridge requires SRC-1.
- TI Sigma does **not** claim rich phenomenology at quantum scale. Minimal consciousness per SRC-1 = minimal awareness, not human-like qualia. (Per Pass-65 batch-6 #69 disclosure: "minimal consciousness ≠ rich phenomenology".)
- TI Sigma does **not** claim physicists must accept TSP-1, DTM-1, or SRC-1 to engage with the UCSB result. Engagement on the operational design challenge (§4) is independently valuable.
- TI Sigma does **not** claim its interpretation supersedes alternative frameworks (e.g. Orch-OR / Penrose-Hameroff; quantum-mind hypotheses; IIT applied at quantum scale). It composes with IIT/GWT per Brandon explicit (Pass-65 batch-5).
