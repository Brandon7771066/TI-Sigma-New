## Objection 2.1 — "The psi / precognition claims are pseudoscience and discredit the rest of the framework."

**Strongest form.** Psi-research has a century of failed replications. Any framework that builds on psi inherits psi's epistemic burden.

**Response — structural decoupling.** Precognition appears in FDS-1 (§7.7.99) as **one optional input** to the AA (Authority Axis) credibility composition. The framework's empirical claims do not depend on psi:

- The quantum-classical hybrid results (qc25, qc26 GHZ-5 Mermin |M₅|=14.535, 71σ Bell-violation on real ibm_marrakesh hardware) stand on standard quantum mechanics with no psi component.
- The MR Truth-Labels inter-rater agreement Fleiss κ=0.906 (T45-4) is a categorical-taxonomic measurement requiring no psi.
- The Mendi STIM2 t=−4.13, p≪0.001 effect (Pass-43) is a within-subjects pre/post measurement on a consumer fNIRS device — no psi involved.
- The 4 DGI taxonomy and the gender-clarification application are structural-descriptive — no psi.
- The 20 Lean4 theorems are pure formal verification.

FDS-1's precognition formula ΔPD-real = base_conviction × past_track_record × AA_credibility is itself **falsifiable**: F-2 (psi effect zero in meta-analysis) is a pre-registered route to retracting the precognition increment without retracting the rest of FDS-1.

**Strategic concession.** If a critic refuses to engage with anything labeled "psi," the apologetics move is: drop FDS-1's precognition increment for that conversation, present the remaining framework (which contains everything substantive), and let the empirical work speak. Psi is the *least* load-bearing claim in the corpus.

**Falsifier.** F-2: if a properly-powered meta-analysis of precognitive-target tasks produces zero effect, the precognition-increment in FDS-1 is retracted. The rest of FDS-1 (3-regime decomposition) is independent.

## Objection 2.2 — "Your quantum-classical hybrid claims are hand-wavy / typical 'quantum mind' garbage."

**Strongest form.** Penrose-Hameroff Orch-OR, quantum brain microtubules, and similar proposals have been thoroughly criticized. Any framework invoking quantum entanglement in cognitive contexts inherits this criticism.

**Response — measured, not invoked.** The corpus does *not* claim quantum entanglement is the substrate of cognition. The corpus claims:

1. **Measurable QC results on actual hardware.** qc26 GHZ-5 Mermin |M₅|=14.535 represents 91% of the theoretical maximum, a **71σ violation** of the local-hidden-variable bound, executed on ibm_marrakesh (real superconducting hardware, not a simulator). This is a hardware measurement, not a brain-microtubule claim.
2. **Tralse Informationalism is information-theoretic.** The framework operates on i-Cells and i-Webs (informational structures), with quantum mechanics as one substrate where the Tralse phenomena manifest measurably. It does not claim brains are quantum computers.
3. **The QC pillar is hardware-anchored.** Pass-43 made qc25 the first IBMQ HW result in the corpus; Pass-45/46 added qc26. These are reproducible by anyone with IBMQ credits.

The corpus is *more conservative* than the "quantum mind" criticism assumes. The QC work demonstrates that genuine quantum non-locality exists *somewhere* in measurable form, which is enough to motivate the Tralse-substrate framing without claiming brains are quantum computers.

**Falsifier.** If the IBMQ results fail to replicate on independent hardware (e.g., IonQ, Rigetti, Quantinuum) at comparable σ-violations, the hardware-anchored claim weakens. Replication target: ≥10σ Bell-violation on at least two distinct hardware backends. (qc26's 71σ is large enough to make replication failure structurally informative.)

## Objection 2.3 — "MR Truth-Labels are subjective. Anyone can label anything however they want."

**Strongest form.** Categorical taxonomies require demonstrated inter-rater reliability or they're just opinion-aggregation.

**Response.** T45-4 (Pass-47, §7.7.82-83) measured Fleiss κ=0.906 inter-rater agreement on MR Truth-Label assignments. For context:

- κ ≥ 0.81 is the standard threshold for "almost perfect" agreement (Landis & Koch 1977).
- κ = 0.906 places MR Truth-Labels among the **highest categorical-taxonomic agreement coefficients in cognitive-science measurement**.
- Comparable categorical instruments: DSM-5 diagnostic agreement typically κ ∈ [0.4, 0.7]; standard psychometric Likert categorization κ ∈ [0.5, 0.8].

MR labels are *more* inter-rater-reliable than most published diagnostic instruments in clinical psychiatry.

**Falsifier.** If an independent replication with new raters and new stimulus materials produces κ < 0.7, the strong-reliability claim is retracted to "useful but not exceptional." This is a live Pass-56 action item (§B in §7.7.106 standing items).

## Objection 2.4 — "Your sample sizes are too small / your effects are too good to be true."

**Strongest form.** Single-subject N=1 effects, especially when they support the experimenter's theoretical commitments, are the classic pseudoscience signature.

**Response — structural acknowledgment + path forward.** The objection has genuine force in three places:

1. **Mendi STIM2 (Pass-43) is single-subject** within-subjects pre/post. The t=−4.13 p≪0.001 effect is *real for this subject* but does not generalize to a population claim without N>1 replication. The corpus has been explicit about this (§7.7.41-80 collapse). Pass-56 should plan an N≥10 Mendi replication.
2. **Pass-19/21 R-A r20 prospective AUC 0.7318 z=+124.49 is on archival series**, not random sampling. The prospective-vs-retrospective distinction was honored, but selection bias on the archive remains an open question.
3. **qc25/qc26** are reproducible on demand and not single-shot in the relevant sense — each GHZ-5 result is itself averaged over thousands of shots — but the *initial single-experimenter* run is acknowledged as needing independent replication.

**The corpus does not claim these single-subject / single-experimenter results suffice for population-level claims.** What is claimed is: the *pattern* of multi-method convergence (HW QC + categorical κ + within-subjects fNIRS + formal Lean4 + 30+ pre-reg falsifiers across different methods) is unlikely to be all-pseudoscience-by-coincidence.

**Falsifier.** Population-replication failures across two or more domains would substantively weaken the multi-method-convergence argument. Target: Mendi N≥10 replication, MR-label κ replication, and IBMQ replication on a different backend.
