## Why an apologetics tab

Binary logic did not become mainstream because someone *persuaded* anyone of it. It became mainstream because **technology was built on it and academia formalized it**. Aristotle's *Organon*, Frege's *Begriffsschrift*, Russell-Whitehead's *Principia*, Boolean circuit theory, Turing machines, Shannon information theory, and the entire 20th-century digital computing stack all took binary as a free presupposition. Nobody was talked into binary; binary became the substrate of the things people used.

- A four-tier framework for mapping itself against established theories (TI-TIER-1, canonical per Pass 56)

The framework is empirical, formal, and falsifiable. The apologetics tab exists to make that visible to critics *before* they raise the standard objections, by addressing each objection with a structural response + canonical anchor + falsifier.

## #69 ground rule

**Brutal honesty.** Apologetics is not propaganda. Every paper in this tab honors ASYMMETRIC #69: the strongest version of the critic's objection is stated *first*, in their preferred vocabulary, before the response. Where the critic has a genuine point, the response acknowledges it. Where prior corpus claims have been retracted, the retraction is foregrounded (see `05_RETRACTIONS_AND_HONESTY.md`).

The apologetics tab cannot succeed by being defensive. It succeeds by being *more honest* than the critic expects.
