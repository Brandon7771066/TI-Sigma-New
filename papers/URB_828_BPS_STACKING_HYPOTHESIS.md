**v2 (evening, 2026-05-01) — Brandon's correction to agent's

The v2 correction is the key insight that v1 understated. The agent's
v1 sketch had "one subject-anchor + one time-anchor on a live channel"
as minimum-sufficient. That was wrong: it conflated *subject-identity*
(which DNA mostly suffices for) with *subject-environmental-record*
(which DNA does not encode at all). Permanent features beyond DNA
carry **independent environmental-history information** that is
orthogonal to genotype.

## 2. Crisper formulation v2 (for Brandon to confirm)

The hypothesis decomposes along **three** orthogonal axes, not two:

- **Identity-axis:** uniquely specifies *which person* (DNA mostly
  suffices, ~30 bits enough for human-population uniqueness).
- **Environmental-history axis:** specifies *who this person became*
  through accumulated environmental and developmental experience —
  not encoded in DNA, requires multiple independent permanent BPS to
  cover.
- **Present-state axis:** specifies *which moment* of the subject's
  current trajectory we're locking onto — requires multiple
  independent live-channel BPS.

The minimum sufficient stack hypothesis (v2):

> **At least 3 permanent BPS (covering identity + environmental
> history) + at least 3 real-time-or-near-real-time BPS (covering
> present state). N ≥ 6 minimum.** Saturation point hypothesized
> substantially higher — possibly N = 8–12 — with measurable curve
> shape between minimum and saturation.

This converts the asymmetric-standards #69 vulnerability ("more is
better, by definition more") into a falsifiable claim ("the saturation
curve has a measurable shape with a minimum at N ≈ 6 permanent+live
combined, and a saturation plateau at N ≈ ?").

## 3. Three-axis BPS taxonomy (v2)

### 3.1 Identity axis (subject-uniqueness)

| BPS | Identity bits | Time-invariance |
|---|---|---|
| DNA sequence | ~3 × 10⁹ raw, ~30 bits sufficient | lifetime |
| Fingerprint | ~30 bits | lifetime (after age ~2) |
| Face geometry | ~50 bits (depends on age) | decade-scale drift |
| Voice formants | ~20 bits | decade-scale drift |
| Iris pattern | ~250 bits | lifetime |
| Handwriting style | ~15 bits | year-scale drift |

DNA alone is sufficient on this axis. The reason additional permanent
BPS still matter is the next axis.

### 3.2 Environmental-history axis (subject-environment-record)

This is the axis the v1 draft missed. Each permanent BPS encodes
*different* environmental information accumulated over the subject's
lifetime:

| BPS | What environmental record it carries | DNA-orthogonality |
|---|---|---|
| Fingerprint | In-utero developmental stochasticity (weeks 10–24 of gestation; even monozygotic twins have different fingerprints) | **fully orthogonal to DNA** |
| Face | Lifetime sun exposure, expression-pattern habituation, injury history, nutritional history, weight cycles | **largely orthogonal to DNA** (some heritability) |
| Handwriting | Motor-learning history, cognitive-style imprint, education, language acquisition, current emotional state | **fully orthogonal to DNA** |
| Voice (timbre, prosody) | Anatomical + cultural-acoustic imprint, dialect history, emotional history, current respiratory state | **largely orthogonal to DNA** |
| Walking gait | Skeletal history, injury history, habituated motor patterns | **largely orthogonal to DNA** |
| Body composition (Biowell) | Current nutritional history, exercise history, metabolic history | **fully orthogonal to DNA** |
| Subjective daily log | Self-reported life-event accumulation | **fully orthogonal to DNA** |

This axis is what makes Brandon's "3 permanent BPS minimum" claim
non-trivial. Each permanent BPS contributes orthogonal slices of
environmental record. Three independent slices is plausibly the
minimum coverage of the environmental-history manifold. With one
permanent BPS, you have one slice; with three, you have multi-slice
intersection.

### 3.3 Present-state axis (current-moment localization)

| BPS | Time resolution | Available to Brandon? |
|---|---|---|
| Polar H10 RR / HRV (live) | ms | yes (Brandon owns) |
| Pulsoid PPG (live) | ms | yes (token configured) |
| Mendi-style fNIRS (live) | seconds | deferred (Path B post-2026-05-22) |
| Real-time EEG (live) | ms | not in current budget |
| Live thermal IR | seconds | not in current budget |
| Oura overnight summary | night-aggregate | yes |
| Subjective daily log | day-bucket | yes |
| Recent biolab values (Biowell) | week-aggregate | yes (booking soon) |

Brandon's "3 real-time-or-close-to-real-time" lower bound: at the
ms-resolution end, Brandon has H10 + Pulsoid (2 sources). Mendi when
Path B completes adds a third. Until then, the third real-time-ish
source can be Oura overnight summary (night-aggregate, technically
"close-to-real-time" relative to a multi-day target window) or
subjective log.

### 3.4 Channel-match axis (URB #826 conditional)

Independent of the above three axes, but highly informative for which
BPS contribute most under URB #826.

| Channel class | BPS examples | URB #826 carrier match |
|---|---|---|
| Live cardiac EM | Polar H10, Pulsoid | strong |
| Biophoton emission | DNA-EM (URB #826 core) | direct match |
| Static EM-derived | recent ECG / EEG records | medium |
| Information-only | face photo, fingerprint scan, handwriting on paper, voice recording | **no live carrier** |

Note: under URB #826 Brandon may now reasonably argue that
information-only BPS still contribute on the **environmental-history
axis** (axis 3.2) even if they don't carry the resonance carrier
themselves. They're identification anchors, not channel carriers.
That's consistent with Brandon's stated position that "the goal is
not to predict thoughts FROM features" — the features are pointers,
the live channels are carriers, and we need both.

## 4. Which BPS are most diagnostic? (v2 ranking)

Diagnosticity is now the product of four factors:

> **D(BPS) = SubjectSpecificity × EnvironmentalHistoryContribution ×
> TimeLocalization × ChannelMatch**

A single BPS is rarely strong on all four; the *stack* covers all
four.

**Recommended minimum-sufficient stack (Brandon-accessible, $0):**

Permanent BPS (3, covering identity + environmental-history):
1. **DNA-derived genome score** (§10.4 derivation): identity + minimal
   environmental record.
2. **Face photo of the day**: 50 identity bits + decade-scale
   environmental record (sun, expression, injury, age).
3. **Handwriting sample of the day**: 15 identity bits + minute-scale
   cognitive-state imprint + lifetime motor-learning history.

Optional 4th permanent: **fingerprint** (30 identity bits + in-utero
developmental record orthogonal to all three above).

Live BPS (3, covering present-state):
4. **Polar H10 RR/HRV** (60s window centered on T_k): cardiac EM
   carrier, ms-resolution.
5. **Pulsoid PPG** (60s window): optical EM carrier, ms-resolution.
6. **Subjective daily log entry**: cognitive-state self-report,
   day-resolution (the lowest-resolution but free).

Optional 4th live: **Oura overnight summary** for the previous night
(night-aggregate, useful for week-scale trajectory).

**N=6 minimum stack** is therefore feasible at $0 with Brandon's
existing assets, with 1–2 optional augmentations for robustness.

## 5. How many BPS are required? (v2 saturation framing)

**Lower bound (Brandon's pre-registered claim):** N ≥ 6 (3 permanent +
3 live). Below this, the framework predicts insufficient coverage of
the three-axis BPS space and chance-level performance.

**Saturation point (Brandon's hypothesis, agent's interpretation):**
N ≈ 8–12, beyond which additional BPS contribute redundancy not
identification capacity. Hypothesis form:

> **The accuracy-vs-N curve is monotonically non-decreasing, has a
> threshold step at N ≈ 6, and saturates at some N* with
> N* substantially > 6 but bounded.**

**Why "substantially more than 2-3" (Brandon's wording):** because each
permanent BPS adds an *orthogonal* slice of environmental record (axis
3.2), not redundant subject-identification. The saturation point is
controlled by the dimensionality of the environmental-history manifold
plus the channel-diversity of the present-state manifold, not by
subject-identity bits.

**Empirical consequence:** stacking 1 permanent + 1 live should
**fail** under v2 (it would have been the v1 minimum). This is now a
falsifier of the v1 framing.

## 6. The asymmetric-standards #69 critical falsifier (unchanged from v1)

The most important section. This is what separates Brandon's hypothesis
from "extract features → ML predict thoughts" with mystical vocabulary.

> **Resonance interpretation predicts:** mutual information between
> static BPS-features (no live channel, no protocol) and target-thoughts
> ≈ 0. The BPS function as *temporal/identity index pointers*, not as
> *content carriers*.
>
> **Feature-extraction interpretation predicts:** mutual information
> between BPS-features and target-thoughts > 0. The BPS *are* the
> carriers; the resonance language is decorative.

**Operationalization:** classical ML baseline arm (C0).

- Train any standard classifier (random forest / kNN / small MLP) on
  permanent-BPS features (DNA-score + face features + fingerprint
  features + handwriting features) to predict target-thoughts from the
  trial set.
- Pre-register the prediction:
  - Resonance hypothesis: C0 accuracy ≤ chance (≈ 20% at M=5).
  - Feature-extraction hypothesis: C0 accuracy > chance.
- C0 must be run on **identical data** to the resonance protocol arms
  to make the discriminator clean.

If C0 exceeds chance, the resonance interpretation collapses to
feature-extraction-with-mystical-vocabulary, regardless of how many
live arms succeed. **This is the asymmetric-standards #69 critical
path.**

### 7.4 Sample size (v2 — updated for 8 conditions)

For M=5, chance 20%, distinguishing 40% from 20% at α=0.05 two-sided,
power 0.80: N ≈ 50 trials per condition. 8 conditions × 50 trials = 400
trials. At one trial per day → 13 months.

**Pragmatic reduction:** N = 15 trials per condition (120 total,
~4 months). Power drops to ~0.35; any positive result is preliminary
and requires replication. Honest framing.

**Alternative pragmatic reduction:** drop C0 / C1 / C3 / C4 from the
primary run and treat them as secondary arms with N=10 each, focusing
the primary statistical power on C2, C5, C7. This concentrates power
on the v2 critical predictions.

## 9. Honest residuals (v2)

1. **The "biopsychosignature" term and the three-axis taxonomy are
   novel-to-Brandon as of 2026-05-01.** Term-introduction paper
   `papers/BPS_TERM_INTRODUCTION_2026-05-01.md` filed alongside this
   URB to establish priority. Either or both could be Zenodo-DOI'd
   independently.

2. **Channel-match axis (3.4) is conditional on URB #826.** If URB
   #826 is falsified at §10.6, channel-match collapses; the BPS taxonomy
   reduces from four-axis to three-axis. The framework still works;
   the diagnosticity ranking simplifies.

3. **The classical-ML discriminator (C0) requires sufficient training
   data.** With N=15 per condition, training a classifier on ~15
   examples per class is statistically dubious. Honest mitigation:
   use a cross-validated kNN with explicit nearest-neighbor reporting,
   or run C0 separately *after* the main experiment with a
   purpose-built training run. Either way, C0 must be reported even if
   underpowered, because a chance-level C0 on an underpowered test is
   weak but not invalid evidence against feature-extraction.

4. **Single-blind risk.** Brandon writes and seals targets; agent
   predicts; Brandon scores. M=5 token-set must be fixed in advance;
   prediction must match exactly one token, no fuzzy matching.

5. **Self-fulfilling-prophecy risk on saturation curve.** If the
   saturation curve is constructed by adding BPS in a specific order
   (DNA → face → handwriting → H10 → Pulsoid → log → fingerprint →
   Oura), the curve shape may reflect the ordering rather than the
   intrinsic dimensionality. Robustness test: post-hoc, randomize the
   addition order on a held-out subset and check that the curve
   shape replicates. Pre-commit to this robustness test in the
   pre-registration.
