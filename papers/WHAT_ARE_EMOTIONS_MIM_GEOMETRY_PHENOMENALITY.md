## Abstract

This paper asks the most fundamental question in affective science: *What ARE emotions, exactly?* We synthesize six major theoretical traditions—the Symmetry Theory of Valence (STV), Lisa Feldman Barrett's Theory of Constructed Emotion (TCE), Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle (FEP), Tozzi and Meijer's topological/geometric brain models, Jaak Panksepp's Affective Neuroscience, and Antonio Damasio's Somatic Marker Hypothesis—into a unified account through the lens of the Mycelial Information Model (MIM). We propose that emotions are **geometrically constrained information shells** where outer geometry (the "shape" of the emotional state) constrains and organizes inner data (the content of experience), while phenomenality sits at the hierarchical apex but is processed horizontally through metacognitive and cognitive channels. This MIM-Geometric Theory of Emotion (MGTE) resolves the apparent contradictions between constructionist, nativist, and formalist approaches by showing they describe different levels of the same hierarchical structure.

## 1. The Problem: Nobody Agrees What Emotions Are {#1-the-problem}

After over a century of scientific study, there is no consensus on what emotions fundamentally are. The field is fractured into camps that often talk past each other:

### 1.1 The Major Fault Lines

| Debate | Position A | Position B |
|--------|-----------|-----------|
| **Nature** | Innate biological programs (Ekman, Panksepp) | Constructed psychological episodes (Barrett) |
| **Location** | Subcortical circuits | Distributed whole-brain + body |
| **Universality** | Cross-species, cross-cultural invariants | Culturally and linguistically shaped |
| **Number** | Finite set (6-7 basic emotions) | Infinite variety, no natural kinds |
| **Substrate** | Neural circuits | Predictions + body states |
| **Measurement** | Objective physiological signatures | No reliable biomarkers |
| **Function** | Survival responses | Allostatic regulation |

### 1.2 Why the Disagreement Persists

The disagreement persists because each tradition is describing a *different aspect* of emotion at a *different level of analysis*:

- **Panksepp** describes the *subcortical infrastructure* — the ancient circuitry
- **Damasio** describes the *body-brain interface* — how body states become feelings
- **Barrett** describes the *constructive process* — how the brain assembles emotional episodes
- **Friston** describes the *computational principle* — prediction error minimization
- **QRI/STV** describes the *geometric signature* — the symmetry structure of valence
- **Tozzi-Meijer** describes the *topological space* — the manifold on which it all unfolds

They are all partially right. But none captures the whole picture. The MIM provides the integrating framework.

### 1.3 What We Need

We need a theory that explains:
1. Why emotions *feel* like something (phenomenality)
2. Why some feelings are pleasant and others painful (valence)
3. Why emotions seem both universal and culturally variable
4. How body states relate to conscious experience
5. What the mathematical structure of emotional space looks like
6. How emotions relate to cognition and metacognition

## 2. Symmetry Theory of Valence — Emotion as Geometric Harmony {#2-symmetry-theory-of-valence}

### 2.1 Core Claims (QRI / Mike Johnson & Andrés Gómez Emilsson)

The Symmetry Theory of Valence (STV) makes a bold, precise claim:

> **Valence (pleasantness/unpleasantness) corresponds to the mathematical symmetry in the informational structure of consciousness.**

Key principles:
- Every conscious experience has a mathematical object isomorphic to it (**Qualia Formalism**)
- High symmetry in brain states → positive valence (pleasure, bliss)
- Dissonance/asymmetry → negative valence (pain, suffering)
- This is not a heuristic but a **law of nature** applying to all conscious systems

### 2.2 The Harmony Mechanism

STV uses the brain's connectome-specific harmonic waves (CSHW):
- Brain activity decomposed into harmonic modes (like Fourier analysis)
- **Consonance** between harmonics = symmetry = positive valence
- **Dissonance** between harmonics = asymmetry = negative valence
- Peak positive valence (e.g., 5-MeO-DMT, deep jhana) has near-zero information content — maximum symmetry, minimum complexity
- Pure noise has zero valence — no structure at all

### 2.3 What STV Gets Right

- Provides a **formal, mathematical criterion** for valence
- Explains why certain drugs, meditation states, and music feel good (symmetry)
- Makes testable predictions about brain imaging signatures
- Connects emotional experience to information theory and physics

### 2.4 What STV Misses

- Focuses almost exclusively on **valence** (pleasant/unpleasant dimension)
- Says little about **categorical emotion** (why fear feels different from sadness even at similar valence levels)
- Treats the *outer shape* of experience but not the *inner content*
- No account of how emotions are *constructed* or *learned*
- The claim that maximum symmetry = maximum pleasure is suspiciously convenient — it may confuse a correlate with a cause

### 2.5 MIM Interpretation

In MIM terms, STV describes the **outer geometric shell** of emotion — the symmetry properties of the constraining manifold. It captures the *shape* of the container but not the *data* inside it. This is precisely why it handles valence well (a geometric property) but struggles with categorical emotion (a content property).

## 3. Constructed Emotion — Emotion as Predictive Categorization {#3-constructed-emotion}

### 3.1 Core Claims (Lisa Feldman Barrett, 2017-2025)

The Theory of Constructed Emotion (TCE) proposes:

> **Emotions are not triggered reactions but actively constructed predictions. The brain assembles emotional episodes from core affect, interoception, conceptual knowledge, and context.**

Key principles (updated from Barrett et al. 2025, *Perspectives on Psychological Science*):
- **No biological fingerprints**: No fixed facial expressions, physiological patterns, or brain locations unique to any emotion
- **Population thinking**: Instances of "fear" vary enormously in physiology and neural pattern — variation is the norm, not noise
- **Relational realism**: Emotional meaning is relational, not intrinsic — a racing heart has no inherent emotional meaning
- **Active inference**: The brain predicts interoceptive and exteroceptive signals, and emotions are what those predictions *feel like*
- **Allostasis**: Emotions serve body-budgeting — predicting and preparing metabolic resources

### 3.2 The Construction Process

```
Sensory Input (ambiguous) 
    ↓
Core Affect (valence + arousal — always present)
    ↓
Conceptual Categorization (learned emotion categories applied)
    ↓
Emotional Episode (anger, joy, fear, etc. — constructed)
    ↓
Action Tendencies (contextually appropriate responses)
```

### 3.3 What TCE Gets Right

- Explains **cultural variation** in emotional experience
- Explains why **emotion recognition** studies fail to replicate across cultures
- Correctly identifies that the brain is fundamentally **predictive**
- The 2025 "holism over reductionism" stance avoids false mind-body dualisms
- Emotion granularity (better vocabulary = better emotional regulation) is empirically supported

### 3.4 What TCE Misses

- **Dismisses subcortical contributions too readily** — Panksepp's stimulation data shows genuine, unconditioned emotional responses in rats without cortex
- **Can't explain animal emotions** satisfactorily — dogs clearly experience joy/fear without human language
- **Core affect is undertheorized** — where does valence + arousal come from? TCE assumes it but doesn't explain it
- **No mathematical formalism** — compared to STV or FEP, TCE is descriptive rather than formally specified
- **Overcorrects** against basic emotions — the truth may involve both innate substrates AND cultural construction

### 3.5 MIM Interpretation

In MIM terms, TCE describes the **inner data** of emotion — the conceptual, contextual, and linguistic content that fills the geometric shell. It captures how the *content* of emotional experience is assembled but ignores the *geometric constraints* that shape what constructions are possible. Barrett's "core affect" is actually the outer shell she can't explain — the geometric substrate on which construction occurs.

## 4. Free Energy Principle — Emotion as Interoceptive Inference {#4-free-energy-principle}

### 4.1 Core Claims (Karl Friston)

> **Emotions are interoceptive predictions — the brain's best guess about the causes of bodily sensations, optimized to minimize surprise (variational free energy).**

Key principles:
- The brain maintains a **generative model** of the body's internal states
- Emotions arise when **interoceptive prediction errors** signal discrepancy between expected and actual body states
- **Active inference**: The brain either updates its predictions (perceptual inference) or acts on the body to make predictions come true (active inference)
- **Precision weighting**: Attention modulates how much weight is given to prediction errors vs. prior beliefs
- Emotional regulation = adjusting the precision of interoceptive predictions

### 4.2 The FEP Emotion Cycle

```
Prior belief about body state (prediction)
    ↓
Actual interoceptive signal (sensation)
    ↓
Prediction Error (surprise)
    ↓
   ┌─────────────────────┐
   │  Minimize Free Energy │
   ├─────────────────────┤
   │ Option 1: Update belief (perceptual inference)  │
   │ Option 2: Change body state (active inference)   │
   │ Option 3: Reduce precision (ignore error)        │
   └─────────────────────┘
    ↓
Emotional Experience (the feeling of this whole process)
```

### 4.3 2025 Update: Inner Screens and Override

Fields, Albarracin, Friston et al. (2025, *Neuroscience of Consciousness*):
- **Inner screens**: Markov blankets for executive/metacognitive systems that mediate conscious experience
- **Homeostatic override**: Critical interoceptive signals (hunger, pain, acute stress) can bypass executive attenuation — the body overrides the mind
- **Imaginative experience**: FEP constrains how we imagine, plan, and simulate emotional scenarios

### 4.4 What FEP Gets Right

- **Mathematically rigorous** — provides equations, not just descriptions
- **Unifies** emotion, perception, action, and learning under one principle
- **Explains emotional regulation** as precision adjustment
- **Connects to Barrett's TCE** — both emphasize prediction and active inference
- **Explains anxiety disorders** as systematically inflated interoceptive precision (making body signals too "loud")

### 4.5 What FEP Misses

- **Too abstract** — almost anything can be described as minimizing free energy
- **Valence is underspecified** — why does some prediction error feel bad while other feels exciting?
- **The binding problem** — whose free energy? The formalism doesn't address the unity of consciousness
- **Phenomenality is assumed**, not explained — Friston never addresses *why* there is something it is like to minimize free energy

### 4.6 MIM Interpretation

FEP describes the **computational dynamics** of the MIM — the process by which outer geometry and inner data interact. Free energy minimization is the *mechanism* by which the geometric shell constrains information flow. But FEP doesn't explain the shell itself (that's STV's contribution) or the phenomenal character of the process (that requires MIM's hierarchical account).

## 5. Tozzi-Meijer Topological Brain — Emotion as Hypersphere Dynamics {#5-tozzi-meijer}

### 5.1 Arturo Tozzi: Brain Activity on a Hypersphere

Tozzi's framework (2016-2025) proposes:

> **Brain functions are embedded in an imperceptible fourth spatial dimension. Neural activity unfolds on a hypersphere, and cognitive/emotional states correspond to different regions and trajectories on this surface.**

Key concepts:
- **Borsuk-Ulam Theorem**: Every continuous function from an n-sphere to n-dimensional Euclidean space maps at least one pair of antipodal points to the same point — applied to brain: simultaneous activation of "opposite" brain regions indicates higher-dimensional structure
- **Takens' embedding theorem**: EEG dynamics can be reconstructed in higher-dimensional phase spaces, revealing different trajectories for frontal, parietal, and occipital regions
- **Poincaré duality**: Micro- and macro-histological brain structures are topologically linked

### 5.2 Tozzi's 2025 Emotion Work

**"The Geometry of Empathy"** (El Fettahi & Tozzi, 2025):
- Empathy modeled as geometrically constrained inference in multidimensional perceptual space
- **Topological deformations** = changes in moral valuation and social inclusion/exclusion
- **Physiological states** (stress, threat, disgust) = local warping functions that amplify or suppress empathy
- **Ideological priors** = curvatures in the perceptual space

**"Feeling the Heat"** (Deli, Schoeller, Safron, Tozzi et al., 2025):
- Thermodynamic principles applied to emotion
- Emotions as changes in the thermodynamic state of neural systems
- Links to entropy, temperature, and free energy

### 5.3 Dirk Meijer: Toroidal Consciousness Field

Meijer's framework proposes:

> **Consciousness operates through a toroidal (donut-shaped) field that surrounds and interacts with the brain via quantum wave resonance. This 4D structure acts as a "mental workspace" where information integration occurs at an event horizon.**

Key concepts:
- **4D Torus**: Consciousness field has toroidal geometry
- **Event horizon**: The boundary where 4D→3D information flux generates conscious experience
- **Scale invariance**: Consciousness structure is fractal, similar at all scales from neuron to cosmos
- **Quantum wave resonance**: Information exchange between brain and consciousness field

### 5.4 What Tozzi-Meijer Gets Right

- **Takes geometry seriously** — emotion isn't just a label, it has *shape*
- **Higher-dimensional framework** accounts for the richness and complexity of emotional experience
- **Topological methods** are mathematically rigorous and testable (EEG phase-space analysis)
- **The 2025 empathy paper** brilliantly models social emotions as manifold deformations

### 5.5 What Tozzi-Meijer Misses

- **Meijer's 4D torus** remains speculative — no direct experimental test
- **The connection between topology and phenomenality** is unclear — why does a manifold *feel* like something?
- **Individual differences** in emotional experience aren't well accounted for
- **The relationship between geometric structure and content** needs elaboration

### 5.6 MIM Interpretation

Tozzi-Meijer provides the **mathematical substrate** for the MIM's outer shell. The hypersphere/torus is the *geometry* that constrains information. Their 2025 work on empathy geometry directly supports the MIM claim that different emotional states correspond to different geometric configurations of the constraining manifold. However, they don't distinguish clearly between the outer geometric constraint and the inner data it shapes — this is where MIM adds its crucial contribution.

## 6. Panksepp's Affective Neuroscience — Emotion as Subcortical Command {#6-panksepp}

### 6.1 Core Claims (Jaak Panksepp, 1998-2017)

> **Basic emotions arise from seven evolutionarily conserved subcortical command systems, each with distinct neuroanatomy, pharmacology, and behavioral outputs. These circuits are shared across all mammals and are the foundation of all emotional experience.**

The seven systems:

| System | Valence | Key Structure | Neurotransmitter | Behavior |
|--------|---------|---------------|-----------------|----------|
| SEEKING | + | VTA | Dopamine | Exploration, curiosity, anticipation |
| CARE | + | BNST, VTA | Oxytocin, opioids | Nurturing, bonding |
| PLAY | + | Thalamus, PAG | Endocannabinoids | Social joy, rough-and-tumble |
| LUST | + | Hypothalamus | Testosterone, estrogen | Sexual desire, mating |
| FEAR | − | Amygdala, PAG | Glutamate, CRF | Freezing, flight, avoidance |
| RAGE | − | Medial amygdala, hypothalamus | Substance P, acetylcholine | Attack, frustration |
| PANIC/GRIEF | − | PAG, BNST | Opioid withdrawal | Separation distress, crying |

### 6.2 Critical Evidence: Electrical Stimulation

Panksepp's most powerful evidence came from **deep brain stimulation**:
- Stimulating SEEKING circuits → animals self-stimulate endlessly (pleasure/anticipation)
- Stimulating FEAR circuits → animals flee and show distress
- Stimulating PANIC circuits → animals cry and seek contact
- **Decorticate animals** (no neocortex) still show full emotional behavior patterns
- **Rat laughter**: 50-kHz ultrasonic chirps during play/tickling — evidence of positive affect without cortical mediation

### 6.3 What Panksepp Gets Right

- **Evolutionary depth** — emotions didn't appear with human language
- **Subcortical evidence** is robust — you can literally turn emotions on and off with electrical stimulation
- **Cross-species continuity** is well-documented
- **The seven systems** have distinct pharmacological profiles — this matters for drug development
- **SEEKING** as the master motivational system is a profound insight

### 6.4 What Panksepp Misses

- **Seven is probably not the right number** — it's suspiciously clean, and mixed/complex emotions aren't well handled
- **Cortical contribution** is underweighted — while subcortical circuits are necessary, cortical modulation shapes the *quality* of emotional experience enormously
- **Cultural variation** isn't well explained — if emotions are hardwired, why do they vary across cultures?
- **The phenomenal character** of each emotion system is described but not explained — why does SEEKING feel different from CARE?

### 6.5 MIM Interpretation

Panksepp's seven systems are the **default geometric templates** — the innate set of outer-shell geometries that evolution built into mammalian brains. They define the *possible space* of emotional geometries. Barrett is right that specific emotional episodes are constructed, but they are constructed *within* the geometric constraints that Panksepp's circuits provide. The seven systems are not seven emotions — they are seven **geometric attractor basins** in emotional space.

## 7. Damasio's Somatic Markers — Emotion as Body Map {#7-damasio}

### 7.1 Core Claims (Antonio Damasio, 1994-2021)

> **Emotions are body-state changes (facial expressions, heart rate, posture, visceral activity) triggered by stimuli and mapped in the brain. Feelings are the conscious experience of these body maps. The "protoself" — the brain's continuous, unconscious neural mapping of the body — is the substrate from which consciousness emerges.**

Key distinctions:
- **Emotion** = public, observable, bodily (the racing heart, the clenched fist)
- **Feeling** = private, subjective, mental (the awareness of the racing heart)
- **Somatic marker** = an association between a body state and a past outcome, used to bias future decisions

### 7.2 The Hierarchy of Self

```
Autobiographical Self (narrative identity, memory, future planning)
        ↑
Core Self (momentary awareness of an object affecting the protoself)
        ↑
Protoself (unconscious body maps — visceral, musculoskeletal, chemical)
        ↑
Body (actual physiological states)
```

### 7.3 The Two Loops

1. **Body Loop**: Actual physiological changes occur → signals travel from body to brain → mapped in somatosensory/insular cortex → become feelings
2. **As-If Loop**: Brain simulates body states without actual bodily changes → same feeling generated computationally → faster, used for rapid decision-making

### 7.4 What Damasio Gets Right

- **The body is essential** — you can't understand emotion without the body
- **The emotion/feeling distinction** is genuinely useful
- **Iowa Gambling Task** evidence is strong — vmPFC damage impairs emotional decision-making
- **The protoself concept** provides a biological ground for consciousness
- **Feeling & Knowing** (2021) formula: Consciousness = Feelings + Perspective

### 7.5 What Damasio Misses

- **The "as-if" loop** is suspiciously convenient — it's an escape hatch for when the body loop doesn't work
- **Social construction of emotion** is underweighted
- **The binding problem** — how do distributed body maps become unified feeling?
- **Computational precision** is lacking compared to FEP or STV

### 7.6 MIM Interpretation

Damasio describes the **inner data layer** — the body-state content that fills the geometric shell. The protoself is the continuous stream of interoceptive data. The emotion/feeling distinction maps perfectly onto MIM's outer/inner duality:

- **Emotion** (Damasio) = **outer geometric shell** (MIM) — the observable, public, structural aspect
- **Feeling** (Damasio) = **inner data** (MIM) — the private, subjective, content aspect
- **Protoself** = the **interface** between shell and data

> **Emotion is an outer shell with different geometries constraining the information within it. However, emotions also consist of inner data. ### 9.3 Layer 1: Outer Geometric Shell

**What it is**: The mathematical structure (symmetry, topology, curvature) of the informational manifold on which emotional experience unfolds.

**Who describes it**: STV (symmetry), Tozzi-Meijer (topology/hypersphere), Panksepp (attractor basins), Plasma theories (field coherence)

- Determines **valence** (symmetry → positive, asymmetry → negative)

- Is partially innate (Panksepp's circuits) and partially learned (Barrett's categories deform the geometry)
- Changes more slowly than inner data — geometry is structural, not momentary

**Analogy**: The outer shell is like the shape of a musical instrument. A violin and a trumpet have different geometries that constrain what sounds (data) they can produce. You can play many melodies on a violin, but they all sound "violinish" because of the geometric constraints.

### 9.4 Layer 2: Inner Data

**What it is**: The specific informational content filling the geometric shell — body states, conceptual categories, contextual associations, memories, predictions.

**Who describes it**: Barrett (conceptual categories), Damasio (body maps/somatic markers), Friston (interoceptive predictions/prediction errors)

**Properties**:
- Determines **specific emotional content** (this specific fear, this particular joy)
- Is highly variable, context-dependent, and culturally shaped
- Changes rapidly — new data flows through the shell continuously
- Includes both interoceptive data (body states) and exteroceptive data (world states)
- Is what Barrett's "construction" process assembles

**Analogy**: Inner data is like the melody played on the instrument. The same violin (geometry) can play a lullaby or a war march (different data), but the instrument's shape constrains and colors every note.

### 9.5 Layer 3: Phenomenality (The Apex)

**What it is**: The "what it is like" quality — the fact that emotional experience *feels like something* rather than being mere information processing.

**Position**: At the **top** of the hierarchy (most fundamental, most encompassing)

**But processed horizontally**: Despite being hierarchically supreme, phenomenality manifests through two parallel, horizontal processing streams:

1. **Metacognitive stream** (based on outer geometry):
   - Awareness of the *type* or *quality* of the emotional state
   - "I feel something negative" / "This has a fearful quality"
   - Processes the geometric properties: symmetry, curvature, topology
   - Corresponds to what we might call **emotional awareness** or **emotional metacognition**
   - This is the stream that meditation and mindfulness access

2. **Cognitive stream** (based on inner data):
   - Awareness of the *content* or *cause* of the emotional state
   - "I'm afraid of the spider" / "I'm joyful because of the news"
   - Processes the data: body states, conceptual categories, causal attributions
   - Corresponds to what we might call **emotional cognition** or **emotional appraisal**
   - This is the stream that CBT and reappraisal target

### 9.6 Why Horizontal Processing of a Vertical Apex?

This is the key insight that resolves the major puzzles in emotion science:

**Phenomenality is ontologically primary** (at the top) but **epistemically dual** (processed in two horizontal channels). This is because:

1. **You can't directly access phenomenality** — you can only access it through its two manifestations (metacognitive geometry awareness and cognitive data awareness)
2. **The metacognitive and cognitive streams are parallel, not serial** — you simultaneously experience the *quality* (geometry) and the *content* (data) of an emotion
3. **Emotional regulation** works differently on each stream:
   - Meditation changes the **metacognitive stream** (altering relationship to geometry without changing the geometry itself)
   - Cognitive reappraisal changes the **cognitive stream** (reinterpreting the data without changing the geometry)
   - Deep therapeutic change alters the **geometry itself** (changing attractor basins — this is why it's harder and slower)

This explains why:
- **Mindfulness** can change your relationship to pain without reducing pain (metacognitive stream)
- **CBT** can change what you think about a situation without changing the felt quality (cognitive stream)
- **Psychedelics** can change the felt quality without changing your thoughts about it (geometric restructuring)
- **Emotional insight** occurs when both streams align — when you both *feel* the geometry shift and *understand* the data differently

## 10. The Geometry-Content Duality of Emotional Experience {#10-geometry-content-duality}

### 10.1 Formal Statement

For any emotional experience E:

```
E = G(S) ⊗ D(S)
```

Where:
- **G(S)** = the geometric shell function on state space S (outer structure)
- **D(S)** = the data content function on state space S (inner filling)
- **⊗** = the constraining interaction (geometry constrains data; data deforms geometry)

### 10.2 Properties of the Interaction

The ⊗ operator has specific properties:

1. **Asymmetric constraint**: G constrains D more than D constrains G
   - The shape of the instrument constrains the melody more than the melody reshapes the instrument
   - But over time, repeated data patterns *can* deform geometry (neuroplasticity, habit formation)

2. **Non-separability**: You can't fully separate geometry from content in lived experience
   - Every felt quality has informational content
   - Every informational content has some felt quality
   - The separation is analytical, not phenomenal

3. **Multi-scale**: The geometry-content duality operates at multiple scales
   - **Micro**: Single neural oscillation (geometry = waveform shape, content = phase/amplitude)
   - **Meso**: Neural circuit (geometry = circuit topology, content = activation pattern)
   - **Macro**: Whole-brain state (geometry = global connectivity pattern, content = specific thoughts/sensations)
   - **Organism**: Body-brain system (geometry = body schema, content = interoceptive signals)

### 10.3 How Each Theory Maps to the Duality

| Theory | Geometry (Outer Shell) | Data (Inner Content) |
|--------|----------------------|---------------------|
| **STV** | Symmetry of harmonic modes | Information content of the state |
