## Background of the Invention

### The Problem: No Unified Framework for Consciousness-System Coupling

Existing biofeedback and consciousness-interaction technologies address narrow, separate use cases:
- Heart rate variability (HRV) biofeedback targets autonomic regulation but provides no mechanism for influencing external systems
- Remote viewing protocols (e.g., Stargate/SRI) target information extraction but provide no biometric measurement of the practitioner's coherence state during the session
- Animal training and behavioral medicine protocols provide no mechanism for the practitioner's internal state to influence animal physiology beyond gross behavioral cues
- Financial technical analysis tools provide no channel for consciousness-coherence information to inform trading decisions
- No existing technology unifies these applications under a single measurable quantity (LCC) with a single set of threshold conditions

### The Insight: LCC as Universal Coupling Parameter

The Law of Correlational Causation (LCC) states that systems in sufficient internal coherence exhibit non-random correlations with other systems — correlations that cannot be explained by classical causal channels alone. LCC is formally defined as:

```
LCC(t) = C_L(t) × C_C(t) × φ-scaling_factor

where:
  C_L(t) = limbic coherence (emotional integration, measured via HRV spectral analysis)
  C_C(t) = cortical coherence (neural coherence, measured via EEG or fNIRS)
  φ = (1 + √5) / 2 ≈ 1.618 (golden ratio scaling)
```

LCC is bounded to [0, 1] and has six empirically-derived threshold levels:
- LCC_TRALSE = √2 − 1 ≈ 0.414 (minimum signal-to-noise for reliable coupling)
- C_EMERICK = 1/(φ√2) ≈ 0.437 (minimum for consistent psi reception)
- LCC_EMERICK = 1/√2 ≈ 0.707 (minimum for bidirectional influence)
- LCC_HIGH ≈ 0.851 (reliable two-way information transfer)
- LCC_RADIANT = √(e/π) ≈ 0.930 (maximum coherence, all-channel coupling active)

When a practitioner achieves LCC above threshold, the coupling between their internal state and target systems becomes measurable and exploitable for the applications described herein.

### The LCC Virus Principle

A critical application of LCC Entrainment is the **LCC Virus** — a method for extracting comprehensive information about any target i-cell (any complex system with internal state structure: person, company, economy, ecosystem) by resonating the practitioner's LCC state with the target system's characteristic frequencies, then reading out the resonant return signal. Like a sonar system that infers the shape of a submerged object from reflected sound, LCC Virus infers the internal state of a target system from the pattern of resonant coupling that the target system enables. This is described in full in Claim 5.

## Detailed Description of the Invention

### Component 1: LCC Measurement Subsystem

The LCC measurement subsystem acquires physiological signals from the practitioner and computes real-time LCC values. Acceptable signal sources include:

**Cardiac signals:**
- Photoplethysmography (PPG) — fingertip, wrist, or earlobe
- Electrocardiography (ECG/EKG) — single-lead or multi-lead
- Polar H10 or equivalent HRV sensor

**Neural signals:**
- Electroencephalography (EEG) — consumer grade (Muse 2, OpenBCI) or clinical grade
- Functional near-infrared spectroscopy (fNIRS) — Mendi, Kernel Flow, or equivalent
- Multi-channel EEG with alpha/theta coherence extraction

**Computed LCC from cardiac signals alone (minimum viable configuration):**
```
LCC_proxy(t) = SDNN_normalized(t) × HF_power_normalized(t) × φ_scaling
```

**Full LCC from cardiac + neural:**
```
LCC_full(t) = [HRV_LF/HF_coherence × EEG_alpha_coherence]^(1/2) × φ_scaling
```

The subsystem outputs a continuous LCC time series at minimum 4 Hz sample rate, with real-time threshold crossing detection.

### Component 2: Target System Interface

The target system interface varies by application domain:

**Domain A — Human Physiology (Mood Amplification):**
The practitioner's own body is the target. LCC Entrainment induces coherent coupling between limbic and cortical systems via resonant breathing, heart-lock-in protocol, and attention management. Output: measurable improvements in HRV, prefrontal coherence, and subjective affect.

**Domain B — Interpersonal / Animal Systems:**
The practitioner's LCC state couples with a co-present biological system (another person, animal, group). The target system's physiological state is optionally monitored for resonant response. LCC above LCC_TRALSE produces measurable co-regulation in the target system's HRV and autonomic balance. The practitioner's LCC state is the "transmitter"; the target system's physiological state is the "receiver/responder."

**Domain C — Market and Economic Systems:**
The practitioner enters a high-LCC state and queries financial price series or economic indicators for anomalous resonance patterns — price levels, temporal turning points, or correlation structures — that the LCC-elevated practitioner detects above chance. The Grand Stock Algorithm (GSA) provides the analytical framework for integrating LCC-state information with technical and fundamental signals.

**Domain D — Organizational and Network Systems:**
A practitioner or team of practitioners resonates with an organizational target (company, department, network) and extracts information about internal state (morale, stress, hidden conflicts, emerging opportunities) via pattern-reading of available public signals filtered through the LCC-enhanced practitioner's perceptual system.

**Domain E — LCC Virus (Full Information Extraction):**
See Claim 5 — dedicated information extraction mode described below.

### Component 3: Protocol Engine

The protocol engine delivers structured entrainment sequences. Key protocols:

**Protocol 1 — Heart Coherence Baseline (5-15 min)**
- 5-second inhale / 5-second exhale rhythm
- Heart-lock-in: deliberate generation of appreciation/warmth affect
- LCC monitoring: confirm crossing LCC_TRALSE before proceeding
- Eyes open (active) or eyes closed (receptive) variants

**Protocol 2 — Radiant Expansion (add-on, 4-8 min)**
- After Heart Coherence Baseline is achieved
- Attention expands outward from chest center to room, environment, target system
- LCC monitoring: confirm proximity to LCC_EMERICK
- Practitioner orients toward target system without effortful analysis

**Protocol 3 — Query Resonance (3-5 min)**
- Practitioner holds a specific question or target parameter in mind
- Monitors for felt sense of resonance vs. dissonance
- Records all impressions without analysis
- Analysis occurs post-session when LCC returns to baseline

**Protocol 4 — Group Entrainment (2+ practitioners)**
- Multiple practitioners synchronize LCC rhythms (shared breathing protocol)
- Group LCC coherence is estimated via inter-practitioner HRV synchrony
- Group LCC elevation produces √N threshold coupling enhancement (Maharishi Effect — Paper #370)

### Component 4: Output Analytics

The output analytics system processes:
- Practitioner LCC time series during and after session
- Target system signal response (where measurable)
- Session impression records (text, audio, or gesture-recorded)
- Cross-session accuracy tracking for information-extraction applications
- Running calibration of individual practitioner LCC-coupling efficacy

### Component 5: Resonance Memory System

The resonance memory system stores:
- Practitioner LCC baseline and peak profiles
- Target system signatures (characteristic resonance frequencies, response patterns)
- Historical coupling outcomes with accuracy metadata
- Cross-practitioner comparison data for team applications

## Claims

### Claim 1: Core LCC Measurement and Threshold Detection System

A system for measuring the Law of Correlational Causation (LCC) comprising:
- (a) one or more biometric sensors acquiring cardiac and/or neural signals from a practitioner in real time;
- (b) a signal processing module computing LCC from said signals using a formula incorporating at least one physiological coherence measure scaled by the golden ratio φ;
- (c) a threshold detection module identifying when computed LCC crosses one or more of the following thresholds: LCC_TRALSE (≈0.414), C_EMERICK (≈0.437), LCC_EMERICK (≈0.707), LCC_HIGH (≈0.851), LCC_RADIANT (≈0.930);
- (d) a real-time feedback display communicating LCC level and threshold status to the practitioner.

### Claim 2: Mood Amplification via LCC Entrainment

A method for amplifying mood and emotional regulation comprising:
- (a) measuring the practitioner's LCC state via the system of Claim 1;
- (b) guiding the practitioner through a Heart Coherence protocol comprising resonant breathing and deliberate positive affect generation;
- (c) confirming LCC elevation above LCC_TRALSE threshold;
- (d) optionally extending the session through a Radiant Expansion protocol to target LCC_EMERICK or LCC_RADIANT threshold;
- (e) storing pre-session and post-session LCC values for longitudinal mood tracking.

### Claim 3: Bidirectional External System Influence via LCC Coupling

A method for influencing the state of an external complex system comprising:
- (a) the practitioner achieving LCC above LCC_EMERICK (≈0.707) as confirmed by the system of Claim 1;

- (c) the practitioner holding an intention for a specific state change in the target system;
- (d) monitoring the target system for measurable state changes over a specified time window;
- (e) logging LCC level, session duration, and observed target system response for cross-session analysis;
wherein the target system may be a human body, animal, group of persons, ecological system, financial market, or organizational network.

### Claim 4: Animal Behavior Entrainment and Mood Amplifier Training

A method for training non-human animals using LCC entrainment comprising:
- (a) the human trainer achieving LCC above LCC_TRALSE via the system of Claim 1;
- (b) the trainer coupling their LCC state with the animal by maintaining physical proximity and shared attention;
- (c) reinforcing desired animal behaviors during periods of trainer LCC above threshold;
- (d) tracking animal behavioral response as a function of trainer LCC level across training sessions;
- (e) using animal behavioral improvement rate as an indirect measure of LCC coupling efficacy;
wherein the method applies to any domesticated or captive animal species and extends to mood amplifier training protocols designed to optimize animal wellbeing for subsequent human interaction.

### Claim 5: LCC Virus — Information Extraction via Resonant Coupling

A method for extracting information about the internal state of a target complex system (target i-cell) comprising:
- (a) the practitioner achieving LCC above C_EMERICK (≈0.437) as confirmed by the system of Claim 1;
- (b) the practitioner orienting attention toward a target system (which may be a person, organization, financial market, or other complex system) without being given explicit information about the target's current internal state;
- (c) the practitioner entering a Query Resonance protocol, holding a specific parameter of the target's state in mind (e.g., "what is this entity's primary stressor?", "is this market trending?", "what is this organization's dominant emotional climate?");

- (f) cross-session accuracy tracking to calibrate individual practitioner LCC-coupling efficacy for information-extraction applications;

A method for group LCC entrainment comprising:

- (b) the group synchronizing LCC oscillations via shared breathing, music, or attention protocols;

## Abstract

A unified system and method for entraining the practitioner's internal coherence state, quantified by the Law of Correlational Causation (LCC), with any target complex system for purposes including mood amplification, bidirectional system influence, animal behavior entrainment, and non-classical information extraction (LCC Virus). The LCC is computed in real time from cardiac and/or neural biometric signals and compared against six empirically-derived threshold constants. Above-threshold LCC states enable measurable coupling between the practitioner and target systems that cannot be explained by classical causal channels. The invention provides protocols, hardware, and analytics for exploiting this coupling across applications in wellness, clinical psychology, financial markets, animal training, organizational intelligence, and scientific psi research.
