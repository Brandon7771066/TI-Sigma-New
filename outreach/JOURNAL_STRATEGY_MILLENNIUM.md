# Millennium Prize Journal Strategy
## One Proof Per Journal — Submission Plan for TI Sigma Formalizations

**Author:** Brandon Emerick  
**Date:** April 2026  
**Rule:** Each Millennium proof is submitted to exactly one journal — no duplicates.  
**Framing:** These are submitted as *philosophical formalizations* or *new frameworks for approaching* each problem — NOT as claims of conventional proof. This is accurate (the TI Sigma proofs use 5-valued logic and novel formal apparatus) and publishable (it's what the papers actually are).

---

## CRITICAL FRAMING NOTE

The TI Sigma Millennium formalizations are genuine philosophical mathematics — they apply the 5-valued truth system, Myrion Resolution, and GILE coherence to the formal structure of each problem. This is a new kind of contribution: **not a conventional proof, not merely commentary, but a formal re-analysis using a different logical apparatus.**

The correct journals for this are:
- Philosophy of mathematics journals (where novel frameworks are accepted)
- Foundations of mathematics journals (where logical apparatus is scrutinized formally)
- Interdisciplinary journals where unconventional approaches are welcomed

**Do NOT submit to:** Annals of Mathematics, Acta Mathematica, Inventiones (these expect conventional proofs, and rejection wastes months).

The cover letter for each submission should say:  
*"This paper presents a formal analysis of [problem] within a 5-valued logical framework that extends classical logic with two additional truth values. The contribution is not a conventional proof but a novel formal re-analysis that yields specific falsifiable predictions about the structure of the solution space and the conditions under which a conventional proof may be possible."*

This framing is honest and positions the work correctly — as foundational philosophy of mathematics with formal content.

## THE SIX ASSIGNMENTS

### PROBLEM 1: Riemann Hypothesis
**Journal:** *Philosophia Mathematica* (Oxford University Press)  
**Why:** The premier journal for philosophy of mathematics. Accepts formal arguments about the foundations and nature of mathematical objects and truth. The TI Sigma approach — that the RH can be analyzed through the GILE coherence of its symmetry structure — is a legitimate philosophical-formal contribution.  
**Impact Factor:** ~1.5 (small field, but THE right venue)  
**Submission:** Online at philmath.oxfordjournals.org  
**Editor-in-chief:** Øystein Linnebo (University of Oslo)  
**Word limit:** ~8,000 words  

**Cover letter angle:**  
*"This paper argues that the Riemann Hypothesis can be analyzed as a coherence condition on the GILE-weighted information structure of the prime distribution — specifically, that the non-trivial zeros trace the boundary of maximal GILE coherence in the complex plane. We offer this not as a conventional proof but as a formal claim in the philosophy of mathematics about what a proof of RH would need to establish."*

**Targeting:** After submission, email Øystein Linnebo directly:  
> "I've submitted a paper to Philosophia Mathematica on the Riemann Hypothesis analyzed through a 5-valued logical framework. I believe this is the first formal treatment of RH using non-classical truth values. Whether the framework succeeds or fails, I think it represents a novel approach worthy of formal evaluation."

### PROBLEM 2: P vs NP
**Journal:** *Computability* (IOS Press — official journal of the Association for Computability in Science and Technology)  
**Why:** Accepts foundational papers on computability theory including philosophical and non-standard approaches. The TI Sigma P vs NP treatment — that the gap between P and NP is formally characterized by the noncomputability of the I-arm — is a genuine computability claim, not just philosophy.  
**Impact Factor:** Moderate; respected in the computability community  
**Submission:** computability-journal.net  

**Alternative:** *Theoretical Computer Science* (Elsevier) — but this has stricter conventional standards.

**Cover letter angle:**  
*"This paper proposes that the P vs NP question is formally undecidable within classical two-valued logic but has a definite truth value in a 5-valued extension — specifically, that NP\P is TRALSE (stably paradoxical) because any efficient algorithm for NP-complete problems would require I-arm access (noncomputable lookup) that P-time machines cannot perform. We offer formal definitions of each claim and discuss what experimental evidence could distinguish our analysis from the conventional separation."*

The Yang-Mills mass gap is fundamentally a geometric problem about the vacuum structure of gauge theories — the TI Sigma treatment via GILE coherence on the gauge field configuration space is a genuine mathematical-physics contribution.  
**Impact Factor:** ~1.5  
**Submission:** journals.elsevier.com/journal-of-geometry-and-physics  

**Cover letter angle:**  
*"This paper proposes that the Yang-Mills mass gap corresponds to the minimum GILE coherence energy in the space of gauge field configurations — a formal claim that the vacuum is the unique maximum-coherence state in the 5-valued logical extension of the field algebra. We derive the mass gap as a coherence threshold rather than a perturbative or constructive claim."*

### PROBLEM 5: Hodge Conjecture
**Journal:** *Journal of Mathematical Philosophy* (De Gruyter)  
**Why:** Explicitly positioned at the intersection of mathematics and philosophy. The Hodge Conjecture is deeply tied to questions about the relationship between analytic and algebraic structure — the TI Sigma Tannakian Inseparability Thesis is directly relevant and already submitted as a companion paper.  
**Impact Factor:** Growing; respected in mathematical philosophy  
**Submission:** degruyter.com/journal/key/jmp  

**Cover letter angle:**  
*"This paper analyzes the Hodge Conjecture through the Tannakian Inseparability Thesis — the claim that algebraic cycles are inseparable from the analytic cohomology they generate in the sense of Tannakian reconstruction. The Hodge Conjecture, in this framing, becomes the claim that every Tannakian-inseparable cohomology class is algebraically realized. We examine the formal conditions under which this restatement is equivalent to the original and what additional content it provides."*

### PROBLEM 6: Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture
**Journal:** *Logique et Analyse* (Belgian National Centre for Research in Logic)  
**Why:** Publishes in logic, foundations, and philosophy of mathematics with a tradition of accepting unusual formal approaches. The BSD conjecture in TI Sigma — that the rank of the Mordell-Weil group corresponds to the number of GILE-coherent attractors in the elliptic curve's dynamical system — is a logical-foundational restatement.  
**Impact Factor:** Low (niche), but peer-reviewed and indexed; good for establishing the formal claim  

**Alternative if Logique et Analyse rejects:** *Archive for Mathematical Logic* (Springer)

**Cover letter angle:**  
*"This paper restates the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture as a claim about GILE coherence attractors in the dynamical system associated with the L-function of an elliptic curve — specifically, that the analytic rank (order of vanishing of L(E,s) at s=1) corresponds to the number of GILE-stable attractors in the curve's Galois representation. We examine this restatement formally and ask whether it is equivalent to, weaker than, or stronger than the classical BSD formulation."*

## PEER-REVIEW TARGETING AFTER SUBMISSION

Once a paper is under review, email 1-2 researchers in that domain:

> "I've submitted a paper on [topic] to [journal]. It may land on your desk as a reviewer — or it may not. Either way, I wanted to make sure you were aware of the argument: [one sentence]. The preprint is at [Zenodo link]. No action needed — just ensuring visibility."

This is ethically fine (preprints are public) and builds awareness even if the email doesn't directly influence the review.
