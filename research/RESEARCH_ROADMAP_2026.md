## Phase 2: Core Verification Studies (Q3-Q4 2026)

### Milestone 2.1: Sleep Stage R-Profile (Prediction 9)
**Timeline**: July-August 2026

**Protocol**:
1. Source 50+ polysomnography recordings
2. Calculate neural complexity (Lempel-Ziv)
3. Estimate R for each sleep stage
4. Statistical validation

**Expected Outcome**: R-profile matches prediction (R decreases through sleep stages)

**Resources**: Public datasets (PhysioNet), collaboration with sleep labs

### Milestone 2.2: Mammalian R-Gradient (Prediction 12)
**Timeline**: September-October 2026

**Protocol**:
1. Collect published animal EEG data (dogs, primates, rodents)
2. Standardize complexity measures
3. Estimate R by species
4. Correlate with encephalization quotient

**Expected Outcome**: R scales with EQ (r > 0.7)

**Resources**: Published datasets, literature synthesis

### Milestone 2.3: Enhanced PSI Analysis (Prediction 2 Extension)
**Timeline**: November-December 2026

**Protocol**:
1. Expand meta-analysis to 200+ studies
2. Calculate study-level LCC estimates
3. Test moderator variables (distance, relationship, training)
4. Bayesian meta-analysis with JASP

**Expected Outcome**: Refined LCC estimate with confidence intervals

**Resources**: Published literature, Dean Radin database

**Resources**: Zoolife.tv, Smithsonian webcams, PyTorch Wildlife

### Milestone 3.2: Cross-Species Consciousness Estimation
**Timeline**: April-June 2027

**Expected Outcome**: UCME predicts cross-species cognitive capabilities

**Resources**: Published animal cognition studies

**Timeline**: May-June 2027

2. Compare to GCP event effects
3. Calculate animal-specific LCC estimates
4. Test non-local correlation hypothesis

**Expected Outcome**: Animals show behavioral synchrony during global events

**Resources**: Archived webcam footage, GCP event registry
