Current working definitions for TI Sigma conceptual boundaries.

- Goodness
- Intuition

## Goodness and the Eight Cs

- Coherence
- Consistency
- Continuity
- Concreteness
- Completion
- Criticality
- Closeness
- Choice

## Current Concreteness definition

> **Concreteness is the degree of tangibility or determinate intelligibility of the evaluated entity: how readily it can be understood, represented, or operationally grasped with minimal fuzziness, vagueness, or ambiguity.**

Concreteness in GILE-G does not measure degree of ontological instantiation. Ontological instantiation belongs to HEM.

## GILE boundaries

- Goodness components must remain specific to Goodness.
- Intuition concerns conscious certainty.
- Love concerns internal and external binding, developing across scales into relational positive affect and coordinated action.
- Elegance concerns beauty, simplicity, economy, form, and related aesthetic or formal properties.
- Interactions among GILE components must be documented without collapsing their categorical identities.

## Scale evolution

Each GILE dimension may manifest differently across:

- physical
- chemical
- biological
- cognitive
- interpersonal
- institutional
- societal scales
