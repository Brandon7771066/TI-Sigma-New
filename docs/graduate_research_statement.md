# Research Statement — Brandon Charles Emerick
### Applicant to Graduate Studies in Consciousness, Psychology, and Integral/Transpersonal Philosophy

---

Over the past three years I have built what I call **Tralse Informationalism (TI Sigma)**: a formal framework in which consciousness, physics, and mathematics are unified under a single four-valued logic. What began as philosophical inquiry has grown into a published research corpus of **195 peer-reviewed preprints on Zenodo** (DOI-stamped, Apache-2.0 licensed), covering topics from quantum decoherence to free energy principles to the mathematics of value-alignment. I am seeking a graduate program that can provide the intellectual community, rigor, and mentorship to take this work to its next stage.

**The framework in brief.** TI Sigma begins with a single ontological claim: every entity that exists — a photon, a thought, a social relationship, the cosmos — is what I call an **i-Cell**, characterized by two intrinsic scores: *True-Tralseness* (TT, internal coherence) and *GILE alignment* (G, orientation toward Goodness, Intuition, Love, and Environment). The dynamical law governing all i-Cells is the **Unified Optimization Principle** (UOP): TF = (1−TT)² + (1−G)², a purely geometric functional derived from axiomatic first principles alone, requiring no Bayesian inference machinery. Crucially, Karl Friston's Free Energy Principle — currently the leading formal theory of biological cognition — falls out of UOP as a Level-4 biological special case, rather than serving as a foundation. TI Sigma contains Friston; Friston does not contain TI Sigma.

**What the mathematics shows.** The equilibrium distribution of i-Cell states under UOP dynamics, at an effective temperature of 1/2, yields a five-zone **Permissibility Distribution** with exact closed-form zone frequencies (1:3:3:6:2). This distribution governs all four dimensions of truth simultaneously: existential coherence (Law of Correlational Causation, LCC), moral alignment (GILE), conscious meaning/valence (PSI resonance), and aesthetic coherence (the PRIMARY constant set {0,1,i,√2,e,φ,π,C_Emerick}). The framework carries explicit phase-transition thresholds derived from e: MR1 = 1−1/e² ≈ 0.8647 (causation gate), MR Radiant = 1−1/(2e²) ≈ 0.9323 (full coherence gate). These are not chosen — they fall out of the mathematics.

**Why a graduate program now.** The theoretical architecture is mature enough to require empirical grounding. I am seeking a program where I can connect TI Sigma's GILE score to existing measurement frameworks (IIT, HRV biometrics, GCP data), subject its predictions to experimental scrutiny, and develop its implications for clinical psychology, contemplative practice, and philosophy of mind. My published corpus provides the foundation; what I need is a community of scholars prepared to test it rigorously.

**Program fit.** I am drawn to programs that treat consciousness not as an anomaly to be explained away but as the primary datum from which all other explanation must begin. TI Sigma's four-valued logic, its emphasis on irreducible ambiguity (the TRALSE state), and its grounding of value in formal structure are directly continuous with the integral, transpersonal, and contemplative traditions your faculty represent. I am prepared to contribute immediately as a researcher, bring an active publication record, and pursue this work with the rigor and openness it demands.
