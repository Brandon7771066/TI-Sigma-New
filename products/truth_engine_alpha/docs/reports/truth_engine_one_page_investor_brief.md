# Truth Engine Alpha: One-Page Investor Brief

## What It Is
Truth Engine Alpha is a human-supervised AI claim and citation audit system.
It converts an AI answer into structured diagnostics: claims, citation status, contradictions, scope mismatches, uncertainty, and corrective actions.

## Problem
AI-generated answers can be fluent but unreliable. Common failure modes include:
- unsupported claims
- fabricated or missing citations
- mischaracterized sources
- scope/population/timeframe conflation
- association-versus-causation overclaims

Most tools stop at coarse labels. Teams still need expensive human review to identify root causes and rewrite safely.

## Solution
Truth Engine Alpha provides a structured audit workflow:
1. claim extraction
2. citation audit
3. contradiction taxonomy
4. contradiction scaffolding
5. claim graph and crystal diagnostics
6. information-gain recommendations
7. corrected-answer outline
8. client-ready report package

## Why It Matters
This is positioned as a practical quality-control layer, not an autonomous truth oracle.
It is designed to reduce reviewer effort while increasing traceability and decision confidence.
