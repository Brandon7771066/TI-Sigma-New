# Client Offer Descriptions

Truth Engine Alpha turns complex public evidence into a structured map of claims,
contradictions, assumptions, evidence quality, unresolved questions, and next actions.

## Offer 1: Claim Audit

- Scope: Up to 20 claims
- Deliverables: contradiction/citation review + structured markdown report
- Price hypothesis: $49-$99

## Offer 2: Evidence Map

- Scope: Up to 10 public sources
- Deliverables: claim table + evidence hierarchy + contradiction map + recommendations
- Price hypothesis: $149-$299

## Offer 3: Deep Research Triage

- Scope: 20-50 public sources
- Deliverables: structured evidence database + mechanism and contradiction analysis + opportunity map
- Price hypothesis: $399-$1,000+
