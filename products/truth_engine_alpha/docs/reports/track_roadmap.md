# Evidence-First Roadmap

## Track 1: AI Hallucination Auditor (highest priority)

Deliverable focus:
- claim extraction
- contradiction and citation mismatch detection
- missing citation table
- corrected answer outline
- confidence calibration summary

## Track 2: Biomedical Contradiction Engine

Deliverable focus:
- contradiction map
- mechanism split
- evidence hierarchy
- unresolved experiment list
- opportunity memo

## Track 3: Dataset QA

Deliverable focus:
- inconsistency candidate ranking
- priority table for human review
- expected information gain by relabeling tranche

## Track 4: Patent Prior-Art Contradictions

Deliverable focus:
- claim conflict map
- assumption differences
- overlap matrix
- strongest prior-art evidence shortlist

## Track 5: Research Due Diligence

Deliverable focus:
- scientific unknowns table
- contradiction clusters
- critical experiment priority list
- risk memo

## Release strategy

- Alpha: transparent, explainable reports
- Beta: optional experimental MR operator and benchmark comparisons
