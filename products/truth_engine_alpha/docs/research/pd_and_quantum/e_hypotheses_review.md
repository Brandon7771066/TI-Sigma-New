# e Hypotheses Review

Status: OPEN

Three distinct hypotheses are tracked independently.

## H-e1: Radix Economy

Hypothesis: e is the abstract optimum of b / ln(b) and might motivate encoding economy.

Use case: encoding strategy and representation efficiency.

## H-e2: Natural-Log Evidence

Hypothesis: PD units correspond to log-evidence or log-odds style quantities.

Use case: additive evidence accumulation and probabilistic interpretation.

## H-e3: Threshold-Generating Constant

Hypothesis: powers or fractions of e generate PD boundaries.

Use case: threshold construction and transition behavior.

## Separation Rule

- H-e1 does not validate H-e2.
- H-e2 does not validate H-e3.
- Conventional radix optimization is not proof of TI Sigma threshold validity.

## Benchmark Plan

- Evaluate each hypothesis separately on the same held-out set.
- Compare calibration and utility against non-e baselines.
- Keep all e-derived thresholds as research-only until replicated.
