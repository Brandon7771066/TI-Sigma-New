# Sedenion Zero Divisor Hypotheses

Status: PROPOSED_THEORETICAL_EXTENSION

## Motivation

Sedenion constructions can introduce zero divisors, which may collapse separability in high-dimensional feature algebra.

## Hypotheses

1. Zero-divisor neighborhoods may correspond to contradictory evidence clusters with unstable routing.
2. Crystal structural instability may rise near these neighborhoods.
3. Some benchmark failure modes may be better detected as algebraic degeneracy than as scalar threshold errors.

## Risks

- False pattern attribution due to high dimensionality.
- Overfitting to benchmark idiosyncrasies.
- Reduced explainability for non-technical reviewers.

## Falsification Conditions

- No incremental detection gain versus graph/crystal detectors.
- No reproducible relation between proposed degeneracy score and benchmark error clusters.
- Material decrease in explanation clarity.
