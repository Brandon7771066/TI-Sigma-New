# PD Truth Engine Roadmap

## Phase 0: Recovery

- recover thresholds
- recover ratios
- recover historical semantics
- recover Kaggle artifacts
- recover Penrose artifacts

## Phase 1: Standalone PD

- continuous scalar model
- ternary decoder
- soft ternary model
- calibration plans
- standalone research reports

## Phase 2: Shadow Integration

- Truth Engine computes PD fields internally
- PD fields are not used for decisions
- PD fields are not shown in standard outputs

## Phase 3: Explanatory Integration

- PD appears in research reports as additional explanation
- no production decision dependency

## Phase 4: Comparative Integration

A/B evaluation:
- Truth Engine without PD
- Truth Engine with PD

## Phase 5: Production Candidate

- require held-out gains and reliability criteria
- require safety and interpretability checks

## Phase 6: Algebraic Representation

- compare quaternion, octonion, sedenion variants
- maintain provenance and falsification checks

## Phase 7: Quantum Simulation

- qutrit and qumode simulation research
- no hardware or noncomputability claims by default
