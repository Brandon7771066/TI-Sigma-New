# AI Claim Audit Offer (Truth Engine Alpha)

## Service statement
We provide a human-supervised hallucination-audit product that converts public AI answers into client-ready evidence reports.

## What the client receives
- executive_summary.md
- claim_table.csv
- contradiction_map.csv
- citation_audit.csv
- scaffolding_analysis.csv
- corrected_answer_outline.md
- recommended_actions.md
- full_result.json
- claim_graph.json and claim_graph.graphml
- crystal_diagnostics.json and crystal_matrix.csv

## Delivery workflow
1. Intake a public AI answer or claim file.
2. Run Truth Engine Alpha analysis in standard mode.
3. Produce contradiction, citation, and scaffolding diagnostics.
4. Generate client report package.
5. Complete analyst review and sign-off.

## Scope boundaries
- No legal, medical, or investment advice is provided.
- Outputs are analyst support artifacts and require human judgment.
- PD research modules are not part of standard commercial scoring.

## Engagement profiles
- Single-answer hallucination audit.
- Batch claim audit for policy, research, or due diligence.
- Recurring quality-control reviews for AI-assisted teams.

## Evidence policy
- Public source review only unless client provides licensed datasets.
- Citation statuses are explicitly labeled for unsupported, missing, or mischaracterized references.
- Any unresolved contradictions are retained with explicit uncertainty notes.

## Commercial readiness status
Track 1 baseline and release verification artifacts are available under results/verification for audit.
