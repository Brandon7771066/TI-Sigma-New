# Truth Engine Alpha One-Page

## Problem
Current AI systems frequently produce unsupported, mischaracterized, or contextually invalid claims.

## Solution
Truth Engine Alpha audits each factual claim, maps its citations and contradictions, identifies the cause of error, and produces a corrected answer outline.

## Deliverable
A human-reviewed AI Claim and Citation Audit.

## Introductory scope
- One AI answer
- Up to 20 factual claims
- Up to 10 cited sources
- Citation audit
- Contradiction and scope review
- Corrected-answer outline
- Executive summary
- One revision

## Introductory price
$49.

## Turnaround
1-2 business days for one-answer pilot audits.

## Limitations
Research and quality-control support; not autonomous medical, legal, investment, or patent advice.

## Typical output package
- Executive summary
- Claim audit table
- Citation-status audit
- Contradiction and scope analysis
- Corrected answer outline
- Recommended verification actions
