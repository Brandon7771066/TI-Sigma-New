# Limitations

## Truthfulness and safety controls

Every report must distinguish:

- SOURCE-DERIVED FACT
- ENGINE INFERENCE
- USER-SUPPLIED CLAIM
- UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS
- SPECULATIVE COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY

## Warnings

- Medical claims require caution and must not be presented as treatment advice.
- Legal conclusions must be framed as analysis, not legal advice.
- Investment conclusions must not be fabricated.
- Patentability requires formal legal review.
- Safety-critical engineering claims need explicit uncertainty treatment.

## Scope limits

- No paid API requests in the first implementation phase.
- No PDF scraping first.
- No Streamlit app before the CLI works.
- No claim that the full MR operator is empirically validated.
- Optional TI Sigma fields are research-layer hypotheses, not commercial requirements.
