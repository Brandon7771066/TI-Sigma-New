### 1. Physiological synchrony → group cohesion

Tomashin et al. studied synchronous interbeat-interval dynamics and group cohesion, while Gordon et al. found physiological and behavioral synchrony predicted group cohesion and later group-task performance. This is the cleanest LCC-EMP-F1 follow-up.

### 2. Heart-rate synchrony → decision correctness

### 3. Depresjon / OBF-Psychiatric actigraphy

For mood-amplifier relevance, move to within-person dynamics. Here LCC should not be inter-person synchrony. It should be **within-person rhythm coupling**:

[

[

Outcome:

[

This is better for Mood Amplifier than ds007471.

### 4. DANDI / NWB rodent neurophysiology

For live-rodent Mood Amplifier testing, DANDI is still the right ecosystem: it hosts searchable NWB neurophysiology datasets with electrophysiology, optophysiology, and behavioral data. ([Neurodata Without Borders][3])

But the dataset must include an **intervention or behaviorally meaningful state transition**, not just passive recording. Search specifically for:

[
\text{rodent + stimulation/optogenetic + behavior + NWB}
]

Then apply LCC to pre/post stimulation network changes.

If any of these fail, record a negative and move on.
