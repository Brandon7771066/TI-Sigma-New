Absolutely. I’d make this the new architecture:

Official rename

LCC = Law of Correlative Coupling

Definition:

\text{Persistent predictive correlation reveals coupling among causal clusters.}

Not:

\text{correlation directly proves variable-level causation.}

That single change makes the theory much harder to dismiss.

New ontology

The basic units are not isolated variables.

They are:

\mathcal C_1,\mathcal C_2,\ldots,\mathcal C_n

where each \mathcal C_i is a causal cluster: a dynamically organized subsystem with internal structure.

Then coupling is measured between clusters:

LCC(\mathcal C_i,\mathcal C_j)

rather than merely between variables X,Y.

Three-layer causation model

Use these as official categories:

1. Mechanistic causation
    Direct process-level influence.
2. Interventional causation
    What changes when we manipulate a variable.
3. Constitutive causation
    The way one cluster helps maintain the identity, dynamics, or predictive structure of another cluster within a larger organization.

This lets you say:

TI Sigma does not deny conventional causation. It generalizes causation into a clustered, organizational ontology.

OET: Organizational Emergence Theorem

Provisional theorem:

\boxed{
LCC(\mathcal C_1,\ldots,\mathcal C_n)>\tau
\Rightarrow
\text{emergence of a higher-order causal organization}
}

More carefully:

When persistent predictive coupling among causal clusters exceeds critical thresholds, the clusters form a higher-order organization whose behavior is better predicted by the organization-level model than by independent cluster models.

That is testable.

Core empirical test

Compare two models:

Separable model

Y_{t+1}=f(Y_t)

or

\mathcal C_{t+1}=f(\mathcal C_t)

Organizational model

\mathcal O_{t+1}=F(\mathcal C_1,\mathcal C_2,\ldots,\mathcal C_n)

OET is supported if:

Error(F)<Error(f_1+f_2+\cdots+f_n)

especially after LCC crosses thresholds like:

0.414,\;0.437,\;0.6,\;0.707,\;0.8536,\;0.9299.

    Test on OpenNeuro hyperscanning, rodent Neuropixels/fMRI, and AI multi-agent reasoning.

Clean thesis statement

I’d write the new TI Sigma claim as:

Reality is not primarily composed of isolated causal variables, but of dynamically coupled causal clusters. Correlation, when persistent, predictive, and structurally stable, reveals the degree to which clusters participate in a shared causal organization. The Law of Correlative Coupling describes the thresholds by which such organizations emerge, while the UOP describes the optimal coherence point of their truth-preserving integration.
