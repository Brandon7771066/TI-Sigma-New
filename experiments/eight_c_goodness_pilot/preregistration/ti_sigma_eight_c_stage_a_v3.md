## Reconstruction statement

The prior Eight-C pilot archive was not recovered locally. Stage A v3 is therefore a newly constructed, preregistered study based on the current canonical TI Sigma framework and documented conversation-derived development. It does not reuse or claim the empirical status, hashes, manifests, test results, or outputs previously attributed to the lost pilot.

## Framework freeze

Goodness is evaluated as a single scalar independently from the Eight Cs.

The Eight Cs for Stage A v3 are:

1. Coherence
2. Consistency
3. Continuity
4. Concreteness
5. Completion
6. Criticality
7. Closeness
8. Choice

### Concreteness

> **Concreteness is the degree of tangibility or determinate intelligibility of the evaluated entity: how readily it can be understood, represented, or operationally grasped with minimal fuzziness, vagueness, or ambiguity.**

Concreteness in GILE-G does not measure ontological instantiation. Ontological instantiation and concrete physical mechanisms belong to HEM.

### Category boundaries

- Intuition: conscious certainty.
- Love: internal and external binding, extending across scale into relational positive affect and coordinated action.
- Elegance: beauty, simplicity, economy, ideal form, and related aesthetic or formal qualities.
- Goodness: structural and evaluative adequacy expressed through the Eight Cs.
- Cross-component interaction does not imply categorical identity.

## Corpus design

Stage A v3 uses a new 21-item balanced corpus organized into seven three-item clusters:

1. Coherence versus Consistency
2. Continuity versus Closeness
3. Concreteness versus Completion
4. Criticality and accuracy requirements
5. Choice amount versus Choice alignment
6. Local target success versus overall Goodness
7. Cross-scale and cross-domain manifestations

Each cluster contains:

- A: one dimension high, nearby dimension low
- B: reverse profile
- C: coupled or ambiguous case

## Rating and accounting

- 21 items
- 3 independent ratings per item
- 63 planned logical ratings
- maximum 3 attempts per logical rating
- maximum 189 API attempts
- retries never increase logical sample size
- stopping rule fixed at 63 terminal logical ratings

The sample will not be extended because of poor reliability, unexpected results, lack of TI Sigma support, weak contrasts, or lack of significance.

## Contradiction taxonomy

Each rating records $0$-$3$ scores for:

- incomplete_information
- competing_domains
- category_error
- genuine_tradeoff
- self_reference
- temporal_inconsistency
- measurement_uncertainty
- true_incoherence

Contradictions are classifiable features, not automatic disproofs.

## Study goal

Stage A v3 is an instrument-debugging and reproducibility study.

### Primary analyses

- per-C means and dispersion
- schema validity
- first-attempt and eventual validity
- within-item reproducibility
- ICC or suitable agreement estimates
- engineered contrast checks
- Choice versus Concreteness discrimination
- Concreteness versus Completion discrimination
- Closeness versus Continuity discrimination
- contradiction selectivity

### Exploratory analyses

- correlations with Goodness
- conditional mutual information
- leave-one-C-out prediction
- incremental value of Choice
- effective-rank diagnostics
- candidate residual dimensions

Strong factor-structure or completeness claims will not be made from only 21 independent items.
