
MAMEM SSVEP Database (PhysioNet)
================================
- 256-channel EEG recordings
- 11 subjects under flickering light stimulation
- Steady-state visual evoked potentials (SSVEP)
- Frequencies: 6.66, 7.50, 8.57, 10.00, 12.00 Hz
- Duration: ~20 seconds per trial

SSVEP = Neural Entrainment
--------------------------
When the brain is exposed to rhythmic visual stimulation,
neural oscillations ENTRAIN to the stimulus frequency.
This creates measurable peaks in the EEG power spectrum
at the stimulation frequency and its harmonics.

Relevance to LCC/Consciousness:
- Entrainment shows how external rhythms can influence brain states
- AVE (Audio-Visual Entrainment) is used therapeutically
- Tests whether consciousness responds to external rhythmic influence
